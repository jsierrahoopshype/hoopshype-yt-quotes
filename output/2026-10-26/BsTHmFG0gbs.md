**3. Steve Smith — "It is straight up what he said. It's not beef. It's great PR team." — Steve Smith analyzes the competitive nature of Tatum and Brown** [01:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BsTHmFG0gbs&t=96s)

**Steve Smith:** "The damn grill is so fired up and it is ready to barbecue whatever is going to be thrown on the grill. And I'm going to tell you what ain't thrown on the grill: ain't no fish, it ain't no planks. It is straight up what he said. It's not beef. It's great PR team. You got one guy who ain't about that life and you got another guy who he said, be ready, stay ready. That means I'm going to pull up, and when I pull up, stay less."

**Peter Schrager:** "What does that even mean?"

**Steve Smith:** "It's ego, man. It's ego, but competitiveness. So you're going to be competitive. That means on the court, when I high-five, when I, oh, we're former champs, let's hug and embrace, no, it's like, hey, you better bring your A-game, because I'm coming at you. If I get an opportunity to dunk on you, I will. If I get an opportunity to steal a ball, give you an extra little, you know, throw a little hot sauce on that chicken wing, then I'll do that."

Steve Smith: "These are two superstars who one believes he was wronged in his exit and another guy is playing the, I don't know what happened, I'm just as shocked as you. That's the part in the competitive sports that you go, stop. I mean, you got to say, okay, when I see you, we might have to have a talk because this I didn't know came out of the blue. I could tell you as being a guy on the team, when they were key players on the team, I had the opportunity to call, like, yo, what are we doing? Are we really getting rid of this person? Well, how is that helping me? So you have that kind of conversation with management. So to say that he had no idea, I disagree just a lot."
