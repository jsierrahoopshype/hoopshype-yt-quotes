**2. Shams Charania — "The Thunder have lost both Jalen Williams and Cason Wallace for most, if not the entire, regular season." — Shams Charania reports on Jalen Williams and Cason Wallace injuries** [00:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szwl61iD-jk&t=58s)

**Scott Van Pelt:** "The Thunder have lost both **Jalen Williams** and **Cason Wallace** for most, if not the entire, regular season."

**Shams Charania:** "**Jalen Williams** has been diagnosed with a dislocated left ankle as well as a fracture in that same left leg. **Cason Wallace** has been diagnosed with a torn labrum. He's going to undergo surgery. Both players will have surgeries that will cost them several months of the season, and these are devastating blows to the Thunder who had championship hopes this season."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szwl61iD-jk&t=58s

**3. Shams Charania — "They do have three first-round picks they can use in trade, they do also have an open roster spot." — Shams Charania on Thunder's roster depth and potential trade moves** [02:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szwl61iD-jk&t=168s)

Shams Charania: "The fact that the Thunder took San Antonio to seven games was a lot of an accomplishment in and of itself because they were down not only **Jalen Williams** but **AJ Mitchell** as well. **AJ Mitchell** is healthy, he's ready to go. **Jared McCain**, a full season of him. You see **Chet Holmgren** so far this season has looked much more aggressive. **Shai Gilgeous-Alexander** already this year is looking like the best player in the world. So they have that going for them. But accounting for **Jalen Williams** as well now as **Cason Wallace** being out for the entire season potentially, that is something that's going to be potentially insurmountable for this Thunder group. But they're going to be all steam ahead to try to see if this group as currently constructed can get the job done. They do have three first-round picks they can use in trade, they do also have an open roster spot. So we'll see what happens between now and the February trade deadline as well."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szwl61iD-jk&t=168s
