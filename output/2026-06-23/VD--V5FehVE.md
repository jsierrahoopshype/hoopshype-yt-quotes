**3. Tim Legler — "The number one thing they need is shooting. They were just anemic." — Tim Legler identifies shooting as Dallas's biggest roster weakness** [02:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VD--V5FehVE&t=159s)

Tim Legler: "The number one thing they need is shooting. They were just anemic. When you talk about modern NBA basketball, you can't make 11 threes a night and expect to really compete. 29th in makes, bottom five in attempts and percentage. For me, that's the most glaring weakness on the roster. I think you could also use some size at the power forward position. So those Michigan guys, they might come into play with that ninth pick. We'll see where he wants to go with that, but I think shooting for me has to be it. **Cooper Flagg** is a dynamic playmaker, you expect to get **Kyrie Irving** back, you need guys off the ball that can space the floor to give them more opportunities to work."

**4. Jay Bilas — "I think it's going to be a little bit of a learning curve for him." — Jay Bilas evaluates Dusty May's transition to the NBA** [03:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VD--V5FehVE&t=197s)

**5. Jay Bilas — "He's not like one of these yellers and screamers. He brings everybody in." — Jay Bilas on Dusty May's coaching style and leadership** [03:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VD--V5FehVE&t=231s)

Jay Bilas: "**Dusty May** is from Indiana, and he went to IU, and he was a manager for **Bob Knight**. So think about that journey. One of his jobs for Coach Knight was on road trips, he'd have to get him no-yeast donuts and orange juice and leave it at his door. Then he goes to FAU as the head coach, takes them to the Final Four where he rode his bike to work every day, wins a national championship at Michigan, but he's not like one of these yellers and screamers. He brings everybody in. One of the things that he does in practice is he wants the guys to make mistakes in practice in a safe environment, feels like they learn better that way. So I think the way he deals with people and brings everybody on board is really key. He's not just a coach, he's a leader."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VD--V5FehVE&t=231s
