**2. Zainab Johnson — "I remember my first year of comedy, I just would not plan anything." — Zainab Johnson on her early stand-up comedy development process** [14:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E6A5OrcIUzE&t=867s)

Zainab Johnson: "Honestly, I think I take from everybody from the people that we know like **Eddie Murphy** or **Dave Chappelle**, like people that I have been watching for years. And just like people that I work with like every night. Very early when I first started doing stand-up, I would talk to my friends and they would always say, that is so funny, you should say that on stage. And then I would say it on stage and it would not be funny at all. I remember my first year of comedy, I just would not plan anything. I would write down a word, like if it was like, I don't know, maybe if I had a story about traffic or something, I would just write down traffic. And then I would record myself to see like, when I'm talking, when I'm thinking, like what do I do?"

**3. Zainab Johnson — "Beyoncé said if you don't get nervous, you don't care." — Zainab Johnson discusses managing pre-show nerves and stage confidence** [14:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E6A5OrcIUzE&t=867s)

**Chris Spencer:** "Do you get nervous?"

**Zainab Johnson:** "Yeah, of course. **Beyoncé** said if you don't get nervous, you don't care."

**Dwyane Wade:** "**Beyoncé** said."

**Zainab Johnson:** "Yeah, I get very nervous every single show."

**Chris Spencer:** "I have never seen it on you. You have this confidence."

**Zainab Johnson:** "Yeah, I get very nervous and I just have to in my mind say, it's okay, just do what you do. Over the years I've gotten okay with it's only about me in these 10 minutes and I just have to stay the course of myself. And so I'm very intentional about that. So if I seem comfortable, it's because I could kill or I could bomb, but I'll only appreciate either one of them if I'm truly myself."
