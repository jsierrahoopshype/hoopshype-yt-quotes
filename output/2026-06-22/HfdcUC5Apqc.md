**Matt Barnes:** "First off, he was our MJ. So we had to go at him. And I always saw how highly he spoke of you. He said you were the best that ever guarded him as a one-on-one defender. So I always wanted to say congratulations on that."

**2. Matt Barnes — "He just went out and got motherfuckers like me and Ron. He was tired of dealing with us." — Matt Barnes on Kobe Bryant's competitive nature and work ethic** [00:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HfdcUC5Apqc&t=33s)

Matt Barnes: "He just went out and got motherfuckers like me and Ron. He was tired of dealing with us. The year before, Ron tried to choke him and then I had the ball fake and I was ready to whip him. He was like, 'Fuck dealing with these motherfuckers, I'm going to play with them.' I just always saw it as a huge challenge. It's well documented that he was really young on an older, on a pro team, so he was on our campus all the time, eating. I found out he was taking classes, I didn't even know he was taking classes. But he would always be on our campus."

Just a sicko. And then he came back that next season shooting a bunch of left-handed shots. It was always a badge of honor to have to guard him. Every single night I'm guarding the best players in the world: Melo, Ginobili, Dwyane Wade, Kobe, LeBron, KD, Paul Pierce, Tracy McGrady. You name it, every single night those were our matchups, so I really looked forward to that shit."

**5. Matt Barnes — "Anyone crazy enough to fuck with me is crazy enough to play with me, you want to be a Laker?" — Matt Barnes on his free agency and joining the Lakers** [02:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HfdcUC5Apqc&t=171s)

Matt Barnes: "I was a free agent after that summer and they, Boston beat us in the Eastern Finals, and Lakers got their payback that year and I was a free agent. I was actually talking to **Pat Riley** and **Dwyane Wade** about going to Miami and this is the year that LeBron and Bosh and them were coming. He was there trying to tell me what the line was to be in crunch time and I'm sold. I'm already in Orlando, fuck it, I'm going to go up the freeway to Miami. I get a call from a number I don't know and it's Kobe. We start talking, I congratulate him and he literally told me, 'Anyone crazy enough to fuck with me is crazy enough to play with me, you want to be a Laker?' When I tell you I was in Orlando when I took that call, four days later I'm in LA signing a contract with the Lakers."

**6. Matt Barnes — "Kobe started popping up with me, like how the fuck you get Kobe out?" — Matt Barnes on Kobe Bryant's off-court friendship and lifestyle** [03:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HfdcUC5Apqc&t=224s)

Matt Barnes: "I like to get down in my spare time, smoke something and get out in the streets sometimes. Kobe started popping up with me, like how the fuck you get Kobe out? Kobe doesn't come out. It would be times where, 'MB, what you doing?' I'm like, 'You know, I'm going to go smoke, you want to come smoke?' I was like, 'If we win a championship, I'll smoke with you, but hit me when you're done smoking and we'll go do something.' So we'd be going places and then that was when I kind of saw some of the Laker shit I saw with Kobe was like, it was like Batman. We used to go from the Staples Center under the street to his condo with the Ritz. He had a spot at the Ritz, you know, and we were able to, we didn't even see the street. We would just go all underground."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HfdcUC5Apqc&t=224s

**7. Matt Barnes — "My favorite thing about him was the way he embraced the twins." — Matt Barnes on Kobe Bryant's relationship with his daughters** [04:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HfdcUC5Apqc&t=294s)

Matt Barnes: "One thing I always loved about him that was my favorite thing about him was the way he embraced the twins. From like four years old when I started playing with the Lakers, every time a shoe dropped, he would send it to the twins. And when they started getting older and playing, he would send it to their whole team in the locker room. That was Uncle Kobe. He would wrestle with them and give them jerseys. I showed a little bit of that workout when they were ten. I surprised them and took them to Orange County and had him train them."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HfdcUC5Apqc&t=294s

**8. Matt Barnes — "I can't show these motherfuckers that." — Matt Barnes on the private side of Kobe Bryant** [05:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HfdcUC5Apqc&t=357s)

Matt Barnes: "I remember two things. I remember when the **Drake** and **Rick Ross** song came out, 'Stay Scheming' and you weren't with me shooting in the gym. I remember when that shit came out and how mad he was about that. It was crazy. And I'm going to kind of leave that where that was. But then I also remember another time where we were just having a good time. We were in Phoenix on the bus about to get on the plane and we were joking, talking shit because I came in there off the rip just talking shit. I, you know me, I talk shit. I'm talking shit to Kobe and he's talking back so it kind of changes the whole mood. But one day I was literally like, 'Bro, how come you don't show the rest of the world this side of you?' And he like thought for a second, he's like, 'MB, I can't show these motherfuckers that.' You know what I mean? Because he always showed the rest of the world the Mamba. I got to know Kobe, the person and the businessman and the father, how much he loved my kids and just the man behind the mask. And that's what I treasure most about our friendship and how our journey and our past crossed."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HfdcUC5Apqc&t=357s

**9. Matt Barnes — "They don't know so they think, 'Oh, I work hard.' Like, nah, nigga, you don't work like Kobe." — Matt Barnes on the 'Mamba Mentality' myth** [07:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HfdcUC5Apqc&t=438s)

Matt Barnes: "They don't know so they think, 'Oh, I work hard.' Like, nah, nigga, you don't work like Kobe. I remember one time I went out to Orange County, he was like, 'All right, MB, you probably should get a hotel.' I'm like, 'No, I'm just going to drive down in the morning.' He's like, 'No, we're going to be on the track at 4:30.' I said, 'What you mean 4:30? The sun don't come up till 6.' He's like, 'I know.' So motherfucker, we hit the track, ran all morning, went to, and I pride myself, you know me, we had to work hard. Like I pride myself on working hard, bro. The time I work out with him, I was dead. Like we hit the track, then hit the weight room and lift so much weight you feel like you can't even motherfucking tie your shoes or coach your dick to piss, you know what I mean? And then right from there, we're in the gym shooting. I'm just like, 'Hold on, bro, let me get my arm. My arms are dead.' Right, you know, but just his relentlessness on how hard he really worked and how much he really worked. You hear stories about he didn't, he didn't really go out, you know what I mean? That's why people would trip out when they would see him with me. Like, 'What the fuck, how you get Kobe out?' Like, he wanted to work all the time."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HfdcUC5Apqc&t=438s
