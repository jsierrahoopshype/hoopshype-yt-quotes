**Thanasis Antetokounmpo:** "Let's just say you had a connection to someone who might have an NBA Finals jersey, right? Let's just call it Game 6 from 2021, hypothetically. If that thing hits the market, number 34 on it, do you have any clue what an item like that goes for when someone comes up to you and says I want to consign this with you?"

**Ken Goldin:** "I always use comps. What I look at is the ultimate high-end. You can't compare anybody to Jordan. But then you can look at a contemporary and say, okay, LeBron is going to be the most out of any active player and some of his Finals jerseys have sold. They are seven-figure pieces, depending if they won or lost the game, how he did. And then you try to find somebody else that's not LeBron. Like a Curry jersey done it, has a Durant. And then that's kind of where I figure it out. But you can't ever predict is are there going to be two people who don't give a damn about the comps, who are massive fans, who say this is a one-shot once-in-a-lifetime, and something that I may think is going to go for 500 or 600,000 goes for 3.6 million. And that's happened to me before."
