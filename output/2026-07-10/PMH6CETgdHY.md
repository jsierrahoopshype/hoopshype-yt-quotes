**5. Brian Windhorst — "they didn't do a move that affected their glaring need" — Pistons' offseason moves fail to address their glaring needs** [05:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PMH6CETgdHY&t=355s)

Brian Windhorst: "Their move was to trade for **Isaiah Joe**, who is definitely a shooter, but he's a backup. They resigned **Kevin Huerter**, which, okay, you're signing a shooter, that's what you want, but **Landry Shamet** got six million dollars a year, **Kevin Huerter** got nine million. Why don't you just go sign **Landry Shamet** for nine million? They literally signed them on the same day with like in an hour. Huerter, nine million, Shamet, six million. Shamet shot 10% more on threes last year than Huerter did. And then they let **Tobias Harris** walk. Tobias goes for 15 a year to San Antonio. That hurts losing him. They signed **John Collins**, it's a three-year contract, but really it's a one-year contract, only one-year guaranteed, so you're already admitting that it's a stopgap. I'm worried about them not maxing out this group."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PMH6CETgdHY&t=355s

**6. Brian Windhorst — "the way they handled this Giannis situation, they made the best of it" — Heat maximized their trade assets in the Giannis Antetokounmpo situation** [09:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PMH6CETgdHY&t=553s)

Brian Windhorst: "The way they handled this Giannis situation, they made the best of it because they had Boston and Miami bidding against each other in the end and Miami put so much stuff in that trade that Milwaukee, obviously they would have gotten more for Giannis if they traded him two years ago, but the situation they were in, they maximized their situation. It's not even so much that the players that Miami sent were transformational players. I honestly think that **Jaime Jaquez** would be the best player ever in history of Giannis's career next to him. But because it cost so much, the picks and the swaps and all those players, that it depleted what Miami has, not only what they can put on the court, but also what they can trade for other pieces."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PMH6CETgdHY&t=553s
