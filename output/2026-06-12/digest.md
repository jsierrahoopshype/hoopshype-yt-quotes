**7. Rasheed Wallace — "I did a blunderhead move myself against those same damn Spurs." — Rasheed Wallace on his regret over a specific play against the Spurs** [24:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eD9v1E6Q_W0&t=1463s)

Rasheed Wallace: "I did a blunderhead move myself against those same damn Spurs. We playing in the Palace, and so it was a sideline out of bounds play, and Robert Horry taking the ball out. I'm on the ball, he throws it into the coffin corner to Manu, and something instinctively was like, 'Oh, let's trap this motherfucker.' I go over, I try to trap him, Manu jumps out of bounds and just kicks it right back to Big Shot Bob and the rest is history."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eD9v1E6Q_W0&t=1463s
