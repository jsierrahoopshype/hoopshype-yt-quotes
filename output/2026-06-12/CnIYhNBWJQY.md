# Tim Legler reveals how De’Aaron Fox can bounce back from Game 4 woes 👀 | The Rich Eisen Show — *NBA on ESPN*

**1. Tim Legler — "The play was to veer off, take the foul, eat clock, and put your team up three." — De'Aaron Fox's decision-making on final play against the Pistons** [01:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnIYhNBWJQY&t=85s)

Tim Legler: "The decision-making in that situation is you veer off, you kill a couple more seconds, they are going to grab you. You are going to get fouled, but you probably ate up another two seconds there. And then you have to go to the line and make two very big, important, pressure free throws, which the guy has been an excellent free-throw shooter throughout his career. That is the play to make. I think he was just counting on his speed. He is one of the quickest guys in the league and he thought he could get his hands on that basketball and beat whoever was trailing him to the rim."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnIYhNBWJQY&t=85s

Tim Legler: "They hit 14 threes in the first half. It was a historic shooting performance by Finals standards. They have this big lead. They come out to start the third quarter, you are still dipping your toe in that water. They were getting whatever they wanted, they were in great flow, and they continued to take those shots."

**3. Tim Legler — "The only way you can solve this math problem in terms of the deficit is quick shots." — Knicks' math problem regarding deficit and time** [04:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnIYhNBWJQY&t=240s)

Tim Legler: "I keep referring to this as a math problem for the Knicks. The only way you can solve this math problem in terms of the deficit and the time on the clock is you are going to need help. And how do you get that help? There is two ways: quick shots by the Spurs that do not go in and turnovers, because you need more possessions and you need more time."
