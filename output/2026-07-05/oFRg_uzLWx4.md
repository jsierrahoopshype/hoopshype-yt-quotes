**1. Mike Brown — "I feel like I can work just as hard as anybody out there." — Mike Brown on his coaching philosophy and work ethic** [00:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oFRg_uzLWx4&t=20s)

Mike Brown: "I always felt like I could work with anybody. When I say work with them, I am talking outwork them. I never will sit in front of anybody and say I am the smartest cookie in the jar. Do I think I am dumb? No. Do I think I am the smartest guy? No. But I feel like I can work just as hard as anybody out there. I also feel like if I want to, I can outwork anybody out there. Those two things I have control over."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oFRg_uzLWx4&t=20s

**2. Mike Brown — "If I had a practice plan that was two and a half hours, you best believe it was." — Mike Brown's rigid and methodical approach to team practices** [01:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oFRg_uzLWx4&t=70s)

Mike Brown: "If I had a practice plan that was two and a half hours, you best believe it was boom, boom, 10 minutes here, 15 minutes here, these coaches here, this here. When I used to meet, I used to ask the coach whoever is leading that drill, okay, show me what you are going to do in front of players because I did not want to look bad in front of a group of players as a staff. But if I had a two and a half hour practice, you best believe we were getting through it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oFRg_uzLWx4&t=70s

**3. Mike Brown — "If the night before I had pulled 45 clips, you best believe we watched all 45 clips." — Mike Brown's intense preparation and film study habits** [01:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oFRg_uzLWx4&t=97s)

Mike Brown: "If the night before I had pulled 45 clips, you best believe we watched all 45 clips. So, I was pretty rigid and methodical during my approach that first few years. Did it help? Yes, because I had a young team led by LeBron James that probably needed some structure and some guidance and some direction when it came to being diligent about what is in front of you to be prepared for games."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oFRg_uzLWx4&t=97s

**4. Mike Brown — "Watching Pop, watching Steve Kerr, their ability to message and be flexible to me is off the charts." — Mike Brown on the importance of messaging and flexibility** [02:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oFRg_uzLWx4&t=157s)

Mike Brown: "Watching Pop, watching **Steve Kerr**, their ability to message and be flexible to me is off the charts. I actually used to when I worked for Steve, I used to carry a little notepad in my back pocket and my little red pen, and I take notes. I took very, very few notes on the X's and O's part. The notes I took were about how he delivered his message to the team, when he did it, how long he went, what his body language was, all those other things."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oFRg_uzLWx4&t=157s

**5. Mike Brown — "My ability to message to you guys more than the X's and O's." — Mike Brown's shift towards prioritizing player connection and messaging** [03:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oFRg_uzLWx4&t=210s)

Mike Brown: "Going in this year, for me, the most important thing was my ability to message to you guys more than the X's and O's. And that is why when some of the stuff did not fit Cat initially, if I was younger, I would have kept Cat after practice, made him go through more of this, more of that. But it is about, okay, it is not always the X's and O's. Sometimes it is about putting people in the right spot to make them feel appreciated."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oFRg_uzLWx4&t=210s

**6. Josh Hart — "We all need to stay locked in because we have all been doubted before." — Josh Hart on Mike Brown's messaging during the playoffs** [04:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oFRg_uzLWx4&t=264s)

**Josh Hart:** "One of the messaging aspects was that you were like, it was perfect. I think it was after Game 3 or Game 4. It was like, we are all in a good situation now, but we have all needed to stay locked in because we have all been doubted before. And I was like, sending you going through the list of everybody. I am like, you are the doubter of being too small, unathletic, not good enough, not fast enough, not quick enough."

**Mike Brown:** "I appreciate it. And I will tell you, throughout the course of this run, I got help with the messaging from different people. And I am going to tell you guys, you guys may know this, you may not, Dylan was fabulous. I spoke to Dylan often during this playoff run because he had just gone through it with Oklahoma City."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oFRg_uzLWx4&t=264s

**7. Mike Brown — "You remember the video that we showed of you guys when you lost the Eastern Conference Finals?" — Mike Brown on the origin of the team's playoff motivation** [06:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oFRg_uzLWx4&t=366s)

Mike Brown: "You remember the video that we showed of you guys when you lost the Eastern Conference Finals to the Pacers? That was his idea. And he wanted to show still shots of the, you know, because they did something similar to that because they lost from the year before. And so we just took it another level and Corey put together the video just to remind everybody that, hey, this is what it felt like."

**8. Jalen Brunson — "When you mess around, other people think it is okay to mess around." — Jalen Brunson on the need for seriousness during practice** [09:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oFRg_uzLWx4&t=566s)

**Jalen Brunson:** "How many times, not even practice days, there be days where like, we are not doing drills, we are walking through, we are talking stuff. I am like, Josh, stop. Like, if you want to mess around in your head, just do that, but don't do it with other people because they will follow your lead."

**Mike Brown:** "I even went to him a couple of times and I am like, I know Josh is, is Josh okay? He is like, yeah, don't worry about him. I am like, okay, good."
