Muggsy Bogues: "It started early in my career when I was eight to nine years old. But I knew you had 10 seconds to get the ball across half court. I knew that it starts with the point guard. Point guard is the one that put everybody in situations and run the team to the extension of the coach. So I always knew if I can disrupt him, it's going to make it tougher for the other guys."

Being around the players that I played with, the **Reggie Williams**, the **David Wingate**, maybe **Reggie Lewis**, may he rest in peace. Being with that type of group, and Coach Wade understood what I was and what I was capable of doing, and he gave me the freedom to be me. I had all the schools that was coming at me. I didn't have the Georgetown because they had a guy by **Michael Jackson** at the time, but Wake Forest, choosing Wake, also allowed me to continue to be on that national stage."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9e0qGoJAfQ&t=294s

**5. Muggsy Bogues — "We were the last collegiate team that won the gold medal before they sent the Dream Team." — Muggsy Bogues on winning the gold medal in 1986** [06:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9e0qGoJAfQ&t=411s)

Muggsy Bogues: "I think the turning point was when we played in the last collegiate team that won the gold medal. We won it in '86. We went ahead and myself, the **David Robinson**, and the **Kenny Smith**, we beat USSR, which is Russia, Sabonis and them. And getting that and being the one that led that team, it really got the NBA on notice in terms of, well damn, he's too small, maybe he can't do that. He can't play on that next level, they're too big. But then going to Port Smith and going to the combine and doing what I do, the same thing was happening there. So that allowed me to be the 12th player chosen in 1987."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9e0qGoJAfQ&t=411s

Muggsy Bogues: "Everybody have a tendency, right? So me knowing that once the ball goes down, you can bring that back up. It can't stop it. The dribble goes down, you cannot stop it. They think they run by you, I let them go by me because I play defense from behind. And one thing about pressing the ball, nobody want to get stripped at half court."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9e0qGoJAfQ&t=545s

So those type of guys, of course, Spud and **Michael Adams** and those guys, you know, but it wasn't one guy that really stood out that really just gave me a lot of difficulties because, you know, I'm pressing the ball. ## Mike Brown Reveals His Secrets to NBA Coaching Success — *Roommates Show*

Do I think I am dumb? No. Do I think I am the smartest guy? No. But I feel like I can work just as hard as anybody out there. I also feel like if I want to, I can outwork anybody out there.
