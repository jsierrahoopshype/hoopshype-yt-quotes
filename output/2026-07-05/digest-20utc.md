# HoopsHype YT Quotes — 2026-07-05

## Zach Randolph lost 500k over ONE POUND 😳 — *Out The Mud*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j0rscG2AmmU

_Speakers identified: Zach Randolph, Tony Allen_

**1. Zach Randolph — "I was with Raymond from my second year to the end of my career, 17-18 years." — Zach Randolph on his agent selection and career longevity** [00:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j0rscG2AmmU&t=29s)

**Tony Allen:** "How did your agency process go? Because I know you had to work out with a lot of teams. Was the agent process difficult?"

**Zach Randolph:** "My agent process really wasn't difficult. I had my first agent, then I changed after my first year. I went to **Raymond Brothers**. So I was with Raymond from my second year to the end of my career, 17-18 years. Shout out to Raymond, man."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j0rscG2AmmU&t=29s

**2. Zach Randolph — "I had a weight clause. I had to be 250 pounds to get the money." — Zach Randolph explains losing a 500k bonus due to weight** [01:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j0rscG2AmmU&t=60s)

**Tony Allen:** "Why did you switch? I know a lot of guys may switch based on where they fell in the draft, and there were 13 guys who went ahead of you."

**Zach Randolph:** "I had a weight clause. I'm the 19th pick, so the money is slotted already. But you get an extra percentage when you come into rookie camp. On your contract, you'll get an extra 4% or something like that. Mine was like a half a million, and I had a weight clause. I had to be 250 pounds to get the money to get the 500,000. I didn't know this, and I came in at 251."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j0rscG2AmmU&t=60s

**3. Zach Randolph — "My agent was named Mike Harrison, and I found out some way, and I'm like, 'Bro, come on.'" — Zach Randolph on learning the business side of NBA contracts** [02:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j0rscG2AmmU&t=140s)

**Tony Allen:** "When did you catch on to it though?"

**Zach Randolph:** "To the end, when my agent was recruiting me, and he told me that you missed this money for the one pound. My agent was named **Mike Harrison**, and I found out some way, and I'm like, 'Bro, come on. How the fuck? I need that five, man. I'm coming from zero. I never had nothing.'"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j0rscG2AmmU&t=140s

**4. Zach Randolph — "Perk used to have to make weight. And bro, the man used to get up, we had practice." — Zach Randolph on Kendrick Perkins' work ethic and conditioning** [03:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j0rscG2AmmU&t=182s)

**Tony Allen:** "I used to see Perk do that, because Perk used to have to make weight."

**Zach Randolph:** "Perk used to have to make weight. And bro, the man used to get up, we had practice. We already got conditioned before we go on the court. We got a weight room workout, conditioning, and stretching before we go on the court. Man, Perk, a being that motherfucker doing his conditioning before we even come in and stretch. And I'm like, 'Damn, what he doing?' He doing that because he got to make weight. And that's when I really started realizing that's business of basketball."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j0rscG2AmmU&t=182s

**5. Zach Randolph — "Raymond stuck by my side every single step of the way." — Zach Randolph on Raymond Brothers' mentorship and support** [04:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j0rscG2AmmU&t=291s)

**Tony Allen:** "Just dealing with Raymond, and Raymond prepping me for that, understanding, watching all my games. Raymond watched all my games, bro."

**Zach Randolph:** "Raymond stuck by my side every single step of the way. I'm talking about from family members when I was living check to check when I first came in the league, and hitting him like, 'Man, you know, he always was there for me.' Now, only that, just talking. I lost my mom, my brother, he was there for me, my grandma. So shout out to Ray."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j0rscG2AmmU&t=291s
