**3. Carmelo Anthony — "It's a lot when you're at that point of your career to make those decisions." — The impact of free agency rumors on a veteran player** [03:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NOsjK89UQoA&t=186s)

**4. Carmelo Anthony — "The idea of what they want from a guard has changed." — The evolution of the point guard position in the NBA** [05:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NOsjK89UQoA&t=331s)

Carmelo Anthony: "The idea of what they want from a guard has changed. You have to be able to shoot the ball. And that's the only thing that I think came to work against him towards the end of his career because now, for all the years you were able to use speed, you were able to use attack, force, athleticism, that got you to who you are today. That got you as a Hall of Famer playing that way. But now the game changes and now you got to shoot more threes and now he's still playing downhill, the way you should play. You should play downhill, but now the games is shifting and it keeps shifting."
