**5. Trevor Ariza — "he literally texted me, this is he told me on the plane, 'All right, I'm a text you like a formula that I want you to follow.'" — Trevor Ariza on receiving a specific workout formula from Kobe Bryant** [06:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hZWaw0t9sCw&t=363s)

Trevor Ariza: "He literally texted me, this is he told me on the plane, 'All right, I'm a text you like a formula that I want you to follow.' And then a week went by, so I'm like, 'Man, this nigga is full of shit.' And then the next day a text come through and it's like, 'All right, young fella, this is the formula right here. I want you to do this, this, this, this, this.' And I did that shit every single day, along with the shit that I was already doing. But every single day I just rebound, screen, cut. I mean, people that was in the triangle know what that mean. So it's like I was doing that, I was like what do you call it, switch cuts. I was doing all of the little things, working on things inside of the system that I was going to get chances to score at."
