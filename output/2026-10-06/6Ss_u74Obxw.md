# Donovan Mitchell On Knicks Conference Finals Loss, Entering Year 10 & Becoming A Leader In Cleveland — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

**1. Donovan Mitchell — "We had a chance to close both teams out in six. We didn't take care of business." — Donovan Mitchell on the Cavaliers' playoff series against the Knicks and Pistons** [00:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6Ss_u74Obxw&t=46s)

**Vince Carter:** "How do you evaluate your team's performance and what are you looking for to this year?"

**Donovan Mitchell:** "We lose perspective a lot. We got swept and we deserved the criticism that comes with that. We went to Game 7s, but we had a chance to close both teams out in six. We didn't take care of business. If we would have taken care of Detroit at home and not lost by 20, we have four days off heading into the next round. I am not saying we win or lose, but it gives you the opportunity. We didn't take care of business."

**2. Donovan Mitchell — "We were 17 and 15 at one point with Darius Garland out, Evan Mobley out." — Donovan Mitchell on the Cavaliers' injury-plagued season and roster challenges** [01:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6Ss_u74Obxw&t=105s)

Donovan Mitchell: "The whole year as a whole, as an evaluation, we were 17 and 15 at one point with Darius Garland out, Evan Mobley out. Shout out to our two-ways, I give them a ton of credit because we had our two-ways and our rookie starting, and we were still trying to find a way to keep ourselves afloat. You know how long the season is. To go from that to now you are winning 50 games, that is a big step. Then we trade for **James** halfway through the season. There is so much that goes into that that I think is more that we look at as, okay, we took a step."

Vince Carter: "That also is an advantage in some way when you have those type of injuries because it gives those other guys an opportunity to get a lot of reps. Now we are in the playoffs, okay, who do I trust? So there is a process that I am sure you go through and then you can point out who you can add to your roster when you are in the playoffs."

**4. Donovan Mitchell — "Was fatigue an issue for sure? But in the moment, do you feel that? No." — Donovan Mitchell on fatigue and the mental toll of the playoffs** [03:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6Ss_u74Obxw&t=194s)

Donovan Mitchell: "Was fatigue an issue for sure? But in the moment, do you feel that? No. In the moment, I am not sitting there, man, I am gassed because we played for how many days. If you look back at the schedule, we were playing every other day for a month straight, sure. But you have to give credit where credit is due, they swept us. It takes a lot to sweep a team. We had an opportunity to win Game 1 and we blew it."

**5. Donovan Mitchell — "You start to lose sight on the intentionality of every single detail." — Donovan Mitchell on losing focus on details during playoff games** [05:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6Ss_u74Obxw&t=346s)

Donovan Mitchell: "You start to lose sight on the intentionality of every single detail. Because you are up 25 with nine to go, you get away from the principles. And then you got role players on their team knocking shots down. I think **Landry Shamet** was big that game. Obviously **JB** was doing his thing. There is different things we could have done. We could have switched coverages, we could have called timeout, we could have knocked down a shot, could have drove to the basket instead of settling for a jump shot."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6Ss_u74Obxw&t=346s

**6. Donovan Mitchell — "I understand what my body needs, so I am prepared if we do go 30 days." — Donovan Mitchell on his training and physical preparation for long playoff runs** [07:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6Ss_u74Obxw&t=448s)

Donovan Mitchell: "I have never been to the conference finals until this year, so I have gotten done later. So now I understand what my body needs, so I am prepared if we do go 30 days, whatever it is. I bet, okay, cool, I need to run more or I need to shoot more when I am tired. Those catch and shoots, I cannot miss those opportunities. Every rep, I was just telling somebody this when I was working out this morning, I am talking to myself when I miss like three in a row. When you are tired and you feel like, man, I am just so exhausted, it just puts that in the front of your brain."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6Ss_u74Obxw&t=448s

**7. Donovan Mitchell — "Mike really showed me how to navigate that, how to speak to the individual." — Donovan Mitchell on the leadership lessons learned from Mike Conley and Ricky Rubio** [10:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6Ss_u74Obxw&t=625s)

Donovan Mitchell: "I got to give **Ricky Rubio** your credit for this too, because having both of them to start my career. You go from **Ricky Rubio**, who we all know, legend in himself, and then **Mike Conley**, who managed a lot of personalities in that locker room. You had an international player, **Mark**, you had **Zibo**, and you had **Tony Allen**. Then you had coming **Rudy Gay** coming in there. Mike really showed me how to navigate that, how to speak to the individual. I was not the greatest at it in Utah because I had Mike, I had Joe, I had vets. But when you become the older guy in the locker room when I got to Cleveland, you start to realize, okay, how much can I pull from like an Ed, or a JA, DG, now James, Jaylen Tyson, Sam Merrill, Max Strus. How can I speak to each different player, different person? And then sometimes it is not saying nothing and just leading by example."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6Ss_u74Obxw&t=625s
