Mike Greenberg: "Here's the answer to your question. The answer to your question is what is the alternative to success? Because if the alternative to success is failure, then yes, it's a success. If they play a representative series and lose to a really good **San Antonio** or **Oklahoma City** team, then yes, I think the season is a success.
