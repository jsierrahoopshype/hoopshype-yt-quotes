# Vince Carter & T-Mac On Social Media’s Impact On Players, Potential Franchise Stars & Player Loyalty — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6w_dajNrGLA

_Speakers identified: Vince Carter, Tracy McGrady_

**1. Vince Carter — "the motivation is who is saying what about them on social media at halftime" — young players are negatively affected by social media criticism** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6w_dajNrGLA&t=0s)

Vince Carter: "Especially today's kid gets caught up into what is said on these things. That was probably the most frustrating thing for me, year 22, going into a locker room. I have told you this a lot of times. At halftime, we are walking into a locker room at halftime, you know that screen is there, the film is watching for halftime. These guys are not even paying attention. They are on the phone and the motivation is who is saying what about them on social media at halftime."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6w_dajNrGLA&t=0s

**2. Tracy McGrady — "how can one of your teammates say the exact thing somebody on Twitter, Instagram said" — teammates should not let social media affect their team dynamics** [02:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6w_dajNrGLA&t=133s)

Tracy McGrady: "I do not understand how can your team, one of your teammates, say the exact thing somebody on Twitter, Instagram, or whatever social media platform said, and that affect you more than what your teammates. That is what I do not understand. That is the craziest thing to me, that what I say to you being your teammate won't affect you, but somebody that you don't even know is going to have an effect that is going to be able to penetrate to your mental to where it affects your work, your job."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6w_dajNrGLA&t=133s

**3. Tracy McGrady — "I think B. Miller can turn the corner, LaMelo is gone, and I think him having a bigger role" — Brandon Miller is poised for a breakout season** [04:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6w_dajNrGLA&t=285s)

Tracy McGrady: "I am going with **Brandon Miller**. I think B. Miller can turn the corner, LaMelo is gone, and I think him having a bigger role this year, I think coming into this season, he should be a guy that averages 25 to 26 points this year, taking that next step. And depending on what I see from him this year, it is going to determine, all right, he is going to be the franchise."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6w_dajNrGLA&t=285s

**4. Vince Carter — "Scottie Barnes in Toronto is a guy that has put his time in" — Scottie Barnes has proven himself as a franchise player** [05:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6w_dajNrGLA&t=329s)

Vince Carter: "**Scottie Barnes** in Toronto is a guy that has put his time in. He has established himself as a rookie of the year, was in the running for all-defensive player of the year, and a guy that was willing to do anything for that franchise when they needed him. That man played the five and at that stretch was leading the league in blocks. And I think **Scottie Barnes** is a guy that has withstood the test of time with all that has come in and out of that locker room, and he is still that guy on top of all that has come in there with like **Brandon Ingram**, obviously **RJ Barrett**, so on and so forth. I think he is kind of that guy that has the potential to be just that."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6w_dajNrGLA&t=329s

**5. Tracy McGrady — "he was with Portland for over 10 plus years, then he goes to Milwaukee for a year and a half" — Damian Lillard's departure from Portland was a difficult decision** [06:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6w_dajNrGLA&t=407s)

Tracy McGrady: "You look at Dame Lillard at his example. I am going to use his as an example. He was with Portland for over 10 plus years, then he goes to Milwaukee for a year and a half. I guarantee you, the experience of what he went through, he would never take that back. Whatever he learned in that situation, but this is towards the end of his career. So my opinion, I think if he had to do that all over again, because Portland is so near and dear to his heart and what he did for the franchise, what that franchise did for him, and what they mean to each other, I don't think he goes to Milwaukee."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6w_dajNrGLA&t=407s

**6. Tracy McGrady — "I gave you everything I have for like seven, eight years, man, I gave you everything" — player loyalty is tested when franchises change direction** [08:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6w_dajNrGLA&t=496s)

Tracy McGrady: "You do have those cases where like ticket, it is time to go, bro. I gave you everything I have for like seven, eight years, man, I gave you everything and then, you know, the franchise is not potentially, it was more than, it was more than that. It was like what, 10 years he was with Minnesota, right? They are going in a different direction, ownership, they are not on the same page, not giving me the respect and everything that I have done for this franchise, it is time to go."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6w_dajNrGLA&t=496s

**7. Vince Carter — "he left to learn how to win and he wanted that old thing back" — LeBron James' return to Cleveland was a necessary move** [09:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6w_dajNrGLA&t=548s)

Vince Carter: "Here is another example, LeBron leaving Cleveland. He left to learn how to win and he wanted that old thing back. And but when he came back, he was a better man and he delivered to the original franchise that he left, which was nasty, ugly, but here we are, he comes back and, you know, so sometimes it is needed."
