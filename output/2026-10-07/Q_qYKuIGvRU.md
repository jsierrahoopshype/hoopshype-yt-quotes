**4. Tracy McGrady — "I exhausted everything to prepare for this. So if you could walk away and feel like you gave" — Tracy McGrady on the necessity of exhaustive preparation to handle unpredictable game results** [03:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q_qYKuIGvRU&t=192s)

Tracy McGrady: "Are you prepared? When you step on that court, you got to know deep down inside, I've done everything in my willpower to prepare for this situation. So if you could walk away and feel like you gave it all, like you was prepared mentally, physically, spiritually, all that, and the results didn't work out for you, well then the cards, it wasn't for you.
