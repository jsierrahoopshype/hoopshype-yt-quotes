**5. Chiney Ogwumike — "It is all about defense. These are the top defensive teams in the WNBA." — Chiney Ogwumike on the WNBA's top defensive teams** [51:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1PYliSowPGA&t=3077s)

**Iman Shumpert:** "The finals are underway now. Can you tell us what you're seeing both out of the Valkyries and the Dream so far that you like?"

**Chiney Ogwumike:** "So, this is the most Iman Shumpert WNBA Finals in history because it is all about defense. These are the top defensive teams in the WNBA in the regular season. The Golden State Valkyries led the league in defense. In the postseason, the Atlanta Dream led the league in defense, which is why we saw the worst shooting percentages ever in history in Game 1. I think they averaged around collectively 32 percent. But, you know, after the game I hit up my sister, who was there in Golden State at Ball Hall, and I was just telling her, I was like, Neka, that game was so bad in some ways that it was good. Meaning, there were a lot of missed shots, but you would have never known because these are ladies that are competing. They're playing defense and also, let's be real here, because you talked about the format being different, it being best of three, best of five, best of seven, right? They had eight days off in between games. Anytime you're out for eight days, you're not going to shoot the ball really well."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1PYliSowPGA&t=3077s
