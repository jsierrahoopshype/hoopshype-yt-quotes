# Keaton Wagler: From Illinois to a Top-10 NBA Draft Pick and How a 46-Point Game Changed His Life — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cq6iOrE13z8

_Speakers identified: Tracy McGrady, Keaton Wagler, Vince Carter_

**1. Keaton Wagler — "My thought process going into Illinois was definitely not one and done. I was just doing whatever I can to help the team win." — Keaton Wagler on his mindset entering his freshman year at Illinois** [00:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cq6iOrE13z8&t=21s)

Keaton Wagler: "My thought process going into Illinois was definitely not one and done. I was not really thinking like that. For me it was just doing whatever I can to help the team win and get a spot on the team to contribute to winning. That is what I did throughout the whole summer, just continue to work hard and prove myself. I knew how good of a player I was, but I never thought that it would end up being the season that I had and being a one and done, honestly. My mindset was really just doing whatever I can to get a spot and contribute to winning and everything after that kind of took care of itself."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cq6iOrE13z8&t=21s

**2. Tracy McGrady — "I was considering coming out because this guy didn't he didn't really go to college." — Tracy McGrady and Keaton Wagler discuss the decision to enter the NBA draft** [00:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cq6iOrE13z8&t=57s)

**Tracy McGrady:** "I was considering coming out because this guy didn't he didn't really go to college. So when I was considering coming out, my mom and I we reached out to people just to see where we potentially would be drafted because at that time I said if I wasn't going in the top eight, I was going to go back to school. So what was your process to help you make that decision?"

**Keaton Wagler:** "I think it was talking with family, Illinois coaches, and my agents just seeing the pros and cons of each. My agents had talked to some NBA teams and said that they all think that I should come out, like I'm a guaranteed top 10 pick basically what they were saying. Hearing that it was a good thing to hear, but then also hearing what Illinois had to say about I'd be preseason number one player, all that, face of college basketball basically. It was weighing both of them, but I just think that the best decision for me was going to the draft and living out my dreams and goals."

**Vince Carter:** "Going and hearing all of that, how are you processing all of this? Because that's got to be very overwhelming for a young fella to receive that type of information knowing that that wasn't even the plan for you to do one and done."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cq6iOrE13z8&t=57s

**3. Keaton Wagler — "I'm a really loyal person and what we did at Illinois was like super special." — Keaton Wagler on the difficult decision to leave Illinois for the NBA** [02:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cq6iOrE13z8&t=157s)

Keaton Wagler: "It was a little bit of a tough decision. I think it was probably harder than what most people thought that it should have been. But for me, I'm a really loyal person and what we did at Illinois was like super special and I feel like we were short of our goal of winning a national championship. In the back of my head, I was like, it would be really cool to go back and do this and win a national championship. It was a little bit of a tough decision, but ultimately I feel like I did the right decision."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cq6iOrE13z8&t=157s

**4. Tracy McGrady — "I played in the tournament and I had a very good tournament." — Tracy McGrady on the importance of tournament performance for draft stock** [03:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cq6iOrE13z8&t=189s)

**Tracy McGrady:** "You did three years, but I want you to go to and I'm sure your mindset your freshman, sophomore, and junior year, you knew you was going to the NBA, right? You knew that. I knew you was going to the NBA when I saw you in high school. But my question is because we have it's the moments, right? We have moments. In those three years, what was that moment for you that you felt like, yeah, I could play on the next level? In the tournament."

**Keaton Wagler:** "I played in the tournament and I had a very good tournament. First my sophomore year, I had a very good tournament, but I still had hesitation of is it the right time? We had our whole dynamic of going back and forth and I had a great summer leading into my next year. Then going through my junior year, I had in my opinion, an even better tournament run where I contributed to the highest level. Obviously, Keaton was doing what he was doing, but I showed that I could put the ball on the floor, I could shoot the ball, I was athletic, and we fell short of winning that championship, but I felt the buzz and the time was right doing my due diligence, talking to people and they are saying, wait, you can go as high as three."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cq6iOrE13z8&t=189s

**5. Keaton Wagler — "That was the craziest moment of my life, I think. I'd never played a game like that." — Keaton Wagler on his 46-point game against Purdue** [05:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cq6iOrE13z8&t=327s)

Keaton Wagler: "It was the craziest moment of my life, I think. I'd never played a game like that, scored that many points in a game, even in high school. That was the most points I ever scored in a game. It was a crazy moment, but after that is when I really started to get on the map and people started to really pay attention to me and know how good of a player I was. I kind of just kept that momentum going throughout the rest of the year, just to keep doing that and keep playing with that type of confidence to be able to go out there and perform to the highest level."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cq6iOrE13z8&t=327s

**6. Keaton Wagler — "Before the game, I really didn't feel that good. I was kind of like lightheaded before." — Keaton Wagler on his mindset before his 46-point game** [07:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cq6iOrE13z8&t=445s)

Keaton Wagler: "Before the game, I really didn't feel that good. I was kind of like lightheaded before. I remember it was when we came back out from tunnel and there was three minutes left to the game started. I remember I was lightheaded and I was asking managers like, can you get my water? I don't really feel that good. I was like, come on, I just got to feel good. This is a big game, one of our best players is hurt so he's not playing. I was like, I was kind of nervous at first, but once I got into the game and I got the first bucket, I was like, all right, I'm settled in. Then I scored the first 14 points for our team and I was like, all right, it's this is my game. I'm on fire."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cq6iOrE13z8&t=445s

**7. Keaton Wagler — "My teammates kind of knew that I needed to have the ball in my hands." — Keaton Wagler on his team's role in his 46-point performance** [08:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cq6iOrE13z8&t=496s)

Keaton Wagler: "I feel like it was a mix kind of. I mean that game I only shot 17 shots, so it wasn't like I was just chucking up shots. I feel like my teammates kind of knew that I needed to have the ball in my hands, but they also knew that I wasn't just going to go out there and just try to play hero ball. I was also going to make the right play and that's kind of what I did throughout the game. I only shot one shot when there was like under five minutes left in the game because they started double teaming and hard hedging off of ball screens, so I got the ball out of my hands and moved it to my bigs and they made plays too."
