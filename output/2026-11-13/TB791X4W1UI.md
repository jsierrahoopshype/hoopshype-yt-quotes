If you remember, **Gary Trent Jr.** had a couple of middling seasons with Milwaukee, one bad one a year ago, and the Bucks turned around and gave him a four-year, $64 million contract, which was crazy because they did it late in the cycle and to my knowledge there was no market for **Gary Trent Jr.** The supposition people are going with is that **Gary Trent Jr.** took short-term contracts from the Bucks cheaper, signed there to play with **Giannis Antetokounmpo** and **Damian Lillard**, and this is him being rewarded on the back end. A very **Joe Smith**ian type of situation potentially developing in Milwaukee."

**Grant Liffmann:** "To them, this is one of the most obvious cap circumvention examples of practically all time. There is no potential way that you could possibly explain or justify that contract.
