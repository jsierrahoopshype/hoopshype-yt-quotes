# What is the 76ers’ 'secret sauce?!' Monica McNutt & Brian Windhorst weigh in | Get Up — *NBA on ESPN*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fowBvunQHA

_Speakers identified: Brian Windhorst, Monica McNutt_

**1. Brian Windhorst — "coming back from 20 points down is better for chemistry than winning by 20" — Rallying from 29 points down builds better team chemistry than winning by 20** [02:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fowBvunQHA&t=124s)

Brian Windhorst: "When you are trying to build a team like this, coming back from 20 points down is better for chemistry than winning by 20. Watching the way that this team has come together and figured out ways to win these first two games has been very impressive. I was sounding the alarm to expect a slow start from the 76ers. When LeBron has started with new teams, it has not gone well. The Lakers were terrible in 2018. The Cavs got off to a very shaky start. This team leaps and bounds ahead of where those teams were chemistry-wise."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fowBvunQHA&t=124s

**2. Monica McNutt — "the secret sauce has been the speed that this group can play with" — The 76ers' speed and transition play are the secret to their early success** [02:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fowBvunQHA&t=177s)

Monica McNutt: "To me, the secret sauce has been the speed that this group can play with. Obviously, LeBron was at different points in his career with those call-outs that Windhorst just made, but in this iteration, the dream is rip the ball from the net and throw it 90 feet down the floor to one of those guys running out. Because **Tyrese Maxey** is so energized, because LeBron has won a championship at a high level, and in the moment when **Joel Embiid** fell after a play, I thought it was an interesting moment where LeBron was like, 'Yo, you all right?' It feels as if LeBron has already raised the level of accountability for **Joel Embiid** in particular."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fowBvunQHA&t=177s

**3. Brian Windhorst — "Chet Holmgren was brutalized in the Western Conference Finals last year by the Spurs and Victor Wembanyama" — Chet Holmgren's defensive impact is key to the Thunder's strong start** [04:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fowBvunQHA&t=276s)

Brian Windhorst: "**Chet Holmgren** was brutalized in the Western Conference Finals last year by the Spurs and **Victor Wembanyama**. Imagine what he thought when the schedule came out and he saw his first game was against Wembanyama and his second game was against **Nikola Jokic**. He has come out and had two brilliant performances. He has 24 rebounds and eight blocks in the first two games of the season. They defend as a team. This is not Chet on Wembanyama, not Chet on Jokic. They have a great overall defensive big man rotation, but they held Wembanyama to one of 13 and they held **Nikola Jokic** last night to five of 19. **Chet Holmgren** is leading a great defensive start to their big men to start the season."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fowBvunQHA&t=276s

**4. Brian Windhorst — "there were officiating changes this year that were designed to take away some of the arm bar" — Officiating changes are impacting Shai Gilgeous-Alexander's free throw attempts** [06:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fowBvunQHA&t=398s)

Brian Windhorst: "There were officiating changes this year that were designed to take away some of the arm bar. By the way, it is not just Shai. A whole bunch of guys use the arm bar for separation and then they can get defenders off balance. It very likely will probably normalize in the coming weeks. But three days into the season, talking about a guy who has been a prolific scorer and a guy who has averaged nine free throw attempts a game for four years now, only averaging three over the first two games, just bears a little asterisk to keep watching."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fowBvunQHA&t=398s
