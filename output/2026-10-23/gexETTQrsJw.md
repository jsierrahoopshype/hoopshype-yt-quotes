**1. Kon Knueppel — "If I could tell myself a year ago, just chill. It's going to be all right." — Kon Knueppel on managing rookie anxiety and finding his confidence** [00:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gexETTQrsJw&t=9s)

Kon Knueppel: "I was telling **Suli**, our head coach, last year I came in every day, we were at Queens College because our practice facility was getting redone, and I was nervous every day. I would get to 9:00 p.m. the night before and think, man, I have to go, I have to make a good impression. I thoroughly enjoyed this summer because I came in thinking, I am getting better at basketball today, this is fun, I love this. That is the biggest adjustment. If I could tell myself a year ago, just chill. It is going to be all right. Focus on what you can control, what you can work on, and less so about what are my teammates thinking of me, what are the coaches thinking of me, am I going to carve out playing time?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gexETTQrsJw&t=9s

**5. Cam Johnson — Cam Johnson and Kon Knueppel on the robotic perfection of Shai Gilgeous-Alexander** [04:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gexETTQrsJw&t=293s)

**Cam Johnson:** "We did one with **Dub** and **AJ** and **Jared McCain** last week, and those guys, and they are not even saying this to kiss up to their teammate, all their moment with this was just with him specifically. It was literally like playing with him in pickup, and you are basically just like, this guy is robotic. Everything about every motion he makes is perfect, and so if you are not prepared for basically how he is going to play, you have no chance to defend him."

**Kon Knueppel:** "He is just so consistent and it is like seven points a quarter, eight points a quarter, and then it is 15 in one quarter. But it is just like the difficulty of the shots he shoots that he makes look easy is probably the thing that is most impressive about him."

**6. Kon Knueppel — "Growing up you just think the NBA is perfect. There is always things to work out." — Kon Knueppel on the realities of the NBA as a business** [05:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gexETTQrsJw&t=348s)

Kon Knueppel: "Growing up you just think the NBA is perfect. There is always things to work out, there is always things that players want to fight for. Having a little bit more grace for that and understanding that has been good, because everybody is just thinking, dang, why isn't this guy playing well tonight?"

**8. Kon Knueppel — "The part of Duke that prepares you is just the big all the social media that surrounds Duke." — Kon Knueppel on the professional standards of the Duke program** [09:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gexETTQrsJw&t=543s)

Kon Knueppel: "The part of Duke that prepares you is just the big all the social media that surrounds Duke, and it is super mainstream and it is probably one of the most famous, if not the most famous, college basketball team to play. People breaking down your game, it is like, do I suck at that? It is like, oh, this guy doesn't know what he is talking about, or it is just some random dude on Twitter."
