# Kon Knueppel Names the Players Who Shaped His Game — *The Old Man and The Three*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1fT4ndSehns

_Speakers identified: Cam Johnson, Kon Knueppel_

**1. Kon Knueppel — "My dad says he took me to a Shaq, LeBron, Cavs, Bucks game." — Kon Knueppel's earliest memory of watching the NBA** [00:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1fT4ndSehns&t=4s)

Kon Knueppel: "My dad says he took me to a **Shaq**, LeBron, Cavs, Bucks game. I think he said they were both out, or maybe just LeBron was out, or just **Shaq** was out. I vaguely remember him telling me stories about that, but then I remember more of the Bucks games. I feel like I saw every Bucks, Suns game for a four-year stretch because they were the worst team in the West and the Bucks were the worst team in the East, so the tickets were five bucks."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1fT4ndSehns&t=4s

**2. Kon Knueppel — "As they got good and Giannis came along, we went to less and less games." — How the Bucks' team quality impacted attendance** [01:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1fT4ndSehns&t=71s)

Kon Knueppel: "I remember we went to more games when they were really bad. And then as they got good and Giannis came along, we went to less and less games because they were more expensive. It was easier to get in the door. Maybe we're going to go on vacation this year instead of going to 45 games."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1fT4ndSehns&t=71s

**3. Kon Knueppel — "The LeBron Heat years, I was a little bit of a bandwagon." — Kon Knueppel's fandom of the Heat during the LeBron era** [01:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1fT4ndSehns&t=114s)

Kon Knueppel: "The LeBron Heat years, I was a little bit of a bandwagon. If the Bucks were out, I would jump on the Heat bandwagon because **Ray Allen** was my favorite player growing up. So, when he was on the Heat, I was like, all right, I'm in."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1fT4ndSehns&t=114s

**4. Kon Knueppel — "There's a famous story about the game six shot against San Antonio." — Ray Allen's legendary Game 6 shot against the Spurs** [02:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1fT4ndSehns&t=156s)

Kon Knueppel: "There's a famous story about the game six shot against San Antonio where he's basically just like, I practiced that, so it wasn't hard. I'm cheering for the Heat, I don't want the Spurs to win, and that's game six. My dad was like, all right, it's time to go to bed, the Heat were down by 15. I was devastated."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1fT4ndSehns&t=156s

**5. Kon Knueppel — "I loved Chris Middleton, just the way he played." — Chris Middleton's influence on Kon Knueppel's game** [03:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1fT4ndSehns&t=228s)

Kon Knueppel: "I loved **Chris Middleton**, just the way he played. **Ray Allen** was a little bit in there just because he's an awesome shooter. Those were the guys that were still playing when I played. But in my house, my dad was like, the three best are Michael, Magic, and Larry. Those three, how they made their teammates better, elevated their teammates, just made me fall in love with basketball. That's how my dad raised us, basketball is you obviously play to win, but it's also how can you elevate your teammates and make them better."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1fT4ndSehns&t=228s

**6. Kon Knueppel — "He's a center and he's like one of my favorite players to watch." — Nikola Jokic's unique style of play** [04:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1fT4ndSehns&t=280s)

Kon Knueppel: "He's a center and he's like one of my favorite players to watch. He's not even playing that well, and I looked up, he's 25, 12, and 11, late third quarter."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1fT4ndSehns&t=280s

**7. Cam Johnson — "It was one of the highest pressure shots he's had in his career." — Cam Johnson on Cam Thomas's high-pressure shot** [05:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1fT4ndSehns&t=310s)

**Cam Johnson:** "I saw **Cam Thomas** last week in LA, and we were talking about that pass in the preseason on the baseline. He said it was one of the highest pressure shots he's had in his career. He's like, I can't fuck this up."

**Kon Knueppel:** "Did he know it was coming?"

**Cam Johnson:** "He said he knew it was coming because he's now played with him for a year. He saw him look, and Cam is a really smart guy and he's spatially aware. If he looks at you in any way, you have to be ready. He basically saw him look while he was getting to the corner, and then he just was ready to shoot and the ball just appears."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1fT4ndSehns&t=310s

**8. Kon Knueppel — "Late high school, maybe summer after junior year, I had a really good summer for AAU ball." — Kon Knueppel's early realization of NBA potential** [06:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1fT4ndSehns&t=386s)

Kon Knueppel: "Late high school, maybe summer after junior year, I had a really good summer for AAU ball. And even maybe the summer before that, a family friend of my dad, his brother was a scout for the Pelicans and kind of said I had a chance if things broke right. So that's when I started to take it a little more seriously."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1fT4ndSehns&t=386s
