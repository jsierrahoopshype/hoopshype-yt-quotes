**6. Draymond Green — "Until a guy brings his second hand to the ball, it's technically not considered a gather." — Draymond Green defends LaMelo Ball's travel on game-winning play** [23:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Ivi1M5r6Ws&t=1412s)

Draymond Green: "The one thing that I've been told about this travel stuff is until a guy brings his second hand to the ball, it's technically not considered a gather. And because it's not considered a gather yet, you can move side to side as long as you don't bring the second hand to the ball. I saw the referee's tweet that this is a travel, but by their rule, I'm looking at it and once he brings the second hand, he one-two and he's up. **Seth Curry** was saying, yo, by rule this ain't a travel. And I must say, I agree with **Seth Curry**. He never gathers, he never brings the second hand. When he does, he starts to rise up and shoot one-two. I don't think it's right. In any basketball world outside of the NBA, it is a travel, but the NBA doesn't have the same rules as all the basketball leagues. The NBA, if I fall down with the ball, I can stand up. In other basketball leagues, that's a travel. But if I fall down with the ball, I can actually stand up. So I don't really know that I agree that this is a travel. In theory, yes. But in the NBA, we really need to see the other angle to see when that left hand actually gets to the ball. But it looked like he one-two, he maybe took one extra one after the hand got to the ball. But it's the fucking NBA. NBA don't call travel."
