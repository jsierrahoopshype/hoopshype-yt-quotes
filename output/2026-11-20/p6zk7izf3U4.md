# The Grizzlies Have a Legit 15-Man Rotation | Chris Mannix & Evan Turner — *Sports Illustrated*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p6zk7izf3U4

_Speakers identified: Chris Mannix, Evan Turner_

**1. Evan Turner — "to go eight and zero on a road with a young team, that's legit." — Memphis Grizzlies' identity and development of a young roster** [01:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p6zk7izf3U4&t=95s)

**Chris Mannix:** "How serious should we take the Memphis Grizzlies right now? Are we going to be talking about them after Christmas in the same way we are talking about them right now?"

**Evan Turner:** "I take them pretty serious because you know as well as anything to go eight and zero on a road with a young team, that's legit. Even though obviously the teams haven't been as sexy, they show up. I think they've been able to cut off a couple of months because they've been in their development because they've been able to build an identity. They compete, they play ten deep. One thing that we bring up on **Cam Boozer**, he's playing far beyond his years. Not only is this a good playmaker, a good passer, a solid scorer, but he reads the game so efficiently and he has size to him."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p6zk7izf3U4&t=95s

**2. Evan Turner — "Ty Jerome is an all-star right now. He's an all-star, full stop." — Zach Edey's role and impact on the Grizzlies' floor spacing** [02:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p6zk7izf3U4&t=135s)

Evan Turner: "You look at **Zach Edey**, there's a discussion on is this a role player, is this a budding star? Regardless, he's got this type of frame dimensions to really have something to hang your hat on. And then the one thing I love about **Ty Jerome** is not only is he one of the most efficient scorers in the NBA, but to have him on the court, his presence does so much for the geometry of the floor. You have to, whether you want to or not, you got to damn near guard him 30 feet out."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p6zk7izf3U4&t=135s

**3. Chris Mannix — "They've got 15 guys averaging 10 or more minutes per game." — Grizzlies' depth and roster construction around Taylor Jenkins' style** [04:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p6zk7izf3U4&t=240s)

Chris Mannix: "One of the most remarkable things about the Grizzlies is that they've got 15 guys averaging 10 or more minutes per game. Nobody is playing more than 31 minutes per game. Only **Scotty Pippen Jr.** has played five or less games. Everybody else has played more than nine games. Everybody else has played more than half the season on this team. They've taken some heat for how they've deconstructed the roster, and it's been painful over the years. **Desmond Bane** is gone, **Jaren Jackson Jr.** is gone, **Ja Morant**, the last one to go. But they did that to create a roster that was built for the **Taylor Jenkins** style of play. This guy comes from that European culture. He wants to play a whole bunch of guys, he wants to play a free-flowing offense, he wants the ball to move, and they are playing that way."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p6zk7izf3U4&t=240s

**4. Chris Mannix — "They are as good in the league at identifying talent as anyone." — Grizzlies' front office success in identifying and developing talent** [07:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p6zk7izf3U4&t=458s)

Chris Mannix: "I got to give credit to this front office. They are as good in the league at identifying talent as anyone. They've been around for a while now. They built the previous version of that team around guys like Jackson and Desmond Bane was a late first-round pick, **Brandon Clarke** was a late first-round pick. They identified talent that way. And here you go again, where they plucked **Ty Jerome**, who is not a celebrated signing when they got him a couple of years ago, but a good one. **Zach Edey** was a back half of the lottery guy, they nailed that. **Jaylen Wells**, they nailed that pick. **Cam Spencer**, they develop him up until this point. **Jeremy Grant** is there, giving them double figures. They're just really good."
