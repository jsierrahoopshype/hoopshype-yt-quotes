**1. Steve Kerr, on team dynamics, player legacy**

"People know about the fight I got into him. That we got into together and that was just about him kind of testing me. And I probably the best thing I ever did was not, you know, not take his his crap and stood back up and he respected it and we got along ever since."

_Why it matters:_ Steve Kerr recounts his physical altercation with Michael Jordan, explaining it as a test that, by standing his ground, earned him Jordan's respect and improved their relationship.

**2. Steve Kerr, on team dynamics, player legacy, coaching**
[00:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_5Kl64bQF_Q&t=46s)

"Being his teammate was hard. He was really tough on everybody because his whole philosophy was he had to toughen us up to get us ready for the playoffs and the finals. And so he he came after us and you had to you had to stand up to him. You had to sort of survive the MJ test, and the guys who who survived it, he had immense respect for him."

_Why it matters:_ Steve Kerr details Michael Jordan's demanding leadership style, emphasizing how Jordan's 'MJ Test' was designed to toughen teammates for playoff success and earned his lasting respect.

**3. Steve Kerr, on player legacy, team dynamics**
[00:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_5Kl64bQF_Q&t=14s)

"There's a mutual respect that exists and we don't really stay in touch, but we see each other. We just seem to run into each other once in a while because we're kind of traveling the same circuit. And there's always it's always a great reunion. It's so much fun to to kind of relive those days and to ask about our teammates and talk about the good times back then."

_Why it matters:_ Steve Kerr describes his enduring relationship with Michael Jordan as one of mutual respect, despite not staying in regular contact, and cherishes their occasional reunions to reminisce about their championship days.
