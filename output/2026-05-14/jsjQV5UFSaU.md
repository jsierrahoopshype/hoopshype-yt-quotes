# Did NBA Get Rookie of the Year Wrong?! Roommates React to 2025-26 Awards — *Roommates Show*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jsjQV5UFSaU

_Speakers identified: Josh Hart, Jalen Brunson_

**1. Jalen Brunson — Jalen Brunson on play-in games influencing Rookie of the Year voting**
[00:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jsjQV5UFSaU&t=23s)

"Yeah, I thought Cunningham was going to get it too, but Cooper ended the season really strong. I think they also looked at team success, but I feel like they looked at his two play-in games because he didn't play that well in those two play-in games. And I think I forgot who it was, I forgot what analyst was like, 'Oh, if they make the playoffs, he's going to have my vote. If they don't, I'm giving it to **Cooper Flagg**.' I feel like that's so idiotic."

**2. Jalen Brunson — Jalen Brunson on why play-in games shouldn't influence regular season awards**
[01:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jsjQV5UFSaU&t=70s)

"Does the play-in count as a regular season? No, it's not. So then why is that even accounted for? Exactly. So I feel like if you're going to make these awards or whatever, you got to give these ballots out and then once the regular season's over, like that Tuesday, obviously it's over on Sunday. Collect those ballots because at the end of the day, I feel like if Cunningham had, I don't know when the ballots are collected, but I feel like if say Cunningham had 25 in that first play-in game, I feel like some of the people probably would have thought that and like, 'Okay, that's solidified this.' Or, 'Oh, he didn't play that well, he didn't shoot well, so I'm going to give it to **Cooper Flagg**.' So I feel like if it's a regular season award, collect the ballots before the play-in starts because the play-in isn't the playoffs, but it's not the regular season. That's its own thing."

**3. Jalen Brunson — Jalen Brunson questions Anthony Edwards' Clutch Player nomination despite All-NBA denial**
[02:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jsjQV5UFSaU&t=144s)

"Ant, his appeal got denied, right? But he was up for Clutch Player of the Year. How? If you can't be All-NBA. You can't make All-NBA, but you're nominated for Clutch Player of the Year. I thought the requirement for this is like postseason awards. So how is someone like that able to be nominated for that? He did appeal. And it got denied because Luka and Cade got it. I just find that confusing."

**4. Jalen Brunson — Jalen Brunson explains Anthony Edwards' award eligibility rules**

**7. Josh Hart — Josh Hart argues adding points is harder at higher scoring averages**
[06:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jsjQV5UFSaU&t=372s)

"I do think though as your points per game average gets higher, it becomes harder and harder to add incremental on top of that. It's not just a matter of this person added 11, this person added eight, because going from 16 to 24 is different for example than going from 12 to 20 or 8 to 16 or whatever it is. When you're already averaging 16, Jalen's squinting like, 'No?'"
