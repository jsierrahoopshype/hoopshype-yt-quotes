Jerry Stackhouse: "When you look back in history, there hasn't been a lot of small guards to do it. I think you look at **Isaiah Thomas**, and probably before all of us, but I remember my brothers always talking about **Gus Williams** with Seattle. He was 6'2", he won a championship, but most of the guys weren't able to get it done. I think it was more about the supporting cast. They just didn't have the supporting cast to beat **Shaq** and **Kobe**. **Jalen Brunson** is different. I don't think any of us saw this when he left Dallas. I thought he was really good, and I've always been a fan of him. I coached against him in AAU when he was 13. What he's been able to do in New York is magical, and if he's able to pull this off, he's definitely going to go down as one of the best small guards ever."
