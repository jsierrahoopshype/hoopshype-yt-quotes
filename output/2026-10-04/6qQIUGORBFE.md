**1. Chris Mannix — "it's very much going to be a work in progress" — The Sixers' offense will be a work in progress with overlapping skill sets** [00:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6qQIUGORBFE&t=21s)

**Chris Mannix:** "You can tell from hearing from **Jaylen Brown**, **Tyrese Maxey**, and **Nick Nurse**, it's very much going to be a work in progress. They have a bunch of guys whose skills overlap. **Jaylen Brown**, **LeBron James**, **Joel Embiid**, these are high-post type of guys offensively. Neither of them play with a big like **Joel Embiid**, who needs to get touches in the paint. There's going to be a lot to figure out."

**Grant Liffmann:** "There's definitely going to be some clunky stuff. The spotlight is going to be so great on them. We're going to be searching for everything. There's going to be some late-minute game stuff where everyone's going to be like, is it going to LeBron, is it going to **Jaylen Brown**, how about Maxey, how about Embiid? Everyone's going to be searching for these things."
