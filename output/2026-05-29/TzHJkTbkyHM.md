**2. Chiney Ogwumike — "It was not necessarily just by Victor Wembanyama. It was a supporting cast of guys raining threes." — Chiney Ogwumike on the Spurs' supporting cast stepping up in Game 6** [05:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TzHJkTbkyHM&t=346s)

**Chiney Ogwumike:** "What did the Spurs do? They made it rain and their supporting cast truly stepped up. I want to dissect things from every angle, but honestly it comes down to this. This is truly a heavyweight battle. The punch in Game 6 by the Spurs was an absolute haymaker and it was not necessarily just by **Victor Wembanyama**. It was a supporting cast of guys raining threes. **Dylan Harper** had 18 points, Devin Vassell, all these points, four threes, 12 points came from beyond the arc. They were balling from the jump."

**Scott Van Pelt:** "It is interesting you point out first to throw because that is something **Dylan Harper** mentioned when he joined me earlier, the 20-year-old who looks just phenomenal on this stage. He said we have to be the aggressor here and that is something I think both teams will try to bring in Game 7."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TzHJkTbkyHM&t=346s

**3. Chiney Ogwumike — "On the other end, Dylan Harper, the man you talked to, the man of the night, knocked down a three." — Chiney Ogwumike on the Spurs' aggressive play against the Thunder** [07:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TzHJkTbkyHM&t=457s)

Chiney Ogwumike: "There was one specific play where **Devin Vassell** was in a two-on-one. Nine times out of ten, a pro-level player would go out there and score. Instead, he got the contest and on the other end, **Dylan Harper**, the man you talked to, the man of the night, knocked down a three. He put his hands, he said thank you Lord. That is how much energy and work that they have been doing in order to compete with the Oklahoma City Thunder. They will have to carry that on the road for Game 7. It will take everybody. And I do think they bought **Victor Wembanyama** some time because he did not have to be the primary feature of this offense because everyone had that aggressive mentality."
