# Fabolous On Working With Lil Wayne, Wanting A Bar-Heavy Drake Project & His Approach To Features — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kHtjcCSJ8as

_Speakers identified: Tracy McGrady, Fabolous, Vince Carter_

**1. Fabolous — "I want a whole project of that from him" — Fabolous wants a bar-heavy project from Drake** [05:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kHtjcCSJ8as&t=311s)

Fabolous: "Off the top of my head, I would say **Drake**. I think **Drake**, when he makes projects, he has to cover all those bases too. But **Drake** will also have a song or two that is just him going bar-crazy. I want a whole project of that from him. I know the girls are going to be a little upset that you are not giving them their joint, but I want a whole project of that from him."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kHtjcCSJ8as&t=311s

**2. Fabolous — "I always represent myself to the highest level" — Fabolous on his approach to guest features on songs** [07:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kHtjcCSJ8as&t=452s)

**Fabolous:** "My approach is to always represent myself to the highest level. I do not ever want to be like, 'He gave you a throwaway verse.' And I also feel like with a feature, they are calling you to bring more to the song. They did what they did and they want to add to it. So I always approach it that way. I want to add something to the song and not just be on the song."

**Tracy McGrady:** "So appreciation for your craft."

**Fabolous:** "Yeah, so I always kind of approach it that way. I want to add something to the song and not just be on the song."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kHtjcCSJ8as&t=452s

**3. Fabolous — "I like the music to dictate how you do it, what flow you are going to use" — How hearing a verse or hook influences Fabolous's writing** [08:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kHtjcCSJ8as&t=497s)

Fabolous: "Sometimes if they have a verse or hook, they will send you just the beat and tell you. So I am like, 'What is this song about? What do I do?' Hearing their verse or the hook can give you a direction on which way you want to go. I like to be inspired by whatever the music is because that takes you, that lets the music dictate where you are going to go. I like the music to dictate how you do it, what flow you are going to use, what how deep you want to touch on a subject, or just speak on everything all in once."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kHtjcCSJ8as&t=497s
