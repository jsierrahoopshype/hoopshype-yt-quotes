# Iman Shumpert, J.R. Smith, Rasheed Wallace & More Tell Their Best KnicksTape Moments — *7PM in Brooklyn*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M3pb5BZX7YI

_Speakers identified: Rasheed Wallace, Iman Shumpert, Monica McNutt, J.R. Smith, Steve Novak, Carmelo Anthony, Baron Davis_

**1. Rasheed Wallace — "I said if you look closely, it's three fingers, it's not a gun. It's three to the dome." — Rasheed Wallace on the origin of the 'three to the dome' celebration** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M3pb5BZX7YI&t=0s)

**Rasheed Wallace:** "Everywhere I play, I try to do some type of hand gesture with the three. When I was with Boston, I would tuck it in my pocket. When I got here, I shot a three and I was mad. I said three to the dome, so I kept doing it every time I made a three. Then in practice one day, I got a call to go into the coach's office. They said we need you to stop that reference, we don't like the violence. I said what are you talking about? They said you're making the gun sign and putting it to the head. I said no, no, no. If you look closely, it's three fingers, it's not a gun. It's three to the dome."

**Iman Shumpert:** "The part that you missed, he was mad. He was going through stuff and he was fucking pissed. He would shoot them fucking threes and he would be fucking pissed. He would just boom."

**J.R. Smith:** "Shump had to hold my phone. Shump started that shit, hold my phone to the fans. We were mad. We were angry as players because of just being here in New York, what you got to go through in the season, the fans, and the media."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M3pb5BZX7YI&t=0s

**2. Steve Novak — "To me, it's like the behind-the-scenes stuff and the relationships. We all came together." — Steve Novak on the camaraderie and behind-the-scenes culture of the Knicks** [04:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M3pb5BZX7YI&t=261s)

**3. Carmelo Anthony — "I was coming from All-Star weekend in LA, and I got the call that Monday night." — Carmelo Anthony on the 'I'm Coming Home' game against the Bucks** [08:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M3pb5BZX7YI&t=490s)

**Timothée Chalamet:** "Tell me about that first game against the Bucks when you were on the Knicks. They had 'I'm Coming Home' playing. What was that feeling like, coming back to New York?"

**Carmelo Anthony:** "When I came, mind you, it was 6:00 in the morning when I landed in New York. I had to go play that night. I was coming from All-Star weekend in LA, and I got the call that Monday night. Everything happened, I wound up in New York. They were like, 'Yo, you can play in a couple days.' I'm like, 'Nah, I want to play the first game.' I don't have my sneakers, I'm still in Denver Nuggets sneakers. I'm in the white and the navy Melos at this point in time. I don't even have my sleeves, my sleeves are off. I look nasty out there."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M3pb5BZX7YI&t=490s

**4. Iman Shumpert — "I could tell Shorty done checked out. The game was over with for Melo." — Iman Shumpert on Carmelo Anthony's intense focus during games** [12:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M3pb5BZX7YI&t=732s)

**Iman Shumpert:** "By the time you see in the game where Melo literally, he shot a shot and the shot went in, but he doesn't even process the ball no more. He just turns his whole attention to this man, bro. He running back, he's not looking at the game. The game was over with for Melo. It didn't matter, unless it was going to be one-on-one. If you going to throw me this ball and you going to guard me, then it meant something. Other than that, it was like how many shots can I miss and we bump each other and do. It was wild. I could tell Shorty done checked out."

**Carmelo Anthony:** "After the game, I storm off. I don't want to be calmed down, I don't want to be touched. Remember, I'm in my suit. I don't got to shower, nothing. I already showered at halftime."

**J.R. Smith:** "He didn't drink no chocolate milk, ain't no protein shakes, ain't no, you remember they had the little wire thing, the orange thing for your abs? He used to be doing that after the game, got the bar, he got the little jump rope. He got all type of shit he be doing after the game to keep himself sharp."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M3pb5BZX7YI&t=732s

**5. J.R. Smith — "When I got off the plane, he was one of the first people I meet." — J.R. Smith on his early days with the Knicks and Carmelo Anthony** [18:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M3pb5BZX7YI&t=1099s)

**J.R. Smith:** "When I got off the plane, he was one of the first people I meet, and he was a rookie at the time. He didn't really know what to expect, he always heard about me and all this other shit. So, he's like, 'Yo, what you want to do?' I was like, 'Yo, you got a license?' because I had no license at the time. I had no license for like 10 years at the time. So, I was like, 'Yo, you got to drive me around, you the rookie.' So, he take me to the crib, take me to the, I pick up my daughter. He was like, 'Yo, what you want to do?' I was like, 'We going to Dave & Buster's.' He was like, 'Where?' I was like, 'In Times Square.' He's like, 'Bro, we play for the Knicks!'"

**Carmelo Anthony:** "I don't care what you talking about, I'm taking my daughter to Dave & Buster's. You going to drive."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M3pb5BZX7YI&t=1099s

**6. Baron Davis — "I was like, 'Yo, we should make a mixtape.' And Amar'e Stoudemire said, 'A KnicksTape? What's a KnicksTape?'" — Baron Davis on the origin of the 'KnicksTape' brand** [29:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M3pb5BZX7YI&t=1749s)

**Baron Davis:** "It was almost like everybody was saying like nobody got along or dudes didn't get along and like what we was on the road, like we all went to eat. You know what I mean? We was all hanging out and so that's kind of like how the KnicksTape start happening and it was like it was really like a misunderstanding. Remember we was walking to dinner and I was like, 'Yo, we should make a mixtape.' And Amar'e Stoudemire said, 'A KnicksTape? What's a KnicksTape?' I said, 'Man, that's a brand, dog. I don't know, that's us.' So Shump started freestyling, you know what I mean? We get to the restaurant and we just start talking about all how we can like what they thought, like how we was going to come back to New York, what our role was on the team."

**Carmelo Anthony:** "I don't know, that's us."

**Iman Shumpert:** "Tyson, Chandler, he loved photography. He had a photography show. We all there, gang."
