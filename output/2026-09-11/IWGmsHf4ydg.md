**5. Draymond Green — "Most people are paid every first and the 15th all year. There are ways that you can get" — Draymond Green explains the NBA's bi-weekly pay structure** [13:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IWGmsHf4ydg&t=786s)

Draymond Green: "Most people are paid every first and the 15th all year. There are ways that you can get chunks of money depending on how you negotiate your contract. You could get 25% of your salary on October 1st or August 1st, whatever dates it is that y'all work out. It's all a part of negotiation. You think about it, for the team, it's best for you that you pay everybody on a first and the 15th basis all year. That way that money that you're paying out, you're collecting on that money until you pay it. As opposed to if you just give the lump sum in the beginning. And so, the teams ultimately want to pay you on a two-week structure, which is the first and the 15th."
