# Did Russell Westbrook Underachieve? | Rachel Nichols & Chris Mannix — *Sports Illustrated*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1ypFM-r0wK8

_Speakers identified: Chris Mannix, Rachel Nichols_

**1. Chris Mannix — "I will remember Russell Westbrook as an overachiever and an underachiever." — Chris Mannix evaluates Russell Westbrook's career as an overachiever and underachiever** [02:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1ypFM-r0wK8&t=136s)

Chris Mannix: "As a basketball player, I will remember **Russell Westbrook** as an overachiever and an underachiever. The first 13 years of his career he was an overachiever because he came into the NBA as basically a two-guard. He played alongside **Darren Collison** at UCLA. He molded himself with the help of the Thunder into one of the best point guards in the NBA. He did it while taking a lot of arrows from a lot of different people along the way who said he wasn't a point guard and could never be a point guard alongside **Kevin Durant**. Remember **Magic Johnson** in the 2012 Finals? He said he was the worst point guard in Finals history. Come on, Magic. He was just a kid back then. He was only in the league a few years. So he overachieved there."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1ypFM-r0wK8&t=136s

**2. Chris Mannix — "From the moment he left, he became an underachiever in my eyes." — Chris Mannix argues Russell Westbrook failed to adapt his game after leaving Oklahoma City** [03:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1ypFM-r0wK8&t=226s)

Chris Mannix: "From the moment he left, he became an underachiever in my eyes because he could never quite evolve his game to tailor it to the team that was around him. He never was able to adapt and he never evolved his game as he got older. He still relied on that overwhelming athleticism. Could you imagine what **Russell Westbrook** could be and still be if he followed that **Jason Kidd** path? **Jason Kidd**, when he was coming up, not as athletic as Russ, but ran the floor, was a paint guy, a playmaker. Late in his career, evolved into a shooter, figured it out, got him a championship in Dallas, earned him some extra years with the Knicks. We never saw that evolution with **Russell Westbrook**."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1ypFM-r0wK8&t=226s

**3. Rachel Nichols — "The first part of his career came from him being bullheaded and believing he could do things." — Rachel Nichols and Chris Mannix debate Westbrook's career evolution and team fit** [05:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1ypFM-r0wK8&t=336s)

**Rachel Nichols:** "The first part of his career came from him being bullheaded and believing he could do things that most people around him didn't think he could do. So it doesn't surprise me that in the last five years of an 18-year career he behaved exactly like he behaved the first 13. If this is how you evolve a career, it doesn't surprise me. Would it have been great if he took a **Jason Kidd** track? Of course, but the reason why we make such a big deal out of what Jason did was because it is special, because not everybody does it."

**Chris Mannix:** "It doesn't surprise me either. You get a little bit older, you start to, you know. Look, would it have been great if he took a **Jason Kidd** track? Of course, but the reason why we make such a big deal out of what Jason did was because it is special, because not everybody does it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1ypFM-r0wK8&t=336s

**4. Rachel Nichols — "He was Jason Kidd, right? No J, that was the joke about Jason Kidd." — Rachel Nichols highlights Westbrook's unique impact and Hall of Fame career** [06:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1ypFM-r0wK8&t=360s)

Rachel Nichols: "He was **Jason Kidd**, right? No J, that was the joke about **Jason Kidd**. And then he turns into a three-point shooter, right? I mean, he helped the Dallas Mavericks win that title with his shooting. And I think that is special, that is unusual. It is the reason we keep bringing it up to hold every single other player to that standard. I'm not saying it's not unfair or it wasn't available to them, but the reason why it's special is that most people don't do it. And so the fact that **Russell Westbrook** didn't do it, does that surprise me? No, it doesn't surprise me. Most people don't do it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1ypFM-r0wK8&t=360s

**5. Chris Mannix — "He could have been an incredibly valuable sixth man on a winning team." — Chris Mannix suggests Westbrook could have been a valuable sixth man on a winner** [09:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1ypFM-r0wK8&t=586s)

Chris Mannix: "He could have been an incredibly valuable sixth man on a winning team and just never quite became that. So for the vast majority of his career, overachiever. The back end, underachiever. Still going to the Hall of Fame, still one of the best point guards of this generation."
