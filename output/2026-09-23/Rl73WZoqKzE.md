**1. Richard Jefferson — "If you haven't played a hundred and something games over the course of your career, then those last 30 games are the most excruciating." — Richard Jefferson on the importance of playoff experience for NBA teams** [00:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rl73WZoqKzE&t=8s)

**Kendrick Perkins:** "When you say lack of experience, what do you mean? Lack of experience offensively, assignments, making adjustments?"

**Richard Jefferson:** "If you haven't played a hundred and something games over the course of your career, then those last 30 games are the most excruciating, emotionally, physically. If you haven't played a team four or five times in a row, 90% of their roster hasn't. That's experience. Playoff experience means that you've gone through the ups and downs. It doesn't mean that your offense isn't in game, your game isn't nice, you don't have the best players, you don't know the systems. But if you've never done anything along that journey that starts in mid-April and goes to the end of June, that is two months of basketball that is very different basketball."

**2. Kendrick Perkins — "the whole playoff experience shit is overrated to me." — Kendrick Perkins argues that playoff experience is overrated in the NBA** [01:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rl73WZoqKzE&t=112s)

**Kendrick Perkins:** "I also know that the whole playoff experience shit is overrated to me. Really, when it comes to the postseason, it comes down to talent is one, luck is another, but it comes down to who are the guys in the locker room and what type of basketball IQ they have."

**Kendrick Perkins:** "Every game in the series is different, and all you have to do is be locked in. If you're an immature group, experience may kick in, but I'm not getting those vibes from this group right here."

**4. Channing Frye — "Can you sacrifice for your team? He's never been doing that." — Playoff experience requires players to sacrifice and adapt their roles** [07:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rl73WZoqKzE&t=440s)

Channing Frye: "Playoff experience is like having vets. When I went to Phoenix, they had already had playoff experience and they were like, 'Hey Channing, throughout the year, we're going to need you to work on these things that are uncomfortable so that people don't have counters to you throughout the playoffs so that we can have offensive versatility.' So that's one thing that they have never seen. A lot of teams are not going to put out their best defense versus the Spurs. They're going to try something maybe once or twice, but then they're going to go back on film and they're going to work on that secretly if they need to play the Spurs. The second thing is, can some of these guys sacrifice? They may say, 'You know what, **Devin Vassell**, we're going to double team you and you're not going to be able to score the ball so you're going to become one-dimensional.' Can you sacrifice for your team? He's never been doing that. Doing that for one game is different than getting criticized or hearing the noise outside of those playoff situations. And then can you keep your head for guys coming off the bench? Let's say **Luke Kornet**, he's obviously a champion, but you go, 'Hey Luke, we can't play you this series.' Is he going to be able to still work out, be professional? Think about some of the other young guys. Can they continue to be available even though coach says, 'Hey, this may not be the matchup for you.' Can you do that?"
