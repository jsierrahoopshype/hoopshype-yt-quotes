**1. The Kid Mero — "I need people to understand the mentality of actors. And I'm not mad at people getting an opportunity." — The Kid Mero and Carmelo Anthony on basketball players acting in films** [03:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=meHIMJqs0HA&t=227s)

**The Kid Mero:** "It's not that I don't like basketball players acting, it's just I need people to understand the mentality of actors. And I'm not mad at people getting an opportunity who don't do any particular thing, but then it's like once you're doing it, you got to be doing it on the same level as other people that are there. Because if again, to use that same example, I could maybe get in the building. Marvin Gaye had a tryout with the Detroit Lions, right? But at a certain point, you're out of here."

**Carmelo Anthony:** "Yo, it's one of those spots. You better be hitting or catching or running or something on the same level as everybody else, otherwise, we don't watch you act with an asterisk. You know what I'm saying? So that's the fact, though. I'm a basketball player, never going to disrespect the art form when it comes to putting the time and putting the effort into really diving into the script and diving into the world of whatever that character and script is. I don't have the time to do that."
