# EPISODE 2: Aday Mara Left Spain to Chase His NBA Dream | Michigan Star’s Incredible Journey — *Dwyane Wade*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tpOR1G-Gn2Y

_Speakers identified: Aday Mara, Javier Mara, Gely Gómez, Dusty May_

**1. Aday Mara — "it was easier for scouts or NBA teams to watch me play, changing your whole life" — Aday Mara on the strategic decision to play college basketball** [03:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tpOR1G-Gn2Y&t=211s)

Aday Mara: "I never thought that I could be playing for college. For me it was something new, so I felt that it was a good decision just because it was easier for scouts or NBA teams to watch me play, changing your whole life, but also language. I didn't have a really good English, I'm still getting better. When we went to UCLA, we have some expectations that maybe didn't happen. So I felt that after two years of trying to get better, definitely getting better, it was time to find maybe another place to keep finding that opportunity that I wanted."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tpOR1G-Gn2Y&t=211s

**2. Dusty May — "he was looking for a place that valued big men and the post position" — Dusty May on Aday Mara's transition and fit at Michigan** [04:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tpOR1G-Gn2Y&t=274s)

Dusty May: "His couple seasons in the US hadn't gone as well as he had hoped, and so he was looking for a place that valued big men and the post position, and Aday and I clicked immediately. You could feel how good of a person he is, you could feel the benevolence of being a giving, caring person. I think he lacked a little bit of confidence at that point, but I just felt like the way our environment would allow him to thrive."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tpOR1G-Gn2Y&t=274s

**3. Gely Gómez — "after many years and a long career, it is his dream to have arrived here" — Aday Mara's parents on his long-term dream of reaching the NBA** [09:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tpOR1G-Gn2Y&t=577s)

**Gely Gómez:** "We are super excited to be here today. It's a very special day for him and for us. After many years and a long career, it is his dream to have arrived here where very few can be. And well, to be supporting him and see how he has achieved his dream."

**Javier Mara:** "To celebrate and be here. We are very happy that he was able to achieve his childhood dream. And today is the day that he is going to be chosen to play in the NBA, which was his childhood dream."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tpOR1G-Gn2Y&t=577s

**4. Gely Gómez — "that's what we would like him to achieve: a team where he can develop in peace" — Gely Gómez on Aday Mara's development and need for patience** [10:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tpOR1G-Gn2Y&t=627s)

Gely Gómez: "The truth is that we've always been behind him. We always thought things would be harder for him and then when he played in higher categories, he showed everyone including himself, that he could do it. That's what we would like him to achieve: a team where he can develop in peace. Have patience with him because in the end it will pay off. For sure."
