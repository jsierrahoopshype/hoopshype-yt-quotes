# Jalen & Josh Talk Knicks' Strategy After ECF Win | How Much Should Players Celebrate? — *Roommates Show*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5gcuVUP_1Wo

_Speakers identified: Jalen Brunson, Josh Hart, Matt Hillman_

**1. Jalen Brunson — "I think we need to manage it better than what we did last time and not have the start that we did." — Jalen Brunson on managing the break before the NBA Finals** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5gcuVUP_1Wo&t=0s)

**Josh Hart:** "Couple days off after the Cleveland Cavaliers series. Jalen, how do you think we're managing this after the last sweep we had?"

**Jalen Brunson:** "I think we need to manage it better than what we did last time and not have the start that we did. I think our last two days have been productive. We need to keep moving forward and make sure we're not wasting any days leading up to Game 1. Stay mentally focused and physically in tune. Trying to do a lot without doing too much."

**Matt Hillman:** "At the time of recording, it's May 29th. What have the last couple days looked like for you guys since you advanced?"

**Jalen Brunson:** "Playing a little bit. We've been playing in practice a little bit, getting some live action in. It's short, sweet days. We've been watching film of us and them, obviously both teams at this point. It's been a lot about us and what we need to be the best team we can be."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5gcuVUP_1Wo&t=0s

**2. Matt Hillman — "Statistically been the most dominant month in NBA playoff history." — Matt Hillman on the Knicks' historic playoff run** [01:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5gcuVUP_1Wo&t=92s)

**Matt Hillman:** "You guys have had a dominant last month. It's statistically been the most dominant month in NBA playoff history. What do you attribute this to? Are you guys just finding your stride at the right time?"

**Jalen Brunson:** "I think everything is just clicking. Everyone is being very unselfish. They don't really care who gets the praise, who gets the shots, who gets the touches. It's strictly about wins, and I think everyone is focused on that and doing whatever we can to help the team, putting the team's success over the success of us as individuals."
