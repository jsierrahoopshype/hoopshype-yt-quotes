**1. Stephen Jackson — "Anybody that go to Arizona, you going to die in Arizona." — Marvin Harrison Jr. needed a fresh start away from Arizona** [00:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MZGZkYq0t4g&t=34s)

**Matt Barnes:** "What have you been making of what you've been seeing of him?"

**Stephen Jackson:** "Anybody that go to Arizona, you going to die in Arizona. I am not talking about because of the heat. I am talking about playing for the Cardinals. I do not know how **Larry Fitzgerald** managed to stay alive. Well, they gave him **Kurt Warner** for a handful of years. But even still, before and after, you are like, oh my God. With **Marvin Harrison Jr.** coming out of Ohio State, he comes out of there with such high praise because he can really play. But when you are stuck in a situation where they did not draft him, meaning the front office, they do not feel obligated to make sure that they cuddle him to a certain degree. He had to be mentally tough, and it seems like from an outsider he was not there where he needed to be. So the best thing for him is to find a new location to keep his career alive."

**2. Stephen Jackson — "When he touchdown in Houston, he going to be a different dude." — Marvin Harrison Jr. will be a different player in Houston** [02:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MZGZkYq0t4g&t=128s)

**Matt Barnes:** "So nothing to worry about in your opinion?"

**Stephen Jackson:** "No, not at all. He can catch. That is why they took him at the fourth pick of the draft. But when you are in a situation in a funk, you are going to miss shots, you are going to drop balls in a bad situation, especially when they are treating you wrong. I can only imagine when he is walking down the hallway, the coaches are probably looking at him sideways. I can almost guarantee you to a fault, when he touchdown in Houston, he going to be a different dude. Because now you got somebody in **CJ Stroud** who is going to wrap his arms around him. I am looking forward to him pairing him up with **Nico Collins** whenever they get **Tank Dell** back. That crew is going to be something special."

**3. Stephen Jackson — "You just got to put in the back of your mind and remember how you got there." — How young players maintain mental toughness during losing streaks** [02:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MZGZkYq0t4g&t=177s)

**Matt Barnes:** "Also speak to how hard is that on your mental? For younger guys, how hard is that mental to be able to, the world is down on me, how do I keep myself up?"

**Stephen Jackson:** "You got to fight through it. We are not all going to be successful immediately. We all have some sort of trials and tribulations that we have to overcome in the sport that we play. I do not care if it is catching the ball, getting jammed at the line of scrimmage, dropping balls, not scoring enough touchdowns. You just got to put in the back of your mind and remember how you got there. That is the way even when I was on bad teams, I just be like, I got to do what I need to do. If I do what I need to do, then I am going to outlast them anyway."
