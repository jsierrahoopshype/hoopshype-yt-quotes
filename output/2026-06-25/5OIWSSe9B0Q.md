**5. Nigel Sylvester — "I grew up in a Caribbean household. That's a very strict household and school comes first." — Nigel Sylvester on his parents' reaction to him dropping out of college** [09:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5OIWSSe9B0Q&t=559s)

Nigel Sylvester: "I grew up in a Caribbean household. That's a very strict household and school comes first. My parents are immigrants from Grenada. They left their home to come to America for opportunity and worked non-stop. My mom worked seven days a week, double shifts, maybe sometimes triple shifts to provide. So for them, it's like school is the only option to reach success. I got to a point where I'm like, I know that I'm almost there. It's a matter of 12 to 18 months before I turn pro and I know it's going to happen. I just believed I was working my ass off. So I waited to the very last minute to tell them. The opportunity I got was to go to Europe for a month. I signed as I am to this European company called We The People. They were like, cool, come over to Europe. We're going to do a three-week tour in the UK and then we'll take you over to Germany for a week to the headquarters. I knew by the end of that trip, they were going to offer me a pro deal. So I went on the trip and I got back and I missed a month of college. You know if you miss a month of school, it's a wrap. It's over. So I was like, cool, I'm going to tell my parents I'm dropping out because there's no point."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5OIWSSe9B0Q&t=559s

**6. Nigel Sylvester — "I would say stay in school. Unless you have a straight up full proof plan." — Nigel Sylvester on the importance of staying in school** [16:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5OIWSSe9B0Q&t=989s)

Nigel Sylvester: "I would say stay in school. Unless you have a straight up full proof plan, and nothing's full proof, but unless you have a solid plan and you understand what you're trying to attack, I would say stay in school. The thing about school that I realized after I dropped out that was so important, it was it teaches you structure. It's not necessarily what you're learning every single day in the book and everything like that. It's the structure. Being on time, showing up, periods one through eight, whatever the case may be, and that was the important part that I feel contributed to even my success and where I'm at now. It's the structure I learned all those years. I'm able to structure my career and okay, cool, I know I'm going to put a certain amount of time for the business stuff, a certain amount of time for working on my body, a certain amount of time for riding. And that also contributed to where I'm at now. So for a kid, I'm like, man, like, if you don't know what you actually want to do, stay right there and like get that degree."
