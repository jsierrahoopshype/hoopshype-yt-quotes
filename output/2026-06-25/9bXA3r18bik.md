**2. John Calipari — "He was such a good scorer, I didn't give a shit about his defense." — John Calipari on coaching Darius Acuff's defensive development** [01:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9bXA3r18bik&t=107s)

**DeMarcus Cousins:** "As the draft goes and you hear about guys, their strengths and their weaknesses in their games, one of the things that came up about Darius was his impact on the defensive end not being as strong as teams would like it. As his coach at the college level, how did you put him in the best positions possible for his defense and whatever that he lacked to be able to translate to the next level?"

**John Calipari:** "He was such a good scorer, I didn't give a shit about his defense. Let me just say, scoring is at a premium, coach, tell them the truth. But here is what I will tell you, I have already had this talk. I was in not only the final stuff, but the playoffs before that watching. If you are a target on defense, you cannot stay in the game because they will weave and pick and roll and down screen until you switch on the ball and they are driving."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9bXA3r18bik&t=107s

**3. John Calipari — "If you are a target on defense, you cannot stay in the game." — John Calipari on defensive switching and being a target** [02:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9bXA3r18bik&t=150s)

John Calipari: "If you are a target on defense, you cannot stay in the game because they will weave and pick and roll and down screen until you switch on the ball and they are driving. What New York did with Jaylen, which was kind of, I kind of liked it, he would step up on a ball screen so high and then run back to his own man because he was not going to switch on the guy with the ball. And when he did, he did get beat on a bounce and they had some help behind, but I told him, you do not want to be the target. You are good enough to defend, you are strong enough to defend, you are quick enough to defend, and if you can get by people on the bounce like you do, then you can stop them from dribbling by you. The biggest thing is your chest now. Back in the day, it was a hand check, it was a forearm. Now, it is a chest bump. You drive, you got a chest. Well, you got to be strong enough to do that. He is. I do not think defense will be a problem."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9bXA3r18bik&t=150s

**5. John Calipari — John Calipari on being surprised by the draft order** [04:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9bXA3r18bik&t=277s)

**6. John Calipari — "It was easy because he wanted to play for me." — John Calipari on recruiting Darius Acuff to Arkansas** [05:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9bXA3r18bik&t=328s)

John Calipari: "It was easy because he wanted to play for me. So, when I went in, mom and dad remembered the meeting, which was one, body language got to change. Body language screams, it has got to change. You have to appear that you want to be coached. You have to learn to be a great teammate, flow of the game, get people involved. You have to be a leader. You cannot just be playing and locked into your own thing. And that is all we worked on. There were things I did not know there were some of the things he could do. And I let him go, you know why? Because he could make the play, so go make it. And late in the game with four or five minutes to go, I would ask him, where do you want the ball? How do you want this? What are you going to do? What do you see? And he could go do it. I think again, the recruiting was easy. The NIL was easy. I just said, here is what we are going to do. And again, folks, I got to build a team. I cannot give you everything. I have to have a team, and you cannot have a team of four guys. And they were fine with it. They wanted to win."
