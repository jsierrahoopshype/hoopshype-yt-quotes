# Richard Jefferson Explains Why the 76ers WON the Offseason — *Road Trippin*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z9jUB39O5OU

_Speakers identified: Allie Clifton, Richard Jefferson, Channing Frye_

**1. Richard Jefferson — "I think Minnesota is. Here is why I say no. I think in the next two years, for sure they are." — Richard Jefferson argues Minnesota had a better offseason than Philadelphia** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z9jUB39O5OU&t=0s)

**Allie Clifton:** "Would you guys say that Philadelphia was the clear-cut winner this offseason?"

**Richard Jefferson:** "No. I say no. I think Minnesota is. Here is why I say no. I think in the next two years, for sure they are. For sure, Philadelphia. But for the long run of my organization, for the next two years, because they are going for a chip, Minnesota is like, what can we do for our future to keep **Anthony Edwards**? I think getting **LaMelo Ball** for what they did really is going to revamp that team and make them even more exciting to play with over the next five to 10 years because they will be able to retain some of these great players."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z9jUB39O5OU&t=0s

**2. Channing Frye — "They have a short-term fix, they have a long-term fix." — Channing Frye evaluates the 76ers' short-term and long-term roster moves** [00:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z9jUB39O5OU&t=54s)

Channing Frye: "They have a short-term fix, they have a long-term fix. They added **Jaylen Brown** who is what, 29? They added **Anthony Simons**, so they got shooting and scoring off the bench. They got KCP, they got shooting and defense off the bench. You add a **Dean Wade**, they have got another defensive hybrid four-five guy that can knock down shots. So when I look at short-term, LeBron add, yes. Long-term, **Jaylen Brown** could be the best player on your team for at least another five years."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z9jUB39O5OU&t=54s

**3. Richard Jefferson — "After looking at who they got on the bench, I have to agree with Richard." — Richard Jefferson changes his stance on the 76ers' offseason success** [02:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z9jUB39O5OU&t=169s)

Richard Jefferson: "After I see the bench and this little thing here, I forgot about, I think **Dean Wade** very underrated. I think getting **Anthony Simons** shooting off the bench, I have seen that guy play. **Kentavious Caldwell-Pope** is big. He is just a winner, everywhere he goes they win. Obviously, look at the spacing, the floor spacing, they understand their roles. So I retract my statement. I still think Minnesota is close too, but after looking at who they got on the bench, I have to agree with Richard."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z9jUB39O5OU&t=169s

**4. Channing Frye — "You traded a guy that wasn't an All-Star for an All-NBA player in the exact same position." — Channing Frye explains the trade logic behind acquiring Jaylen Brown** [03:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z9jUB39O5OU&t=226s)

Channing Frye: "I said Philly might have won even if they didn't get Brown, because they traded the **Paul George** contract for **Jaylen Brown**. You traded a guy that with all due respect, **Paul George** is a future Hall of Famer in my opinion. So you traded a guy that wasn't an All-Star for an All-NBA player in the exact same position that is significantly younger. That right there is one of the biggest wins that you can have."
