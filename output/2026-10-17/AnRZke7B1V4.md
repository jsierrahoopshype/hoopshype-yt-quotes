# Vince Carter & T-Mac On The Pressure Of Being A Franchise Player & Leading Through Adversity — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnRZke7B1V4

_Speakers identified: Vince Carter, Tracy McGrady_

**1. Vince Carter — "Now you're the guy, you've developed the leadership skills, vocal skills to be that guy." — Vince Carter on the pressure of being a franchise player** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnRZke7B1V4&t=0s)

**Vince Carter:** "Now you're the guy, you've developed the leadership skills, vocal skills to be that guy, and now you're having a bad stretch. Bad game or two, which turns into a bad week, turns into a bad month. Every time you step in front of the media, every time you turn on the TV or read the paper, it was about you and your bad play or you and your funk. How were you able to handle that?"

**Tracy McGrady:** "Man, bump that week. That was my young self and young frustrated self with the team direction, with losing players that I had an attachment to that I felt like I can win with."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnRZke7B1V4&t=0s

**2. Tracy McGrady — "The leadership is now on my shoulder as a young player. We don't have the talent." — Tracy McGrady on the frustration of leading a struggling team** [02:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnRZke7B1V4&t=122s)

Tracy McGrady: "Now I'm in a season where our leader is gone. The leadership is now on my shoulder as a young player. We don't have the talent. I'm on my fourth year here, and I'm extremely frustrated because I feel like I'm at my best right now in terms of what the work that I put in, the player that I'm becoming. I'm frustrated with a particular person in this franchise, and I don't know how to deal with this right now. I'm leading the league in scoring, we're not winning games, information is being leaked out about what I'm not doing by a particular individual at this time. So there is a lot of stuff that this young 23, 24-year-old is trying to juggle on this losing street."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnRZke7B1V4&t=122s

**3. Vince Carter — "I went from one of the young guys in the league to being the oldest guy." — Vince Carter on his evolution from young player to veteran leader** [06:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnRZke7B1V4&t=366s)

Vince Carter: "I went from one of the young guys in the league on the team to one of the young guys in the league, to in the middle, to the 20 of us, to being the oldest guy in the league. So as I got older, it was easier. I'd been around, seen a lot, and the league got younger. The young guys coming in got mad respect for it, changing its mentality. You get the guys who are willing to listen, and you get other guys around the league coming in. We had **Donovan Mitchell** on, and he was one of those guys that wanted to talk. I never had a problem being a leader, vocal leader, saying what I had to say."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnRZke7B1V4&t=366s

**4. Tracy McGrady — "I've always had great leaders on my team. Darryl Armstrong was a great leader." — Tracy McGrady on learning from veteran leaders in his career** [08:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnRZke7B1V4&t=515s)

Tracy McGrady: "In my career, I've always had great leaders on my team. We spoke about Toronto, our leader is there. **Darryl Armstrong** was a great leader along with some of the other guys that were on our team in Orlando. When we played with **Pat Garrity**, **Darryl Armstrong**. But Houston, **Juwan Howard**, **Bob Sura**, **Shane Battier**, I've always been around just natural-born leaders. So even though I'm the best player, I let them thrive in what they do best. I don't need to show that I'm the big dog of this locker room because I already got some big dogs in terms of how they lead, and I love how they lead, so I'm going to follow."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnRZke7B1V4&t=515s
