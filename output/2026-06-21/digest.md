## Giannis Trade INCOMING?!? + Kawhi To Warriors?? — *Run It Back*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zQ0GgCOOEhQ

_Speakers identified: Michelle Beadle, Lou Williams, Chandler Parsons, DeMarcus Cousins_

**1. Chandler Parsons — "If they are not winning, you have to look at the reality of the situation." — Why the Milwaukee Bucks should consider trading Giannis Antetokounmpo** [02:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zQ0GgCOOEhQ&t=135s)

Chandler Parsons: "If you are the Milwaukee Bucks, you have to be worried. We have seen this movie before with star players in small markets. If they are not winning, you have to look at the reality of the situation. **Giannis Antetokounmpo** is a top-five player in this league, but if the roster around him is aging and the cap space is tied up, you are just spinning your wheels. At some point, you have to have the hard conversation about whether you can actually build a championship contender around him again before his prime window closes."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zQ0GgCOOEhQ&t=135s

**2. DeMarcus Cousins — "Kawhi Leonard on the Warriors would be a nightmare for the rest of the league." — Feasibility of Kawhi Leonard joining the Golden State Warriors** [12:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zQ0GgCOOEhQ&t=760s)

**DeMarcus Cousins:** "The idea of **Kawhi Leonard** going to the Golden State Warriors is fun for the fans, but let's be real about the logistics. You are talking about a guy who needs load management and a team that is trying to squeeze every last drop out of **Stephen Curry**. Does that fit? I think it is a pipe dream."

**Lou Williams:** "I disagree. **Kawhi Leonard** on the Warriors would be a nightmare for the rest of the league. If he is healthy, he is the ultimate plug-and-play superstar. He does not need the ball to be effective, and he gives them that secondary scoring option they have been searching for since Klay Thompson left."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zQ0GgCOOEhQ&t=760s

**3. Chandler Parsons — "I just don't see a world where the Clippers trade Kawhi Leonard to the Warriors." — The feasibility of a Kawhi Leonard trade to Golden State** [1:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zQ0GgCOOEhQ&t=3600s)

**Michelle Beadle:** "Do you think there is any reality where **Kawhi Leonard** ends up with the Golden State Warriors?"

**Chandler Parsons:** "I just don't see a world where the Clippers trade **Kawhi Leonard** to the Warriors. It makes zero sense for the Clippers. If they are moving on from him, they are looking for a total rebuild, young assets, and draft picks. Golden State is trying to win right now with Stephen Curry. They do not have the assets to make that trade happen without gutting their entire roster."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zQ0GgCOOEhQ&t=3600s

**4. Chandler Parsons — "If the Milwaukee Bucks continue to struggle, Giannis Antetokounmpo is going to want out." — Giannis Antetokounmpo's long-term future with the Milwaukee Bucks** [1:00:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zQ0GgCOOEhQ&t=3625s)

Chandler Parsons: "If the Milwaukee Bucks continue to struggle, **Giannis Antetokounmpo** is going to want out. It is not about him being unhappy today, but the reality of the NBA is that superstars want to compete for championships. If they are a play-in team or a first-round exit, the pressure on that front office to make a move becomes unbearable. He has been loyal, but loyalty has an expiration date when the roster around you is aging and the cap space is non-existent."
