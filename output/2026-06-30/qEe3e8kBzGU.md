# Warriors' pursuit of LeBron & AD, Blazers' plans after acquiring Ja Morant & more | SportsCenter — *NBA on ESPN*

_Speakers identified: Nicole Briscoe, Bobby Marks, Chiney Ogwumike, Shams Charania, Kendrick Perkins_

**1. Bobby Marks — "If you sign LeBron James to a $15 million contract, you don't have room to fill out the roster." — Golden State's salary cap constraints limit pursuit of LeBron and Anthony Davis** [01:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qEe3e8kBzGU&t=84s)

Bobby Marks: "The Golden State Warriors are at $177 million in salary right now. With the **Kristaps Porzingis** contract, the first apron hard cap is $209 million, which leaves them $32 million below that number. Remember, **Draymond Green** opted out of that $27 million number. You are expecting that it is probably going to come in somewhere between that $20 million range. If you sign **LeBron James** to a $15 million contract, you don't have room to fill out the rest of your roster here. So, you will have to go out and make a trade and clear out some room to do so."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qEe3e8kBzGU&t=84s

**2. Shams Charania — "Ja Morant's value for all intents and purposes had just splat out plummeted over the last several months." — Memphis traded Ja Morant to Portland to reset their roster** [03:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qEe3e8kBzGU&t=223s)

**Shams Charania:** "Memphis felt like it was time. They had to turn the page and **Ja Morant**'s value for all intents and purposes had just splat out plummeted over the last several months, especially once they made **Ja Morant** available last season. It was time for them to move on. They scoured the league for different trade options and they landed on this deal with the Portland Trail Blazers. They get a veteran forward back in **Jerami Grant**, but clearly for Portland, this is all about the Trail Blazers and this was a swing. It is a high-upside talent swing that they are able to do for a guy that has got a more favorable contract than even **Jerami Grant**'s deal."

**Nicole Briscoe:** "Ja was the last of the three they thought were going to be the franchise cornerstones. There was **Desmond Bane** and **Jaren Jackson Jr.** being the other two. All three have now been traded."

**Kendrick Perkins:** "It is a new start not only for **Ja Morant**, it is also new management, ownership-wise, and a new head coach."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qEe3e8kBzGU&t=223s

**3. Chiney Ogwumike — "Ja Morant in my eyes will have to own the transition game and inspire this young squad." — Ja Morant must improve efficiency and transition play in Portland** [05:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qEe3e8kBzGU&t=327s)

Chiney Ogwumike: "There is a lot of new in Portland, but they want to have the same competitive edge that they finished the season with. A lot of their top star players all have chips on their shoulders. **Damian Lillard**, **Danny Avdija**, and **Ja Morant** all have chips on their shoulders. How does he fit? I think that this is a versatile group. **Ja Morant** in my eyes will have to own the transition game and inspire this young squad to keep coming along because I think you will have a little bit more room to operate. This is a guy that last year, per qualified players, was still sixth best in the NBA in drives, but he shot under 50% from the paint for the first time ever in his career. So hopefully a little bit less pressure, a little bit less usage, owning the transition game, and making sure the guys like **Scoot Henderson** and **Shade Sharpe** still can also be dynamic as well."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qEe3e8kBzGU&t=327s

**4. Shams Charania — "The conversation hasn't been about Jaylen Brown's game. Everyone knows Jaylen Brown is an elite player." — Celtics engaged in trade talks for Jaylen Brown due to contract value** [07:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qEe3e8kBzGU&t=433s)

Shams Charania: "The conversation hasn't been about **Jaylen Brown**'s game. Everyone knows **Jaylen Brown** is an elite player, but you combine the contract that he is on, he has got three years left on his supermax clip, and where we are at in the off-season right now, what you are seeing is a price variance based on the player he is and the contract he is at and the assets that are available. There is a stark variance among those three categories. The Celtics have gone out in the marketplace, we know they offered **Jaylen Brown** and two first-round picks for **Giannis Antetokounmpo**. They made a calculation that we believe Giannis is a better player than **Jaylen Brown**, so we are going to make that trade."
