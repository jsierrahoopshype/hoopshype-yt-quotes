You got **Naz Reid** going out, but you also traded **Jaden McDaniels** into cap space. And so, once you saw that, everybody's like, oh man, what are the Timberwolves preparing for? They're preparing for something and moving Jaden out. So that's how they created the cap space. Now I said not deplete your roster. You look at this now though and the Timberwolves does not have a four. Their starting four and their backup four is now gone. **10. Draymond Green — "The second apron is a hard cap. And once you hit that hard cap, it is debilitating." — The NBA second apron is a debilitating hard cap** [33:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F7PXwNRGRLc&t=2037s)

Draymond Green: "The second apron is debilitating. I said this before the deal was signed. I said it after the deal is signed. I'm going to say it again. Going into the second apron is debilitating. If you go into the second apron, everybody's like, oh man, he should just be willing to go into the second apron. No, he should not. The second apron is a hard cap. And once you hit that hard cap, it is debilitating. You lose draft picks, you lose exceptions, you lose roster spots. You lose everything. And guess what? You no longer can compete for a championship. That's why you keep seeing everybody duck under the second apron. The second apron has hurt everyone. It has hurt the NBA as a whole. As an entirety, the second apron has been awful. It'll be interesting to see if the league and the NBPA will figure this out and get rid of the second apron. It has unintended consequences like I said from the very beginning. It's awful. It's not good for anyone. It caps the amount of money players can make. It caps the amount of things that team owners can do. I don't think anyone's happy about the second apron. Anyone. They kind of need to be a ratification because it's fucking things up in a major way."
