Shams Charania: "Shortly after the NBA Finals, Lakers ownership and front office met with **Austin Reaves** and his agents at the team's practice facility in Los Angeles. It was in a private event room, and they had all the personal touches for **Austin Reaves** in this meeting. They played his favorite country music. They had personalized AR-initialed pillows for **Austin Reaves** and everyone else that was in attendance. It all comes down to the money. The Lakers came to the understanding that **Austin Reaves** had multiple max contracts available to him in free agency if he hit the market on July 1. One of those teams, the Detroit Pistons, were coming aggressively for him. So that's why today, the Lakers stepped up, gave him this deal, four years, $185 million max contract for **Austin Reaves**. He becomes the richest player ever to sign a contract at this level for an undrafted player in NBA history."

Brian Windhorst: "The Lakers can spend whatever they want on **Austin Reaves** and it not impact their cap position, but whatever they spend on **LeBron James** will take away their cap space.
