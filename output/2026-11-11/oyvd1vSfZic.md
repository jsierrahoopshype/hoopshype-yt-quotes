# Why Zack Martin Didn’t Want to Play for Rich Rodriguez at Michigan — *Road Trippin*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oyvd1vSfZic

_Speakers identified: Kendrick Perkins, Channing Frye, Allie Clifton, Richard Jefferson_

**1. Zack Martin — "If Michigan offers me, I'm going to Michigan." — Zack Martin on his recruitment process and choosing Notre Dame over Michigan** [00:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oyvd1vSfZic&t=8s)

Zack Martin: "I got recruited by a lot of the Midwest. I was all in on Michigan. I was obsessed with Michigan. I loved the helmets, I loved **Jake Long**. **Jake Long** was number one pick in the draft, left tackle. I was playing offensive tackle. I said, if Michigan offers me, I'm going to Michigan. So I go up there and visit, they offer me, okay, yep. And a month or two later, the head coach retires, **Lloyd Carr**. Famous grandfather of the current quarterback at Notre Dame, **CJ Carr**. So he retires, and **Rich Rodriguez** came in. I went up there and visited, I didn't really love what they had going on. They played the spread. I was like, I'm going to the NFL, I want to go to an NFL-style offense. So my dad was like, you need to go to Notre Dame. You need to go visit Notre Dame, they're recruiting you. I grew up Catholic, I grew up, you know, I came to Notre Dame and I hung out with the guys and I'm like, this is the place for me. So I was like, I'm going to Notre Dame. Within two weeks, I'm going to Michigan, two weeks later, I committed to Notre Dame."

**3. Channing Frye — "No one in my family played basketball. I started playing basketball later." — Channing Frye shares his college recruitment story as a high schooler** [02:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oyvd1vSfZic&t=134s)

Channing Frye: "I'm a high school kid. No one in my family played basketball. I started playing basketball later. No one really knew who I was, but I started getting letters like my sophomore, junior year. Letters start coming in. Everyone starts recruiting. And I don't know anything. My parents don't know anything. I don't know anything. And this is probably 1996. My high school coach goes, so like, okay, all these schools, have you ever had a favorite school? Is there any school that you're fan? And I was like, I don't know, I kind of like Rudy. I like Notre Dame. And he was like, okay, I'll take it from here. I'll take it from here. But Notre Dame was the first school that ever came to my mind when somebody asked me about a school that you would be interested in."
