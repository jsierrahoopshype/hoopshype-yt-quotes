# God Shammgod On How His Iconic Move Was Created, Who Did It Best & His Basketball Legacy — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k9Glm4So77M

_Speakers identified: Tracy McGrady, God Shammgod, Vince Carter_

**1. God Shammgod — "I have done every variation that ever been put on video." — God Shammgod on the origin and evolution of his signature move** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k9Glm4So77M&t=0s)

**God Shammgod:** "It is funny that we have this conversation because every time somebody does a variation of the move they call Shammgod, people act like I jumped on some podcast before the game and said, 'Yo, I am going to do the Shammgod, y'all going to love it.' It is funny when people come at me and say, 'Well, somebody did something like this.' I laugh because I have done every variation that ever been put on video. The difference is the kids now and us growing up, we knew how to dribble, we did not know how to do moves. Whatever the defense did, that is what you reacted to. The problem now is no matter if I back all the way in the paint, you are euro-stepping. That makes no sense."

**Vince Carter:** "Just doing the move just to do it."

**God Shammgod:** "If I am playing you to the left, you worked on left-to-right cross, so you are going to cross no matter what when you could just go left. When people do a move and say, 'Oh, he upgraded this, he upgraded that,' I am like, 'No, you know how many times I practiced those things with my friends before 97?' In that game, that was what was called for. I was actually trying to do something else and he reacted, so I reacted."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k9Glm4So77M&t=0s

**2. God Shammgod — "I would say Tony Wroten. He did it to a double cross and he shifted." — God Shammgod identifies Tony Wroten as the best user of his move** [06:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k9Glm4So77M&t=400s)

**Tracy McGrady:** "What is the best Shammgod you have seen? Who has done the best?"

**God Shammgod:** "I would say **Tony Wroten**. He did it to a double cross and he shifted. He got drafted to Philly. He threw it out, he crossed, and then the dude went and he crossed it back and the dude behind him shifted too and he laid it up. I give **Tony Wroten** love. Of course, the natural ones are **Chris Paul** and **Kyrie Irving**, and **Wimbley** had a good one. He did. I give **Wimbley** love and shout out to all the other people that did it. I was there in attendance, you remember **L.O.**, the Blackout?"

**Tracy McGrady:** "I was there. That was sick."
