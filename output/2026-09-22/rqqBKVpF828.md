# Colin Kaepernick On The Front Office Pushback Before The Super Bowl — *ALL THE SMOKE*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rqqBKVpF828

_Speakers identified: Colin Kaepernick, Matt Barnes, Stephen Jackson_

**1. Colin Kaepernick — "When you know coaches have confidence in you, you know you can go out and let things loose." — Colin Kaepernick on the importance of coaches having confidence in players** [01:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rqqBKVpF828&t=91s)

**Matt Barnes:** "You touched on something that I think a lot of athletes, but the fans may not understand the importance of the coaches having confidence in you. You mentioned **Alex** cleared protocol, but you kept the keys to the car. What did that immediately do to your confidence?"

**Colin Kaepernick:** "To your point, very underappreciated by fans. When you know coaches have confidence in you, it changes everything. You know you can go out and let things loose. When they don't, you know every decision you make is going to be questioned, looking over your shoulder. And for me, having that confidence was like, oh, we just got to go. It's all gas, no brakes."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rqqBKVpF828&t=91s

**2. Colin Kaepernick — "Harbaugh came to me after the season before we kicked the playoffs off. I think he did this intentionally." — Colin Kaepernick on Jim Harbaugh's decision to start him over Alex Smith** [02:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rqqBKVpF828&t=132s)

Colin Kaepernick: "What made this even more definitive for me, and it was interesting, is **Harbaugh** came to me after the season before we kicked the playoffs off. I think he did this intentionally because he knew my mentality at that point, my approach. I would take things very personally and be like, oh, I'm going to prove you wrong. He was like, I just want you to know, when I made the decision to start you, the GM didn't agree with the decision. There were some other coaches that didn't agree with the decision. Management didn't agree. But you give us the best shot to go win a Super Bowl. And that was one of the moments that was like, I have to make you right. I got to let all of them know you made the right decision, and I'm the one that's going to take us there."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rqqBKVpF828&t=132s

**3. Colin Kaepernick — "The one that people don't talk about that we actually had a game plan a lot for, Haloti." — Colin Kaepernick on the strategic importance of Aldon Smith and Haloti Ngata** [08:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rqqBKVpF828&t=497s)

Colin Kaepernick: "The one that people don't talk about that we actually had a game plan a lot for, **Sugs**. **Sugs** was one of them. **Haloti**. **Haloti Ngata**. He was on the internal defensive line. He was a problem. He was a problem. He was one of those guys you had to double. You always had to know where he was on all the play calls. And it was like, we had audibles based upon where he was at. He was that much of a factor, which is to get that in the internal defensive line, like that's normally you're not game planned that way."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rqqBKVpF828&t=497s

**4. Colin Kaepernick — "There was a moment where there was enough focus, enough momentum that if we collectively got players to stand up" — Colin Kaepernick on the missed opportunity for players to create institutional change** [12:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rqqBKVpF828&t=735s)

Colin Kaepernick: "I think as players, and I think more broadly, I think there was a moment where there was enough focus, enough momentum that if we collectively got players to stand up, we could have created significant institutional change. And I think that goes well beyond the NFL. We saw some of this in the summer of 2020 with the NBA, and I think there was broader opportunity to push the balance there, too. Getting players to realize the power and influence that they have, I think that becomes really important. I think there was a missed opportunity there."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rqqBKVpF828&t=735s
