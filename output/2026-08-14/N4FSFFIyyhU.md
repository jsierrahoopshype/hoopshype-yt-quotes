# Perk Says Rookie Rondo Had Confidence He'd NEVER Seen Before — *Road Trippin*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N4FSFFIyyhU

_Speakers identified: Allie Clifton, Rajon Rondo, Richard Jefferson, Kendrick Perkins, Channing Frye_

**1. Kendrick Perkins — "Rondo came into the league with a level of confidence like I've never seen in my fucking life." — Rajon Rondo's extreme confidence as a rookie** [00:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N4FSFFIyyhU&t=52s)

**Kendrick Perkins:** "Rondo came into the league with a level of confidence like I've never seen in my fucking life. So when he got on the team, we were trash as hell. It was a battle of the point guard position, and **Sebastian Telfair** had just signed and came over. Every day, Rondo walked in and had it on his mind like, 'Y'all keep anointing this motherfucker.' You could tell he was like, 'I need to get everybody out of my way that's in my position.' Every single day at practice, he was going at motherfuckers at his position. It was non-stop."

**Rajon Rondo:** "He used to try to get me to do little rookie things. He used to pay me to do stuff, but he was only like what, second year, third year player at the time?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N4FSFFIyyhU&t=52s

**2. Kendrick Perkins — "We lost 18 straight that year, but we kicked it. We lost 18 straight and we kicked it." — The Celtics' 18-game losing streak and team culture** [02:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N4FSFFIyyhU&t=133s)

**Kendrick Perkins:** "We lost 18 straight that year, but we kicked it. We lost 18 straight and we kicked it. Paul wasn't even on the team at the time. We had a thing: Rondo, myself, **Al Jefferson**, **Delonte West**, **Tony Allen**. When I tell you we turned up, it wasn't a city that we touched down in that we weren't out at the club. That was our younger days."

**Rajon Rondo:** "First it was the mall, then the club. We had to go to the mall first."

**Richard Jefferson:** "Can you imagine the Spurs with Rondo going to the mall to get a belt?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N4FSFFIyyhU&t=133s

**3. Kendrick Perkins — "We maxed out all of our credit cards so much in the gentlemen's club that me, Delonte West, and Tony Allen took a cab back." — Celtics players maxing out credit cards in Memphis** [03:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N4FSFFIyyhU&t=205s)

**Kendrick Perkins:** "We maxed out all of our credit cards so much in the gentlemen's club that me, **Delonte West**, and **Tony Allen** took a cab back. We were all loaded up in taxis because it wasn't Ubers and stuff. We were in the hood of Memphis. We left there and we got back to the hotel and we didn't have no more money. We went so broke in the club we couldn't even pay the cab."

**Richard Jefferson:** "Didn't even pay for the cab?"

**Kendrick Perkins:** "No, we ran out."

**Channing Frye:** "Because they had maximized all their charges, so when they went to go pay for the 12 dollar cab it was like, 'Declined, declined, declined.'"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N4FSFFIyyhU&t=205s

**4. Rajon Rondo — "I think every team has aspirations to do, in a sense, especially if you don't take every day for granted." — Rajon Rondo on the possibility of a reunion trip** [04:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N4FSFFIyyhU&t=275s)

Rajon Rondo: "I haven't spoken about it. Obviously it's something that I think every team has aspirations to do, in a sense, especially if you don't take every day for granted, being able to be alive and celebrate your accomplishments even from many, many years ago. So for us, that is something that should be on our to-do list. I'm definitely open to doing it. I love traveling, I'm a planner. But last time I got to say so or go ahead to do it, we weren't on one accord. I think now I think we will be, or we can be. We still have a lot of guys we reach out to, **Scott Pollard**, obviously, but a lot of the guys that were on that team, see what guys' availability is and then try to get to it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N4FSFFIyyhU&t=275s
