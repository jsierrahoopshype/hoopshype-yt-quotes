**4. Shams Charania — "The Celtics were not going to commit an extension to Jaylen Brown." — Shams Charania on why the Celtics traded Jaylen Brown** [10:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xt52SNYYNRI&t=600s)

Shams Charania: "I saw what **Brad Stevens** said, tying up 70% to two players with **Jaylen Brown** and **Jason Tatum**. Where that argument doesn't hold up is that **Paul George** and **Jaylen Brown** are basically making the same amount. So the next two years **Paul George** is on a contract on a max deal, I think around $55 million, **Jaylen Brown** is around 57. So you're still tying up your salary cap with 70%. So that leads teams around the league to ask the question, like why did you trade **Jaylen Brown** now? Couldn't you have waited as the summer went on, as next season went on, because he had three years left on his deal? Now the answer to that is very simple: the Celtics were not going to commit an extension to **Jaylen Brown**. They clearly did not want him or felt strongly enough about him long-term for whatever reason internally that when his extension was going to come around in late July, they knew they were not going to offer **Jaylen Brown** the two-year $140 million plus."
