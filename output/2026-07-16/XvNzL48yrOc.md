# Golfing with LeBron & Kevin Hart & Summer League Standouts With Draymond Green — *The Draymond Green Show*

_Speakers identified: Draymond Green_

**1. Draymond Green — "I didn't shoot a 123. When I walked up, Kevin said, 'Draymond, what you shoot a 123?'" — Draymond Green clarifies his golf score with LeBron James and Kevin Hart** [00:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XvNzL48yrOc&t=36s)

Draymond Green: "There is this common misconception going around the internet that I shot a 123. Where you all are wrong is, I didn't shoot a 123. When I walked up, Kevin said, 'Draymond, what you shoot a 123?' And I said, '123?' He said, 'Yeah, you shot a 123.' I said, 'No, I didn't shoot a 123.' I said, 'What you shoot?' He said, '119.' He was trying to beat LeBron and **Steve Stoute** to the punch of saying what he shot. So he threw it out there. I'm like, 'Whoa, I didn't expect that he shot a 119.' So I'm like, 'Oh, shit. You shot a 119?' He said, 'Yeah, I shot a 119, and what you shoot? What's your score?' Once he announced his 119, I was baffled because the last time I shot a 119, it was a while ago."

**2. Draymond Green — "When you have length and some athleticism and you can shoot the ball, there's always a spot." — Draymond Green evaluates Yaxel's potential fit on the Warriors** [03:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XvNzL48yrOc&t=210s)

Draymond Green: "**Yaxel** has been balling, averaging 17 and 7 boards. The Dubs are having a good Summer League. I've seen all the talk like, 'Where does he fit on the Warriors this upcoming year?' And the reality is, when you have length and some athleticism and you can shoot the ball, there's always a spot. You will find minutes wherever you need to. There's always a spot for that. Where does he fit? I don't know. That'll get figured out as you put the pieces in the place and who plays together. There's so much more that goes into that, but with that skill set, there's always a spot. There's always a role."

**3. Draymond Green — "I know you don't like rookies that much, but I'm not the average rookie." — Draymond Green critiques Yaxel's pitch to LeBron James** [04:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XvNzL48yrOc&t=277s)

**4. Draymond Green — "When I said, 'Yo, Caleb Wilson's floor could be Kevin Garnett,' everybody like, 'Yo, you're an idiot.'" — Draymond Green explains why Caleb Wilson's floor is Kevin Garnett** [06:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XvNzL48yrOc&t=416s)

Draymond Green: "When I said, 'Yo, **Caleb Wilson**'s floor could be **Kevin Garnett**,' everybody like, 'Yo, you're an idiot.' And I'm like, 'No, let me help you understand why I said **Caleb Wilson**'s floor could be **Kevin Garnett**.' Because **Caleb Wilson** comes in an era where big men can dribble the ball, big men can shoot threes. Big men pretty much have free rein if you can do it to do whatever you want. **Kevin Garnett** didn't have that. **Kevin Garnett** played in a very structured era of basketball where the power forward shot 18 to 20 footers and they banged down low just like a center, and **Kevin Garnett** banged with those guys. And **Kevin Garnett** is one of the greatest power forwards we've ever seen, top three power forward we've ever seen. And so when I said **Caleb Wilson**, **Kevin Garnett** could be the floor, I said that with all due respect to **Kevin Garnett**. But just saying, yo, this kid can be that special."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XvNzL48yrOc&t=416s

**5. Draymond Green — "It's a great backcourt. I think ultimately you have to figure out how does that work." — Draymond Green analyzes the new-look Trail Blazers backcourt** [13:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XvNzL48yrOc&t=815s)

Draymond Green: "We haven't truly discussed Ja Morant to the Blazers. Their backcourt now consists of **Damian Lillard**, **Scoot Henderson**, and **Shaedon Sharpe**, which I think is a great backcourt. I think ultimately you have to figure out how does that work, right? Like, who's guarding bigger? Obviously we know Scoot can guard bigger, but between **Damian Lillard**, **Shaedon Sharpe**, and Scoot, who's going to guard bigger? How do you make those lineups work, you know, together? As opposed to like, 'Oh man, when Shaedon's on, **Damian Lillard**'s not.' How do you, what can you do to make those lineups work together? Who are the other pieces around them to make those lineups work together? Because I think ultimately that's going to be the key to figuring out how well this fit works, how good ultimately this team can be."
