And then you look at now they have **Brandon Miller** and they have **Kai Jones** and they have kind of an exciting young team that won a lot of games last year. And it's clear by the decision that **Jeff Peterson** and the front office made by sending them all, LaMelo here, is that they looked at last year's success and determined, you know what, we are not quite there yet. We are going to actually more so add another draft some more draft pick compensation, we are going to get LaMelo, a player that we really, really like, and we're sort of going to take a deep breath and wait a little bit more."

**5. Jim Souhan — "Finch is pretty adamant that he does not see Jaden McDaniels as a power forward." — Chris Finch's flexible approach to the Timberwolves' power forward rotation** [16:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sCtgW3aCIy0&t=978s)

Jim Souhan: "I talked to **Chris Finch** in Vegas about this, and I know that **Chris Hines** did as well, and what struck me and what was surprising to me was that Finch is pretty adamant that he does not see **Jaden McDaniels** as a power forward. That he still thinks that he keeps him at the three, he keeps him guarding the perimeter, and wants him kind of in more of like a wing playmaking, you know, shot creation mode on offense than he would be if he was playing power forward. And so, you know, Finch told me that he's actually envisioning being very malleable night-to-night on who gets those minutes depending on the matchup. One night it could be **Terrence Shannon Jr.** if they need a little bit more scoring punch in that role. One night it could be **Jaylen Clark** if they need kind of a stouter, defensive-minded player in that mode. One night it could be Trey Lyles if they're playing a bigger team and they need a little bit more height and a little bit more rebounding."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sCtgW3aCIy0&t=978s
