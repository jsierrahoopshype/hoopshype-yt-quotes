**4. Jeezy — "He just said you got to do it third person. I'm like, what is that?" — Jeezy on the importance of rapping in the third person** [07:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vcMB_4Rdf1k&t=425s)

Jeezy: "I was trying to rap and not tell on myself. He just said you got to do it third person. I'm like, what is that? So he told me what third person was and I was like, oh, I can do this shit all day. And that's when **Jeezy** was born. You know what I'm saying? Because I was able to rap in third person and that's when my career started to take off. And as far as like one foot in, one foot out, I'm going to be honest, I don't think a lot of people even know. And I'm proud of that now because like when I put out Thug Motivation, I spent all the money to put that album together. I got all, I did everything myself. I got a deal at Def Jam at the end, right? And this deal I got was around the time that the streets started going upside down. And I was just lucky enough to get a deal because I wanted to get my mom a house, I want to set myself up and this was something that I wanted to do. And so the whole Thug Motivation, like I was one foot in, one foot out, you know what I'm saying? Like I was literally like trying to figure it out, you know what I'm saying?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vcMB_4Rdf1k&t=425s

**7. Jeezy — "My grandma always told me like, you want something from somebody else, you got to give them something first." — Jeezy on the importance of giving away music for free** [18:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vcMB_4Rdf1k&t=1088s)

Jeezy: "Also, too, I think that the way that I was doing the music, like giving it away because my grandma always told me like, you want something from somebody else, you got to give them something first. So I gave them my best work for free. And then by the time I got ready to get on, which was, I mean, you know, at first I thought it was, was, was, was a curse, but it was a gift, is when my album got leaked, you know, like two months before it came out. Thug Motivation got leaked two months before it came out, you know what I'm saying? So I'm like, I'm done. This is over with. But really, and truly, that's what happened. It leaked in the middle of me giving out free music. So all the bootleggers had it. And that's another thing streaming helped with. But literally, like, bootleggers would get your record, and I ain't talking about, like, I'm talking about they got a whole operation set up. You know what I'm saying? It's like, they still have that, though. Right. What is it about hip hop that like, if you don't see that in entertainment, really, like, it's still even in the streaming era, it's a huge thing that people try to. I don't, I don't think, I think that, you know, culturally, we'll pay for some shit early. You know what I'm saying? And I can't feel bad because you know how many **Juvenile** and **Hot Boys** CDs I brought in front of Magic City? You know what I'm saying? I couldn't wait."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vcMB_4Rdf1k&t=1088s
