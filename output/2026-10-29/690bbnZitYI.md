# Fred VanVleet Joins the Show as Road Trippin’s Player Correspondent — *Road Trippin*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=690bbnZitYI

_Speakers identified: Allie Clifton, Fred VanVleet, Richard Jefferson, Channing Frye, Kendrick Perkins_

**1. Fred VanVleet — "You got to have that internal belief and that comes from working hard and having confidence in yourself." — Fred VanVleet on the importance of self-belief for undrafted players** [01:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=690bbnZitYI&t=75s)

Fred VanVleet: "Just believing in yourself and not waiting on somebody else to come and give you that nod and that notoriety that we all desperately want. But you got to have that internal belief and that comes from working hard and having confidence in yourself. That came out of necessity obviously. I wouldn't have had the tweet that if I got drafted. To be there and have to try to find my way into the league, there's a lot of different ways to make a living and a lot of different ways to make it to be in the NBA. I had an unusual path."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=690bbnZitYI&t=75s

**2. Fred VanVleet — "I used the G League to get up to speed to the NBA game." — Fred VanVleet on adjusting his game from G League to NBA** [02:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=690bbnZitYI&t=168s)

Fred VanVleet: "Every step of the way had always been kind of a floor general, winner, run the show, steady presence. And when I got to the league, it was a completely different game. I used the G League to get up to speed to the NBA game. I remember coaches, management, GMs telling me, 'Bro, you got to shoot the ball. Nobody, we're not paying nobody to go out there and not shoot the ball.' So I had to learn the speed of the game. I had to learn how to find my spots and be a scorer. But my whole life I really just kind of played like a quarterback out there on the floor. That's what I naturally do. I just talked to **Chip Engelland** this morning who's been around for 25, 30 years, to never take a minute in the NBA for granted, to never miss an opportunity, to always be ready when your number is called."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=690bbnZitYI&t=168s

**3. Fred VanVleet — "I'm really just a translator out there on the court just trying to be the buffer." — Fred VanVleet on his coaching dynamic with Ime Udoka** [04:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=690bbnZitYI&t=278s)

Fred VanVleet: "I think we both see things the same way. We both have the same approach, we just speak different languages. I'm really just a translator out there on the court just trying to be the buffer between him and the guys because we always got three to four guys on the court that's pissed off at all times because Ime is yelling at them, cussing at them. So I got to be the good cop, he's the bad cop. But no, it's one of my favorite coaches that I ever worked with, played for, been around. He just makes coming in to work every day a joy and he's just intense for those two and a half, three hours during the game, but the rest of the time it makes coming in to work fun and you know that he's all about winning and I can live with that."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=690bbnZitYI&t=278s

**4. Fred VanVleet — "Can you have championship habits versus let's focus on winning championship, let's just focus on having championship habits." — Fred VanVleet on the Rockets' need for championship habits** [06:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=690bbnZitYI&t=360s)

Fred VanVleet: "I think last year proves to not get ahead of yourself. We had a little bit of front runner energy last year and when adversity hit, we never really recovered from that and we were trying to scramble and figure it out. So I think this year is more so back to the basics, stick to the details day in and day out. Can you have championship habits versus let's focus on winning championship, let's just focus on having championship habits every day."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=690bbnZitYI&t=360s

**5. "I was wondering how was you and Amen Thompson going to fit alongside one another." — Kendrick Perkins and Fred VanVleet on Rockets' team chemistry** [06:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=690bbnZitYI&t=391s)

**Kendrick Perkins:** "I was wondering how was you and **Amen Thompson** going to fit alongside one another, right? And I watch you take a backseat at times and allow him to bring the ball up. I'm also watching you guys utilize him in the screen and roll and having him as a diver going to the basketball. By the way, that was one of the most athletic blocks I've ever seen in my life, that block he had on him to save the game."

**Fred VanVleet:** "Yeah, he's taking another step. Incredible worker, incredible athlete, just got that can score at all different levels. And I mean, look, we don't have to keep talking about it, but to judge us and judge individual guys based upon what you saw last year, I just think wasn't the best thing for anybody. Nobody looked their best self last year. So you get him back, he's a year better, a year older, you're stronger, had experience last year, and you give him his point guard back, yeah, he's going to look like how he's looking right now where he's getting the ball in spots, he's getting it closer to the basket. They got to respect my shot, they triple teaming Kevin. If he's getting one-on-one coverage, it's going to be scary. So I hope that they keep guarding us the way that they've been guarding us because he's going to continue to put these numbers up. It's not many people in the league that can guard him one-on-one with no help."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=690bbnZitYI&t=391s

**6. Fred VanVleet — "The main thing that I noticed right now is just having a joy for the game back." — Fred VanVleet on regaining the joy of playing basketball** [11:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=690bbnZitYI&t=661s)

Fred VanVleet: "The main thing that I noticed right now is just having a joy for the game back and really just taking a step back and really just being appreciative that I can walk, that I can run, that I can jump. It's just starting there. I had five points last night. Two years ago if I would have had five points, I would have locked myself in the room and not talked to nobody. And so just to be back now and just to be happy to be able to be back on the court, having a new found perspective on the game and on life in general is just having fun and really going back to the basics of like this is a child's game that we play."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=690bbnZitYI&t=661s

**7. Fred VanVleet — "There's no ego should ever be involved right now." — Fred VanVleet on the importance of team buy-in** [13:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=690bbnZitYI&t=787s)

Fred VanVleet: "The same message that I always preach, it's just easier now because everybody paid. Everybody paid up and what we tripping about? We in the West, it's only going to be one or two All-Stars if we lucky unless we win 65 games and we run away with it then we might get three. But what we tripping about? Everybody paid, there's nothing to worry about, the roles is what they is. There's no ego should ever be involved right now. And the second part is you have to have a team good enough to respect the buy-in because if we actually think that we're good enough to win it all, then the buy-in comes naturally. But when you have them fringe teams where it's like, I don't know, that's where the selfishness creeps in."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=690bbnZitYI&t=787s

**8. Fred VanVleet — "There is no nights off. It is so much talent spread around the league." — Fred VanVleet on the lack of easy games in the NBA** [16:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=690bbnZitYI&t=1011s)

Fred VanVleet: "There is no nights off. It is so much talent spread around the league. Every team has talented players now. Nobody had Memphis going four and 0 to start the year. This team, unfortunately a couple of injuries this week which sucks for the game, but I'm just watching basketball all day, it's some high-level hoops going on early on in the season so it's going to be a great year."
