# The Thunder Lost More Than Just Their Title Odds | Rachel Nichols & Chris Mannix — *Sports Illustrated*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hBVUie-Kj5A

_Speakers identified: Chris Mannix, Rachel Nichols_

**1. Chris Mannix — "the best way to describe this injury is a Gordon Hayward type of injury" — Jalen Williams' season-ending injury and its impact on the Thunder** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hBVUie-Kj5A&t=0s)

Chris Mannix: "The unfortunate news yesterday that **Jalen Williams** will be out for a substantial amount of time. I would imagine by the time people are listening to this, it will be revealed that **Jalen Williams** is done for the season. He goes down late in the game between the Thunder and the Clippers, and the best way to describe this injury is a **Gordon Hayward** type of injury. People remember how **Gordon Hayward** went down for Boston in 2018, first game after signing with the Celtics. **Paul George** same kind of thing, but I think the injury itself was **Gordon Hayward**-esque because it was the same type of injury where the leg just went the wrong direction, dislocated."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hBVUie-Kj5A&t=0s

**2. Rachel Nichols — "in truth it's going to be AJ Mitchell who's going to next man up" — The Thunder's path forward without Jalen Williams and the role of AJ Mitchell** [01:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hBVUie-Kj5A&t=99s)

**Rachel Nichols:** "I don't think it goes out the door at all. Do I think it will be a little harder without their second-best player? Yeah, I do. I heard some of the capologists amongst us talking about they can apply for an injury exception, I think it's around $14 million, but with the roster and with the amount of money already going out the door, would they even use that? In truth it's going to be **AJ Mitchell** who's going to next man up. And look, he's a guy who we've been talking about for a couple seasons now, who absolutely is starter quality in this league."

**Chris Mannix:** "They have the resources because they're such a deep team, but there's a reason also that **Jalen Williams** was the guy starting, because he is exceptional."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hBVUie-Kj5A&t=99s

**3. Rachel Nichols — "it was very reminiscent of back in 2014. You remember in 2013, the Heat obviously won" — Comparing the Thunder's current situation to the 2014 Heat** [02:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hBVUie-Kj5A&t=169s)

Rachel Nichols: "For me, it was very reminiscent of back in 2014. You remember in 2013, the Heat obviously won the NBA title on the Spurs with that **Ray Allen** shot, absolutely devastated the Spurs. A lot of people don't realize that was Game 6. They actually played a Game 7. San Antonio had the chance to still come back and win that series, but they were so broken by what had happened to **Ray Allen**. It took them the whole summer. **Greg Popovich** said, 'I think about Game 6 every day,' right? And then they came back the next year on a mission and they were just not only on a mission to win the title, which they did, but they were on a mission to completely game plan and beat the Heat, which they did."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hBVUie-Kj5A&t=169s

**4. Chris Mannix — "they won 80% of the games that Jalen Williams didn't play in" — The Thunder's ability to compete without Jalen Williams** [05:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hBVUie-Kj5A&t=300s)

Chris Mannix: "I'm cautiously optimistic that they still can be, mostly because they already have been. **Jalen Williams** for more than half the season was out last year. The Thunder, they won 80% of the games that **Jalen Williams** didn't play in. That's a remarkable number. They went 8 and 2 in the playoffs without **Jalen Williams**. Now, they lost the last two, which really is all that matters. They have shown that they can compete without him. Now they did lose some backcourt talent, some wing talent. **Lou Dort** is gone, **Aaron Wiggins** is gone, to a lesser degree **Isaiah Joe** is gone. But you've still got **Cason Wallace** still there, you've still got **Alex Caruso**, you've still got **AJ Mitchell** still there, and you've got **Jared McCain** that's still there. So you've got a lot of pieces."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hBVUie-Kj5A&t=300s

**5. Chris Mannix — "I'm in favor of expanding training camp, making that longer, giving guys maybe more time" — The need to lengthen training camp to reduce early-season injuries** [08:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hBVUie-Kj5A&t=530s)

Chris Mannix: "I'm in favor of expanding training camp, making that longer, giving guys maybe more time to ease into the speed of the regular season. I mean, three of those injuries with the Achilles did happen during training camp. So there's that, but you know, I had this conversation with **Will Hardy** last week and I think he's in favor of lengthening training camp as well for more practice time, fewer exhibition games, just give these guys time to get into the kind of shape you need to be in to play at that breakneck pace that is the NBA."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hBVUie-Kj5A&t=530s
