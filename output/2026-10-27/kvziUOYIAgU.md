# Kevin Garnett opens up on WHY he really left the Wolves and MAKING it work with Paul Pierce 👀 — *Out The Mud*

**1. Kevin Garnett — "He didn't want to resign Sam or he didn't want to resign Sprewell." — Kevin Garnett on the Timberwolves' failure to retain Sam Cassell and Latrell Sprewell** [00:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kvziUOYIAgU&t=11s)

Kevin Garnett: "I won an MVP and then the very next year, it was like some Dolan shit, bro. He didn't want to resign Sam or he didn't want to resign Sprewell after we had just got close like that. I had been through this conversation with him a couple times to get to the point where we was at, and yeah, it was the last straw. I think he fired Flip and then next thing you know, I got a new coach probably two, three years whatever."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kvziUOYIAgU&t=11s

**2. Kevin Garnett — "You're going to look up, you're going to be exhausted all your great years here, bro." — Kevin Garnett's decision to leave Minnesota after advice from Chauncey Billups and Tyronn Lue** [00:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kvziUOYIAgU&t=44s)

Kevin Garnett: "I talked to Chauncey and I talked to Ty Lue, who was real friends. We was in the summertime, we was all working out, and they was like, 'Man, you got to move on, man. I know you don't want to, but you're going to look up, you're going to be exhausted all your great years here, bro. You could have, you know what I mean?' That was the first time I heard that, like I actually listened to it and touched. I just called Kevin and I said, 'Yeah, let's go ahead and start to find another. I don't think I'm going to, let's find something different. This ain't working for me.'"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kvziUOYIAgU&t=44s

**3. Kevin Garnett — "I had played with Paul before and I had played with Ray, but not officially." — Kevin Garnett on his decision to join the Boston Celtics** [01:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kvziUOYIAgU&t=75s)

Kevin Garnett: "I had played with Paul before and I had played with Ray, but not officially. Ray is from South Carolina, so I knew him. So I liked it. I liked the idea, other than the other options I had. I think I had a Phoenix option, I had a Laker option, couldn't get Kobe on the phone. It was acting real arrogant, so I was like, 'You know what? I'm just going to fuck with P and them,' and that's how I ended up in Boston."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kvziUOYIAgU&t=75s

**4. Kevin Garnett — "If you're going to play with Paul, Paul cares about these things." — Kevin Garnett on Paul Pierce's advice regarding team dynamics** [01:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kvziUOYIAgU&t=97s)

Kevin Garnett: "I had to holler at Antoine before I went to Boston, though. That's when he gave me the lick on P, like, 'If you're going to play with Paul, Paul cares about these things and, you know, blah blah blah. This worked with us, this what didn't work,' you know what I mean? And I took that to heart. Shout out to Antoine for that."

**5. Kevin Garnett — "In Boston, man, nothing is given. Everything you get, you earn." — Kevin Garnett on the culture of earning respect in Boston** [02:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kvziUOYIAgU&t=123s)

Kevin Garnett: "It was legendary. You know, in Boston, man, nothing is given. Everything you get, you earn. So, you know, I was there for six years. I wasn't there for the whole time that P was there, so I wasn't expecting fans to, you know, cling or even for me on how they did, but I immediately attached myself with the city when I saw hard-working people, how people just moved, and how people just ten toes. That really resonated with me."

**6. Kevin Garnett — "I think Ray and I was established players. It was nothing that was going to." — Kevin Garnett on why he and Ray Allen clicked in Boston** [03:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kvziUOYIAgU&t=190s)

Kevin Garnett: "If I'm being honest, I think Ray and I was established players. It was nothing that was going to, when I mean by established, meaning that we had won things. I think Ray had been to a Western Conference Finals, I have been the one. We had won some awards, had been in countless All-Star shit. P, he was just coming into himself and being a young superstar, and I know he cared about first team, second team, third team. I knew he cared about All-Star and all that shit. He cared about that shit, and being honest, I'm 32 at this time. I don't care about none of this. You still, yeah, you still being young, moving around. I ain't moving like this. I've been league '95, you came in '99. So I was just an older, more mature and had more things to establish that some of the things that P cared about wasn't on my radar."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kvziUOYIAgU&t=190s

**7. Kevin Garnett — "All that personal shit got put to the side once this Larry O'Brien was the goal, man." — Kevin Garnett on putting personal goals aside for the team** [04:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kvziUOYIAgU&t=251s)

Kevin Garnett: "I got that and I said some of the realest shit to him. 'Hey bro, this your team. This your team, bro.' Because I understood the marathon. I wasn't going to let some petty shit that can be fixed between players and guys come in between, you know? So I attacked the cadence right away. I let him know, 'Hey man, some of this shit, I don't care about that shit.' All that personal shit got put to the side once this **Larry O'Brien** was the goal, man. Everybody put their personal shit aside, man. We all locked in on that. So I couldn't be no different from that."
