**2. Rasheed Wallace — "try your best not to let him get a post-up dunk." — Rasheed Wallace on the defensive strategy to contain Shaquille O'Neal** [01:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aRCD8aFHWRM&t=114s)

**The Kid Mero:** "What was the scouting report on a young Kobe and **Shaq**?"

**Rasheed Wallace:** "Try your best not to let him get a post-up dunk. If you break him down and you go to the rim and lob it, all right, that's different. Try not to let him get a post-up dunk, try to make him go right shoulder, and the double has to come from that B-gap. For those not familiar, when you got the ball on the right side and it's the right elbow that's A, the B is like middle of the foul line, and then the C is the opposite elbow. So coming from that B-gap because the way he take up his space, so you try everything. You trying to double from A, B, and C gap."
