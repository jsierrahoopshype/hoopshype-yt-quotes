**1. Rachel Nichols — "I would ask Adam about how they vet potential owners and whether the events of the last year" — Rachel Nichols on NBA vetting process for potential team owners** [01:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cX4v1L8He9A&t=77s)

**Rachel Nichols:** "I would ask Adam about how they vet potential owners and whether the events of the last year have made them rethink a stronger process. Because it's not just what happened with the Lakers and the **Mark Walter** situation, it's what's going on in Portland. You are talking about a battle between the team and its city. You've got fans in fear of their team being taken away, you have employees being fired right and left. It is hard to argue that the Portland Trail Blazers, having fired medical staff, travel people, long-time broadcasters who know the game and the history of the team, it's hard to argue they're better off now than they were before **Tom Gores** took over as owner."

**Chris Mannix:** "Wait, you said **Tom Gores**. You meant Jody Allen."

**Rachel Nichols:** "I meant Jody Allen. Yes, thank you for the correction."

**2. Rachel Nichols — "Sports teams are a public trust. They just are. You don't own them, you take care of them." — Rachel Nichols on NBA's responsibility to set ownership standards** [05:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cX4v1L8He9A&t=307s)

Rachel Nichols: "Sports teams are a public trust. They just are. You don't own them, you take care of them until the next owner. And who gets to decide whether that's the case or not? The NBA, the sports leagues. I live in a neighborhood where for some of the houses, the HOA says that you can't build a second story onto your house because there's a great view and it would block the view for the rest of the entire neighborhood. That's the rule. You know that's the rule when you decide to buy the property. Property values for those houses are lower than they would be for elsewhere in the neighborhood because you can't build a second story onto the house. On the other hand, they get first shot at this great view. You can make rules that affect what these teams go for and who buys them. They could decide, these are the things that we need an NBA owner to do, including, I'm not going to move the team for 15 years. I've got to sign that. You can make decisions that hedge funds aren't in. They only recently decided to let them in. You can say, great, ownership has to be 30 percent. You can decide what kind of people own your teams."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cX4v1L8He9A&t=307s
