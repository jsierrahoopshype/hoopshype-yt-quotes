# MPJ Admits His Preparation SLIPPED After the All-Star Snub — *Road Trippin*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4kM5P08h3q4

_Speakers identified: Michael Porter Jr., Kendrick Perkins, Allie Clifton, Richard Jefferson, Channing Frye_

**1. Michael Porter Jr. — "I think as a number one option on a team, in the playoffs, when the game gets tight, every team knows each other's plays." — Michael Porter Jr. on his potential as a number one option** [00:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4kM5P08h3q4&t=11s)

Michael Porter Jr.: "I think as a number one option on a team, in the playoffs, when the game gets tight, every team knows each other's plays. You need some guys who are very good off the dribble. That was one of my best skill sets all throughout my high school career. My injuries were very unique in the fact that I had to reshape my entire game. I'm a good shooter, but that's not what I relied on in high school. I was trying to get downhill, off the dribble, and create and dunk on you. If the mid-range was there, the mid-range was there. If the three was there, the three was there. But that was my game. I'm not about to sit here and say that I don't think I can take on a big responsibility on a championship team, because I do. But I definitely would need some help with me."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4kM5P08h3q4&t=11s

**2. Michael Porter Jr. — "Once I didn't make that All-Star game, I kind of let my foot up off the gas." — Michael Porter Jr. regrets letting his preparation slip after All-Star snub** [01:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4kM5P08h3q4&t=85s)

Michael Porter Jr.: "For the majority part of the year, I was shooting about 40% from three. One thing I regret is that once I didn't make that All-Star game, I kind of let my foot up off the gas because there was really nothing we were playing for anymore. We couldn't make the playoffs, I couldn't be an All-Star, so I let my foot off the gas. I wasn't in the weight room as much, I wasn't preparing as much, and my three-point percentage dropped. But even the crazy shots I was shooting this year, I still was almost 40%."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4kM5P08h3q4&t=85s

**3. Michael Porter Jr. — "The biggest challenge would be I felt like we could have competed better." — Michael Porter Jr. on the mental challenge of losing in the NBA** [02:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4kM5P08h3q4&t=176s)

Michael Porter Jr.: "The biggest challenge would be I felt like we could have competed better. I know the business side of things. I know when you're not playing for a championship, now in the NBA, a lot of teams are almost competing for the top draft picks. That's a conversation that needs to be had about how do we fix that, because fans don't come here to watch teams attempt to lose. But I think the biggest challenge was in my whole basketball career, I've never been a losing player. I've never lost that many games. I've never laced them up just to go out there and lose like that consistently. So that was hard mentally. And then I think it was almost like another rookie season in the fact that this was a completely different role, completely different energy output than I've ever had in my whole career, because in Denver, I'm getting created for so much with Jamal and Nikola in the two-man game. I'm pretty much just chilling on the offensive end, and if it comes my way, I'm letting it fly or I'm driving a closeout."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4kM5P08h3q4&t=176s

**4. Michael Porter Jr. — "Tim Hardaway, he's willing to take some of those same shots I was shooting." — Michael Porter Jr. on playing with Kentavious Caldwell-Pope** [04:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4kM5P08h3q4&t=241s)

Michael Porter Jr.: "When they traded me for Cam, I knew going over there, he wouldn't put up the same numbers as I did in that role because he probably wouldn't be willing to take the same shots. Now Tim Hardaway, he's willing to take some of those same shots I was shooting. He might shoot just as crazy shots as I was shooting. He's willing to jump on his jumper and shoot with a guy right here. And I knew over there in Denver, when you have the opportunities to get a shot off, yeah, you should probably try to be aggressive. So it just felt like a completely different year this year."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4kM5P08h3q4&t=241s

**5. Michael Porter Jr. — "I think I got to really work on like Steph does, really being in shape." — Michael Porter Jr. on his goal to become a better off-ball player** [04:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4kM5P08h3q4&t=294s)

Michael Porter Jr.: "My energy output this last year was so much higher than in Denver because of how much I was relied on and the defenders I had on me. Every team's putting their best defender on me. I'm going from **Asar Thompson** to **Jaden McDaniels** to this guy to that guy every game. My energy output, I just think I got to really work on like Steph does, really being in shape, so all game I can just move off the ball. I think I could be the best off-ball player in the NBA because I'm good at cutting around the rim. My finish percentage around the rim is like real high. And then, you know, catch and shoot threes and things like that. I think if I'm in good shape, I could be the best off-ball player in the NBA. So I think that's the main thing off the court. And then on the court, I think getting in that mid-post, face up, when stuff gets tough at the end of the game, mid-post face up, not a ton of dribbles, you know, one-dribble pull-up, jab shot. I think I got to master that a little bit better."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4kM5P08h3q4&t=294s
