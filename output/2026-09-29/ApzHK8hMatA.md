**1. Keyshawn Johnson — "I never really wanted him here to begin with. And that was because I was protecting Anthony Davis." — Keyshawn Johnson on his initial opposition to LeBron James joining the Lakers** [00:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ApzHK8hMatA&t=38s)

Keyshawn Johnson: "I never really wanted him here to begin with. And that was because I was protecting **Anthony Davis**. That is how I felt when they were talking about him coming here. My protection over the legacy was more of a protection of what I was afraid that he might do to eclipse **Anthony Davis**. That was in my mind, although it would never happen. It didn't happen, but if it would have, then what? That is how I felt, because obviously, you know, the Kobe Bryant relationship, the whole thing. So I was always like, I don't need him."
