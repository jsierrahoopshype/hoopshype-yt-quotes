# Nobody Has Bet On Themselves More Than Jalen Duren | Chris Mannix & Sam Amick — *Sports Illustrated*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xMTHC09Yp6U

_Speakers identified: Chris Mannix, Sam Amick_

**1. Chris Mannix — "Jalen Duren, who turned down a contract extension offer from the Pistons last year, played a tremendous regular season." — Jalen Duren's contract standoff and qualifying offer deadline** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xMTHC09Yp6U&t=0s)

**Chris Mannix:** "We are going to talk about the Lakers in a minute, but I have to get your take on the biggest free agent that is out there right now, and that is **Jalen Duren**. Restricted free agent in Detroit, still has not signed the long-term extension, believed to be around five years, 200 million dollars. He has until Thursday to sign the qualifying offer. Sam, how did we get here with this? **Jalen Duren**, who turned down a contract extension offer from the Pistons last year, played a tremendous regular season. And I stand by the belief that if he had pulled his hamstring in game 82, we would not be sitting here right now, that the Pistons would have stepped up, paid him probably what he wants to be paid, and we would not be having this conversation."

**Sam Amick:** "After yesterday, it could not be more toxic. I was pretty surprised by the way that the Pistons, headed by their front office president of basketball ops, Trajan Langdon, decided to frame things at media day. On the one hand, told reporters I am not going to talk about negotiations, but then had a couple of zingers where he compared Jalen to his time in New Orleans and presumably Zion Williamson, which, as you know, there is a weight clause in Jalen's latest offer, which is also analogous to the Zion situation. Around the league, people are scratching their heads, going, apparently, clearly there is some concerns about Jalen's conditioning or dedication."

**2. Sam Amick — "I guarantee you it does not put Jalen in a better head space as this qualifying offer deadline comes." — The impact of the qualifying offer deadline on Jalen Duren** [02:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xMTHC09Yp6U&t=136s)

Sam Amick: "I was just talking to an executive before we hopped on about just handicapping, do you think he will have the stomach to actually take the QO, assuming the Pistons do not up the offer or if he does not take the current one? And I do not know the answer to that because yes, he has a lot of intel and confidence regarding his market next summer that has buoyed his belief that the QO might be a good way to go because over the course of five years, if you can get a max four-year deal next summer, then you make your money up. He can get the money back, but you are skipping over the scary part, which is the season in between when you make 9.6 million dollars and you have to be concerned about getting hurt."

**3. Chris Mannix — "Jalen Duren does not look like someone that has a need for a weight clause in a contract." — Questioning the weight clause in Jalen Duren's contract** [04:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xMTHC09Yp6U&t=240s)

Chris Mannix: "I cannot understand, nor can I find anyone that can really explain the weight clause thing. Zion had his well-publicized weight issues. He would balloon up, it would affect him physically, he would not be available because of that. **Jalen Duren** looks like a Greek god. **Jalen Duren** does not look like someone that has a need for a weight clause in a contract. What is your understanding of why this is a thing in Detroit?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xMTHC09Yp6U&t=240s

**4. Sam Amick — "There was a connection made between playoff performance and his body." — The connection between playoff performance and Jalen Duren's weight clause** [04:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xMTHC09Yp6U&t=276s)

Sam Amick: "I was as surprised as anybody when we learned about the weight clause. I will say, reverse engineering it, it was interesting from a reporting standpoint to then look into it, and credit to our beat writer in Detroit, **Hunter Patterson**, who this is his intel. When we connected on this topic, Hunter immediately said, oh, that is fascinating. And I can share this because we already reported it, but he said because I remember literally in the playoffs, somebody with the Pistons casually mentioning to me like, we kind of need Jalen to drop a little weight for next year. There was a connection made between playoff performance and his body. Since then, they have stumbled into some insight regarding, I do not think it is on the Zion level, nor do I think it should be, nor am I endorsing the concern, but just how they see it, that there is a belief around Trajan that he has some level of concern that if you hand Jalen this much money, that he is going to grind in the way that you would want him to."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xMTHC09Yp6U&t=276s

**5. Chris Mannix — "If Duren signs the qualifying offer, it will rank up there among the ballsiest moves that I have ever seen." — The audacity of Jalen Duren potentially signing the qualifying offer** [07:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xMTHC09Yp6U&t=434s)

Chris Mannix: "If Duren signs the qualifying offer, it will rank up there among the ballsiest moves that I have ever seen. Because I get what you are saying, like you can on the back end, you can make some of that money up. But when you take the qualifying offer, the reality is you are taking 9.6 million dollars instead of 40 million dollars. I remember years ago, remember when Porzingis with the Knicks and he was like threatening to sign the qualifying offer? Laughable to me. Porzingis was never leaving money on the table to sign a qualifying offer when he could have the extra money guaranteed. Guys do not do this. When they have a chance to sign long-term, they figure it out."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xMTHC09Yp6U&t=434s

**6. Chris Mannix — "I think back to Walker Kessler and what he did in Utah." — Comparing Jalen Duren's situation to Walker Kessler's contract standoff** [08:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xMTHC09Yp6U&t=500s)

Chris Mannix: "I wonder if Duren and his team, and maybe we have now reached the point of no return, but I think back to **Walker Kessler** and what he did in Utah. **Walker Kessler** had an offer on the table from the Jazz. It was not what the Lakers wanted to pay him. They believed that they were worth what the Lakers ponied up, the four years 130 million dollars, whatever that number ultimately was. He played that game of chicken and they won. I wonder if throughout this process Duren was kind of playing that same game, and now with the Pistons dug in and Duren seemingly dug in, maybe he has reached a point he did not think they were going to get to."
