**3. Channing Frye — "I think it's awesome that Tyrese Maxey is such a north and south player." — Tyrese Maxey's versatility as a transition and half-court playmaker** [01:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yppYz2AAKU8&t=98s)

Channing Frye: "I think it's awesome that **Tyrese Maxey** is such a north and south player. Because if you have **Jaylen Brown**, **LeBron James**, and **Joel Embiid**, who are all elite in transition, when he gets downhill, even at the end of a game, if you leave him, you are either giving him a layup and you are contesting late, or it's a drop-off pass to **Joel Embiid**, who is one of the best scorers in the NBA. Or it's a kick-out to guys who have been point guards in their own right. So for me, I love that they are working through **Tyrese Maxey** because he is giving them such easy shots."

**4. Richard Jefferson — "My game was predicated on angles, someone closing out to me." — Richard Jefferson explains the tactical advantage of his playing style** [02:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yppYz2AAKU8&t=167s)

And thinking about it, **Jaylen Brown** is going to have people closing out. You are at an advantage. I can pump fake, I can go against the grain right back at you. There are so many things versus me staring at five players trying to stop me. How much easier is it in the last 10 seconds if I got a player closing out to me?
