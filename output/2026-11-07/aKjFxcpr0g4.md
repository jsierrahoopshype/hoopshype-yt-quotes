# "The Presence of Giannis Has Withdrawn From the Heat Culture" | Chris Mannix & Evan Turner — *Sports Illustrated*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aKjFxcpr0g4

_Speakers identified: Chris Mannix, Evan Turner_

**1. Evan Turner — "the presence of Giannis has withdrawn from the Heat culture and what they hang their hat on" — Giannis Antetokounmpo's arrival has negatively impacted Miami's established team culture** [00:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aKjFxcpr0g4&t=7s)

Evan Turner: "I think one thing I always respected so much about the Miami Heat was their culture. You respected the culture, you count on the culture, even in the down times. I believe when you bring over such a dominant, impactful player, you are going to take on a lot of the characteristics. When it comes down to it, the presence of Giannis has withdrawn from the Heat culture and what they hang their hat on, which is great ball movement and defense. They have been terrible defensively this whole year. They are giving up so many 120-point games."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aKjFxcpr0g4&t=7s

**2. Chris Mannix — "they should not be 26th in the NBA in defensive rating" — Miami's defensive struggles and poor performance against the Clippers** [02:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aKjFxcpr0g4&t=131s)

**Chris Mannix:** "When I am watching the second half of that game, this is how Miami should be defensively. They gave up 40 points total in the second half. They have the horses to do that. I know Tim McMahon made the point that Giannis has not been a great defensive player for a couple of years. I do not know if I completely agree with that. When I talk to coaches, he is still a pretty intimidating presence in the paint. He and Bam are still a pretty formidable defensive duo. **Davion Mitchell** is in the league because of his defense. Wiggins is still, he won a championship a few years ago with his defense. They should not be 26th in the NBA in defensive rating. That should not be the case."

**Evan Turner:** "And it should not have been that either. How do you leave **Clay Thompson** open in the corner for a three? That was just a young mistake."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aKjFxcpr0g4&t=131s

**3. Chris Mannix — "Giannis throughout his career from start to finish has just been go, go, go" — Doc Rivers' comments on Giannis Antetokounmpo's execution and decision-making** [05:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aKjFxcpr0g4&t=303s)

Chris Mannix: "I do not think Doc was saying it in a negative way, but it is a negative thing to say. He was in a part of a broader point about conversations he has had with Giannis. He claims he has had the exact same conversation with Giannis before, particularly when it comes to execution, understanding the value of it. Giannis throughout his career from start to finish has just been go, go, go. I am going to get to the rim, I am going to overpower you, I am going to overwhelm you, and he has not really evolved."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aKjFxcpr0g4&t=303s

**4. Evan Turner — "he's been hooping like an evolved Jaylen Duren" — Giannis Antetokounmpo's unique playstyle and the challenge of building a team around him** [06:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aKjFxcpr0g4&t=393s)

Evan Turner: "I am a big Giannis fan. When you are a basketball purist, you have to compare him to some of the other players. It will always make you scratch your head because you cannot really scream a skill set killed you more so than his presence and his physicality and his natural sheer determination. I remember Dame saying, one thing about Giannis, say what you want, but he is a dog and he goes hard every single play. To try to adapt to play with him is a unique situation. He has been hooping like an evolved **Jaylen Duren**. He is hooping like a five-man with opportunity."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aKjFxcpr0g4&t=393s

**5. Chris Mannix — "they need to slow the game down, try to win 90-style basketball, be physical" — Miami's need to adopt a slower, more physical style of play** [09:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aKjFxcpr0g4&t=593s)

Chris Mannix: "I will die on this hill, man. They need to slow the game down, try to win 90-style basketball, be physical, grind out possessions on offense, get to the free throw line, make it ugly. **Pat Riley** basketball, make it ugly out there. Because playing high possession, you are going to trade a lot of two for threes. It is what you have to do."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aKjFxcpr0g4&t=593s
