**1. Bobby Marks — "Duren was a restricted free agent. There was really not much of a market out there." — Jalen Duren's restricted free agency and the Pistons' contract decision** [00:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t0T9ctgxqNU&t=30s)

Bobby Marks: "**Jalen Duren** was facing an October 1 deadline to sign that qualifying offer. As many of you know, Duren was a restricted free agent. There was really not much of a market out there for teams with cap space. We heard a lot of people talk about sign-and-trades, which would have been extremely difficult just because of base-year compensation. Basically, half of his salary would count in a trade. If his salary is starting at 35, half of it would count in a trade, so basically 17.5 million dollars."

Bobby Marks: "He agrees, as Shams reported, to five years, 200 million dollars. That puts him in the top 10, probably right around eight, in salary for this year among all the top centers. What was negotiated a year ago when he was a rookie extension, he basically doubled his total amount here. He bet on himself and he made probably an extra 100 million dollars by doing so."

**5. Bobby Marks — "What Detroit could not have done was let him sign that qualifying offer." — Why the Pistons avoided a one-year qualifying offer for Jalen Duren** [06:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t0T9ctgxqNU&t=390s)

Bobby Marks: "What Detroit could not have done was let him sign that qualifying offer. It would have been a mitigated disaster for the Pistons. Maybe you could have worked out a sign-and-trade in the next off-season, but if there are teams with cap space, that ain't going to happen here. They are just going to sign him outright."
