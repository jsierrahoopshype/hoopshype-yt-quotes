**4. Grant Liffmann — "The nightmare of all front offices is when things get publicized like this." — The negative impact of public contract negotiations** [08:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bni5wwnbf3c&t=535s)

Grant Liffmann: "The nightmare of all front offices is when things get publicized like this. Because the minute it becomes public and then it becomes toxic out there, because you can hide things, trust me, every front office has so much drama and tension and everything behind the scenes that they're dealing with every day all season, and it never gets out there so you never have to worry about it in that regard. For players, for agents, for all of this, there's nothing worse than when it gets out in the public because then fans jump in and now anything that happens from here on out, the team loses, if Duren struggles or something like that, is now into this huge thing where everyone's going to have an opinion about it. So that's the worst thing that could have happened in this situation."
