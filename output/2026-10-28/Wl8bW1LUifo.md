# Are the Trailblazers Top 4 in the West? Chandler APOLOGIZES to Ja Morant — *Run It Back*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wl8bW1LUifo

_Speakers identified: Michelle Beadle, Chandler Parsons, DeMarcus Cousins, Lou Williams_

**1. Lou Williams — "This league is about talent. It's about putting the best talent next to the best talent." — The Trailblazers have the talent to be a top-four team** [00:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wl8bW1LUifo&t=21s)

Lou Williams: "They are going to be competitive. At the end of the day, this league is about talent. It's about putting the best talent next to the best talent, and the Trailblazers have a lot of it. **Ja Morant** looks comfortable, **Damian Lillard** looks like he is back to being himself, and we are not even talking about **Drew Holiday**. A lot of conversations people will consider **Drew Holiday** one of the best players on this team, if not top two. They have talent. They have **Drew Holiday** coming off the bench. He can come in and get **Damian Lillard** first or he can come in and get **Ja Morant** first, which gives them the dynamic, and then they can finish games with all three of those guys on the floor."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wl8bW1LUifo&t=21s

**2. Chandler Parsons — "My first apology of the season is to Ja Morant." — Chandler Parsons apologizes to Ja Morant for past criticisms** [01:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wl8bW1LUifo&t=115s)

Chandler Parsons: "When it comes to the **Ja Morant** thing, and I was a victim of this too, with all the off-the-court distractions and stuff that he did and then the time he missed and the suspensions, you forget how good he is at basketball and how productive he still can be. I was a victim of it too. My first apology of the season is to **Ja Morant**. It also shows you where you don't want to be somewhere and that franchise is not where you want to be and they are not backing you and that fan base doesn't give you what you feel you deserve, that matters. It takes a toll on athletes and basketball players. I love just to see **Ja Morant** back to that aggression and finishing and using his, you know, bumping him, doing little floaters. He is still an elite point guard in this league. I think **Scoot Henderson** is going to be the odd man out, it's going to really stunt his growth and just his development just because these guys are ahead of him and getting more opportunity."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wl8bW1LUifo&t=115s

**3. DeMarcus Cousins — "The person that gets the short end of the stick is usually the player." — DeMarcus Cousins defends Ja Morant against media criticism** [03:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wl8bW1LUifo&t=214s)

DeMarcus Cousins: "It is good to see **Ja Morant** playing with that joy again. That is the part of the media that I hate with all of this. It can become a bad situation for multiple reasons, but the person that gets the short end of the stick is usually the player. It can be so much on top of it to the point where it can be results like this, to the point where we forget what the talent really looks like. This isn't a guy that went through injury, this isn't a guy that had a career-ending type of thing. It was none of those type of things. It was a guy that was unhappy in his situation and the situation wasn't supporting him correctly. So you get bad basketball out of that. It is no different than a bad relationship. You are not going to be the best husband or wife in a bad toxic situation. So I think that is the case for **Ja Morant**."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wl8bW1LUifo&t=214s

**4. Michelle Beadle — "Anthony Edwards is going to undergo an arthroscopic procedure, which means he is going to miss up to two months." — Anthony Edwards to miss two months after knee surgery** [05:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wl8bW1LUifo&t=326s)

**Michelle Beadle:** "We got some news from yesterday, which I think we can all agree sucked. The **Anthony Edwards** situation, he is going to undergo an arthroscopic procedure, which means he is going to miss up to two months. Right knee, that is the issue. He was seen grabbing the knee during Sunday's game against the Raptors. Chandler, I will start with you. This is obviously we all hate it and he was off to the best start of his career. He is averaging a little over 33, 48% shooting over his first four. They are 3-1 as a team. It seemed like he and **LaMelo Ball** were just like besties. How devastating is this news?"

**Chandler Parsons:** "Very. I hate to see anybody get injured, but especially **Anthony Edwards**. He is one of the biggest, brightest stars in the league and so much hype and excitement around this team pairing him with **LaMelo Ball**, them getting up to such a hot start. To see him go down for this long, too, and you see the possibility of him not being able to get the supermax now if he doesn't reach this stupid 65-game rule, it just sucks."

**DeMarcus Cousins:** "It sucks for sure. He was my MVP pick for this season. Regardless of him being out, I still think the Timberwolves have a chance to win at the end of the year. I think they have enough talent on that roster to kind of carry the load until he is able to return, but it sucks for him individually, obviously financially as well."
