**1. Chandler Parsons — "That was just an absolute choke job." — Chandler Parsons calls the Spurs' loss a historic choke job** [1:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NKJSww0fk_w&t=67s)

Chandler Parsons: "I still don't believe my eyes. I still can't believe it happened. I probably got to imagine the entire city of San Antonio is still in shock. That wasn't supposed to happen. That was just an absolute choke job. You got to credit the Knicks before you talk about San Antonio. They were resilient. OG was phenomenal, Brunson was great, Alvarado came in and he changed the game. They did do a lot of things to attribute the win, but in no way, shape, or form do you have a 29-point lead in the playoffs, regular season, preseason, and completely change the way you're playing that got you the lead, start getting sloppy with the ball, take early bad three-point attempts that you just weren't doing early. This was really bad and this is going to sting for a long time."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NKJSww0fk_w&t=67s

**2. DeMarcus Cousins — "Part of being an inexperienced team is learning how to close out the win, seal the game." — DeMarcus Cousins blames Spurs' inexperience for blowing double-digit leads** [3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NKJSww0fk_w&t=181s)

DeMarcus Cousins: "It goes to show that we talk about the inexperience of this Spurs group. We've watched them time and time again throughout this series have leads and completely lose them. Part of being an inexperienced team is learning how to close out the win, seal the game. I think that's the next step for that Spurs group, figuring out that part. Obviously, they had the talent to even put themselves in the position to be able to win. Double digits up down the stretch and you find a way to lose that game. It's no one person's fault, I think that falls on the entire group, but I just have to blame it on inexperience. You have to learn how to close out these games. They've made the same mistakes time and time again throughout the series and this same bug is continuing to bite them down the stretch."

**4. Chandler Parsons — "This was just a bonehead play." — Chandler Parsons critiques De'Aaron Fox's late-game decision-making** [7:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NKJSww0fk_w&t=425s)

Chandler Parsons: "**Sean Elliott** came on our show earlier this week and he was saying how one play doesn't lose the game. There were a lot of plays that were bad, there were a lot of mistakes they made, but that one play that **De'Aaron Fox** didn't dribble the ball out, it might not have lost the game, but it could have won the game for them. And so I think that is just a huge play. You are there for that experience, you are there for that breath of fresh air that can kind of slow everybody down and make the right decision. This was just a bonehead play and I hate it for him because I know this is going to go with him for a really long time. Fans are going to hold this against him for probably the rest of his career."
