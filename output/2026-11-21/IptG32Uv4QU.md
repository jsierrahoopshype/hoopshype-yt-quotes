**3. Caron Butler — "I started watching film with him on every plane trip, just analyzing the rotations, the schemes." — Caron Butler on Kobe Bryant's obsession with film study and game preparation** [02:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IptG32Uv4QU&t=135s)

Caron Butler: "Basketball genius. All he talked about was the game. I started watching film with him on every plane trip, just analyzing the rotations, the schemes, the double teams, where they was coming from, how to manipulate the defense. I learned a whole bunch from Kobe. And also too, when you talk about life after basketball, he gave me like a jewel that stuck with me. The roar of the crowd is not for you, it's for what you can do. And as soon as you can't do it, they be cheering for someone else. So work on your second act while you're in the midst of your first."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IptG32Uv4QU&t=135s

**4. Caron Butler — "The first thing I saw, he was just standing on business." — Caron Butler on Kobe Bryant's business-focused personality off the court** [03:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IptG32Uv4QU&t=205s)

Caron Butler: "It's unique because I saw him literally put his guard down. I heard all these things about him before I got there like, don't fuck with him, he's selfish, he's this, he's that. And once I got there, the first thing I saw, he was just standing on business. So I was just like, I fuck with that. All he doing is he's standing on his business. And then I start asking him stuff and he like, man, sit right here, let's talk. And he just became like so welcoming."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IptG32Uv4QU&t=205s

**7. Caron Butler — "He was a dog when it came to that work." — Caron Butler on Gilbert Arenas's work ethic and status as a superstar** [06:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IptG32Uv4QU&t=392s)

Caron Butler: "He was one of the hardest workers. **8. Caron Butler — "We showed him literally every coverage in the playoff series and he dissected it." — Caron Butler on the Wizards' defensive strategy against LeBron James** [07:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IptG32Uv4QU&t=472s)

Caron Butler: "This is when I knew LeBron was special. This all happened in one play. We played him three years in a row in the playoff series but this is when I knew he was special. We tried to go under on pick and rolls, that didn't work. We tried to go over, tried to bully over, we tried to shoot the gaps, we tried to stay connected, we tried to meet him in transition, tried everything. We tried to weak him, send the double team, late blacks. We showed him literally every coverage in the playoff series and he dissected it. And I was just like, he different. That's when I knew. As long as he stayed available and durable, he's going to probably shatter just about every record."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IptG32Uv4QU&t=472s

**9. Caron Butler — "Whether people like it or not, you want to be a household name." — Caron Butler on the competitive checklist for NBA players** [10:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IptG32Uv4QU&t=616s)

Caron Butler: "It was dope because I think as players, obviously we have a checkbox of our own like when we come into the league. One, whether people like it or not, you want to be a household name. You want to be respected by your peers, you want to get the bag, you want to be an All-Star, you want to get a chip, and you want to get the hell out. That's the checklist that all of us have as players, as competitive players. And I was able to check one of those things by making an All-Star team."
