**3. Lionel Hollins — "Mike Conley 175 pounds and Mike Conley took a charge and got crushed." — Lionel Hollins on Marc Gasol's refusal to take a charge** [03:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HIdfgquaGW4&t=205s)

Lionel Hollins: "Somebody came to the basket and **Marc Gasol** wasn't going to take the charge. It would have been a block, but **Marc Gasol** was about to take the charge and he jumps out of the way. I am pissed like crazy. After the game we come in the locker room and I said, 'Marc, I am disappointed, man.' I said, 'You had the chance to take a charge and you jumped out of the way.' **Marc Gasol** stopped and stood up. He said, 'What you trying to say, coach?' I stood there for a moment because I didn't have a second point. Finally I said, 'All I am trying to say is **Mike Conley** 175 pounds and **Mike Conley** took a charge and got crushed. You the biggest man out there.' And he went and sat down."

**4. Lionel Hollins — "These guys aren't perfect and I don't expect them to be perfect." — Lionel Hollins on why he didn't coddle his players** [04:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HIdfgquaGW4&t=284s)

I said, 'There is a lot of stuff that you could write that would be good in terms of true, but it would just turn people off.' These guys aren't perfect and I don't expect them to be perfect. I can't tell them what to do or how to do. I can give my advice and tell them what I think, but they are all grown men. I try to help them become more mature."
