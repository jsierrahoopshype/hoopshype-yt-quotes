**1. David Dennis Jr. — "I am not going to have a situation where I am putting Joel Embiid as favorites." — David Dennis Jr. on why LeBron James wouldn't make the 76ers favorites** [00:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5dTDUQ88HVc&t=48s)

David Dennis Jr.: "I am not going to have a situation where I am putting **Joel Embiid** who may have injury issues again as favorites. We don't know on a given year what **Joel Embiid** we are going to get. So I am not going to put them as favorites for that reason. I am going to put them in the mix in the East. I see them as similar to where I had New York last year. They are in the mix in the East, there may be one or two teams that I would put above them in the Eastern Conference, which means you have a fighter's chance to come out of the East and a fighter's chance to win a championship."

**2. Kendrick Perkins — "I need you to think logical. The keys have been handed over to Tyrese Maxey." — Kendrick Perkins and David Dennis Jr. debate Joel Embiid's status as a top player** [02:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5dTDUQ88HVc&t=122s)

**Kendrick Perkins:** "Who is their best player?"

**David Dennis Jr.:** "**Joel Embiid**."

**Kendrick Perkins:** "Is he? No he is not. You are tripping. I know you are ready for 4th of July, I know you are ready to go have a barbecue and pop fireworks, but damn it we are at work. I need you to think logical. The keys have been handed over to Tyrese Maxey since the beginning of last season. He is the franchise, he is the face of that franchise with the addition of VJ Edgecombe and now Jaylen Brown. You don't need **Joel Embiid** to play at an All-NBA or MVP level no more."

**3. Kendrick Perkins — "If you could go out and you go get LeBron James, you damn right they should be top two." — Kendrick Perkins on LeBron James's impact on the 76ers** [02:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5dTDUQ88HVc&t=158s)

Kendrick Perkins: "If you could go out and you go get **LeBron James**, you damn right they should be top two in the league for as a title favorites. Because **LeBron James** just brings so much. And when you think about **LeBron James**'s versatility, one, he is still a guy that is capable of guarding one through five. He doesn't have to worry about a guy like **Nikola Jokic** or **Victor Wembanyama** until he actually gets to the NBA Finals. It is not a center in the Eastern Conference that **LeBron James** couldn't guard in the seven game series, and that is including **Karl-Anthony Towns**. And I say that respectfully. So again, adding a guy like **LeBron James** who could come in and on that squad, all you would need him to do is give you a solid 18, 5 and 7, which we know he could do that in his sleep."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5dTDUQ88HVc&t=158s

**4. Joe Fortenbaugh — "He might not be elite, but he can still play. He is also coming at a discount." — Joe Fortenbaugh on why the Lakers are not better off without LeBron James** [06:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5dTDUQ88HVc&t=387s)

Joe Fortenbaugh: "Number one, he can still play. He might not be elite, but he can still play. He is also coming at a discount to wherever he is going to go, so the value he adds to a team on a cost per basis is going to be extraordinary. We just watched **Jaylen Brown** get moved out of Boston because in part of how much he costs. LeBron is going to be so cheap he is going to come off as one of the most valuable players in the NBA this year. Number two, the interest level in the Lakers plummets with him gone. It is still the Lakers, we are still interested, but we are not nearly as interested. And if we are not nearly as interested, tickets, jerseys, all that stuff goes out the window."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5dTDUQ88HVc&t=387s

**5. David Dennis Jr. — "They want to move on to building around him and they really could not do that with LeBron James." — David Dennis Jr. on the Lakers moving into the Luka Doncic era** [07:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5dTDUQ88HVc&t=450s)

David Dennis Jr.: "For the long haul, they needed to move on from **LeBron James**. They don't want to do the this is the final hurrah for LeBron. They want to get into the **Luka Doncic** era. They want to move on to building around him and they really could not do that with **LeBron James** because as you said **LeBron James** is taking a discount, he was not taking a discount to come back to LA. For LA, he was going to tax them for what he had been doing there and what he felt like he owed from that team, so he was going to take up a massive amount of that cap room. He is only taking that discount to go somewhere else to make him happy. So they were not going to be able to make these moves in LA."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5dTDUQ88HVc&t=450s
