**2. Myles Turner — "It used to be that way with the men's Olympics, but now they just pick." — The shift from tryouts to selection in international basketball** [03:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SNmUaCY9Zqs&t=180s)

**Myles Turner:** "We had a tryout for the team. There was no we pick you, we pick you, we pick you. They invited 30 of us and we had a tryout and actually make the team. It used to be that way with the men's Olympics, but now they just pick, which has its advantages and disadvantages."

**Breanna Stewart:** "I don't think it takes much time. The respect thing is so high, you respect everybody that you're going to share the court with. The moment that you do get there, you're excited about it."

**3. Breanna Stewart — "You cannot open step. You cannot rip and go. If you rip and go, it's a travel." — The fundamental differences between FIBA and NBA basketball rules** [05:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SNmUaCY9Zqs&t=312s)

**Breanna Stewart:** "The biggest thing is you cannot open step. You cannot rip and go. If you rip and go, it's a travel. You have to really change your footing, you have to drop the ball early. That's my biggest adjustment."

**Myles Turner:** "It's like old school NBA. You weren't going to run through the lane without getting an elbow to the throat or an elbow to the chin. It's a physical game and a lot of people refuse to go inside because there's no defensive three seconds."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SNmUaCY9Zqs&t=312s

Myles Turner: "There's a viral clip, it's hilarious to me when this happened, but **Kobe Bryant** and **Pau Gasol** were teammates with the Lakers, and the USA was playing Spain in the Olympics. **11. Myles Turner — "Everyone in the gym knows the ball is going to Stephen Curry, so use that to your advantage." — Using star players as decoys in late-game situations** [16:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SNmUaCY9Zqs&t=1016s)

**12. Myles Turner — "I never tune into the TV part of things, the ESPN, the SportsCenter, the first take." — Myles Turner on managing social media negativity** [17:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SNmUaCY9Zqs&t=1060s)

Myles Turner: "The one thing I make sure I never do is I never tune into the TV part of things, the ESPN, the SportsCenter, the first take. They're quite literally programmed to spread negativity. The fact that they're actually trying to spread negativity or trying to spark a narrative is just toxic. I have to try to find my ways to go off of that."
