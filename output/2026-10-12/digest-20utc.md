## Olivia Miles On Her Basketball Influences, Unique Playing Style & Joining Unrivaled — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KdNEEso8gZY

_Speakers identified: Tracy McGrady, Olivia Miles, Vince Carter_

**1. Olivia Miles — "I'm fascinated with Luka Doncic's pace and his manipulation of the game." — Olivia Miles on her basketball influences and playing style** [00:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KdNEEso8gZY&t=25s)

Olivia Miles: "On the men's side, definitely **Steve Nash**, **Jason Kidd**. I watched **Stephen Curry** a lot growing up. More recently, I'm into **Luka Doncic**, more so his pace and his manipulation of the game. He's not always the fastest guy out there, but no one can stop him, and I'm fascinated with that. Obviously **LeBron James**. I didn't grow up in the **Michael Jordan** era, which I'm really sad about, but my dad always made me jealous with all the stories he has just watching games on the TV. I grew up watching a lot of great point guards, **Jason Williams**, and just how they manipulated the game. Then on the women's side, **Sue Bird**, **Maya Moore**."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KdNEEso8gZY&t=25s

**2. Tracy McGrady — "I felt the pressure of just being a top pick, you're just supposed to perform." — Olivia Miles on the pressure of being a top draft pick** [01:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KdNEEso8gZY&t=71s)

**Tracy McGrady:** "You're the number two pick overall, highly talented roster with a history of Hall of Famers, **Maya Moore** being one of them. When you heard your name called, did you feel any pressure now of all, like now you're following the footsteps of some greats?"

**Olivia Miles:** "Yeah, for sure. I felt the pressure of just being a top pick, you're just supposed to perform. And then obviously when you go to a franchise like the Lynx, if you go in our practice facility, there's the four championships lined up right as you walk in and the four rings right as you walk out. So yeah, the standard is always set. You have Hall of Famers on staff, **Rebecca Brunson** and **Lindsay Whalen**, and obviously **Cheryl Reeve** at the helm. So it's like you're constantly surrounded by it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KdNEEso8gZY&t=71s

**3. Olivia Miles — "It may look like no one's guarding you, but it's just the pace that you play at." — Olivia Miles on her unique pace and playing style** [02:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KdNEEso8gZY&t=173s)

Olivia Miles: "There's a lot of narratives going around that no one guards me, but it's like, you guys understand, if you play at a certain pace, no one can stop it. It doesn't matter, it may look like no one's guarding you, but it's just the pace that you play at. That dictates everything, especially as a point guard. I watch a lot of film and I just try stuff. I literally just try things in practice, like I'm listening to **Jason Kidd** and **Steve Nash**, and I got the opportunity to play with those two guys."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KdNEEso8gZY&t=173s

**4. Olivia Miles — "There's a lot of male coaches who will put their ego in it and want you to do." — Olivia Miles on overcoming restrictive coaching in women's basketball** [03:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KdNEEso8gZY&t=220s)

Olivia Miles: "Luckily I've always been around coaches who have encouraged me to do stuff in practice and be creative and be flashy. For women, it's tough because there's a lot of male coaches who will put their ego in it and want you to do what they want you to do, put you in a box and make you play into their system. And luckily I've been around coaches who have encouraged me to do stuff in practice and be creative and be flashy, and if I was able to show them I can do it in practice, then they'd let me do it in the game."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KdNEEso8gZY&t=220s

**5. Olivia Miles — "They love you until they hate you, the feeling is so fleeting." — Olivia Miles on the challenges of being a professional athlete** [04:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KdNEEso8gZY&t=282s)

Olivia Miles: "They love you until they hate you, the feeling is so fleeting. And it's so, you can't, the praise is poison, honestly. You know who's really for you and who's really fans of you and genuine, and it's guys like you in the NBA who actually watch and have played basketball that understand and really appreciate the game. But there's a lot of people who just like players and then hate on you because you're not their favorite player. And then the second thing is to flush losses quickly. In college, we only lost five to six times. In the pros, we only lost eight times, which is really, really good, but obviously there's more losses and more adversity that you have to fight through quickly."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KdNEEso8gZY&t=282s

**6. Tracy McGrady — Tracy McGrady on the importance of watching bad game film** [05:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KdNEEso8gZY&t=354s)

**Tracy McGrady:** "I had a young fellow, a dad come up to me and say, 'Hey, what's one of the things you can tell your kid about trying to make it?' And I'm like, 'They always want to hear these like, oh, keep working on your game.' I'm like, 'That's cool, but we have a lot of guys and one of the things we tell them is about watching film.' But I say about watching film, you don't always watch your good games. Sometimes you get caught up in the, 'Oh, look what I did to him.' But are you learning something? I told him, 'You need to watch the bad games, the games that you go home and you pout because you didn't get the shots or you only made one shot and you took seven of them.' That's the game you need to watch. Have you done that?"

**Olivia Miles:** "Yeah, absolutely. I definitely have those. It's hard, it's uncomfortable. You want to sit there and go like this and cringe and not watch it. But obviously we watch it as a team and then we have some position coaches and we'll go through it. But in terms of me, yeah, I'll sit there and we just lost to Golden State, like I'll sit there and watch the game and be like, 'Oh, I should have made that read or I should have made that move quicker or I should have gotten her open or rotated better.' Those are the hard ones, it's like you want to cringe, but you have to, it's the only way you're going to get better."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KdNEEso8gZY&t=354s
