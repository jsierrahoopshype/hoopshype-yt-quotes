**1. Zach Randolph — "I got to selling dope my freshman year." — Zach Randolph on his difficult upbringing and selling drugs as a freshman** [00:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6qOeb3q_Y54&t=27s)

Zach Randolph: "My freshman year, I told you I was messed up, my family was messed up, so I got to selling dope my freshman year. Towards the end of that year, I had stopped. I remember I had stood up in the crack house and I told the story I stood up in the dope house, and I was a freshman, I was tall. And I'm all there with all my homies, everybody in there grinding in the house, and I stood up. And the house don't have a high ceiling, the ceiling is not high, on the corner of Boots in the hood. And I stood up and I felt like a giant. I felt big, and everybody looked like midgets. I'm like, damn, if the police see me, if the gene team see me, I'm going to be the first one they see. I said, bro, this ain't for me. That shit wasn't for me, so after that, I sold my last little dub. That shit was over with for me. I got to focus on the hoop."
