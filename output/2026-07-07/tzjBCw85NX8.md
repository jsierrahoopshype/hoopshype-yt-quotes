# T.I. Reflects On ATL Legacy, Skating Bootcamp & Denzel Washington's Lessons During American Gangser — *7PM in Brooklyn*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tzjBCw85NX8

_Speakers identified: Carmelo Anthony, T.I., Monica McNutt, Kazeem Famuyide_

**1. T.I. — "we wouldn't know it would withstand the test of time and mean so much to so many generations" — T.I. on the impact and legacy of the ATL soundtrack** [00:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tzjBCw85NX8&t=42s)

**Carmelo Anthony:** "It is said that King was supposed to be the soundtrack to ATL."

**T.I.:** "They were asking me to do a soundtrack. I was working on an album, if y'all want to use that, cool. As I was filming ATL, I was coming back to the studio after the filming with the movie in mind, recording. So, there were things that I definitely tried to implement to tie it together. But I was not trying to make it an ATL soundtrack for real. I just wanted it to represent the city, and I felt like if it represented the city, then it would also represent the movie."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tzjBCw85NX8&t=42s

**2. T.I. — "the most challenging part of all that shit was the skate, the skate shit" — T.I. on the challenges of balancing acting and music** [02:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tzjBCw85NX8&t=136s)

**Monica McNutt:** "What was it like doing that at the same time in terms of the muscles or the sides of your brain? Because that's two different skills."

**T.I.:** "First of all, I feel like the music part is second nature. That is not really anything that I have to think about. I go to that, if you put Melo on the court, he is just going to go to what he knows. Now the film part, I feel like the most challenging part of all that shit was the skate, the skate shit. Going to the skate camp and learning the moves and all that old shit, and then having to do the move while we are acting."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tzjBCw85NX8&t=136s

**3. T.I. — "it made me take it a lot more seriously, because ATL, for one, I was number one" — T.I. on how American Gangster increased his focus on acting** [05:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tzjBCw85NX8&t=315s)

T.I.: "It made me take it a lot more seriously, because ATL, for one, I was number one on the call sheet. It was my first movie, but I had a lot of responsibility there. But still, it was in Atlanta, I was working on an album, so at the time it is kind of, I am still in my element a lot. So I still had a lot of T.I. off. When they yell cut, I am signing all the autographs, I am signing the trailer and stuff like that. With American Gangster, I wasn't the biggest star on the set, and it made me take the acting a lot more seriously. Plus I am in New York, so you got **RZA** on set, who is a huge dealer up here, and so it made me take the acting a lot more seriously."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tzjBCw85NX8&t=315s

**4. T.I. — "you can't sit around and watch them work and not learn anything" — T.I. on learning from the cast of American Gangster** [06:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tzjBCw85NX8&t=405s)

T.I.: "I enjoyed myself and learned so much from everybody, from the director **Ridley Scott**, who is a phenomenal director, one of the greatest ever to do it. You got **Denzel Washington**, **Russell Crowe**, and **Chiwetel Ejiofor**, **Idris Elba**, you got so many phenomenal actors that you can't sit around and watch them work and not learn anything."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tzjBCw85NX8&t=405s
