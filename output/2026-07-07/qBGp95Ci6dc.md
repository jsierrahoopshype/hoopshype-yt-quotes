# Carmelo Anthony & Nigel Sylvester Debate the Greatest Air Jordan Collabs Ever — *7PM in Brooklyn*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qBGp95Ci6dc

_Speakers identified: Carmelo Anthony, Nigel Sylvester, Kazeem Famuyide_

**1. Carmelo Anthony — "I wanted to be the .5 athlete because MJ had the ones, the twos, the threes, the fours, the fives." — Carmelo Anthony on his vision for the Jordan Brand .5 sneaker** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qBGp95Ci6dc&t=0s)

Carmelo Anthony: "The .5 is a story within itself. In my eyes, there was only one. **Michael Jordan** was one. I wanted to be the .5 athlete because MJ had the ones, the twos, the threes, the fours, the fives. So now I was taking what MJ already had and built with his sneaker line and with Tinker and them built, and I am coming with this post-Jordan era, this new culture, this new Jordan brand looking ahead. I am not about to fuck the shit up. We are going to match these and we are going to do our own version because this is the new version of what is already there."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qBGp95Ci6dc&t=0s

**2. Kazeem Famuyide — "I am not a low-top guy, so I could never pull off those Travis Scott Jordan 1s." — Nigel Sylvester and Carmelo Anthony debate Jordan 4s vs Jordan 1s** [00:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qBGp95Ci6dc&t=52s)

**Kazeem Famuyide:** "**Nigel Sylvester** Jordan 4 brick by bricks versus the **Travis Scott** Jordan 1s. Hell of a matchup."

**Nigel Sylvester:** "I am not a low-top guy, so I could never pull off those **Travis Scott** Jordan 1s."

**Carmelo Anthony:** "See, I am a low-top guy. I am a rojo guy. I am a rojo guy. So we are team rojo."

**3. Carmelo Anthony — "Dior came and presented the whole line. They did the wallets, they did the lows." — Carmelo Anthony recalls the Dior Jordan 1 collaboration launch in Mexico** [07:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qBGp95Ci6dc&t=427s)

**Carmelo Anthony:** "They came to the Board of Governors. We were in Mexico somewhere, and Dior came and presented the whole line. They did the wallets, they did the lows. They did the lows with those and they did a bag, I think. The way that they presented that, I am going with those."

**Kazeem Famuyide:** "We got to break this tie. They both got crazy stories for both of these."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qBGp95Ci6dc&t=427s

**4. Carmelo Anthony — "He shifted the culture when it comes to athlete collaborations." — Carmelo Anthony on the cultural impact of his Jordan 4 collaboration** [14:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qBGp95Ci6dc&t=840s)

Carmelo Anthony: "For him to take an already staple of a signature, which is the four, the Jordan 4, and to bring his culture, which was biking and being Mexican and doing wheelies and some queens, and he brought that shit and really shifted the culture when it comes to athlete collaborations. It wasn't too many other collaborations, non-athlete collaborations. Like a Travis Scott is a non-athlete collaboration. A Drake is a non-athlete collaboration. He is an athlete with that collaboration, so he kicked the door in. Which is why them shits are selling like this. People are starting to understand your cultural relevance and see your vision."
