# RJ Breaks Down Why The Coaches Association Is Calling This Deal Disrespectful — *Road Trippin*

_Speakers identified: Allie Clifton, Richard Jefferson, Kendrick Perkins_

**1. Kendrick Perkins — "Paul Allen had endless money and was letting them do things how they wanted to do it." — Kendrick Perkins on Chauncey Billups and the Portland Trail Blazers' ownership** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XDTraPWtJbE&t=0s)

**Kendrick Perkins:** "Can I address this thing, I know you have your thing, the Blazers' ownership. Can I ask you guys a question? Do you know who just won the NHL Cup? His team. He is a different type of owner. When you buy a house, you usually remodel the house so you want it. The Blazers, I live in Portland, I adore the Blazers, I love what they are, but **Paul Allen** had endless money and was letting them do things how they wanted to do it. This man bought the team and is like, this is bad business because I bought it, this is not how I do business. And let me clear away the weeds, let me change the paint on the wall, but at the end of the day, my main goal is to win."

**Allie Clifton:** "They just went to the postseason for the first time."

**Kendrick Perkins:** "I know that, but we are talking about infrastructure, not necessarily the team. He is saying, I want to start over my way because I paid for it. If I paid for a burger with bun, burger, bun, that is disgusting. But if I give you a dollar fifty, make me the burger the way I want to make a burger."

**Allie Clifton:** "The Portland Trail Blazers hired **Mike Dunleavy**, who is an outstanding coach, man, human, brilliant mind, as their new head coach. **Kendrick Perkins:** "Somebody signed it, so it must not be that disrespectful. Somebody signed it. If nobody, and we know this coach, he has 30 years experience, he signed this contract. So if it is that disrespectful, hey, no thank you. Everyone should be on the same page, no thank you. But somebody took it. Somebody took it pretty quickly. So there is obviously, there must be, this is his first coaching job? This is what your thoughts, Allie?"

**3. Richard Jefferson — "There is one of 30 jobs in the entire world. There is 30 jobs." — Richard Jefferson on the scarcity of NBA head coaching jobs** [03:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XDTraPWtJbE&t=213s)

**Richard Jefferson:** "To your point, I agree with what you are saying, somebody signed it. We have friends that are coaches, **JB Bickerstaff**, Walt, and **JJ Redick**. Do you know what all of them say? There is one of 30 jobs in the entire world. There is 30 jobs in the entire world. There is 30. And certain guys like Mike, Mike worked me out getting ready for the draft up in Toronto. He was leading our workouts and our draft workout. Mike has been an elite dude, great dude for years. And a lot of these guys like **Jamal Mosley**, I think **Jamal Mosley** might have interviewed for head coaching jobs, he might have did like seven or eight different interviews, and then he gets the job. Why would anybody ever take the Sacramento job? It is one of 30. It is one of 30."

**Allie Clifton:** "That has to be why. Because there is no way he doesn't feel undervalued."

**Richard Jefferson:** "Yes, but you can be undervalued and still work and prove yourself in one of 30. And betting on yourself in the one of 30, because it is hard to get that. And you see coaches like, you see Tiago Splitter went to Chicago, **Jamal Mosley** went to New Orleans, once you are in the in that loop, but you got to get in that loop. And so for him to take a bet on himself because it is one of 30, there is only 30 of those jobs. It is statistically harder to be a coach in the NBA than it is to be a player."
