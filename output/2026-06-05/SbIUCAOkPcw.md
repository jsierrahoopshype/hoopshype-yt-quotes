# How has the PERCEPTION of Karl-Anthony Towns changed? 👀 Wilbon breaks it down | First Take — *NBA on ESPN*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SbIUCAOkPcw

_Speakers identified: Karl-Anthony Towns, Iman Shumpert, Michael Wilbon, Brian Windhorst_

**1. Karl-Anthony Towns — "Whatever the team needs me to do to impact winning and to help us get over the hump" — Karl-Anthony Towns on his evolving role with the New York Knicks** [00:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SbIUCAOkPcw&t=4s)

Karl-Anthony Towns: "I just want to impact winning. So, whatever the team needs me to do to impact winning and to help us get over the hump and get that win on that night, I'm willing to do. Last night was a night where a lot of things happened. Jalen got hurt, changed my role. Jalen came back, I had to change my role again. Throughout the game you're changing your role three, four times, but whatever my role is, I got to be the best at it and impact winning."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SbIUCAOkPcw&t=4s

**2. Iman Shumpert — "You're not going to have to shoot it 30 times, but you're going to be involved 30 times." — Iman Shumpert on the shift in perception of Karl-Anthony Towns** [01:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SbIUCAOkPcw&t=62s)

Iman Shumpert: "I think at one point we felt like we have to keep KAT happy. Everybody kind of felt like the consensus around the league was, if he starts pouting, you got him. And I think it was beautiful for him and his coach to get together and say, hey man, you're not going to have to shoot it 30 times, but you're going to be involved 30 times. And I think that provides a comfort for a player to say, hey, no matter what, I'm going to touch this basketball."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SbIUCAOkPcw&t=62s

**3. Michael Wilbon — "It just seems to me that he's in a place where being involved is important to him." — Michael Wilbon on Karl-Anthony Towns's need to be involved** [02:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SbIUCAOkPcw&t=137s)

Michael Wilbon: "People don't really think, and I'm glad you spoke to this, about the emotional and psychological components of certain players. Everybody's not built the same, wired the same. And it just seems to me that he's in a place where being involved is important to him. And the more he's involved, the better off he is probably at both ends of the floor, because that reward offensively leads to, it seems to me, a more involved KAT."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SbIUCAOkPcw&t=137s

**4. Brian Windhorst — "Now, not only does he have an incredible role in the offense, but his confidence" — Brian Windhorst on Karl-Anthony Towns's growth and confidence** [04:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SbIUCAOkPcw&t=245s)

Brian Windhorst: "I was with the Knicks in Abu Dhabi in October for a week, and **Karl-Anthony Towns** was not in a good place because he was watching how **Mike Brown** was putting this offense together and there was not a place for him. Now, not only does he have an incredible role in the offense, but his confidence, I'm not sure a year or two ago he could have the confidence to play the type of game against **Victor Wembanyama**."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SbIUCAOkPcw&t=245s

**5. Iman Shumpert — "People knew that they had question marks on their offense and they got fixed right at the right time." — Iman Shumpert on the Knicks' late-season tactical adjustments** [05:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SbIUCAOkPcw&t=319s)

Iman Shumpert: "It's actually good that they found it so late, because if you're in Abu Dhabi, you're showing that and people are prepping for that for the playoffs, I think this sort of came left field. People weren't too worried about them. I think people were more worried about Boston, even though they were going to be Boston anyway before **Jason Tatum** went down. But I look at that and I say, hey man, if they had a chance to prep for that, I think that looks different starting the playoffs. People knew that they had question marks on their offense and they got fixed right at the right time."
