# Jalen & Josh On Their New Mindset After Last Year's Game 1 Heartbreak — *Roommates Show*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oa7QQykUzn0

_Speakers identified: Matt Hillman, Jalen Brunson, Josh Hart_

**1. Jalen Brunson — "You have to have a certain type of mental edge to move on from that." — Jalen Brunson on the mental challenge of overcoming playoff heartbreak** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oa7QQykUzn0&t=0s)

**Matt Hillman:** "Looking back, now that you guys are on the opposite side of that, do you feel that? Do you feel like blowing a historic lead in the first game sets the tone for the series? Do you think that's when they became discouraged?"

**Jalen Brunson:** "I don't think that game was discouraging. I don't think it was the end-all-be-all. There's still a lot of basketball to be played. Having a 22-point lead and losing with eight minutes left to go, and finding a way to win that game is insane. You have to have a certain type of mental edge to move on from that. Someone has to be able to step up and say we can bounce back from this. As a team last year, I failed to do that."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oa7QQykUzn0&t=0s

**2. Josh Hart — "I think that second game was the biggest game. If you have a letdown in that second game, that's where that doubt really starts to show." — Josh Hart on the importance of winning Game 2 after a tough loss** [02:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oa7QQykUzn0&t=144s)

**Josh Hart:** "At the end of the day, especially on the road, you go get one and then you get home-court advantage. Whenever you have a team that is starting on the road, that's your thought process. Let's get one of these two and we get into home-court advantage. For them, that was a gut punch. I think that second game was the biggest game. If you have a letdown in that second game, that's where that doubt really starts to show. You get a big lead, you probably should have won, and it's tough to overcome."

**Jalen Brunson:** "Especially tough since they won analytically."

**Josh Hart:** "Yeah, I'm curious to see Game 4. I guess we lost analytically. I know we won analytically, so analytically speaking we weren't there for Game 5. I think technically they're up 3-2."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oa7QQykUzn0&t=144s

**3. Josh Hart — "There are a lot of things that analytics don't show or don't take into account." — Josh Hart on why he is skeptical of advanced basketball analytics** [04:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oa7QQykUzn0&t=258s)

Josh Hart: "There are a lot of things that analytics don't show or don't take into account. I always thought analytics were for geeky, unathletic white guys that wanted to be a part of the league but just couldn't make it playing basketball, so they decided to put math into the equation."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oa7QQykUzn0&t=258s

**4. Josh Hart — "You can't place effort and intensity of certain things into a mathematical equation." — Josh Hart explains why analytics cannot predict player fatigue** [06:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oa7QQykUzn0&t=364s)

Josh Hart: "You can't place effort and intensity of certain things into a mathematical equation. People sit there and say, 'Oh, you can play this person at these four minutes because they get tired.' It's like, not every person is the same, not every game is the same. That person might get tired in those four minutes one game and then another game he's good for six minutes. You can't really analytically put into a mathematical equation and come out with the best-case scenario."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oa7QQykUzn0&t=364s
