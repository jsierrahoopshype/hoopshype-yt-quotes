# Nikola Jokic Still Trying to Find His Footing After Knee Injury | Chris Mannix & Evan Turner — *Sports Illustrated*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uUV1pJhUtfw

_Speakers identified: Chris Mannix, Evan Turner_

**1. Chris Mannix — "the numbers for Jokic have ticked down this season" — Nikola Jokic's recent performance dip and its impact on the Nuggets** [01:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uUV1pJhUtfw&t=77s)

**Chris Mannix:** "This has short and long-term ramifications for the Denver Nuggets. When you watch Jokic, do you see a diminished player? Do you think that we may have seen the end of the best years of **Nikola Jokic**, who is still just 31 years old?"

**Evan Turner:** "I don't want to say that we've seen the end of Jokic. There's only been eight games. But I am wondering, has that knee gotten better? Because last year he was balling out of control. It was a knee hyperextension."

**2. Chris Mannix — "they are 24th in the league in defensive rating. That is very bad" — Denver's defensive struggles and the impact of roster changes** [08:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uUV1pJhUtfw&t=481s)

**Chris Mannix:** "Let's talk about the Nuggets. They have the sixth-best offensive rating in the NBA, that's good. But they are 24th in the league in defensive rating. That is very bad. They have been peeling off layers of their success. It began with **Caldwell-Pope** and **Bruce Brown**, it continued over the years, **Peyton Watson** is the most recent loss, **Tim Hardaway Jr.**, which has cost their bench."

**Evan Turner:** "Even the year they won that championship, they were rated 18th or 19th best defense. That's not a recipe for winning. Defensively they're a liability. You can't win championships when you're playing four on five on one end."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uUV1pJhUtfw&t=481s

**3. Chris Mannix — "when you struggle, generally the coach is the first one that gets scapegoated" — Concerns regarding Michael Malone's job security in Denver** [11:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uUV1pJhUtfw&t=675s)

**Chris Mannix:** "Already there is chatter about **David Adelman** in the league. You got **Dave Joerger** sitting on that bench in Denver. Adelman has earned every opportunity to help dig this team out of it, but when you struggle, generally the coach is the first one that gets scapegoated in these situations."

**Evan Turner:** "It wasn't an easy job to take over. They just won a championship 18 months prior. **Michael Malone** got canned at the end of the season. Anything you do looks crazy."
