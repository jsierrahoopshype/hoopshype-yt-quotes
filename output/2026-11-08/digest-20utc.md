## Gilbert Arenas reveals he ALMOST teammed up with Dwyane Wade 🤯 — *Out The Mud*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ohqtg52oQWc

_Speakers identified: Gilbert Arenas, Zach Randolph, Tony Allen_

**1. Gilbert Arenas — "couldn't afford me" — Gilbert Arenas on his short stint with the Golden State Warriors** [00:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ohqtg52oQWc&t=14s)

**Tony Allen:** "Why do you think the stint was so short in Golden State?"

**Gilbert Arenas:** "Couldn't afford me. I was most improved, averaging 18. Nobody ever did that. The most that was paid by a second rounder that played two years was like 42, which was mid-level. No one overpaid the mid-level. What I did in that time frame, most improved, averaging 18, they were like, 'Wait, hold on, we can't afford him.' Basically, it was either take a bad deal, sign a one-year deal for 4.9, and then I had like a seven-year, 79 million waiting, or hit free agency and see my value."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ohqtg52oQWc&t=14s

**2. Gilbert Arenas — "when they need a bucket at the end, they're going through me" — Gilbert Arenas on his role and value with the Washington Wizards** [01:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ohqtg52oQWc&t=73s)

Gilbert Arenas: "Technically, I am a second rounder, but this is my team. I know we have a max player on this, but as long as I get the outlet, I can pass in the ball later. **Antoine Jamison** is the first option, but when they need a bucket at the end, they're going through me. It was me and **Earl Boykins**. We were shooting all the shots at the end of the game. When I went to free agency, it was more like I want to stay here because I know what the offense is. Going somewhere else, I don't know what it is. I don't know if they're going to let me play the same style."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ohqtg52oQWc&t=73s

**3. Gilbert Arenas — "we want to try him at the one" — Gilbert Arenas on the Miami Heat's interest in his free agency** [02:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ohqtg52oQWc&t=137s)

Gilbert Arenas: "Miami was free. Miami was like, 'We want you, but we don't want you for the ticket you're asking for.' But we just drafted a kid named **Darius Miles**. We want to try him at the one. But having y'all at the one, two, we kind of like that. We might want somebody probably bigger or something. So **Lamar Odom** was a free agent too. So Lamar was the number one free agent. I think I was two. So he was waiting for me to sign so he can get more than me. I'm trying to have him sign so I can get under him."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ohqtg52oQWc&t=137s

**4. Gilbert Arenas — "Jason Richardson fought for me to start and play" — Gilbert Arenas on his decision to leave the Golden State Warriors** [03:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ohqtg52oQWc&t=210s)

Gilbert Arenas: "I wanted to stay because I trained with these guys. We came out of college. **Jason Richardson** fought for me to start and play, so those are my brothers. But I had to have the **James Harden** joint. Get on the phone and sign that big one. I didn't even realize that I'm rich. I was broke so long. I was pouring in the NBA. College, I was the man. When I got to the NBA, I was penny-pinching. I already spent my money, so I needed the two years. From there, I got to spend 500 a month. That was my free money."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ohqtg52oQWc&t=210s
