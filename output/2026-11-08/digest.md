And he was a younger guy than me and the player said to me many, many years ago, **Kevin Love**, who it was. Yeah, **Kevin Love**. Minnesota. It wasn't like terrible, but for me, it was terrible for the simple fact that he did it because, and this is so random, but when he won Gatorade Player of the Year in high school, he received the award, he looked at me and he said, oh, I'm going to be up there with you one of these days. I said, cool, I'll be there. And then we fast forward, he dunked it and when he dunked, I was like, oh my god, in Minnesota, if I'm not mistaken, and I just remember running down like, oh my god."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JJgO6QUGXek&t=332s

So we playing the Knicks, I'm guarding **Allan Houston**. And I always, like for me, I can read if I'm quicker than my guy and I just got a good feel for how I need to guard this dude. I misjudged him because I didn't think he was, you know, uber athletic. I wasn't worried about him trying to go up and explode over and dunk. Man, he made a move, I'm guarding, he made a move and boom, boom, went up. I thought it was **Allan Houston**. Yeah, he had bounce."
