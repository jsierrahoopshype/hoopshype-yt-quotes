## How Muggsy Bogues Helped Shape Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady In Toronto — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oR2OuC3Azbg

_Speakers identified: Muggsy Bogues, Tracy McGrady, Vince Carter_

**1. Muggsy Bogues — "all I needed was finishers. Guys who love to run up and down the floor" — Muggsy Bogues explains his decision to join the Toronto Raptors** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oR2OuC3Azbg&t=0s)

Muggsy Bogues: "I had no idea that I was going to be coming to Toronto till **Butch Carter**, who was our coach back then, came and recruited me and **Dell Curry** to come. It was on the down end of my career at the time, but I knew of you guys. Seeing what you guys were capable of, you two were the main reason why I decided to come, because all I needed was finishers. Guys who love to run up and down the floor, who get up, and I could throw the ball wherever I wanted in the air and y'all were going to catch it."

**2. Tracy McGrady — "it's important to have those vets to teach you how to be a pro." — Tracy McGrady on the importance of veteran leadership for young players** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oR2OuC3Azbg&t=0s)

Tracy McGrady: "We tell the story all the time about how important the vets were for us at a young age. I came in at 18, and for me, you come in out of high school, you don't know anything about this game. There is so much you have to juggle, finances. I'm a kid that signed a $12 million shoe contract and then I get drafted, I'm a lottery pick. So there is so much that I'm juggling, and at that point, I think it's important to have those vets to teach you how to be a pro."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oR2OuC3Azbg&t=0s

**3. Vince Carter — "We had you guys to help us, mold us, and put us in the direction" — Vince Carter reflects on the guidance provided by veteran teammates** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oR2OuC3Azbg&t=0s)

Vince Carter: "We were fortunate. I don't think I know for a fact you didn't, and I know I didn't either, we didn't realize how lucky we had it as far as each and every day we had a vet that you played with. **LJ**, who was a superstar, you were a star yourself. We had you guys to help us, mold us, and put us in the direction of, 'Now young fella, that's not the way to go.' We had multiple guys to do that."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oR2OuC3Azbg&t=0s

All of us were on pretty much the down side of our careers, myself, Dell, Oak, Antonio, **Kevin Willis**, D-Brown, all of us. **6. Muggsy Bogues — "I've seen talent my entire career and what it is and what it can become." — Muggsy Bogues on the potential of Carter and McGrady** [08:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oR2OuC3Azbg&t=495s)
