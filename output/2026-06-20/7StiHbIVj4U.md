**5. Vince Carter — "It's a no-brainer to me. You keep that group together." — The Knicks must keep their core group together** [06:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7StiHbIVj4U&t=391s)

**6. Tracy McGrady — "If it wasn't for Mike Brown making that adjustment, we won't be having this discussion." — Mike Brown's adjustment made the championship possible** [07:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7StiHbIVj4U&t=449s)
