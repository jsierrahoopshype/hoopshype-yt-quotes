# Who's To BLAME For Cavs Embarrassing Loss - Harden or Kenny Atkinson? — *Run It Back*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lZIaHD4KSP4

_Speakers identified: Michelle Beadle, Lou Williams, Chandler Parsons, DeMarcus Cousins_

**1. Lou Williams — reaction to Kenny Atkinson's 'unlucky' comment about Cavs loss — "He's just as responsible as anybody else that didn't get stops. James Harden, anybody else."** [00:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lZIaHD4KSP4&t=18s)

**Michelle Beadle:** "**Kenny Atkinson** after the game: 'We got a little unlucky.' What is that quote about?"

**Chandler Parsons:** "That's crazy."

**Lou Williams:** "I hate having this conversation. **Kenny Atkinson** is one of my favorite people in the world. He is the person responsible for getting my career back on track. But last night I just hated the optics. I hated the decision making on this and he got to share the responsibility of anybody that was in charge of any decision making or going out there and competing last night. He's just as responsible as anybody else that didn't get stops. **James Harden**, anybody else."

**2. Lou Williams — Lou Williams defends Mike Brown's honesty about attacking James Harden — "You can't blame a coach for being honest. And what he said isn't some kind of secret sauce."** [04:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lZIaHD4KSP4&t=258s)

**Michelle Beadle:** "Have you ever heard a coach be so candid and say, and I quote, 'It's no secret we were attacking Harden.' Like I don't know that I've heard them name."

**Lou Williams:** "You can't blame him. When your team goes on a 44 to 11 run to finish a game, you can't blame a coach for being honest. And what he said isn't some kind of secret sauce. He's saying, we're seeking out the guys. I got one of the best 1-on-1 players in the game. I'm going to seek out the guy that I feel like gives me the best chance to get a bucket if this guy is guarding my best 1-on-1 ISO player. I don't have a problem with my coach saying that. And if anybody does have a problem with it, I don't know how you can make a case of being upset when you saw the results of it."
