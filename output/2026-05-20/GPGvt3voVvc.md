**2. Chandler Parsons — Chandler Parsons criticizes Kenny Atkinson's scared coaching decisions — "Kenny Atkinson allowing a 23-4 run without calling one timeout when he had two timeouts left."** [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GPGvt3voVvc&t=73s)

"**Kenny Atkinson** allowing a 23-4 run without calling one timeout when he had two timeouts left. **3. Chandler Parsons — Chandler Parsons questions Cavaliers' player performance and defensive substitutions — "Why did it take so long to get James Harden out of there when he wasn't really going nuts offensively?"** [1:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GPGvt3voVvc&t=102s)

"**Jalen Brunson** was sublime in this fourth quarter, he hit so many tough shots. But this is what he does. Why did it take so long to double team him to get the ball out of his hands? And **Max Strus**, who's been doing a great job defensively all playoffs long, is sitting on the bench. **Sam Merrill** was on the bench that started. **Mobley** was on the bench that started, he'd be one of the best defensive players, he'd win Defensive Player of the Year. So this was a combination of just I think bad coaching and just bad executing, bad substitutions, just a real combination of everything bad that happened for Cleveland and **Jalen Brunson** exposed them. **4. Lou Williams — Cleveland Cavaliers' coaching adjustments and Kenny Atkinson's failure to adapt — "Nothing was adjusted from the coaching to James Harden to having Dennis Schroder on the bench."** [2:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GPGvt3voVvc&t=178s)

"But every once in a while you got to call a spade a spade. You don't even know where to place blame in this. It was so many opportunities for the **Cleveland Cavaliers** to make adjustments to stop this from happening. Nothing was adjusted from the coaching to **James Harden** to having **Dennis Schroder** on the bench. I understand he's one for nine from the field, but that's not what you ask **Dennis Schroder** to do anyway. You ask him to junk up the game, guard your best players and give you an opportunity to get stops. In a collapse like that where you got a guy like **James Harden** in 19 straight switches that a team is seeking him out, you put somebody else on the floor that can kind of patch that up for you. So that's on **Kenny Atkinson**. Also, like **Chandler** just said, you have two timeouts that you can go to to go to those adjustments to put other people in the game to give you an opportunity. Didn't do that as well."

**5. Lou Williams — James Harden's defensive struggles and the Cavaliers' overall collapse — "James Harden didn't take the challenge as well. When a team is seeking you out, you got to sit down."** [2:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GPGvt3voVvc&t=178s)

"**James Harden** didn't take the challenge as well. We can talk about all of these other guys, but when a team is seeking you out, you got to sit down. **El Frank** said that to you, I also had a coach tell me, it's no help when somebody is straight line driving you. If somebody is going straight down the gut of your defense, we can't even help you with that. That's something that you got to be able to keep the ball in front of you. So time and time again last night from the fourth quarter to the overtime, it was a total collapse from the **Cleveland Cavaliers**. But it started with **James Harden** and it ended with **Kenny Atkinson**."

**6. DeMarcus Cousins — DeMarcus Cousins believes Cavaliers should have pulled James Harden — "I thought the best decision for the Cavaliers to try to close that game out was to pull James Harden."** [4:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GPGvt3voVvc&t=253s)

"I agree. To piggyback off Lou, it started with **James Harden** and it ended with **Kenny Atkinson**.
