**14. Draymond Green — Cleveland's offensive stagnation due to 'Hack-a-Mitchell' strategy — "The ball stopped moving when they started going 'hack-a-Mitchell Robinson.'"** [43:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YK5jMZVc-jc&t=2590s)

"The Cleveland Cavaliers, everybody said the ball stopped moving. Let me tell you when the ball stopped moving: it stopped when they started going 'hack-a-**Mitchell Robinson**.' They started hacking **Mitchell Robinson**. **Mitchell Robinson** was missing free throws. Cleveland wasn't gaining more separation, staying around 14, 15, 17, 14 points. In turn, they lost all their offensive flow because you're playing off a free throw every play against a set defense. That's when they lost all their offensive flow. It wasn't that the ball just stopped moving; they deployed the 'hack-a-Mitchell' strategy. When they did that, they lost all their offensive flow and were never able to get it back. That's when the game changed. Ultimately, you out-think yourself sometimes; it happens in this business. I think if **Kenny Atkinson** could take that one back, he would, because they were rolling and getting the stops they needed. I don't think we'll see that one again. Changing their offense changed the game."
