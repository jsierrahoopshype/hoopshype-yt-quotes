# Stephen A. says ANYONE who was involved with the Luka Doncic trade DESERVES to be fired | First Take — *NBA on ESPN*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IV_dIBILO7c

_Speakers identified: Wendy, Tim McMahon, Stephen A. Smith_

**1. Tim McMahon — Kidd's past interest in other roles and Ujiri's stance on Doncic trade — "You always get paid when somebody else wants to hire you, right?"** [00:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IV_dIBILO7c&t=32s)

"I wasn't surprised because a year ago **Jason Kidd** wanted to coach the **Knicks**. After the trade he wanted to coach the **Knicks** and that's how he got that contract. You always get paid when somebody else wants to hire you, right? And then during the season this year after **Nico Harrison** got fired, he had interest in becoming the team president. He wanted to replace Nico, which obviously didn't happen. And, how many times you got to be told you don't want to be the coach? The other thing is that **Masai Ujiri** has made very clear: if you had anything to do with the **Luka Doncic** trade, you're in trouble."

**2. Tim McMahon — Mavericks head coach job highly desirable due to Cooper Flagg — "Going forward this is a very highly sought after job because of Cooper Flagg."** [02:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IV_dIBILO7c&t=120s)

"Going forward this is a very highly sought after job because of **Cooper Flagg**. You look at **Victor Wembanyama** and the **Spurs**. The **Spurs** are one up one-zero in the conference finals now because of decisions the **Spurs** made two years ago. Who they decided to draft. The **Stephon Castle** pick was big, and the **Dylan Harper** pick. And then deciding after **Greg Popovich** had the stroke that they would go with **Mitch Johnson**. That was a big decision. Those were big decisions that the **Spurs** made that put them in position now. We are at that spot with **Cooper Flagg**. Who the Mavericks draft in this spot, they have a lottery pick. Who they hire for this job, very, very important for the future balance of power because I am as high on **Cooper Flagg** almost as I was on Victor. I am as high on **Cooper Flagg** as any prospect basically other than Victor in the last 20 years. I loved the way this young man played. I loved the way he competed at both ends. I think he is set up to be a transformational player there. So who gets this job going into his second year, very sought after, very important hire. **Masai Ujiri** has got to get it right."

**3. Stephen A. Smith — Jason Kidd's coaching record and association with the Luka Doncic trade — "anybody who had something to do with that should lose their jobs."** [04:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IV_dIBILO7c&t=248s)

"I like **Jason Kidd**. I think he's a damn good coach. I think he will be a head coach again in this league because he deserves it. Having said that, his record with **Luka Doncic** was 136 and 87. Trip to the Western Conference Finals, the trip to the NBA Finals. His record without **Luka Doncic** was 69 and 118. You are talking about a superstar basketball player who is a global iconic brand. And this team traded him for somebody injury prone who was six years older, who by the way is no longer even with the franchise because you didn't trade in **Anthony Davis** to the Washington Wizards. When you think about it, **Jason Kidd** has been on the record, particularly after **Mark Cuban**, the former majority owner for the Dallas Mavericks, accused **Jason Kidd** of being fully aware of the Doncic situation where **Jason Kidd** had to come out and clarify and say that he wasn't aware of anything until the 11th hour. Nevertheless, he's associated with the **Luka Doncic** trade, and if we're being honest, anybody who had something to do with that should lose their jobs."

**4. Stephen A. Smith — Nico Harrison's GM moves, good and bad, including Doncic trade — "He will forever be attached to this deal as one of the worst in NBA history."** [04:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IV_dIBILO7c&t=248s)

"**Nico Harrison** is a good dude and I think a smart basketball mind who outside of this situation with **Luka Doncic**, you can make an argument he was doing a hell of a job. They got to the Finals. He acquired **PJ Washington**, he acquired **Daniel Gafford**, obviously drafted **Dereck Lively** and all of these guys, acquired **Kyrie Irving**. There's a lot of good that he did. Obviously they should have kept **Jalen Brunson**, that was a mistake, but the bottom line is that he was doing a good job. He will forever be attached to this deal as one of the worst in NBA history."

**5. Stephen A. Smith — Trading Luka Doncic was a business mistake for the Mavericks — "You do not give up on somebody who essentially can pay for themselves."** [08:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IV_dIBILO7c&t=504s)

"**Luka Doncic** is gone. And yes, he did not play in these playoffs. But let me tell you something, before he got injured, he was averaging damn near 40 over a stretch. He was an MVP candidate. He was absolutely positively elite and the **Lakers** were benefiting from it. Not just on the basketball court, but at the box office. Not just at crypto.com arena where people walking through those turnstiles, but nationally and abroad because he's that kind of magnet. You do not give up on somebody who essentially can pay for themselves because of the popularity that they have and the money and the revenue that they pour in for you. You just don't do it. And that's what the Dallas Mavericks did under **Nico Harrison** and **Jason Kidd** and it will live with them in infamy. Much less **Jason Kidd**, much more for **Nico Harrison**."
