Rajon Rondo: "Unless you are playing a tough schedule every day, AAU has the weight of it, I think, right now. The colleges come out in that particular time, the dead periods where you go to these camps all summer, and that is where you are playing against the smoke. High school, you are going to go home, you are going to go to your team, you might shoot 30 times a game, and you can kind of develop bad habits for the most part in high school.
