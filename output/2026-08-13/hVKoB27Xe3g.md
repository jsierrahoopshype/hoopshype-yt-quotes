**4. Tim MacMahon — "I took that whole trip and it was when he was on the verge of breaking the triple-double record." — Russell Westbrook's pursuit of the triple-double record** [03:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hVKoB27Xe3g&t=213s)

Tim MacMahon: "I took that whole trip and it was when he was on the verge of breaking the triple-double record. I took it because our former colleague, **Royce Young**, had some unfortunate family circumstances so they needed me to fill in for him. This trip started in Memphis and Russ, off the top of my head, I want to say he ended up with 45 points, 13 assists, and 9 rebounds. He had one of many, many, many just unbelievable clutch closes where he had a clutch flurry of scoring, brought them back, incredible comeback win, but he was a rebound short of the triple-double. So he does his media and I didn't know Russ real well at the time, but I went up to him after his scrum broke up and I said, 'Hey Russ, I'm going to be on this trip, just want to let you know you'll be seeing me on the duration of this trip.' He said, 'Okay, cool.' I said, 'And **John Reed**, who is standing right next to me, he's one of the Thunder PR guys, **John Reed** said that we could sit down for an hour in Phoenix.'"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hVKoB27Xe3g&t=213s
