# John Smoltz Played in Magic Johnson’s League… And Says It Made Him BETTER at Baseball — *Brice Butler Studios*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zu_mVs6U9jc

_Speakers identified: John Smoltz, Brice Butler_

**1. John Smoltz — "I played in Magic Johnson’s league. That’s where Magic, Jay Vincent, Sam Vincent, they all came out of there." — John Smoltz on playing in Magic Johnson's basketball league** [07:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zu_mVs6U9jc&t=460s)

John Smoltz: "I played in **Magic Johnson**’s league. That’s where Magic, **Jay Vincent**, **Sam Vincent**, they all came out of there. We were a school that was not known for basketball, and we did not have any success. I transferred there in my junior year, and we almost got to the Elite Eight and we should have gotten to the semis. I rolled my ankle with a minute 30 to go and we lost. But that competition, playing against those guys, really helped me for baseball."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zu_mVs6U9jc&t=460s

**2. John Smoltz — "If you convey that you’re the weaker one or that the other person has an advantage, you’re in trouble." — John Smoltz on the mental advantage of not showing weakness** [08:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zu_mVs6U9jc&t=516s)

John Smoltz: "I’m a big believer that if you convey that you’re the weaker one or that the other person has an advantage, you’re in trouble. So when I stand on the mound and I know the guy at the plate is better than me, I’m never letting him know that. And I’ll fake it. If I’m playing against guys that are better than me in basketball, I am never going to let them know that they’re better than me."
