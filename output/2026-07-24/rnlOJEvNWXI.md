# LeBron to Philly...But This Could Get Complicated | Chris Mannix & Evan Turner — *Sports Illustrated*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rnlOJEvNWXI

_Speakers identified: Chris Mannix, Evan Turner_

**1. Chris Mannix — "I just couldn't see LeBron James going to Philadelphia to be a third, fourth or fifth option." — LeBron James joining the 76ers as a secondary option** [03:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rnlOJEvNWXI&t=195s)

Chris Mannix: "I just couldn't see **LeBron James** going to Philadelphia to be a third, fourth or fifth option on that team. We just saw LeBron chafe for a long time at being the third option in Los Angeles. Now he's going to Philadelphia where on paper you're talking about him being at least the third option behind **Tyrese Maxey** and **Jaylen Brown**. Maybe a fourth option when **Joel Embiid** is healthy and out there on the floor. Maybe if **V.J. Edgecombe** takes another leap in year two, he could be a fifth option out there. He's just being a de facto point guard on the floor. It didn't make a ton of sense to me."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rnlOJEvNWXI&t=195s

**2. Chris Mannix — "The front line though now is not a high-efficiency three-point front line." — Concerns about the 76ers' lack of high-efficiency three-point shooting** [05:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rnlOJEvNWXI&t=346s)

Chris Mannix: "The front line though now is not a high-efficiency three-point front line. **Joel Embiid** is 33% from three. He's much better suited to playing in the post even though his legs probably force him out on the perimeter a lot more than they used to. **Jalen Brunson** can shoot threes but he is more of an inside-the-three-point-line type of player as well. **LeBron James** last year shot around 32% from three. Is that a concern with this group? When we were talking about Miami and the fit for LeBron in Miami, we said that LeBron didn't solve the Heat's three-point shooting problem because Bam and Giannis are both non-three-point shooters. LeBron does not solve any shooting problems in Philadelphia, and **Paul George** for all of his weaknesses is a pretty good three-point shooter."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rnlOJEvNWXI&t=346s

**3. Evan Turner — "You have to rely on them what type of shots they get and open shots." — Evan Turner on the 76ers' need for open shots** [06:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rnlOJEvNWXI&t=418s)

Evan Turner: "You have to rely on them what type of shots they get and open shots. Sometimes when you sit there, LeBron and Joel, a lot of their shots were contested threes, no open catch and shoots all the time, even though you play with Luka. I believe what **Tyrese Maxey** is able to do and the pace that he has, if they're able to get stops, **Tyrese Maxey** is able to go downhill. I believe he's going to continue to keep enough pressure on a defense to be able to open up enough shots and hit the defense on their heels where they have to rotate, rotate, rotate."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rnlOJEvNWXI&t=418s

**4. Chris Mannix — "I still truly love this game and I have more to give." — LeBron James's statement on his decision to join the 76ers** [13:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rnlOJEvNWXI&t=800s)

Chris Mannix: "I thought I was done when the season ended. I wasn't ready to announce it and I knew I needed some time to really decide, but I was pretty sure I played my last game. I was honest at that last press conference when I said I needed to look at myself and decide if I still love this game. I still truly love this game and I have more to give. The last few weeks have been really special. This is my last decision. I'm not going for the money, I'm not going for family. What am I really playing for at this point? I still want to sacrifice, I still want to work, I still want to grind, I still want to compete to win, to have a chance at the feeling of winning another championship."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rnlOJEvNWXI&t=800s

**5. Chris Mannix — "He made it because he wants to pursue another championship." — LeBron James's primary motivation is winning another championship** [14:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rnlOJEvNWXI&t=863s)

Chris Mannix: "He made it because he wants to pursue another championship. He saw all the teams out there that wanted him, that Philadelphia offered him the best chance to win that championship. And I think he's right because I think if you go to Golden State, you're still in the West. You've still got to deal with **Victor Wembanyama** and Oklahoma City and all those guys. Cleveland, you're pretty good, but Harden, Mitchell, LeBron, definitely some flaws in that rotation. Miami has got great front-court players, but they cannot shoot the basketball and LeBron does not help them shoot the basketball."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rnlOJEvNWXI&t=863s

**6. Chris Mannix — "If he plays more than 30 minutes a game in any game in the regular season, Nick Nurse should be pushed out the door." — Managing Joel Embiid's minutes for the regular season** [17:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rnlOJEvNWXI&t=1061s)

Chris Mannix: "If he plays more than 30 minutes a game in any game in the regular season, **Nick Nurse** should be pushed out the door. He should be on the **LaMelo Ball** track there where he's playing less than 30 minutes per game. He should be on like the early **Jayson Tatum** track when he came back. Just ease your way in, don't play in back-to-backs, don't play in three games in four nights. Aim for 50 games. Try to get on the floor for 50 games, most of which will come in the second half of the season so you can rev up. And I think having LeBron there gives this team some flexibility to win while he's out of the lineup."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rnlOJEvNWXI&t=1061s
