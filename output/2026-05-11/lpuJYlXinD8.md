**1. LeBron James, on team dynamics, coaching, player dynamics**
[00:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lpuJYlXinD8&t=23s)

"Our team lost so much in Luka and AR. We had to rely on Marcus and Luke, and at times Bronny, to be able to create a blender. How can we create us getting into the paint, where we can get the defense shifted from one side to the other side? We noticed early on, even before the injuries happened, that Luke had that ability. Because his ability to shoot so well, a lot of teams run him off the line, but they don't also realize that he can also play in the blender where he can make plays for not only himself with his pull-up jumpers and his step-throughs, but he can also make plays where he's spraying out for other guys, and now they're playing against close outs and things of that nature. We kind of saw that a little sample size of it when AR and Luka were playing. And then when the injuries happened, we knew we were going to need more. We had no choice. We needed that more from him, and he stepped up."

_Why it matters:_ LeBron explains how the Lakers adapted their offensive strategy due to key injuries, leveraging Luke Kennard's versatile scoring and playmaking ability to create new opportunities and maintain team success.
