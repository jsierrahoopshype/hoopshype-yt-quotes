**3. Channing Frye — "There is no team that we have talked about that has bigger what-ifs than the Cavs." — Channing Frye and Richard Jefferson discuss the Cavaliers' disappointing playoff performance** [03:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wG7RXWoJcys&t=197s)

**Channing Frye:** "There is no team that we have talked about that has bigger what-ifs than the Cavs. The Cavs have the most pressure going into the thing. At a certain point, we have to just say, listen, if you are going to pay all that money for something, it is like going to an M. Night Shyamalan movie. You are like, oh, this is going to be great, finally. And then you are like, oh, here we go again, they got me. They got me. Thanks for believing."

**Richard Jefferson:** "I agree. I just think that every time we do that, it gets to a space of, at the end of every season, we should trade everybody. At the end of the season when everyone loses, we should trade everybody. There is truly only six number ones out there."

**4. Richard Jefferson — "There is only six or seven number ones out there. A lot of those guys are getting past their prime." — Richard Jefferson explains why it is difficult to find a true number one option** [05:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wG7RXWoJcys&t=310s)

Richard Jefferson: "There is truly only six number ones out there. There is **Shai Gilgeous-Alexander**, there is **Kevin Durant**, there is **Stephen Curry**, there is **LeBron James**, you can put **Jayson Tatum** or **Jaylen Brown** in that space, **Nikola Jokic**, **Giannis Antetokounmpo**, or **Kawhi Leonard**. Now, a lot of those guys are getting past their prime. They are getting past their prime, so it makes it more difficult. In the last six years, the number shrinks. So who do we think is a true number one that has proven it? To trade, you are basically a number two potential number one if you can prove that you can do it."
