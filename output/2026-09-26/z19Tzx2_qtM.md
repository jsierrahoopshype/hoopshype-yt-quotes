**6. Draymond Green — "The two-year extension was the maximum deal that the Raptors could do with Kawhi." — Kawhi Leonard's contract extension with the Raptors** [19:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z19Tzx2_qtM&t=1148s)

Draymond Green: "**Kawhi Leonard** does it again, gets a two-year, $115 million contract extension with the Raptors, a lot like Jimmy's deal that he signed two years ago with the Dubs. The two-year extension was the maximum deal that the Raptors could do with Kawhi due to the over-38 rule. So that rule has always been in play. However, when CP and Bron were president and vice president of the union, the rule used to be 34, like you could only get a max up to 34 years, and they got it raised to 38 during the last CBA that CP was on. So that's what the over-38 rule is. It's not very old because they raised it, but it is an old rule, it just used to be less. However, guys start, you know, rewriting how long people can stay in their prime. So it used to be 34 where you couldn't get the max years, now it's 38. That's why Kawhi got a two-year extension as opposed to a three or four-year extension."
