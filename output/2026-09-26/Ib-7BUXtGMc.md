**1. Jason Sudeikis — "Fort Scott was the one me and two dudes from my high school team in class we went" — Jason Sudeikis on his junior college basketball experience at Fort Scott** [00:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ib-7BUXtGMc&t=51s)

**Jason Sudeikis:** "I went to Fort Scott Community College, which was the Jayhawk Conference West. All of them came after me after senior year, like Barton County, Butler, Hutch. Fort Scott was the one me and two dudes from my high school team in class we went down there an hour and a half south, you know, one highway. And I was ineligible my second semester. I didn't go to a lick of class."

**Stephen Jackson:** "That's what junior college is for, people that didn't take high school serious. What made you think you were going to get it in a couple months?"

**2. Jason Sudeikis — "I remember watching we played against Independence Community College against Ruben Patterson, he had 40 points" — Jason Sudeikis recalls watching Ruben Patterson play in junior college** [02:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ib-7BUXtGMc&t=134s)

**4. Jason Sudeikis — "Jaron Rush played, yeah, Jaron was incredible. I knew him as an eighth grader." — Jason Sudeikis discusses the talent of Jaron Rush** [05:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ib-7BUXtGMc&t=318s)
