**5. Chris Mannix — "You sign the qualifying offer, that's money you're never going to get back." — The risks of Jalen Duren signing a qualifying offer** [09:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=st-4fJr3CZA&t=574s)

**Chris Mannix:** "The qualifying offer is different because in a lot of contracts, let's use **Jalen Brunson** as an example. **Jalen Brunson** took less money from the Knicks. Well, **Jalen Brunson** can make that up. **Jalen Brunson** can get all that money back when he signs the next contract. You sign the qualifying offer, that's money you're never going to get back. You're never going to be able to recoup the difference between the one-year $10 million qualifying offer that you signed and the $50 million average salary that you would sign if you're **Jalen Duren** there. So it's a lot of money, and it's a big bet on yourself."

**Evan Turner:** "I also think, man, if you give him $250 million and you're trapped with him having those playoff performances and being a regular season superhero, it's going to be held to tell the captain regardless."
