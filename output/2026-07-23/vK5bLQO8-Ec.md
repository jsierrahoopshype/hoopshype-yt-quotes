# Shams Charania speaks on the odds of LeBron James going to GOLDEN STATE 😳 | The Pat McAfee Show — *NBA on ESPN*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vK5bLQO8-Ec

_Speakers identified: Pat McAfee, Shams Charania_

**1. Shams Charania — "On July 23rd, we are literally just waiting on LeBron James." — LeBron James' free agency decision is the primary focus for NBA teams** [01:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vK5bLQO8-Ec&t=106s)

**Pat McAfee:** "How does this end up shaping up if you had to project it on July 23rd, Shams?"

**Shams Charania:** "On July 23rd, we are literally just waiting on **LeBron James**. That is the choice, his decision. The Warriors conversation was definitely alive right before free agency started. That is why Draymond Green opted out. He opted out of $27.7 million, that surprised the Warriors, that surprised everyone around the league, but that was done to give the Warriors the flexibility to try to get **LeBron James** and Anthony Davis."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vK5bLQO8-Ec&t=106s

**2. Shams Charania — "Those conversations for Anthony Davis have gone nowhere for the Warriors from my understanding." — Warriors' pursuit of LeBron James and Anthony Davis is over** [02:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vK5bLQO8-Ec&t=133s)

Shams Charania: "Those conversations for **Anthony Davis** have gone nowhere for the Warriors from my understanding. I do not sense any direction that they are picking back up in. I do not think that is going to be a conversation because it is at a point now where if the Warriors were to get in that ball game, they would have to give up multiple first-round picks, multiple draft picks, multiple swaps, all this other stuff. And the question is just, do you want to give that price up?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vK5bLQO8-Ec&t=133s

**3. Shams Charania — "LeBron has everything he needs to make a decision." — LeBron James has all the information needed to make his decision** [02:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vK5bLQO8-Ec&t=174s)

Shams Charania: "Now that we are 20 days in, there is nothing left. The teams are the teams, the information is the information. LeBron has everything he needs to make a decision. The conversations have happened. My sources tell me **LeBron James**, his agent Rich Paul, that inner circle, they are not waiting for another domino right now. They are not waiting for Anthony Davis to get traded anywhere, they are not waiting for anyone else to move around anywhere."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vK5bLQO8-Ec&t=174s

**4. Shams Charania — "LeBron James was very clear from the very beginning, this is going to be based on happiness." — LeBron James' potential contract will be based on team contention** [03:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vK5bLQO8-Ec&t=202s)

Shams Charania: "The teams, Cleveland, Miami, Golden State, Philadelphia, Minnesota, they are set. Those are the teams. Wherever he ends up, you are looking at minimum deals, you are looking at exceptions. The contracts are not going to be this grand part of this deal. **LeBron James** was very clear from the very beginning, this is going to be based on happiness in terms of chance to contend for a championship, a chance to compete at a high level, join a culture. That does not come from going to a team and then gutting that team from multiple different players."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vK5bLQO8-Ec&t=202s

**5. Shams Charania — "DeMar DeRozan, he is a six-time All-Star, he needs to find a spot." — Several NBA stars are still waiting to finalize their contract situations** [04:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vK5bLQO8-Ec&t=295s)

Shams Charania: "Once that happens, you are going to get a string of other things. **DeMar DeRozan**, he is a six-time All-Star, he needs to find a spot. **Draymond Green** needs to sign his deal, **James Harden** needs to sign his deal. **Jonathan Kuminga**, where is he going to end up? **Bradley Beal**, he is a multi-time All-Star. There is a guy abroad right now by the name of **Mario Hezonja**. He is coming from the EuroLeague back to the NBA. He is being held up by LeBron James."
