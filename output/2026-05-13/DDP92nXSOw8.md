**1. Stephen A. Smith, on team dynamics, player legacy**
[00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DDP92nXSOw8&t=0s)

"It's scary when you get a call from Kobe or Melo. Certain players scared me. CP3 scared me. Kobe scared me because they'll call you directly. You better know your stuff because they know. Melo was in Brazil and I said something about the Knicks. He thought I got it wrong, and I pride myself thinking I'm batting a pretty strong percentage with what I'm right about. But he had me dead to rights because I didn't call him. He said, 'You didn't call me. You just went on the air and said the stuff.' I said, 'Man, you were in the Olympics.' He said, 'That never stopped you before.' I was like, 'Damn, he got me.' Because he's right. I went on the air the next day. He got me."

_Why it matters:_ This anecdote reveals the direct and often intense relationship between prominent NBA players and media personalities, highlighting how players like Carmelo Anthony hold reporters accountable for their commentary.
