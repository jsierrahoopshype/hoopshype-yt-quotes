**1. Patrick Peterson — "It all predicates off where the ball is. If the ball is further away from you," — how defensive backs use ball position to predict route concepts** [04:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oOVbV27lNtc&t=255s)

**Patrick Peterson:** "It all predicates off where the ball is. If the ball is further away from you, nine times out of ten you are going to get in-breaking routes, everything back into the quarterback's vision. If you are away from the ball, if you are to the near hash and you are that DB, short side, you are only going to get a couple routes: speed outs, maybe a deep out, and a drag."

**Brice Butler:** "Right."

**2. Patrick Peterson — "If the ball is to me, in the receiver hash, a normal split, that's a plus two." — predicting routes based on receiver splits and ball position** [05:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oOVbV27lNtc&t=315s)

**Patrick Peterson:** "If the ball is to me, in the receiver hash, a normal split, that's a plus two. If the ball is to you, plus two is normal. If the ball is to me, plus two, nine times out of ten, depending on what the back is as well, you can get a slant, you can get a hitch, you can get, depending on the stem, you can get a dig. You are not going to do that on the base. I was going to say you can get a seven route too, but that's the only route you possibly not going to get. Because they are not on the numbers. And it depends on the stem. Because the receiver out of Houston, number 12, **Nico Collins**, he got me on that. Literally, I thought he got me on a short side. Ball was to me, gave me that dig stem, straightened it up, and got me on that."

**Brice Butler:** "He got you on that."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oOVbV27lNtc&t=315s

**3. Patrick Peterson — "Tarrar Arnold called me the other day. He said, 'Man, how did you jump that dig route?'" — how film study helps defensive backs jump specific routes** [07:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oOVbV27lNtc&t=427s)

**Patrick Peterson:** "**Tarrar Arnold** called me the other day. He said, 'Man, how did you jump that dig route when y'all was in cover three?' I said, 'Bro, I knew it was coming. So therefore I am not waiting on nobody else to make the play for me.' Everything lined up how I wanted it to be. It was third and ten, their main routes is daggers. So therefore, I am going back in my rolodex when I watch film, third and ten, daggers, seams, just process eliminated."

**Brice Butler:** "Yeah, yeah, yeah."

**4. Patrick Peterson — "Anytime we went up against Aaron Rodgers, Tom Brady, I played Peyton Manning once, Philip Rivers," — the difficulty of covering elite quarterbacks like Rodgers and Brady** [09:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oOVbV27lNtc&t=542s)

**Patrick Peterson:** "Anytime we went up against **Aaron Rodgers**, **Tom Brady**, I played **Peyton Manning** once, **Philip Rivers**, you just know you are not going to have opportunities. You are going in the game knowing that you are not going to have opportunities. But if that opportunity do come, you have to make the best of it. Those guys were so good to where they were throwing their receivers open, or even if they had a good feel of, 'Oh man, Patrick Peterson might got a beat on this, I am going to go somewhere else.' The good quarterbacks knew how to navigate games and the ball placement was just on a whole another level."
