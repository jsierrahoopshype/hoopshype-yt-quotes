**1. Jaylen Clark — "As a two-way player the most games you can play, I think it's 51 or 52 games." — Jaylen Clark explains the mechanics of a two-way NBA contract** [00:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ndLomDFhqxc&t=46s)

Jaylen Clark: "As a two-way player the most games you can play, I think it's 51 or 52 games. After that amount of games, you have to be converted to a standard contract. That's why a lot of times you see guys that are fully healthy that can't play, they just sit out. It's because their team doesn't want to exceed the maximum days that they can play on their two-way contract."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ndLomDFhqxc&t=46s

**2. Jaylen Clark — "If you're on a two-way past that date, your contract is guaranteed for the rest of the year." — Jaylen Clark explains the guarantee date for two-way contracts** [02:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ndLomDFhqxc&t=144s)

Jaylen Clark: "The cool things about two-ways is they have guaranteed dates. It's a date somewhere near January, that's when you normally see teams switching or dropping their two-way players. If you're on a two-way past that date, your contract is guaranteed for the rest of the year. Essentially with that means is since I got converted after my guaranteed date, they had to pay me out the rest of my two-way and then on top of that, they had to give me the NBA salary which I signed for, but it was just prorated by days."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ndLomDFhqxc&t=144s

**3. Jaylen Clark — "Instead of LeBron James just making 30 million dollars, all the players have come together." — Jaylen Clark explains the purpose of the NBA licensing check** [07:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ndLomDFhqxc&t=431s)

Jaylen Clark: "Instead of **LeBron James** just making 30 million dollars, all the players have come together and said we're going to include everybody. So everybody gets an equal share, no matter if you're the top dog on your team or you're the bottom dog, we all get the same amount of licensing checks. So that's also a big, big, big plus."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ndLomDFhqxc&t=431s

**4. Jaylen Clark — "To get partial licensing check, I'm pretty sure you have to play 21 or be active." — Jaylen Clark on the eligibility requirements for licensing checks** [07:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ndLomDFhqxc&t=453s)

Jaylen Clark: "To get partial licensing check, I'm pretty sure you have to play 21 or be active for 21 games. If you're not active for 21 games, you do not receive a licensing check. For example, my first year in the NBA I did not receive the licensing check, even though I was in 2K and all that type of stuff, because I didn't meet the minimum requirement of games, I didn't receive the licensing check."
