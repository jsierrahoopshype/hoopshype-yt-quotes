# Ajay & Jared Break Down Their Roads To The NBA — *The Old Man and The Three*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m24PwRnYUZ8

_Speakers identified: Cam Johnson, Jared McCain, Ajay Mitchell, Tommy Alter_

**1. Ajay Mitchell — "Santa Barbara was the first school to recruit me, and it just happened." — Ajay Mitchell on his recruitment process with UC Santa Barbara** [02:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m24PwRnYUZ8&t=146s)

**Ajay Mitchell:** "I always wanted to play college basketball, but it is different when you are in Europe throughout high school. I was playing for the national team under-16 and one of the assistants at Santa Barbara, **John Rillie**, started recruiting me there. Santa Barbara was the first school to recruit me, and it just happened. COVID hit when the recruitment process started, so I could not really visit any schools. It was all on Zoom, but I got to see what Santa Barbara was, and I was like, I am definitely going to Santa Barbara."

**Tommy Alter:** "Santa Barbara might be the nicest place in the world to go to college."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m24PwRnYUZ8&t=146s

**2. Jared McCain — "He was like, come be with your girlfriend. I was like, I will take a visit." — Jared McCain on his recruitment visit to UC Santa Barbara** [03:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m24PwRnYUZ8&t=194s)

**Jared McCain:** "The coach called me when I was in high school and my first ex-girlfriend was at Santa Barbara. That was his pitch: come be with your girlfriend. I was like, I will take a visit. I went down there, but it never followed through."

**Tommy Alter:** "You took an official?"

**Jared McCain:** "No, not an official. I just went through one of the players."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m24PwRnYUZ8&t=194s

**3. Jared McCain — "Once they offered me like my senior year, I went to Duke and I was like, it's over." — Jared McCain on his decision to commit to Duke** [03:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m24PwRnYUZ8&t=225s)

**Jared McCain:** "I took a visit to USC and UCLA. I love UCLA, and if they would have got on me earlier, it would have been one of the schools that I considered more. Once they offered me like my senior year, I went to Duke and I was like, it's over. I was trying to find something wrong with Duke and I just couldn't."

**Tommy Alter:** "It's a pretty good spot. I feel like it's getting better now with NIL too."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m24PwRnYUZ8&t=225s

**4. Jared McCain — "I always looked up to my family friend Devin Askew, and we were always tight." — Jared McCain on his development during his freshman year** [05:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m24PwRnYUZ8&t=351s)

Jared McCain: "I think it was probably COVID, my freshman year of high school, when I was like, I think I can do this. I always looked up to my family friend **Devin Askew**, and we were always tight. He was a number one point guard in high school, so I always just followed his path and whatever he did. Once I moved down to Southern California, I was like, I think I can do this. Let me try a lot harder, and I fell in love with that process of it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m24PwRnYUZ8&t=351s

**5. Jared McCain — "When I got to Duke, it wasn't like I'm going to be one and done." — Jared McCain on his mindset entering his time at Duke** [07:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m24PwRnYUZ8&t=461s)

**Jared McCain:** "When I got to Duke, it wasn't like I'm going to be one and done. I was like, I'm here for four years or here for three years. But I always knew it was a possibility. I always wanted it to happen, but I was ready for whatever happened in my life. Once I got to Duke and started playing, I was like, this could be my career."

**Tommy Alter:** "What about you?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m24PwRnYUZ8&t=461s

**6. Ajay Mitchell — "I played one year pro in Belgium, and that's when I was like, I can do that." — Ajay Mitchell on his professional experience before college** [08:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m24PwRnYUZ8&t=496s)

Ajay Mitchell: "Probably my first year before college, I played one year pro in Belgium, and that's when I was like, I can do that for a living. At that time, I was obviously like, I want to make it to the NBA, but it is super rare to do it, especially coming out of Belgium. But that was always a goal of mine. Once I got to college and got a lot more exposure, I was like, I can definitely reach that goal."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m24PwRnYUZ8&t=496s

**7. Ajay Mitchell — "Adam Silver comes up and he's like, with the 23rd pick, Milwaukee selects AJ." — Ajay Mitchell on his draft night experience** [09:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m24PwRnYUZ8&t=541s)

**Ajay Mitchell:** "I was in Santa Barbara. I didn't know what was going to happen at all. My agents were like, there's a chance you get picked late first. I remember Milwaukee had the 23rd pick or something. I went there, they really liked me. My sister lived in Wisconsin, so I could see that connection. My whole family was like, you might be in Milwaukee, and that would be great. Then the 23rd pick comes up and **Adam Silver** comes up and he's like, with the 23rd pick, Milwaukee selects AJ. Everyone in the room is like, oh my gosh, what's going on? And then they say Johnson, and it gets so awkward."

**Tommy Alter:** "That's so awkward."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m24PwRnYUZ8&t=541s

**8. Ajay Mitchell — "Sam did his thing and got me to OKC, so that was it." — Ajay Mitchell on his draft night trade to OKC** [10:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m24PwRnYUZ8&t=621s)

Ajay Mitchell: "The next day, we knew I was going to get picked, we didn't know where. I didn't even know Oklahoma was going to pick me at first. The second round starts and they are like, Oklahoma might trade up for you. My agents told me it was all going to be two ways, and I don't care, I just want to get drafted. Sam did his thing and got me to OKC, so that was it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m24PwRnYUZ8&t=621s

**9. Jared McCain — "I thought OKC was an option. I thought I was going to Miami though." — Jared McCain on his draft night expectations** [13:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m24PwRnYUZ8&t=802s)

**Jared McCain:** "I thought OKC was an option. I thought I was going to Miami though. Once Miami didn't pick me, I was like, I'm going to Toronto, maybe with Drake. That was my immediate reaction, because I didn't go to Philly for any workouts."

**Tommy Alter:** "Had you ever talked to them before?"

**Jared McCain:** "I did the combine interview, that was the only thing I ever did with them. So I was kind of shocked."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m24PwRnYUZ8&t=802s
