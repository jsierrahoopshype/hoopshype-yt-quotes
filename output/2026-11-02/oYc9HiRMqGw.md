**1. **6. Fabolous — "I think there's a lot of mixtapes that I even believe they provided the foundation of the success of me." — Fabolous on the mixtape era and song structure** [05:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oYc9HiRMqGw&t=340s)

**Fabolous:** "We was a little blurred on how, because in the mixtape era, me and Kiss would have went probably a different, but as it being on Def Jam and you got to make songs and songs they can structure to playlists and stuff like that, we had to just approach it a little bit different."

**Vince Carter:** "So if it's not, then there's no competition."

**Fabolous:** "I think there's a lot of mixtapes that I even believe they provided the foundation of the success of me. I would say mixtapes was the foundation of my so you knew I could rap. And then when me even doing radio songs, it was like, oh, he could do that too. But the mixtape was the foundation. Somebody like **50 Cent** was another one who had mixtapes and it provided the foundation of his. And I don't think they say it much, but I think like **Wiz Khalifa** kind of had that in a sense too. He had like a mixtape catalog that provided the foundation and then he kind of spearheaded it from there."
