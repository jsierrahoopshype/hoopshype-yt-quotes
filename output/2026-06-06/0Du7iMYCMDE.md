# ‘IT’S GOING TO BE INSANE’ 🔥 Jeremy Lin on MSG's NBA Finals Game 3 atmosphere | SportsCenter — *NBA on ESPN*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Du7iMYCMDE

_Speakers identified: Scott Van Pelt, Jeremy Lin, Tim Legler_

**1. Jeremy Lin — "the last 30 seconds, just so much inexperience. Whether you want to call it youth, or fatigue" — Jeremy Lin on the Spurs' inexperience in the final 30 seconds** [00:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Du7iMYCMDE&t=33s)

**Jeremy Lin:** "The Spurs have so much heart, 14-0 run to come back into the game, but the last 30 seconds, just so much inexperience. Whether you want to call it youth, or fatigue, it just wasn't it. The first play, another isolation for **Victor Wembanyama** with 30 seconds, tough shot over **Mitchell Robinson**. Then you have a turnover, then you have a foul. They had the game where you have the ball with 12 seconds left, it's a tie game. There's no way you should be able to ever lose that game."

**Scott Van Pelt:** "Would you have preferred a timeout at that moment when **Victor Wembanyama** gets the ball?"

**Jeremy Lin:** "I think a timeout would have been perfect, especially given that this is their first time in this situation. I personally would have called a timeout and I think that was the move to make."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Du7iMYCMDE&t=33s

**2. Jeremy Lin — "what he has done in all facets for this team is amazing. On the offensive side" — Jeremy Lin on Mike Brown's offensive system and player trust** [01:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Du7iMYCMDE&t=119s)

Jeremy Lin: "What **Mike Brown** has done in all facets for this team is amazing. On the offensive side, we can see habits, principles. Here we see three people drive the ball. **Jalen Brunson**, **Landry Shamet**, and **Mikal Bridges**, they all drive and re-space. It ends up with so much flow and **Mikal Bridges** ends up with an open three. There is just so much connectivity with what they are doing. The biggest thing about **Mike Brown** that I love, he is trusting the bench. **Landry Shamet**, veteran's minimum, almost out of the league, and he is a difference maker."

**4. Tim Legler — Tim Legler on the Spurs' missed opportunities and costly turnovers** [09:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Du7iMYCMDE&t=564s)

**Scott Van Pelt:** "How do you describe what you just witnessed for the last three hours of your life?"

**Tim Legler:** "To win a championship, you need an awful lot of heart and you have to be able to dig deep when the situation requires it. Both of these teams, it's a very physical game. Both teams completely laid it on the line. I was really impressed with the Spurs' ability to cut into that lead and get it to a situation where you had a winnable game. They just weren't able to make the plays down the stretch, a very costly turnover which we've talked about. And then **Victor Wembanyama**, you can't ask for a better look from a guy that size, 18 feet. He leaned back a little bit, I'm not sure why he lost his balance on the landing, but he had a clean look over **Mitchell Robinson** and the opportunity for your best player to win a game."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Du7iMYCMDE&t=564s

**5. Tim Legler — "he had bench guys on the floor and he trusted them to go make plays" — Tim Legler on Mike Brown's rotation and trust in bench players** [11:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Du7iMYCMDE&t=705s)

Tim Legler: "There was a critical stretch to the end of the third quarter. You had **Karl-Anthony Towns** out of the game with foul trouble, you took **Jalen Brunson** out because it was his normal time to get a blow. He looked really tired at that point. They don't have a lot of other offense generators on their roster. He had bench guys on the floor and he trusted them to go make plays. **Miles McBride** with big buckets, **Mikal Bridges** played big during that stretch. He keeps them in there at the start of the fourth and gets another three minutes or so of rest for his top guys. I can't say enough in a game like this, how much that means, the trust he put in them. It seemed like every button he pushed was the right one in terms of his rotation."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Du7iMYCMDE&t=705s
