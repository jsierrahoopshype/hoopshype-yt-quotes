## Is Paul George Making the Celtics Better Than Jaylen Brown Did? | The Story with Rachel Nichols — *Sports Illustrated*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tP3KM45Nzfw

_Speakers identified: Rachel Nichols, Chris Mannix_

**1. Chris Mannix — "I think the Knicks are still going to be disciplined about this." — The Knicks' cautious approach to Karl-Anthony Towns' contract extension** [01:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tP3KM45Nzfw&t=67s)

Chris Mannix: "I think the Knicks are still going to be disciplined about this. They have been very clear that they want to stay under that second apron to give themselves the flexibility to do some other deals. They have a lot of contracts coming up in the years to come, not the least of which is a big extension for **Jalen Brunson**. They are trying to manage this. Now, **Karl-Anthony Towns** has not made it easy. The Knicks are certainly happy that he is playing the way he is playing. It has kept them afloat while some other guys have struggled in that lineup, but Towns is not making it easy. If there was any chance that he might stumble a little bit, have some injury issues, maybe decide to take the last offer the Knicks made, which I believe is right around $50 million, that is not the case right now. He is playing like a guy that is going to be worthy of a full max contract if he opts out of his deal at the end of the season."

**2. Chris Mannix — "I am not here to rival Jayson Tatum. I am not here to spark a debate." — Paul George's seamless integration and impact on the Celtics** [04:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tP3KM45Nzfw&t=260s)

Chris Mannix: "It is the attitude in part of **Paul George** coming in saying, look, I am not here to rival **Jayson Tatum**. I am not here to spark a debate over who is the number one option on this team. He came out right away and said, I am here to make **Jayson Tatum**'s life easier. And he has done that better than I think they even expected in Boston coming into this. His three-point shooting has been outstanding, his defense has been really high level. As far as changing opinions on the trade, I am going to reserve judgment on that until midseason because the issue with **Paul George** wasn't is he a good player. Of course he is a good player. He was a great player in that first-round series against the Celtics last year, which is among the reasons they kind of blew this whole thing up because they didn't get out of that first round. The question is, how healthy can he be and how effective is he at the end of a season."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tP3KM45Nzfw&t=260s

**3. Chris Mannix — "They don't believe that reducing the number of games makes sense if players are never going to give money back." — Coaching staff perspective on the NBA's current load management trends** [07:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tP3KM45Nzfw&t=426s)

Chris Mannix: "This is a point of view that I have adopted from a lot of the coaches that I have talked to. Like this is their point of view. They don't believe that reducing the number of games makes sense if players are never going to give money back and owners aren't either. They don't believe that reducing the amount of minutes per game makes sense because they as a coaching staff are just going to play their stars more. It is not going to reduce the number of minutes **Luka Doncic** plays, it is going to reduce the number of minutes that **Zaire Williams** plays. It is just the reality of it. What they tell me consistently is that they would like to see a longer preseason, a longer ramp up, more preseason games."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tP3KM45Nzfw&t=426s

**4. Chris Mannix — "There is a lot of concern around the league. A lot of teams are frustrated." — League-wide frustration over the current NBA draft lottery order** [09:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tP3KM45Nzfw&t=545s)

They have been injury-ravaged and have largely been bad. Brooklyn, I think half of Brooklyn's roster is a first-round pick. They are not trying to be bad this year, they just are bad.
