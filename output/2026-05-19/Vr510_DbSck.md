In order for this to work, we need to have this. And he was available and the conversation came to me on hey, we got something that we might we never thought we had a chance to get him. And it was him. At that point it's like, what the fuck are we talking about? Bring him on. We need this. I know what I need for this team. We need this."

**2. Steve Novak — Steve Novak's journey before the Knicks, including being cut and almost going to China — "I had been cut by the Dallas Mavericks, the year before, I took a month off, not because I wanted to."** [01:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vr510_DbSck&t=60s)

"No. I was so you got to remember it was the lockout. And I almost went I almost took a deal in China. Cuz I had been cut by the **Dallas Mavericks**, the year before, I took a month off, not because I wanted to, because I was trying to find a team and couldn't. Had re-signed at the end of the year before the lockout with the **San Antonio Spurs**. The lockout happens. I go back to San Antonio for camp when the lockout ends, just decided not to go back to China. I was almost there."

**3. Steve Novak — Gregg Popovich orchestrates Steve Novak's release to New York Knicks — "Coach Pop, he said, we don't want to give you up. We want to keep you."** [01:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vr510_DbSck&t=89s)

"While I'm in camp with the San Antonio Spurs on a non-guaranteed camp contract, my agent calls me and says everything's happening fast, only two preseason games before the 66-game season starts. **Mark Bartelstein** calls me and says, when you get to practice today, **Coach Pop** is going to call you in the office. He's going to talk to you. I want you to be respectful. It's going to be good what's going to happen, but go in there and make sure you're grateful. You say thank you. I said, Mark, what kind of weird message is this? So I go into practice, **Coach Pop** says, come on, talk to you real quick. I go in, he says, we're going to release you. I said, oh, that's not good. He said, but no, hear me out. You are released, you are going to go get on a plane today and you're going to fly to New York. **Coach D'Antoni** had called and he said, look, we want **Steve**. **Coach Pop**, he said, we don't want to give you up. We want to keep you. So I talked to **Coach Pop** and **Coach D'Antoni**. I told him if you guarantee him a one-year deal since he's on a non-guaranteed contract here, and if you guarantee he's going to be a big part of what you're doing, I'll release him."

**4. Steve Novak — How Popovich, D'Antoni, and Bartelstein orchestrated Novak's Knicks move — "Coach Pop released you because he made Coach D'Antoni agree if he took you, he was going to be a one-year deal."** [03:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vr510_DbSck&t=180s)

"And then had to go through waivers. **Mark Bartelstein** had to call the teams, say leave him. Get to the **New York Knicks**. And I find all this out while I'm living in Milwaukee. **Coach Budenholzer** is the assistant for the **Spurs** at that time. He was the **Bucks** coach when I had dinner with him like five years ago. He said, do you know how this all really happened? I said no. He said **Coach Pop** released you because he made **Coach D'Antoni** agree if he took you, he was going to be a one-year deal and a big part of the team. And the rest was history."

**5. Steve Novak — Coach Popovich's personal message to Novak about his release — "This is going to be better for you and it's going to be better for your family."** [03:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vr510_DbSck&t=180s)

"I think back to **Coach Pop** and the legend that he is. He said this when he pulled me in the office: 'This is going to be better for you and it's going to be better for your family. Trust me, we want you. We pulled you out of the G-League for a reason. We have you back in camp for a reason. We have **Matt Bonner**, **Tim Duncan**, **Manu Ginóbili**, **Tony Parker**. We have a squad. We believe in you. This is better for you.' At that time, I didn't realize all that."

**6. Carmelo Anthony — The Knicks' strategic need for a shooter under Mike D'Antoni — "He wanted more threes. He wanted to play faster. He wanted somebody that's when I'm double team to get like he he wanted that."** [04:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vr510_DbSck&t=243s)

"**Mike D'Antoni**, because of the style of play we wanted to we was trying. Well his style of play was seven seconds or less. So we knew we needed to shoot the ball more. He wanted more threes. He wanted to play faster. He wanted somebody that's when I'm double team to get like he he wanted that and we knew we we needed a real shooter at this point in time. So when the deal happened, that ain't when the conversation started. So it was already as players, it was already like rumblings of like who would be available around the elite. And his name was one of the names that we kept coming up. Man, **Steve** might be available, **Steve** might be available. I'm like, yo, now you put the watch on. Listen, if **Steve** becomes available, you go grab that motherfucker."

**7. Steve Novak — Steve Novak's ideal role playing alongside Carmelo Anthony's gravity — "That's exactly what's a dream come true for me. I want someone else to have the ball."** [05:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vr510_DbSck&t=313s)

"I looked back, I've looked at some of my career statistics. Playing for me, playing with a guy like **Melo** was so important because my first year with the Knicks, this is a real stat: 93% of my made field goal attempts were assisted. My second year with the Knicks, 95% of my made field goals were assisted. People would say to me sometimes, 'What is it like playing with **Melo**? Oh, he likes to keep the ball in his hands and he likes to shoot and he likes to post, he likes to mid-range, he likes to shoot.' I said, 'That's exactly what's a dream come true for me.' I had a year with Oklahoma City Thunder where I had 100% of my made field goals were assisted. I said, 'I want someone else to have the ball. I need a superstar, someone who has the gravity.' It's the best shot on that possession if **Melo** has it in the post, and it's the best shot if he has it in mid-range, and the best shot from three. But when the defense says, 'We're going to put two guys, our best two defenders, at double team on **Melo**,' I was in the corner and I was ready. I just love the fact that **Melo** was like, 'I see you. A lot of these possessions are mine and they should be.' I was just thrilled to be in the corner spacing and moving how I had to, and he was a guy who always was a willing passer and always found me."
