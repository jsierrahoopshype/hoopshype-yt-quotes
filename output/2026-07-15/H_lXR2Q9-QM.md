# Is The Second Apron Bad For The NBA? Breaking Down It’s Impact Through Wemby’s Deal — *NBA on ESPN*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H_lXR2Q9-QM

_Speakers identified: Brian Windhorst, Tim Bontemps_

**1. Brian Windhorst — "We are not fans of the second apron. We did not propose the second apron." — David Kelly's critique of the NBA's second apron system** [01:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H_lXR2Q9-QM&t=87s)

Brian Windhorst: "We are not fans of the second apron. We did not propose the second apron. We should have done a better job of fighting back against the second apron. In the future, we will have a much more unified union, and we will do a better job of fighting back. We are seeing the apron system decimate teams and force decisions to be made that are not basketball decisions."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H_lXR2Q9-QM&t=87s

**2. Tim Bontemps — "The whole point of the supermax is to position teams to keep their stars." — Tim Bontemps argues for softening the second apron rules** [01:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H_lXR2Q9-QM&t=116s)

**Tim Bontemps:** "The whole point of the supermax is to position teams to keep their stars, especially your small market teams, teams like Oklahoma City. The supermax was hatched in part because of **Kevin Durant**'s departure from the Thunder, teams like San Antonio. I don't think the extra 5% should count towards the cap, I don't think it should count towards the luxury tax, and I don't think it should count towards aprons. For example, what I'm saying is I don't think **Victor Wembanyama** should have had to take a discount. He is without question a supermax player. He should sign the supermax and then in this case the Spurs would only have 25% of the cap count towards all these things."

**Brian Windhorst:** "Legislate in the fun-max."

**Tim Bontemps:** "Yes. And again, the same thing with Shai Gilgeous-Alexander in Oklahoma City, and we can go on down the list, but teams shouldn't be punished for drafting well and developing well."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H_lXR2Q9-QM&t=116s

**3. Brian Windhorst — "the players are guaranteed 51% of the basketball revenue." — Players are guaranteed 51% of basketball-related income** [04:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H_lXR2Q9-QM&t=272s)

Brian Windhorst: "The players are guaranteed 51% of the basketball revenue. So no matter what the aprons are, no matter what happens, they are guaranteed to get their money. For every $100 that the league takes in, the players get 51 cents. If in one year they end up getting 48 cents, every player gets a check to equal it out. And if some years they get 52%, then the players have to write a check to the owners to make it square."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H_lXR2Q9-QM&t=272s

**4. Brian Windhorst — "it forces the redistribution of talent, which forces parity, which is why it acts like a hard salary cap." — The second apron forces talent redistribution across the league** [05:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H_lXR2Q9-QM&t=356s)

Brian Windhorst: "It forces the redistribution of talent, which forces parity, which is why it acts like a hard salary cap. And that is what the owners want, and getting star players to take less than their market value is also something owners want because a star player is already worth way more than what his salary is."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H_lXR2Q9-QM&t=356s

**5. Tim Bontemps — "I don't know if this does set a precedent, although you could argue Brunson set a precedent." — Tim Bontemps questions if Wembanyama's deal sets a precedent** [06:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H_lXR2Q9-QM&t=391s)

Tim Bontemps: "I don't know if this does set a precedent, although you could argue **Jalen Brunson** set a precedent. And you could argue this is **Victor Wembanyama**'s response to seeing Brunson celebrate on his home floor in San Antonio after winning a championship. But I would just be cautious in thinking because Wembanyama did this, that when **Stephen Curry** is negotiating next summer, he's going to accept a similar deal. And when **Dylan Harper** is negotiating two summers from now, he's going to accept a similar deal."
