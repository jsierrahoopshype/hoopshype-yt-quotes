When you have a guy that just broke **Stephen Curry**'s single-season rookie record in con can nipple in three-pointers made, then obviously you are cooking with something. But a name that is not mentioned enough, who that organization is very high on, that they also took with a lottery pick a few years ago, is **Brandon Miller**. So now all of a sudden you are saying, hey, we have started to develop a culture there. **3. Richard Jefferson — "In the NBA, the most important stat is not how many tickets you sell, it's about wins." — Richard Jefferson on the importance of winning over box office appeal** [03:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hPctNbiVwuw&t=231s)

Richard Jefferson: "We know that LaMelo is box office. He has been box office since he was a freshman in high school. We know about his popularity. We know how many of the kids love him. And for them to never have made the postseason, they try to see if you are the one guy that they can build around, or they try to see if you are the second guy that they can build around the number one guy."
