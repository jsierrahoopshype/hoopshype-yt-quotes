**1. Shams Charania — "finding a team that he feels gives him a chance to compete at the highest of levels" — LeBron James' priorities for his next team in free agency** [01:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a8SSqt7oIYY&t=89s)

Shams Charania: "**LeBron James** right now is in Ohio spending family time, getting back to the golf course after making his decision to leave the Lakers on Tuesday. The priorities for him with his next team are clear, which is finding a team that he feels gives him a chance to compete at the highest of levels. He wants to play meaningful basketball and be a championship threat with whatever team he is on. That is what quantifies as happiness for him. This is not going to be a financially driven decision, which means though that if you are a team with a minimum, an exception, salary cap, whatever you have to offer **LeBron James**, if you can offer him a chance to contend and compete, you are going to be calling his agent **Rich Paul** and trying to get a pitch and a proposal in front of him."

**3. Brian Windhorst — "I have been thinking for nine months about LeBron playing for the Denver Nuggets." — Brian Windhorst's case for LeBron James joining the Nuggets** [05:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a8SSqt7oIYY&t=309s)

Brian Windhorst: "I have a confession Hannah. I have been thinking for nine months about LeBron playing for the Denver Nuggets. I refused to talk about it during the season, but I just thought as soon as they didn't extend LeBron last year and they extended Luka, I kind of knew it was probably going to come to an end. I thought about the Denver Nuggets because him alongside Jokic is an incredibly perfect fit. They are so intelligent, the way they see the game. And here is the thing, it was all predicated on him taking less money. I was never ever going to allow myself to think about him going to a team whether it was Cleveland or Golden State for less money until I heard specifically from the LeBron operation that he was willing to take less money. So now that he has done that, I will open the window and I think Denver absolutely should get in front of him or get in front of Rich Paul and make their pitch."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a8SSqt7oIYY&t=309s
