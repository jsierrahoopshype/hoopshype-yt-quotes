# FULL Western Conference Finals Preview - All-Time Series Brewing? — *NBA on ESPN*

_Speakers identified: Bobby Marks, Brian Windhorst_

**1. Bobby Marks — Current series an all-time great, better than Warriors-Thunder**
<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qcLD93lqYX0&t=4s" target="_blank" rel="noopener">00:04</a>

"Look, this series has got a chance to be an all-time great series. I spent the weekend talking to a bunch of people in the league for a story that's going to run Monday, previewing the series and sort of from what people in the league are going to be watching. And I've rarely had people be as excited to talk about something as they were to be breaking down this series. And from a pure basketball perspective, this series has everything you could ask for. I think from a conference finals standpoint, it's automatically the best series since when Tim and I covered a decade ago between the Warriors and Thunder, which looked like it was going to be the start of an unbelievable rivalry and obviously was immediately short-circuited by **Kevin** going to the other team. I don't think we have to worry about **Victor** or **Shai** doing that this time around."

**2. Brian Windhorst — Decade-long rivalry potential like Magic/Bird, Jordan/Celtics/Pistons**
<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qcLD93lqYX0&t=4s" target="_blank" rel="noopener">00:04</a>

"That's what somebody said to me for the story. They said the thing that I'm most excited about as a basketball person is that these teams are going to be going head-to-head and having to almost certainly beat one of the other to get through or one of them is going to have to win against the other to make it through for maybe the next decade. And they brought up **Magic** and **Bird**. They brought up **Jordan** against the Celtics and the Pistons."

**3. Bobby Marks — Victor Wembanyama has a chance to claim the league from Shai Gilgeous-Alexander.**
<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qcLD93lqYX0&t=133s" target="_blank" rel="noopener">02:13</a>

**Brian Windhorst:** "This quote from an assistant coach that you had in your story, **Bontemps**, was I think one of my favorite and really I think is a fair sort of assessment as the way the league kind of looks at this. **Shai** is bleeping good, but he ain't a cheat code. **Wemby** is a cheat code."

**Bobby Marks:** "Yeah, and this is this series is a clear-cut opportunity for **Wemby** to say, 'Hey, I told you, and I'm showing you. I am the most dominant player in the league. He's back-to-back MVPs with a Finals MVP in between. **Wemby**'s got a chance to take it."
