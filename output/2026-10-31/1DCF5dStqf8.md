So when I hear people say stuff, it throws me off because I'm like, dude, different."

It's like you talking to Magic Johnson. You would say, yo Magic, look, I'm about to do this, this is what I'm thinking, what do you think?"

**4. Jeezy — "I think they hurt. I think they don't got no outlets. I think they don't got no big bros." — Jeezy on the struggles and lack of guidance for young rappers** [03:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1DCF5dStqf8&t=191s)

Jeezy: "I think they hurt. I think they don't got no outlets. I think they don't got no big bros like we just talking about. And I feel for them. The music is the truth. **5. Jeezy — "I don't look at it as if they're wrong, I look at it as if they're hurt." — Jeezy on why he views young rappers' behavior as pain rather than malice** [04:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1DCF5dStqf8&t=249s)

Jeezy: "I don't look at it as if they're wrong, I look at it as if they're hurt. And this is what they're telling. They got to kill each other for them to feel significant. It ain't even about what I'm going to accomplish. It's about I'm significant, I can take your life. And I'm cool with that. And to me, that's who I feel like we failed them."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1DCF5dStqf8&t=249s

**6. Jeezy — "Our generation was we was trying to survive. Their generation is they're trying to learn how to deal." — Jeezy on the generational shift from survival to trauma-based pain** [06:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1DCF5dStqf8&t=402s)

Jeezy: "Our generation was we was trying to survive. Their generation is they're trying to learn how to deal with all this pain, all this trauma that they got. They can't process it. Their friends are dying, their cousins are dying. And it's just like for me, I can't save everybody, but I just hope some of the things that I'm doing showing them that it's okay to grow."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1DCF5dStqf8&t=402s
