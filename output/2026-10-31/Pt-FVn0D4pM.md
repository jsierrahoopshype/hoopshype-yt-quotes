Kendrick Perkins: "He is one of the best to ever do it. I was having this conversation with the legend **Mike Wilbon**. I was like, phone stop ringing. But you had the option as a player to know, now it's time. I'm walking away. Those players who know, Ernie knows. **2. Kendrick Perkins — "He is at a desk and he has these papers scattered out everywhere." — Kendrick Perkins on learning professional preparation from Ernie Johnson** [01:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pt-FVn0D4pM&t=94s)

Kendrick Perkins: "This is how I learned from Ernie. I am getting into the media space. I go to Atlanta and I am going on KG Certified, which was in the same studio during the same time. I go up, I see everybody, I dab up Charles, I dab up **Shaq**, and I go to Ernie. He is not even at a little office. He is at a desk and he has these papers scattered out everywhere. He stands up. He is like, hey, Perk, what is going on? I am ready to have a conversation, but I could tell he sits right back down and he is writing this stuff down, no help, no laptop. It is like writing it down, getting ready for his show. The focus that he had on that, it was a wake-up moment like this is some serious shit. This is not just going on TV and talking out your ass."

Mike looks, he is like, that is so cool. It is just to see a 65-year-old who is **Mike Breen**, and he is kind of like a kid that he gets a toss from Ernie for the first time ever.
