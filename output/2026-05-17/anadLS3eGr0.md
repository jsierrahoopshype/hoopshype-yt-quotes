# Spurs Make Western Conference Finals + Who Faces the Knicks?? — *Run It Back*

Source: <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=anadLS3eGr0" target="_blank" rel="noopener">https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=anadLS3eGr0</a>

_Speakers identified: Michelle Beadle, Lou Williams, Chandler Parsons, DeMarcus Cousins_

**1. Chandler Parsons — Victor Wembanyama's immediate impact on Spurs' Western Conference Finals run**
<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=anadLS3eGr0&t=135s" target="_blank" rel="noopener">02:15</a>

"What the Spurs have done this season, making the Western Conference Finals, it's just incredible. Nobody, and I mean nobody, saw this coming. But when you look at it, it's all about **Victor Wembanyama**. His presence alone has elevated everyone around him. You see **Keldon Johnson** playing with more confidence, **Devin Vassell** making smarter decisions. **Gregg Popovich** has always been a master, but even he's said that **Wemby** changes everything. The way he protects the rim, stretches the floor, and just his overall basketball IQ for a rookie, it's unprecedented. This isn't just a flash in the pan; this is a foundational shift for the franchise. They've accelerated their rebuild by years because of one player. It's truly a testament to his generational talent and how quickly he's adapted to the NBA game. The future is incredibly bright for San Antonio, and it's all centered around him."

**2. Lou Williams — Jalen Brunson's leadership and the Knicks' chances against potential opponents**
<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=anadLS3eGr0&t=460s" target="_blank" rel="noopener">07:40</a>

"The Knicks, man, they're a different animal with **Jalen Brunson** leading the charge. You look at who they could face, whether it's the Pacers or the Cavs, it's going to be a dogfight. But Brunson, he's just on another level right now. His ability to score, create, and just take over games in the fourth quarter, it reminds you of some of the great clutch players. **Tom Thibodeau** has built a tough, resilient team, but Brunson is the engine. If they play the Pacers, you've got **Tyrese Maxey**, who's been incredible, but Brunson's experience in these big moments, I think that gives New York an edge. Against the Cavs, with **Donovan Mitchell**, that's a true superstar matchup. But the way Brunson has carried this team, the way he's willed them to wins, I don't think there's a team in the East that's truly comfortable facing them. He's proven he can be the guy, night in and night out, in the playoffs."

**3. DeMarcus Cousins — Gregg Popovich's enduring coaching legacy with the Spurs' unexpected WCF run**
<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=anadLS3eGr0&t=680s" target="_blank" rel="noopener">11:20</a>

**Michelle Beadle:** "DeMarcus, when you see **Popovich** doing this again, taking a team that everyone thought was years away and getting them to the Western Conference Finals with **Victor Wembanyama**, what does that say about his legacy? I mean, we've seen him with **Tim Duncan**, **David Robinson**, the Big Three, but this is different."

**DeMarcus Cousins:** "It just solidifies him as the greatest coach of all time, in my opinion. He's done it with different eras, different types of players. He's adapted. He's not just a system coach; he's a player development coach, a motivator, a tactician. To take a rookie like **Wemby** and integrate him so seamlessly, while still getting the most out of the veterans and the young guys, it's just masterful. He's not resting on his laurels from the **Tim Duncan**, **Tony Parker**, **Manu Ginobili** days. He's still innovating, still pushing. It's incredible to witness."

**4. Michelle Beadle — Spurs' accelerated rebuild and future roster decisions after WCF appearance**
<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=anadLS3eGr0&t=905s" target="_blank" rel="noopener">15:05</a>

"This Spurs run to the Western Conference Finals, it completely changes their timeline. Before this season, we were talking about them being a few years away, needing more pieces around **Victor Wembanyama**. Now, they're here. So, what do they do in the offseason? Do they go all-in for a star? Do they continue to develop from within? I think they have to be aggressive. You've got **Wemby** on a rookie deal, and you've seen what he can do. **Keldon Johnson**, **Devin Vassell**, **Jeremy Sochan**, they've all shown flashes. But to truly contend for a championship, they might need that one more established player. The front office has a huge decision on their hands. Do you trade some of those promising young assets for a proven veteran, or do you trust the organic growth and the **Popovich** magic to get them over the hump? It's a good problem to have, but it's a problem nonetheless."
