# Why THIS has Jay Williams ‘thinking differently’ about Shai Gilgeous-Alexander 👀 | First Take — *NBA on ESPN*

_Speakers identified: Kimberley A. Martin, Jay Williams, Kendrick Perkins, Stephen A. Smith_

**1. Jay Williams — "the amount of times that I watch SGA look for a call has made me think differently about him." — Jay Williams questions Shai Gilgeous-Alexander's constant search for foul calls** [00:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4i24P-iIoyk&t=27s)

**2. Jay Williams — "I want to root for you. You are cold. Why do you need to play the game like that?" — Jay Williams criticizes Shai Gilgeous-Alexander's constant foul-seeking mannerisms** [01:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4i24P-iIoyk&t=90s)

"Literally, first possession of Game 2, he's throwing his hands up to the ref. We can watch clip by clip of the things that he does on the court where I'm looking at him and say, 'Man, I want to root for you. You are cold. Why do you need to play the game like that? **3. Stephen A. Smith — "He's back-to-back league MVP. And he's been the runner-up the year before that." — Stephen A. Smith highlights Shai Gilgeous-Alexander's consistent elite scoring and MVP contention** [04:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4i24P-iIoyk&t=287s)

"In the last six years of his career, he has averaged 23.7, 24.5, 31.4, 30.1, 32.7 and 31.1. He's back-to-back league MVP. And he's been the runner-up the year before that. So the last three years, he's been a top two candidate in the league for MVP honors. And you got cats rolling around here acting like it's because he can get to the free throw line."

**4. Stephen A. Smith — "He's a brilliant basketball savant. The brother knows what he's doing. And he knows how to manipulate the game." — Stephen A. Smith praises Shai Gilgeous-Alexander's brilliant basketball IQ and game manipulation** [05:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4i24P-iIoyk&t=320s)

"This brother, I'm looking at him. He's 6'7". He's got a handle. He's got a motor. He controls pace. He's a brilliant basketball savant. The brother knows what he's doing. And he knows how to manipulate the game in a fashion that somebody who is a savant would know how to do it to get his way. **LeBron** don't complain about calls, I know he gets hacked all the time, I understand that. And he's absolutely right. He does get hacked a lot, but in the same breath, there are times when he's looking for calls and he didn't deserve to get those calls. I'm just simply making the point that elite players in this game have done what you've accused **Shai Gilgeous-Alexander** of doing. He does it better. He does it a little bit more continuously, but in the end, it works for him. And by the way, it don't stop that mid-range. It don't stop that game that he brings to the table. Let's just make sure we're making sure everybody understands. You might not like that, Jay, and you may have a good point. Let's just not act like the brother is not an elite star in today's game. He is."

**6. Kendrick Perkins — "We're watching a guy master the mid-range, the mid-post. To me, he mimics a whole lot of everything." — Kendrick Perkins praises Shai Gilgeous-Alexander for mastering the mid-range game** [08:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4i24P-iIoyk&t=524s)

"We're not about to sit up here and take away from **SGA**'s greatness or act like we're not watching something special when it comes to a guy actually bringing back the mid-range when the league was trying to take it away. And this game was just going from twos and threes. And all of a sudden, we're watching a guy master the mid-range, the mid-post. To me, he mimics a whole lot of everything if you watch his game. You should have been coming on here praising him because he's doing stuff that we used to see **Jordan** and **Kobe** do in the mid-post, in the mid-post area. So it's not, no, it's not a hard watch. It's not a hard watch. Are the Thunder boring? Yes, they're boring because they have principles, because they play hard. They're nothing fancy. Shai is not flashy. He's not. He's just going to bust your ass and move on. Right? So it's not the most eye candy. So they put you to sleep, I can't lie. But to take away from his greatness, we're not going to do that."
