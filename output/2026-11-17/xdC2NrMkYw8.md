# The Miami Heat Are Learning to Trust Each Other | Chris Mannix & Rachel Nichols — *Sports Illustrated*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xdC2NrMkYw8

_Speakers identified: Chris Mannix, Rachel Nichols_

**1. Rachel Nichols — "both Bam and Giannis in that locker room got very serious and very real and talked about not trusting each other" — Bam Adebayo and Giannis Antetokounmpo's lack of trust in teammates** [00:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xdC2NrMkYw8&t=40s)

**Chris Mannix:** "Are you a believer now? Are you buying the Miami Heat, which I think sit right in that 4-5 range in the Eastern Conference?"

**Rachel Nichols:** "I was at the turnaround game at their low that they turned around from when they played the Lakers and it was a total disaster and both Bam and Giannis in that locker room got very serious and very real and talked about not trusting each other. That is a big deal for guys like that to say. It was in the context of defense, why aren't you doing a full closeout because you don't trust the guy behind you, which we have heard obviously in different iterations over the years from different teams, but that speaks to a larger lack of trust."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xdC2NrMkYw8&t=40s

**2. Chris Mannix — "they are playing a lot more pick-and-roll over the last nine games than they played to start the season" — Miami Heat's tactical shift to increased pick-and-roll and dribble hand-offs** [02:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xdC2NrMkYw8&t=144s)

Chris Mannix: "I give **Erik Spoelstra** a ton of credit for recognizing some things and tweaking some things. If you just dive into the numbers here, they are playing a lot more pick-and-roll over the last nine games than they played to start the season. They are doing a lot more dribble hand-offs. They have doubled the number of dribble hand-offs per game that they are doing this year now than they did last year and I think that has unlocked some things. **Clay Thompson** loves the dribble hand-off game and he has responded. His offensive game has bounced back to a pretty high level."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xdC2NrMkYw8&t=144s

**3. Chris Mannix — "they have been feasting as of late on some pretty bad competition" — Chris Mannix's skepticism regarding the Miami Heat's recent winning streak** [03:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xdC2NrMkYw8&t=213s)

Chris Mannix: "I want to see what they do with the Houston Rockets on Monday night. Houston Rockets are white hot right now. If they beat Houston, then I think they are on to something. They have been feasting as of late on some pretty bad competition. They beat Sacramento, everybody beats Sacramento. They beat Utah, everybody is beating Utah. They got Cleveland when Cleveland was playing without **Evan Mobley**. They got Detroit when Detroit was still without **Jalen Duren**. Still these are still good wins and they put up a lot of points too. The offense is clicking right now."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xdC2NrMkYw8&t=213s

**4. Rachel Nichols — "they don't have the complete team they are going to have around Giannis yet" — Miami Heat's need for additional assets to complete their roster** [04:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xdC2NrMkYw8&t=247s)

Rachel Nichols: "They don't have the complete team they are going to have around Giannis yet. They spent the money, they spent the assets going to get Giannis and yes, it would have been nice if LeBron came to them for basically free, but he didn't. So it is going to take one more asset cycle, if not two more asset cycles. Look at what it took in the Lakers to put the right pieces around Luka. It takes a little bit after you have such a major shakeup."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xdC2NrMkYw8&t=247s

**5. Chris Mannix — "teams that play fast though, I mean especially this team, I still don't like it" — Chris Mannix's concern over Miami Heat's high-paced playstyle** [05:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xdC2NrMkYw8&t=306s)

Chris Mannix: "They are still playing incredibly fast. Third fastest pace in the NBA. They are averaging fifth most points in the league, about 30 assists per game as well, that is good. Teams that play fast though, I mean especially this team, I still don't like it. I still think they need to slow the game down a lot more because I don't think defensively it is going to be a challenge for them against good teams if you are jacking up the number of possessions."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xdC2NrMkYw8&t=306s
