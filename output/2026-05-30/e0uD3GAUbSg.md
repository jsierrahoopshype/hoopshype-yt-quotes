# Perk Breaks Down What He Wants to See From Wemby in Game 7 — *Road Trippin*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e0uD3GAUbSg

_Speakers identified: Allie Clifton, Kendrick Perkins, Richard Jefferson_

**1. Kendrick Perkins — "I'm leaning more towards OKC, and the reason being is because what we've witnessed in Game 4, Game 5, Game 6 is role players play better at home." — Kendrick Perkins favors OKC to win Game 7 due to home-court advantage** [00:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e0uD3GAUbSg&t=14s)

Kendrick Perkins: "I'm leaning more towards OKC, and the reason being is because what we've witnessed in Game 4, Game 5, Game 6 is role players play better at home. We keep saying that, but it's been a real thing in this series. The role players stepped up at the crib. **Dylan Harper** had a bad—by the way, shout out to **Dylan Harper**, because I picked you on our group thing, baby. Fanatics Sportsbook, 8.5 points, you had that in the first half. But look, they play better at home. I believe OKC role players are going to respond at home. They have no choice. Guys are going to make shots, guys are going to defend."

**2. Kendrick Perkins — "The Spurs walked out of that bitch too confident." — Kendrick Perkins and Richard Jefferson debate the Spurs' confidence level** [01:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e0uD3GAUbSg&t=100s)

**Kendrick Perkins:** "I was looking at some faces, man. I don't—I kind of saw some defeat there. The shit-talking and aggressiveness that the Spurs were bringing, I wasn't feeling OKC response. I know **Stephon Castle** is smooth, I know Chet is the 7-1 smooth motherfucker, I get that. But that shit Vassel did, the block with the shove and the shit-talk, it's like Chet kind of—I'm looking for a little bit more somebody to ruffle the feathers a little bit. The Spurs walked out of that bitch too confident."

**Richard Jefferson:** "That's fine, that's great. That's great. Why can't they just be them? Why can't they just be them?"

**Kendrick Perkins:** "No, no, bro. No, no, because the Spurs walked out of that bitch too confident."

**3. Richard Jefferson — "If Wemby has a great game, if Wemby goes and gives me 30 plus and 12 plus, the blocks are going to be there just because he's the human elastic man." — Richard Jefferson on Victor Wembanyama's impact in a potential Game 7** [04:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e0uD3GAUbSg&t=250s)

Richard Jefferson: "If **Wemby** has a great game, if **Wemby** goes and gives me 30 plus and 12 plus, the blocks are going to be there just because he's the human elastic man. If you give me those numbers, it's over. I think the players around them will do enough, but if you have one of those games—if **Steph** goes **Steph**, there's nothing you can do, right? And so, if one of those dudes have that game, if **Wemby** gives me that game, I don't think that there's anything that can be done. If **Wemby** gives you one of those 35 and 12 nights, one of those nights, I think they win, and that's rare."
