**3. Josh Hart — Knicks' 'punch first' mentality creates doubt in opponents during playoffs**
[12:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pUf0LjuK9PI&t=745s)

"It's always tough in those close out games. Your goal always is to come out aggressive the beginning of the game, that first quarter, especially that first half. And then you want doubt to kind of seep into the opponent. Like we're up 3-0 in the series and we're up 12 points in this game right now, this close out game. And then you want that that doubt to start seeping in of like, okay, I don't know if we can come back from this one or this might be the end for the season. And more that that doubt continues to seep in, it aids in your goal to close it out. And I feel like that's something that we did very well the last two close out games was punched them at the beginning of the game and just keep punching."

**4. Josh Hart — Not giving opponents momentum or opportunity is crucial in playoffs**
[14:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pUf0LjuK9PI&t=890s)

"I'm curious to see tonight of that **Cleveland** and **Detroit** series because obviously **Cleveland** was up. They handled business in that second half. They got up like 15-20 and then the end of the game, at one point it got to like an eight-point game with a minute left in the game. Those guys in **Detroit** came out and they came off the bench and kept the energy. So it's like you never want to give teams or players or whoever it is in the postseason momentum or opportunity because that could be a difference maker. **Sasser** played well. Not saying that's going to happen tonight, but you don't want any of those opportunities."

**10. Josh Hart — Players want fans to enjoy, but block out noise during season.**
[34:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pUf0LjuK9PI&t=2048s)

"During this process, we always talk about it. We want the fans, the supporters, and all that to have as much fun as they can. We want them to enjoy these moments, these games, and series. But for us, we don't really want to think about it. Maybe in the summer, when we're removed from actual games and that kind of thing, you can take a step back and talk to a fan or whatever, and you see the impact they have, like that's cool. But while you're in it, you really don't think about it. You don't really even want to hear it because once you start doing that, you start opening the door to the outside noise."

**11. Josh Hart — Outside noise hurts focus; players appreciate fan support later.**
[34:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pUf0LjuK9PI&t=2048s)

"And now you're kind of locked in on the task at hand of the Eastern Conference Finals, but you're also subconsciously listening to these people telling you you're great or what these people say on a sports show or whatever it is. So now you're not fully 100% locked in. You might be 80 and 20, but sometimes that 20 could be what could win or lose you a game in terms of focus and those kind of things. So while we're in the midst of it, we don't really want to hear it, we don't really want to think about it because we have a goal and we haven't got to that goal yet. But down the road in the summer or something, we kick back and chill and drink some wine and talk, maybe we enjoy those moments then."
