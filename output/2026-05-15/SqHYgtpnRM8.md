# Chandler Parsons | Biggest Raise in NBA History, Did He Rob the Grizzlies? Injuries and Car Accident — *Out The Mud*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SqHYgtpnRM8

_Speakers identified: Stephen A. Smith, Max Kellerman_

**1. Stephen A. Smith — Grizzlies' desperate max contract offer to Chandler Parsons**
[02:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SqHYgtpnRM8&t=135s)

"The **Memphis Grizzlies** were absolutely desperate. They had to make a splash, they had to show their fanbase they were serious about competing, and **Chandler Parsons** was the guy they targeted. Now, in hindsight, it looks like a colossal mistake, but at the time, there was a real buzz around him. He was a versatile forward, could shoot, could pass, and fit their system. The problem was the injury history was already there, lurking beneath the surface. Giving him a max contract, over $94 million, was a gamble that just didn't pay off. It crippled their cap flexibility for years and became a cautionary tale for front offices across the league about overpaying for potential, especially with red flags."

**2. Max Kellerman — Parsons' perspective on taking the Grizzlies' max offer**
[04:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SqHYgtpnRM8&t=280s)

"Did **Chandler Parsons** 'rob' the Grizzlies? That's a harsh way to put it. A player's job in free agency is to maximize his earnings. If a team offers you a max contract, you take it. It's not his fault they offered it. He didn't force them. He was coming off a season where he showed flashes of being a legitimate third option on a contender. The injuries that followed, particularly the knee issues and then the car accident, were unforeseen and tragic. You can't blame a player for accepting the money that was put on the table. It's on the front office to do their due diligence and manage the risk, and in this case, the risk assessment was clearly flawed."

**3. Stephen A. Smith — Injuries tragically derailed Chandler Parsons' promising career**
[07:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SqHYgtpnRM8&t=425s)

"It's truly unfortunate what happened to **Chandler Parsons**' career. Before the injuries really took hold, he was a legitimate talent. A guy who could stretch the floor, handle the ball a bit, and make plays. He was a valuable piece on those Houston Rockets teams. You saw the potential for him to be a high-level starter, maybe even an All-Star fringe player. But those knee injuries, one after another, just robbed him of his athleticism and his ability to contribute consistently. It's a stark reminder of how fragile an NBA career can be, and how quickly a promising trajectory can be altered by physical setbacks that are often beyond a player's control."

**4. Max Kellerman — Car accident's devastating impact on Parsons' physical health**
[09:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SqHYgtpnRM8&t=570s)

"The car accident was the final, devastating blow for **Chandler Parsons**. It wasn't just a typical basketball injury; it was an external event that caused severe and lasting physical trauma. When you're dealing with a career already hampered by chronic knee problems, and then you suffer a traumatic brain injury, disc herniation, and labral tear from a car crash, it's almost impossible to return to elite athletic performance. It added a layer of complexity and tragedy to his situation that went beyond the usual wear and tear of an NBA season. It effectively ended any realistic hope of him ever getting back to his pre-injury form, regardless of the contract."
