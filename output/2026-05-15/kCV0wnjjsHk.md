**1. Draymond Green — Draymond Green's personal NBA Combine experience and its limited value**
[00:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kCV0wnjjsHk&t=40s)

"The NBA Combine is taking place right now in Chicago and man, do I remember those days. The NBA Combine is cool because you kind of get to know some guys that you know of, but you don't really know. One relationship that I remember first beginning at the NBA Combine, and has stretched throughout the tenure of our careers, is that's the first time I met **Dame Lillard**. And immediately right then we hit it off. I think it was just a respect for, like, I didn't come from a smaller school, he come from a smaller school, but almost being overlooked. And you kind of just understood, had an understanding for each other. I think that's one of the good things about the Combine. If I'm honest though, it's one of the shittiest days of your career, because you're under this huge microscope. You're doing all these meetings where you get asked these dumbass questions and they're just trying to catch you up. You're going to all these medical appointments where they only want to red flag you and drop your draft stock. It's kind of this thing that doesn't add a bunch of value, but it subtracts it. When I came through the NBA Combine, we didn't play five-on-five, which I think would have actually helped my draft stock tremendously. We only did workouts, and I am just not a big workout guy. Never have been. Put me in a game, let's go. Workouts, nah. We only did drill stuff, which I think was actually to my detriment."
