**3. Colin Kaepernick — "The waiver the NFL wanted me to sign waived all of my employment rights." — Kaepernick on the NFL's restrictive waiver for his 2019 workout** [06:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9vjxFk2u1Qw&t=370s)

**Colin Kaepernick:** "The waiver the NFL wanted me to sign waived all of my employment rights. They had an outside law firm draft a waiver specifically for me. Whether it was collusion, discrimination, anything within my employment rights, holistically, were out the window if I signed that waiver. My lawyers were like, 'It's malpractice if we tell you to sign this.'"

**Matt Barnes:** "The waiver that the NFL wanted me to sign waived all of my employment rights."

**4. Colin Kaepernick — "Eric Reid, who was our first-round pick as a safety out of LSU, he was like, 'Look, I see what you're doing.'" — Eric Reid's role in Kaepernick's decision to take a knee** [09:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9vjxFk2u1Qw&t=543s)

**Colin Kaepernick:** "During that fourth preseason game, or prior to it, I sat down with **Eric Reid**. **Eric Reid** from Baton Rouge, he was our first-round pick as a safety out of LSU. He was like, 'Look, I see what you're doing and what you're trying to accomplish. I believe in it and I want to be a part of it. Would you be open to, if we take a knee and allows us to address a number of issues that people have brought up as far as being part of the protest?' I was like, 'Yeah, I'm good with that.'"
