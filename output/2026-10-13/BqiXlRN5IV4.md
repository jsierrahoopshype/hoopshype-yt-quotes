# Olivia Miles On Learning From Her Lynx Vets & Inspiring Young Fans With Her Goggles — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BqiXlRN5IV4

_Speakers identified: Tracy McGrady, Olivia Miles, Vince Carter_

**1. Olivia Miles — "She has a very maternal approach to our team and even though she was hurt" — Olivia Miles on the leadership and impact of Minnesota Lynx veterans** [00:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BqiXlRN5IV4&t=19s)

**Vince Carter:** "Who has made the game easiest for you in your rookie season?"

**Olivia Miles:** "**Nafisa**, she is just the epitome of a great basketball player and a great leader. Obviously she is a mom, so she knows what she is doing. She has a very maternal approach to our team and even though she was hurt for a majority of the season, she was still so engaged. **Courtney** has been great. She brings a different fun, swaggy energy to the team. She is a great vet off the court as well. They let me be me, and that is the most that I could ask for."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BqiXlRN5IV4&t=19s

**2. Tracy McGrady — "They held us accountable. They made sure that we understood how to go about being professionals." — Tracy McGrady on the importance of veteran mentorship for young players** [01:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BqiXlRN5IV4&t=112s)

**Tracy McGrady:** "I want you to speak to what she was talking about with her vets and how that helped you out when you came to our team with the vets."

**Olivia Miles:** "It was day two when Oak put his arm around me and was like, I am going to help you with this league. I never felt like I didn't know, and I was one of the players that was willing to ask questions. We had four young guys and the rest of our locker room was veterans of eight years or more. I never felt like I wasn't prepared or didn't know. They held us accountable. They made sure that we understood how to go about being professionals. That was a key element to helping us become who we are because that took away a lot of things that some rookies or young guys are still fighting with."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BqiXlRN5IV4&t=112s

**3. Olivia Miles — "She has run the one during the dynasty years, so she knows what it takes to win." — Olivia Miles on learning from Cheryl Reeve's championship experience** [07:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BqiXlRN5IV4&t=431s)

**Tracy McGrady:** "How has she helped you this season in your rookie year?"

**Olivia Miles:** "What is most helpful is that she has been with **Cheryl** for so long, so I can get a different perspective from **Cheryl**. She has run the one during the dynasty years, so she knows what it takes to win and she knows what **Cheryl** is expecting. That has been really helpful just picking her brain. She is a great person, she is herself unapologetically, and I just admire that so much. She has really helped me to just be loose and play and have fun and allowed me to make mistakes. She is the one that I really watch film with a lot. She picks everything apart, like here is what works, here is this, and here is the pass I threw to **Maya**. It is crazy because she got to play with all those girls and she has such success with them."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BqiXlRN5IV4&t=431s

**4. Olivia Miles — "It is a symbol of me just not caring. It has become a part of my brand." — Olivia Miles on embracing her brand and wearing goggles** [09:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BqiXlRN5IV4&t=544s)

Olivia Miles: "It is so cool like little kids especially, people are DMing me saying like I started wearing glasses because of you. It is a slippery slope because I got bullied or made fun of for wearing them and kids get made fun of for wearing glasses. But it is a symbol of me just not caring. Everyone is always like, why don't you wear contacts? I am like, because I don't want to. It has become a part of my brand. Why change just because you want me to change? I hope that I can be that inspiration to kids who may get made fun of for wearing them. I know I am being me, don't say you hope, you are."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BqiXlRN5IV4&t=544s
