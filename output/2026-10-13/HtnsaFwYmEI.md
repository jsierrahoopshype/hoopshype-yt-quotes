**1. Giannis Antetokounmpo — "34 is a huge chapter in my life and now I want to move on" — Giannis Antetokounmpo explains why he switched from number 34 to 7** [02:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HtnsaFwYmEI&t=153s)

Giannis Antetokounmpo: "It was a huge chapter in my life and now I want to move on and not sit in the past. The reason why I am now wearing 34 is because out of respect for the organization, the people that were there with me, supported me, cared for me. 34 will only be worn in Milwaukee. You see a lot of players do that, like **Kevin Durant** wore 35, then he went to the Warriors wore 35, then he wore 7, then he went back to 35. **LeBron James** wore 23, went to 6, back to 23, back to 6. A lot of people wonder why do they do that, is it for jersey sales? It is not for jersey sales. I knew that if there was a possibility of me changing the team, I knew that the jersey sales will be up because I have a lot of fans around the world that would love to get the jerseys."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HtnsaFwYmEI&t=153s

**2. Giannis Antetokounmpo — "I think Miami is way more faster pace than Milwaukee" — Giannis Antetokounmpo on adjusting to life in Miami versus Milwaukee** [04:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HtnsaFwYmEI&t=259s)

Giannis Antetokounmpo: "It is different. I think Miami is way more faster pace than Milwaukee. I only live downtown, so I am able to have a little bit of peace and quiet. But I am a dad, I am a regular guy, I go to Costco and I try to get stuff. You know, once I walked in there, I had people coming up to me, like, what are you doing here? And I was like, I am just trying to feed my kids."

**3. Giannis Antetokounmpo — "Nikola Jokic doesn't like it and he is being short, maybe sometimes rude with fans" — Giannis Antetokounmpo discusses Nikola Jokic's frustration with public attention** [06:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HtnsaFwYmEI&t=415s)

Giannis Antetokounmpo: "My wife showed me **Nikola Jokic** saying about how he deals with all that attention and he does not like it and he is being short, maybe sometimes rude with fans. Not because he wants to be, just because he has been doing this for 12, 13, 15 years plus if you add that yes he played in Europe. So at the end of the day, you get tired of it. For me, I love to do normal stuff. I have had great interactions with fans, people that come and hug me and cry and give me gifts and all that, and you know, compliment my mom and my brothers and you know my dad."

**4. Giannis Antetokounmpo — "You can take pictures of me, but not my kids" — Giannis Antetokounmpo on setting boundaries regarding his children in public** [07:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HtnsaFwYmEI&t=453s)

Giannis Antetokounmpo: "I was sitting in the airport and I had my, I was waiting in the baggage claim and there was like this lady that I was sitting with my son and I had, I fly, I fly commercial. I fly private sometimes, you know, if I have a friend that pays or my brother pays. And I was sitting in the airport and I had my, I was waiting in the baggage claim and there was like this lady that I was sitting with my son and she had her phone out and she was like zooming in and she was like taking pictures of my kid and all that. I was like, excuse me, like please don't do that. She was like, why? I am like, you are a public figure. I was like, okay, I get it, but not my kids. You can like take pictures of me, but not them."

**5. Giannis Antetokounmpo — "We've chosen this life, let's say, to be basketball players, to be famous" — Giannis Antetokounmpo reflects on the choice to be a professional athlete** [09:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HtnsaFwYmEI&t=556s)

Giannis Antetokounmpo: "Me, I try to be as polite as I can, you know, when I have energy. Some days I am drained from being a dad, from, you know, basketball. But I have said this, like, we've chosen this life, let's say, to be basketball players, to be famous, whatever. How do you choose to be famous? I don't understand, how do you choose to be famous? But these kids are kids in general, they don't choose that. You know, if there is Liam grows up or my kids grows up and they say like, hey, like dad, I want to be in the spotlight, I want to be, maybe, you know, maybe okay, but they, they should choose that, let's say. Don't force it upon them."
