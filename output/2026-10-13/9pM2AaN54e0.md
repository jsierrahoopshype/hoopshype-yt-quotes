**2. Draymond Green — "These mutual options, it is kind of got a little weird. We are starting to see them pop up." — Draymond Green questions the rise of mutual options in NBA contracts** [01:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pM2AaN54e0&t=92s)

Draymond Green: "I will say about this deal, it has the mutual option in the fifth year. And these mutual options, it is kind of got a little weird. We are starting to see them pop up more and more in the NBA. You used to see the player option or team option, but lately we have been seeing these mutual options. I think **Isaiah Hartenstein** recently had a mutual option that him and the Oklahoma City Thunder worked through. **Pele** Larson from Miami recently just signed one with a mutual option. We are starting to see these things pop up more and more. And what it is, is ultimately a structure where the final year of the deal requires both the player and the team to opt in for the contract to continue."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pM2AaN54e0&t=92s

**6. Draymond Green — "My biggest concern is that Rudy Gobert lost a lot of allies, guys who understood him." — Draymond Green worries about Rudy Gobert losing his veteran allies** [21:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pM2AaN54e0&t=1304s)

Draymond Green: "There has been a ton of hype over the Timberwolves and their new roster with **LaMelo Ball** and **Julius Randle**. However, Coach Finch had this to say when asked about it. He said, I feel like the mood of the team is good. I feel like **LaMelo Ball** has a really good feel for the game. Edwards' feel for the game is improving all the time. My biggest concern is that **Rudy Gobert** lost a lot of allies, guys who understood him, knew how to talk to him. And as I go into the season, I am just conscious to make sure that there are people on the team that can still speak Rudy. A lot of people has assumed that that is **Mike Conley** being gone, that possibly **Julius Randle** being gone, maybe **Naz Reid** being gone. But I find that very interesting. **Rudy Gobert** is now in maybe year 13, and the fact that you are on a very young team and other guys got to know how to talk to him is kind of tough."
