**1. Gabby Williams — "I honestly went in with the mindset of, yeah, I'm going to help grow this team." — Gabby Williams on joining the Seattle Storm and the WNBA's growth** [01:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RhXVkHLb9Yg&t=79s)

**Draymond Green:** "First year in the Bay, see you come out here where the Bay Area in its entirety has embraced the Valkyries in year two of the organization. You've come out here, just explain to me first what that experience was like, coming back to the Bay where you already had ties and then obviously playing for the Valkyries."

**Gabby Williams:** "Obviously I was already really impressed playing in Ball Arena as an opponent. It sucks to play in that atmosphere, but in year one I was already blown away, but not surprised because I know what the Bay is like and how much they're just down for women's basketball. I honestly went in with the mindset of, yeah, I'm going to help grow this team, I'm going to go back home and try to build something, but it was honestly already built. It was built, I just had to do my thing and it was the coolest thing to see and just a full-circle moment for me, just knowing there wasn't a WNBA team there when I was young."

**2. Gabby Williams — "I honestly think that's what has gotten us here, because that was what our team identity was." — Gabby Williams on the Seattle Storm's winning culture and coaching** [05:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RhXVkHLb9Yg&t=326s)

**Draymond Green:** "You won a championship in year two with the Valkyries. What is it that the front office, the coaching staff, you said it was already built, what is it that was built that a, sold you in year one, but then also was kind of that driving force to ultimately accomplishing what you accomplished in your first year to the Valkyries, second year."

**Gabby Williams:** "I think it was obviously a combination of a few things. One, the Bay just being so hungry for a WNBA team played a huge part in that. The investment is huge. Obviously you know **Joe Lacob** and how competitive he is and how he just wants to be the best in everything. So he definitely put his money where his mouth is and he gave us the best infrastructure. He wanted to make sure that you see the Valkyries everywhere. He wanted to make sure that fans were filling up that arena. And then one thing talking to Coach Nat, she was asking about how she should go about hiring her staff and he said, just get good people. And I honestly think that's what has gotten us here, because that was what our team identity was. That's how our ownership is, our staff, our front office, everyone you could tell was just really unselfish, great people who put winning and put the team first."

**4. Gabby Williams — "Every single team was like, we want UConn kids, we want UConn kids." — Gabby Williams discusses the professional culture instilled by UConn basketball** [12:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RhXVkHLb9Yg&t=739s)

Gabby Williams: "All the time. We would always bring it up. We'd always talk about what messages we got from Coach and CD after the game too, like a reminder of where we came from. I think I remember coming into the league for the first time and everyone kept telling me when I was on my free agency calls, pre-draft calls, because we don't have a draft is so soon after March Madness that we can't do workouts and stuff, we can only have calls. And every single team was like, we want UConn kids, we want UConn kids. And it wasn't just for the pedigree of the talent, but it was because of what being a UConn kid meant and that meant the culture, that meant the unselfishness, that meant the professionalism that you get taught at UConn. And so having three other Huskies in the locker room, you felt that. Kia has won four out of the last five championships because she's willing to do whatever the team needs to win without getting all the credit or the light that she deserves. Kaitlin came from Princeton, played one year at UConn, won, came here and you see she is our biggest energy giver on the bench, always comes in as the same person every single day. Tip is such a freaking pro. I learned so much from Tip this summer."

**5. Gabby Williams — "I can't feel like I'm doing nothing on the defensive end in order for me to be aggressive." — Gabby Williams on her role and defensive mindset in game seven** [22:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RhXVkHLb9Yg&t=1344s)

**Draymond Green:** "It's a different type of athleticism, Gabby. How is it for you, adapting back and forth to the styles of games that you play? You're playing in Europe, you're playing overseas, you're playing in the WNBA. How is it for you adapting back and forth to the styles of games that you play?"

**Gabby Williams:** "It's less now than it was before. Before it was really difficult because also personally my role was so different. Now my role on the national team isn't too far away from my role on the Valkyries, where before my role from France was like opposite of what like Seattle was asking me to do. It is, but it is an adjustment for sure, even like the defensive three in the key, like that's always one that always gets me. Or just like little forgetting little rules and stuff, jump balls. So there is like a little bit of a learning curve every time, but I think now that I've done it so much, I will say like, I'm glad that I grew up having like a double culture, nationality, because I've always respected basketball outside of the US. And playing overseas and like finding my way overseas was easy for me because I just had respect for it. I was going to compete at my best no matter what."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RhXVkHLb9Yg&t=1344s

**6. Draymond Green — "playing year-round is just not sustainable for me anymore" — Gabby Williams explains her decision to join the Unrivaled League** [27:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RhXVkHLb9Yg&t=1626s)

**Draymond Green:** "This will now be the first year that you don't play in Europe during the off-season. You recently just signed a multi-year deal to play in the Unrivaled League. What was it that intrigued you to join Unrivaled as opposed to going to Europe where you've built a huge name for yourself?"

**Gabby Williams:** "It's weird not seeing everyone in EuroLeague right now. I would have loved to have continued with Fenerbahçe in Turkey. They were a great club, they really took care of us. Obviously, I would love to add more EuroLeague titles to my resume, but I think at this stage of my career, I'm just kind of thinking about longevity. And yeah, playing year-round is just not sustainable for me anymore. I think back in the day when our salaries weren't as high in the W, that was just kind of the thing you did. It wasn't, you didn't think twice about it, everyone just played year-round. When I first started playing, your contract would literally say like, after your last game you have 48 hours to get to the overseas. Yes, it was even worse back then. So now, I'm like, I need to rehab certain things, I need to focus on my body, I need to have a bit of an off-season. Even just as much as the travel or personal development, because when you're playing year-round you're not doing PD work. So I just kind of have to unfortunately switch my mindset to that, but I'm also really intrigued by Unrivaled and I'm always interested in something that invests so much in women's sports and has grown so quickly and it's pushed the envelope."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RhXVkHLb9Yg&t=1626s
