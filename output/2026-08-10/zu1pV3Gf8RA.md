# Steve Novak and Thanasis Antetokounmpo debate the GOAT from THREE. — *Thanalysis Show*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zu1pV3Gf8RA

_Speakers identified: Thanasis Antetokounmpo, Steve Novak_

**1. Steve Novak — "the conversation went from we can't win shooting threes to he was winning shooting threes" — Stephen Curry and Damian Lillard's impact on three-point shooting** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zu1pV3Gf8RA&t=0s)

**Thanasis Antetokounmpo:** "Give me a current top five first."

**Steve Novak:** "Shooters. I mean, Steph is right there, he's on top. All-time and current. Just for what you said, the way he changed the game, the way he turned the conversation from we can't win shooting threes to he was winning shooting threes. And the whole world said, all right, we subscribe to that. And the analytics said, oh, and by the way, we can back that up. So to me, it's hands down."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zu1pV3Gf8RA&t=0s

**2. Steve Novak — "Klay was the literal opposite. He was the catch-and-shoot version of Steph" — Klay Thompson's role as a catch-and-shoot king** [00:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zu1pV3Gf8RA&t=40s)

Steve Novak: "The current number two, in my opinion, and he's benefited from Steph, is Klay. The fact that he's a different player than Steph, he's not a point guard, he's not out there averaging ten assists, but he is a catch-and-shoot king. You look at **James Harden**, the way he played, it was like he was a hundred million dribbles. I'm not saying good or bad, I'm just saying Klay was the literal opposite. He was the catch-and-shoot version of Steph and they played together, that's why they won championships."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zu1pV3Gf8RA&t=40s

**3. Steve Novak — "to see not only was he a guy who didn't shoot threes, who now does stroke it" — Brook Lopez's evolution into a high-volume three-point shooter** [01:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zu1pV3Gf8RA&t=117s)

Steve Novak: "Our guy **Brook Lopez**, to your point, I'm putting the Bucks back in it. To see not only was he a guy who didn't shoot threes, who now does stroke it from three, but not just from three, where he shoots it from, and the fact that the guy who's guarding him is the other team's five-man. That changes everything. He's not even shooting the regular three, he's shooting it from five to seven feet further away."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zu1pV3Gf8RA&t=117s

**4. Steve Novak — "the fact that he can hit a fadeaway from 40 feet away on multiple occasions" — Damian Lillard's ability to hit deep fadeaway shots** [02:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zu1pV3Gf8RA&t=169s)

Steve Novak: "When you see guys like **Damian Lillard**, the fact that he can hit a fadeaway from 40 feet away on multiple occasions. I don't even care what his percentage is, you know he's got the gene. Those are the guys I like watching shoot that thing because I'm looking going, what is it about how he shoots it?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zu1pV3Gf8RA&t=169s

**5. Steve Novak — "he was a jump shooter, but he was shooting the three unapologetic" — Ray Allen and Dirk Nowitzki's unique shooting styles** [03:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zu1pV3Gf8RA&t=222s)

Steve Novak: "For me, **Ray Allen**. I watched the Bucks growing up. He was a jump shooter, but he was shooting the three unapologetic and he was doing it in stretches where you're like, I never seen this. And Dirk the same, a seven-footer."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zu1pV3Gf8RA&t=222s

**7. Steve Novak — "some guys like Steph Curry who have so much more to do" — Stephen Curry's multifaceted role beyond just being a shooter** [05:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zu1pV3Gf8RA&t=303s)

Steve Novak: "Some guys like Steph Curry who have so much more to do. They're running point, they got to assist, they got to establish the offense, they got to play big minutes, they got to make big shots early and late. Other guys are just shooting it and some guys are that superstar, all-star level that I think people don't realize. He's not just shooting."

**8. Steve Novak — "it's the question how do they get their threes" — The importance of mid-range and post games for three-point success** [05:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zu1pV3Gf8RA&t=346s)

Steve Novak: "It's the question how do they get their threes, not how many can you get up. The answer always I find tends to come back to they got a good post game, they got a great mid-range game, they got great shooters, they got playmakers. It's never just one thing."

**9. Steve Novak — "the only way you can end the moment in a big moment in a game" — The necessity of putting in the work to become a shooter** [07:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zu1pV3Gf8RA&t=433s)

Steve Novak: "To me, you have to spend the time shooting the shots because the only way you can end the moment in a big moment in a game, feel like you're supposed to be shooting a shot, is if you've put the reps in and you've put the work in. Regardless of how jacked up your form is or not, if you've put the work in and if you've shot enough reps that you feel like when you're on the court, it's your shot, it doesn't matter how your form looks."
