# Jared McCain GETS REAL On His Trade To OKC — *The Old Man and The Three*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YczgUqm_hxE

_Speakers identified: Cam Johnson, Jared McCain, Tommy Alter_

**1. Cam Johnson — "We're not supposed to be good for another three years and we should have went to the finals" — Cam Johnson and Jared McCain on the pressure of joining a winning team** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YczgUqm_hxE&t=0s)

**Cam Johnson:** "Your situation is funny because we had just won, so it's a little more nerve-wracking. Hearing him is like the year before, we're kind of like, don't know if we're good or not for real. Everybody is kind of nervous and doesn't know what's going on. So it's funny to hear you say you're nervous because everybody is in the building like, so we're not supposed to be good for another three years and we should have went to the finals last year, so now what?"

**Jared McCain:** "I just wanted people to like me. There's obviously people who have preconceived notions about me and I was just hoping they understand when I walk into this building, I want to be a nice person to them, and I hope they're nice back to me."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YczgUqm_hxE&t=0s

**2. Cam Johnson — "The load and the weight and gravity that he ended up playing with, nobody would have thought" — Cam Johnson on the impact of Jared McCain's mid-season arrival** [02:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YczgUqm_hxE&t=131s)

**Cam Johnson:** "His is different because yours happened in the middle of the year. When Sam is bringing somebody in the middle of the year, which doesn't happen often, he obviously sees the same thing. That usually gives you a lot of confidence. The way he was playing, we didn't expect that to happen, but that's just what it is. The load and the weight and gravity that he ended up playing with, nobody would have thought that coming off of how Philly was doing."

**Jared McCain:** "I didn't even think I was going to play when I got traded."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YczgUqm_hxE&t=131s

**3. Cam Johnson — "That was the first time I realized that he was famous" — Cam Johnson and Jared McCain on the reaction to the trade** [03:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YczgUqm_hxE&t=197s)

**Cam Johnson:** "Did you follow how much the Philly fans freaked out when they traded him? That was the first time I realized that he was famous."

**Jared McCain:** "Yeah, they like freaked out. But obviously, people don't know, I talked to him, what was that, your draft? I did a whole Q&A about the NBA thing, and he was the only person asking questions. Mind you, my brother is in this, not asking anything. So that was the first time we had it, and I had seen him, he's been famous since being homeschooled, so it wasn't the first time we had seen him."
