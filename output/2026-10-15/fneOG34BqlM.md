# Tina Thompson Reveals The Story Behind Her Signature Lipstick + Talks WNBA Playoffs & MVP Race — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

_Speakers identified: Vince Carter, Tina Thompson, Tracy McGrady_

**1. Tina Thompson — "After that moment, it became a part of my uniform and I just wore it." — Tina Thompson on how her signature lipstick became part of her uniform** [00:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fneOG34BqlM&t=23s)

Tina Thompson: "I'm rushing everywhere. We practice at a certain time and we have an exhibition game. So we play our first one and I was rushing to the game because I was in class and then I didn't play that well. I played okay. So I was kind of pissed and then the second one I'm like, I got to do better. I just didn't play well. But I'm again, I'm rushing. My team is going out to warm up and I'm just getting to the locker room. So I'm getting undressed or whatever. I put my hair in a ponytail and put on my uniform and shoes and I run out and then one of my teammates says, you still have on your lipstick. But the lipstick that I wore was a matte lipstick, so it's not one that you could just wipe off and it's going to be gone. It will look crazy. So I was just like, I'm just going to leave it on and then I'll just deal with it at the end of the game or halftime or whatever. But lo and behold, I start cooking. I was balling, right? So I was like, yo, I need to keep this lipstick on. And afterwards I ended up having a good game. So again, just being immature and a kid, I'm like, it must be the lipstick. After that moment, it became a part of my uniform and I just wore it for the rest of the time."

**4. Tina Thompson — "I agree with Tracy, I think that Olivia Miles should definitely be in the conversation." — Tina Thompson evaluates the WNBA MVP race** [05:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fneOG34BqlM&t=357s)

Tina Thompson: "I agree with Tracy, I think that Olivia Miles should definitely be in the conversation. There's only been one rookie of the year and that was Candace. I think that Olivia has, if you ask Candace, Candace will probably say the same thing, that she has a chance to be the second because Olivia has been cold for a long time. She is exactly who she says she is. She shows up exactly as who she is and I just love and I respect that. And then there are the usual. I think that Nafisa, she probably is not going to be in that conversation because she missed too much time. She missed a lot of time, but she's just so solid as a player. She's so good. And then there's Asia. I feel like Asia is always in that conversation. And there's a lot of players that you can say. I like Paige. I think that Paige is a dynamic young player and personality in this league. But **Kelsey Mitchell**, she's so nice. She's so cold. She's so cold. Kelsey has been very good for a really long time. What she does every night, she gets overlooked a little bit because of Caitlin. But those scouting reports, real hoopers, they know. **Kelsey Mitchell** is that girl. I also think that Angel needs to be in that conversation. Like she went there and that Atlanta team is revived. They're different. I think what she brings is impactful."
