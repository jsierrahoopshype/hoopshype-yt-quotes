# Tina Thompson On Her Houston Comets Dynasty & Wanting JuJu Watkins In Houston — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OugnL0p_L7w

_Speakers identified: Tina Thompson, Vince Carter, Tracy McGrady_

**1. Tina Thompson — "I didn't decide that I was going to join the WNBA until less than 48 hours before the draft." — Tina Thompson on her last-minute decision to join the WNBA** [00:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OugnL0p_L7w&t=4s)

Tina Thompson: "I didn't decide that I was going to join the WNBA until less than 48 hours before the draft. They offered me 30-something thousand dollars and I live in Los Angeles. I am studying for the bar. Do you know that salary? I am going to be a professional athlete and I get applied for public assistance. How is that going to work? I can't do that. So future is in the balance. I have said that I wanted to be a judge half of my life. I can't make that decision for 32 or 33 thousand dollars. That sounds like a lot of money for a kid, but I actually have to live."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OugnL0p_L7w&t=4s

**2. Tina Thompson — "That inaugural season, they didn't even pick us to make the playoffs." — Tina Thompson on the Houston Comets' unexpected championship run** [04:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OugnL0p_L7w&t=252s)

Tina Thompson: "The first one is probably the most memorable because that inaugural season, they didn't even pick us to make the playoffs. We were not predicted to be a playoff team. They had the Sparks at that time, they had New York, I think they had Don Staley, the Sting, Charlotte, and someone else. It got to a point where I don't need to remember because it was us, but we were not picked to make the playoffs at all. They did not have an expectation of us not only winning, but not even making the playoffs."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OugnL0p_L7w&t=252s

**3. Tina Thompson — "The one that I hold closest to my heart is our third championship." — Tina Thompson on the emotional toll of the third championship** [05:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OugnL0p_L7w&t=307s)

Tina Thompson: "The one that I hold closest to my heart is our third championship and that was the one that we dedicated to **Kim Perrot**. She was battling cancer and it was so hard because we were so emotional and we missed her. There was a lot of fear just because we didn't know what was going on. She was battling, she was really fighting. When we were in our playoff run, she was somewhere in Mexico doing this trial and experimenting with this drug. They didn't know if it would be successful or not. We just didn't know."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OugnL0p_L7w&t=307s

**4. Tina Thompson — "The locker room before we were going out, Cynthia Cooper and Kim Perrot were really, really close." — Tina Thompson on the close bond between Cynthia Cooper and Kim Perrot** [06:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OugnL0p_L7w&t=370s)

Tina Thompson: "The locker room before we were going out, **Cynthia Cooper** and **Kim Perrot** were really, really close. So she was extremely emotional. If she was crying, my heart was breaking for her. We were all close with Kim, but they were best friends. It was just a really, really tough season."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OugnL0p_L7w&t=370s

**5. Tina Thompson — "She just went to the doctor because she wasn't feeling good and then that's when they found out." — Tina Thompson on Kim Perrot's brain tumor diagnosis** [06:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OugnL0p_L7w&t=397s)

Tina Thompson: "She just went to the doctor because she wasn't feeling good and then that's when they found out that she had this tumor in her brain. As soon as they basically found out, she was rushed into treatment because of the placement of it and just how large it was at the time and how fast it was growing. It was pretty immediate."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OugnL0p_L7w&t=397s

**6. Tina Thompson — "Sheryl Swoopes was the golden child." — Tina Thompson on the legacy of Sheryl Swoopes** [08:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OugnL0p_L7w&t=518s)

Tina Thompson: "**Cynthia Cooper** was coming here to prove herself. She had played so long overseas. **Sheryl Swoopes** was the golden child. She wasn't just the face on the Olympic team, she was the female Jordan. She was the face of the league. The only reason why she didn't have the impact right away is because she found out she was pregnant with Jordan. She didn't start with us, but you just think about how she came back after she had the baby and still played and was impactful. She wasn't her full self, but it just goes into the mindset of who she was and who we were as a team."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OugnL0p_L7w&t=518s
