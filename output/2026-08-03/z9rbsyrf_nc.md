**2. Tracy McGrady — "Nobody can touch him. I'm talking about the big picture of who Jay is." — Tracy McGrady on Jay-Z's performance and Blue Ivy's stage presence** [01:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z9rbsyrf_nc&t=78s)

**Tracy McGrady:** "Nobody can touch him. I am talking about the big picture of who Jay is and what he does. **3. Tracy McGrady — "You got Beyonce and then you got Blue Ivy blessing the stage, playing the piano." — Tracy McGrady on Blue Ivy's piano performance at the concert** [02:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z9rbsyrf_nc&t=142s)

**Tracy McGrady:** "You got **Beyonce** and then you got **Blue Ivy** blessing the stage, playing the piano. That was it. That trumped everything because to learn how to play the piano, you are putting some hours in. Right? And I have never seen her perform in front of 50,000 people before. So to come out on that stage at this magnitude and perform like that, it is. She was dancing for her mom."

**Vince Carter:** "Dancing is one thing, performing, playing the piano is a totally different thing."

**Tracy McGrady:** "She had her own segment. It is a totally different thing. This is the setup for her for what we will be seeing from **Blue Ivy** in the future. She is going to be performing and you can tell she has it already. She is not afraid of the moment, she delivered."

**5. Vince Carter — "I played in the American Century Championship and this is year nine or ten." — Vince Carter on his experience at the American Century Championship** [05:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z9rbsyrf_nc&t=347s)

Vince Carter: "I played in the American Century Championship and this is year nine or ten. You can play all the golf you want, casual golf is casual golf, competing and doing tournament golf is a different level. I am finally feeling comfortable where I obviously know the course. It is just putting it all together to compete. Shout out to **Marty Fish** who won it again and he is a guy who is shooting in the 60s, he is shooting with the pros. He gets after it. It was great to see that for many years there wasn't that many basketball players representing at the American Century Championship. Obviously you would see Ray, you would see Steph, you would see Dell, then all of a sudden we started seeing Seth. But now we have **Alex Caruso**, we have **Austin Reaves**, Kyle has been there, I was there, Barkley is there, and that is really it. We have had **Iguodala** play with him. It is not many of us, so it is just great to see Ray always up in contention, Steph finished third. My goal was to come in there feeling in the top half so it was 90 players and I wanted to finish in the top third, and I finished 36th this year."
