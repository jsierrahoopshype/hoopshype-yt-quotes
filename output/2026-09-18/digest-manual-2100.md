## Michael Porter Jr. Explains What Went WRONG With Cam Thomas in Brooklyn — *Road Trippin*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gTMjT4y5LHE

_Speakers identified: Michael Porter Jr., Richard Jefferson, Kendrick Perkins, Allie Clifton_

**1. Michael Porter Jr. — "It's a lot more to the NBA and sticking around than just that." — Michael Porter Jr. on Cam Thomas's struggles in Brooklyn** [00:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gTMjT4y5LHE&t=38s)

Michael Porter Jr.: "I don't know really what the public narrative is about Cam. I know that when it comes to putting the ball in the hoop and making tough shots, I know he is one of the best. But there's a lot of things, as you know in the NBA, that it's a lot more to the NBA and sticking around than just that. I think for Cam, it was a mixture of he was frustrated with a lot of things and then also his personality. He was just a certain type of way, real quiet, stuck to himself, always had his headphones in, listening to YoungBoy every day, and all he wanted to do is hoop. He doesn't really socialize. He'll come to the gym sometimes and he'll say two words all day, all practice. He doesn't really talk to nobody."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gTMjT4y5LHE&t=38s

**2. Michael Porter Jr. — "I don't know if he was willing to break out of his personality and be talkative." — Why Cam Thomas struggled to adapt to a larger role** [01:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gTMjT4y5LHE&t=98s)

Michael Porter Jr.: "When it comes to a team willing to pay you and come off that money and you're a number one option and it comes with so much more, I don't know if he was willing to break out of his personality and be talkative and try to be a leader and try to bring guys together. I think that's kind of what happened here in Brooklyn. He was frustrated about the contract the year before and the fact that Brooklyn didn't really pay him how he wanted to. From his perspective, he's thinking talent-wise, he's as good as **Austin Reaves**, he's as good as **Jalen Green**, he's as good as this guy, that guy, and they're getting paid $100 million contracts."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gTMjT4y5LHE&t=98s

At times, you want to see your best players not hijack the offense. A lot of guys on bad teams hijack an offense where everyone is just standing around. Some of the best scorers in the league do that at times, but if you can't pass, get the ball back, and then allow other people to touch the ball and you just go, that's not going to work because every game we're not just going to throw you the ball when you're playing with other players who are getting $80, $90, $100 million."

**Richard Jefferson:** "And then also, a crazy stat is basketball, this isn't football. You got to play defense too. And I think statistically he was the worst defender per minute in the game."
