# “I Thought I Wasn’t Wanted” John Smoltz on the Tigers Trade That Sent Him to the Braves — *Brice Butler Studios*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F9-1-4VQ6qY

_Speakers identified: John Smoltz, Brice Butler_

**1. John Smoltz — "I was ticked when I got drafted 22nd round. And part of the reason I got drafted 22nd round is I signed really fast to go to Michigan State." — John Smoltz on being drafted by the Detroit Tigers** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F9-1-4VQ6qY&t=0s)

John Smoltz: "I was ticked when I got drafted 22nd round. And part of the reason I got drafted 22nd round is I signed really fast to go to Michigan State. So the first chance I could get, I knew where I wanted to go and made one trip to the University of Michigan. I said, heck no, I'm going to Michigan State. And so that draftability became harder for those teams because back then you had to sign your first-round pick. And college, to me, gives the player leverage to negotiate."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F9-1-4VQ6qY&t=0s

**2. John Smoltz — "My grandfather worked on a ground crew for 10-12 years. He worked at Tiger Stadium." — John Smoltz on his connection to the Detroit Tigers** [00:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F9-1-4VQ6qY&t=43s)

John Smoltz: "The Tigers drafted me. They brought me in a room. My grandfather worked on a ground crew for 10-12 years. He worked at Tiger Stadium. There was some connectivity there and I'm a die-hard Tiger fan. So even though I wasn't happy about 22nd round, I was like, man, my hometown team drafted me. They put an offer in front of me. It was $15,000. I said, thank you but no thank you."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F9-1-4VQ6qY&t=43s

**3. John Smoltz — "The coach for Michigan State was Tom Smith and he helped me. The only scholarship player they had ever had was me." — Tom Smith helping John Smoltz negotiate his contract** [01:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F9-1-4VQ6qY&t=89s)

John Smoltz: "The coach for Michigan State was **Tom Smith** and he helped me. The only scholarship player they had ever had was me. And he goes, if you get first-round money, you have to go. I don't know any other coach that would have said that. He would have wanted to say, you know, come to my program and we'll enhance it and you'll get more money later. So that was a big relief."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F9-1-4VQ6qY&t=89s

**4. John Smoltz — "I was the lowest I've been in probably ever. At that time. I had a poor view of it." — John Smoltz on his trade from the Detroit Tigers to the Braves** [02:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F9-1-4VQ6qY&t=164s)

**Brice Butler:** "So in Detroit, it seems like it didn't work out so soon and you get traded to the Braves. Obviously you just said Detroit was your hometown team. Your stock was rising. I'm sure your confidence was going up and then you get to Detroit and it doesn't work out the way you envisioned it. So when you're going to the Braves, are you the John that just sat there and was literally like, I'm not signing until you get to that number, or were you a little bit, where was your confidence level?"

**John Smoltz:** "I was the lowest I've been in probably ever. At that time. I had a poor view of it. I thought I wasn't wanted, even though somebody wanted me. So I was having a miserable year. We were on a miserable team. We were 30 games out of first place. We had eight projected Major League Baseball players on prospects on that team. It was just a miserable year and I was having a bad year. And I get the notice that I'm getting traded and I'm just like devastated."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F9-1-4VQ6qY&t=164s

**5. John Smoltz — "I realized that my real goal was to be a Major League Baseball player, not necessarily a Tiger." — John Smoltz on his goal to be a Major League Baseball player** [04:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F9-1-4VQ6qY&t=240s)

John Smoltz: "I realized that my real goal was to be a Major League Baseball player, not necessarily a Tiger. So I got spoiled by being a Tiger and then getting shipped out to Atlanta, the worst organization in baseball at the time. I'm like, nothing's clicking. So my dad offered to fly down to Glens Falls so that he would drive with me to Richmond, which is where I was going. I said, no, I got to do this myself. And that 14 hours, I thought about everything from seven years old to where I am."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F9-1-4VQ6qY&t=240s

**6. John Smoltz — "Detroit had one coach on our team. No pitching coach, no first base coach, no third base coach." — John Smoltz on the lack of coaching in the Detroit minor leagues** [05:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F9-1-4VQ6qY&t=318s)

John Smoltz: "Atlanta had an overkill of coaches. This was so long ago. Detroit had one coach on our team. No pitching coach, no first base coach, no third base coach. Just one coach, the manager. We had no help. And I saw a pitching coach twice in the year. So I'm a lost puppy. I don't know what I'm doing. And I get to Atlanta and they had an overkill of coaches. And **Leo Mazzone** really helped me. He said one thing that really changed the dynamic for me. He said, I want you to be an athlete. Throw a pitch athletically."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F9-1-4VQ6qY&t=318s

**7. John Smoltz — "Detroit changed my entire motion, my entire mechanics. I was as lost as you can be." — John Smoltz on his mechanical changes in Atlanta** [06:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F9-1-4VQ6qY&t=360s)

John Smoltz: "Detroit changed my entire motion, my entire mechanics. I was as lost as you can be. It would be like getting a lesson on the golf course and saying, you got to go play in a tournament now. Well, nobody could do that. So I threw in an athletic pitch and he goes, that's perfect. And I went, really? Like I was beat up mentally. And he goes, that's perfect. Threw it again. I did. He goes, that's perfect. Now all we got to do is upgrade your pitches. And it gave me all the confidence in the world to go, I am pretty good. I lost my confidence and now I was on a trajectory going in the right direction."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F9-1-4VQ6qY&t=360s
