**1. Carter Bryant — "you don't take for granted, just understanding the mental fortitude you have to have on any given night" — Carter Bryant on the mental challenge of NBA rookie rotations** [00:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCAC9ZyPn_4&t=9s)

Carter Bryant: "It is one of those things you don't take for granted, just understanding the mental fortitude you have to have on any given night. I mean, to start the season off, they are playing a lot more guys, and then as the season progresses, you are trying to win games and you crack down on the rotation. You can go from any given night, you played 22 the night before, and then you might get three DNPs in a row. So just me being a competitor, I am like, what is this? I just want to play. But understanding the greater good for the team, as the season progressed, I think I just learned the game a lot better."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCAC9ZyPn_4&t=9s

**2. Carter Bryant — "He taught me the game inside out from pounding the ball to making a right-hand layup" — Carter Bryant on the impact of assistant coach Sean Sweeney** [00:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCAC9ZyPn_4&t=33s)

Carter Bryant: "Our assistant coach **Sean Sweeney**, that just got the job in Orlando, he was one of the only reasons I played this year. He taught me the game inside out from pounding the ball with your right hand to making a right-hand layup to the complexities of the game. I am forever in debt to him because he broke everything down to me so simple. I am a person that has a million and one things going on in my mind, and I want to do everything, and I think I understand everything. So he taught me how to, yeah, he taught me how to."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCAC9ZyPn_4&t=33s

**3. Richard Jefferson — "you get to the NBA and you realize how much basketball you have no fucking clue about" — Richard Jefferson on the reality check for top NBA prospects** [01:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCAC9ZyPn_4&t=97s)

**Carter Bryant:** "Yeah, it is embarrassing. I will never, yeah, it is embarrassing."

**4. Carter Bryant — "I've never seen a human being hit that many contested shots in a row" — Carter Bryant on his first experience guarding Devin Vassell** [03:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCAC9ZyPn_4&t=187s)

Carter Bryant: "I'll never forget, it was my second day, I got to San Antonio and I'm working out. I hear a ball bouncing and we have a smaller shooting gym. I walk over there and it's **Devin Vassell** working out with our coach **Jimmy Baron**. Jimmy is like, oh Carter, come over here, guard Devin. When I tell you, I've never seen a human being hit that many contested shots in a row. I'm playing amazing defense. I'm like, okay, I assume he's going to bust my ass today. But understanding I've never done this before, so just going and play hard, I promise you he probably hit 18 shots in a row. And I'm just like, yo, I should not have put my name in the draft. I am not ready to be here."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCAC9ZyPn_4&t=187s

**5. Carter Bryant — "I did not know Kelly Olynyk was that good" — Carter Bryant on being humbled by Kelly Olynyk's post game** [05:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCAC9ZyPn_4&t=303s)

Carter Bryant: "I did not know **Kelly Olynyk** was that good. Kelly, what? Yes. I knew Kelly was very good, like he had the games where he put teams away and stuff like that, especially in Boston and then Toronto and Utah. He's a hell of a player. When I tell you, he caught it in the post and I had no idea what was going on, and he's shooting a one-leg step-back and he pump fakes and then shoots it off the glass. I'm like, I remember my first two weeks guarding Kelly, it was nasty for me."
