**1. Steve Nash — "This generation, the last two, three generations, it's the first line of attack." — Steve Nash on the evolution of point guard play** [18:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QNyWBnyolZo&t=1090s)

Steve Nash: "Just looking at the two of us, Jalen grew up more in a generation, probably shifted during your childhood, but when I grew up, a point guard, your dad is a great example, I played against Jalen's dad in the NCAA tournament, he's a great example, classic point guard: run the team, make the passes, score if you need to. That was the job of a point guard. I think this generation, the last two, three generations, it's the first line of attack. Why are we waiting around? Try to, especially in pace and space, let's try to break the defense down before it gets set and score. So to me, that is number one for our position. I think the culture of the game has changed around the position. Analytics has driven that in many ways. Also the amount of athletes that I think that weren't point guards that have become point guards in the league."

**9. Steve Nash — "The biggest one is the rules for me, it's a different game" — Steve Nash on the differences between NBA and international basketball** [1:01:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QNyWBnyolZo&t=3713s)

Steve Nash: "The biggest one is the rules for me, it's a different game. Court's a little smaller, the three-point line's a little closer, there's no three seconds in the lane, 2.9.
