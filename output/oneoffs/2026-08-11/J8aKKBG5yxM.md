**1. Sandra Brunson — "What Jalen and his teammates have done on the court has amplified the goals that he has" — Sandra Brunson on the elevated mission of the Second Round Foundation** [01:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J8aKKBG5yxM&t=86s)

**Dexter Henry:** "Does it hit differently in any way, Jalen and Sandra, coming off the NBA championship?"

**Sandra Brunson:** "It is not different. The awareness and the exposure is slightly different. It is elevated. What Jalen and his teammates have done on the court has amplified the goals that he has as a philanthropic individual. It has amplified our mission and vision and we are truly grateful for that. We could not imagine having the amount of exposure that we have had for what he has done on the court to promote and amplify what we were trying to do off the court."

**Jalen Brunson:** "Definitely felt a little bit. Like Mom said, it does not really change that much, but you can feel the excitement, you can feel the energy a little bit differently. But the approach and everything that we have done and gone about has been the same."

**4. Jalen Brunson — "Everyone sees the championship and sees you are on top, but no one sees the majority of" — Jalen Brunson on the work required to win an NBA title** [06:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J8aKKBG5yxM&t=377s)

Jalen Brunson: "It is very important because it is most of your life. There are so many things that go into a day-to-day to get you to that point, and so the journey is beautiful and I think everyone should cherish it and understand it."
