# Isaiah Stewart Opens Up About Leaving Detroit For Memphis | Andscape — *Andscape*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cJJYVvzsTuw

_Speakers identified: Isaiah Stewart, Marc Spears_

**1. Isaiah Stewart — "It was quiet in the locker room, a lot of people scratching their heads trying to figure out how we lost the series." — Isaiah Stewart reflects on the Pistons' loss to the Cavaliers** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cJJYVvzsTuw&t=0s)

**Marc Spears:** "Game 7, second round, lose to Cleveland at home. What was going through your mind at the time?"

**Isaiah Stewart:** "It was quiet in the locker room, a lot of people scratching their heads trying to figure out how we lost the series. We had a great season, 60 wins, number one in the East, and that's why I always pictured Detroit being, bringing back that identity, that toughness. That series didn't go good. I didn't play as much, and I just knew it was my last game as a Piston."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cJJYVvzsTuw&t=0s

**2. Isaiah Stewart — "You work so hard and you have a goal and you're putting your all into something and you're not able to reap that kind of reward." — Isaiah Stewart on the emotional toll of his final season in Detroit** [00:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cJJYVvzsTuw&t=33s)

**Marc Spears:** "At what point did you think, you know what, maybe I won't be in Detroit forever?"

**Isaiah Stewart:** "This past season, I was in a situation where you work so hard and you have a goal and you're putting your all into something and you're not able to reap that kind of reward. It will make you question yourself, stress yourself out, because you know the passion and love you have for the game. Me and my wife were just talking and we both felt that, hey, this may be our last season here in Detroit."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cJJYVvzsTuw&t=33s

**3. Isaiah Stewart — "Trajan called, he got a deal from Memphis that he can't turn down." — Isaiah Stewart describes the moment he learned of his trade to Memphis** [00:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cJJYVvzsTuw&t=57s)

**Marc Spears:** "So you get the call, you tell us about when you get the call that you got traded?"

**Isaiah Stewart:** "I got a call from my agent. He was thinking it's maybe the Wizards or the Lakers, and called me back a couple minutes later and said, Trajan called, he got a deal from Memphis that he can't turn down. So a few minutes later it came out I got traded to Memphis."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cJJYVvzsTuw&t=57s

**4. Isaiah Stewart — "I'm thankful because I'm somewhere where I'm really wanted and the fan base already respects and love who I am." — Isaiah Stewart on his reaction to being traded to the Grizzlies** [01:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cJJYVvzsTuw&t=76s)

Isaiah Stewart: "At first it was like, okay, it's the first time I got traded in my career. I'm thankful because I'm somewhere where I'm really wanted and the fan base already respects and love who I am. I'd rather be where I'm wanted and head it back into a situation where it's not the best."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cJJYVvzsTuw&t=76s

**5. Isaiah Stewart — "He's very versatile and he has a frame as a 18-year-old, he's going to be a problem." — Isaiah Stewart evaluates Cam Boozer's potential in Memphis** [02:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cJJYVvzsTuw&t=130s)

Isaiah Stewart: "I got a chance to watch **Cam Boozer** in Summer League and see how he operates, how he practice, and I think he's tough, man. He's very versatile and he has a frame as a 18-year-old, he's going to be a problem."
