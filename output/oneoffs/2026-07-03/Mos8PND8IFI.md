# Inside the Celtics' Decision to Trade Jaylen Brown With Chris Mannix — *The Ryen Russillo Show*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mos8PND8IFI

_Speakers identified: Ryen Russillo, Chris Mannix_

**1. Chris Mannix — "I still think it is one of the more inexplicable deals I've seen since the Luka Doncic trade." — Chris Mannix questions the Celtics' urgency in trading for Paul George** [01:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mos8PND8IFI&t=109s)

**Ryen Russillo:** "I know no one likes this right now, but where are you today with having more information about what happened?"

**Chris Mannix:** "I'm still in the same place where I don't think this is a defensive trade as currently constructed. If this is all they get out of it going into next season, if they don't use this draft capital to go out and get another piece to this puzzle, then I still think it is one of the more inexplicable deals I've seen since the **Luka Doncic** trade. Mostly because I don't believe that this particular deal was going anywhere. I think they could have done this deal two weeks from now, two months from now. There was no market for **Paul George**. This kind of package would have been on the table from Philadelphia at any point in time over the next few months. The biggest thing I struggle with is the sense of urgency that the Celtics had in getting this deal done and pushing it through as fast as they did given the return that they got."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mos8PND8IFI&t=109s

**2. Chris Mannix — "Fundamentally, the Celtics believed that the Jaylen Brown, Jayson Tatum on-court partnership had run its course." — Celtics believed the Jaylen Brown and Jayson Tatum partnership had run its course** [04:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mos8PND8IFI&t=248s)

**Ryen Russillo:** "I do think as to the why of this deal, I do think fundamentally the Celtics believed that the **Jaylen Brown**, **Jayson Tatum** on-court partnership had run its course."

**Chris Mannix:** "I do believe they thought that there was a good chance it was going to deteriorate even further if this duo, this group was kept together. I do think they believed that the way Jaylen played last year, the All-NBA second team, the sixth place for MVP, that that was going to make it even more difficult for him to settle into the kind of role that he would have to play in for this team to be successful. So I think that's part of the why the Celtics did it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mos8PND8IFI&t=248s

**3. Chris Mannix — "I still don't understand the urgency when you're getting back a shell of Paul George." — Paul George's health and contract status make the trade difficult to justify** [05:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mos8PND8IFI&t=304s)

**Chris Mannix:** "I still don't understand the urgency when you're getting back a shell of **Paul George**, who has played 78 games over the last two years. I've heard from some people in Boston that say, well, he looked great in that first-round playoff series. Okay. Well, that came after a two-month league-mandated vacation there after he tested positive for a banned substance. It's a little bit easier to look great in a first-round series when you've got fresh legs. **Paul George** does not have healthy legs. **Paul George** is a jump shooter at this stage of his career. He's not a lot more than that, and he has one of the worst contracts in the NBA."

**Ryen Russillo:** "We're in agreement on that, and I don't like the trade. If this is what's on the table, then wait. Let's get to training camp and see if somebody blows out an ACL."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mos8PND8IFI&t=304s

**4. Chris Mannix — "I think Giannis is signing a long-term extension wherever he was whenever he was eligible." — Jaylen Brown was likely to sign a long-term extension regardless of team** [08:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mos8PND8IFI&t=496s)

**Ryen Russillo:** "I would disagree on the extension stuff, because I think Giannis is signing a long-term extension wherever he was whenever he was eligible, because I think Giannis and his team also understand the physical risk that he has right now."

**Chris Mannix:** "If you're Giannis and an offer is on the table, whatever it may be, three-year extension, four-year extension, whatever he's eligible for wherever he was, I think he was going to sign that. I think any suggestion that he might not have was probably going to be a bluff. Now maybe it was a good bluff, and if you're giving up real assets, that's."

**Ryen Russillo:** "We agree. He's signing that four-year thing."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mos8PND8IFI&t=496s

**5. Chris Mannix — "It wasn't just about offloading Jaylen Brown. These last few weeks felt about offloading Jaylen Brown." — The Celtics' trade was focused on offloading Jaylen Brown** [10:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mos8PND8IFI&t=602s)

**Chris Mannix:** "In the past, with all the guys we've talked about, it's been about those guys. It was about **Kevin Durant** in 2022. It was about **Anthony Davis**. It was about **Kawhi Leonard**. It wasn't just about offloading **Jaylen Brown**. These last few weeks felt about offloading **Jaylen Brown**. And clearly, that's what this was, because ultimately, they took a package that does not make them a better team in 2026-27, and unless they can use this draft capital to go get another player, it doesn't make them a better team moving forward."

**Ryen Russillo:** "I'm with you. I would have just said, if it's this bad, I'm going to wait."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mos8PND8IFI&t=602s

**6. Chris Mannix — "I talked to one team that absolutely made an offer to Boston for Jaylen Brown." — A mystery team made a better offer for Jaylen Brown than the Celtics accepted** [17:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mos8PND8IFI&t=1072s)

**Chris Mannix:** "I talked to one team that absolutely made an offer to Boston for **Jaylen Brown**. A good team. They made an offer to Boston. Mostly because they knew their offer wasn't very good, but they told me, this person told me, that the offer would have gotten better. Like that they were like Raptors testing the fence here. They were probing to see how low can they go. Look, nobody's going to come at you with their best offer right away, especially when the perception is that you're trying to make a deal and you're desperate to get out of there."
