**1. Brandon Ross — "MSG Sports, the owners of the Knicks and the Rangers, said they are planning to spin off" — MSG Sports plans to spin off the Knicks and Rangers** [00:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=54cNQUqhL5g&t=48s)

**Mike Vorkunov:** "Earlier this year, I am going to do the quick TLDR, MSG Sports, the owners of the Knicks and the Rangers, said they are planning to spin off those two teams into separate companies. You are a guy who follows MSG closely, you know how finance works. What exactly does that mean?"

**Brandon Ross:** "It means exactly what you said. The Knicks and Rangers are both part of the same entity right now called MSG Sports, ticker MSGS, and the board has now approved for them to break up into two companies. So you will theoretically have two publicly traded companies, one for the Knicks and one for the Rangers."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=54cNQUqhL5g&t=48s

**2. Brandon Ross — "There is a spin-off happening now to unlock value and to finance things separately" — Spinning off teams is a strategy to unlock value** [01:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=54cNQUqhL5g&t=106s)

Brandon Ross: "It used to all be one company called Cablevision. Throughout the years, you are seeing that there is a spin-off happening now to unlock value and to finance things separately, get different investor groups involved that want pure plays. They have done a series of spins. MSG was spun out of Cablevision. It had MSG Entertainment in it and MSG Sports. MSG Entertainment, the ticker, is the New York venues essentially. The sports teams were broken apart from there to trade on their own for people who just wanted to invest in sports."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=54cNQUqhL5g&t=106s

**3. Brandon Ross — "There is a new tax law called 162(m). The five highest paid players on your sports teams" — Tax law 162(m) affects player salary deductions for public companies** [05:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=54cNQUqhL5g&t=347s)

Brandon Ross: "There is a new tax law called 162(m). What that tax law says is if you are a public company, the five highest paid players on your sports teams, their salaries cannot be deducted for tax purposes. This goes into effect in the 2027-2028 season. You are essentially going to pay an additional 35 million in taxes per year for just the Knicks piece, maybe for the Rangers another 10 million. Together as separate companies, an additional 45 to 50 million dollars in taxes if you are a public company."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=54cNQUqhL5g&t=347s
