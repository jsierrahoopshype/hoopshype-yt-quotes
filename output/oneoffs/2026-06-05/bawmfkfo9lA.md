**1. Bobby Marks — "I think you have to be careful treating him like a restricted free agent." — Austin Reaves' contract status and market value as an unrestricted free agent** [00:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bawmfkfo9lA&t=43s)

**Brad Wallace:** "How would you approach the **Austin Reaves** conversation? We know the max of what he can make is five years, 241 million. He's definitely been playing on a discount for a handful of years. How would you approach their situation?"

**Bobby Marks:** "I think you have to be careful treating him like a restricted free agent. He's unrestricted once he declines his player option. I think you have to understand what the market is. Although we can say there are three teams with cap space—Brooklyn, Chicago, and the Lakers—why would he want to go to those two teams? I don't think either team would plop down a four-year, 177 million dollar contract for him."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bawmfkfo9lA&t=43s

**2. Bobby Marks — "I personally do not believe he is a max player." — Bobby Marks' assessment of Austin Reaves as a non-max player** [01:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bawmfkfo9lA&t=114s)

Bobby Marks: "I personally do not believe he is a max player. The system or the market tells you what a player is or is not. If you are offering him, let's say the starting number is 37 and they want 39, don't get cute. Don't get cute."

**3. Bobby Marks — "His new salary has no impact on what the Lakers have to spend in free agency." — Austin Reaves' new salary impact on Lakers' free agency flexibility** [02:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bawmfkfo9lA&t=147s)

Bobby Marks: "His new salary has no impact on what the Lakers have to spend in free agency this summer because his cap hold is at 21 million dollars. You can basically hold that against the salary cap, fill out whatever you want to do, and then come back and sign him as the last player and then exceed the salary cap."

**4. Bobby Marks — "I would probably offer him in year one that 41 million dollar number." — Proposed contract structure for Austin Reaves to manage cap space** [02:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bawmfkfo9lA&t=176s)

Bobby Marks: "I would probably offer him in year one that 41 million dollar number and then I would decline it, so then I would go 41, 38, 34. I would do a declining, but for me, to do a five-year 239 is a big number. That is your locked in because it is really not about this year, it is about next year."
