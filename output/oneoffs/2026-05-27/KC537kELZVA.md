# Kendall Gill unpacks his draft ideas and makes his NBA Finals prediction | By The Horns — *Chicago Sports Network*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KC537kELZVA

_Speakers identified: Cam Smith, Kendall Gill, K.C. Johnson_

**1. Kendall Gill — "I like his vision, I like his philosophy on the type of players that he likes, two-way players that are long-rangy athletic." — Bryson Graham's vision for two-way, athletic players and its success in the league** [01:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KC537kELZVA&t=112s)

Kendall Gill: "Really does. I like his vision, I like his philosophy on the type of players that he likes, two-way players that are long-rangy athletic, that play both offense and defense. I think that that has been the recipe for success in the league. You look at the **OKC Thunder**, you look at the **San Antonio Spurs**, you look at the **New York Knicks**. All of those franchises have the types of players that **Bryson Graham** talked about. And I think that eventually, a couple years down the road, this team will look similar to the ones you see playing in the Eastern Conference and Western Conference Finals right now."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KC537kELZVA&t=112s

**2. K.C. Johnson — "He's like saying, 'Look, we got a long way to go.' K.C. Johnson: "One thing that really jumped out to me in his brief tenure is how transparent he's been about where the roster stands. He's like saying, 'Look, we got a long way to go.' Kendall Gill: "So it's interesting you say that, Casey, because I was saying the other day that this could possibly be a **Detroit Pistons** or **Orlando Magic** type rebuild where, you know, you build through the draft, you get your young guys, you take your lumps for a couple years. Right. And then now you get to the playoffs and then you start to move the franchise."

**4. Kendall Gill — "Charlotte was a small market team. You got to build through the draft to get free agents in. Chicago is somewhat of a free agent destination if you have the pieces here to win." — Contrasting Charlotte's small market draft-build with Chicago's free agency potential** [05:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KC537kELZVA&t=310s)

Kendall Gill: "Very similar, but the only thing is, you know, **Charlotte** was a small market team. You got to build through the draft to get free agents in. I mean, to build a franchise in **Charlotte** because free agents, that's not a free agent destination. Chicago, you know, is somewhat of a free agent destination if you have the pieces here to win. And, uh, you know, but this isn't the first place that free agents look. So he understands that. It could possibly be done, you know, with the way that **Charlotte** did it with myself, **Larry Johnson**, **Alonzo Mourning**, then put other pieces around us like we had **Muggsy Bogues**, **Dell Curry**, **Johnny Newman**, guys like that. So, you know, it is possible. I'm interested to see, you know, what his first couple of moves are."
