## Evan Turner Critiques His Former Team's Cost-Cutting Moves | Chris Mannix & Evan Turner — *Sports Illustrated*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eN0RCBlutPU

_Speakers identified: Chris Mannix, Evan Turner_

**1. Chris Mannix — "I think he is running the Carolina Hurricanes playbook down to the last detail." — Tom Dundon is applying the Carolina Hurricanes' model to the Portland Trail Blazers** [03:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eN0RCBlutPU&t=185s)

Chris Mannix: "I think he is running the Carolina Hurricanes playbook down to the last detail. Everything that he did in Carolina when he took over, he is now doing in Portland. He got to Carolina and he shook up the front office. The head coach was gone within a matter of months, the general manager was gone. Efficiencies is the kind way to term some of the cost-cutting, but he gutted some of the personnel in that office. He also had a similar battle with the city of Raleigh over some of the money for the stadium that he is having right now with the city of Portland. But he has taken the Carolina Hurricanes playbook and he has dropped it into Portland and on the Trail Blazers."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eN0RCBlutPU&t=185s

**2. Chris Mannix — "One of the things that makes team success, small market especially, is the investment they make in brain power." — Small-market success requires investing in front-office brain power** [05:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eN0RCBlutPU&t=305s)

Chris Mannix: "One of the things that makes team success, small market especially, is the investment they make in brain power. The money they spend on front-office work, on scouting, all the things that make some of these small-market teams great. Oklahoma City is great because of **Sam Presti** at the top, but they also have a deep staff of basketball operations, a detailed scouting department. They invest a lot of money in personnel. It is a nice thing to say I am going to spend the money on the roster. They went out and they traded for **Ja Morant**. You can disagree with the basketball fit, many do, but **Ja Morant** makes a ton of money and he was not a sought-after commodity. So they are trying to buy this distressed asset and turn it around. I just wonder if it is going to work if they do not invest in top front-office guys."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eN0RCBlutPU&t=305s

So when it comes down and Dundon is like, hey, if I take all this money in this run-in and I spend it on the product, and no matter whether we sit here or not, and I am telling you I got **Phil Jackson** and we got the best practice facility, he is like, nobody is coming to Portland for that regardless. We got to make sure if I am throwing money in a second apron, shout out to all the staff which I know is very, very important, but he is like, I am going to just go get a big gun."

**4. Chris Mannix — "Somebody told me recently that he was asking some personnel about why Christian Wood was no longer in the NBA." — Tom Dundon is questioning why Christian Wood is no longer in the NBA** [10:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eN0RCBlutPU&t=651s)
