Darko Rajaković: "The first thing is lack of time to practice. That's an obvious one and that's a big one. When I came from Europe, teams were playing one, two games a week. EuroLeague, there was no double headers every week in EuroLeague. So the number of games all together, it was much, much less. And coming to NBA where you play 82 games, it's absolute art how to coach the team with so little practice and just like walk-throughs and shoot-arounds and to get guys ready and to make sure that you touch up on and coach all the things that are important.
