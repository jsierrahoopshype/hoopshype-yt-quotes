# Will Hardy on how Keyonte George's extension impacts the future🎙️ | Roundball Roundup — *Utah Jazz*

**1. Will Hardy — "Keyonte is our first homegrown guy, drafted, developed, signs an extension here." — Keyonte George's development as the Jazz's first homegrown player** [00:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dRcZj1h7dRg&t=34s)

Will Hardy: "Keyonte is our first homegrown guy, drafted, developed, signs an extension here. The growth that he's shown on the floor, I think is relatively obvious to basketball fans, but you're getting to see how he's conducted himself off the floor. He's still young. He's 22 years old. When you meet somebody at 19, you have to remember that they're 19. Sometimes fans and media can forget how young these guys are, and Keyonte is growing up right in front of us. He's growing up alongside us."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dRcZj1h7dRg&t=34s

**2. Will Hardy — "Keyonte has just gotten something that a lot of his teammates want, an extension, security." — Keyonte George's contract extension provides him with security** [02:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dRcZj1h7dRg&t=150s)

Will Hardy: "The truth is that Keyonte has just gotten something that a lot of his teammates want, an extension, security. And I think that Keyonte and the way that he's gone about his work is something that he has modeled for some of the younger players that we now have on our team. While Keyonte is young, we do have some guys who are behind him, and it's great to see him get rewarded for the hard work and the improvements, because then the guys that are coming up behind him will understand that if X, Y, and Z are accomplished, there is something that can happen for you."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dRcZj1h7dRg&t=150s

**3. Will Hardy — "His ability to draw fouls is really probably the biggest driver of his efficiency." — Keyonte George's need to improve shooting efficiency and defensive impact** [04:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dRcZj1h7dRg&t=258s)

Will Hardy: "Everybody is obsessed with points, but you can always get points more efficiently. He can shoot a higher field goal percentage. His shooting discipline has continued to improve over the last couple of years. It's why we saw an uptick in his efficiency last year. His ability to draw fouls is really probably the biggest driver of his efficiency going up last year. So we'd like to see the shooting discipline continue to improve. But the biggest one is the impact on the defensive side of the ball. He's not alone in that. To win at the highest level, you have to have players who impact the game on both ends."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dRcZj1h7dRg&t=258s

**4. Will Hardy — "He had never really played point guard. He had been an off-ball, quote-unquote, two-guard." — Keyonte George's ongoing development as a primary ball-handler** [05:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dRcZj1h7dRg&t=352s)

Will Hardy: "Keyonte comes to us out of college, he had never really played point guard. He had been an off-ball, quote-unquote, two-guard for the majority of his life. So he's still young in his development as a point guard, a primary ball-handler making decisions in pick-and-roll. I think we've seen a ton of improvement as assist numbers went up last year. But we want it all, man. I want it all. I want assists to go up, turnovers to go down, I want shooting percentage to improve. My job is to not let him or really any of our guys get complacent."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dRcZj1h7dRg&t=352s
