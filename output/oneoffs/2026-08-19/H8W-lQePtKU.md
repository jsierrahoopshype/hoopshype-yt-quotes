# ESPN NBA Insider Ramona Shelburne Talks Lakers Sale Drama with Rich Eisen | Full Interview — *The Rich Eisen Show*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H8W-lQePtKU

_Speakers identified: Rich Eisen, Ramona Shelburne_

**1. Ramona Shelburne — "Jeanie Buss and her lawyers believe that this court order from 2017 is going to stand up." — Jeanie Buss's legal standing regarding the Lakers' ownership trust** [01:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H8W-lQePtKU&t=67s)

Ramona Shelburne: "**Jeanie Buss** and her lawyers believe that this court order from 2017 is going to stand up. The thing is, she sold the team to Mark Walter initially, and she had her deal with Mark Walter, and yes, it's in writing, and yes, it's for four more years. But she doesn't have the majority of the shares, and Mark's not going to be that guy anymore, and there's two new guys who are going to pay a valuation of 12.5 billion. So there was always a question of how long would that arrangement hold."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H8W-lQePtKU&t=67s

**2. Ramona Shelburne — "The siblings believe that they can sell because of what's called a tag-along provision." — The Lakers' tag-along provision and the Buss family trust** [03:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H8W-lQePtKU&t=221s)

Ramona Shelburne: "The siblings believe that they can sell because of what's called a tag-along provision. They had it in writing in the sale to **Mark Walter** that was completed 10 months ago, that the family can tag along to any sale that he does at the valuation he gets. They have the option to tag along, and they believe that that tag-along option only takes four of the six votes to do so, and they are invoking that and they don't need her approval here. She's saying, 'Nope, nothing can happen, and none of the shares can change hands because I control the shares of the family trust.'"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H8W-lQePtKU&t=221s

**3. Ramona Shelburne — "The NBA wants the valuation. When your neighbor's house sells for 12.5, it makes your property value go up." — The NBA's perspective on the Lakers' ownership valuation** [08:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H8W-lQePtKU&t=493s)

Ramona Shelburne: "The NBA wants the valuation. When your neighbor's house sells for 12.5, it makes your property value go up, so they love that. They love that these were, the reason why they were probably sitting together at the finals, they were a very well-known bid group in Las Vegas. I think that is going to go for somewhere between 8 and 9 billion dollars when all is said and done. I think Seattle will go 4.5 to 5, maybe 6 when all is said and done. That's really good for the owners. They all get a cut."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H8W-lQePtKU&t=493s

**4. Ramona Shelburne — "There was a vote on whether to sell three or four months ago between the siblings." — The history of the Buss siblings' involvement with the Lakers** [10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H8W-lQePtKU&t=648s)

Ramona Shelburne: "There was a vote on whether to sell three or four months ago between the siblings, and they had three votes at that time. I believe that was the older siblings, the two older brothers and **Jeanie Buss** were on board at that point. Remember, all of them have been fired. **Jim Buss** got fired in 2017, I think. Johnny hasn't worked for the Lakers for years. Jeanie got fired recently among in that bloodletting they had around Thanksgiving. The two younger brothers got fired this past year once Mark took over."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H8W-lQePtKU&t=648s

**5. Ramona Shelburne — "Luka Doncic has chartered a private jet and he's flying 14 of his teammates to his hometown." — Luka Doncic's team retreat and the Lakers' roster stability** [15:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H8W-lQePtKU&t=953s)

Ramona Shelburne: "**Luka Doncic** has chartered a private jet and he's flying 14 of his teammates to his hometown in Slovenia this week. The team is kind of locked in, too. They all, a lot of them signed long-term contracts, four-year deals. There's not much flexibility. They traded away their two first-round draft picks to get Walker Kessler. There's not much that can be done with the roster. I think **JJ Redick** is well-regarded as a head coach, I don't see him going anywhere. Obviously, **Rob Pelinka** is the general manager, he's responsible for constructing this team."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H8W-lQePtKU&t=953s

**6. Ramona Shelburne — "I really think he was done with the Lakers after that last game." — LeBron James' departure from the Lakers was inevitable** [18:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H8W-lQePtKU&t=1106s)

Ramona Shelburne: "I really think he was done with the Lakers after that last game. I don't think there was anything they could have done differently except for maybe give him an extension last year or give him a third year on the first extension he signed. He did a one-and-one, I think he wanted three years, they weren't willing to do that because they wanted it to line up with Luka or they wanted the salary cap flexibility that summer. I don't know if they had Luka yet. But I think that fate was sealed last August."
