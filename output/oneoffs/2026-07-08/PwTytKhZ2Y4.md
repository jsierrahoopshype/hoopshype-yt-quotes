**2. Austin Dufault — "One thing we saw with Noa as far as his ability to make an impact is versatility." — Austin Dufault on Noa Essengue's versatility and potential impact** [02:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PwTytKhZ2Y4&t=121s)

Austin Dufault: "As a coaching staff, I think one thing we saw with Noa as far as his ability to make an impact when he steps on the floor is just his versatility. Prior to his injury, I think Noa showed a lot of flashes. We were really excited about what was going to happen. Offensively, he can do a lot of different things. He can shoot the ball, attack closeouts. The size that he brings, how long he is, athletic he is, he is able to cover a ton of ground."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PwTytKhZ2Y4&t=121s

**3. Michael Orr — "Once you anchor it down, it has to heal. Your body has to adhere to it." — Michael Orr explains the surgical and physical therapy process for shoulder injuries** [03:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PwTytKhZ2Y4&t=195s)

Michael Orr: "Initially, so when the labrum with the kind of the gasket or cup around the shoulder that keeps everything intact, when it peels off or if it gets torn, they first have to kind of tack it back down. So they are using anchors and surgical technique to anchor that back down. Once you anchor it down, it has to heal. Your body has to adhere to it. So there is a period of less mobility that you have to keep the shoulder in a certain position, below shoulder height where you are not pulling on the structure that was just repaired."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PwTytKhZ2Y4&t=195s
