# Giannis says he's scared of change after trade to Miami & is open to return to Milwaukee — *Chaz NBA*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLakRikJ8wY

_Speakers identified: Jim Paschke, Giannis Antetokounmpo_

**1. Giannis Antetokounmpo — "Hopefully when people see me and think about me, they think about the things that I've done" — Giannis reflects on his legacy and impact in Milwaukee** [00:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLakRikJ8wY&t=13s)

Giannis Antetokounmpo: "Hopefully when people see me and think about me, they think about the things that I've done for the team and things that I've done for the city and how I carried myself and how I grew in this city and hopefully they remember those moments then what other people say I want."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLakRikJ8wY&t=13s

**2. Giannis Antetokounmpo — "I'm scared of change. I'm scared that the grass is not always greener." — Giannis admits fear of change and leaving Milwaukee** [01:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLakRikJ8wY&t=74s)

Giannis Antetokounmpo: "I'm scared. I'm scared of change. I'm scared that the grass is not always greener. I have things, people love me here, people respect me here, people allow me to be myself here in the city and I'm scared that I might never find that ever again. But there's something that I wake up every single day and I think I'm like, maybe I've waited too long."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLakRikJ8wY&t=74s

**3. Giannis Antetokounmpo — "You'll always be welcomed here." — Giannis discusses the possibility of returning to Milwaukee** [02:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLakRikJ8wY&t=150s)

**Jim Paschke:** "I would say that you could always come home."

**Giannis Antetokounmpo:** "That would be awesome. You've earned that. You'll always be welcomed here. That would be awesome. That would be awesome if I can go and play and maybe come back. **Kevin Garnett** did the same thing. I saw this clip of him walking into the arena and they gave him a standing ovation and people to this day love him."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLakRikJ8wY&t=150s

**4. Giannis Antetokounmpo — "It's different when you change your life for a better future for your family." — Giannis explains his motivation for potential team changes** [03:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLakRikJ8wY&t=185s)

Giannis Antetokounmpo: "Change is scary, Mr. Jim. Change is scary, it's not easy. It's different when you change your life for a better future for your family. I wasn't scared then. My brothers will have a better education, my mom and my dad will have a better life because they deserve it, they worked hard, they sacrificed for us. I was like, I'm going to do this."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLakRikJ8wY&t=185s

**5. Giannis Antetokounmpo — "The city of Milwaukee is a blue-collar city." — Giannis on his connection to the city of Milwaukee** [03:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLakRikJ8wY&t=234s)

Giannis Antetokounmpo: "If the city of Milwaukee and the state of Wisconsin, I want them to remember this from me. Don't worry about the basket, don't worry about the championship, don't worry about the winnings, don't worry about the losses. I try to be like them. I believe the city of Milwaukee is a blue-collar city. It's people that work extremely hard every single day, nine to five, and pay $10, $5, $15 to come watch the Milwaukee Bucks and the Brewers and other organizations play. They give all their hard-earned money to the Bucks to come and feel something, to come to be a part of us. I hope that I was able to represent them in the best that I could."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLakRikJ8wY&t=234s
