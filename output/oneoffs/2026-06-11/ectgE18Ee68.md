**1. Anthony Slater — "they're heading into next year with both of their starting wings rehabing serious knee injuries" — Warriors face a difficult half-measure rebuild with aging stars** [01:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ectgE18Ee68&t=70s)

**Michael Wallace:** "With **Steve Kerr** confirming that he's back, with **Steph Curry** still around, this is a win-now team as long as those two guys are here. But how much of this is a pivot sort of off-season in terms of trying to get some assets and some talent to bolster that depth that they need?"

**Anthony Slater:** "It's where they're kind of in this half-measure rebuild, which I think has actually become a little bit more appealing with the new lottery odds. They want to, as they say, honor the final **Steph Curry** years. He just turned 38. They still have veterans that he's comfortable with, obviously **Draymond Green**. But they're heading into next year with both of their starting wings rehabing serious knee injuries. **Jimmy Butler** coming off an ACL, I don't think we'll see him until January, February. **Moses Moody**, their other starting wing, **Toryes Patella** late in the season. I don't know that we'll see him at all next season."

**2. Anthony Slater — "this is a franchise that needs some youth infusion" — Warriors need youth infusion rather than marginal veteran improvements** [03:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ectgE18Ee68&t=210s)

**Michael Wallace:** "Is this team set up to go young? Is this maybe the best use of this #11 pick might be in a trade scenario?"

**Anthony Slater:** "The big thing is they don't want to just try to get marginally better by giving up their best young asset to go get a decent veteran who will help as a seventh man. Because the seventh man for what? Maybe you get the six seed, but you're probably a first round and out type of team. So I don't think they're just going to use it for a small improvement. If it's part of a package that gets them Kawhi, Giannis, a big-time franchise-changing maneuver, it'll certainly be available. But assuming that they can't get that done, this is a franchise that needs some youth infusion. Yes, big whiff on the **James Wiseman** pick. I would say less of a whiff on the Kuminga pick. I like the talent, but obviously the fit was wacky. **Mike Dunleavy** has drafted differently the last few years. I think he's hit with some guys in the 50s who now can help the back end of the rotation, Will Richard, for example, Quinten Post, and also the 19th pick **Brandin Podziemski** a couple years ago. That was good value. If you redraft that, he's probably 8, 9, 10. So this is **Mike Dunleavy**'s biggest swing at it since he's got the GM job."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ectgE18Ee68&t=210s

**3. Anthony Slater — "I go Brayden Burries from Arizona. And the truth is as you look at this draft there's a lot of pretty good options" — Brayden Burries is a high-value draft target for Warriors** [06:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ectgE18Ee68&t=371s)

Anthony Slater: "I go **Brayden Burries** from Arizona. And the truth is as you look at this draft and you look at who's still available even in your mock draft, there's a lot of pretty good options. And I know part of the reason the Warriors don't necessarily want to move this pick is because this is a high-value 11th pick. Burries is a kid who played at Arizona, was really good on a winning team, which I know matters to them. He fit within a winning culture. It wasn't every game high usage rate, it's all about his numbers. It was some defense, some shooting, some secondary playmaking. Some stuff that would be needed in the immediate in the Warriors environment and then growth potential beyond. And I will say **Steve Kerr**, huge Arizona fan, went to a few games, I know likes this kid, just from being a fan of watching Arizona. And then I know **Mike Dunleavy** in that front office scouted him a few times in person."
