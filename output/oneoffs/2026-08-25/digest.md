## How Does the Negativity Affect Me? Q&A With Brandin Podziemski! — *Brandin Podziemski*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fhe21TPY_rk

_Speakers identified: Brandin Podziemski_

**1. Brandin Podziemski — "the culture that Steph, Stephen, and Draymond have built over the years and the sustained winning" — Brandin Podziemski on the winning culture built by the Warriors** [00:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fhe21TPY_rk&t=18s)

Brandin Podziemski: "I think just the people, the organization, being around some of the greatest fans in the world, the culture that Steph, Stephen, and Draymond have built over the years and the sustained winning. I think that's very important as a young player to be introduced to a winning culture and to carry that forward."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fhe21TPY_rk&t=18s

**2. Brandin Podziemski — "the charge I took my rookie year against Shaden Sharp to win the game in Portland" — Brandin Podziemski on the most significant play of his career** [02:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fhe21TPY_rk&t=145s)

Brandin Podziemski: "Probably the charge I took my rookie year against **Shaden Sharp** to win the game in Portland. It's probably the most significant."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fhe21TPY_rk&t=145s

**3. Brandin Podziemski — "a lot of nuance and detail that they focus on and really hone in on" — Brandin Podziemski on learning from Warriors veterans** [03:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fhe21TPY_rk&t=192s)

Brandin Podziemski: "I think there's just a lot of nuance and detail that they focus on and really hone in on in different facets. **Chris Paul** from a point guard perspective, Draymond from a defense perspective and a communication and being a leader, and then Klay and Steph just being able to be in elite shape and shoot the ball the way they do. Seeing their daily processes and how they go about things, you start to understand and make sense of why they are Hall of Famers."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fhe21TPY_rk&t=192s

**4. Brandin Podziemski — "it was actually easier for me because the floor is bigger, your teammates are more talented" — Brandin Podziemski on adjusting to NBA defense versus college** [03:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fhe21TPY_rk&t=238s)

Brandin Podziemski: "I think it was actually easier for me because the floor is bigger, your teammates are more talented and more skilled. Obviously I played and still do play with the greatest shooter of all time, so it gives more space to operate. So I don't think there was a big mental adjustment or anything like that. It was just getting reps and repetitions over and over again and seeing different things in different games and just going from there."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fhe21TPY_rk&t=238s

**5. Brandin Podziemski — "people's opinions outside the people in my circle are pretty irrelevant" — Brandin Podziemski on ignoring outside negativity** [05:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fhe21TPY_rk&t=340s)

Brandin Podziemski: "The easy answer for me is just being process-oriented and understanding that people's opinions outside the people in my circle are pretty irrelevant. They don't see me on the day-to-day, what I go through. So to let someone affect my perception on basketball or my game, I just don't let it affect me."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fhe21TPY_rk&t=340s

**6. Brandin Podziemski — "all the things that come my way both good or bad are things that he's put in place" — Brandin Podziemski on incorporating faith into his athletic career** [06:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fhe21TPY_rk&t=372s)

Brandin Podziemski: "I think it's important. I think everybody is their own individual when it comes to that. But just knowing that everything I'm able to do with my platform is through him and understanding that all the things that come my way both good or bad are things that he's put in place on my journey. And that's kind of what goes back to the first question, why I don't let negativity that surrounds you in your game and I stay numb to it, because I know it's all a part of my journey."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fhe21TPY_rk&t=372s
