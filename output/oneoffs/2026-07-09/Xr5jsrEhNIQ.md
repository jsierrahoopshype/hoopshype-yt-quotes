# Marc Stein on LeBron's Free Agency, the Lakers' Future & the Jaylen Brown Trade | A Must-Win Game — *A Must Win Game with Maggie Gray *

_Speakers identified: Maggie Gray, Marc Stein_

**1. Marc Stein — "within the chasing pack, the feel there is that Cleveland is the scenario to beat" — Cleveland is the team to beat in the LeBron James sweepstakes** [01:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xr5jsrEhNIQ&t=71s)

Marc Stein: "Within the chasing pack, the feel there is that Cleveland is the scenario to beat. But based on what we know, it is not just going to be basketball. It is not just looking at the field and saying this is my best chance for ring number five. That does not seem to be the way they are approaching this. I think one of Rich Paul's quotes was, happiness is the priority and it is a bucket of happiness. There is a bunch of things in the bucket and I think that is why these other teams fear the Cavs the most because the Cavs do have the storybook ending. The Cavs have the heartstrings ending that really nobody else can offer."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xr5jsrEhNIQ&t=71s

**2. Marc Stein — "LeBron held up the whole league that summer. Nothing of significance could move in free agency" — LeBron James' 2014 free agency decision held up the entire league** [04:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xr5jsrEhNIQ&t=270s)

Marc Stein: "I covered so closely and I was actually on the set, July 11th, 2014. **Jay Crawford** was the host, **Chris Broussard**, **Brian Windhorst** and me. We were on live TV when LeBron threw the Sports Illustrated essay and announced that he was going back to the Cavs. That was July 11th and LeBron held up the whole league that summer. Nothing of significance could move in free agency until LeBron decided. This time, we are almost certainly going past July 11th, but it is different because 90% of the off-season business is done. The bulk of the moves and signings and trades are done. And also, LeBron is not going for cap space. It is not like teams that have hoarded max salary slots aside to try to lure him. So he can take his time."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xr5jsrEhNIQ&t=270s

**3. Marc Stein — "you can't commute in the strictest sense of the word from Los Angeles to Golden State" — The Warriors' ability to 'sneak home' is a major advantage for LeBron** [07:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xr5jsrEhNIQ&t=456s)

Marc Stein: "Los Angeles has been his home and to me, speaking purely as an observer and analyst, I thought that was one of the Warriors' advantages here. That the Warriors have players he loves, **Stephen Curry**, **Draymond Green**. Obviously, **Draymond Green**, he might be as close to as anyone in the league. A coach he respects and has already played for at the Olympics in **Steve Kerr**. And you cannot commute in the strictest sense of the word from Los Angeles to Golden State, but you can sneak home a lot. The Warriors I am sure would help him. Hey, game tonight, no practice tomorrow, just go back to LA. And I thought that was something that was really a feather in Golden State's cap."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xr5jsrEhNIQ&t=456s

**4. Marc Stein — "Walker Kessler was a restricted free agent and the way restricted free agency works, you have to overpay" — The Lakers paid a steep price to acquire Walker Kessler** [14:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xr5jsrEhNIQ&t=883s)

Marc Stein: "**Walker Kessler** was a restricted free agent and the way restricted free agency works in the NBA, if you want to swipe a restricted free agent away from a team, you have to overpay. And the Lakers paid a very steep price to get **Walker Kessler**. But they are of the belief that he is going to prove to be the best center who has ever played with **Luka Doncic**. We know **Luka Doncic** and **Austin Reaves** have formed a very strong partnership. So as a trio, they look like they have strong potential and I imagine Luka is quite pleased to know that he has really the best center who was available to the Lakers."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xr5jsrEhNIQ&t=883s

**5. Marc Stein — "there was as little of a personal relationship as could possibly be" — The personal relationship between Jaylen Brown and Jayson Tatum** [18:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xr5jsrEhNIQ&t=1121s)

**Maggie Gray:** "Now we are getting reporting from Shams and I would be curious to get your thoughts on it. Just the relationship between **Jaylen Brown** and **Jayson Tatum**. Shams reported there was as little of a personal relationship as could possibly be. Is that what you were also hearing from your sources?"

**Marc Stein:** "I got to say, I have been more focused on the Celtics' rationale, which we still don't have great answers for. Now, I will say this, the Celtics on the first allowable day, Brad Stevens and Bill Chism, their new majority owner, they did a press conference. They sat there for 45 minutes, they took every question, which was great. Again, to rewind to Dallas, the Mavericks traded Luka Doncic on a late Saturday night before the trade deadline with no warning. They were on the road in Cleveland. They did a press availability in Cleveland for the reporters who were traveling. But when you make a trade of that magnitude, you come back to town and you do a press conference in the city where you operate. And that didn't happen for months. And so the questions couldn't even be answered."
