**1. Dejounte Murray — "I'm excited to just be around and see him lead by example" — Dejounte Murray on the culture Jamal Mosley is building** [00:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=raIxZZbSmLE&t=32s)

**Cassidy Hubbarth:** "You've played for a lot of different coaches and now you're getting to play for a new coach in **Jamal Mosley**. What have you noticed about the culture he's building for you guys?"

**Dejounte Murray:** "It's always been mutual respect between me and him, especially when I was in Atlanta. We played Orlando a lot, so we're familiar with each other. He's a great guy. I'm excited to just be around and see him lead by example because I always feel like a head coach has to be the one that's leading by example and then everything falls into place. I'm just excited to be around. Like I tell him, the only way we could grow together is being together."

**2. Dejounte Murray — "The main thing about it is health, health is wealth" — Dejounte Murray on the Pelicans' potential and health** [01:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=raIxZZbSmLE&t=81s)

**Cassidy Hubbarth:** "What is it feeling like amongst you guys? You're sitting next to Zion, the future is bright, it's young. What are you noticing about what your squad can be this year?"

**Dejounte Murray:** "The main thing about it is health, health is wealth. Everybody got to be healthy, I think that's number one. Then at the end of the day, you just want a bunch of competitors on the floor, play together and just want to see everybody succeed. Off the floor, it's mandatory you got to be a great person, and when you mix all that together, great things can happen."

**3. Dejounte Murray — "I was able to come back due to the work I put in, believing in God" — Dejounte Murray on his recovery from Achilles injury** [02:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=raIxZZbSmLE&t=125s)

Dejounte Murray: "I was able to come back due to the work I put in, believing in God, most importantly. But I think it was all about the work. I'm dedicated, I'm passionate about it. I'm just excited to have a summer where I'm healthy working and then get to open gym, get to training camp, and preseason, and then get going. No limits, it's ready to go."

**5. Dejounte Murray — "You got to be on time, you got to be the ultimate pro, put your work in" — Dejounte Murray on leadership and accountability for the Pelicans** [03:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=raIxZZbSmLE&t=216s)

**Cassidy Hubbarth:** "You're sitting next to Z there, you guys are the veteran leaders now of this group. How do you feel like you can lead the two of you this Pelicans squad this year?"

**Dejounte Murray:** "You got to lead by example. You got to be on time, you got to be the ultimate pro, put your work in day in, day out, take care of your body. Those unseen hours, when you're in your hotel room, when you're on the recovery table, you got to take care of yourself so we're available. The best ability is availability, and I understand that. Hopefully Z understand that. He's doing really great, I think he feels the same way of just being healthy and being with whoever he's working with and just trying to be consistent and put the work in. We're going to need everybody, it's not about one, two, or three guys, you're going to need the whole organization."
