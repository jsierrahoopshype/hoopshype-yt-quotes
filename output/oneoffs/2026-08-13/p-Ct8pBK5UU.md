**1. Doc Rivers — "they tell you a time period. Like, hey, the Hall is going to call between 9 and 11." — Doc Rivers on the Hall of Fame selection process** [00:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p-Ct8pBK5UU&t=25s)

Doc Rivers: "So funny, Marc, because they tell you a time period. Like, hey, the Hall is going to call between 9 and 11. That call doesn't mean it's a good call. **2. Doc Rivers — "college coaches get to coach and be called Hall of Fame coach, where NBA coaches rarely get that." — Doc Rivers on the prestige of being a Hall of Fame coach** [01:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p-Ct8pBK5UU&t=93s)

Doc Rivers: "I wanted to get in while I was still coaching and I achieved that. I really fought early on for Pop, **Steve Kerr**, Spo, myself. I just think college coaches get to coach and be called Hall of Fame coach, where NBA coaches rarely get that."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p-Ct8pBK5UU&t=93s

**3. Doc Rivers — "I think Rajon Rondo is going to be a hell of a coach." — Doc Rivers' evaluation of Rajon Rondo as a future coach** [02:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p-Ct8pBK5UU&t=143s)

Doc Rivers: "I think **Rajon Rondo** is going to be a hell of a coach. Now, he's a savant, and that a lot of times doesn't go well at times because he sees it. We talk about it all the time. He said, man, you're a hell of a point guard coach. I said, yeah, I was a good point guard. I wasn't a hell of a point guard. I was a solid point guard. And he's like, coach, you see it and they don't. How do you handle that? I said, you breathe. Because you learn patience."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p-Ct8pBK5UU&t=143s

**4. Doc Rivers — "part of the word in Ubuntu is that you don't live in isolation." — Doc Rivers on the importance of collective success** [03:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p-Ct8pBK5UU&t=188s)

Doc Rivers: "Part of the word in Ubuntu is that you don't live in isolation. And that's what I teach. If you do anything in life, you didn't do it alone. So my speech should be more about the people who helped me get there and less about me. That's where I'm headed today. Or I may get up there like Snoop and say I want to thank me."
