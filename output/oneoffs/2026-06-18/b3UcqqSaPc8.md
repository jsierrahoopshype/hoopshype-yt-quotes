**1. Jose Alvarado — "You know how hard it is to make it to the NBA, especially in my size?" — Jose Alvarado on the difficulty of making the NBA at his size** [00:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b3UcqqSaPc8&t=44s)

Jose Alvarado: "I'm also ruining myself. You know how hard it is to make it to the NBA, especially in my size? I knew that was the X-factor of a lot of stuff, but I obviously put that behind me once I started getting my feet wet and putting myself in the rooms where people are making it to the NBA and I'm like, I could do this. **2. DJ Envy — "When you were undrafted, did you know that I'm going to have to put in this extra work?" — DJ Envy and Jose Alvarado on his undrafted mindset** [01:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b3UcqqSaPc8&t=79s)

**DJ Envy:** "On TV, you look way smaller than six-foot. When you were undrafted, did you know that I'm going to have to put in this extra work or did you think I was going to have to go overseas? What was the mindset then, because your whole life was basketball?"

**Jose Alvarado:** "I didn't really have no second option. No plan B, I swear. But my junior year I had my daughter, so going into my senior year, I'm like, I need to figure something out. Money-wise, at least financially-wise. I was living with her parents and I was 22 years old asking my dad for money. So it was a lot going on. When I was getting my feet wet with this, I was like, I'm going undrafted, I was going overseas. Whoever is my agent, let's try to get this money overseas and build it up. And I was fine with that. I wasn't tripping."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b3UcqqSaPc8&t=79s

**3. Jose Alvarado — "I still got a chip on my shoulder because people don't understand like, I'm normal height." — Jose Alvarado on maintaining a chip on his shoulder** [02:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b3UcqqSaPc8&t=161s)

Jose Alvarado: "I still got a chip on my shoulder because people don't understand like, I'm normal height, I guess you could say, right? Or I don't know. I'm small in the NBA. I just knew every time I had to prove it. If you look down the line of my story, I never really had consistent minutes. I never really had consistent as a role. I always made it my way. Coaches always tell me, you're not going to play today or this is not going to go your way, but then the game needs some energy. They might have told me that before the game, but the game needed somebody to change it up and it was always me."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b3UcqqSaPc8&t=161s

**4. DJ Envy — "Would you take a short to stay with the Knicks, like Jalen Brunson did?" — DJ Envy and Jose Alvarado on taking a pay cut to stay with the Knicks** [04:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b3UcqqSaPc8&t=250s)

**DJ Envy:** "Would you take a short to stay with the Knicks, like **Jalen Brunson** did?"

**Jose Alvarado:** "It got to make sense though. **Jalen Brunson**, he took a short, but he knew at the back end that he's going to get that back. So I'm not going to get no 100 million back, so I got to see what makes sense for everybody. I do truly feel like if it's a great opportunity to stay home, I stay home."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b3UcqqSaPc8&t=250s

**5. Jose Alvarado — "We know he was going to be a problem, but we never had like, I ain't going to lie." — Jose Alvarado on the Pelicans' series against the Spurs** [04:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b3UcqqSaPc8&t=276s)

Jose Alvarado: "We know he was going to be a problem, but we never had like, I ain't going to lie, we won because we was on that. We knew we was like, whatever happens, we going to make this work.
