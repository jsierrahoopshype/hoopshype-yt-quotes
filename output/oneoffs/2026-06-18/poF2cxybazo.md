# Jorge Sedano Reacts "Could the Lakers land Jalen Duran & Peyton Watson?" — *ESPN LA*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=poF2cxybazo

_Speakers identified: Jorge Sedano, George Sedano, Scott Kapla_

**1. Jorge Sedano — "I think Jalen Duren has more upside than Peyton Watson." — Lakers potential pursuit of Jalen Duren and Peyton Watson** [00:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=poF2cxybazo&t=3s)

**Scott Kapla:** "Of the people that you are talking about here, **Jalen Duren**, **Peyton Watson**, if you are getting two of these, which two of these would you think would be the best that could actually fit? Would like **Jalen Duren** and **Peyton Watson** be your best overall? If you are going to go to LeBron and **Anthony Davis** to take less money, which two?"

**Jorge Sedano:** "If you are telling me they can afford them both, I think **Jalen Duren** has more upside than **Peyton Watson**. I think **Peyton Watson** is just okay offensively. He can be limited. I think **Jalen Duren**, despite his challenges this postseason, people forget, **Jalen Duren** is only 22 years old. He has so much growth to do professionally, emotionally, mentally, skill-wise. To me, he has a much higher ceiling than **Peyton Watson**."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=poF2cxybazo&t=3s

**2. Jorge Sedano — "The Lakers are interested in retaining Marcus Smart and sharp shooter Luke Kennard." — Lakers interest in retaining Marcus Smart and Luke Kennard** [03:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=poF2cxybazo&t=182s)

Jorge Sedano: "The Lakers are interested in retaining **Marcus Smart** and sharp shooter **Luke Kennard**. They believe that **Rui Hachimura** is probably gone. Not saying that it is a definitive, but they believe that Hachimura could be the odd man out and he could end up somewhere else."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=poF2cxybazo&t=182s

**3. Jorge Sedano — "Peyton Watson is a more well-rounded player while I don't believe he's as good a shooter as Rui." — Comparing Peyton Watson and Rui Hachimura as players** [04:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=poF2cxybazo&t=249s)

**Jorge Sedano:** "**Peyton Watson** is a more well-rounded player while I don't believe he's as good a shooter as **Rui Hachimura**. **Peyton Watson** is a better defender. I think he has more wiggle room to his offensive game than Rui has."

**Scott Kapla:** "**Peyton Watson** is also, just as we are talking about Jalen Duren, this is a kid who is 23 years old and was also drafted in 2022. But by the way, **Peyton Watson** is a good three-point shooter, turned into one, let's call it that. But he is younger and so when I keep saying to you it is a young man's game, look at how the Knicks are kind of all in their late 20s and in their primes."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=poF2cxybazo&t=249s

**4. Jorge Sedano — "You can build a team with LeBron on it that has younger, athletic players." — Building a competitive Lakers roster with LeBron James** [07:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=poF2cxybazo&t=423s)

Jorge Sedano: "You can build a team with **LeBron James** on it that has younger, athletic players who are veterans, who can continue to grow and have room to grow and have not hit their ceiling. You build a team that can win with LeBron, **Austin Reaves**, and **Luka Doncic**, and a team that is also still on the path to winning when LeBron is gone. You can do that.
