Tiago Splitter: "As a 15-year-old, because that is when I moved there, I was tall and not very skilled yet. I learned how to play basketball over there in Spain and how to be a professional. When you are at that age, 15, you are still very raw as a person as well. Tiago Splitter: "I signed a 10-year deal as a professional. I had to finish that contract over there and that is why I stayed three more years after I got drafted at 22. That contract was great at 15 years old, but at 22 it was not that good for me. I had to stay there and I had a good career. I am very grateful for everything I had in Spain. But at 25 I was kind of on the older side of a rookie. I was a little bit more experienced than the regular rookie, but also I was in a team like the San Antonio Spurs which was already competing for a championship. From the first day in the NBA I was already high performance, we got to win now. It is a different league, the athleticism is different, the quality of the players is different. It is a different game as well. I think the NBA because there is such quality here, the game becomes way more one-on-one, way more ISO. I had to adapt a little bit of the speed, the size of the players, the 82 games, the traveling."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oOmrJ8sa5JM&t=391s
