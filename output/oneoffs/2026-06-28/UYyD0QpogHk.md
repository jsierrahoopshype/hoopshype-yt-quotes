# Bobby Marks' insight on how LeBron's free agency impacts the Lakers | SportsCenter — *NBA on ESPN*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UYyD0QpogHk

_Speakers identified: Randy Scott, Bobby Marks_

**1. Bobby Marks — "a Laker team that's potentially sitting on 50 million dollars in cap space to go out" — How LeBron James' free agency impacts the Lakers' salary cap** [00:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UYyD0QpogHk&t=23s)

**Randy Scott:** "LeBron about to become a free agent here, how does a potential deal impact the Lakers' free agency outlook?"

**Bobby Marks:** "It's significant. A lot of it has to do with the timing of **LeBron James**. Here is a Laker team that's potentially sitting on 50 million dollars in cap space to go out and use in free agency, and that does not include **LeBron James**. The number LeBron comes in at, if he returns to the Lakers, will play a significant role as far as what they have to go out and spend."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UYyD0QpogHk&t=23s

**2. Bobby Marks — "Can the Lakers wait or do they have to go out and get their business done" — Lakers' free agency timeline if LeBron James delays his decision** [00:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UYyD0QpogHk&t=55s)

**Randy Scott:** "What happens if there's not a decision by July 1st or July 2nd?"

**Bobby Marks:** "As we know, free agency is usually done in 48 hours. Can the Lakers wait or do they have to go out and get their business done without a commitment from James?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UYyD0QpogHk&t=55s

**3. Bobby Marks — "There's trade suitors out there and he's owed 183 million dollars over the next three years" — Challenges in trading for Celtics star Jaylen Brown** [01:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UYyD0QpogHk&t=77s)

**Randy Scott:** "How realistic is a trade considering both the talent and that contract?"

**Bobby Marks:** "There's trade suitors out there and he's owed 183 million dollars over the next three years. He had signed that supermax contract a few years ago. I think a couple things complicate it with the trade, and just based on where this collective bargaining agreement is, roster construction. You've got to have the right talent around **Jaylen Brown** or another player making that significant amount of money, and what's left of the roster, what you're trading out here."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UYyD0QpogHk&t=77s
