**1. Donovan Mitchell — "Everything you see now here with me, I wanted to do with baseball." — Donovan Mitchell on his transition from baseball to basketball** [02:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ag6YSWHSdsk&t=120s)

**Browney:** "Donovan actually didn't play basketball when he was young."

**Donovan Mitchell:** "I played baseball my whole life. I wanted to be a baseball player. Everything you see now here with me, I wanted to do with baseball. Because Mitchell's dad was actually a really good baseball player. My sophomore year of high school, I break my wrist. One day I'm just like, I want to hoop. I had three college offers when I made that decision, and the year after I had 26."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ag6YSWHSdsk&t=120s

**2. Donovan Mitchell — "I'm trying to go as low as I can so I can go as high as I can for camp." — Donovan Mitchell on his caloric intake and training routine** [07:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ag6YSWHSdsk&t=461s)

**Browney:** "Do you know how many calories you're eating?"

**Donovan Mitchell:** "In the day, typically, I'm trying to get before the camp, like before we got to camp earlier this week, I was trying to get to 900. I'm trying to go as low as I can so I can go as high as I can for camp. I've gotten to 900 four times this week."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ag6YSWHSdsk&t=461s

**3. Donovan Mitchell — "If I'm coming off this way, that low man wing is coming over hard as hell." — Donovan Mitchell on the importance of reading defenders' eyes** [09:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ag6YSWHSdsk&t=559s)

**Browney:** "How important are your eyes on the defenders?"

**Donovan Mitchell:** "If I'm coming off this way, I come off this way, that low man wing is coming over hard as hell. I make the corner, I throw that to the corner, right? Makes sense?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ag6YSWHSdsk&t=559s

**4. Donovan Mitchell — "There's nothing that I haven't seen. It's just a matter of how you solve it." — Donovan Mitchell on his experience reading game situations** [09:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ag6YSWHSdsk&t=585s)

**Browney:** "Would you say you've basically seen every single situation you could be in?"

**Donovan Mitchell:** "There's nothing that I haven't seen. It's just a matter of how you solve it. And some teams do it at a higher level, at a better level than others, but there's nothing out here that I haven't seen."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ag6YSWHSdsk&t=585s

**5. Donovan Mitchell — "If you're not losing the ball, you're not going hard enough." — Donovan Mitchell on the necessity of playing hard in drills** [11:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ag6YSWHSdsk&t=665s)

**Donovan Mitchell:** "One thing I've learned is that if you're not losing the ball, you're not going hard enough. Make sure you're going hard enough that you lose it four or five times. It's not a sign that your ball handling is weak. It's just a sign that you are going hard."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ag6YSWHSdsk&t=665s

**6. Donovan Mitchell — "It's the competitive nature. That's what got me to this point." — Donovan Mitchell on the competitive nature of making the NBA** [17:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ag6YSWHSdsk&t=1026s)

**Donovan Mitchell:** "It's the competitive nature. That's what got me to this point. So if I'm doing golf, I believe in my mind that I'm Tiger Woods. You gotta have that mindset. I had 16,000 college athletes, only 500 guys that make it to the NBA. So for me, to believe that I can get there, you have to be crazy."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ag6YSWHSdsk&t=1026s

**7. Donovan Mitchell — "I think on TV it looks way slower than actually in real life." — Donovan Mitchell on the difference between TV and real-life basketball** [18:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ag6YSWHSdsk&t=1128s)

**Browney:** "What is one of the biggest basketball clichés people get wrong?"

**Donovan Mitchell:** "I think people think it's way easier. It's way slower than actually in real life. If you see it in real life, you realize how fast the game is actually going. I think on TV it looks way slower."
