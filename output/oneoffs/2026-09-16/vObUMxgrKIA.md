# Taj Gibson is honored to return to Chicago as a new Bulls assistant coach | Spiegel & Holmes — *104.3 The Score*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vObUMxgrKIA

_Speakers identified: Matt Spiegel, Danny Parkins, Taj Gibson_

**1. Taj Gibson — "I'm so blessed, I'm so thankful for this organization to even give me a chance." — Taj Gibson on his new role as a Bulls assistant coach** [00:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vObUMxgrKIA&t=36s)

Taj Gibson: "It just feels like something has a hold of me and it's just directing me to my next path. The thing about basketball when you are going through your career, you always want to be around awesome people and try to make something out of nothing. I'm so blessed, I'm so thankful for this organization to even give me a chance. Words can't explain how blessed I am and how happy I am to be back in Chicago."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vObUMxgrKIA&t=36s

**2. Taj Gibson — "I was around a great system for nurturing players and I was around a great team." — Taj Gibson on the Bulls' team culture and his teammates** [01:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vObUMxgrKIA&t=75s)

**Danny Parkins:** "For you, throughout your career, no matter where you were, every roster and every fan base appreciated what you brought to the table. But here in Chicago, I'm curious whether or not you felt that from Chicago fans and what it was like to not be the superstar? You're the guy that's there helping the superstar, in some cases being the bodyguard for the superstar. Did you feel that appreciation that Bulls fans seemingly have for you?"

**Taj Gibson:** "Without a question. One of the best things about being in Chicago around the time I got here, I got to be here around a young **Derek Rose**, a young **Joakim Noah**, a whole young group of guys. I was around Kurt Hinrich. I was around a great system for nurturing players and I was around a great team of just great people. A lot of my career is just being known as that guy that's always there in the different utilities, but I give a big testament to my career to my teammates always having my back."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vObUMxgrKIA&t=75s

**3. Taj Gibson — "Stacy is like the uncle for me because that's how solid he was from." — Taj Gibson on his relationship with Stacy King** [04:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vObUMxgrKIA&t=296s)

Taj Gibson: "What I remember about **Stacy King** is first, he was just that veteran that was there for me. He was that guy that I could always ask him questions and he would always give me the greatest answers because it's not often when you can get to a league and you have a veteran that's kind of been in your position where he's coming off the bench, he's doing all the different things and he understands how to be professional. But Stacy took me right underneath the wing. A lot of my highlights, he gave me my nickname. Stacy is like the uncle for me because that's how solid he was from."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vObUMxgrKIA&t=296s

**4. Taj Gibson — "When we look at a real OG vet, you can always see the scars on him." — Taj Gibson on the importance of veteran leadership in locker rooms** [08:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vObUMxgrKIA&t=516s)

Taj Gibson: "When we look at a real OG vet, you can always see the scars on him from a mile away. Just how he carries himself, how he carries his mannerisms, and how he speaks to people. One thing I noticed, the rookies or all the young players, they always respected me because I was always trying to better my teammates. I was always challenging my teammates and I was always trying to teach them the ways that I had to get taught when I came into the NBA. I was teaching them different attributes of like a Derek Rose or a Russell Westbrook."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vObUMxgrKIA&t=516s

**5. Taj Gibson — "Once you get this age fellas, you kind of let everybody just do their thing." — Taj Gibson on mentoring young players across his career** [10:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vObUMxgrKIA&t=606s)

Taj Gibson: "Once you get this age fellas, you kind of let everybody just do their thing. You're the oldest guy in the locker room, you're the oldest guy in the class. I never tried to go into any locker room or any place and just try to put down 17 years. I always acted as if I was just a new guy in the class and you would get to know me through the ups and downs of the season. And then once we got through that, you would understand it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vObUMxgrKIA&t=606s

**6. Taj Gibson — "Derek Rose is a first-class human being. Before basketball, he's the kind of guy you want." — Taj Gibson on Derek Rose as a person and leader** [13:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vObUMxgrKIA&t=827s)

Taj Gibson: "Forget all the attributes, forget all the MVP, forget all the scoring and all that. **Derek Rose** is a first-class human being. Before basketball, he's the kind of guy you want to give your shirt off your back for. That's how he is and he was a leader amongst men. That just speaks a lot. I try to tell guys, being a point guard in this league, you're going to be looking at a lot of guys and guys are going to be looking at you like where to next."
