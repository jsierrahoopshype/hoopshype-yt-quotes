# Dirk Nowitzki On Broadcasting, The New Era Mavs, Cooper Flagg & More | Shan & RJ — *105.3 The Fan*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4uemCmTglAE

_Speakers identified: Shan Shariff, RJ Choppy, Dirk Nowitzki_

**1. Dirk Nowitzki — "It was hard at the beginning. I've been out obviously seven years out of the league." — Dirk Nowitzki on the challenges of transitioning to a media role** [01:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4uemCmTglAE&t=76s)

**Shan Shariff:** "I am dying to know how Amazon year one went on the media side. Did you love it? Are you like, I am in this for a long time? How was the media side full-time?"

**Dirk Nowitzki:** "It was hard at the beginning. I've been out obviously seven years out of the league, and so at the beginning, it was just trying to catch up. The players have turned over, the front offices, the coaching, and the style is all different. I played catch-up there for the first couple of weeks. I wanted to know all the teams that were on, so I was watching a lot of hoops and going back the next day and trying to analyze. I was freaking myself out a little bit there in the first couple of weeks."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4uemCmTglAE&t=76s

**2. Dirk Nowitzki — "We wanted to explain the game, we want to celebrate the game, and where it is." — Dirk Nowitzki on the Mavericks' new approach to broadcasting games** [02:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4uemCmTglAE&t=164s)

**Shan Shariff:** "Dirk, you guys were given a lot of credit for covering the game in a different way, which a lot of fans like, comparing it to some of the other network shows. Did you come up with a bunch of different ideas of the way that, I know you love the TNT guys, but did you brainstorm some ways of let's present it this way in a different way for basketball fans?"

**Dirk Nowitzki:** "It is great to be part of something new because they wanted our inputs. They were trying to pick our brains and see what we are wanting to do. Nobody can be TNT guys or now they are obviously on ESPN, but nobody is Charles and Shaq. They get away with saying stuff that we cannot say. We had a different approach. We wanted to use the court as much as we can with the LED court, and you can really walk through stuff and show some technical stuff, obviously not too deep, but deep enough for some fans to learn some stuff. We did not want to get too deep, but we do want to explain the game, we want to celebrate the game, and where it is, and not sit there and be miserable and say it was better in the old days."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4uemCmTglAE&t=164s

**3. Dirk Nowitzki — "Nowadays, if there is a two-on-one fast break, somebody is pulling up for three." — Dirk Nowitzki on the evolution of modern NBA playstyles** [04:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4uemCmTglAE&t=258s)

**RJ Choppy:** "Is that hard to do to stay away from the back in my day? Because the game is different, especially from when you first came in. The game is much, much different. Is it because we nobody wants to be a back in my day guy, we just kind of become that as you get older? I know I did. But was that difficult to stay away from that?"

**Dirk Nowitzki:** "You are just trying to see the game in a different light now, and you try to not sit there and say, man, he should have done this, I would have done this. Nowadays, if there is a two-on-one fast break, somebody is pulling up for three. Obviously, I would be like, what is he doing in the green room? But that is just part of the game now. The game is played different and these guys have a different mentality on how they see the game, and so that is part of our job is explaining that and staying up to date and analyzing that part that is just so different from back in the days."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4uemCmTglAE&t=258s

**4. Dirk Nowitzki — "The goal obviously now in the next couple years has to be build around Cooper." — Dirk Nowitzki on the Mavericks' roster and front office changes** [06:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4uemCmTglAE&t=396s)

**Shan Shariff:** "Dirk, they have obviously gone through a massive evolution over the last 12 months in terms of personnel and decision-makers, and it has been a painful at times growth process. How do you evaluate where the Mavericks are today structurally, roster-wise, and some of the broader changes they have made?"

**Dirk Nowitzki:** "It was time to move on, and hired Nico, who is a proven winner in this league and has turned around a bunch of franchises. He came in and did a bunch of changes, brought in a bunch of his own people, and brought a new GM, brought a new coach. Everything is kind of new, but I think the goal obviously now in the next couple years has to be build around Cooper. He has obviously shown in year one that he is ready, he is ready to take the franchise to the next level at age 19. Anything you do now is build around Cooper. They brought in some interesting international talent, Kyrie is coming back, I cannot wait to see how Kyrie and Cooper play off each other."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4uemCmTglAE&t=396s

**5. Dirk Nowitzki — "Some people say we should just do best of three, but honestly, like we do in other tournaments." — Dirk Nowitzki on the debate over five-set tennis matches** [14:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4uemCmTglAE&t=868s)

**RJ Choppy:** "Their CEO said it might be time to bring an end to five-set tennis. How do you feel about that?"

**Dirk Nowitzki:** "That is heavily discussed. I saw Djokovic came out and said he wants sets to four. Some people say we should just do best of three, but honestly, like we do in other tournaments, not only the Slams, all other tournaments are best of three. But if you look at some of these epic battles we just watched and Shelton had some incredible matches, I mean that is what Shelton Alcaraz, that is what people talk about, the five hours, the finish at 3:30 a.m. in the morning. That is the epic battles that people always remember and talk about. But I do get, it is a lot and five and a half hours to sit there to watch a match is incredibly long."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4uemCmTglAE&t=868s
