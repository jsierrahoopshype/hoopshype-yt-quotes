Stephen Curry: "The passion for the game and the understanding of what the game of golf provides in the sense of not just the competition, but the community. **3. Stephen Curry — "this is an expensive sport and it has been inaccessible for generations" — Stephen Curry on removing financial barriers for junior golfers** [04:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLhLyWcKV-Y&t=286s)

Stephen Curry: "This is an expensive sport and it has been inaccessible for generations when it comes to kids being able to stay on track and in that pipeline, especially through the competitive age from 13 to 18. If we can eliminate as many barriers as possible, giving kids access to coaching, equipment, apparel, travel, room and board when they are on the road, and for it to be a family thing, that hopefully encourages them to continue to work, to be free out there on the course and play, and hopefully there is some payoff when it comes to chasing golf scholarships at the next level."

Stephen Curry: "Having the balance of basketball and golf through high school stretched different sides of me. Just the idea of exploring and challenging myself in different areas, playing competitive golf at Charlotte Christian for three years, challenged me in a way mentally, physically, put me out of my comfort zone, and I think that has helped."

**5. Stephen Curry — "when I am in flow state, I am thinking about absolutely nothing" — Stephen Curry on the mental state of flow in golf** [09:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLhLyWcKV-Y&t=580s)

Stephen Curry: "Everybody says, 'What do you think about when you shoot?' And when I am in flow state, I am thinking about absolutely nothing. It is all reactive, it is rhythm, it is confidence, it is blocking out all the noise. The same thing, the rare times that I have been able to experience it in golf, it is kind of the same thing where you have a little swing tip on the range or something, but when you are out there on the course, it is more reactive when you are in that flow state, which is very hard to get to because of the pace of the game."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLhLyWcKV-Y&t=580s

**6. Stephen Curry — "I am still against the specialization kind of like before 13" — Stephen Curry on the dangers of youth sports specialization** [12:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLhLyWcKV-Y&t=772s)

Stephen Curry: "I am still against the specialization kind of like before 13, because that is how I grew up, playing every sport, whether it was organized teams or on the blacktop at school, recess, whatever. I was trying to expose myself to as many things, so basketball, baseball, football, and golf were my four that I rotated through until I was 13 seasonally. I know the landscape is a little different than 20 plus years ago in terms of the youth leagues and the demand of scheduling and the overlap of the competing events, but if you can get to that 13 level and really understand what it means to work and be passionate about one sport that you are going to give everything to, I feel like that is a great age where you kind of understand what it takes."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLhLyWcKV-Y&t=772s

**7. Stephen Curry — "every case is different to the point of had Roger said in that moment I am all about tennis" — Stephen Curry on the importance of autonomy in youth sports** [14:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLhLyWcKV-Y&t=874s)

Stephen Curry: "Every case is different to the point of had **Roger Federer** said in that moment I am all about tennis and that is all I want to play, I am sure they would have supported that too. But the optionality is huge. Especially the demand on a parent is whatever your kid does, you want them to be all in on it. And if that is seasonally with different sports, that means you are giving everything you have to the focus to the demand of that season and then when it is time to move on, you move on. There is not one size fits all situation."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLhLyWcKV-Y&t=874s
