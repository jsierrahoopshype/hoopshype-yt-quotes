# Cavs Media Day Interview | Donovan Mitchell and Serena Winters — *Rock Entertainment Sports Network*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l2Y2Qn0d2rU

_Speakers identified: Serena Winters, Donovan Mitchell_

**1. Donovan Mitchell — "actions speak louder than words" — Donovan Mitchell on why he signed his extension with the Cavaliers** [00:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l2Y2Qn0d2rU&t=3s)

**Serena Winters:** "You always talked about how much you loved Cleveland. So you sign an extension, it's no surprise. But I think the fact that you signed the extension right when you were eligible, it sends a message. What is that?"

**Donovan Mitchell:** "I think the biggest thing was actions speak louder than words. I was just talking to one of the radio guys about it and they were like, 'Why do you think people didn't believe you?' I said a lot of that probably predates me. There are a lot of things that have happened beforehand. For me, I get it. I'm not offended by those things, but for me, showing is better than telling. I'm here."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l2Y2Qn0d2rU&t=3s

**2. Donovan Mitchell — "It's so fragile. I think we know that being in this for as long as we've been." — Donovan Mitchell on the fragility of NBA playoff success** [01:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l2Y2Qn0d2rU&t=116s)

Donovan Mitchell: "It's so fragile. I think we know that being in this for as long as we've been. I think you know it's fragile, but I think you don't realize the margins. It's really two stops, or it's really another rebound, or it's one more sprint, or it's one more little thing. It's the first time I've been in the conference finals in my career, so I only know the first two rounds, and understanding that that's fragile. Losing Game 1 against Indiana at home was so fragile. It changes the whole thing."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l2Y2Qn0d2rU&t=116s

**3. Donovan Mitchell — "no matter how much experience you have in a locker room, time spent is key." — Donovan Mitchell on the importance of time spent together in the locker room** [04:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l2Y2Qn0d2rU&t=244s)

Donovan Mitchell: "The biggest thing is time spent. There's nothing that can cure that. No matter how much experience you have in a locker room, no matter how much you go through different things in a short amount of time, time spent is key. And that's just life, that's not just basketball. We made strides; we got to the conference finals, the farthest than we've ever gotten in my time here. But there was more to be had. Now, understanding the nuances, understanding each other—understanding **James**, **James** understanding me, even more so than I thought we did. Understanding **Evan**, understanding **JA**. Now we got **P-Wat** and **Mario**. Understanding what the younger guys like **Quan**, **JT**, **Tyrese**, what they can bring to the table, **Sam**. Having that whole summer, and some of it is just hanging out and talking shop—it's not even about being on the court. That goes such a long way."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l2Y2Qn0d2rU&t=244s

**4. Donovan Mitchell — "He brings a dynamic that I don't think we've had since I've been here." — Donovan Mitchell on the dynamic Max Strus brings to the Cavaliers** [06:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l2Y2Qn0d2rU&t=373s)

Donovan Mitchell: "He brings a dynamic that I don't think we've had since I've been here. You have an athletic wing who can shoot, who can handle, can defend, and can get on the rim. Playing in the half-roll, can pass. You saw what he was doing when **Jokic** was out last year. He's so dynamic. First and foremost, whether he brought us a lot or brought us nothing, I'm always going to welcome the new guy into the team just because I feel like it's different. Everybody's been with each other, everybody knows each other. Him and James talked a lot during that process, more so than him and I did. So to understand that, I'm equally as excited, we're all equally as excited. We want you to be here, we want you to be a part of this, and we see you not just for this year—this is a run that we're trying to go on here."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l2Y2Qn0d2rU&t=373s

**5. Donovan Mitchell — "It's the fine print, it's the little things that really get you to that next spot." — Donovan Mitchell on the importance of fine details for team improvement** [07:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l2Y2Qn0d2rU&t=466s)

Donovan Mitchell: "It's all in the details. People ask me what I'm working on, what must be working on something new. It's the fine print, it's the little things that really get you to that next spot. I've been doing this 10 years. I am who I am. We are who we are. We know who the Cavs are. James has been 17 years, JA's 10 years, Evan—we know. But it's the little—are we willing to do the little, the little details on a consistent basis? And some of that isn't a workout, some of that is mental. I think 'Finish' was Game 7 of Detroit, into uncharted territory. Not saying that the conference finals was the goal, but into uncharted territory. Now you achieve that goal, now it's so finite. It's the little details when you're most tired, when you're mentally fatigued, when you've played every other day for the past 25, 30 days. It's the little, little, little details."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l2Y2Qn0d2rU&t=466s
