**4. Patrick Beverley — "I don't think it's fair that you can quit the game of basketball and come back." — Patrick Beverley criticizes Ben Simmons' return to the Sacramento Kings** [07:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N2X5I_DTWFA&t=446s)

Patrick Beverley: "I don't think it's fair that you can quit the game of basketball and come back. You can quit when not having a ton of success. You can quit because the game is hard and it's stressful when you're not playing well and the fans are on you and the media is on you, you can quit and have an opportunity to come back. When we've been looking at **Victor Oladipo**, who's been competing, who's been degrading himself, went to the G League, we witnessed **Victor Oladipo** work out in front of a ton of coaches and GMs and writing letters. This is the guy you pick? No disrespect from **Ben Simmons**, you feel me? Like I'm not with quitting the game of basketball because it's hard at the time and because I'm not playing right, I don't feel right. Everywhere I go, it's an issue and an excuse. I'm injured, and I understand injury, but to quit the game of basketball and to come back to get an opportunity, and you got people like **Ben Simmons** who don't have a job, you got **Cam Thomas** who don't have a job, who like putting in that work year after year and like a guy can just quit and come back anytime. I don't I don't like that at all."
