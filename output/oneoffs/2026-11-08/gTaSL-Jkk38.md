**2. Dwight Powell — "You get used to being on your own, you get used to being misunderstood." — Dwight Powell on the sacrifices required from a young age** [00:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gTaSL-Jkk38&t=41s)

**4. Dwight Powell — "They feel like you reached your ceiling, which is kind of crazy to me." — Dwight Powell on the pressure of being a third or fourth-year player** [01:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gTaSL-Jkk38&t=110s)

Dwight Powell: "When you're a third-year player and even a fourth-year player, they feel like you reached your ceiling, which is kind of crazy to me because you're still 21, 22 years old. A lot of things that you go through that you've seen break other people or made other people quit or not want to push as hard or find an easier route, I stuck with it."
