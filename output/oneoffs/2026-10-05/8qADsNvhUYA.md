# What 10 Years in the NBA Looks Like: Dillon Brooks Media Day — *Dillon Brooks*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8qADsNvhUYA

_Speakers identified: Dillon Brooks, Cory Cash, Devin Booker, Kevin Durant, Bradley Beal, Josh Okogie, Jakob Owens_

**1. Dillon Brooks — "the life expectancy of a basketball player is very small, so I don't want to take it for granted" — Dillon Brooks on his career longevity and maintaining high performance** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8qADsNvhUYA&t=0s)

Dillon Brooks: "I want to keep going. I probably only got, I want 10 more years in this, but the life expectancy of a basketball player is very small, so I don't want to take it for granted or ease up, because that's the most human nature thing is to have a great year like this and then ease up. You see it all the time in the NBA. So, I just want to take this momentum and keep going with it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8qADsNvhUYA&t=0s

**2. Dillon Brooks — "I wanted to showcase who I am and what I can bring to a team" — Dillon Brooks on earning the Phoenix Suns' trust and culture** [03:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8qADsNvhUYA&t=189s)

Dillon Brooks: "It just goes to the work I put in during the year, the trust that they put in me from then, before even the contract was even a conversation. I wanted to gain that trust. I wanted to showcase who I am and what I can bring to a team, and I think the Suns really liked it, and we changed a culture together. So, when me signing the deal was inevitable, and I want to be here for a long time."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8qADsNvhUYA&t=189s

**3. Dillon Brooks — "you got to find something different than what everyone else is doing to put yourself out there" — Dillon Brooks on evolving his defensive approach and mental focus** [04:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8qADsNvhUYA&t=279s)

Dillon Brooks: "To begin with, to do something different. I feel like in the NBA, you got to find something different than what everyone else is doing to put yourself out there and to make yourself in singular ways. So, it was for intimidation, like whoever would look, I would just stare. And then it slowly got into me trying to be still in the moment and let my body and let my mind feel everything around. To look at a person that's so far away and just try to keep my eye on that one person. But now it's like more of calming, it's more of the same thing of like feeling the event, the intensity, the crowd, the chatter, the ball dribbling, people shooting around me, to be in okay with things around me and getting ready to be into the game."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8qADsNvhUYA&t=279s

**4. Dillon Brooks — "for me it was just a new beginning, I feel a lot more fresher a lot more better" — Dillon Brooks on his evolving hairstyle and new beginnings** [05:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8qADsNvhUYA&t=354s)

Dillon Brooks: "I kind of want to grow it back out in later years so I can get these nice little side braids. But for me it was just a new beginning, I feel a lot more fresher a lot more better and I don't got to sit in the chair every week to get my hair done for an hour, hour and a half."
