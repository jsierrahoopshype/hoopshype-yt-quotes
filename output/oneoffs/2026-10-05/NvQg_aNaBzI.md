**4. Collin Murray-Boyles — "Try not to let him get to the paint. I'm going to try to make his shots difficult." — Murray-Boyles on his approach to guarding Victor Wembanyama** [05:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvQg_aNaBzI&t=307s)

Collin Murray-Boyles: "I enjoyed those difficult matchups. Even at any matchup, there's always an advantage, either with my speed or my strength. When you guard **Victor Wembanyama**, obviously I can't reach and I can't really block the shot, but my approach is to be as physical as I can. Try not to let him get to the paint. I'm going to try to make his shots difficult as possible. He's 7-4, I'm not going to be able to contest his shots, so me trying to limit how many good, smooth shots he can get up is probably the only thing that I have to use against him."
