**3. Tim Connelly — "we're cautious and optimistic that we're going to see Dante this season" — Dante's injury recovery and outlook for the season** [02:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_YW9mQPz8wA&t=165s)

We don't want to put any added pressure on him, but we're cautious and optimistic that we're going to see **Dante** this season and we know how much he brings to our team."

**4. Tim Connelly — "Anthony's best chance to be the best player in the world is to be the best two-way player" — Anthony Edwards' potential to become an elite two-way player** [06:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_YW9mQPz8wA&t=374s)

Tim Connelly: "Anthony's best chance to be the best player in the world is to be the best two-way player in the world. His competition is so good and so talented to be in that mix of the top four or five guys to be an MVP candidate. I think he'd be the first to tell you at times last year, and some of it was our fault, we put him in positions where he had to do so much offensively. We think a lot of the ball-handling stuff will be out of his hands now with more traditional point guards next to him. But he's got to be a two-way player. He's got to be a lead on both ends."

**6. Tim Connelly — "there's very few guys in the NBA that can make offense easier for others" — Mike Conley's impact on team offense and spacing** [10:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_YW9mQPz8wA&t=621s)

Tim Connelly: "There's very few guys in the NBA that can make offense easier for others.
