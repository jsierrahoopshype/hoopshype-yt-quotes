# EXCLUSIVE: Walker Kessler on Luka's Court Vision - Mason & Ireland at Lakers Media Day — *ESPN LA*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FUQiNesUq8s

_Speakers identified: Mason, Ireland, Walker Kessler_

**1. Walker Kessler — "to hit the ground running on our chemistry is going to be really important this season." — Walker Kessler on building chemistry with the Lakers** [03:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FUQiNesUq8s&t=206s)

**Ireland:** "Are you starting to feel like there's some chemistry developing?"

**Walker Kessler:** "No question. I think starting with the Slovenia trip and just spending this time together in LA, playing pickup, I mean we've known each other for probably six weeks now and training camp hasn't even started. So to hit the ground running on our chemistry is going to be really important this season."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FUQiNesUq8s&t=206s

**2. Walker Kessler — "his court vision is unreal. There are so many times where I'll be near the basket." — Walker Kessler on Luka Doncic's elite court vision** [04:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FUQiNesUq8s&t=271s)

**Mason:** "Have you noticed anything about playing with **Luka** that maybe you didn't notice when you were playing against him?"

**Walker Kessler:** "You got to be ready to catch a ball no matter where you are on the floor. His court vision is unreal. There are so many times where I'll be near the basket and I'm like, he doesn't see me. I'm not, and then all of a sudden he'll just look this way and just throw it the other way and the ball will be right there. I'm basically touching the ball and it's going in."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FUQiNesUq8s&t=271s

**3. Walker Kessler — "you just try to make it as difficult as possible for them the whole night." — Walker Kessler on defending elite centers like Nikola Jokic and Victor Wembanyama** [05:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FUQiNesUq8s&t=315s)

**Ireland:** "You're going to run into some other centers, guys like **Jokic** and guys like **Wemby**. Tell me about how you get ready for those matchups and how do you defend **Wemby**?"

**Walker Kessler:** "**Wemby** is obviously a generational player, super talented, extremely hard worker, very skilled. I think guys like that, you just try to make it as difficult as possible for them the whole night. It's a long night and just getting scored on once or twice doesn't mean that you're not going to stop a couple shots and make it tougher or just expend their energy. So just making it as difficult as possible."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FUQiNesUq8s&t=315s

**4. Walker Kessler — "he's done it before. He's fantastic. Obviously on the court, unbelievable competitor, unbelievable rebounder." — Walker Kessler on the value of Kevon Looney as a veteran teammate** [05:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FUQiNesUq8s&t=359s)

**Mason:** "Does it help you that you got a veteran, a guy with a few rings in **Kevon Looney**?"

**Walker Kessler:** "He's done it before. He's fantastic. Obviously on the court, unbelievable competitor, unbelievable rebounder. Has done it before, the championship level. But off the court, unbelievable person as well. So just being around that is going to be really important to our success."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FUQiNesUq8s&t=359s
