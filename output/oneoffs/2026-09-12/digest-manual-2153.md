**1. Norman Powell — "the further you're back, the less you care because it's a deeper shot" — Norman Powell on his shooting mechanics and muscle memory** [07:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H9_EjWCdKng&t=443s)

**N3on:** "I feel like you shoot better further from the line. Why is that?"

**Norman Powell:** "I think the further you're back, the less you care because it's a deeper shot, so now you're just letting the muscle memory take over. When you're closer, you have defenders and you have more to think about. When you're back, guys aren't really guarding you because it's kind of a free shot."
