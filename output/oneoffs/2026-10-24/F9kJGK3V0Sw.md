# Blake, Dirk, Steve & UD speak on Jayson Tatum and Jaylen Brown "beef" — *Chaz NBA*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F9kJGK3V0Sw

_Speakers identified: Blake Griffin, Chiney Ogwumike, Dirk Nowitzki, Steve Nash, Udonis Haslem_

**1. Jayson Tatum — "I don't have any animosity or beef or ill will towards him" — Jayson Tatum denies any animosity toward Jaylen Brown** [00:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F9kJGK3V0Sw&t=19s)

Jayson Tatum: "I don't have any animosity or beef or ill will towards him. The frustrating part is since he's not with the Celtics anymore, the narrative or the conversations haven't been enough about what we accomplished, all the games we won, winning a championship, how we've grown together. It's turned into this drama type of thing. At this point in our lives, we're too old to be having our relationship discussed on the internet."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F9kJGK3V0Sw&t=19s

**2. Blake Griffin — "I just feel a little bit disappointed. You can hear the disappointment in his voice" — Blake Griffin expresses disappointment in the narrative surrounding Tatum and Brown** [01:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F9kJGK3V0Sw&t=78s)

Blake Griffin: "I just feel a little bit disappointed. You can hear the disappointment in his voice because he's exactly right. The narrative, the conversation should be these two guys were All-NBA, they were in the playoffs every year leading a franchise, winning a championship from 19 on. They were both 19 when they got drafted. Being their teammate, being inside the locker room, I know **Jayson Tatum** cares about him. I thought he said the right thing. He reached out to him after he got traded, called him, texted him, and everybody processes it in their own time."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F9kJGK3V0Sw&t=78s

**3. Udonis Haslem — "Don't give the world a satisfaction of watching what's going on between you guys" — Udonis Haslem urges Tatum and Brown to resolve their issues privately** [03:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F9kJGK3V0Sw&t=191s)

**Chiney Ogwumike:** "With that perspective, what do you want for those two?"

**Udonis Haslem:** "I want them to hash it out. I know it takes time and feelings are hurt and there's some frustration there, but you've been a critical part of each other's journey up until this point. Neither one of you guys are going to get to where you're at right now without each other. Just understand that. Don't give the world a satisfaction of watching what's going on between you guys. Handle that as men. It doesn't have to happen right now, maybe you take the season and talk about it throughout the summer. But you guys have accomplished things that not many are going to accomplish. Protect that."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F9kJGK3V0Sw&t=191s

**4. Dirk Nowitzki — "I don't want to see all this drama between these guys" — Dirk Nowitzki hopes Tatum and Brown can move past the drama** [03:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F9kJGK3V0Sw&t=237s)

Dirk Nowitzki: "I don't want to see all this drama between these guys. They've had an incredible run, they've had an amazing partnership, they won a championship. That's what it's all about. You've been through all the tough times and you come out on top and that really should form a bond that lasts a lifetime. I agree with both you guys, I don't want them to have any bad feelings. Hopefully they can hash it out and move forward. They're both terrific players, terrific people, and they'll figure it out. So please, no more drama, let's focus on hoops."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F9kJGK3V0Sw&t=237s

**5. Steve Nash — "Everyone's got to have a story or a tweet or a post" — Steve Nash on the inevitability of player narratives in modern media** [04:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F9kJGK3V0Sw&t=293s)

Steve Nash: "It's just the way the world nowadays. Everyone's got to have a story or a tweet or a post or whatever it may be, whether you're the media or you're a player. But I think it just depends on the personality of the player. Everyone's going to respond differently. You can see that obviously these two guys kind of responded differently in this situation so far. It's normal. Guys are going to have their own approach, their own feelings, their own take on the way they want to handle something."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F9kJGK3V0Sw&t=293s
