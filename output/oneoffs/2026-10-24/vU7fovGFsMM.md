**1. Jayson Tatum — "I don't have any animosity or beef or ill will towards him." — Jayson Tatum addresses the drama surrounding Jaylen Brown's trade reaction** [01:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vU7fovGFsMM&t=94s)

Jayson Tatum: "I understand we are in the entertainment business. Me personally, I am not much of an off-the-court entertainer type of guy. For me, it is like, I don't have any animosity or beef or ill will towards him. The frustrating part is since he is not with the Celtics anymore, the narrative or the conversations haven't been enough about what we accomplished, all the games we won, winning a championship, how we have grown together. It has turned into this drama type of thing. I have reached out to him the day he got traded. I called him, I texted him. I understand people process things differently. At this point in our lives, we are too old to be having our relationship discussed on the internet. There is a conversation to be had, and we can definitely have that conversation. I welcome that."

**4. Jayson Tatum — "Our mindset wasn't let's repeat. 2024, we won the championship. We weren't trying to defend the title." — Jayson Tatum on the challenge of defending an NBA championship** [10:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vU7fovGFsMM&t=608s)

Jayson Tatum: "Our mindset wasn't let's repeat. 2024, we won the championship. We weren't trying to defend the title. Our mindset was every year starts in training camp, everybody is 0-0. Some teams have some real expectations, some teams are a little delusional, but everybody is feeling great about themselves. So our mindset was we are not defending anything. We can't change it, we already won. We are trying to go win a championship. We didn't try to have a target on our back. We tried to attack the season with that desperation as if, and it is hard. You can't erase the feeling of knowing what it is like to win, but we are trying to tap into that feeling of being desperate and trying to go win another title."
