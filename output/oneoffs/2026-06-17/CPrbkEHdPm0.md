Meet 19-year-old **Karim Lopez**, a 6-8 projected lottery pick who most recently played professionally for the New Zealand Breakers before making history at this year's draft."

**Marc J. Spears:** "Do you get a sense back in Mexico how excited people there are for this history that you're going to make?"

**Karim Lopez:** "For me, I always thought of it as a given that I was going to make the NBA, even though it's the craziest thing ever, especially coming from Mexico, right? It's only been what, six players? I hope my story helps people see that you have to believe in yourself and you can do it."

**3. Karim Lopez — Karim Lopez on learning from NBL veterans and professional preparation** [04:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CPrbkEHdPm0&t=261s)

**Marc J. Spears:** "Australia, the league you played in, the NBL in recent years, it's LaMelo Ball, **Josh Giddey**, Sarr. Have you been inspired by what they did?"

**Karim Lopez:** "It's honestly a big part of the reason why I went there and it's a big part of the reason why players keep going there because it's a great program and it's producing talent.
