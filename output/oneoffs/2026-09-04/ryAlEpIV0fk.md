**9. David Patrick — "his ability to read pick and roll is the best I've seen it in a long time" — Ben Simmons' defensive IQ and pick-and-roll reading** [18:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ryAlEpIV0fk&t=1110s)

David Patrick: "Most point guards at that young can't deal with switching defenses, whether it's you're trapping a ball screen, switching a ball screen, icing a ball screen. His ability to read pick and roll is the best I've seen it in a long time. He's like, no, he didn't, and then he goes to Alabama like two days later and has like 45 or 52 or something. His ability to pass out of pick and roll, score out of pick and roll, because you can't go under, he shoots threes. If you go over, he's so strong, he gets in the lane. If you get two on the ball, he gets the open guy. He's so strong and he's got a very good knack on the offensive end."
