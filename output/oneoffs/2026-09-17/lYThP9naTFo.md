# Kwame Brown describes who was a Better Teacher to Him Michael Jordan or Kobe Bryant and Explains Why — *The BasketBall Show*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lYThP9naTFo

_Speakers identified: Kwame Brown, Host_

**1. Kwame Brown — "I would say Mike because Kobe did like to isolate himself when he worked out." — Kwame Brown explains why Michael Jordan was a better teacher than Kobe Bryant** [01:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lYThP9naTFo&t=77s)

**Host:** "When it comes to preparation for the game, understanding the game, work ethic for the game, who taught you more? Was it Mike or was it Kobe? Because you played with Kobe on the Lakers as well."

**Kwame Brown:** "I would say Mike because Kobe did like to isolate himself when he worked out. You see it, but it is like you see it in passing. You will know that he swam already because they were infamous for that. But you will see him leaving the weight room when you are coming in, and you will see him going out on the court doing his individual stuff. So you get to see him sometimes getting it in, but MJ would do it with you. MJ had the breakfast club. You get to up close and personal see the way he worked, the way he talks, the way he interact with people. So he was a more personable person than Kobe, but he worked just as hard."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lYThP9naTFo&t=77s

**2. Kwame Brown — "Kobe would practice carrying the little 45-pound dumbbells all around the court because his hands weren't as big." — Kobe Bryant's intense obsession with hand strength and training** [02:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lYThP9naTFo&t=138s)

Kwame Brown: "I think Kobe worked a little bit harder on defense, and he was obsessing over **Michael Jordan** a little bit. I remember he would practice carrying the little 45-pound dumbbells all around the court because his hands weren't as big as **Michael Jordan**. I think that is the only thing that set them apart. **Michael Jordan**'s hands are like mitts. Kobe's hands are regular. So he had to work on his hand strength, so he would walk around the court with these 45-pound dumbbells on each hand, and every morning you see him doing that just to make sure he had his hand strength together. I am like, damn, let me try that too. He would pick up different traits. I never seen somebody do that."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lYThP9naTFo&t=138s

**3. Kwame Brown — "Kobe is holding a different standard than we are holding him to." — Kobe Bryant's high standard for players and his desire to win** [03:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lYThP9naTFo&t=216s)

Kwame Brown: "Any flaw that a player had, like **Dwyane Wade**, I think **Dwyane Wade** is one of the greatest two guards I ever played against. We on the back of the bus one day, and **Lamar Odom** heard it too. We on the back of the bus, Kobe watching the little game with **Dwyane Wade**. I think Lamar brought him up. Man, y'all seen **Dwyane Wade** last night? Kobe like, ah, he ain't me. He ain't one of me. He suck. Everybody looked like, what? You got to think about it. He is holding a different standard than we are holding him to. Kobe look at the superstar players, or if you are going to put them into that category, if you are in that category, you had to be a closer on every level."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lYThP9naTFo&t=216s

**4. Kwame Brown — "He would always work on fading against the double team." — Kobe Bryant's specific training for game-winning scenarios and double teams** [04:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lYThP9naTFo&t=296s)

Kwame Brown: "He had the game broken down because he had watched so much tape of **Michael Jordan** that he knows sometimes you have to just catch, turn and shoot. Sometimes you only have time for put it down and pull up. And he would always work on fading against the double team. I guess he had seen **Michael Jordan** do that because he is like, it is not my job to pass you the ball during times when it is time to win, because you don't get paid to do that. It is my job to find a way to win the game. So I need to work on situations that I am going to be in so that I can win the game despite of. And he would literally bring up scenarios and make coaches put him in scenarios to where he can hit those type of shots."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lYThP9naTFo&t=296s
