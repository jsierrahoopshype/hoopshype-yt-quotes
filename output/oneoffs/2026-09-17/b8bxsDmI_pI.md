# Steph Curry on Li-Ning deal: Company's product reigns supreme, following brand 'free agency' — *CNBC Television*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b8bxsDmI_pI

_Speakers identified: Carl Quintanilla, Alex Sherman, Stephen Curry, Sean Quill_

**1. Stephen Curry — "the product kind of reigned supreme and so Dwyane Wade, Jimmy Butler, two guys that have been" — Stephen Curry on his decision to switch to Li-Ning** [02:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b8bxsDmI_pI&t=138s)

**Alex Sherman:** "The last time we spoke, which was last year, you still had your long-term deal with Under Armour. We haven't really talked about that in length about what happened there. I'm just wondering, why exactly did that partnership end?"

**Stephen Curry:** "It was a great run. Under Armour had a great experience over the 12, 13 years I was there. I developed Curry Brand underneath Under Armour. They were able to take my brand out and search for a new partnership that for me is looking at the longevity and legacy of what I think is possible not just here in the US, but globally across basketball, golf, lifestyle, and training. Li-Ning has been an unbelievable partner. It's a long-term scope of what we're trying to do. It's crazy just from a product perspective, I got to try out a bunch of different shoes and I lived up to the free agent tag for sure. I tried literally everything and there was a reason that Li-Ning stood tall at the end because the product reigned supreme and so **Dwyane Wade**, **Jimmy Butler**, two guys that have been with the brand for a very long time, they kind of set the stage. I'm excited, we're five months in, I just got back from a tour the last week, so I'm excited about the opportunity."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b8bxsDmI_pI&t=138s

**2. Stephen Curry — "I've always stated that I want to be a lifer with Golden State, what we've built there." — Stephen Curry on his commitment to the Golden State Warriors** [04:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b8bxsDmI_pI&t=253s)

**Alex Sherman:** "Speaking of free agency, you have one more year with the Warriors and then your contract ends, and you have a chance to become a free agent. We just saw your buddy **LeBron James** go through this process. Is that appealing to you at all?"

**Stephen Curry:** "Not at all. I've always stated that I want to be a lifer with Golden State, what we've built there. The fact that I get to run it back this year with Draymond and Coach Kerr, so all the contract stuff, this NBA thing from year to year is so crazy, things change really fast, but I do know my interest and mission to stay in Golden State and be competitive is there and the contract stuff will take care of itself."
