**4. RJ Barrett — "from being a fan to now being able to play with him, that's going to be so special" — RJ Barrett on Kawhi Leonard joining the Raptors** [05:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vtAi_UvT8cw&t=347s)

RJ Barrett: "When it first happened, I was excited. Then it was the delay, so I'm like, let me try to not think about it and just wait for it to happen. It was easy until we got to September, because we have training camp at the end of the month, so now you're like, okay, when is this thing going to start happening? Now that he's officially a Raptor, it's been amazing. It's cool. I got to cross paths with him a couple of times this summer, so we're excited for him to come here. Surreal for me, I've been a Raptors fan my whole life, so from being a fan to now being able to play with him, that's going to be so special."

**5. RJ Barrett — "my dad's always been the guy that's like, act like you've been there before" — RJ Barrett on his father's advice about acting like you've been there** [08:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vtAi_UvT8cw&t=486s)

RJ Barrett: "In those moments, I feel like things that your parents have taught you really stick with you. My dad's always been the guy that's like, act like you've been there before. So if you make a big shot or you make a big play, act like you've been there before. So when I made that shot, I was like, all right, let me just act like I've done this before, like I'm here. So that's how I acted in the moment, but on the inside, I was screaming. On the inside, I was so happy, but I just was focused."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vtAi_UvT8cw&t=486s
