## The Full Story of Devin Booker's Jersey Number Change — *Phoenix Suns*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnbwX_WWQXk

_Speakers identified: Devin Booker, Melvin Booker_

**1. Devin Booker — "I never really had a true meaning for wearing number one, even though I feel like I did a lot of good things in it." — Devin Booker on the significance of his jersey number change** [00:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnbwX_WWQXk&t=53s)

**Devin Booker:** "I would definitely say this is a new chapter in my career. I try to do it after year 10, but the NBA, you have to alert them over a year and a half in advance. I never really had a true meaning for wearing number one, even though I feel like I did a lot of good things in it. But people would ask me why do you wear number one? I would say because I have been chasing 15 my whole career."

**Melvin Booker:** "The only time that I got to wear 15 was when I got to make my own jerseys or grassroots, back my first travel team."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnbwX_WWQXk&t=53s

**2. Melvin Booker — "I always thought like this kid, this number means something to this kid." — Melvin Booker on Devin Booker's childhood connection to number 15** [01:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnbwX_WWQXk&t=112s)

Melvin Booker: "Your mom used to send me pictures of you, you playing in this jersey right here, and I always thought like this kid, this number means something to this kid. And just the fact that you were striving to be that in that 15, I knew one you would be a great kid. I also thought you would be much better than me. It's my first time saying that, but doing it in this number, and to think about it, it's been 15 years since you moved to Mississippi and we started this journey, and to change the 15 at the same time is the perfect time."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnbwX_WWQXk&t=112s

**3. Devin Booker — "Every video game that I played where you could choose a number, I would always make my player number 15." — Devin Booker on his lifelong attachment to the number 15** [02:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnbwX_WWQXk&t=146s)

Devin Booker: "It's always been 15. Every video game that I played where you could choose a number, whether it was NCAA football or NCAA basketball, I would always make my player number 15. I'm blessed to have a father that played professional, I know that's an advantage, growing up trying to take this sport serious. So watching the highlights, the old old highlights of him at Moss Point, him at Missouri, rocking number 15, it felt like a family number."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnbwX_WWQXk&t=146s

**4. Melvin Booker — "He's always had that knack, that tenacity, the confidence to be great." — Melvin Booker on Devin Booker's development and professional mindset** [03:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnbwX_WWQXk&t=198s)

Melvin Booker: "Words can't explain, to be honest, because I felt like the kid would be special from youth. He's always had that knack, that tenacity, the confidence to be great. To walk in that arena and see people with Booker on the back and how they cheer for him in this city, it's a feeling that is really hard to describe."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnbwX_WWQXk&t=198s

**5. Devin Booker — "He opened my eyes to what being a professional really looked like." — Devin Booker on the professional training he received from his father** [03:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnbwX_WWQXk&t=236s)

Devin Booker: "When I moved to Mississippi after my freshman year of high school, he opened my eyes to what being a professional really looked like. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnbwX_WWQXk&t=236s

**6. Melvin Booker — "It wasn't just about the basketball movement, it was about building the man that he is today." — Melvin Booker on the importance of family time during Devin's development** [04:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnbwX_WWQXk&t=261s)

Melvin Booker: "Part of it at the time, I'm not sure he understood all the work that he had to put in to reach his goal of being making it to the NBA level. I felt like it was important to have him not just around me in the gym, but also around my family, because we missed so much of family time with me being out the country and playing overseas. So it was a lot of making up on the court and off the court, so it wasn't just about the basketball movement, it was about building the man that he is today."

I don't think the NBA was in my mind frame at the time, and we didn't skip any steps."
