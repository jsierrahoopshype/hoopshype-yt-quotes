# Clippers Falllout: ESPN reporter defends ESPN report on Steve Ballmer? — *Nothing Personal with David Samson*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hEWn8nSvEus

_Speakers identified: David Samson_

**1. David Samson — "the NBA had found no evidence Steve Ballmer funneled money through aspiration to Kawhi Leonard" — ESPN's report on Steve Ballmer's alleged funneling of money to Kawhi Leonard** [09:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hEWn8nSvEus&t=568s)

David Samson: "We reported that the NBA had found no evidence **Steve Ballmer** funneled money through aspiration to **Kawhi Leonard**. The Watchell report confirmed this. It did not identify direct evidence and that allegation was not a central focus. I am sorry, I must be living in La La Land. The 35-page report from Watchell specifically has the evidence that money was funneled from **Steve Ballmer** to Aspiration. Specifically it is in there, with evidence, 50 million dollars worth of legal fee evidence."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hEWn8nSvEus&t=568s

**2. David Samson — "the NBA's investigation was focused on the Clippers' introductions of Kawhi Leonard's representative Dennis Robertson" — ESPN's report on the Clippers' introductions of Dennis Robertson** [10:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hEWn8nSvEus&t=643s)

David Samson: "We reported that the NBA's investigation was focused on the Clippers' introductions of **Kawhi Leonard**'s representative **Dennis Robertson**, you know him as Uncle Dennis. The introductions of Dennis to team sponsors and whether those interactions constituted salary cap circumvention. That issue became a primary focus of the report. I read that from Don Van Natta and I said, uh-oh, I better go read the report again, because there is no way that the report I read from Watchell has this as the primary focus."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hEWn8nSvEus&t=643s

**3. David Samson — "when you pay a player, you can't give him anything other than what he gets" — The rule against paying players outside of their team salary** [12:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hEWn8nSvEus&t=747s)

David Samson: "When you pay a player, you can't give him anything other than what he gets every two weeks from the team. That's the rule. If you're giving a player anything outside of that, you better disclose it and you better make sure that you count it towards your salary cap. Whether it's a private plane, whether it's a sponsorship with one of your companies, it doesn't matter. The rule is simple and we all know it, even **Lawrence Frank**. Any money outside of the every other week paycheck, it counts."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hEWn8nSvEus&t=747s
