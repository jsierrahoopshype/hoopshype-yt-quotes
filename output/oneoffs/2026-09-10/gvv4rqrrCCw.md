I love the infatuation, the obsession from everyone with the Lakers. I have preached this to youngsters, to kids, my kids, to radio audiences, to TV audiences. You love this as a Laker fan. The fact that people think about you, people hate you, and people want to beat you means that you have done something in this life and that you are relevant in this sport. **3. Mike Bresnahan — "He is the goat, and I don't think it's even up for debate" — Phil Jackson's legacy as the greatest coach in NBA history** [14:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gvv4rqrrCCw&t=858s)

Mike Bresnahan: "Phil is to me, and I said this the other day on Laker Talk on ESPN Radio, he is the goat, and I don't think it's even up for debate. He won 11 rings as a coach, three three-peats. Then a back-to-back post-Shaq Kobe era, did it again with a new group of guys with Kobe. Two as a player with the New York Knicks, so he has 13 rings total to his credit. To me, just the greatest of all time and what made him so great, and Chris covered him so knows this more than I do, but the fact that he was able to blend personalities better than any person I think that's ever coached basketball."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gvv4rqrrCCw&t=858s

**4. Chris McGee — "Lakers sweep Utah in 2010 in round two of the Western Conference playoffs" — The Lakers' 2010 playoff series against the Utah Jazz** [15:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gvv4rqrrCCw&t=933s)

Chris McGee: "The last game **Carlos Boozer** ever played in Utah, Lakers sweep Utah in 2010 in round two of the Western Conference playoffs. I am in the media bus, Phil Jackson is on the bus as well. We are backing up and we absolutely smash and run into this black parked beautiful SUV. Windows smash out, we are stuck in it, rip it up to shreds. So we are sitting there kind of waiting to see who comes out. It is **Carlos Boozer** and he comes out and it is his final game in Utah and he looks at the car, he is like, 'Damn, what what what happened?' Someone kind of explains it to him, he just walks away. We don't know if that car is still there. No one ever saw him in Utah again."
