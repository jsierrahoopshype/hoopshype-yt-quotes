# The most intriguing destinations for LeBron James 👀 | SportsCenter — *ESPN*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ihDG3f7xQi0

_Speakers identified: Scott Van Pelt, Vincent Goodwill, David Dennis Jr., Elle Duncan, Tim MacMahon_

**1. Vincent Goodwill — "If you add LeBron James next to Nikola Jokic, they would be right back into the championship conversation." — Denver Nuggets as an intriguing landing spot for LeBron James** [00:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ihDG3f7xQi0&t=43s)

**Vincent Goodwill:** "On Rich Paul's board, there was something that looked like the Denver Nuggets. You think about what the Nuggets have not accomplished since winning the championship a few years ago. Knocked out in the second round once, knocked out in the first round once. This team has been far away from the championship discussion, but if you add **LeBron James** next to **Nikola Jokic**, and on a team that had the best offense in the league last year, they would be right back into the championship conversation."

**David Dennis Jr.:** "I think that's an intriguing destination we haven't talked much about from a basketball standpoint, and in the Western Conference, that would be a little bit scary."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ihDG3f7xQi0&t=43s

**2. David Dennis Jr. — "LeBron James could be the third option on that team that gives him a little respite." — Potential synergy of a LeBron James and Nikola Jokic pairing** [01:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ihDG3f7xQi0&t=95s)

**Elle Duncan:** "What would a LeBron-Jokic pairing look like?"

**David Dennis Jr.:** "They could roll the basketball out and these dudes could just figure out how to play seamless basketball with one another. They are just that smart. On a given night, **LeBron James** could be the third option on that team that gives him a little respite with **Aaron Gordon** there. If he's healthy, defensively he helps. Part of this though, a little bit of this is on Denver. They have to figure out what they are going to do with **Peyton Watson** and if they are going to try to make that move for LeBron, which would put them in the second apron, which is something they do not really want to do, but Jokic deserves a real shot and trying to get **LeBron James** there gives him another real shot and a crack at a championship."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ihDG3f7xQi0&t=95s

**3. Vincent Goodwill — "LeBron making a reunion with the Miami Heat, letting Giannis do all the things for the first 45 minutes." — Miami Heat as a logical landing spot for LeBron James** [02:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ihDG3f7xQi0&t=165s)

**Vincent Goodwill:** "The Miami Heat, a reunion with **Pat Riley** and **Erik Spoelstra**. When you think about what they have, **Giannis Antetokounmpo**, **Bam Adebayo**, you can even take away what we are seeing now LeBron when he was at the peak of his powers. He doesn't have to be that with the Miami Heat. He can play off of Bam, he can play off of Giannis. **Erik Spoelstra** commandeered one of the better offenses in the league last year without a true number one option. You slide LeBron in there with the shooting of **Tim Hardaway Jr.** with the Heat's sort of know-how of finding guys on the edges, the way that they have historically found guys like the **Gabe Vincent**s, the **Max Strus**es of the world. The single easiest quality to find in the NBA is shooting. LeBron making a reunion with the Miami Heat, letting Giannis do all the things for the first 45 minutes, LeBron playing off of him, and then LeBron turning into a closer in the last five minutes of the game, that's something that makes logical sense to me."

**David Dennis Jr.:** "I feel so much better, nature is healing because once again I'm writing Vinnie wrong."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ihDG3f7xQi0&t=165s

**4. David Dennis Jr. — "When him and Steph Curry are together, it is basketball nirvana." — Golden State Warriors as the most logical destination for LeBron James** [03:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ihDG3f7xQi0&t=235s)

**Elle Duncan:** "Where do you believe is the most logical landing spot for LeBron?"

**David Dennis Jr.:** "To me, it's Golden State. Golden State just makes perfect, perfect sense for **LeBron James**. When him and Steph Curry are together, it is basketball nirvana. We saw this at the Olympics. These two guys know how to play together. Just the idea of **LeBron James**, **Stephen Curry**, pick and roll, Draymond helping run that offense, the old guys getting together. This just feels like a storybook ending, a way to end that rivalry that defined so much of a decade ago. These guys giving it one more run, it makes basketball sense, it makes storytelling sense, it makes sense from a fan standpoint. This is just the perfect ending for the **LeBron James** and **Stephen Curry** rivalry."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ihDG3f7xQi0&t=235s

**6. Tim MacMahon — "The Nuggets are an especially intriguing situation for the simple fact that they have a three-time MVP." — Denver Nuggets' unique appeal to LeBron James** [05:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ihDG3f7xQi0&t=354s)

Tim MacMahon: "The Nuggets are an especially intriguing situation for the simple fact that they have a three-time MVP in **Nikola Jokic**. They have the proven core of a championship team. LeBron does have relationships with people in the building, including the top of the building, **Josh Kroenke**, the chairman, the day-to-day ownership presence. **Jared Dudley** was a championship teammate of his with the Los Angeles Lakers, he's now an assistant coach there. **Peyton Watson**, a restricted free agent, also represented by **Rich Paul**, that's an interesting little dance. The Clippers also have interest in him, by the way."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ihDG3f7xQi0&t=354s
