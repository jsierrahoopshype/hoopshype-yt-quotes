# One Word from Deuce Tatum Helped Joe Mazzulla Find Perspective on Life & Basketball — *Boston Celtics*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NblYtNCLffs

_Speakers identified: Joe Mazzulla, Mark Daigneault, Brad Stevens_

**1. Joe Mazzulla — "I think when you enter someone else's environment or arena, you just let your guard down more." — Joe Mazzulla on building deeper relationships with players during the offseason** [00:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NblYtNCLffs&t=56s)

Joe Mazzulla: "It was a two-year project, so I did some of it last year. It felt like a good opportunity to get to know the guys better. By the time the season starts, and even anytime they are in the building, everything is geared towards the system that we want you to work on and how can you make us better. When you enter someone else's environment or arena, you just let your guard down more, you learn more about them, and you grow a different relationship. So that is something that I try to do. I have to be better and understand the guys better, appreciate them more, get to know the people around them, and why they are the way they are. We are all just a product of the people we have around us, so if you can be around those people, you get a really good understanding and get to know them better."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NblYtNCLffs&t=56s

**2. Joe Mazzulla — "I think I struggle with the idea of deserving everything that I have gotten." — Joe Mazzulla on struggling with the idea of deserving his success** [05:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NblYtNCLffs&t=336s)

Joe Mazzulla: "I think I struggle with the idea of deserving everything that I have gotten. I grew up with a ton of people who I think deserve it just as much, if not more than I do. So struggling with the idea of, man, I don't deserve this. There are a lot of people who have done it, but then also being grateful for the people who have helped me get to there. So I would hope you see a product of everybody from when I was a baby to when I left to go to West Virginia. Seventeen years of relationships that people sacrificed a little bit of them to help me get to where I am."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NblYtNCLffs&t=336s

**3. Joe Mazzulla — "We should have our kids in the locker room after losses and wins so we can teach." — Joe Mazzulla on the importance of having players' children in the locker room** [08:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NblYtNCLffs&t=506s)

Joe Mazzulla: "It opened me up to be like, okay, we should have our kids in the locker room after losses and wins so we can teach our kids how to handle that. You don't want them to only see you after a win because then they think that is how the world is. And then Al started bringing his kid. He was on the bench. I go to sit down and he has got the clipboard. I was like, this is kind of what it is about. So, long answer to the perspective of how I would imagine it changes all of us."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NblYtNCLffs&t=506s

**4. Joe Mazzulla — "We have a group of guys who have tasted winning and have now tasted how much losing hurts." — Joe Mazzulla on managing different career stages within the Celtics roster** [12:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NblYtNCLffs&t=731s)

Joe Mazzulla: "We have three very different perspectives that we all need to learn from this year. We have a group of guys who have tasted winning and have now tasted how much losing hurts even more after you win. We have **Mike Muscala** who is fresh off of winning, and then we have a couple of guys in Mike and **Payton Pritchard** that have not gotten to that point yet. The ability to build relationships and perspective of the three different kinds of careers that we have is a good opportunity."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NblYtNCLffs&t=731s

**5. Joe Mazzulla — "I feel like a bunch of teams are free-flowing and adjustments are happening on the fly." — Joe Mazzulla on the shift toward free-flowing, non-rigid NBA team structures** [17:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NblYtNCLffs&t=1051s)

Joe Mazzulla: "There used to be a lot more constants, like I am playing this amount of minutes tonight, this is the sub pattern, this is it. And now I feel like a bunch of teams are free-flowing and adjustments are happening on the fly. Things are changing in the middle of the game, sub patterns are changing regularly. So I think it is more free-flowing as opposed to the rigidity of this is what we do versus this is what we don't."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NblYtNCLffs&t=1051s

**6. Joe Mazzulla — "I've been pretty blessed, lucky to be where I'm at right now that I would struggle accepting that." — Joe Mazzulla on the struggle of balancing professional success with personal gratitude** [20:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NblYtNCLffs&t=1221s)

Joe Mazzulla: "I've been pretty blessed, lucky to be where I'm at right now that I would struggle accepting that, because I feel like there's so many other people who are just as, if not better, capable of doing that. So I think that that struggle is there, right? And I think you also take a look at it of like, when you have your dream job at such a young age, you have to surrender to the fact of not everybody gets to have that for their whole life. And so you have to be able to have this perspective of your own mortality, so to speak, because in the world today, if I could be here for the next 20 years, I would do that."
