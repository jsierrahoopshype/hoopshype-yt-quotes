# Darius Miles on the Vet Who Looked After the Young Clippers: "Rooks Was Our Guy" — *Straight Game Podcast *

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_e8PfX3Wi_M

_Speakers identified: Darius Miles, Quentin Richardson_

**1. Quentin Richardson — "Sean Rooks was our guy. He was our guy, man. He was our guy." — Sean Rooks' role as a veteran mentor for young Clippers** [00:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_e8PfX3Wi_M&t=9s)

**Darius Miles:** "Who is the old school vet? Maybe a guy that took you under your wing or was just there and held it down for all the young kids?"

**Quentin Richardson:** "Oh man, that was my guy **Sean Rooks**. **Derrick Strong** was really quiet, he was an introvert, he really wanted it, but he was a good dude. But the guy that was really rocking with us was **Sean Rooks**, man. He was our guy."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_e8PfX3Wi_M&t=9s

**2. Quentin Richardson — "We went to New York, man, he took us out with him. Rooks would just look after us." — Sean Rooks' mentorship of young players in New York** [00:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_e8PfX3Wi_M&t=34s)

Quentin Richardson: "We went to New York, man, he took us out with him. Rooks would just look after us when we get because we had to police our own. The guys that were playing were like the young guys. Rooks was like the only older guy that was actually in the rotation with us. So, Rooks was like one of the good guys that I really rock with."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_e8PfX3Wi_M&t=34s

**3. Quentin Richardson — "Dennis Johnson was like my god. He used to put us in check, but he was our guy." — Dennis Johnson's impact as an assistant coach for the Clippers** [01:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_e8PfX3Wi_M&t=63s)

**Quentin Richardson:** "Him and our assistant coach, man, **Dennis Johnson** was like my god. Oh, DJ? Oh man, DJ used to put us in check, but he was our guy. I love DJ, man. RIP DJ."

**Darius Miles:** "He was cool?"

**Quentin Richardson:** "What? The best. You wouldn't even think he was DJ. He was just like, I'm like, man, this **Dennis Johnson**, like how he talking to us, like he straight off the street. We related to him so much that he was the guy that we can lean on for anything when we got to tripping or getting in our feelings and stuff. He would come talk to us and check us and all that stuff. You always find that assistant coach that you really, really rock with."
