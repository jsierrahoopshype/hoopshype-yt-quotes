# Trying to understand the Jaylen Brown trade | End of the Tatum & Brown era — *NBC Sports Boston*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=48lSIUdPm8k

_Speakers identified: Michael Holley, Chris Forsberg, Gary Washburn_

**1. Gary Washburn — "It's a horrible ending to a great ten-year run for him and a horrible way for this guy to end." — Gary Washburn on the shock of the Jaylen Brown trade** [01:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=48lSIUdPm8k&t=69s)

**Gary Washburn:** "I was bewildered. All the be befuddled, bewildered. I had to pull out my thesaurus. I couldn't believe it. The return that they got, they were really trying to trade this guy. There was no going back after the Giannis thing failed. They were just trying to, they just held him up like the guy at the dollar store selling a bag of oranges. I don't get it. It's a horrible ending to a great ten-year run for him and a horrible way for this guy to end, to kick him out basically, to put him out on the curb. Terrible."

**Michael Holley:** "Had you heard anything, Washburn, anything about **Jaylen Brown** during the season? I know Tracy McGrady and Vince Carter had that conversation where McGrady, who is close to Jaylen, says he had some deep frustrations with the Celtics organization. Anything during the season from either of you where you thought, 'Hey, something might happen in the offseason?'"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=48lSIUdPm8k&t=69s

**2. Chris Forsberg — "It felt like there was a unified front about, 'Hey, Jaylen's having a really good year.'" — Chris Forsberg on the unified front after Jaylen Brown's comments** [02:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=48lSIUdPm8k&t=137s)

**Chris Forsberg:** "Actually in the aftermath of that, Jaylen came out and he's like, 'I feel bad that our president of basketball operations has to answer those questions.' So it felt like there was a unified front about, 'Hey, Jaylen's having a really good year.' And it is jarring. I don't know what the tipping point was, right? It doesn't make sense that it went from, 'Man, this guy carried you through a great season and put up an MVP-caliber year,' to all of a sudden you're just trying to offload him at all costs, including taking on one of the worst contracts in the NBA."

**Michael Holley:** "I had not heard anything. I know if you look at the press conference after Game 7, there were some issues he had with Mazzulla's decisions playing guys too late. A lot of people had problems with how that the final three games of that series was coached, but I didn't see where, 'Okay, they've got to get this guy out of here.'"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=48lSIUdPm8k&t=137s

**3. Gary Washburn — "When the brain trust met after that Game 7, they decided we've got to trade Jaylen Brown." — Gary Washburn on the Celtics' decision to trade Jaylen Brown** [03:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=48lSIUdPm8k&t=187s)

**Gary Washburn:** "Obviously, when the brain trust met after that Game 7, they decided we've got to trade **Jaylen Brown**. That was unquestionably the decision because then the Giannis thing comes up. 'Hey, let's do that.' But if you're going to do that, Mike, you better do it. You better close the deal. You cannot leave him dangling for ten days. You probably have to overpay."

**Michael Holley:** "You better do it."

**Chris Forsberg:** "In hindsight, I gave them the benefit of the doubt and said, 'Oh, there must be a plan B. There's got to be something they're falling back to that they feel comfortable that if the Giannis doesn't get to the finish line, they would never bring Jaylen into that without having some sort of backup plan.'"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=48lSIUdPm8k&t=187s

**4. Dan Greenberg — "Can't build a championship roster because Jaylen Brown makes 35% of the cap." — Dan Greenberg on the financial logic of the Jaylen Brown trade** [03:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=48lSIUdPm8k&t=216s)

Dan Greenberg: "Can't build a championship roster because **Jaylen Brown** makes 35% of the cap but can build a championship roster with **Paul George** making 32% of the cap. The fact that it went from, 'We can't give up Ugo and Baylor,' and I am champion of the Ugo fan club and I think Baylor with his mullet is awesome and definitely a rotational player in the NBA, and yet you had a sure-fire top-10 player and a top-10 combo in that instance and now you have **Paul George**."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=48lSIUdPm8k&t=216s

**5. Michael Holley — "You got to be thankful that Jaylen Brown has a platform and uses it." — Michael Holley on Jaylen Brown's platform and outspoken nature** [04:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=48lSIUdPm8k&t=252s)

Michael Holley: "For all the people when Jaylen came out and said, 'Hey, I'll double down and this is my favorite season,' all those people who wanted him to stop talking, you got to be thankful that **Jaylen Brown** has a platform and uses it. Because I think there's going to be more, as we have these questions, I think **Jaylen Brown** will continue to talk. He'll keep talking and there will be some information. We're going to get some stuff, we're going to get some content out of this and you'll be appreciative that an athlete is talking and using his platform in a way that is that benefits all of us."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=48lSIUdPm8k&t=252s

**6. Gary Washburn — "Jaylen did everything asked. They won a championship, five-time All-Star, multi-time All-NBA, Finals MVP." — Gary Washburn on the success of the Tatum and Brown era** [04:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=48lSIUdPm8k&t=296s)

Gary Washburn: "Really successful. Remember, there were Celtic fans that wanted the Celtics to take **Chris Dunn** instead of **Jaylen Brown** because he was from Providence and he had a good career there. Jaylen did everything asked. They won a championship, five-time All-Star, multi-time All-NBA, Finals MVP. I don't understand. Could they have done better against Miami in '23? Of course. Could they have won that series against Golden State? Could it have been a pristine two or three titles? Of course it could have been. But that's not all on Jaylen. I think it was successful and he ended it with his best season. He stepped his game up."
