**2. Shams Charania — "Bam Adebayo struck Tyler Herro in the face area as they were walking off the practice court." — Shams Charania reports on the physical altercation between Bam Adebayo and Tyler Herro** [01:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XMkIt-8_YCw&t=76s)

**Kevin Negandhi:** "Shams, you have a wild story in Vegas featuring former teammates. What exactly happened?"

**Shams Charania:** "Now former Miami Heat teammates **Bam Adebayo** and **Tyler Herro** got into a physical altercation on Friday morning here in Las Vegas. Sources tell me that **Bam Adebayo** struck **Tyler Herro** in the face area as they were walking off the practice court at a Las Vegas hotel. This happened when **Bam Adebayo** approached **Tyler Herro** about comments that Herro made on social media critiquing Bam after Herro was traded from Miami to Milwaukee over the last couple of weeks. They were teammates for seven years together and we know over the last couple years Herro's name has been in trade talks, but after some social media comments **Bam Adebayo** approached **Tyler Herro** on it. That's when they got into a confrontation, a back and forth that culminated in **Bam Adebayo** striking **Tyler Herro**."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XMkIt-8_YCw&t=76s

**Kevin Negandhi:** "Shams, are you hearing anything about injury sustained in this fight by **Tyler Herro**?"

**Shams Charania:** "There was nothing as far as I'm told as far as an injury or a noticeable cut on **Tyler Herro**, but there was a strike to the face area and now we'll see how much the league, the players union, what involvement they're going to have here in the coming days."
