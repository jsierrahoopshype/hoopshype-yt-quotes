## Pablo S. Torre calls Kawhi Leonard trade pause a 'giant, avoidable PR disaster' | SportsCenter — *NBA on ESPN*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EfQ3e5Tq9Z0

_Speakers identified: Shams Charania, Tony Kornheiser, Pablo S. Torre_

**1. Shams Charania — "it remains to be in the hands of the NBA and its investigation into the Clippers" — Kawhi Leonard trade remains on hold pending NBA investigation** [00:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EfQ3e5Tq9Z0&t=25s)

Shams Charania: "Even though this **Kawhi Leonard** deal has been stalled, the expectation from both the Clippers and the Raptors is that eventually, it will be processed and go through. But it remains to be in the hands of the NBA and its investigation into the Clippers regarding the aspiration case alleging that **Kawhi Leonard** was funneled money. This case has been going on for over 10 months, but now, all these months later, both of these two teams, including three players in **Kawhi Leonard**, **Grady Dick**, and **Brandon Ingram**, are now in limbo. This does not mean though that the trade has been cabashed. Both the league office and the independent law firm are going to continue to go through their process and both teams will wait for the outcome of that."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EfQ3e5Tq9Z0&t=25s

**2. Pablo S. Torre — "I started this investigation that launched all of this 10 months ago" — Pablo Torre discusses his investigation into the Clippers and Kawhi Leonard** [01:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EfQ3e5Tq9Z0&t=97s)

**Tony Kornheiser:** "All right, Pablo, what is your reaction to the **Kawhi Leonard** trade being put on hold?"

**Pablo S. Torre:** "In some ways, you're talking to the worst possible person, but also, I guess, the ideal. I started this investigation that launched all of this 10 months ago, published 11 episodes, found a whistleblower complaint naming the Clippers that was submitted under penalty of perjury, found money in, money out financial statements, documents from inside of this company Aspiration in which the Clippers co-owner **Dennis Wong** is putting in $2 million nine days before Aspiration pays **Kawhi Leonard** while the company is otherwise in default. And I can go on and on. So I'm the guy who seems to have, at the very least, a track record of saying, there is a lot here you could rule on already. You could make this decision. And yet the question I have, Tony, is why did this particular development take nine days to become public after the trade was made public on June 30th? That's what's confusing to me. It doesn't really make a ton of sense. It seems like, if nothing else, a giant PR disaster that could have been avoided through some coordination if that's what did not happen. But really, I am wondering, did they find something that might align with the independent journalism that I've done, or is this just a smokescreen of a different kind?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EfQ3e5Tq9Z0&t=97s

**3. Tony Kornheiser — "I honestly believe when they literally traded him out of the country, to me that was a sign" — Tony Kornheiser questions the likelihood of punishment for the Clippers** [01:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EfQ3e5Tq9Z0&t=97s)

**Tony Kornheiser:** "I have the normal cynicism that anybody who worked for a newspaper for 40 years has about events like this. And I honestly believe when they literally traded him out of the country, to me that was a sign that nothing was going to happen and everything was going to be fine with the NBA. And I also have always believed that they would have an internal investigation and they would announce that nothing rose to a level of standard that any punishment was involved. But if you're going to do this, if you're going to actually put that trade on hold, which surprises me, does that indicate to you at all a greater chance for any punishment for the parties involved including the **Clippers** and Kawhi?"

**Pablo S. Torre:** "I continue to do reporting on that very question. My thesis this entire time, according to my reporting, stands up, which is that there is a public relations concern that governs the decision-making. But in terms of the substance here, I will tell you that there should be, and it's not particularly close."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EfQ3e5Tq9Z0&t=97s
