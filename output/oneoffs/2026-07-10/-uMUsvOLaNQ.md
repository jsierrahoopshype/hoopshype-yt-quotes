**2. Dave McMenamin — "Bob Myers is saying this is the most talented team." — LeBron James views the 76ers as a talented, championship-contending team** [02:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-uMUsvOLaNQ&t=135s)

Dave McMenamin: "Of whatever amount of teams that LeBron is considering, **Bob Myers** is saying this is the most talented team. And then the legacy play is a long-suffering fan base. I was there. A part of the reason why I was excited to take the job and move from Los Angeles to Cleveland in 2014 is I believed that LeBron would be able to break that championship drought with the Cavs. And I knew what it was like as a Philadelphia fan growing up. Having a 25-year championship drought snapped felt like the best thing in the world. If he could do a 44-year one with the Sixers, the town will go nuts."

**3. Dave McMenamin — "If it is a circus, that also means relevance." — The circus surrounding LeBron James is a sign of relevance** [03:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-uMUsvOLaNQ&t=227s)

Dave McMenamin: "Compared to the average NBA team, yes, it is a circus. Also, that also means relevance. That means competitive basketball, that means an event-type feel to the regular season games. I do not think you replicate that by too many other superstars or too many other teams. You could have teams that are successful that there is not the anticipation of all the games the season brings. It is going to mean that when you go on the road, you are playing full, packed arenas. I have covered sold-out games in New Orleans when **LeBron James** is on the team."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-uMUsvOLaNQ&t=227s
