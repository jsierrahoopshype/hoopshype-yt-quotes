**1. Bobby Marks — "When you're 35 years old and you have 115 million dollars in guaranteed money coming your way" — Kawhi Leonard's two-year extension with the Toronto Raptors** [00:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dkLe7Icsi70&t=35s)

Bobby Marks: "**Kawhi Leonard** signs a two-year, 115 million dollar extension. He was eligible for two for 124. The max was two years because of the over-38 rule. If he had waited to the offseason, he could have signed for four new contracts with Toronto. But when you're 35 years old and you have 115 million dollars in guaranteed money coming your way, I think it was a smart move by **Leonard**. He becomes the eighth player of the 500 million dollar club."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dkLe7Icsi70&t=35s

**2. Bobby Marks — "Buddy Hield comes their way and Ryan Nembhard, who I really like as part of the deal." — Buddy Hield and Ryan Nembhard trade to the Charlotte Hornets** [04:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dkLe7Icsi70&t=294s)

Bobby Marks: "**Buddy Hield** comes their way and **Ryan Nembhard**, who I really like as part of the deal. Hield is on a two-year deal, 97, 101, non-guaranteed for next year. There is a trigger date for three and change right the day after the draft that would become guaranteed. They can certainly waive him. It is right before the start of the moratorium. There is a waiver date here. What this trade does for them is that they have got some decisions to make as far as with their depth."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dkLe7Icsi70&t=294s

**3. Bobby Marks — "The thing to remember, Mohamed Gueye, he is hurt and because he is hurt" — Mohamed Gueye's injury impact on the Atlanta Hawks roster** [06:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dkLe7Icsi70&t=414s)

Bobby Marks: "The thing to remember, **Mohamed Gueye**, he is hurt and because he is hurt, even though his contract is non-guaranteed, if they cut him, they will owe him until he gets healthy. If it is determined that he is out for the year, then they would owe him 2.4 million. If they cut him and he declares that he can be back on February 15, they would owe him the guaranteed amount up to that date."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dkLe7Icsi70&t=414s

**4. Bobby Marks — "If he signs the QO, it is a position nobody wants to be in." — The downside of signing a qualifying offer** [10:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dkLe7Icsi70&t=659s)

Bobby Marks: "If he signs the QO, it is a position nobody wants to be in. It is a position where the player is basically turning down 38 million dollars for 9.6 million, so you are in a hole 30 million. And if you are, as I have mentioned before, if you are Detroit, he is basically going to play out the year, maybe you can trade him along the way and sign with a team that has cap space next summer."
