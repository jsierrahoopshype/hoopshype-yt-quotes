# CHAMPIONSHIP OR BUST! Allen Iverson Reacts to LeBron James Joining the 76ers — *Stephen A. Smith Sports*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f1pKAigQnXs

_Speakers identified: Stephen A. Smith, Allen Iverson_

**1. Allen Iverson — "Definitely championship or bust, man. That's the added piece that we needed." — Allen Iverson expects a championship run for the 76ers** [02:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f1pKAigQnXs&t=140s)

Allen Iverson: "Definitely championship or bust, man. That's the added piece that we needed. Obviously, you put him on any team, to me, you definitely have a chance. But obviously, a whole lot of things got to go right during the season, definitely health. But as far as the leadership, the talent, the wars that he's been in, and him being familiar with every type of situation that happens on the basketball court, he just boosts the percentage of any team having a chance to win a championship and having big-time success. He's one of them ones, man. He's the king for a reason."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f1pKAigQnXs&t=140s

**2. Allen Iverson — "Michael Jordan was everything to me. He's the reason I play basketball." — Allen Iverson on LeBron James's legacy and the GOAT debate** [07:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f1pKAigQnXs&t=437s)

Allen Iverson: "For me, it's different when you speak of who's the GOAT. **Michael Jordan** was everything to me. He's the reason I play basketball. The world wouldn't know Allen Iverson if it wasn't for **Michael Jordan**. He's the reason I played basketball. I literally wanted to be like Mike. So, it's different for me. I'm biased in that sense because I love him so much. But LeBron, I have a relationship with him. I love him. That's my little brother. I've been around those people for years and they are part of me, they are part of my heart. But the way I look at it, like Michael is always going to be the GOAT to me. And LeBron to me, I feel like if you go in the dictionary and you look up basketball player, you'll see **LeBron James**."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f1pKAigQnXs&t=437s
