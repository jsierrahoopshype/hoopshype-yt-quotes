Jalen Brunson: "It's definitely difficult. My dad, he's my dad first, and so he wants the best for me. And so when you have a guy who's so has been invested for you for 29 years, he's going to put everything he has into your craft. I think the best part about it, he lets me be myself. When he wasn't coaching me on the team, he let the coach coach me. It wasn't like, oh, your coach said this, but I disagree, you should be doing this.
