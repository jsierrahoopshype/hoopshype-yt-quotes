**2. LeBron James — "This is a Basquiat piece called Minor Success. All these A's that you see in the middle, he was a big fan of Hank Aaron." — LeBron James on the significance of his Basquiat artwork** [07:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G7QspzZIpCY&t=440s)

LeBron James: "This is a Basquiat piece called Minor Success. If I had to think into his mind maybe a little bit, I just think it's just the beginning. As he's making his works, making his rounds, and some of his early works, he didn't have the money or the resources to actually put some of his works on canvas. This is some of the first works where he started to use the crown, and it resonated to not only me, but it resonated to our family. All these A's that you see in the middle, he was a big fan of **Hank Aaron**, the great baseball player that played for the Atlanta Braves, so that's kind of a nod to him there."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G7QspzZIpCY&t=440s

**4. Savannah James — "Bronny taught me unconditional love. Bryce taught me that I can love even more. And Zhuri has taught me patience." — Savannah James on lessons learned from her children** [09:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G7QspzZIpCY&t=570s)

Savannah James: "The lessons that each of them have taught me, I would say Bronny taught me unconditional love. That was my firstborn, my first boy. There's a connection that you get in that moment that is indescribable, so I feel like that was just true love. Bryce taught me that I can love even more, like the expansion of being able to love another human being. And Zhuri has taught me patience. Also, she has given me so much perspective. I feel like having a girl is very different than boys. For me, it just put different things in perspective just about who I wanted to be and how I wanted to show up for her."
