**5. Patrick Fertitta — "there's owners that want to win a championship and there's owners that need to win a championship." — Patrick Fertitta on the Rockets' long-term championship goal** [12:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O2Kw-VoxZ88&t=725s)

Patrick Fertitta: "I've heard this before. People say there's owners that want to win a championship and there's owners that need to win a championship. And what I mean by that is, of course, everybody wants to win one. That's the ultimate goal. That's the epitome of success. But are you willing to take on the sacrifices and use the resources and do everything possible to give yourself the best chance possible in order to get there? And obviously, we haven't had that moment yet, but that's what we strive for every day. That's why we have a lot of the leadership in place that we have is because we wanted to take the approach of our ultimate goal is to win a title."

**6. Patrick Fertitta — "you can have a team that has 1, 2, 3 percent odds that jumps in to one or two." — Patrick Fertitta on the NBA draft lottery and parity** [17:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O2Kw-VoxZ88&t=1041s)

Patrick Fertitta: "Even the prior system, you can have a team that has 1, 2, 3 percent odds that jumps in to one or two. The same market was able to get two subsequent year. So, if you're going to get away from just rewarding teams that are at the bottom, then you'll also open up the possibility that a team that maybe shouldn't deserve to be picking in the top three is going to be picking in the top three. So, like I said, there's not a perfect situation."
