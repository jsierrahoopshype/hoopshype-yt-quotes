**Vernon Maxwell:** "You decided to make your way on the CBA. What prompted the move?"

**Stephon Marbury:** "When I left, it was pretty much at that point there was nothing else to do. I wasn't going to conform. I wasn't that type of person or player, so I left. I had to clear that up to see the mental illness is real."

**3. Stephon Marbury — "Access for all for people who couldn't afford. I come from where I come from." — Stephon Marbury on the mission behind his Starbury shoe brand** [07:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tr6mQaOsnvE&t=471s)

Stephon Marbury: "14.98. Right? Access for all for people who couldn't afford. I come from where I come from. My mom had seven kids, she couldn't afford to do it. We grew up on food stamps, WIC, everything like everybody else from the hood. But understood the importance of giving back, making sure that we created something that people would be able to attach themselves to with someone who comes from where they come from. My ideology is treat people the way you want to be treated, and it paid back sevenfold."
