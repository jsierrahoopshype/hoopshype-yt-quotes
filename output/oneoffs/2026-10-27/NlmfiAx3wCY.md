**2. Jordan Walsh — "I have a good idea of what each star player likes to do and what they don't like done to them." — Jordan Walsh on learning star player tendencies for better defense** [01:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NlmfiAx3wCY&t=66s)

Jordan Walsh: "At this point now, I have a good idea of what each star player likes to do and what they don't like done to them. If they don't like pressure, if they don't like driving left, or if they feel pressure, they're immediately going to start backing you down, or are they going to go to a behind-the-back move? I've learned those things along this journey and now I'm trying to apply that and take away that to make me a better defender."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NlmfiAx3wCY&t=66s

**3. Jordan Walsh — "Having Porzingis come and Mitchell is a big change on offense." — Jordan Walsh on finding his role with Porzingis and Mitchell** [02:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NlmfiAx3wCY&t=122s)

Jordan Walsh: "Where I'm at now is just trying to find a fit to this new team. Obviously, having Porzingis come and Mitchell is a big change on our offense. I feel like during those times I found a rhythm with those guys that were playing and the guys that were out there. Now it's just about trying to find that same rhythm with these guys. I think defensively we're really good."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NlmfiAx3wCY&t=122s

**4. Jordan Walsh — "Porzingis being able to be an off-ball playmaker takes a load off him." — Jordan Walsh on Porzingis as an off-ball playmaker** [02:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NlmfiAx3wCY&t=143s)

Jordan Walsh: "I think Porzingis being able to be an off-ball playmaker, obviously he's a really good on-ball defender, but being able to be an off-ball playmaker and not worry about having to guard the best guy, I think that takes a load off him and he gets to play more free. He gets to play however he likes and I think that's my job, to open that up for them and everybody else."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NlmfiAx3wCY&t=143s

**5. Jordan Walsh — "He's telling me to try to be a player, trying to read whatever the moment is." — Jordan Walsh on Paul Pierce's mentorship and defensive mindset** [03:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NlmfiAx3wCY&t=180s)

Jordan Walsh: "He's shown me a couple of things, some physicality things. But the best information I got from him is just telling me to try to be a player, trying to read whatever the moment is and act in that moment. Not to be sped up, just slow down and read the game. Trying to change me into more of a, I guess what I would call a vet mindset. Playing slower, playing more control, not just running all over the place, but still trying to cause havoc with under control."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NlmfiAx3wCY&t=180s

**6. Jordan Walsh — "My job isn't to go score the ball necessarily. That's not my role." — Jordan Walsh on his role alongside Tatum, Brown, and Holiday** [03:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NlmfiAx3wCY&t=226s)

Jordan Walsh: "I'm on the court with **Jayson Tatum**, **Jaylen Brown**, **Jrue Holiday**, and **Kristaps Porzingis** a lot of the time. So my job isn't to go score the ball necessarily. That's not my role. That's all that extra stuff, it's just icing on the cake. What I'm there for and what I'm trying to provide to the team is just being the best defender. Being a guy that keeps us out of rotations, keeps the stress off of our other guys so that they can focus on the bigger picture."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NlmfiAx3wCY&t=226s

**7. Jordan Walsh — "We're all trying to get to one goal, which is to win a championship." — Jordan Walsh on team depth and shared championship goals** [04:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NlmfiAx3wCY&t=293s)

Jordan Walsh: "We have a lot of really good guys who can step in and play that starter role. It's more about having the next guy ready and supporting them through that and passing on whatever knowledge you can in the moment. Because we're all trying to get to one goal, which is to win a championship. Whether I'm playing, or **Neemias Queta** is playing, or **Kristaps Porzingis**, or **Luka Doncic**, whoever is playing in that position, those minutes, we want the best for him. I think we're just full of a lot of talented guys who want to play hard as possible and try to win."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NlmfiAx3wCY&t=293s
