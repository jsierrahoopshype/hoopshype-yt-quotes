**2. Allen Iverson — "I inspire them and they inspire me." — Allen Iverson on his relationship with the Philadelphia fans** [00:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6eyPzoX2Hs&t=52s)

Allen Iverson: "The support system and the way they look at me. I inspire them and they inspire me. Just for the fact that I can make them feel like I make them feel is an awesome thing. I used to be these people. I had my heroes. I felt like that about Michael Jordan, and if I was ever to have the opportunity to meet Mike when I was growing up, it would have been everything to me."

**3. Allen Iverson — "I think he has a plan for me. I do not think he plays about me at all." — Allen Iverson on LeBron James wearing a 76ers jersey** [01:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6eyPzoX2Hs&t=110s)

Allen Iverson: "It is life, it is God. I think he has a plan for me. I do not think he plays about me at all. I am his guy. A lot of these things I am shocked about it a lot, but sometimes I am not. It is just the blueprint of my life and the things that he has done for me and enabled me to see and go through in life."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6eyPzoX2Hs&t=110s

**4. Allen Iverson — "I know for a fact it is going to happen. We are going to have a parade." — Allen Iverson predicts a 76ers championship parade** [02:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6eyPzoX2Hs&t=157s)

Allen Iverson: "I know for a fact it is going to happen. I am confident in the ability of the guys we have on the roster. I am confident in our coaching staff. I am confident in **Kevin Johnson** the trainer and all the people that we have in place to help us make this thing go. Most importantly, we have the best fans in the world and they are going to show up and show out for us. This is it. We are going to have a parade, I promise you that."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6eyPzoX2Hs&t=157s

**5. Allen Iverson — "We got some guys coming off the bench that can make some noise." — Allen Iverson evaluates the 76ers roster and bench depth** [03:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6eyPzoX2Hs&t=204s)

Allen Iverson: "With **Tyrese Maxey** and with the big fella **Joel Embiid**, and then **Jaylen Brown**, and then the pieces that we implemented. We got some guys coming off the bench that can make some noise. I am looking forward because I feel it. I am the number one Sixers fan in the world, so every year I feel like we have an opportunity and I am going to believe that in my heart because I wear it on my sleeve. We got a real good shot."
