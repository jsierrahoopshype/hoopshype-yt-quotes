**1. Evan Turner — "I think the Pistons are handling the correct way to be completely honest with you." — Pistons are handling Jalen Duren's contract situation correctly** [00:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d7eGkPsqL8Y&t=41s)

Evan Turner: "I think the Pistons are handling the correct way to be completely honest with you. I think they're rewarding him for a high level. I think they're trying to think for the future. They're going to give him $40 million a year to play basketball. They're trying to build a great team around you, but most importantly, a top-five player like **Cade Cunningham**. So, I think where they're trying to get to, especially as long as Cade is there, you want to try to win as much as possible."

**2. Chris Mannix — "What is giving the Pistons reason for pause is what happened in the playoffs." — Jalen Duren's poor playoff performance caused Pistons' contract hesitation** [02:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d7eGkPsqL8Y&t=153s)

Chris Mannix: "The facts are that if **Jalen Duren** had pulled his hamstring on the final day of the regular season, we wouldn't be having this conversation because the Pistons would have maxed him out. What is giving the Pistons reason for pause is what happened in the playoffs, which were a disaster for **Jalen Duren**. His numbers were basically cut in half. He was outplayed by other bigs, and he just didn't look like the franchise center that he looked like for most of the regular season."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d7eGkPsqL8Y&t=153s

**3. Chris Mannix — "The Pistons are between a rock and a hard place too here." — Pistons' roster moves have left them in a difficult position** [03:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d7eGkPsqL8Y&t=224s)

Chris Mannix: "The Pistons are between a rock and a hard place too here because they can't just lose him for nothing. It's already been a weird off-season in Detroit. They trade away **Isaiah Stewart**. Say what you want about his anger issues out there on the floor, but he was a part of the fabric of that new old-school Detroit basketball. He was the **Rick Mahorn** of this era. They lose **Tobias Harris**, a productive guy for the two years. The Pistons' last two years have engineered one of the great revivals in the NBA. You're replacing him with **John Collins**, who's younger, I guess, but **John Collins** has been moved around a lot. He's seen a few franchises in the last couple of years."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d7eGkPsqL8Y&t=224s

**4. Chris Mannix — "If he signs that qualifying offer, the Pistons are in a world of trouble." — Signing the qualifying offer would be a disaster for Detroit** [05:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d7eGkPsqL8Y&t=310s)

Chris Mannix: "If he signs that qualifying offer, the Pistons are in a world of trouble because I don't think you can trade a guy after he signs the qualifying offer, at least there's a no-trade clause attached to a qualifying offer. And if that's the case, you lose **Jalen Duren** for little or nothing. You're kind of right back where you started with Cade Cunningham and a bunch of guys that are just okay. **Jalen Duren** has his flaws, but they need him there, or they need like Domantas Sabonis to replace him."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d7eGkPsqL8Y&t=310s

**5. Chris Mannix — "You sign the qualifying offer, that's money you're never going to get back." — The financial risk of signing a qualifying offer** [06:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d7eGkPsqL8Y&t=368s)

Chris Mannix: "You sign the qualifying offer, that's money you're never going to get back. You're never going to be able to recoup the difference between the one-year $10 million qualifying offer that you signed and the $50 million average salary that you would sign if you're **Jalen Duren** there. So it's a lot of money, and it's a big bet on yourself. That's why you never see guys do it. Remember when **Kristaps Porzingis** was leaving the Knicks and he was threatening to sign the qualifying offer when he got to Dallas? He didn't do that because guys don't do that. They don't leave that kind of money on the table."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d7eGkPsqL8Y&t=368s
