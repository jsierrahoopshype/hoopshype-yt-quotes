**1. Steve Nash — "I grew up in an era where a point guard wasn't supposed to shoot 25 times" — Steve Nash on the evolution of the point guard role** [00:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=agDpwKpsAFo&t=6s)

Steve Nash: "I grew up in an era where a point guard wasn't supposed to shoot 25 times, 20 times. I tried to be aggressive, but with an open mind to score or playmake when I was at my best."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=agDpwKpsAFo&t=6s

**2. Steve Nash — "If I could loosen my man up before I got to the screen, I just had such" — Steve Nash on the importance of creating separation before the screen** [02:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=agDpwKpsAFo&t=153s)

Steve Nash: "I didn't want my guy to be attached. If I could loosen my man up before I got to the screen where he was arm's length or more, I just had such a big advantage because now I'm isolated on the big. The big has to commit to me."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=agDpwKpsAFo&t=153s

**3. Steve Nash — "When his hips have turned one way, he can't make a play on a ball behind him" — Steve Nash on manipulating defenders' body positions** [05:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=agDpwKpsAFo&t=340s)

Steve Nash: "When his hips have turned one way, he can't make a play on a ball behind him. He tries, but human beings don't have that shoulder mobility. So, when his hips have turned one way, he can't make a play on a ball behind him."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=agDpwKpsAFo&t=340s

**4. Steve Nash — "Being able to pass out of my left hand was something that I never said, I got" — Steve Nash on the necessity of developing a left-handed pass** [09:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=agDpwKpsAFo&t=542s)

Steve Nash: "Being able to pass out of my left hand was something that I never said, I got to be able to pass out of my left hand. Just playing pickup basketball as a kid, I said, if I can get this to them out of my left right now, that's a wide-open shot."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=agDpwKpsAFo&t=542s

**5. Steve Nash — "The hang dribble, hesitation for me was how I played. Essentially my way to articulate is that" — Steve Nash on using the hang dribble to bait defenders** [10:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=agDpwKpsAFo&t=603s)

Steve Nash: "The hang dribble, hesitation for me was how I played. Essentially my way to articulate is that I was just baiting you to get too close. And if you got too close, I was going by you, and if you didn't, I could do it again to bait you again, or I could pull up and shoot."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=agDpwKpsAFo&t=603s
