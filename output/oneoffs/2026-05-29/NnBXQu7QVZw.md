# Austin Rivers On The Dan Patrick Show Full Interview | 5/29/26 — *Dan Patrick Show*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NnBXQu7QVZw

_Speakers identified: Dan Patrick, Austin Rivers_

**1. Austin Rivers — "I thought Wembanyama just did a fantastic job last night just being aggressive." — Austin Rivers on the aggressive play of Victor Wembanyama and Shai Gilgeous-Alexander** [00:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NnBXQu7QVZw&t=32s)

**Dan Patrick:** "Is it just a coincidence that when the Spurs play well or **Wembanyama** plays well the Spurs win, when **Shai Gilgeous-Alexander** plays well, the Thunder win?"

**Austin Rivers:** "No, the two best players of the team, they are the head of the snake. I thought **Wembanyama** just did a fantastic job last night just being aggressive. I know some of his first few shots were deep threes, but it was the non-hesitation in his game that I liked. How sure he was. He came out and he's letting it fly. They were great, they were physical. If the Spurs play like that, it could be an interesting Game 7."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NnBXQu7QVZw&t=32s

**2. Austin Rivers — "I've called games this year where I've seen this guy make like 15 to 20 field goals." — Austin Rivers on Shai Gilgeous-Alexander's free throw shooting and physical play** [01:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NnBXQu7QVZw&t=114s)

**Dan Patrick:** "Was there a market correction last night with **Shai Gilgeous-Alexander** that he only shoots three free throws?"

**Austin Rivers:** "No, because I've called games this year where I've seen this guy make like 15 to 20 field goals in a game. He's unbelievable as a basketball player. I thought they did a great job defending and just coming out of the game with physicality, and they took the win out of Oklahoma City. Oklahoma City hung around for most of the game until that third quarter and they just blew it wide open."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NnBXQu7QVZw&t=114s

**3. Austin Rivers — "I don't think there's anybody I've ever played against or seen in an NBA game be more intense." — Austin Rivers on the unmatched intensity of Kevin Garnett** [05:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NnBXQu7QVZw&t=346s)

Austin Rivers: "I don't think there's anybody I've ever played against or seen in an NBA game be more intense than **Kevin Garnett**. He used to always scare me when I was young. And then obviously when I became more of a fan of basketball, I was obsessed with his aura, his tenacious energy and what he brought to that Celtics team. That 08 Celtics team again, you could Paul Pierce was the guy, but **Kevin Garnett** was the spirit of that team. Playing against him one-on-one was crazy."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NnBXQu7QVZw&t=346s

**4. Austin Rivers — "I have to go with the MVP, man. For a multitude of reasons." — Austin Rivers on choosing between Jalen Brunson and Shai Gilgeous-Alexander in the clutch** [09:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NnBXQu7QVZw&t=594s)

Austin Rivers: "**Jalen Brunson** is so damn good down the stretch. It's impossible to not pick him just because of what we've seen. And yet I still have to go with the guy who's the MVP, man. For a multitude of reasons. It's close. First off, there's no wrong answer because **Jalen Brunson** is probably my favorite player in the NBA just his footwork. But **Shai Gilgeous-Alexander** is taller, can always get a shot off no matter what. I think they're one and two in the NBA in terms of clutch."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NnBXQu7QVZw&t=594s
