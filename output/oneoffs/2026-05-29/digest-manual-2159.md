## DeMar DeRozan joins The Richard Show — *Richard A. Jefferson*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F39qgI5S7fs

**1. DeMar DeRozan — "I'm like Neo now." — DeMar DeRozan compares his playmaking evolution to Neo in the Matrix** [13:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F39qgI5S7fs&t=793s)

**Richard Jefferson:** "A lot of players talk about the game slowing down for them as they elevate their game. Is that something that you can speak to as well as you maybe developed your playmaking in other areas of your game?"

**DeMar DeRozan:** "I'm like Neo now. I made a reference to me being like Neo in the Matrix now. He asked me how the game slowed down for me and I said I feel like Neo in the Matrix now."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F39qgI5S7fs&t=793s

**2. DeMar DeRozan — "Tell your daughter to leave me alone." — Diar DeRozan's defensive impact on opposing free throw shooters** [14:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F39qgI5S7fs&t=885s)

**Richard Jefferson:** "It was a time OG missed two free throws and we're running back. He said, 'Man, tell your daughter to leave me alone.' OG did that?"

**DeMar DeRozan:** "Yes, literally, we're in the game running back and OG says, 'Tell your daughter to stop screaming. Tell her to leave me alone. We keep missing free throws.' I'm like, 'Damn, y'all missing free throws?' So when they win again and they miss two free throws again, I'm hearing it. Throughout the game I'm hearing it and I'm like, 'Yo, this shit is working.'"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F39qgI5S7fs&t=885s

**3. DeMar DeRozan — "I never wanted to increase my shoes when I was young." — DeMar DeRozan's habit of not creasing his basketball shoes** [17:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F39qgI5S7fs&t=1025s)

**DeMar DeRozan:** "It really started because I never wanted to increase my shoes when I was young. I didn't want to tie them too tight, crease them, because my shoes on the court were my school shoes too, so I tried to keep them."

**Richard Jefferson:** "People don't understand that you get one pair of shoes where it's the start of school, that's your first pair of shoes, that's basketball practice, that's church."

**DeMar DeRozan:** "So that's where it started from and it just became like a habit and it just stayed that way."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F39qgI5S7fs&t=1025s
