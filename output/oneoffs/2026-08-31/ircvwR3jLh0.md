**1. Aaron Turner — "The reality is the Warriors never made an extension offer, they never were going to go out past two years." — Aaron Turner on the false narrative surrounding Jonathan Kuminga's contract** [00:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ircvwR3jLh0&t=27s)

Aaron Turner: "The reality is the Warriors never made an extension offer. They never were going to go out past two years, which is what happened when we got into restricted free agency, and we never turned down five years, 150. So the hustling backwards thing doesn't make any sense because there was never anything to capitalize on with that. There were multiple reasons, obviously, the Warriors were trying to figure out if **Jonathan Kuminga** was part of their long-term future, the fit with him and Steve, flexibility to possibly trade him. He was obviously, if they would have gotten Kevin Durant at the deadline, he would have been part of that. Had they extended him, they couldn't even have offered him. And he was a big piece to that."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ircvwR3jLh0&t=27s

**2. Aaron Turner — "That was a deal we wanted to do. We wanted to do 488 with a player option with the Suns." — Aaron Turner on the failed sign-and-trade to the Phoenix Suns** [02:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ircvwR3jLh0&t=137s)

Aaron Turner: "That was a deal we wanted to do. We wanted to do 488 with a player option with the Suns, and the Suns had offered **Royce O'Neale**, **Nick Richards**, three seconds. The Warriors weren't going to take that back, so it's hard when you're working a sign-and-trade. But this whole thing that Kuminga is saying no to everything, Aaron Turner is saying no to everything, it's nonsense. The 5-150 never existed. That's I think what started this whole thing that has moved into this massive misinformation cycle."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ircvwR3jLh0&t=137s

**3. Aaron Turner — "It would make me really reckless with guys' careers. I actually tend to tilt more conservative than aggressive." — Aaron Turner on the importance of correcting the public narrative** [02:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ircvwR3jLh0&t=168s)

Aaron Turner: "It would make me really reckless with guys' careers. And if any of my players know this that I've worked with, I actually tend to tilt more conservative than aggressive in most situations. But this is important to me because it would have made that one of the worst decisions probably of all time. But if you're going to get a five-year 150 million offer, obviously there's going to be a belief from the organization that you're part of the long-term, and that the coach and the player are on the same page. It all makes sense why they didn't offer that, why they wanted clean books. And nobody really has any bad blood towards it. It's not like **Jonathan Kuminga** or myself were like upset about it. What's upsetting to me is the fact that you have to listen to all these things being said that are just so false."

**4. Aaron Turner — "It's really only one year 24 million guaranteed or two years 48 million guaranteed. The option year is not." — Aaron Turner on the contract structure and player option value** [04:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ircvwR3jLh0&t=262s)

Aaron Turner: "It's really only one year 24 million guaranteed or two years 48 million guaranteed. The option year is not guaranteed money. So there was really a one-year 24 million guaranteed with a team option, two-year 48 million guaranteed team option. And it was very important with how things were going to get control of the ship. And so because of that, we felt putting ourselves in the most tradeable vehicle to get him out of that situation was super important. And that's why we took a lesser deal."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ircvwR3jLh0&t=262s

**6. Aaron Turner — "Your bird rights and the team you're on only matter if the incumbent team wants to keep you." — Aaron Turner on the value of bird rights and team fit** [10:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ircvwR3jLh0&t=644s)

Aaron Turner: "Your bird rights and the team you're on only matter if the incumbent team wants to keep you. If not, you're going to be in the same spot. I'd rather not have my bird rights, play good basketball, show the world what I can do, be part of a winning group, then say, oh, I have my bird rights, but I can't play to the ability to my capability, maybe because of that's the roster, maybe that's because of the fit, how you want to play. So, no, I don't think anything, if you really look at the moment, has been fumbled."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ircvwR3jLh0&t=644s
