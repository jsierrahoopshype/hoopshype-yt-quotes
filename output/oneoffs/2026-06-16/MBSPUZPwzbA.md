**3. Mike Brown — "I was an assistant in San Antonio, the reporter that asked me that question, he said" — Mike Brown on the origin of the 'Who Let the Dogs Out' chant** [09:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MBSPUZPwzbA&t=557s)

Mike Brown: "I was an assistant in San Antonio, the reporter that asked me that question, he said, what did you learn during your time here when you coached the seven-year-old dogs flag football team? And so I said, I learned to cheer. We used to have all the parents come into the huddle, all the kids come into the huddle before every game. We put everybody put their hands in, and then I'd go, one, two, three, I go, who let the dogs out? And then one player on our team, he tapped my back, rubbed my shoulder, he looked down at me, gave me the sorry-felt look and he said, don't worry about it, coach. It was **McKale Bridges**."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MBSPUZPwzbA&t=557s
