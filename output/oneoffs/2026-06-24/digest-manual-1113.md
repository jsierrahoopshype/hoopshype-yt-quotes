## The Jaylen Brown/Celtics Drama Is Worse Than It Looks | Rachel Nichols & Chris Mannix — *Sports Illustrated*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZmTgjOijFFA

_Speakers identified: Chris Mannix, Rachel Nichols_

**1. Chris Mannix — "they weren't willing to go deep into their cash of young players" — Celtics' reluctance to trade for Giannis Antetokounmpo** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZmTgjOijFFA&t=0s)

**Chris Mannix:** "It was reported multiple places during the week the Celtics were all in on **Giannis Antetokounmpo**. Clearly they were not, because all in means you go all in and they were not willing to do that. They were willing to part with **Jaylen Brown**, they were willing to part with a couple of first-round picks, but they were not willing to go deep into their cash of young players. They were not willing to go with swaps and future picks and all the things they could have done."

**Rachel Nichols:** "Which I think was smart, don't you?"

**Chris Mannix:** "Well, that is the question. Should they have gone all in and committed **Hugo Gonzalez**, committed **Baylor Scheierman**, committed a couple of pick swaps to this in order to get **Giannis Antetokounmpo**?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZmTgjOijFFA&t=0s

**2. Rachel Nichols — "it's hard to replace that depth if you give everything away including the pick swaps" — The difficulty of replacing team depth in big trades** [00:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZmTgjOijFFA&t=39s)

Rachel Nichols: "It is not just that. It is also about it's hard to replace that depth. It is not necessarily this player is untouchable when **Jaylen Brown** isn't, but it's hard to replace that depth if you give everything away including the pick swaps and more draft assets. So I think when we just had this conversation, if you gut your team to get this big star player, you are not going to end up winning. And in Boston, this is the team with 18 championships, that is the bar."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZmTgjOijFFA&t=39s

**3. Chris Mannix — "Giannis is right now damage goods. He has had multiple years of these kind of soft tissue injuries" — Why the Celtics were right not to trade for Giannis Antetokounmpo** [02:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZmTgjOijFFA&t=177s)

Chris Mannix: "I don't think that the Celtics should have gone all in for Giannis. I don't think they should have gutted the team because this is not 2022. Giannis is right now damage goods. He has had multiple years of these kind of soft tissue injuries, calf injuries that are enormous red flags for a player of his size and the way that he plays. And he is also not a player that has shown that he is evolving as he is getting older. He is still the same guy. He is still dominant, but it is not like he is developing a three-point shot."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZmTgjOijFFA&t=177s

**4. Chris Mannix — "the relationship is more damaged than you think. All the Jaylen Brown trade rumors in Boston" — The impact of trade rumors on Jaylen Brown's relationship with the Celtics** [04:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZmTgjOijFFA&t=261s)

Chris Mannix: "I do think it's more damaged than you think. All the **Jaylen Brown** trade rumors in Boston. It was **Kawhi Leonard** in 2018. It was **Anthony Davis** in 19 and 20. It was **Kevin Durant** in 2022. But this to me feels different. In the early years, **Jaylen Brown** was just like this young kid that had proven nothing. In 2022, he had proven something, and I reported at the time that he was pretty pissed off at those trade talks. He is being dangled for a player that is older than him that hadn't accomplished what he had as recently as he did in **Kevin Durant**."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZmTgjOijFFA&t=261s

**5. Chris Mannix — "the player that I think they covet, the player that now I think they'd love to get their hands on is Evan Mobley" — Evan Mobley as a potential trade target for the Celtics** [09:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZmTgjOijFFA&t=591s)

Chris Mannix: "The player that I think they covet, the player that now I think they'd love to get their hands on is **Evan Mobley**. **Evan Mobley** is kind of Giannis light, right? He is not the dynamic offensive player Giannis is, but he is a defensive, former defensive player of the year who still has room to grow, especially with his perimeter game. That is a player I think they would love to get their hands on. Doing a deal with Cleveland is very complicated for a lot of reasons, not the least of which is the Cavs currently are a second-apron team."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZmTgjOijFFA&t=591s
