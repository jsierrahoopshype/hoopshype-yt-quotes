# Noa Essengue & Joakim Noah discuss life in the NBA 🇫🇷 — *Chicago Bulls*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RIk4dJWbMSg

_Speakers identified: Joakim Noah, Noa Essengue_

**1. Noa Essengue — "I had shoulder surgery in December, so I had been playing with an injury for three years." — Noa Essengue on playing through a shoulder injury before surgery** [00:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RIk4dJWbMSg&t=47s)

**Joakim Noah:** "Tell us a little about your injury."

**Noa Essengue:** "I had shoulder surgery in December, so I had been playing with an injury for three years. But you have played basketball, you know what it is like when you have goals in life. You tell yourself as long as it does not crack, you keep going and you push as hard as you can. That is what I tried to do. I knew I had the draft in mind so I just wanted to keep playing."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RIk4dJWbMSg&t=47s

**2. Noa Essengue — "Everything is faster, more physical, decisions are made faster." — Noa Essengue on the cultural adjustment from European basketball to the NBA** [03:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RIk4dJWbMSg&t=193s)

**Joakim Noah:** "Tell us a little about the change in culture. Was it a big shock for you?"

**Noa Essengue:** "In reality, I would not say so much. I would say more outside basketball than in basketball. I started professional when I was fifteen. I have been through a lot. I played in Orleans, then I went through INSEP. After I went to Germany, I had already discovered several cities, several countries, even different ways of playing. You know what Europe is like. It is very physical, the level is excellent. When you succeeded, you can still see that there is a difference in level between the players. You see that everything is faster, more physical, decisions are made faster. But it is not something where you say to yourself, 'Wow, I cannot do anything.' Once you are on the court, in the end it is a basketball game, it is more about the desire and who will give it the most."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RIk4dJWbMSg&t=193s

**3. Noa Essengue — "The two who helped me a lot at the time of my injury were Coby White and Ayo Dosunmu." — Coby White and Ayo Dosunmu helping Noa Essengue during rehab** [05:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RIk4dJWbMSg&t=334s)

**Joakim Noah:** "Did you learn things, are there teammates who taught you things, who helped you for this year?"

**Noa Essengue:** "Oh yes, and I would say first of all the two who helped me a lot at the time of my injury were **Coby White** and **Ayo Dosunmu**. Because it is two guys who went through this operation too. So talking with them, talking about how rehabilitation was, how long it takes, if it was hard to come back afterwards, for this injury, they helped me a lot."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RIk4dJWbMSg&t=334s
