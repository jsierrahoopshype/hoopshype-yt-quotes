I mean, he was the best player on the floor in that big moment, the the legacy game that a lot of people were kind of placing the title on for him. 35 points, nine assists, 12 for 21 shooting from the field, two for five shooting from deep, three turnovers, but I still felt like he was making the right plays, right decision-making, he was aggressive when he needed to be."
