**1. Bobby Marks — "OKC is in a unique situation just based on where we are with the rules of the CBA." — Oklahoma City Thunder's unique position under the CBA rules** [01:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kBVb4PdAjvU&t=83s)

Bobby Marks: "The Thunder are probably the most anticipated team to keep an eye on this offseason. I think certainly when you look at other teams, of course the Lakers, I think OKC is in a unique situation just based on where we are with the rules of the CBA. People want to know are they going to be another casualty of the second apron, what is going to happen with their roster. You hear crazy trade ideas, are they going to be in play for Giannis, are they going to trade Chad, are they going to do all these things, and I am like, it is why I am probably not getting on social media and reading everything that is out there, which is probably 95% not true."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kBVb4PdAjvU&t=83s

**2. Bobby Marks — "You have 15 players coming back under contract. Your depth, Wallace, Caruso, Topic, McCain, Isaiah Joe, AJ Mitchell." — Thunder roster depth and draft asset situation** [02:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kBVb4PdAjvU&t=133s)

Bobby Marks: "64 and 18, another 61-win season, three picks, 12, 17, and 37. The pick 12 is from the Clippers from the **Paul George** trade. They and Atlanta are the only two playoff teams that have a lottery pick. The 17th pick is from Philadelphia as part of the **Al Horford** trade. You have 15 players coming back under contract. Your depth, Wallace, Caruso, Topic, McCain, Isaiah Joe, AJ Mitchell. Kenrich Williams has a team option, Wiggins, who you drafted last year but was out with an ACL, and of course Jalen Williams."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kBVb4PdAjvU&t=133s

**3. Bobby Marks — "We've been limited in what tools we can use with where we are right now around the second apron." — Brad Stevens on Boston's limited tools due to the second apron** [06:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kBVb4PdAjvU&t=370s)

Bobby Marks: "I pulled a quote from **Brad Stevens** and it said, we've been limited in what tools we can use with where we are right now around the second apron. The tools that he mentioned basically is the inability to send out cash in a trade, use at least 6.1 of the non-tax mid-level exception, the inability to take back more salary in a trade, the inability to aggregate contracts in a trade."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kBVb4PdAjvU&t=370s

**4. Bobby Marks — "Since 2021, they've had the fifth lowest cumulative payroll and for the sixth straight season they have not paid." — Thunder's payroll and luxury tax situation** [08:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kBVb4PdAjvU&t=495s)

Bobby Marks: "Since 2021, they've had the fifth lowest cumulative payroll and for the sixth straight season they have not paid a luxury tax. So, when you're looking at paying a $213 million plus tax penalty, that is kind of where you can basically offset everything like that."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kBVb4PdAjvU&t=495s

**5. Bobby Marks — "When you aggregate contracts, you cannot have your post-transaction salary over the second apron." — Why the Thunder cannot easily make major trades** [10:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kBVb4PdAjvU&t=638s)

Bobby Marks: "When you aggregate contracts, you cannot have your post-transaction salary over the second apron. You cannot trade a $50 million player, which they don't have, for a $58 million because you are taking back more salary. And even if you wanted to say we're going to decline Lu Dort and Kenrich Williams and that's $25 million, you are still a first and second apron team. I don't see a scenario where you're not going to be a second apron team, and that is okay if you're in Oklahoma City."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kBVb4PdAjvU&t=638s

Bobby Marks: "I love when people say, well, they're going to just decline Isaiah and he's going to become a free agent.
