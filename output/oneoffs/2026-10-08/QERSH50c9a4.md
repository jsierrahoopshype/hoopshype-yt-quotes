**1. Damian Lillard — "I was prepared mentally and physically, so I felt like myself. I felt like I was just playing another game." — Damian Lillard on his mental and physical recovery process** [02:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QERSH50c9a4&t=169s)

Damian Lillard: "I feel great. I think the amount of time that went by, I'm happy that I used the time. I didn't procrastinate with trying to figure it out. Right away I got to work and I checked every box. So once it was time to get back on the court, it wasn't on my mind or thinking about what it felt like or what it could be like when it got physical. I was prepared mentally and physically, so I felt like myself. I felt like I was just playing another game."

**2. Damian Lillard — "I get to the gym early in the morning, do on the table, do some manual therapy." — Damian Lillard details his daily rehab routine** [03:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QERSH50c9a4&t=215s)

Damian Lillard: "Pretty much it was show up. I was going through that rehab process still to just make sure I stayed on top of it. So I get to the gym early in the morning, do on the table, do some manual therapy, then I go in the weight room for an hour, do an activation and kind of just keep touching on those things that I have been doing for the whole year just building back and then get on the court for an hour and 15, hour and 20 minutes and get my work in."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QERSH50c9a4&t=215s

**3. Chris Haynes — "What is your rapport like with Ja Morant?" — Chris Haynes asks Damian Lillard about his rapport with Ja Morant** [04:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QERSH50c9a4&t=269s)

**Chris Haynes:** "The talk has been about this team and the plethora of point guards you guys have and the extra ball handlers you guys have. It's going to be adjustment for everybody. I want to ask you how did it feel for you out there today and secondly, what is your rapport like with **Ja Morant**?"

**Damian Lillard:** "Today it felt good. For me, as somebody who's carried a heavy load the majority of my career, I know how the taxing it can be. It's hard to stay healthy that way. It's hard to show up each night and be great when you know it's going to be such a heavy load and your body's not feeling great. Tonight it felt simple. Having Ja, having Deni out there, other guys that can handle the ball and make plays to where I was able to get more catch and shoot opportunities, I was able to find my way off the ball more."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QERSH50c9a4&t=269s

**4. Damian Lillard — "My ultimate goal is to help our team take it a step further than we did last year." — Damian Lillard on his goals for the upcoming season** [06:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QERSH50c9a4&t=397s)

Damian Lillard: "My ultimate goal is to come back off a major injury and be myself. I think one thing that I've learned through this process is that if you do it right and you take time and you don't skip through steps in that process and your mind is in the right place, you can get back to everything that you had before. I feel explosive going both directions. I feel like I can get any shot off from any range. My ultimate goal is obviously to help our team take it a step further than we did last year, getting to the playoffs and try to improve on that, play a little bit further into the postseason."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QERSH50c9a4&t=397s

**5. Rudy Gay — "Him and Ja have found some type of connection during practice." — Rudy Gay and Chris Haynes discuss Lillard and Morant's chemistry** [09:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QERSH50c9a4&t=579s)

**Chris Haynes:** "One thing he has been telling me all camp is that him and Ja have found some type of connection during practice where Ja is finding him for open catch and shoot opportunities. If you know Dame throughout his career, he's usually the one that's setting up plays for his teammates and himself. So it's a luxury to have somebody else that can handle the ball, facilitate and find him open for some easy shots."

**Rudy Gay:** "It's a mental process and getting back to the game, it just makes it all well worth it really."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QERSH50c9a4&t=579s

**6. Rudy Gay — "They have to swarm the offense. They have to be active and they have to cover for each other." — Rudy Gay on the Trail Blazers' defensive strategy** [13:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QERSH50c9a4&t=834s)

Rudy Gay: "They have to swarm the offense. They have to be active and they have to cover for each other. You have **Donovan Clingan** who is by the rim. You have to keep him by the rim to protect guys going past the one and two which will be Dame and Ja. So they have that set to where they can be protected. It's just those matchups where you draw. In basketball, if someone has a mismatch, you take that. You need another person to help them out. So that creates the indecision in the defense. So someone has to be open. If you continue to move the ball, there will be open someone on the other end."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QERSH50c9a4&t=834s
