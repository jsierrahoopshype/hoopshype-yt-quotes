**12. Jalen Brunson — "We just kept chipping away, and what I mean by that is throughout the entire year." — Jalen Brunson on the team's mindset during the playoffs** [23:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C2_jZEAkCGk&t=1391s)

Jalen Brunson: "For us, I feel like we just kept chipping away. And what I mean by that is throughout the entire year, everyone says, you guys are playing great right now or you guys are playing terrible right now, like there's highs and lows of the season, but as a team, we want to be playing the best that we can by the end of the year. So we just kept chipping away, getting better, chipping away, finding ways to get better every single day. That was just our mindset. When it came to us doing what we did, yeah, it seems like, yeah, we were just playing out of this world, but we just kept chipping away, chipping away. Everything was zero-zero. We weren't thinking about the next game, we were focusing on the moment. And the moment we did, think about the next game, we were game two of the Sixers series and game one of the Eastern Conference Finals, yeah, it just wasn't how we wanted to play, but we all put each other accountable, we all understand what we wanted to do, so it was a quick fix because we know what our goals were and we know that we were just doing whatever it took and we had no egos, and nothing needed to be said, nothing needed to be argued about, it just needed to be said and then we were back, we were back in clicking. So we just kept finding a way, that was our motto, just find a way."
