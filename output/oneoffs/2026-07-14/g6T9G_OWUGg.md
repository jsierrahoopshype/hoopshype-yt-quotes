**2. Chiney Ogwumike — "It's time to win. And I know that the Eastern Conference right now is an arms race" — Chiney Ogwumike calls for a new winning era in Washington** [01:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g6T9G_OWUGg&t=92s)

Chiney Ogwumike: "Washington, it's time to wake up. It's winning time, finally, please Lord. Because it has been since the last time the Wizards won 50 games, 1979. The last time they won a playoff series, 2017. The last time they were in the playoffs, 2021. It's time to win. And I know that the Eastern Conference right now is an arms race, but when you look at their roster, you need a big three in a different way, especially when you have **Anthony Davis** coming in at the literal three and D position that can bring in an influx of accountability and just passion for the game. You have **Trae Young**, an all-NBA type of point guard. He's always up there with scoring and assists, and you potentially have **Anthony Davis** as a center. We know what he's capable of. This is a solid roster to get wins."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g6T9G_OWUGg&t=92s

**3. Kendrick Perkins — "this is a moment for him to be selfish. Selfish in a way that you want to find a home" — Kendrick Perkins urges Anthony Davis to embrace his Washington tenure** [02:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g6T9G_OWUGg&t=156s)

Kendrick Perkins: "For **Anthony Davis**, this is a moment for him to be selfish. Selfish in a way that you want to find a home. If I'm **Anthony Davis**, I embrace this situation. I would actually go to **Rich Paul**, his agent, and tell him, I'm not looking to get traded to any other destination. No, I want to be in Washington. I want to show the world that I'm still AD. AD when healthy is still a top 10 to top 15 player in this league. He's still a guy that could go out there and is going to give you 25 points, 12 rebounds, and two blocks a night. And so again, AD, I don't want to look at him on the back part of his career and look at him as a journeyman."

**4. Zach Lowe — "the reason that he's in Washington is because the Mavericks didn't want to deal with this discussion" — Zach Lowe on the financial reality of Anthony Davis in Washington** [03:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g6T9G_OWUGg&t=235s)

Zach Lowe: "Let's be honest, the reason that he's in Washington is because the Mavericks didn't want to deal with this discussion about he wants an extension. And it's not just that he wants an extension, he wants a massive extension, a max or near max type of extension. That's why he went to Washington. That's why there wasn't much of a market for him before the trade deadline last year and the Wizards didn't have to give up a whole lot to get him. There is real pressure on the front office in Washington to produce a winner right now and the last thing they need is some kind of black cloud hanging over AD because you can't get on the same page in terms of how he fits for the future financially."
