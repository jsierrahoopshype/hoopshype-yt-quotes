# Yianni Kourakis goes one-on-one with Miami Heat star Giannis Antetokounmpo — *WPBF 25 News*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uoBJTMkzAEQ

_Speakers identified: Yianni Kourakis, Giannis Antetokounmpo_

**1. Giannis Antetokounmpo — "people talk about Heat culture but until you come in and you witness it first hand" — Giannis Antetokounmpo on the unique culture of the Miami Heat** [01:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uoBJTMkzAEQ&t=69s)

Giannis Antetokounmpo: "I am very impressed. The attitude and the way they approach practice and the day has been amazing. People talk about Heat culture but until you come in and you witness it first hand, you don't really understand. But now that I am here, I understand why they always play hard, they always compete, they always have one another's back. That is why they have been so good for many years. Now I am excited to be part of that culture and hopefully we can make good things happen."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uoBJTMkzAEQ&t=69s

**2. Giannis Antetokounmpo — "I want to prove that I am still one of the best players in the world" — Giannis Antetokounmpo on his personal goals and proving his status** [01:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uoBJTMkzAEQ&t=111s)

Giannis Antetokounmpo: "I want to prove that I am still one of the best players in the world. I want to be able to do this for a long period of time. I am not in the point of proving to other people, I just want to prove to myself that I can go to a team and help them win, help them be good, shape, leave a mark in a different organization, different franchise."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uoBJTMkzAEQ&t=111s

**3. Giannis Antetokounmpo — "The people like sitting right of you, look, Clay Thompson" — Giannis Antetokounmpo on the championship potential of the current roster** [02:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uoBJTMkzAEQ&t=145s)

Giannis Antetokounmpo: "The people like sitting right of you, look, **Clay Thompson**. You know, it is weird because I grew up watching him play when I was 19, 18, 20 years old, playing in the NBA Finals. It kind of shaped our young adulthood. So, being in the same locker room with him is kind of weird. At the same time, being in the same locker room with Bam that we have had so many battles, that is also weird. Seeing Coach Spo that he was creating all these defenses to guard me and face me in the playoffs and now we are on the same side, that is also weird. There is a lot of weird feelings, but I am going to get used to it."
