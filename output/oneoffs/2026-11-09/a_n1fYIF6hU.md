**6. Mike Conley — "The idea behind it was just basically that there is this avenue where good guys can accomplish" — Mike Conley on the 'Good Guys Finish First' brand philosophy** [33:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a_n1fYIF6hU&t=2018s)

Mike Conley: "The idea behind it was just basically that there is this avenue where good guys can accomplish a lot of great things. You don't have to be the meanest person or stepping over people and always clearing a path. And just this idea that was brought to a lot of people that you have to be a certain way in order to be successful. I didn't agree with that, and I think that you should find that power within all the people who try to do things the right way and raise a certain way and maybe a little bit quieter or shy or whatever you may be, but you still have the same chances of success as anybody else."
