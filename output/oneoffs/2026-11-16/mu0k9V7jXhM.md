# Nikola Vucevic on Celtics Playoff Loss and Tatum-Brown Relationship | Exclusive Interview — *Celtics on CLNS*

**1. Nikola Vucevic — "I never got the chance to build a really chemistry with him out there." — Nikola Vucevic on his injury and chemistry with Jayson Tatum** [00:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mu0k9V7jXhM&t=42s)

Nikola Vucevic: "I think for sure it would have been better if I didn't get hurt, especially the timing of it with **Jayson Tatum** coming back and I go out. I never got the chance to build a really chemistry with him out there and it was different than before that when I was playing and he wasn't out there. Unfortunately, injuries are part of the job we do. There was always a little bit of what if, I think for sure it would have been better, but I know I gave it my all while I was here."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mu0k9V7jXhM&t=42s

**2. Nikola Vucevic — "We just never really found our rhythm or groove consistently afterwards." — Nikola Vucevic reflects on the Celtics' playoff loss to the 76ers** [01:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mu0k9V7jXhM&t=112s)

Nikola Vucevic: "Honestly, it is hard to pinpoint one thing. Philadelphia really just played well and we just never really found our rhythm or groove consistently afterwards. Even in that Game 5, we were up a couple points and in that fourth we struggled and then after that we just never found a rhythm. In Game 6 we didn't really play that well and in Game 7, obviously **Joel Embiid** out, it changes things as well and it just wasn't the same. Credit to Philadelphia, they played really well, they picked it up. **Joel Embiid** coming back was big for them as well, **Paul George** had a great series, **Tyrese Maxey**, a lot of the guys. They deservedly won."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mu0k9V7jXhM&t=112s

**3. Nikola Vucevic — "I didn't see anything that made me think that." — Nikola Vucevic on the relationship between Jayson Tatum and Jaylen Brown** [02:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mu0k9V7jXhM&t=176s)

Nikola Vucevic: "No, honestly no. Both seemed to get along well and work well on the court and off the court. During the time I was around, I didn't see anything that made me think that. I think there is a lot of speculation out of the media because of the trade and everything. I think the trade was more so made because of the financial restrictions and it is just sometimes how it goes in sports. Both are great guys, both high-level professionals trying to do things the right way and trying to win."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mu0k9V7jXhM&t=176s

**4. Nikola Vucevic — "They are very good at executing their stuff, they know what they are doing." — Nikola Vucevic on the Celtics' current team dynamics and success** [05:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mu0k9V7jXhM&t=326s)

Nikola Vucevic: "They have been playing really well. The way they play, the system with **Joe Mazzulla** and his staff, they are very detailed. They are very good at executing their stuff, they know what they are doing, they are on top of it. Even the guys that have smaller roles, they stay on them to do what is asked of them and everybody has their role and understands that and sticks to them. You can really see that within the organization and I think that is what also makes them successful."
