**1. Shaquille O'Neal — "The NBA All-Star Dunk Contest has been terrible for the past 10 years." — Shaq criticizes the NBA All-Star Dunk Contest for being terrible for ten years** [00:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=84ohEHZYby0&t=24s)

Shaquille O'Neal: "The NBA All-Star Dunk Contest has been terrible for the past 10 years. If I'm in charge, he's going to be there every year. You got all these other cupcakes that don't want to compete. Let them go out there and put on a show for the people."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=84ohEHZYby0&t=24s

**2. Shaquille O'Neal — "These kids are doing stuff that NBA players can't even imagine to do." — Shaq explains why he started the Dunkman League for professional dunkers** [06:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=84ohEHZYby0&t=361s)

Shaquille O'Neal: "The NBA All-Star Dunk Contest has been terrible for the past 10 years. If you look at the landscape of dunking, you see all these guys. Back when I was growing up, you were considered to be an NBA player if you had the title of being the best dunker, but not anymore. These kids are doing stuff that NBA players can't even imagine to do. That's why we started a league for these dunkers."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=84ohEHZYby0&t=361s

**3. Mac McClung — "It being a sport was all I ever dreamed of." — Mac McClung on the significance of the Dunkman League as a professional sport** [07:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=84ohEHZYby0&t=421s)

Mac McClung: "It being a sport was all I ever dreamed of. I saw these guys traveling all over the world doing this as a job, and I saw them, and I was like, I want to do that. And now that it's actually going to be a sport, I can say I'm a professional dunker."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=84ohEHZYby0&t=421s

**4. Mac McClung — "The fact that we're going to know what we scored and why we got scored means everything." — Mac McClung on the need for a structured scoring system in dunk contests** [09:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=84ohEHZYby0&t=569s)

Mac McClung: "I've been screwed over in contests so many times. I've gone out to contests before, my best 360 between the legs I've ever done, and the judges weren't even looking, and I got eights and I didn't advance on. The fact that we're going to know what we scored and why we got scored means everything. It's needed a scoring system for decades to be honest."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=84ohEHZYby0&t=569s

**5. Shaquille O'Neal — "The top players are scared to compete." — Shaq criticizes top NBA players for being scared to compete in the Dunk Contest** [17:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=84ohEHZYby0&t=1053s)

Shaquille O'Neal: "The top players are scared to compete. And then the guys that are there, it seems they don't want to be there and they don't know how to put on a show. Mac wants to be there, he wants the world to know who he is and he puts on a show every time."
