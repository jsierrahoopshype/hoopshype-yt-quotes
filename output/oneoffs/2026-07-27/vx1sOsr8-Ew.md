Even 15, obviously, has meaning to it from my family. And I went back to 45. I had actually no reason. **2. Donovan Mitchell — "I think that it's bigger than just basketball, and one of my closest friends, Bud, he died." — Donovan Mitchell on honoring his late friend Bud through his jersey number** [01:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vx1sOsr8-Ew&t=98s)

Donovan Mitchell: "I think that it's bigger than just basketball, and one of my closest friends, Bud, he died in a car accident. I think I was my redshirt year at Baylor. I remember I found out, we had a game the next day. I wasn't playing, I was sitting out, but I was just down in the moment. I was super down. And I wouldn't say we were the closest, but he was close enough where you can tell he looked up to me just because I played basketball and he was a little bit younger. And he was a hard worker, he tried to get to the NBA.
