**2. Isiah Thomas — "Jordan was what we called a reluctant passer. He would actually take on the double-team as a challenge." — Comparing 'Jordan Rules' defensive strategy to modern NBA defense** [02:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gCYJ_hxbbN4&t=125s)

**Dan Patrick:** "Could the Jordan rules work now in today's NBA, let's say against Wembanyama or SGA?"

**Isiah Thomas:** "We actually see San Antonio doing that with SGA. Right now the new name for it is boxes and elbows. And where you see where SGA likes to shoot from mid-range, they have a double-team trap set up for him at the elbow. So if he goes right, he's forced into a double-team. If he goes left, he's forced into a double-team. On the backline, they keep Wembanyama as a rim protector. And then they have a smaller player in rotation shooting out to the corners. That's why you see those corner threes right now that OKC is knocking down. Those are the ones that they're giving up because they're taking the elbow shots or the mid-range shots away from SGA. So it's similar to what we did with Jordan. We would force Jordan left, trap him going left. SGA is more of a passer than Jordan was at that particular time. Jordan was what we called a reluctant passer. He would actually take on the double-team as a challenge and try to score over the double-team as opposed to kicking it out. And when **Phil Jackson** came along, put him in the triangle and put those snipers around him, that's when everything changed."

**4. Isiah Thomas — "Ball dominant guards, that's a new term for a guy that don't like to pass and he hogs all the shots." — The evolution of 'ball-dominant guards' in basketball** [05:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gCYJ_hxbbN4&t=304s)

**Dan Patrick:** "Ball dominant guards, now it seems like it's commonplace. Was there such a thing as a ball dominant guard back when you played?"

**Isiah Thomas:** "No, because you had guys in the locker room who would say if you don't pass me the ball, I'm going to stick your little ass up on the wall. Big guys were, you know, they wanted to play too, and it was a team game where everyone shared. But, ball dominant guards, that's a new term for a guy that don't like to pass and he hogs all the shots. We used to call them hogs. Now they call them ball-dominant."

**6. Isiah Thomas — "The teaching in our game has drastically, in my opinion, gone down." — Why Kareem Abdul-Jabbar's skyhook is no longer taught** [08:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gCYJ_hxbbN4&t=480s)

**Dan Patrick:** "Nobody's really tried to master that. Why is that?"

**Isiah Thomas:** "Well, the teaching in our game has drastically, in my opinion, gone down. And I think what Kareem, he had great teachers. And not only did he have great teachers, but they knew how to teach. So when you talk about the greatest shot ever, being coached by Wooden, and then in high school, he had great fundamentals, he had great teaching. We have good coaches in our game now. I don't know if we have great teachers in our game. And remember, Dan, in order to be a coach back then, you had to have dual degrees. You had to have a Master's degree, and then you were called a teacher. And the coaches back then, they referred to themselves as teachers first, and then a coach. Right now we have a lot of coaches, but we don't really have teachers. So, when you're talking about Kareem's fundamental base and that shot, who is taking the time to really teach it, and who knows how to teach it?"

**7. Isiah Thomas — "I can't think of a playoff situation where a team has lost a 22-point lead with seven minutes to go." — The unprecedented challenge of the Cavaliers' playoff loss** [09:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gCYJ_hxbbN4&t=560s)

**Dan Patrick:** "What's it like to come back from a loss? I don't know if you ever experienced what the **Cavaliers** or anything near what they went through to then regroup and then play a day later."

**Isiah Thomas:** "I've never gone through anything like that, and very rarely have we seen it in the history of our game, right? So, they will be a first to see how a team bounces back. Because I can't think of a playoff situation where a team has lost a 22-point lead with seven minutes to go on the road, in Game 1, and now you got to come back and regroup in Game 2 and think you're going to beat that team after you've had your shot. So I don't know how they regroup. I expect them to go down 2-0 and hopefully they can come back at home and regroup. But trying to regroup on the road, that's going to be tough. If almost impossible."

**8. Isiah Thomas — "He was a freak athletically. Running, jumping, rebounding. How smart and brilliant he was about the game." — Dennis Rodman's unique athletic and mental approach to basketball** [12:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gCYJ_hxbbN4&t=746s)

**Dan Patrick:** "Can you see a player trying to be like **Dennis Rodman** today?"

**Isiah Thomas:** "I think we have several who try to imitate his style defensively. And they're known for defending and rebounding in our league right now. What they don't have is his kind of moxie and belief. When Dennis came in, athletically, you saw Dan, he was a freak athletically. Running, jumping, rebounding. And what I really admired about him and got to understand was how smart and brilliant he was about the game and accepting, keyword accepting, what he could do and what he couldn't do. And I see players now trying to maximize and do things that they can't do, but if they focused on rebounding and defending as he did his whole career, I think he went into the Hall of Fame and never averaged double digits in scoring. But, rebounding and defending is what he did and what he was known for. There are a lot of players who have that capability, but I don't know if they have that perseverance and discipline to come to the arena every night and say, this is what I'm going to do."

**9. Isiah Thomas — "Kevin Durant is right there with Curry in terms of being the best shooter." — Kevin Durant's shooting prowess compared to Stephen Curry** [14:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gCYJ_hxbbN4&t=847s)

**Dan Patrick:** "If not Steph Curry, then who is the best shooter in the NBA?"

**Isiah Thomas:** "**Kevin Durant**. I would say **Kevin Durant** is right there with Curry in terms of being the best shooter. The numbers say he is, and then what you just articulated, confirms it. But the numbers say that, I mean, when we talk about Curry and shooting, right? I look at **Reggie Miller**, I look at **Larry Bird**, **Ray Allen**. Those guys were great shooters. It used to be a time if you shot from that distance and you missed, you would get cut, you were out of the league. What Curry has done is made shooting from that range and us accepting people missing from that range. That's where he's changed the perception of the game."
