**6. Caroline Fenton — Chemeka Holdsclaw on new athletes facing family, friends' financial pressure — "I had to, whenever I made it big, that was the sacrifice that I had to make, cutting a lot of people out of my life."** [06:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FchwHluvnug&t=403s)

"**Chemeka Holdsclaw** said, 'I would say there's a majority of these young ladies who may be the first person in their family making this type of money, and so they need someone to educate them on it.' That's another really difficult aspect of this is the family aspect, your friends aspect. I did radio for a long time with a former NFL player, in the league for 15 years, made a lot of money playing in the NFL. He came from humble beginnings. He said, 'All of a sudden, when the checks started to come in, when I started to play well, cousins, family friends, aunts and uncles, friends, just people that he hadn't talked to in a long time, coming out of the woodwork. Hey, can I have some money to pay my rent this month? Hey, can I have some money to pay my mortgage? Hey, can I have some money for whatever it might be?' It might sound really innocent there, like, 'Hey, I promise I'll pay you back. Hey, it's only a little bit here and there.' But he said, 'I had to, whenever I made it big, that was the sacrifice that I had to make, cutting a lot of people out of my life because my only commitment is to my wife and my children.' That is it. If I give out money, if I pay these $10,000 loans here and there, which doesn't sound like a lot when you're making tens of millions of dollars, that really starts to add up. Then people still come by, 'Again, I'll pay you back again. I'll pay you back again.' They keep coming back and asking for more money."
