# Tyrese Maxey speaks on recruiting LeBron James to Philadelphia 76ers — *Chaz NBA*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=apOii_VT6I4

_Speakers identified: Tyrese Maxey_

**1. Tyrese Maxey — "I told him, you are always welcome to come to Philly if you want to." — Tyrese Maxey on his recruitment of LeBron James to Philadelphia** [00:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=apOii_VT6I4&t=23s)

Tyrese Maxey: "I didn't know at first if it was possible or not. I remember, I don't know if he was a free agent two years ago or last year, and we work out in the morning. So I texted him, I said, 'Man, get me LeBron there. I'm about to mess with him real quick,' just on some funny stuff. I hit him, I said, 'Yo, you are always welcome to come to Philly if you want to,' before he resigned back with the Lakers. We knew he was going back to the Lakers, but I'm just messing with him."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=apOii_VT6I4&t=23s

**2. Tyrese Maxey — "I don't know what he's going to do, so man, holler at him if you're thinking." — Tyrese Maxey on his recruitment efforts during LeBron James' free agency** [00:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=apOii_VT6I4&t=45s)

Tyrese Maxey: "This time, I don't know what he's going to do. He doesn't know what he's going to do, so man, holler at him if you're thinking. So I hit him, I'm like, 'Yo, pull up. I think we got a good chance. I feel like you can help us.' You are arguably, if not the greatest of all time, so why wouldn't we want you on our team? I just gave him the rundown on that, and I probably talked to him at least three or four times."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=apOii_VT6I4&t=45s

**3. Tyrese Maxey — "Don't think that this changes anything for you as far as your development and your progression." — Tyrese Maxey on LeBron James' impact on his own development** [01:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=apOii_VT6I4&t=117s)

Tyrese Maxey: "I know people say LeBron going to sacrifice a lot, right? I was in this same kind of predicament my second year when James came on the team. As soon as James got there, he pulled me to the side and said, 'Don't think that this changes anything for you as far as your development and your progression and your aggressiveness.' He was giving me the ball and making me be aggressive, and that's what we're going to do to LeBron. He's a key piece to this team, man. His youth, his explosiveness, the way he defends, his growth, it's going to be big for us."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=apOii_VT6I4&t=117s
