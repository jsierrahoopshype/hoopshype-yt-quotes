**1. Trey Jemison III — "I kept up a lot saying that's my wife, when it was my fiancee." — Trey Jemison III on keeping his wedding a secret from the media** [01:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Z5LrM4Ygdc&t=65s)

**Lindsay Dunn:** "I know it wasn't a fake wedding, but you told your guests at your wedding that, hey, surprise, we were already married for a year. How were you able to keep that under wraps from the media?"

**2. Trey Jemison III — "CJ McCollum is the only guy who could beat the Knicks, bro. He was going out of his body." — Trey Jemison III on the Knicks' playoff run and CJ McCollum** [02:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Z5LrM4Ygdc&t=162s)

Trey Jemison III: "New York is a unique place, man. It was phenomenal to be around and it was kind of crazy the whole time. We went on that crazy run, we were down 2-1 to Atlanta, and after that, it was uphill from there. The city was showing so much love. Everywhere you went, it was Knicks stuff, so it was a great time. **CJ McCollum** is the only guy who could beat the Knicks, bro. He was going out of his body. CJ is a great player and a good friend of mine, so I was proud of him."

**3. Trey Jemison III — "I'm going to be the interior defense, let nothing free inside, no easy screens." — Trey Jemison III on his defensive role and playing style** [05:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Z5LrM4Ygdc&t=328s)

Trey Jemison III: "To be honest, I hate to say bully, but I'm a brick wall. I'm going to be the interior defense, let nothing free inside, no easy screens. Pretty much just bullying guys to get out the way and bringing that pressure so nothing is easy, so the guards can do their job, they can play free, I can do the dirty work."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Z5LrM4Ygdc&t=328s

**4. Trey Jemison III — "I get a text in the group chat saying, I got traded. We thought it was a joke." — Trey Jemison III on being traded to the Lakers** [06:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Z5LrM4Ygdc&t=390s)

Trey Jemison III: "I was in New York the night they traded for **Luka Doncic**. We were supposed to go to team dinner, man, and **Anthony Davis** was like, hey, we're going to dinner tonight everybody, get ready. I get a text in the group chat saying, I got traded. We thought it was a joke. And the next thing you know, Shams' tweets, and when Shams tweets it's pretty much law. It was crazy, man. First off, **Anthony Davis** was phenomenal to play with and to get Luka was super dope. And to play with Luka and **LeBron James** on the same court, I learned so much. It was so cool to be around them."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Z5LrM4Ygdc&t=390s

**5. Trey Jemison III — "I went from being in my mom's house on a Tuesday to Thursday playing in LA." — Trey Jemison III on his whirlwind Lakers debut** [07:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Z5LrM4Ygdc&t=433s)

Trey Jemison III: "I was with New Orleans and I didn't get picked up, so I went home. I went home and worked out, I was in my mom's house and my agent called me, he said, hey, get dressed and get ready to go to LA. I said for what? Lakers going to sign you to a two-way. I was like, let's go. I almost started crying. I thought I was going to practice and then play a game, and they were like, no, you're going to play today. I went from being in my mom's house on a Tuesday to Thursday playing in LA."
