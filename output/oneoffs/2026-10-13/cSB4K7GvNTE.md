**3. Andre Drummond — "I ended up taking the pressure off myself. When I first signed, I'm like, I got to come in." — Andre Drummond on managing anxiety and pressure after signing** [03:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cSB4K7GvNTE&t=187s)

Andre Drummond: "I ended up taking the pressure off myself. When I first signed, I'm like, I got to come in guns blazing because these guys just won. I got to be ready to go. And I found myself getting anxiety. Everything I did, I'm like, I can't. I would, it would trickle into my workouts. Like when I would miss, I would be pissed off because I'm thinking about the game. Like if I miss, I'm not going to play. Or I might, you know, start a run or something crazy might happen. So I just shut my brain off and I'm like, focus on what you can control right now, which is these workouts and getting in shape. And I ended up calling **Mike Brown**. Well, **Mike Brown** called me first."

**5. Andre Drummond — "The Knicks reached out because they were getting ready to let go of Mitchell Robinson." — Andre Drummond on the Knicks' roster changes and his opportunity** [07:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cSB4K7GvNTE&t=451s)

Andre Drummond: "The different phone calls I got from different teams, they were all contender teams. And then the Knicks reached out because they were getting ready to let go of **Mitchell Robinson**. So I'm like, this could be interesting. This could be interesting because after Mitch, there's nobody. It would just be me.
