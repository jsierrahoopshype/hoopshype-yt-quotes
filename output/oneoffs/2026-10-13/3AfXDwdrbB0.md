**3. Dillon Brooks — "I'm honestly I'm staring into nothing, I'm just looking into the crowd." — Dillon Brooks on his pre-game mental preparation routine** [02:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3AfXDwdrbB0&t=164s)

Dillon Brooks: "I'm honestly I'm staring into nothing, I'm just looking into the crowd. I'll have old teammates try to come and play with me like **Jamal**, try to play with **Tari** and these guys. At that point, I'm standing and I'm still, and I'm kind of getting into this meditative thinking focus, and I'm just taking in all everything, senses, people around, the perfumes, hearing the noises, and then once that buzzer sounds, I'm just now, I feel like I'm just locked into the game."

**10. Dillon Brooks — "I guess multifaced because at first, like when I first came to the NBA I played no defense." — Dillon Brooks on his evolution from a non-defender to defensive specialist** [10:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3AfXDwdrbB0&t=621s)

Dillon Brooks: "I guess multifaced because at first, like when I first came to the NBA I played no defense. Like none. Like in Oregon we were playing a zone. So I didn't really know. I feel like it was just a challenge for me, you know, to do. Like Coach Fiz, **David Fizdale**, like it was like, yo, the only way you're going to play is if you're going to guard the best player. So I had that all the way up. But then sprinkled into those times like I could still score the ball, but I just never worked on it as much as I worked on the defense. By just lifting, getting bigger, and then once I started taking the other side of it serious, like with like crafting like what my game is, then I'm like, okay, I can do both. I can have both."

**11. Dillon Brooks — "How Memphis had it is where like you're training like you're doing the band stuff." — Dillon Brooks on his defensive training methods** [11:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3AfXDwdrbB0&t=692s)

Dillon Brooks: "How Memphis had it is where like you're training like you're doing the band stuff and you're doing like slides. You ever had one of those where they had like the slide board? It's like, they're just kind of like mimicking the push-off. For me I don't have long arms and my timing for like the ball is not there, but I have good timing of staying in front of a person. So like me just working on like all these muscles here and like watching hella film, like knowing what plays are going to run, like if there's a stagger or dudes coming off from like an elbow, elbow catch into a pick-and-roll, like I'm already learning all those before the games happening."
