# How This Tiny Georgian Club Signed Dwight Howard — *Chip Overseas*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=85ddCvo2OOA

**1. "Dwight Howard signed for $70,000 over the span of two games." — Dwight Howard's contract details for his stint in Georgia** [06:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=85ddCvo2OOA&t=364s)

"**Dwight Howard** signed for $70,000 over the span of two games. He comes in October, so he skips the whole preseason. Let me tell you, as an overseas player in Europe, preseasons are tough. It is not like what we are used to in America. Those guys still believe in an old-school way: run, run, run, be in tip-top shape, go over hundreds and hundreds of sets. So he is skipping all of that. He is coming in for game time."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=85ddCvo2OOA&t=364s

**2. "This is not too dissimilar to what Magic Johnson got when he came out of retirement." — Comparison of Dwight Howard's deal to Magic Johnson's Swiss stint** [07:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=85ddCvo2OOA&t=446s)

"This is not too dissimilar to what **Magic Johnson** got when he came out of retirement to go play in Switzerland. **Magic Johnson** signed for like five games and he got a nice car out of it. But here is the thing: where is the budget for the rest of the team? It does not exist. So this signing wipes out the budget for them to sign anybody else. You have no PG, you have no four-man stretching the floor. It is literally just Dwight and probably just some young players, or some older players that nobody else really wanted to spend a lot of money on."
