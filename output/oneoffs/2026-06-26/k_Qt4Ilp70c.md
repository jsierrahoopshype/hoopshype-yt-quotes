# "We Were Together The Day We Won": RJ On The 2016 Cavs Reunion — *Road Trippin' Show*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k_Qt4Ilp70c

_Speakers identified: Allie Clifton, Richard Jefferson, Channing Frye_

**1. Richard Jefferson — "Every activity we did, 99 percent of it was us together all the time." — Richard Jefferson on the 2016 Cavaliers team's close-knit bond** [00:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k_Qt4Ilp70c&t=6s)

**Richard Jefferson:** "Every activity we did, 99 percent of it was us together all the time. All you see here is the laughing, the joking, and the dancing. We were around each other 15 to 16 hours a day. There was never a time where I was getting tired of these guys. **Delli** and **Shump** coming and injecting some energy into the group was great."

**Channing Frye:** "It felt like we were on a road trip. But the coolest thing I think when people ask is that we were together, but there wasn't a TV on one time. Everyone was just laughing, drinking, chilling."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k_Qt4Ilp70c&t=6s

**2. Channing Frye — "The most annoying part was Delli and Bron waking up to work out every day." — Channing Frye on Matthew Dellavedova and LeBron James's work ethic** [01:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k_Qt4Ilp70c&t=116s)

**Channing Frye:** "The most annoying part was **Delli** and **Bron** waking up to work out every day. That was the most annoying part. It's like, really, you're going to go work out?"

**Richard Jefferson:** "**Delli** is the most shredded dude right now. It's disgusting. **Delli** looks like he's gained 10, 15 pounds. We were like, **Delli**, did you get taller? What the fuck? He is yoked up. He is yoked up, ready for his league."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k_Qt4Ilp70c&t=116s

**3. Richard Jefferson — "There was a guy making our Aperol Spritz for about two hours straight." — Richard Jefferson on the team's excessive Aperol Spritz consumption** [03:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k_Qt4Ilp70c&t=193s)

Richard Jefferson: "When you order an Aperol Spritz, they just give you the glass. So we said, hey, there's a bunch of us, can we get like 20? Which isn't that a big of a deal, right? There was a guy making our Aperol Spritz for about two hours straight. We were crushing them for breakfast and breakfast and lunch. And you're crushing them. And it was like, you do it, and normally they would bring you like three or four. And when we ordered like 10 of them, they're like, we're just going to have somebody come down here and keep making them."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k_Qt4Ilp70c&t=193s

**4. Richard Jefferson — "Kevin had some days where he was bad and then all of a sudden he got really good." — Richard Jefferson evaluates Kevin Love and LeBron James's golf games** [04:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k_Qt4Ilp70c&t=264s)

Richard Jefferson: "**Kevin** got good. I think **Kevin** shot an 82 at Skibo. But he had some days where he was bad and then all of a sudden he got really good. He was hot in the beginning, slowed down in the middle, got hot again at the end. Most improved goes to **Bron** though. He was so trash. His swing was so trash at the beginning of watching him play and posting clips and literally by the end, it was, bro, you almost look like a golfer."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k_Qt4Ilp70c&t=264s

**5. Channing Frye — "It was the group chat from the championship team, not the championship team group chat." — Channing Frye clarifies the 2016 Cavs reunion guest list** [07:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k_Qt4Ilp70c&t=420s)

**Channing Frye:** "So when we talk about like the group chat from the team, there's like six of us. And it's been the same six. Every, you know what I'm saying? There was the full team group chat, then there was like the guys that would hang. It was **JR**, **Swish**, **Tristan**, **Kevin**, **Channing**, and **Bron**. So that's the group of us. And then a conversation kind of got started is and like, hey, it would be wrong if we didn't invite some of the other guys because it's going to look like we're calling it this championship. No, it was the group chat from the championship team, not the championship team group chat."

**Richard Jefferson:** "We love everybody. We love our guy **Kyrie**. **Jordan McRae** was commenting on everything. So it's nothing but love. It's amazing how you can give everybody everything and they're like, well, where's this? Where's that? And it's like, you know, it takes a lot to get a plan out the group chat."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k_Qt4Ilp70c&t=420s
