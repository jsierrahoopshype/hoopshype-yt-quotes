## Trail Blazers Head Coach Micah Nori ESPN Interview | 2026 NBA Summer League — *Portland Trail Blazers*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wsD6xut8UFE

_Speakers identified: Doris Burke, Micah Nori, Mike Breen_

**1. Micah Nori — "the one thing I will say you talk about Chris Finch and that group is winning helps" — Micah Nori on his coaching philosophy and Chris Finch's influence** [00:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wsD6xut8UFE&t=20s)

**Doris Burke:** "He loves system coaches. How much of his style helped you prepare for what you are about to embark upon?"

**Micah Nori:** "The one thing I will say you talk about **Chris Finch** and that group is winning helps a lot. The situation I was in in Minnesota, winning five playoff series in the last three years and going to two Western Conference Finals really helped. And then the opportunity that I have been given by Joe and Tom and those guys, I am looking forward to it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wsD6xut8UFE&t=20s

**2. Micah Nori — "there's no paranoia with him and the other thing is he will let you coach" — Micah Nori on the collaborative coaching culture under Chris Finch** [01:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wsD6xut8UFE&t=74s)

**Doris Burke:** "How much of his style helped you prepare for what you are about to embark upon?"

**Micah Nori:** "The one thing that coach Finch is, he is very very good at what he does. Number one, there's no paranoia with him and the other thing is he will let you coach and gives you a voice. When those things happen, I was fortunate to be with him for a long time and work with him in Denver. Philosophy-wise and the way we think about basketball is very very similar."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wsD6xut8UFE&t=74s

**3. Micah Nori — "you're adding two all-stars in Ja and in Dame" — Micah Nori on the Trail Blazers' roster and adding Ja Morant and Damian Lillard** [02:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wsD6xut8UFE&t=145s)

Micah Nori: "I think the one thing is being a first-time head coach and these guys taking the chance on me with a playoff team and then you're adding two all-stars in Ja and in Dame. It didn't bother me much that we didn't have a pick in the draft just because you're kind of loaded with your roster and then you're adding those two bodies as well to a playoff team and what Thiago did with that staff, we'd like to pick up the baton and continue that and keep going."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wsD6xut8UFE&t=145s

**4. Micah Nori — "it was big time to Robert Williams to get him back" — Micah Nori on defensive priorities and the return of Robert Williams** [03:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wsD6xut8UFE&t=189s)

Micah Nori: "I think the one thing that is huge for us defensively is with Donovan protecting the rim and then it was big time to **Robert Williams** to get him back. So that gives you 48 minutes if you want of really good rim protection. And then what that allows us to do is on the perimeter, we've got some very good defenders obviously and we can protect some guys."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wsD6xut8UFE&t=189s

**5. Micah Nori — "the more guys that can handle the ball, play in space, create for each other, the better off you are" — Micah Nori on the need for high-level decision-making in the modern NBA** [04:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wsD6xut8UFE&t=252s)

Micah Nori: "Everybody talks about being able to make not only make plays for each other, but make quick decisions. The more guys that can handle the ball, play in space, create for each other, the better off you are. Everybody knows the analytical game now, so everybody is being as efficient as possible and the more guys that you have that can make plays, the better off you are."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wsD6xut8UFE&t=252s

**6. Micah Nori — "the only thing he kind of struggled with was his turnovers" — Micah Nori on the next steps for Deni Avdija's development** [04:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wsD6xut8UFE&t=288s)

Micah Nori: "If you look at the only thing he kind of struggled with was his turnovers. And I think the thing is just making decisions in the paint, making decisions a little bit sooner. You don't worry about guys like Deni that again are willing passers. It's not that the turnovers came from this or that, but it's just making those decisions, reading the defense a little bit quicker."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wsD6xut8UFE&t=288s

**7. Micah Nori — "35 years old doesn't feel old in the NBA anymore when you got LeBron James playing in his 40s" — Micah Nori on Damian Lillard's health and longevity** [07:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wsD6xut8UFE&t=450s)

Micah Nori: "I know **Damian Lillard** is coming off a major injury, but 35 years old doesn't feel old in the NBA anymore when you got **LeBron James** playing in his 40s, Steph Curry, late 30s says he wants to keep going. So it feels like, knowing **Damian Lillard**, he's got some years left."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wsD6xut8UFE&t=450s
