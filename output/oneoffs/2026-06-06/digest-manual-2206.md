**2. Lou Williams — "I think that's one of the reasons why Ice Cube isn't getting bigger names, bigger marquee guys." — Lou Williams on the challenge of attracting top talent to BIG3** [02:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmmUmOVEsNo&t=162s)

Lou Williams: "I think that's one of the reasons why **Ice Cube** isn't getting bigger names, bigger marquee guys, because you spend decades of highly competitive shit. So when you retire and then you go into another thing, that's really not the energy that you're on. That's why you see now it's turned into a hybrid where the NBA players that he does have, they're getting celebrated or they're coaching, and then the young overseas guys, the streetball guys are carrying the league because they're still in such a competitive mind state."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmmUmOVEsNo&t=162s

**3. Lou Williams — "I was just in a space like, listen, I ain't arguing with y'all about nothing." — Lou Williams on why he ultimately decided against playing in BIG3** [03:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmmUmOVEsNo&t=207s)

Lou Williams: "I was ready to do it. I wanted to do it. I started training for it. I actually had got to a place where I was preparing for it, was tailor-making my workouts for it. If I can't have what I want, I'm not doing it. The first little pushback I got, I was just like, I'm cool."
