# Kevin Durant Talks Second Season in Houston from Rockets Media Day 26-27 — *SportsTalk790 - KBME Houston*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GSDaG4keMfk

_Speakers identified: Matt, Ross, Kevin Durant_

**1. Matt — "you guys kind of built this thing together a couple of years ago and now you're able to finally get on the court and compete together." — Kevin Durant's return to the court with Fred VanVleet** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GSDaG4keMfk&t=0s)

**Matt:** "Matt and Ross in the morning here with **Kevin Durant**, second season with the Houston Rockets. It's nice seeing **Fred VanVleet** around. You guys built this thing together a couple of years ago and now you're able to finally get on the court and compete together."

**Kevin Durant:** "Excited for him. Such a competitor, love to play basketball. It's always tough to see a guy sit out a whole year from playing. This is our livelihood. Seeing him back on the court and the last couple of weeks seeing him working out and being on the court with him has been fun."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GSDaG4keMfk&t=0s

**2. Matt — "he was great for adjustments. And that's you can only do so much of that when you're trying to rally you guys off the bench." — Kevin Durant's value as a playmaker and veteran leader** [00:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GSDaG4keMfk&t=30s)

**Matt:** "The number one thing about what he brings, I mentioned this to him when we talked to him a little while ago, is that he was great for adjustments. And that's you can only do so much of that when you're trying to rally you guys off the bench. To actually make those in-game adjustments, you as a guy that's worked with a lot of great point guards in your career, certainly value that."

**Kevin Durant:** "For sure. Another veteran on the floor that's been in big games, that knows how to play in those type of games, that understands the flow of a basketball game. We want him to go out there and be himself. Look to score, look to pass, just be an all-around player like he is. We also have to do our part to make his job easier as well. We're not expecting him to come in and just throw everything on him. We have to be a more well-rounded team to help each other out and put each other in great positions to be successful."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GSDaG4keMfk&t=30s

**3. Kevin Durant — "just more comfortable. Just knowing what it's like going into a game with my teammates and coaches and understanding the philosophy of the team." — Kevin Durant's increased comfort level in his second season** [01:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GSDaG4keMfk&t=77s)

Kevin Durant: "Just more comfortable. Just knowing what it's like going into a game with my teammates and coaches and understanding the philosophy of the team. Just more comfortable as you get a year under your belt."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GSDaG4keMfk&t=77s

**4. Matt — "I feel like we can be even closer as a coach-player. Somebody that I always can lean on throughout a game." — Kevin Durant's evolving relationship with head coach Ime Udoka** [01:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GSDaG4keMfk&t=92s)

**Matt:** "You always had a close relationship with **Ime Udoka**. What has it been like, I guess, another year with him as your coach?"

**Kevin Durant:** "It's growing every day. I feel like we can be even closer as a coach-player. Somebody that I always can lean on throughout a game and vice versa. We need to continue to keep building that. Also having Fred and Stephen back is going to be good for all of us, being in these leadership positions and having younger guys on the team that look up to us. It's a group effort in everything we do, leadership, scoring, defending, everything is a group effort."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GSDaG4keMfk&t=92s

**5. Kevin Durant — "injuries are part of the game. We all understand that. Injuries that I had was me trying to make plays for the team." — Kevin Durant on the nature of his playoff injury** [02:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GSDaG4keMfk&t=156s)

Kevin Durant: "Injuries are part of the game. We all understand that. Injuries that I had was me trying to make plays for the team, stepping on somebody's foot or running into somebody's knee. It's not like I was just ran down the court and I had a sprain or a pull somewhere. It was just a contact injury and that's what we play, it's a contact sport. So that's what you get yourself into when you step into a game and with the quick turnaround in the playoffs I just couldn't get back out there for the team."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GSDaG4keMfk&t=156s

**6. Kevin Durant — "it's strategic. It's planned. It's stuff we talk about when me and my trainers before the off-season, even during the season." — Kevin Durant's disciplined approach to off-season training** [03:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GSDaG4keMfk&t=210s)

Kevin Durant: "It's more than that though. It's strategic. It's planned. It's stuff we talk about when me and my trainers before the off-season, even during the season, what I want to work on. So yeah, it looks good on camera, it's good content, but it's a lot of preparation that goes into that stuff before we even get on the floor and it's a lot more that you don't see. And that's that misery that you love as a player, that's what greatness is, is beating at that rock every single day."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GSDaG4keMfk&t=210s

**7. Kevin Durant — "I love movement in the NBA. I love to see new teams form and GMs do their job." — Kevin Durant's perspective on NBA player movement and team building** [05:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GSDaG4keMfk&t=314s)

Kevin Durant: "It was amazing. I loved it. I love movement in the NBA. I love to see new teams form and GMs do their job. Go out there and find ways to make their teams better. Players work so hard to put themselves in positions to be wanted by multiple teams. So yeah, to see that much movement is incredible. To see how different the league is is incredible. Continuity is amazing in the league and that's what wins you championships, you see it with the New York Knicks, but also a fresh new energy is always good too."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GSDaG4keMfk&t=314s

**8. Matt — "How about the fact that game number two we're going to be in Austin, Texas? That's the KD classic, right?" — Kevin Durant on the upcoming game in Austin, Texas** [05:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GSDaG4keMfk&t=359s)
