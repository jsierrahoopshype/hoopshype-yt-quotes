**4. Mark Cuban — "You always have to use analytics to try to get an edge. But you also have to add the eye test." — Mark Cuban on balancing analytics with the eye test and team culture** [05:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s6JTkYfE1uA&t=357s)

**David Carr:** "What percentage of you says, no, it's all analytics? Let's just leave the emotion out of it and what part of it is just like, no, let's just see how the guy feels?"

**Mark Cuban:** "For me, it's always been you always have to use analytics to try to get an edge. You always want to be smarter. But you also have to add the eye test and you also have to add the culture test. Every team has chemistry and culture and you have to know as an owner, manager, general manager exactly what your chemistry and culture is for your organization. And if you know that by pulling that guy, you might be living up to analytics but he's going to be pissed and he's going to be throwing his glove and he might take it out on the bat boys, you better accommodate the culture first. But on the flip side, if your culture is, look, I've been very communicative with all of our players, the whole organization, they understand that we're going to pull this guy, even though he may seem fresh, he may have more in him, because we know over the course of 162 games, this is what's going to happen and we're not in this just to win one game, we're in this to win a championship, so we have to look at the bigger picture."

**7. Mark Cuban — "All the grunt work that every coach in every sport has ever had to do, AI can do better, faster, cheaper." — Mark Cuban on how AI will revolutionize sports coaching and player development** [11:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s6JTkYfE1uA&t=717s)

**David Carr:** "What does the next 5 to 10 years look like with AI and tech in sport?"

**Mark Cuban:** "All the grunt work that every coach in every sport has ever had to do, AI can do better, faster, cheaper. So much of it is reviewing plays, doing analytics of determining what coach runs what in what circumstance, how does a pitcher play this? AI tracks things that you can't remember. What was a quarterback's tendencies? What's a pitcher's? What's a hitter's tendency? Before you used to have somebody in the video room manually track all that stuff. Look at every minute of video. AI speeds up all of that. So it's nearly in real time. So by the time you get to the locker room at halftime, rather than having a limited amount of things, or by the time the manager is deciding whether or not they're going to go out to the mound, they've already got all this analysis of everything that's been going on. And it's going to get even better and where it will make the biggest difference is with health."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s6JTkYfE1uA&t=717s

**Mark Cuban:** "Where it will make the biggest difference is with health. Like in the NBA, you probably saw a lot of the injuries, right, with the ACLs and everything.
