**1. Bam Adebayo — "when you have the Hall of Famers and some of these people you consider guys goats" — Bam Adebayo on the value of veteran support and handling criticism** [00:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rz0H-E_mdis&t=8s)

**Juju:** "How much of a blessing is it to have the OGs online willing to go to bat for you? The fans willing to fight for you? It speaks to your character."

**Bam Adebayo:** "It has been a big blessing. You know the people that got the negative things about it, but when you have the Hall of Famers and some of these people you consider guys goats, creating what I did, I am not going to argue with somebody who has never played basketball that try to discredit what I done or the guys that have never had that chance to be a part of history."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rz0H-E_mdis&t=8s

**2. Bam Adebayo — "I don't give them enough to say anything about me. The most thing I give couch coaches" — Bam Adebayo on why he ignores couch coaches' opinions** [01:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rz0H-E_mdis&t=84s)

Bam Adebayo: "I don't give them enough to say anything about me. The most thing I give couch coaches is like a lot of them don't even say my name right. So it's like, how I'm going to respect you, you can't even say my name right at the end of the day."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rz0H-E_mdis&t=84s

**3. Bam Adebayo — "Dwyane Wade and Udonis Haslem are my guys. They were a part of my journey" — Bam Adebayo on learning from Dwyane Wade's leadership experience** [01:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rz0H-E_mdis&t=108s)

**Jeremy:** "We were talking with **Dwyane Wade** before and I brought up to him that he had the experience before of the guy who was his star in **Dwyane Wade** welcoming in **LeBron James** and **Chris Bosh**. And as a leader, having to figure out how to make that all mesh with everyone. And now he has you as that culture carrier, welcoming in **Giannis Antetokounmpo**. And he said that in his mind, the comparison was that you are two guys who exemplify winning, and that's what players around this league know. But for you, how do you look at that situation that **Dwyane Wade** was in and find yourself in a similar one?"

**Bam Adebayo:** "I text **Dwyane Wade** often. **Dwyane Wade** and Udonis Haslem are my guys. They were a part of my journey when I first got to the NBA. I tell the story about how much I hated **Dwyane Wade**. They also were behind the scenes trying to talk me off that ledge of not cursing him out every day, even though I wanted to as a rookie, which was crazy. To have that ledge as a rookie is wild. But to have those type of vets that really care and want me to be better in this league, that's all you can really ask for."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rz0H-E_mdis&t=108s

**4. Bam Adebayo — "Everything wasn't perfect. You know, that's the one thing people forget." — Bam Adebayo on the reality of the Heat's Big Three era** [03:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rz0H-E_mdis&t=196s)

Bam Adebayo: "When I talk to **Dwyane Wade** about the Big Three era, everything wasn't perfect. You know, that's the one thing people forget. They remember the championships and them throwing the lobs and all that, but it was days where it wasn't perfect. It got hectic for a minute, but when you win championships, you get to look back at that and when you say you earned something, they earned those."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rz0H-E_mdis&t=196s

**5. Bam Adebayo — "You got two front-court guys that can dribble, shoot, pass. You can rebound." — Bam Adebayo on what makes him and Giannis unique as a duo** [05:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rz0H-E_mdis&t=306s)

Bam Adebayo: "You got two front-court guys that can dribble, shoot, pass. You can rebound. Obviously the defensive side is what it is. We know how we want, how great we want that to be. But you got two guys going downhill that are very mobile, that have high IQ that can pass. I feel like people are under-appreciating it right now because they haven't seen it, but as the time goes by, people will learn to appreciate what we did or what we will do."

**6. Bam Adebayo — "that matters when it comes to building championship rosters" — Bam Adebayo on the importance of family in building championship rosters** [06:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rz0H-E_mdis&t=408s)

Bam Adebayo: "When it comes to that family thing, you know that matters when it comes to building championship rosters because you want to be family when you win something like that. Because at the end of the day, y'all can come back 5, 10, 15 years from now and still talk about how great of a time that was when y'all won it, but also like how hard it was to earn that championship.
