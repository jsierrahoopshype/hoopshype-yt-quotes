# Everyone Thought Dr. J Made $6 Million. They Were Wrong. — *Scripps Sports*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OEa7zrU_6-8

_Speakers identified: Sanya Richards-Ross, Julius Erving_

**1. Julius Erving — "I got a five-year contract for $3 million. I don't know why they call me the $6 million man." — Julius Erving clarifies the reality of his $6 million contract** [01:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OEa7zrU_6-8&t=102s)

**Sanya Richards-Ross:** "What does that mean to you for the athletes that have come behind you, that you opened the door for them?"

**Julius Erving:** "They have so far surpassed economically the numbers we were negotiating for. In mine, they call me the $6 million man because there was a $6 million deal to get me from the New York Nets to the Philadelphia 76ers. The Nets got $3 million and I got a five-year contract for $3 million. I don't know why they call me the $6 million man. I didn't see $6 million."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OEa7zrU_6-8&t=102s

**2. Julius Erving — "They go through like a four-second routine, then they step three steps backwards and they take a shot." — Julius Erving critiques the modern NBA's four-second offensive routine** [03:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OEa7zrU_6-8&t=189s)

Julius Erving: "It seems to be a little bit more flash now. Everybody gets the ball, they go between legs, between legs around the back, and it's a routine. They go through like a four-second routine, then they step three steps backwards and they take a shot. It's all good because the referees have been told, look, the people aren't here to see y'all out there just blowing the whistle all the time. There is a lot of contact and a lot of extra steps that go on that they could blow the whistle on, and they would have maybe back in the day, but they try to keep it flowing."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OEa7zrU_6-8&t=189s

Julius Erving: "There is no goat. There is no one athlete who you can look at in any sport and just say that was the best that ever did it. It's all an opinion."
