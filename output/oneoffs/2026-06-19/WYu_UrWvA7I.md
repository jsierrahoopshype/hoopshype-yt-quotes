# Brandin Podziemski Reveals What The Warriors Need at Pick 11 — *95.7 The Game*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WYu_UrWvA7I

_Speakers identified: Willard, Dibs, Brandin Podziemski_

**1. Brandin Podziemski — "another alpha player, could create her own shot, which is something that we needed" — Brandin Podziemski on Gabby Williams' impact on the Seattle Storm** [00:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WYu_UrWvA7I&t=21s)

Brandin Podziemski: "I would say all for the better. Another alpha player, could create her own shot, which is something that we needed on a consistent basis. And so her doing that and then her two-way capability, I think she should be an All-Star because she really is a game-changer."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WYu_UrWvA7I&t=21s

**2. Brandin Podziemski — "they took a big step forward from last year in what they could do" — Brandin Podziemski on the Warriors' championship contention** [01:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WYu_UrWvA7I&t=95s)

Brandin Podziemski: "They are close. Obviously, they took a big step forward from last year in what they could do. In regards to what do I think they need, I cannot put my finger on that at the moment because they are so close in those games. It is not like they are getting blown out by the top teams. So, I think they are close. A lot of games could go one way or the other, but it has been a big improvement from a year ago."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WYu_UrWvA7I&t=95s

**3. Brandin Podziemski — "extra money in your pocket, you will never complain about and it is definitely a motivating factor" — Brandin Podziemski on the importance of the Commissioner's Cup** [02:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WYu_UrWvA7I&t=150s)

Brandin Podziemski: "Obviously, for us, extra money in your pocket, you will never complain about and it is definitely a motivating factor. And I know for them, playing for a cause that the teams decide on and that are important to them, I know that is a motivating factor as well. So, I think it just boosts the competition early on in the season."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WYu_UrWvA7I&t=150s

**4. Brandin Podziemski — "someone who is ready to play, who you can play right away" — Brandin Podziemski on what the Warriors need in the draft** [07:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WYu_UrWvA7I&t=428s)

Brandin Podziemski: "The obvious answer is just someone who is ready to play, who you can play right away, someone that has the experience, who is physically mature enough to play in the games right away. I think that is kind of where as an organization we are at. We are at a stage where we are trying to win as much as we can, and so I think with the 11th pick, you just got to look for a guy that is ready to come in and make an impact right away."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WYu_UrWvA7I&t=428s

**5. Brandin Podziemski — "just welcome them with open arms just like a bunch of guys did for me" — Brandin Podziemski on welcoming new draft picks to the team** [07:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WYu_UrWvA7I&t=472s)

Brandin Podziemski: "At least for me the last two years, I am just going to see who we pick and just welcome them with open arms just like a bunch of guys did for me when I got drafted and really just try to be a mentor in any way I can for whoever we bring in. I think that is just part of our culture at Golden State. The guys who have been there mentor the young guys and it is just like we are a revolving door."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WYu_UrWvA7I&t=472s

**6. Brandin Podziemski — "the feedback I got best was my interview process" — Brandin Podziemski on his NBA draft combine performance** [08:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WYu_UrWvA7I&t=522s)

Brandin Podziemski: "I do agree that the combine helped my case. I had two really good game scrimmages wise. The feedback I got best was my interview process, how I was able to remember things, put things in a perspective where I kind of knew what I was talking about and it really helped my case. It was good to see a bunch of guys, kind of those fringe guys playing in the combine this year as well."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WYu_UrWvA7I&t=522s

**7. Brandin Podziemski — "Nafisa Collier, her pick and roll abilities, pretty much top tier as a rookie" — Brandin Podziemski on the Minnesota Lynx's roster** [09:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WYu_UrWvA7I&t=575s)

Brandin Podziemski: "Just looking at Minnesota, they have not played yet with **Nafisa Collier** yet and **Olivia Miles**. She missed only like three shots last night in LA. So, she is kind of the head of the snake when it comes to that. Her pick and roll abilities, pretty much top tier as a rookie. I think she is just a key to that. If you try to take her out of the game, I am sure Natalie and her staff will have different things that she is going to try to do."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WYu_UrWvA7I&t=575s
