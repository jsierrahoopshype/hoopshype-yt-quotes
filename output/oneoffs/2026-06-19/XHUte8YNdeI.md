**3. Ramona Shelburne — "I know they're allowed to talk to your own free agents, so they've had some discussions already." — LeBron James' ongoing contract discussions with the Lakers** [02:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XHUte8YNdeI&t=126s)

**Steve Mason:** "Do you have a sense for who those teams are, who are the real contenders if LeBron doesn't come back?"

**Ramona Shelburne:** "For me, it's pretty simple. It's either the Lakers, Cleveland, or Golden State. I would be really surprised if it's not one of those three. I know they're allowed to talk to your own free agents, so they've had some discussions already with LeBron and his agents about that, but I don't think we've even gotten to a numbers stage."

**4. Ramona Shelburne — "It's much easier for LeBron to break up with the Lakers than it is for the Lakers." — Why it is easier for LeBron James to leave the Lakers** [05:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XHUte8YNdeI&t=324s)

**Steve Mason:** "Do you think there's less of a chance LeBron comes back to the Lakers than he stays?"

**Ramona Shelburne:** "I think that it's much easier for LeBron to break up with the Lakers than it is for the Lakers to break up with LeBron. In other words, if another team comes at him with, 'Hey, we really want you, but all we can pay you is the mid-level,' that's not insulting to him because that's all they can. They're giving him the most they can give him. Whereas if the Lakers come to him and say, 'Okay, we'll give you 25 a year,' that's insulting to him because he makes 52 now."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XHUte8YNdeI&t=324s

**5. Ramona Shelburne — "When you look at restricted free agency there's two names, Jalen Duren and there's Walker Kessler." — Lakers' restricted free agency targets** [08:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XHUte8YNdeI&t=507s)

**Steve Mason:** "What are they looking for in free agency?"

**Ramona Shelburne:** "When you look at restricted free agency there's two names, **Jalen Duren** and there's **Walker Kessler**. The Lakers would love to have either of them, but it's restricted free agency. So how do you get someone in restricted free agency? You either make an offer they can't refuse, which means you're probably overpaying for them. If you're doing **Walker Kessler** then maybe you're giving him 35, which is an overpay, but that's the only way you're going to get him."
