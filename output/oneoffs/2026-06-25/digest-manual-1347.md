## The Game Beyond the Game, A Leadership talk with Jaylen Brown — *Jaylen Brown*

**1. Jaylen Brown — "Stephen A. doesn't choose the topics, somebody chooses them for him." — Stephen A. Smith's lack of autonomy at ESPN** [01:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=73BvmhVV0cs&t=114s)

Jaylen Brown: "Stephen A. works for ESPN. ESPN is controlled by Disney. Stephen A. doesn't choose the topics, somebody chooses them for him. His willingness has made him extremely valuable to these companies, but a lot of these companies and corporations, they meet four times a year and talk about amongst various industries how they can socially engineer and influence our minds through the technology that they can control without us really even paying attention or even noticing it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=73BvmhVV0cs&t=114s

**2. Jaylen Brown — "I ended up telling him that sports is used as a mechanism for control." — Jaylen Brown on sports as a mechanism for control** [03:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=73BvmhVV0cs&t=221s)

Jaylen Brown: "A writer came up to me and was asking me questions about politics and sports. I just thought I would share my thoughts, the things that I learned at Berkeley. I ended up telling him that sports is used as a mechanism for control. So, that's exactly what he titled the article: 'Jaylen Brown says sports is used as a mechanism for control.' I got some support, but mostly I got a lot of backlash."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=73BvmhVV0cs&t=221s

**3. Jaylen Brown — "They all used their platforms for something beyond sports." — Muhammad Ali, Bill Russell, and Jim Brown's legacy** [04:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=73BvmhVV0cs&t=294s)

Jaylen Brown: "The people that I admire the most were the exact opposite of that. **Muhammad Ali**, **Bill Russell**, **Jim Brown**, they all used their platforms for something beyond sports. So while I'm here, while I got my platform, those who are listening and those who may be listening in the future back home, I just want to encourage people to not be afraid to be who you are, be proud of where you come from, and to think critically and think for yourself."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=73BvmhVV0cs&t=294s

**4. Jaylen Brown — "I lean in on authenticity more than anything." — Jaylen Brown on prioritizing authenticity over strategy** [06:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=73BvmhVV0cs&t=391s)

Jaylen Brown: "When it comes to how I decided to use my platform, I lean in on authenticity more than anything. When I made a decision about how I'm going to use my platform, I'm okay with the consequence, as long as I mean it, authentic. Sometimes development and strategy is great, but sometimes that doesn't go according to plan. I have a plan, but life has a plan, our lives have a plan. It doesn't always work out the way you think. So being true to yourself, I can live with that."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=73BvmhVV0cs&t=391s

**5. Jaylen Brown — "They have spent times and years to build infrastructures that it's hard to compete against." — The challenge of competing against established corporate infrastructures** [07:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=73BvmhVV0cs&t=445s)

Jaylen Brown: "I don't know if one answer is the answer. They have a plan B for everything. They have spent times and years to build infrastructures that it's hard to compete against. But the way I was raised was that even if you can't win, if the principle is important enough to you, you still fight. My brother used to beat me up all the time. I had no chance. My mother used to ask, 'Why do you keep fighting him if you know you're going to lose?' I would look my mother in her eyes and say, 'It's the principle.'"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=73BvmhVV0cs&t=445s

**6. Jaylen Brown — "There is a programming as an athlete to be and act a certain way." — The pressure on athletes to conform to specific identities** [09:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=73BvmhVV0cs&t=573s)

Now my talent is greater than the level of success that they can limit. But if it was up to them, based upon the things that I've said and I've done, I could expect on the back end of my career I got something coming for me for sure."
