## SUMMER WITH THE BEAR | EP1: Back Home — *Guerschon Yabusele*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VSi17DmyzdE

_Speakers identified: Guerschon Yabusele, Benoit Gomis, Christian Belise, Chris Singleton, Fred Weis_

**1. Guerschon Yabusele — "I didn't play the last two games of the season. I had an arm injury." — Guerschon Yabusele on his arm injury and end of season** [00:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VSi17DmyzdE&t=43s)

Guerschon Yabusele: "My last game was on April 13th. For me, it was a little different because I didn't play the last two games of the season. I had an arm injury. Nothing serious, but I had to take care of it, make sure it wasn't something that would linger. At the time, the play was a little scary. We were worried for a second. But we got it checked out and everything was fine, so I got a little rest."

**2. Guerschon Yabusele — "We had an offer from Denver and that it could be cool. It was a two-year deal." — Guerschon Yabusele on his free agency and offers from Denver and New York** [01:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VSi17DmyzdE&t=119s)

Guerschon Yabusele: "I came back to France. I was a free agent, so I spent some time training, staying in shape because we had Team France coming up after that. Then I got a call from my agent telling me first that we had an offer from Denver and that it could be cool. It was a two-year deal, so I started thinking about that option. I had some time to sit down. I had my family there too, we talked about it and then right after that, honestly, just a few minutes later, my agent calls me and says, "I just got off the phone with New York, and New York is also interested in pretty much the same deal. Two years as well.""

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VSi17DmyzdE&t=119s

**3. Guerschon Yabusele — "Personally, that first season was tough. I wasn't playing much, very few utilized." — Guerschon Yabusele on his challenging first season in New York** [03:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VSi17DmyzdE&t=206s)

Guerschon Yabusele: "Personally, that first season was tough. I wasn't playing much, very few utilized, so naturally a lot of things start going through your head. It kind of put me back in the same shoes as ten years ago when I was already in Boston and I'd show up to games thinking, "I might not play tonight." Maybe a couple of minutes. There were games where I'd play two minutes, three minutes. It's tough because I'm a competitor and at that point I want to play, I want to learn, get better, make mistakes, improve, so I really want to be on the court."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VSi17DmyzdE&t=206s

**4. Guerschon Yabusele — "My arrival in Chicago went really well. I'd never been through a trade before." — Guerschon Yabusele on his mid-season trade to Chicago** [05:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VSi17DmyzdE&t=331s)

Guerschon Yabusele: "My arrival in Chicago went really well. I'd never been through a trade before, so I was wondering a little what it was going to be like because you're arriving on a new team, new players, new staff, new front office, so every person you meet is someone new. I played the game in New York, then the next day I got on a plane and joined the team, which was on the road in Toronto. So I got there and introduced myself to everybody. Then when I got to the game, we introduced ourselves again. I met Coach **Billy Donovan** and what's cool is that ten years earlier, when I did workouts with OKC, he was already a coach who really liked me."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VSi17DmyzdE&t=331s

**5. Benoit Gomis — "My role is first of all to analyze his games, see how he performs." — Benoit Gomis on his role as individual coach for Guerschon Yabusele** [13:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VSi17DmyzdE&t=780s)

Benoit Gomis: "My role is first of all to analyze his games, see how he performs, and try to identify what he's doing well, but also look at the areas where he can still improve. And especially based on his team's needs. We put the focus on his outside shooting. He's a player who plays the 4, and can also play the 5 in small-ball lineups. That's the trend being used by NBA coaches and even with Team France. Then we work on all the catch-and-go and post-up situations."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VSi17DmyzdE&t=780s
