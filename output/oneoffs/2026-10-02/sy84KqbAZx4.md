# Tarik Biberović Says He Didn't Come to Dallas Just to Be an NBA Player | Mavericks Training Camp — *Dallas Sports Journal*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sy84KqbAZx4

_Speakers identified: Tarik Biberović, Reporter_

**1. Tarik Biberović — "I came here to make an impact and they also know that so we have that hunger." — Tarik Biberović on his goal to make an impact in Dallas** [02:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sy84KqbAZx4&t=136s)

Tarik Biberović: "The staff also knew what I gave up, what I left behind me. So they also know I didn't come here just to be happy or just to be a part of the team, just to be an NBA player. That was one of my first goals when I was in Europe. I never wanted to be the guy who just came in the NBA just to say he was an NBA player. I came here to make an impact and they also know that so we have that hunger."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sy84KqbAZx4&t=136s

**2. Tarik Biberović — "The reason why I'm brought here is to make their game easier so the guys can have more space." — Tarik Biberović on spacing the floor for Luka Dončić and Kyrie Irving** [03:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sy84KqbAZx4&t=230s)

Tarik Biberović: "The reason why I'm brought here is to make their game easier so the guys can have more space. They can get my job easier too because they are great one-on-one players. And that gets system in players. I don't know how much is seen in the practices, but they understand the system really well and they are sharing the ball and they understand the coach's idea. So it's been beautiful. Both of them are generation talents and it's a blessing to share a court with them."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sy84KqbAZx4&t=230s
