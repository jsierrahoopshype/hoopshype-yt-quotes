You know how you go on these recruiting trips. For me to go to San Diego State, that was a great, that was a great trip. They were really pulling at me. Also at the time, Tulsa was interested in me as well and that school they changed coaches at the time. **Nolan Richardson** was the famous coach there at the time and he left to go to Arkansas and then things just changed. He was recruiting me, but Coach **John Thompson** was recruiting me at Georgetown, so that weighed a lot on my decision along with the fact that we had a few New Orleans guys that made decisions to go to Georgetown as well, so we had a little crew in the city that was committing to Georgetown."

**4. Jaren Jackson Sr. — "I knew I was going to be in the NBA. I wasn't worried about the draft at all." — Jaren Jackson Sr. on his mindset during his undrafted NBA journey** [13:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EDi7g5u8vhk&t=796s)

Jaren Jackson Sr.: "I knew I was going to be in the NBA. I wasn't worried about the draft at all. That was the first year of two rounds. Before then, I think it was five or seven rounds. So people were really kind of upset that they changed. Something about being drafted, every player is excited about and really wanted to happen for them. So I remember the draft night of the first two rounds and I wasn't expected to get drafted. Although if it had happened, I would have been a late second round at best, but it didn't happen, so cool. My teammate Charles Smith was probably expected more to get drafted than myself. He was Big East player of the year and he was my freshman year roommate. So Charles was a very good college player and I just remember we were in Coach Thompson's office for the draft watching it and it ended, two rounds, it was quick. I could see the disappointment for both of us as two players and but immediately I was like, well, forget it. It's on. I need to go try out for a team. They're about to get loaded with me and from there I ended up getting an invite to Summer League in training camp with the New Jersey Nets."
