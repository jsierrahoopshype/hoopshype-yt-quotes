**1. Draymond Green — "I think it's completely lost its purity in the way the game is covered." — Draymond Green argues the game of basketball has lost its purity** [10:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kkdt4yGGcI0&t=656s)

Draymond Green: "I think it's completely lost its purity in the way the game is covered. Basketball, sports in general, used to be a thing that's celebrated. The reason we all loved **Michael Jordan** so much is because of the way he was covered, the way he was coveted, the way he was celebrated. **Michael Jordan**, arguably the greatest player to ever play this game, I think if he played today, it'd be a totally different ball game. He would not be celebrated the way he was celebrated back in the 90s. He would not be covered in the way he was covered back in the 90s. I started following basketball when I was three years old and really loving the game. I can't recall a time of turning on the TV and someone's like, if **Michael Jordan** loses this game, his legacy is tarnished forever."

**2. Draymond Green — "Basketball, we always said it's a child's game. It's not anymore." — Draymond Green says basketball is no longer a child's game** [12:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kkdt4yGGcI0&t=730s)

Draymond Green: "Basketball, we always said it's a child's game. It's not anymore. It's for the rough, wicked, not for the weak. It's for, you got to have tough skin. And when I say it's lost its purity, I think that's a large part of it. **3. Draymond Green — "I want someone to be able to turn on the television, turn on the podcast, and hear game breakdown." — Draymond Green on the importance of athletes controlling their own narratives** [13:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kkdt4yGGcI0&t=819s)

Draymond Green: "I want someone to be able to turn on the television, turn on the podcast, and hear game breakdown. I want a kid to be able to turn this on and know they can learn something like I learned from inside stuff when I was a kid. That's what sports media used to be about, and I learned so much from that, which ultimately is a part of the reason why I'm in the position I am today. I hang my hat on my high basketball IQ, but that dates back to learning from the television. Well, the kid today can't learn from the television. And so, starting a new media is like, all right, how can we continue to tell these stories? How can we continue to build platforms for athletes, for anyone who wants to do media the right way, anyone who wants to cover the game the right way, how can we do that?"

**5. Draymond Green — "You can't be in sports and it's okay for a league to have certain partnerships but not the players." — Draymond Green on the importance of player-team partnerships** [18:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kkdt4yGGcI0&t=1134s)

Draymond Green: "I think it's great. I think when you look at, you can't be in sports and it's okay for a league to have certain partnerships but not the players. That's hypocritical. You look around the league, there's FanDuel partnerships, there's Fanatics sports book partnerships, there's all these different partnerships in the betting space, and these are actual betting markets, not actual betting houses, not actual prediction markets. I think it's incredible that LeBron has the PolyMarket partnership. I think Giannis may have announced a partnership with PolyMarket. I think it's incredible, and I think the range of what an athlete can participate in needs to continue to expand. We're not in the 1960s anymore where athletes aren't doing business at certain levels. You have athletes doing business at the highest levels these days."

**6. Draymond Green — "I think salary cap as a whole is irrelevant. I think get rid of salary cap as a whole." — Draymond Green calls the NBA salary cap irrelevant** [23:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kkdt4yGGcI0&t=1387s)

Draymond Green: "I think salary cap as a whole is irrelevant. I think get rid of salary cap as a whole, and we won't have these types of things. Ultimately, the circumvention was because that guy was worth more than they could pay. And so, if he's worth more than they can pay, why can't he make that? I don't know if anyone ever seen **Elon Musk** compensation package from Tesla, but it's it's absurd. Well, guess what? If he can negotiate it, he can get it. So, why in sports can we possibly negotiate some, but it's a problem? These are the best business owners in the world. They know how to negotiate. These are some of the best business minds, businessmen, business women in the world. In order to become that, you have to know how to negotiate. So, they they can negotiate with all the salary cap stopping the person who they're negotiating against, so I think salary cap as a whole is doing the league a disservice."
