**3. Colm Dillane — "I felt it was based more on this mind state of being open and optimistic." — Colm Dillane on the core philosophy of the KidSuper brand** [06:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9RqE5OFpsA&t=407s)

**Jaren Jackson Jr.:** "After seeing so much of your work that just scratches the surface of what you are, I want to know in your words, what is KidSuper?"

**Colm Dillane:** "For me, obviously fashion design is the catalyst to enter everything. When I was creating the brand, I felt it was based more on this mind state of being open and optimistic, this childlike sense of wonder and ambitious, and a little bit mischievous as children are. I was truly a KidSuper character. I wanted to be the brand that represented soccer the most true. Is KidSuper football club possible? Is KidSuper food truck possible? Is KidSuper media possible? I loved that it all felt part of the one umbrella."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9RqE5OFpsA&t=407s

**5. Colm Dillane — "I didn't realize I created a human slip and slide." — Colm Dillane on the 'runners fast as you can' show concept** [17:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9RqE5OFpsA&t=1038s)

**Colm Dillane:** "I did this fashion show where the models stepped on paint and walked around this cube video, and the last model was in a white suit and he was a break dancer. He would break dance on the floor, and I would have a path of all the painting footsteps. An hour before the show, the venue was like, you can't put paint on the ground. I was like, this is my whole concept. What if I cover everything in plastic, is that okay? I didn't realize I created a human slip and slide. The models stepped on paint, walked, and slipped."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9RqE5OFpsA&t=1038s

**Colm Dillane:** "As I came out at the end, I said, sometimes you slip and fall, but you have to remember to get up and run as fast as you can. The show was called Runners Fast As You Can. **7. Colm Dillane — "Bape was obviously the OG streetwear brand, but they were kind of one of the first." — Colm Dillane on the Bape collaboration and packaging** [27:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9RqE5OFpsA&t=1636s)

**Colm Dillane:** "Bape was obviously the OG streetwear brand, but they were kind of one of the first streetwear brands to really care about packaging because they came from the Japanese lens. The Japanese are really, really focused on detail, attention-driven, and they cared about stuff that maybe American brands weren't caring about at the time, like the packaging, like the experience. And so when I got the honor of doing a Bape collab, I was like, even before we did the shoe, I was like, the packaging has to be incredible."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9RqE5OFpsA&t=1636s

**8. Colm Dillane — "I think people often ask how ideas come to KidSuper and for me it's all problem solving." — Colm Dillane on his problem-solving approach to design** [29:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9RqE5OFpsA&t=1780s)

**Colm Dillane:** "I think people often ask how ideas come to KidSuper and for me it's all problem solving. Okay, you have 48 different countries. How do you represent all of them within a photoshoot? How do you and then you're like, okay, who represents what country? Okay, you get an athlete from one and then so then I was kept going, I was like, man, what's like unanimous across all these countries? And grandmas, and then New York City is a beautiful place because everyone immigrates here. So then I could find 48 grandmothers."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9RqE5OFpsA&t=1780s

**10. Colm Dillane — "The main thing is become the person amongst your friends that is known for finishing things." — Colm Dillane on the importance of finishing products** [45:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9RqE5OFpsA&t=2753s)

**Jaren Jackson Jr.:** "What advice would you give to someone who looks up to you and wants to do what you've done?"

**Colm Dillane:** "I think the main thing is become the person amongst your friends that is known for finishing things. You don't have to be the best, you don't have to be the worst, but if you're a guy that's like every month finishing a product, releasing a product, you will inspire people around you because they'll be like, that's a guy who shows up and you're going to get better because you're learning and making stuff, but do not be a flake."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9RqE5OFpsA&t=2753s
