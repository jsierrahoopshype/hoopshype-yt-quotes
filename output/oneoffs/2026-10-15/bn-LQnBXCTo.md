# Marc Spears talks Ben Simmons documentary, his road back to the #NBA & making amends with Australia — *ESPN Australia*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bn-LQnBXCTo

_Speakers identified: Marc Spears, Ben Simmons_

**1. Marc Spears — "I could have gone home to Australia, I could have gone and got married and had a family." — Ben Simmons' journey to becoming a Sacramento King** [01:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bn-LQnBXCTo&t=91s)

**Marc Spears:** "How did you get **Ben Simmons**? We were in San Antonio and you called me over at a press conference and I knew you had something good for me and you said there is going to be something cooking with **Ben Simmons**, but he is a private guy. So how did this all come about?"

**Ben Simmons:** "I could have gone home to Australia, I could have gone and got married and had a family. I got money, I don't need this. I want to play, I want to be back, I want to write a different chapter."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bn-LQnBXCTo&t=91s

**2. Marc Spears — "He was going to fight that fish until he beat the fish." — Ben Simmons' competitive nature and physical recovery** [03:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bn-LQnBXCTo&t=220s)

Marc Spears: "We went out fishing and look, I am a bayou baby from families from Louisiana and that is what they do, they fish there. Ben likes deep-sea fishing. One day in Key West, I swear we went out on the Atlantic Ocean about an hour and he had us out there for eight hours and one of the big things he did was catch a sailfish for the first time. This is when I knew he was healthy because he ran around the boat, he jumped over three engines and went to the front of the boat. I am like, 'Ben, don't die on the shoot now.' He was doing a whole lot, but the way he is moving shows me his back is okay, his knee is okay, his body is okay. **Doc Rivers** told me he is very competitive, people don't talk about that. I saw that because he was going to fight that fish until he beat the fish."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bn-LQnBXCTo&t=220s

**3. Marc Spears — "The Kings actually had zero interest in July. What changed things was that Ben went to Australia." — The Kings' interest in Ben Simmons after his Australian mini-camp** [05:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bn-LQnBXCTo&t=322s)

Marc Spears: "The Kings actually had zero interest in July. What changed things was that Ben went to Australia and went into that mini-camp. All 30 NBA teams had the ability in July to go watch Ben work out and he was inviting teams to work out and nobody would go see him. But then he goes to this mini-camp in Australia, paid his own way to go there and from all accounts he was fantastic and arguably maybe the best player there. It was there that David Patrick, his godfather, Australian assistant coach and the guy that coached Ben at LSU, ended up telling the Kings that he was looking great and that they definitely should check him out."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bn-LQnBXCTo&t=322s

**4. Marc Spears — "Scott Perry, who lives 40 miles on the off-season away from Miami, went out to Miami." — Scott Perry's role in evaluating Ben Simmons for the Kings** [06:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bn-LQnBXCTo&t=387s)

Marc Spears: "**Scott Perry**, who lives 40 miles on the off-season away from Miami, went out to Miami and saw Ben work out and Ben told me, 'I knew that when **Scott Perry** committed to coming to see me play that I was going to sign with the Kings.' He said he started looking at real estate in Sacramento before the workout."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bn-LQnBXCTo&t=387s

**5. Marc Spears — "I expect him to play next year. I expect him to be in Los Angeles." — Ben Simmons' commitment to the Australian national team** [08:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bn-LQnBXCTo&t=508s)

Marc Spears: "The fact that he came to the mini-camp basically on his own dime said, 'Hey, I want to make amends, I want to play on the Australian national team in the World Cup and the Olympics.' I expect him to play next year. I expect him to be in Los Angeles if K-Porn wants him to be there, which I assume he will. Ben has to be healthy too, but to me, this chapter could change a lot of that perception."
