**3. Rick Barry — "You don't win a championship just having a couple of good players on your team." — Rick Barry on the Warriors' roster depth and championship requirements** [01:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0SGH_E4dkKE&t=103s)

Rick Barry: "If the Warriors do go that route, what are they going to have to give up to get him? And what are they going to do and who are they going to bring in to try to give the team the depth necessary to win? You don't win a championship just having a couple of good players on your team. It takes a team to win. People get crazy over this, you know, they started this thing where they have two and three star players, but even with those guys, you're not going to win if you don't have the rest of the team to get the job done. You have to have depth."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0SGH_E4dkKE&t=103s

**4. Rick Barry — "Why would you not want to have one of the greatest players in the history of the game." — Rick Barry on LeBron James joining the Warriors** [02:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0SGH_E4dkKE&t=169s)

Rick Barry: "You want your team to win. As a fan, why would you not want to have one of the greatest players in the history of the game wearing the uniform of the team that you love and play with one of the greatest players the Warriors have ever had in **Steph Curry**, one of the true anomalies of the game who's played the point guard position differently than everybody's ever played it. To be able to get to watch those two guys playing together, it would be fascinating."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0SGH_E4dkKE&t=169s

**5. Rick Barry — "When you get older, it's easier to get hurt, it's more difficult and takes longer to heal." — Rick Barry on the physical toll of playing at an advanced age** [08:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0SGH_E4dkKE&t=490s)

Rick Barry: "Yes, you can play longer and still play at a higher level. There's so many good things you can do to keep yourself in great shape, but you are older. Your body is getting older. You're not a kid anymore. The chances of getting hurt are -- and here's what happens when you get older, and trust me because I know this firsthand: when you get older, it's easier to get hurt, it's more difficult and takes longer to heal. And so that's a big, big factor. And when you have as many players as the Warriors would have at that advanced age for the game of basketball, it's a big gamble."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0SGH_E4dkKE&t=490s

**6. Rick Barry — "He can stretch the floor as a big." — Rick Barry on Kristaps Porzingis's role with the Warriors** [09:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0SGH_E4dkKE&t=559s)

Rick Barry: "He can stretch the floor as a big. They got two older guys now who can stretch the floor as bigs, which is always very helpful when you have other players who can take advantage of that. And let's face it, the game today, what is it? 80% of the games where the opponent outscores the opposition from three-point range, they win the game. I mean that's a stat. So you have to have people to be able to knock down three-point shots if you're going to want to win in the NBA in the way that it's constructed these days."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0SGH_E4dkKE&t=559s

**7. Rick Barry — "Why in the world he didn't develop a low-post game." — Rick Barry on LeBron James's lack of a low-post game** [10:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0SGH_E4dkKE&t=617s)

Rick Barry: "I've always been a little bit dismayed by the fact that somebody with his size, his strength, his power, his skill level, why in the world he didn't develop a low-post game. I think he would have abused people down there. And I think the reason is he's never been a great free-throw shooter. I don't think LeBron's ever shot 80% from the free-throw line in any season he's ever played, if I'm not mistaken."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0SGH_E4dkKE&t=617s

**8. Rick Barry — "How in the world can you have a 7'4" player with his talent and have him play 25 feet." — Rick Barry on Victor Wembanyama's play style** [10:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0SGH_E4dkKE&t=649s)

Rick Barry: "How in the world can you have a 7'4" player with his talent and have him play 25 feet from the basket? He's got a 6'3", 6'4" guy guarding him. Put him on the low block, throw him the ball, and now he can pass to anybody. His guys will have set, they'll be set ready to shoot a three if they come to try to help. I was dumbfounded by that. It was one of the real times that I really felt that the opposing coach, Mike Brown, who I was happy for -- I couldn't root for the Knicks because they didn't draft me, and they should have drafted me, and they didn't, but thank God because I loved my time at Golden State -- is that Mike Brown totally outcoached him."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0SGH_E4dkKE&t=649s
