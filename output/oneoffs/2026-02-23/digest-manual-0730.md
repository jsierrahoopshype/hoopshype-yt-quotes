## Giannis Antetokounmpo Goes Sneaker Shopping With Complex — *Complex*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cpgRjX-alS4

_Speakers identified: Joe La Puma, Giannis Antetokounmpo_

**1. Giannis Antetokounmpo — "My game was from 6:00 to 8:00. His game was from 8:30 to 10:30." — Giannis Antetokounmpo on sharing sneakers with his brother Thanasis** [00:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cpgRjX-alS4&t=54s)

**Joe La Puma:** "The stories are out there of sharing shoes with your brothers. Was it literally you would sub in and out of games and take shoes off, and that's the level of sharing the shoes it went?"

**Giannis Antetokounmpo:** "That's not that dramatic. My game was from 6:00 to 8:00. His game was from 8:30 to 10:30. So when I was down playing, because I was younger than my brother three years, I'll come out, give him the shoes, he'll play, and then just vice versa."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cpgRjX-alS4&t=54s

**2. Giannis Antetokounmpo — "I came to the NBA when I was 18. Prior to that, I did not have no money." — Giannis Antetokounmpo on his humble beginnings before joining the NBA** [01:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cpgRjX-alS4&t=104s)

Giannis Antetokounmpo: "Until this day, I don't think I've ever bought a pair of shoes because I came to the NBA when I was 18. Prior to that, I did not have no money to my name. But before, I'm fortunate enough to be with Nike for 13, 14 years now. They've given me a lot of heat for free."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cpgRjX-alS4&t=104s

**3. Giannis Antetokounmpo — "My brother signed a professional deal in the first division of Greece. When he went to training camp, they gave him a pair of shoes." — Giannis Antetokounmpo on his brother Thanasis and his first pair of Kobe 4s** [02:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cpgRjX-alS4&t=137s)

Giannis Antetokounmpo: "My brother signed a professional deal in the first division of Greece. When he went to training camp, they gave him a pair of shoes. But when that fell through, he came back to our team and he had some extra pair of shoes and then he gave me the Kobe 4s. But he loved them so much, he gave them just to work out in them. And I was whining and complaining. I was like, no, I want those shoes. I like Kobe, I like that. And my dad was like, hey, you got to make sure Giannis is able to play on those shoes too."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cpgRjX-alS4&t=137s

**4. Giannis Antetokounmpo — "I realized it's just like, it's tough. It's time to grind." — Giannis Antetokounmpo on the significance of wearing black Air Force 1s in the 2021 NBA Finals** [05:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cpgRjX-alS4&t=308s)

Giannis Antetokounmpo: "Sometimes in the locker room, when we go to like a road trip, we communicate with one another and we say, hey, on this road trip, everybody's wearing black Air Forces. At first, I didn't know the significance behind it and why they were talking about that. And then I realized it's just like, it's tough. It's time to grind. It's time to let people know what you're about. So I usually don't dress up because when I go to the game, I just want to hoop. I really don't care how I look."
