**Steve Mason:** "The news of you joining the Lakers broke very quickly after free agency began, which means those conversations were naturally having being taken place even earlier. What drew you about this opportunity with the Lakers?"

**Collin Sexton:** "I would say just opportunity to win, being on the big stage and being in the situation where I can try to come in and impact winning. I feel like being on different teams where it's been a lot tougher, of course the want to win it's always been there, but it's definitely tough to win. In the NBA it's a hard thing and each and every night we're playing against the best of the best. So, I think just being on this stage and being able to impact winning each and every day and what I bring to the team and bring to the game, I feel like I just fit right in."
