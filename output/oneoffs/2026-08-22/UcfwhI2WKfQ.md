**4. Payton Pritchard — "You just can't let a team feel comfortable in a Game 5." — Payton Pritchard on the Celtics' playoff loss to the 76ers** [04:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UcfwhI2WKfQ&t=290s)

Payton Pritchard: "You just can't let a team feel comfortable in a Game 5. We had them on the hills. **5. Payton Pritchard — "Maybe getting better at how to draw fouls now, not being a flopper but drawing fouls." — Payton Pritchard's goals for improving his game** [10:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UcfwhI2WKfQ&t=625s)
