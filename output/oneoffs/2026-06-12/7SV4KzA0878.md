# Lakers Mailbag: The Ideal Center Next To Luka, Hidden Targets & More — *Jovan Buha*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7SV4KzA0878

_Speakers identified: Jovan Buha_

**1. Jovan Buha — "they need to be able to protect the rim, protect the paint, and be an interior force" — Lakers need a center who can protect the rim and be an interior defensive force** [00:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7SV4KzA0878&t=53s)

Jovan Buha: "I want to prioritize three general things. First and foremost, they have to be a defender of a certain level. I have recently, and frankly throughout the season, talked about the limitations with **DeAndre Ayton** as a rim-protecting center. Whichever center the Lakers add, whether it is someone in free agency, whether it is someone they trade for, maybe it is someone they draft for, whoever it is, they need to be able to protect the rim, protect the paint, and be an interior force on the defensive end."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7SV4KzA0878&t=53s

**2. Jovan Buha — "the Lakers need to also address the rebounding, the defensive rebounding" — Lakers need a center who is a better rebounder than DeAndre Ayton** [02:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7SV4KzA0878&t=165s)

Jovan Buha: "The second thing would be rebounding. If we take the four teams that made the conference finals and just use them as a blueprint here, if you look at **Karl-Anthony Towns**, **Chet Holmgren**, **Isaiah Hartenstein**, **Victor Wembanyama**, and **Jarrett Allen**, **DeAndre Ayton** was a worse rebounder than all five of those players. So the Lakers need to also address the rebounding, the defensive rebounding. I think about that stretch at the beginning of the fourth quarter of Game 3 where the Lakers give up multiple offensive rebounds and they are in the thick of the game and they cannot get a defensive rebound to save their life."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7SV4KzA0878&t=165s

**3. Jovan Buha — "the third and final thing is being a pick-and-roll partner with Luka Doncic" — Lakers need a center who is a pick-and-roll partner for Luka Doncic** [03:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7SV4KzA0878&t=226s)

Jovan Buha: "The third and final thing is being a pick-and-roll partner with **Luka Doncic**. Ideally, that is a high-level lob threat. We have seen the success with **Derrick Lively** and **Daniel Gafford**, and then this past season with **DeAndre Ayton** and **Jackson Hayes**. If you have a big man who can figure out the tango with Luka, the one-two step, and get behind the defense and be a lob threat, be a presence at the rim, be someone who can catch the ball and finish and dunk, of course you would love to have a big who can be a lob threat."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7SV4KzA0878&t=226s

**4. Jovan Buha — "I would easily give up a first-round pick to go get Derek Lively" — Lakers should target Derek Lively in a trade** [13:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7SV4KzA0878&t=785s)

Jovan Buha: "I would easily give up a first-round pick to go get **Derek Lively**. I think he would be a great fit running it back with Luka in LA."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7SV4KzA0878&t=785s

**5. Jovan Buha — "Landry Shamet is an interesting bench option. I know he has not had a great finals" — Lakers should target Landry Shamet as a bench option** [14:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7SV4KzA0878&t=857s)

Jovan Buha: "If you go to New York, **Landry Shamet** is an interesting bench option. I know he has not had a great finals, but compare him to someone like **Luke Kennard** where he has had an awesome postseason shooting-wise, but has also been a point-of-attack defender and held his own in the backcourt defensively for New York, has closed a ton of games for the Knicks, was closing a ton of those games in the Cleveland series and the Eastern Conference finals. So I think he is someone that, for example, I would rather have than a **Luke Kennard**."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7SV4KzA0878&t=857s

**12. Jovan Buha — "I love this, I think this would be close to my dream scenario" — Trading for Nic Claxton and signing Robert Williams is a dream scenario** [33:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7SV4KzA0878&t=1988s)
