**7. Olivia Miles — "We made it to the Elite Eight" — Olivia Miles on the whirlwind schedule leading up to the draft** [04:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yoPWnocuizg&t=288s)

Olivia Miles: "We made it to the Elite Eight and I think it was 10 days because it happened a few days after the national championship. So I lost and then I got flown out to Santa Barbara, California, where I trained and did a bunch of measurements and stuff on my body and then I trained for a few days and then flew straight to New York. During that whole week, it was free agency, team signing. We didn't know who was signing where, that could affect picks and all this stuff. So I literally was like, I'm not even going to look at it, I can't even be bothered paying attention to that stuff."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yoPWnocuizg&t=288s

**8. Olivia Miles — "TCU was straight pick-and-roll offense and that's why I went there" — Olivia Miles on the importance of film study for pick-and-roll** [06:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yoPWnocuizg&t=372s)

Olivia Miles: "I think again, it's a lot of film. TCU was straight pick-and-roll offense and that's why I went there to get me reps, to get me to feel comfortable in that space. The more reps you do, the more comfortable you get in it. I'm still going to make mistakes, but I know the reads are there and I know what to do to fix it. So TCU really allowed me to feel comfortable in it and then Cheryl obviously, she's so smart at knowing what her players are good at, so she switched her offense to be more pick-and-roll heavy and obviously me and Court killing that, so it's been fun."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yoPWnocuizg&t=372s
