**4. Chris Mannix — "He really has given that offense an identity and modernized it in a way." — Micah Nori's influence on Portland's modern offensive identity** [02:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FlegRgWIahA&t=135s)

Chris Mannix: "Shout out to **Micah Nori**, who came into this situation under a big cloud. Everybody was looking at him going, why did you sign that contract?
