**11. Channing Frye — "There's like six of us. It was JR, Swish, Tristan, Kevin, Channing, and Bron." — Channing Frye on the composition of the 2016 Cavaliers group chat** [07:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k_Qt4Ilp70c&t=421s)

Channing Frye: "There's like six of us. It was JR, Swish, Tristan, Kevin, Channing, and Bron. So that's the group of us. Then we start doing, hey guys, we should get together, do some golf and go do this. And then a conversation kind of got started is, and like, hey, it would be wrong if we didn't invite some of the other guys because it's going to look like we're calling it this championship. No, it was the group chat from the championship team, not the championship team group chat."
