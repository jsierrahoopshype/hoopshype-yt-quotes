# Leon Rose Reveals the REAL Reason the Knicks Changed Coaches — *Roommates Show*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8DLZ2f72EqM

_Speakers identified: Josh Hart, Leon Rose, Matt Hillman, Jalen Brunson_

**1. Leon Rose — "felt that just needed a change in voice, a change in philosophy." — Leon Rose on the decision to replace Tom Thibodeau** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8DLZ2f72EqM&t=0s)

**Josh Hart:** "You made a huge change in coaches this past summer. That was tough for you. Talk about the process that goes into changing and finding a coach that fits the timeline and the process."

**Leon Rose:** "It was tough. **Tom Thibodeau** is a great coach, did so much for the organization. We were right on the doorstep and just felt that just needed a change in voice, a change in philosophy. It was a tough move and **Mike Budenholzer** was a guy that really fit what we were looking for. I have known him a long time. I represented **LeBron James** when he coached him in Cleveland. I knew him and followed him through his career. He was a good guy that you could talk to and really felt good about."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8DLZ2f72EqM&t=0s

**2. Leon Rose — "he really was open to things, open to people's suggestions, open to ideas." — Mike Budenholzer's inclusive coaching philosophy and openness to ideas** [01:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8DLZ2f72EqM&t=101s)

Leon Rose: "First of all, just as far as his openness and willingness to share ideas with the front office, his staff, and the inclusion of everyone. He really was open to things, open to people's suggestions, open to ideas. I believe that's what led to some of the changes that were made throughout the season. It wasn't that we started out a certain way and he may have had an idea of how something was going to work and how he wanted it to work, but he evolved throughout the season, as did our team, and I think that all went into the fact that we went to another gear in the playoffs."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8DLZ2f72EqM&t=101s

**3. Matt Hillman — "pairing Jalen Brunson and Jose Alvarado together might have been one of the adjustments that won the series." — Adjustments in the playoff rotation that helped win the series** [02:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8DLZ2f72EqM&t=165s)

**Matt Hillman:** "He was fantastic throughout the whole playoffs, but I think his willingness to just be adaptable and try things and it might not work and then it might. I think like pairing **Jalen Brunson** and **Jose Alvarado** together might have been one of the adjustments that won the series for you guys in terms of taking some of the pressure off him. Look at Game 4, that was it right there and that's not like a rotation that on paper is like, 'Oh, we should do this.'"

**Leon Rose:** "No, absolutely."
