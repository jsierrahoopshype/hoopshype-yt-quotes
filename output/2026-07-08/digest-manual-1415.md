## Why Did the Celtics Trade Jaylen Brown? (With Brian Windhorst) — *Shump Street*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dozlZPhHU_4

_Speakers identified: Iman Shumpert, Brian Windhorst, Arhii Shumpert_

**1. Brian Windhorst — "The Celtics' viewpoint is they traded for Paul George who makes the same amount of money." — The Celtics' decision to trade Jaylen Brown for Paul George** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dozlZPhHU_4&t=0s)

Brian Windhorst: "The Celtics' viewpoint is they traded for **Paul George** who makes the same amount of money. This is where their case falls apart a little bit because nobody, not even analytics, would say that **Paul George** is a more efficient player than **Jaylen Brown**. The Celtics could say, well, we have **Paul George** and we got the two first-round draft picks, so the two of those things put together make it more palatable than **Jaylen Brown**."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dozlZPhHU_4&t=0s

**2. Brian Windhorst — "Jaylen has three years left on his contract, but he's got no player option." — Jaylen Brown's lack of a player option in his contract** [02:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dozlZPhHU_4&t=154s)

Brian Windhorst: "Jaylen has three years left on his contract, but he's got no player option. He didn't get a player option in the deal. A player option significantly reduces the leverage that a team has on a contract. If he had a player option on the third year, then he really only has one year before you're under stress, because you never want a player to be in the last year of his contract."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dozlZPhHU_4&t=154s

**3. Brian Windhorst — "They should have had a backup deal ready if they didn't get Giannis." — The Celtics' failure to secure a backup trade deal** [04:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dozlZPhHU_4&t=263s)

Brian Windhorst: "They should have had a backup deal ready if they didn't get Giannis. They should have quietly gone out there, because everybody knew it was going to be known they were going to try to trade him. There's no way that stayed quiet, and it didn't stay quiet. They should have had a backup deal. What ends up happening is the whole league finds out they're trying to trade him, and then another ten days passes and his value just drops. They botched that."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dozlZPhHU_4&t=263s

**4. Brian Windhorst — "We view Jaylen as our own internal system, we view Jaylen as a 32 million dollar player." — Teams undervaluing Jaylen Brown compared to his actual contract** [05:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dozlZPhHU_4&t=336s)

Brian Windhorst: "I've talked to teams in the league who are like, yeah, we view Jaylen as our own internal system, we view Jaylen as a 32 million dollar player. We wouldn't trade for him because we got to pay him 57 million and we see our value on him is 32 million or 35 million. Four or five years ago, before the aprons, teams wouldn't have thought this way. Now teams all have this system."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dozlZPhHU_4&t=336s

**5. Brian Windhorst — "Teams were falling in love with these international teenagers." — The NBA's history of overvaluing international prospects** [06:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dozlZPhHU_4&t=393s)

Brian Windhorst: "Teams were falling in love with these international teenagers. **Nikoloz Tskitishvili** was taken in the top five. **Joe Dumars**, who is one of the biggest contributors to the NBA of the last 50 years as a player and an executive, multi-time champion as a player, champion as an executive, he got to the point where he said, I'd rather have this 18-year-old skinny kid as opposed to **Dirk Nowitzki**. Why do they draft **Kwame Brown**? You draft **Kwame Brown** because you're afraid on missing **Kevin Garnett** or you're afraid on missing **Kobe Bryant**. People were like, I can't miss the next Pau Gasol. So they convince themselves that this lanky 18-year-old from Serbia, **Darko Milicic**, was a better prospect than **Carmelo Anthony**."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dozlZPhHU_4&t=393s

**6. Brian Windhorst — "The league has this long history of seeing an idea, copying it and saturating it." — The league's tendency to over-correct on three-point shooting** [08:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dozlZPhHU_4&t=494s)

Brian Windhorst: "The league has this long history of seeing an idea, copying it and saturating it. Same with the three-pointers. People are like, we shouldn't shoot 23 three-pointers a game, we need to shoot more. Okay, that's true. Do we need to shoot 65 in every single game no matter what the score is? You kind of lost the plot. Same thing with resting. Okay, let's not play all the players 40 minutes a game for 82 games, maybe they'll be a little bit worn out. All of a sudden, there's players sitting games in November."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dozlZPhHU_4&t=494s
