**1. Brian Windhorst — "Celtics are trying to totally remake their team for the modern league" — Celtics are shifting toward a deep, youth-focused roster strategy** [00:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Hq369BOP2XI&t=44s)

Brian Windhorst: "The Celtics are trying to totally remake their team for the modern league, which is not necessarily top-heavy with superstar players getting paid a lot of money, although I know **Paul George** is getting paid a lot of money alongside **Jayson Tatum**. But to have a lot of depth that they can rely on to use a lot of different formations, a lot of different lineups, get better efficiency for their dollars. The Celtics are trying a new way of doing things and it's not just going to be **Jayson Tatum** and **Jaylen Brown**. They are going to rely on **Payton Pritchard** more, they are going to rely on **Baylor Scheierman** more, they are going to rely on **Hugo Gonzalez**, and even **Mitchell Robinson** and **Neemias Queta**, who they just resigned."
