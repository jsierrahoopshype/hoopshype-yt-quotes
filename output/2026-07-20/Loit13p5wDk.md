# Tracy McGrady & Vince Carter Reveal What Their First Days in the NBA Were Really Like | Cousins — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Loit13p5wDk

_Speakers identified: Tracy McGrady, Vince Carter_

**1. Tracy McGrady — "I'm trying to figure out where the hell is Toronto at." — Tracy McGrady on his first days after being drafted** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Loit13p5wDk&t=0s)

**Vince Carter:** "What were the next three days for young T-Mac like after he's drafted? Now you've fulfilled that dream, you're on cloud nine. 6:00 a.m. you're on the flight, you're now landing in Toronto. What do those next three days look like?"

**Tracy McGrady:** "I'm trying to figure out where the hell is Toronto at. I've been up there before the draft, but so we leave, we go there 6:00 in the morning. I get there, of course you do all your press work, meet everybody in the organization. You probably retain two or three people and that's probably **Isaiah Thomas** because you knew him. There's so many people."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Loit13p5wDk&t=0s

**2. Tracy McGrady — "You probably get to know the PR guy better than most of the people in the organization." — Tracy McGrady on the role of the PR guy** [00:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Loit13p5wDk&t=48s)

**Vince Carter:** "And the PR guy, because usually the PR guy is one of the first people to call you and tell you he welcomes you to the team and I'm the PR guy, I'm the one that's going to navigate all of these interviews. You probably get to know the PR guy better than most of the people in the organization."

**Tracy McGrady:** "You're going to know the PR guy, you're going to know the GM and the president, you're going to know the trainer. They're going to give you the rundown on how things are, what's expected in the upcoming season."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Loit13p5wDk&t=48s

**3. Tracy McGrady — "I went around the city, went apartment hunting. That's what I did while I was there." — Tracy McGrady on finding an apartment as a rookie** [01:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Loit13p5wDk&t=78s)

Tracy McGrady: "For me, just getting acclimated around the city, I got there and I went around the city, went apartment hunting. That's what I did while I was there because I didn't want to come back later on in the summer to do it. So I did it while I was there and found me a place. That was my three days of why I was there."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Loit13p5wDk&t=78s

**4. Tracy McGrady — "I realized, oh, I'm going to be all right. What I see around here." — Tracy McGrady on learning the realities of being a pro** [01:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Loit13p5wDk&t=103s)

Tracy McGrady: "We had a mini camp in Barbados that I went to and that's when I was like, he can't go left? Oh, he only can post up on this block? That's when I got a lot of information. I'm thinking these pros, they should be able to do everything on the basketball court. They should be able to use left hand, should be able to use right hand. But that was the time where they had more specialists in our league at the time. I'm naive of what I'm supposed to be really seeing. But I get my information when I'm at this mini camp and I realize, oh, I'm going to be all right. What I see around here."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Loit13p5wDk&t=103s

**5. Tracy McGrady — "I needed that number one. You couldn't tell me nothing about Penny." — Tracy McGrady on choosing jersey number one** [02:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Loit13p5wDk&t=153s)

Tracy McGrady: "It was available and I don't give a damn who was there, I was getting number one. Because you know Penny, dog. You couldn't tell me nothing about Penny. I needed that number one."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Loit13p5wDk&t=153s

**6. Tracy McGrady — "I got my hand on my breeches and trying to play defense." — Tracy McGrady on his welcome to the league moment** [03:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Loit13p5wDk&t=196s)

Tracy McGrady: "I got two of them really. I got one where I didn't think I was going to play and we were playing Seattle. **GP** was still with Seattle at that time. It was in the second half, didn't think I was going to play, my number got called. I didn't have my shorts tied up. So I'm out there, the knot gets knotted up and you can't loosen it. So my shorts are falling off, so I got my hand on my breeches and trying to play defense. And then the next one, we were playing Milwaukee and one came off the rim and I caught that in front of Ricky Pierce's face and Ricky Pierce bowed me. Pop said, hey young fella, don't do that to Ricky Pierce, bro. Don't do that in Ricky Pierce's face."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Loit13p5wDk&t=196s

**7. Vince Carter — "You have to tell your big man to call the screen." — Vince Carter on his welcome to the league moment** [04:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Loit13p5wDk&t=287s)

Vince Carter: "My welcome to the league moment is I learned you have to keep your head on the swivel. We're playing Utah. **Karl Malone**, the double forearm screen to the back of the neck. I'm laying down there, he bends over and he says, you got to tell your big man to call the screen. No shit. Laid out. And that's when I was like, bro, this is real."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Loit13p5wDk&t=287s

**8. Vince Carter — "Watching, trying to figure out Pip and his holes, his grabs on like on floppy mostly." — Vince Carter on learning to play against veterans** [05:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Loit13p5wDk&t=318s)

Vince Carter: "Watching, trying to figure out **Pip** and his holes, his grabs on like on floppy mostly and not being able to get free. And he's holding me and then letting me go so but at the same time it's like a slingshot himself through the screen. And I was like, and then I learned, especially referees back then, I'm saying something to the ref and the ref told me to man, play ball, shut up. Shut up. Play ball. Start crying, baby. Start crying you baby. Once I kind of figure that out, once I figured out like it was how to keep my hands close to my body or you know the early swipe to get the hands off because they're going to, you can grab a hold a little bit, like being able to get learning how to use forearm, not hand. All of these little things. Once I figured them out, the game was different. I could be an athlete again."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Loit13p5wDk&t=318s
