Then we start to get to act it, then it's a little bit more you. You're always throwing the ball, and once a point guard, always a point guard, I say. You're always trying to make people look good too. But then this had this point of view that I'd had for a while, and it felt more like, with all the shit going on in the world, it was bubbling more to the surface. It almost becomes therapeutic where you start to recognize the things that you were thinking quietly, they're saying out loud, so it gives you the strength to think, I'd like to say it out loud yourself, and you realize how much more similar we are than we are different."
