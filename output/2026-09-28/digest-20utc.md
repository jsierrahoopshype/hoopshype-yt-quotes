## Trevor Ariza gets HONEST about Dwight Howard and if he DESERVED to miss the NBA Top 75 👀 — *Out The Mud*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=da0Sasez5aY

_Speakers identified: Tony Allen, Trevor Ariza_

**1. Tony Allen — "It surprised me that he wasn't part of Top 75." — Dwight Howard's work ethic and exclusion from NBA Top 75** [00:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=da0Sasez5aY&t=12s)

**Tony Allen:** "Man, Trevor, you already know you played with the big fellow, **Dwight Howard**. I'm pretty sure you saw the work ethic. You saw him develop over the years. Tell me about him as a teammate and did it surprise you that he wasn't part of Top 75?"

**Trevor Ariza:** "It surprised me that he wasn't part of Top 75 player. He won three straight Defensive Player of the Year. He controlled the paint his whole healthy career, offensively and defensively. How many people have done that? Kareem, Shaq, who else? He controlled the paint like that."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=da0Sasez5aY&t=12s

**2. Trevor Ariza — "He would be in the weight room lifting an hour and a half before practice." — Dwight Howard's intense work ethic and dedication to improvement** [00:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=da0Sasez5aY&t=55s)

Trevor Ariza: "His work ethic was out of this world. He would be in the weight room lifting an hour and a half before practice. Then he would practice, then he would stay two, three, four hours after practice just shooting, working on the things that he wanted to work on to get better at. So, he is somebody that always worked on his game and just seeing that mentality and work ethic, you would think it should pay off with the accolades that come with it at the end of it. But yeah, he definitely should have been a Top 75 player."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=da0Sasez5aY&t=55s

**3. Tony Allen — "A lot of people criticize him for being just the he-he and ha-ha person." — Dwight Howard's personality and the criticism he received** [01:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=da0Sasez5aY&t=98s)

**Tony Allen:** "A lot of people criticize him for being just the he-he and ha-ha person. Do you think if he wasn't that he-he and ha-ha and been more on a different type of demeanor, like a Demarcus-type of attitude?"

**Trevor Ariza:** "Yeah, it would be different for him for sure. It would be no question what it is, but again, that's not his personality. In order to be his greatness, he has to be who he is, and that's what he was doing. So, I can't be mad at that."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=da0Sasez5aY&t=98s

**4. Tony Allen — "The turnover time is too fast." — The lack of veteran presence in today's NBA** [02:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=da0Sasez5aY&t=128s)

**Tony Allen:** "Why don't you think the vets get the love like it used to be when the league was coming up? What do you think the difference is?"

**Trevor Ariza:** "The turnover time is too fast. To have an older guy, it messes up the younger person's opportunity in one space. But then it also keeps the young dudes young and their minds young too. Because if you think of it like when we were younger players, we had grown-ass men on our team with families who got kids the same age. It is just different now. The vets is 26, 25, 26. You are in a different mental space."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=da0Sasez5aY&t=128s

**5. Trevor Ariza — "I wish they would, though, because it would definitely help the younger players a lot more." — The value of veteran leadership for younger players** [03:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=da0Sasez5aY&t=216s)

Trevor Ariza: "I wish they would, though, because it would definitely help the younger players a lot more. If you just got somebody there helping them and teaching them more things, it would be better."
