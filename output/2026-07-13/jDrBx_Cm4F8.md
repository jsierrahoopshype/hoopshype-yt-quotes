# Keaton Wagler on the NBA Draft Process & His Tyrese Haliburton Comparison | Cousins — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jDrBx_Cm4F8

_Speakers identified: Vince Carter, Keaton Wagler, Tracy McGrady_

**1. Keaton Wagler — "The main thing that I worked on throughout the pre-draft was just getting more consistent at finishing" — Keaton Wagler on his pre-draft focus and toughest workouts** [00:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jDrBx_Cm4F8&t=26s)

**Vince Carter:** "Talk to us about your process, how many teams you worked out for, and what was the most difficult workout you had?"

**Keaton Wagler:** "The main thing that I worked on throughout the pre-draft was just getting more consistent at finishing around the rim and finding different ways to finish in the lane, like floaters, off-hand layups off one, wide layups. Just getting more consistent at that. I only went to Chicago and the Clippers. I probably say they are kind of the same but a little bit different at the same time. It was nice to get out there and play. I probably just say that they were both tough just because you haven't played in a while, so you are not in as good of shape as other people are who get to play more than you do."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jDrBx_Cm4F8&t=26s

**2. Keaton Wagler — "I wasn't super nervous about the playing part because I feel like when I get out there" — Keaton Wagler on managing pre-draft workout nerves and expectations** [01:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jDrBx_Cm4F8&t=83s)

**Tracy McGrady:** "What were the nerves like in your first draft workout? Did you have those times where you doubted yourself?"

**Keaton Wagler:** "In the Chicago one, because it was my first workout, I wasn't super nervous about the playing part because I feel like when I get out there and play, it's just I don't really think about it, I just do it. But at the end, everyone does the 100, which is 103 from different spots. I started off pretty bad and I was like, are they going to think I'm a bad shooter now? I need to get on a roll. I didn't shoot it as good as I normally do, so I was like, wow, what are they going to think now? But at the end of the day, they are not really basing it off of how you are shooting in one workout."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jDrBx_Cm4F8&t=83s

**3. Keaton Wagler — "Seeing all of them and how they played and seeing the type of seasons that they had" — Keaton Wagler on drawing inspiration from successful rookie seasons** [03:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jDrBx_Cm4F8&t=210s)

**Vince Carter:** "How inspired are you by the rookie seasons of guys like **Dylan Harper**, **Cooper Flagg**, and **Carter Bryant**?"

**Keaton Wagler:** "I am definitely inspired, seeing all of them and how they played and seeing the type of seasons that they had and the different ways that they had to maneuver in different situations. **Dylan Harper** didn't even start, but he was still one of the best players on that team and one of the best players in the playoffs for them that they really leaned on. Seeing how all of them maneuvered in their different situations shows that no matter where you are, you can still figure it out and make an impact and win, no matter if you are the best player or second option."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jDrBx_Cm4F8&t=210s

**4. Keaton Wagler — "I would say Tyrese Haliburton, I just think based off of our size that we both have" — Keaton Wagler on his game comparison to Tyrese Haliburton** [06:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jDrBx_Cm4F8&t=404s)

**Vince Carter:** "What NBA player do you think your game is a comp of in the league today?"

**Keaton Wagler:** "I would say **Tyrese Haliburton**, I just think based off of our size that we both have and how we have the ball in our hands and can make plays and knowing when it's our time to score the ball and when it's our time to get others involved and make the right play. That is a big thing for him is that he always makes the right play, and that is something that I always do too, is look to make the right play and just play the right way. I feel like that is a really good comp for me just based off of his playmaking, his ability to shoot it and also shoot it from deep."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jDrBx_Cm4F8&t=404s
