Kendrick Perkins: "Wemby and I relate. We relate because mentality-wise, I'm a guy that I envy and I'm jealous of people that's greater than me because I want their greatness. Wemby wants greatness. So when he sees someone that's ahead of him that has accomplished more than him, he envies them. And that's okay in the game of basketball. For example, **James Posey** was on our team in 2008. He was the only champion outside of **Sam Cassell**. But every game day on the road, he used to wear his suit and that damn championship ring with Miami. And he used to make sure he wore it on the outside hand so when you walk by you could see it. And it used to piss everybody off. And I went up to him one day, Posey, and I was like, damn, bro, like why you got to throw it in our face? He was like, well, go win one. And I said, you know, right? That's real. And I get that from Wemby. Like he don't care, he's chasing greatness."
