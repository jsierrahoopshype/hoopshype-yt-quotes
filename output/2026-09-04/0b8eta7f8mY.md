**1. Jalen Brunson — "Enjoy your steps because you never know which step is actually going to be the most beneficial." — Jalen Brunson on the importance of patience and consistency in development** [00:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0b8eta7f8mY&t=47s)

Jalen Brunson: "My consistency didn't show until high school. You are doing all this stuff, and for me, I was playing up. When I was 11, I was playing 13U; 12, I was playing 14U. I was good enough to play up, but it comes with a lot of different learning experiences. I always played up until 17U AAU-wise. When I was finally playing with my age, it was comfortable. I wasn't rushing. It wasn't anything that I wasn't already prepared for because I've already done it. People say, 'Oh, I was in the gym for two hours today.' Okay, well what did you do in the gym? You can get a much more difficult and better workout in 30 to 40 minutes versus just being in the gym BSing for two hours. It was about being precise. It was about intent to everything you did. Enjoy your steps because you never know which step is actually going to be the most beneficial."

**2. Kendrick Perkins — "I'm literally about to go tell your coach to take the ball out your hands." — Kendrick Perkins on challenging his son to expand his game** [06:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0b8eta7f8mY&t=394s)

**Kendrick Perkins:** "My son's last tournament, I went to him and I said, 'Hey bro, I'm literally about to go tell your coach to take the ball out your hands and you got to find other ways to be able to score the basketball.' You have to run the floor, get out in transition, you have to be able to play off the ball and knock down three-point shots when it's swung to you, and you have to be able to cut in a half-court set. I challenged him that entire weekend to do that, because he's been in the lab shooting the tray ball. I'm like, at some point, these spot-up shots got to happen. It's not going to happen with you dribbling the ball up the floor."

**Jalen Brunson:** "That's why I'm saying, no, the things that you are saying are the things that dads and coaches should say to their son. It's like, don't sit over here and be mad that you're not starring. What does this team need? Okay, they need defense off the bench, they need rebounding, they need leadership, they need scoring. How do we teach the next generation of young men how to focus on the journey?"
