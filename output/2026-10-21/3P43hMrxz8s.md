# Will Hardy on Figuring Out Darryn Peterson Backcourt & Coaching JJJ | Open Floor w/ Chris Mannix — *Sports Illustrated*

_Speakers identified: Chris Mannix, Will Hardy_

**1. Will Hardy — "Every off-season is spent in two pathways. You're looking back and trying to self-assess everything." — Will Hardy on the Jazz's offseason self-assessment and roster development** [01:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3P43hMrxz8s&t=67s)

Will Hardy: "Every off-season is spent in two pathways. You're looking back and trying to self-assess everything. It's the individual players, it's the team, it's programmatic things, it's myself, my staff, things we can do better based on what has already happened. And then there's the pivot point in the summer, which is the draft, where somebody new is joining your program. And now things start to shake out from a roster standpoint through free agency, and you have a clearer picture of what may lie ahead. And so then all of your thinking goes into, okay, how can we help this team? How can we help these individual players? What are the priorities for everybody?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3P43hMrxz8s&t=67s

**2. Will Hardy — "I think they have an opportunity moving forward to be a really dynamic backcourt." — Will Hardy on the potential of a Keyonte George and Darryn Peterson backcourt** [07:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3P43hMrxz8s&t=435s)

Will Hardy: "I think there's a ton of belief and excitement in it. Watching the playoffs, there's so much to learn, and one of the biggest takeaways was you need multiple ball-handlers to get to the finish line. Even the best lead guards in our league, they need secondary ball-handlers. It's a long road to the finish line. So my approach with them has been messaging just that, that you guys need each other. There's going to be a lot made of who's the point guard, and the answer is neither of you. It doesn't matter. Both of you are really good basketball players, and you both have the ability to play on the ball and off the ball. So the flexibility there is going to help both of them. Each opponent is going to be different. Each matchup that they draw could lead us to do different things offensively, and I think it's going to serve both of them well. I think they have an opportunity moving forward to be a really dynamic backcourt."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3P43hMrxz8s&t=435s

**3. Will Hardy — "There are two sides to every story. There's a lot of context that goes into every situation." — Will Hardy on the importance of context in evaluating young players** [10:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3P43hMrxz8s&t=655s)

Will Hardy: "Any player that is in the draft, we're asking a ton of questions about. You're trying to figure out what's real, what's not, what's true, what's not. Where is this coming from? I would say to everybody, don't believe everything you read or hear. There are two sides to every story. There's a lot of context that goes into every situation. Every team is doing research, not just on the player's previous season, but we're going back to high school, middle school, and trying to understand the person. Ultimately, these are human decisions. This is a human being that we're taking a chance on. And going through that process, our questions about **Darryn Peterson**, his character, his competitiveness, his desire, those things were answered relatively quickly."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3P43hMrxz8s&t=655s

**4. Will Hardy — "My job as a coach is to come home, open the fridge, see what's in there and make dinner." — Will Hardy on the Jazz's philosophy regarding lineup size and positionless basketball** [14:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3P43hMrxz8s&t=872s)

Will Hardy: "My job as a coach is to come home, open the fridge, see what's in there and make dinner. And this is who's on our team. We've got some good players, and I think sometimes the combinations can lead people to say, oh, well, that will hurt them in this area, but there's also areas that it can help us. 48 minutes is how I look at our lineups. People make a lot of who starts. I'm more interested in 48 minutes functioning well. I think that Lauri is really the X-factor in the ability to play that way. Because when you say three seven-footers, yes, that is true if we're just measuring them against the wall, but I don't think Lauri plays like a lot of seven-footers. He is a great mover without the ball, he's an elite shooter, he's not like a back-to-the-basket big. So he sort of provides some things that people would say, oh, this is what some small forwards do, but he's just doing it in a little bit bigger frame. He gives us great flexibility to try some of these things."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3P43hMrxz8s&t=872s

**5. Will Hardy — "The most exciting part for me is his defense. He's turning himself into a good perimeter defender." — Will Hardy on Keyonte George's development and defensive growth** [16:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3P43hMrxz8s&t=1002s)

Will Hardy: "I think Keyonte is a massive, massive part of our program right now. I have so much belief in him. His summer has been outstanding. I'm excited for the league to really get to watch him this year because I think you're going to see a young man who's really improved on both sides of the ball. The most exciting part for me is his defense. He's turning himself into a good perimeter defender, and what that can do for us going forward is very impactful. Keyonte is incredibly young, and he's a rookie, and there's going to be some ups and downs throughout his rookie year, but if we're taking a longer view, I really don't worry about the offensive skill part with him. I want to make sure that their focus is on the smaller, uglier parts of the game that ultimately will impact winning."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3P43hMrxz8s&t=1002s

**6. Will Hardy — "My message to Keyonte is you're a good basketball player and we're not going to overly focus on position." — Will Hardy on Keyonte George's versatility and the team's adjustment period** [19:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3P43hMrxz8s&t=1182s)

Will Hardy: "My message to Keyonte is you're a good basketball player and we're not going to overly focus on position because I believe that he can be both. And we're going to need both. We're putting him in some spots and maybe a few actions that he hasn't done in the past, and that's not to say that we're doing it better than anybody else, it's just our team is built differently. And so I think there's an adjustment period for Keyonte with that. I think he's far more comfortable now than he was two weeks ago, and I expect that to continue to have its ups and downs, but to progress through the first part of the season. It's about him learning how he can help our group."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3P43hMrxz8s&t=1182s

**7. Will Hardy — "He wants to make sure that he's doing it perfect and he is concerned constantly about the details." — Will Hardy on Keyonte George's high standards and work ethic** [21:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3P43hMrxz8s&t=1318s)

Will Hardy: "He wants to make sure that he's doing it perfect and he is concerned constantly about the details. Does he know all of the plays? Does he know all the communication and terminology? Is he saying it right? Is that what I was supposed to say on that? He is concerned about whether he is doing it right and whether it's meeting the standard that we have for him. Some people in our world will just kind of blow through the day and they just assume that if they do it wrong, somebody will tell them. Keyonte operates on the other side where he wants to make sure that the things that he's doing are being done the right way. We're pleased with it, and so it's been fun. It's not what I expected. I didn't know what to expect with Keyonte, but usually guys that are a little further along in their career, sometimes they can have an arrogance about them like they already know. Keyonte has really come in eyes wide open from the first day, very, very receptive and coachable. He sets a great example for our young guys."
