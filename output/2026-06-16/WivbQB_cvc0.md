**3. Steve Nash — "You have to have the perfect team around a small guy, especially nowadays." — Steve Nash on the importance of spacing for small guards** [01:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WivbQB_cvc0&t=94s)

Steve Nash: "You have to have the perfect team around a small guy, especially nowadays, the way the game's played where they space the floor so much offensively. **4. Steve Nash — "The number one characteristic for me, and last year's clutch player of the year, is his will to win." — Steve Nash on Jalen Brunson's will to win** [02:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WivbQB_cvc0&t=126s)

Steve Nash: "The number one characteristic for me, and last year's clutch player of the year, this year I believe is Shai, last year was Jalen, is his will to win. One of the things I love about him beyond the shot-making is he's so smart at using his body."

**5. LeBron James — "If you're guarding him, you can't time it because you don't know when he's going to go." — LeBron James on the difficulty of guarding Jalen Brunson** [02:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WivbQB_cvc0&t=161s)

LeBron James: "If you're guarding him, you can't time it because you don't know when he's going to go. It's not natural. He goes and then he just tosses it up there. It gets you leaning and then he's right there."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WivbQB_cvc0&t=161s

**6. Steve Nash — "I try to teach this to young guards, is using the body and cutting guys off." — Steve Nash on teaching young guards to use their body** [03:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WivbQB_cvc0&t=186s)

Steve Nash: "I try to teach this to young guards, is using the body and cutting guys off. There's one using your body to get off two feet and play. There's one where you have half an advantage where you steer them away from the pocket of space so you can then go back to the pocket of space. And the last one is how he gets in front and just stops."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WivbQB_cvc0&t=186s

**7. LeBron James — "He's a lefty, which also changes it too. Our league is 95 percent of our league are righties." — LeBron James on the unique challenge of defending left-handed guards** [04:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WivbQB_cvc0&t=257s)

LeBron James: "He's a lefty, which also changes it too. Our league is 95 percent of our league are righties. And the lefties that's dynamic in our league is sometimes just hard to get a cadence to that. The man who is Jalen, the **James Harden**, now the **Jalen Brunson**, it's a different feel when a guy is coming at you lefty."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WivbQB_cvc0&t=257s

**8. Steve Nash — "He's quite comfortable to shoot a hand-in-the-face three off the dribble if you give him a little gap." — Steve Nash on the difficulty of scheming against Jalen Brunson** [05:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WivbQB_cvc0&t=303s)

Steve Nash: "He's quite comfortable to shoot a hand-in-the-face three off the dribble if you give him a little gap. So that brings you all the way up. So then he creates that little advantage with the body whether he cuts you off, whether he takes you away from the space, or whether he plays off two feet."
