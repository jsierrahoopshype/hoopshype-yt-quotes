**1. Cedric Maxwell — "It was dirty, it was nasty. We turned the air conditioning off in that room." — Cedric Maxwell on the hostile environment at the old Boston Garden** [00:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q3OdKVzNR6s&t=20s)

Cedric Maxwell: "It was dirty, it was nasty. Think about the series of 1984. The Lakers came in, and they said we turned the air conditioning off in that room. We didn't have any air conditioning. It was dirty, it was nasty. There were bugs, rats, and all kinds of stuff. People talk about nastiness. You played down at the nastiest place of all, in Philadelphia. I remember we would pull up in the bus and there would be 300 or 400 people waiting for our bus to pull up, and they would all be standing there, they would all be giving us the finger at one time. It was moms, dads, and kids. I thought Philly was nastier than any other place I played."

**2. Cedric Maxwell — "I was the first pro that really played against Larry Bird in training camp." — Cedric Maxwell on his first impressions of playing with Larry Bird** [01:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q3OdKVzNR6s&t=118s)

Cedric Maxwell: "I was the first pro that really played against **Larry Bird** in training camp. I was an ignorant, selfish black guy who thought that white guys couldn't play. My first day in practice, I remember thinking he walked on the floor, I'm like, 'Shit, here we go. Great white hope. Here we go.' I knocked it, I'm scoring, but he's scoring. I remember after practice, he scored so much on me, the first black person I got to, I said, 'You know what? That fucking white guy can play down there.' I played with the two greatest white players that ever played on a team when you have Bird and McHale."
