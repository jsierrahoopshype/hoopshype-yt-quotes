**5. Nyjer Morgan — "When we were playing, I felt like we were the guinea pigs." — Nyjer Morgan on the evolution of sports science and athlete training** [18:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=umNqFKaQ5Ag&t=1093s)

Nyjer Morgan: "When we were playing, I felt like we were the guinea pigs because they were getting all the science technology when G2 first came out, the G2 drinks. A lady by the name of **Leslie Bonci**, she actually worked for Gatorade, I don't know if she still does, but she used to come in, talk to the players, letting us know about the nutrition, how to stay hydrated, recovery, and all that good stuff. So there was no NormaTec, there was no, what is it, the game ready, the ice machine. There was none of that."
