## Kanye Took Jeezy’s Record and Made “Can’t Tell Me Nothing” — *ALL THE SMOKE*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bJ34VGnonSE

_Speakers identified: Matt Barnes, Jeezy, Stephen Jackson_

**1. Jeezy — "Kanye West put on feature was crazy because I remember that he wasn't doing any records" — Jeezy on Kanye West's creative process for 'Can't Tell Me Nothing'** [00:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bJ34VGnonSE&t=8s)

Jeezy: "I think **Kanye West** put on feature was crazy because I remember that he wasn't doing any records because his mom had passed and he wasn't doing any records and I hit him up and it was crazy because he did a record for me on The Recession and he changed the beat. The name of the song was I Got Money. That was my song. He changed the beat and changed the song. So when he sent it back, I was like, Kanye, these are two different records. So he kept the record and then he called me six months later, it was, you can't tell me nothing. But that was my record."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bJ34VGnonSE&t=8s

**2. Jeezy — "I sent it to him. I got on my tour bus because I was headed out. He called me like 10 minutes later rapping the verse." — Jeezy on collaborating with Kanye West on 'Put On'** [01:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bJ34VGnonSE&t=86s)

Jeezy: "I explained to him what Put On meant. He said, send it. I sent it to him. I got on my tour bus because I was headed out. He called me like 10 minutes later rapping the verse. You know what I'm saying? He had the whole, I was like, go lay it down. He said, I already did. I was like, send it to me. He sent it to me and I sent it straight to LA Reid. I was like, I got my single."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bJ34VGnonSE&t=86s

**3. Jeezy — "doing the record with Usher with Love in This Club, shout out to Polo, because Polo really made that happen." — Jeezy on the collaborative effort behind 'Love in This Club'** [02:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bJ34VGnonSE&t=157s)

Jeezy: "I would definitely say doing the record with **Usher** with Love in This Club, shout out to Polo, because Polo really made that happen. They were trying to put a lot of people on that record. And Polo called me one day and he was like, listen to the radio tomorrow. He just put it out the way it was and it went. And I remember, shout out to Polo the Don."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bJ34VGnonSE&t=157s

He hit me back like two days later, he was like, check your mail. And I pressed play and I was like, you know what I'm saying?"
