**2. Grant Hill — "Contrary to popular belief, I do like Jaylen Brown. I love his game." — Grant Hill explains the roster construction and Jaylen Brown's exclusion** [01:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hGrgAWT8rWw&t=103s)

**Kendrick Perkins:** "How hard was it for you managing that whole situation and him lashing out on social media?"

**Grant Hill:** "Contrary to popular belief, I do like **Jaylen Brown**. I love his game, and things got difficult there with that team. In putting the team together, just to give some context, we had **Kawhi Leonard** on that penciled in on that team, and he got hurt at the end of that season in 2024. He came to camp, he wasn't right, and so we had to pivot. A couple of things happened at that camp, and people want to believe what they want to believe, but at the end of the day, **Kevin Durant** showed up, he was hurt. He had a grade one calf strain, and I think we all have PTSD from the last time he had a calf strain, so we were very patient with him. Jayson Tatum had just had a baby, so he showed up late. Joel Embiid was, you know, we knew we were managing him, but we needed him for **Nikola Jokic** against Serbia, because that was the matchup. We didn't have our full roster, and then we started looking around and saying, oh man, like **Jrue Holiday** was so important for us in the FIBA game. A point guard who can play his role, ball pressure, the whole nine. If Jrue got hurt, our point guards were **Jrue Holiday** and **Stephen Curry**, and defense wasn't necessarily their strong point. It was like, okay, let's bring in insurance for **Jrue Holiday** and like **Derrick White**. So that was the thought process."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hGrgAWT8rWw&t=103s

**3. Grant Hill — "The mistake I made was I started trying to interpret cryptic tweets, which I'm not very good at." — Grant Hill admits mistake in interpreting cryptic tweets and roster communication** [03:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hGrgAWT8rWw&t=220s)

Grant Hill: "The mistake I made was I started trying to interpret cryptic tweets, which I'm not very good at. When I announced the team, there were certain guys that I called before **Adrian Wojnarowski** and **Shams Charania** broke it. I called like **Damian Lillard**, I called **Jimmy Butler**, I called everybody because you never know when they are going to break it, and you want to give guys the respect of hearing it from me. I called **Jaylen Brown**, we had a great conversation, unbelievable conversation. Something got lost there, and the implication that a shoe company, to me that, look, you guys can criticize my leadership, you can criticize the ability to put together a roster. When you're in a role that I'm in, you open yourself up to criticism, I accept that. But to imply that a shoe company told me what to do, that was wrong. I made two mistakes. I didn't understand the sensitivity of, you got three guys from the Celtics, and we're trying to put a roster together and get through this. That was a mistake I made. And the other mistake I made was with **Draymond Green**."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hGrgAWT8rWw&t=220s

**4. Grant Hill — "I didn't have Draymond Green on that list, and I think Draymond wanted to play." — Grant Hill regrets not informing Draymond Green about roster exclusion** [05:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hGrgAWT8rWw&t=318s)

Grant Hill: "In January, you got to put together a list of 40 people that will be finalists for the roster, and you got to submit that to the USOC. We did that in January 2024. So I had guys on the World Cup, I had **Paul George**, **Chris Paul**, all these guys on the list. **Draymond Green** had gone through and had a couple incidents in the fall, not to relive that, and I didn't have **Draymond Green** on that list, and I think Draymond wanted to play. I think he enjoyed his USA basketball experience, and the mistake I made was I should have let him know beforehand that he wasn't going to be on the list instead of seeing it when it came out. That was something I regret. I wish I at least given him a call, let him know, hey, you're not on the list, this is the reason, as opposed to him hearing about it when everyone else heard about it."
