**3. Aaron Judge — "It's something that I feel comfortable in. I've always felt like I've been a leader at some point." — Aaron Judge discusses the responsibility of being Yankees captain** [03:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=odPEM4h2orY&t=235s)

**Aaron Judge:** "It's interesting, honestly. It's something that I feel comfortable in. I've always felt like I've been a leader at some point throughout growing up. I've always been like a leader on a team and never truly been named captain. It's fun because you get to see what type of teammates you have. You get to see how they take criticism, how they take positivity and negativity, and how do you help push them to be better every single day. And sometimes it's just being a good teammate off the court. You don't have to talk about baseball. It's just having great chemistry and doing stuff like that. So honestly, it's fun, it's different, but I mean, it's something I'm comfortable doing."
