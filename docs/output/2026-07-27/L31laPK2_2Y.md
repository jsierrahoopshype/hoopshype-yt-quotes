**3. Marshawn Lynch — "I played 13 years in the league and I didn't have maybe 15 teammates commit suicide." — Marshawn Lynch on teammates struggling with mental health** [02:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L31laPK2_2Y&t=171s)

Marshawn Lynch: "I played 13 years in the league and I didn't have maybe 15 teammates commit suicide. Me not even knowing, when I used to have conversation with niggas, they would say certain shit like that, right? But then at the end of the day, they don't get to reap the benefits that you just was able to reach in the game. So I know for a fact, had they got to that point, then maybe it would have been different. But then I think about how many players out there that get in their head that'll have that same conversation with theirself. I just wish that niggas would really get out their head and understand that you can put in the work, play the game, love that motherfucker, let it do what it's going to do, like you said, make money and all the shit, but understand that at the end of the day, that's just a portion of your life."

**4. Marshawn Lynch — "I look at my life in stages. Got elementary, we got junior high, we got high school, got college." — Marshawn Lynch on viewing his career as a series of stages** [04:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L31laPK2_2Y&t=243s)

Marshawn Lynch: "I look at my life in stages. Got elementary, we got junior high, we got high school, got college, got the league, and then what's after that? The fact of the matter is all of them, all of them levels, they come to an end and you level up and you go to something else. I wish niggas would start looking at their career like, okay, this is just an opportunity, a stepping stone, just like Pop Warner or AAU was, just like high school, college was. Look at the league as, okay, nigga, this is a stepping stone for me to do what I said I had next, World Cup shit, right? So I use the NFL as a stepping stone for what was next for a nigga like me and my life."
