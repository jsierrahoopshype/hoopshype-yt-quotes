# Boogie, Lou & Chandler SOUND OFF on Darryn Peterson ONLY Visting Wizards — *Run It Back*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ULkR2yvlAZY

_Speakers identified: Chandler Parsons, DeMarcus Cousins, Lou Williams_

**1. Chandler Parsons — "You don't want any negative headlines swirling around your name." — Darryn Peterson limiting draft meetings to the Washington Wizards** [00:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ULkR2yvlAZY&t=22s)

Chandler Parsons: "It's not a good move at all. Before your NBA career starts, especially when you have this much potential, when you have a chance to be the number one pick, you don't want any negative headlines swirling around your name. Going back to the college season last year, he had a couple of issues with his time at Kansas, his time with Bill Self, and now this. It's not like he's this for sure number one pick. This is interesting to me because I think AJ is the consensus number one, so it would make more sense if he did that."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ULkR2yvlAZY&t=22s

**2. DeMarcus Cousins — "I think he's trying to position himself to go exactly where he wants." — Darryn Peterson trying to control his draft process** [01:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ULkR2yvlAZY&t=86s)

DeMarcus Cousins: "I think it's more so him trying to position himself, control as much as he can in this process, which is not much. Obviously, the power is in the league's hands as far as who they decide to pick in the draft. I think he's trying to position himself to go exactly where he wants. It's pretty obvious that with Utah having that number two pick, I don't think that's a place that he wants to be. You look at that roster construction, you look at the young talent in **Keyonte George**, a player that needs to have the ball in his hands, and then you obviously think of a guy like **Darryn Peterson** who also needs the ball in his hands. It automatically makes you think it might not possibly work."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ULkR2yvlAZY&t=86s

**3. Chandler Parsons — "Maybe that's that where he's just kind of putting it into the atmosphere." — Darryn Peterson's agency potentially driving his draft strategy** [02:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ULkR2yvlAZY&t=175s)

Chandler Parsons: "The more I think about it, could this just be a push from his agency that look, we're the number one pick, this is what we feel like we are, this is so I'm not working out for anybody else? Maybe that's that where he's just kind of putting it into the atmosphere that look, I'm the number one pick. Wizards should take me. If not, I'm not working out for anybody else."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ULkR2yvlAZY&t=175s

**4. Lou Williams — "All promos that I've seen so far, it's been AJ." — Darryn Peterson's agency dominating his public image** [03:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ULkR2yvlAZY&t=190s)

**Lou Williams:** "It's one thing to put it in the atmosphere, but it's also you got to read the room. All promos that I've seen so far, it's been AJ. It's 100% been AJ from him hosting at the finals to him being a part of the NBA promotional stuff. Some way you got to read the room. Am I not wrong?"

**Chandler Parsons:** "100%. That's what I'm saying. You never even want this kind of negative attention surrounding your name. Is there anything about Boozer right now? Is there anything about Caleb Wilson that's negative? So if I'm a franchise, like a Utah Jazz, who by the way, I don't know why he wouldn't want to go to Utah with that core, with Markkanen and Jaren Jackson and Keyonte George, all of a sudden now add a **Darryn Peterson** to that, you have a really competitive starting five that can actually make noise and win some games next year."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ULkR2yvlAZY&t=190s

**5. DeMarcus Cousins — "It does bring in some concern. It will make a team wonder about personality, character." — Darryn Peterson's draft stock and character concerns** [05:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ULkR2yvlAZY&t=323s)

DeMarcus Cousins: "Obviously, he deserves a chance to get drafted, and I don't think teams are going to hold everything that's happening for this young kid against him. But it does bring in some concern. It will make a team wonder or multiple teams wonder about personality, character, and things of that nature. Everything I've ever heard about this kid is he's a gym rat, super hard worker, good kid, come from a good family. But when there's a lot of smoke around your name, it's easy to get lost in the sauce and narratives can become true. I just want this kid keep his name clean, keep his nose clean, just put your head down to do the work."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ULkR2yvlAZY&t=323s

**6. Chandler Parsons — "I would go AJ. I like his size. I think he would immediately start at small forward." — Chandler Parsons prefers AJ Dybantsa for the Wizards** [07:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ULkR2yvlAZY&t=455s)

Chandler Parsons: "I would go AJ. I like his size. I think he would immediately start at small forward for them with George as a guard. They're big. Looking at Trey, George, AJ, AD, and Sarr, that's a competitive team. Obviously, they need to bulk up their bench. I think once they do get through the draft and then you start adding some vet pieces to the bench, you start adding the Cornets, the Harrison Barnes, the guys like that that are going to have valuable minutes on that team."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ULkR2yvlAZY&t=455s

**7. DeMarcus Cousins — "If you're in the Wizards position, you want to draft the best talent available." — DeMarcus Cousins believes both prospects fit the Wizards** [08:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ULkR2yvlAZY&t=513s)

DeMarcus Cousins: "Honestly, I think either guy could be a fit. When you think about the Wizards, they haven't had much success in the past couple seasons. Obviously, this is a talent-driven league, so if you're in the Wizards position, you want to draft the best talent available. So for me, I think either guy could make this team better. I think both of these guys have an opportunity to become franchise corner pieces. If you insert him next to an **Anthony Davis**, **Trey Young**, I like the way that looks. I like the way that sounds, so I think they can make a push in the East."
