**3. Paul Pierce — Paul Pierce argues Detroit should have championship expectations as a one seed**
<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qw2sHcA6d0E&t=549s" target="_blank" rel="noopener">09:09</a>

"They were a number one seed, right? So as a number one seed comes expectations. What are you trying to do as a number one seed? Win a championship, right? Expectations change. For the **Boston Celtics**, once you got **Tatum** back, the expectations went from, no, not only are we a playoff team, we can win it all. When **Detroit** ended the season as the number one seed, blew **New York** out three times, the expectation was, no, we not only could get to the second round this year because we didn't last year, we might could get to the finals. Look at us, guys, we're pretty damn good. Going into the playoffs, that was their expectation. Believe me. I know that locker room was talking about that. For sure. Why wouldn't you be? You won 60 games and you're the number one seed, you have to be talking championship."

<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qw2sHcA6d0E&t=690s" target="_blank" rel="noopener">11:30</a>

**Wosny Lambre:** "But here's the problem, though, Danny.
