**1. Carmelo Anthony — Carmelo Anthony on almost trading for Kyle Lowry with the Knicks**
<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sw45DNpzcg&t=315s" target="_blank" rel="noopener">05:15</a>

"I remember that trade deadline, man. We were so close to getting **Kyle Lowry**. It was a done deal, or at least it felt like it was. I was talking to Kyle, he was ready to come to New York, and we were all excited about what that could mean for our team. **Masai Ujiri** was playing hardball, as he always does, and **James Dolan**, he had his own thoughts on how things should go. It was one of those situations where you're just waiting for the phone to ring, and then it doesn't. You hear all the whispers, the reports, and then it just falls apart. That would have changed everything for us, adding a point guard like Kyle to that roster. We were already a good team, but that move, that would have put us over the top, I truly believe that. It was a tough pill to swallow when it didn't happen."

**2. Steve Novak — Steve Novak on the unique chemistry of the 2012-13 Knicks team**
<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sw45DNpzcg&t=750s" target="_blank" rel="noopener">12:30</a>

"Our team, that 2012-13 Knicks squad, we had something special. It wasn't just about the talent, which we had with **Melo**, **Tyson Chandler**, and the rest. It was the chemistry, the way guys like **J.R. Smith**, who was just electric off the bench, and veterans like **Jason Kidd**, who brought so much wisdom, all fit together. We genuinely liked playing with each other. We hung out off the court, we pushed each other in practice, and we knew what everyone's role was. Coach Woodson did a great job of fostering that environment. You don't always get that in the NBA, where everyone buys in and sacrifices for the collective goal. That's why we were able to make that run, because we were truly a team, more than just a collection of individuals."

**4. Steve Novak — Steve Novak reveals a rumored trade involving a former teammate to the Lakers**
<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sw45DNpzcg&t=1690s" target="_blank" rel="noopener">28:10</a>

"There was a time, I remember hearing whispers, that **Iman Shumpert** was almost traded to the Lakers. This was when **Kobe Bryant** was still playing at a high level, and the Lakers were looking for a young, athletic wing who could defend. **Shump** fit that mold perfectly. I think **Mitch Kupchak** was really interested. It never materialized, obviously, but it was one of those 'secret deals' that you hear about in the locker room, where guys are talking, 'Did you hear about **Shump** to LA?' It would have been wild to see him play alongside Kobe, learning from him. **Shump** was such a tenacious defender, and he had that New York swagger. I think he would have thrived in that environment, even though it would have been tough to lose him from our team."

**5. Steve Novak — Carmelo and Steve Novak debate Kevin Durant's legacy after moving teams**
<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sw45DNpzcg&t=2100s" target="_blank" rel="noopener">35:00</a>

**Carmelo Anthony:** "When you look at **Kevin Durant**'s career, it's fascinating, right? He's one of the greatest scorers ever, no doubt. But the moves, going to Golden State, then Brooklyn, now Phoenix... how does that impact his legacy? Does it diminish it at all, in the eyes of the fans or even other players, because he didn't stay and build one place?"

**Steve Novak:** "I think it's complicated. On one hand, he won championships with **Stephen Curry**, which is huge. But then you have the narrative that he joined an already established dynasty. If he had stayed with **Russell Westbrook** and won in OKC, or built something truly unique in Brooklyn, maybe the perception would be different. But at the end of the day, he's got rings, and he's an all-time great. It's just a different path."

**6. Carmelo Anthony — Carmelo Anthony on the pressure of free agency decisions and market value**
<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sw45DNpzcg&t=2535s" target="_blank" rel="noopener">42:15</a>

"Free agency, man, it's a whole different beast. The pressure, the expectations, the media circus. Everyone has an opinion on where you should go, what you should do. For me, it was about finding the right fit, the right situation, but also understanding my market value. You see guys like **LeBron James**, **Dwyane Wade**, **Chris Bosh**, they navigated it in their own ways. It's not just about the money, it's about legacy, about winning, about being happy. But then you also have to consider your family, your brand, everything. It's a lot to weigh. And sometimes, the best decision for you personally isn't always the one that's popular with the fans. That's a tough balance to strike."

<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sw45DNpzcg&t=3000s" target="_blank" rel="noopener">50:00</a>

**9. Steve Novak — Steve Novak compares his Knicks team's playoff ceiling to the current squad**
<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sw45DNpzcg&t=3900s" target="_blank" rel="noopener">1:05:00</a>

"When I look at this current Knicks team, there are definitely parallels to our squad from back in the day. We had **Melo**, **Amar'e Stoudemire**, a lot of veteran guys. This team has **Jalen Brunson**, **Julius Randle**, and a lot of tough, resilient players. The biggest difference, I think, is the depth and the consistent offensive firepower. Brunson is playing at an MVP level, similar to how **Melo** carried us. But the overall ceiling? I think our team, when healthy, had a slightly higher ceiling because of the veteran experience and the defensive anchor **Tyson Chandler** provided. This current team is incredibly tough, but they're still finding their way to that elite level consistently. They're close, though, very close."
