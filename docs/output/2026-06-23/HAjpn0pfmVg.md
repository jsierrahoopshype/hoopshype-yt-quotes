# Perk Explains What REALLY Happens The Week Before NBA Draft — *Road Trippin*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HAjpn0pfmVg

_Speakers identified: Allie Clifton, Kendrick Perkins, Channing Frye, John Wall_

**1. John Wall — "Seeing her work two to three jobs when I was growing up, take me to school, pick me up" — John Wall on his mother's sacrifices leading up to his draft night** [00:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HAjpn0pfmVg&t=31s)

John Wall: "Seeing her work two to three jobs when I was growing up, take me to school, pick me up from school, that's all she ever did. She barely could go to my AAU tournaments, sometimes don't pay the light bill so I can play, and I'm 12 or 13 years old. I don't know if I'm making it to the NBA. This is her just giving me opportunity to hope that I can get there. And so many kids across the world that think they're going to be NBA players, and there's only 60 that's going to be drafted."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HAjpn0pfmVg&t=31s

**2. Kendrick Perkins — "My grandmother making $60 a week as a maid, my grandfather making $300 a month as the church janitor" — Kendrick Perkins on the sacrifices his grandparents made for his basketball career** [03:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HAjpn0pfmVg&t=192s)

Kendrick Perkins: "You think about all the things you went through, what your parents had to sacrifice. I was raised by my grandparents. So my grandmother making $60 a week as a maid, my grandfather making $300 a month as the church janitor, the sacrifice behind that. I always tell people I could catch a chicken, clean the chicken, and have it ready for you to cook in 30 minutes. That was my job growing up in Beaumont, Texas. We had a hundred chickens and ducks running across the yard. We ate chicken all throughout the week. The only time we got pork chops was on Sunday."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HAjpn0pfmVg&t=192s

**3. Kendrick Perkins — "I went and worked out for four teams. I worked out for all the Texas squads" — Kendrick Perkins on working out for multiple teams before the NBA draft** [05:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HAjpn0pfmVg&t=313s)

Kendrick Perkins: "I went and worked out for four teams. I worked out for all the Texas squads, because at the time, the Rockets had I believe the 28th pick, the Mavericks had like the 29th pick, and the Spurs were right around that area, I think they had like the 26th pick. I went to Boston, I dominated the 101 matchup with **Brian Cook**. He won national player of the year that year too. I had to give his ass that work. So the Celtics then and there was like, 'Perk, we want you to stay another day.'"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HAjpn0pfmVg&t=313s

**4. Kendrick Perkins — "I called John Calipari. Cal still my guy to this day. I say, 'Cal, listen man'" — Kendrick Perkins on his decision to sign with the Memphis Grizzlies** [06:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HAjpn0pfmVg&t=372s)

Kendrick Perkins: "I had to pick up the phone. I called **John Calipari**. Cal still my guy to this day. I say, 'Cal, listen man, I know we had plans on me coming to Memphis, but that shit ain't happening. I'm going to go ahead and leave my name up in this draft.' I signed with Memphis with Cal. Right before you had, Jay Wall, I had already signed and committed with Memphis."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HAjpn0pfmVg&t=372s

**5. John Wall — "When he came and sat and talked to my mom, my mom cold-hearted. She didn't talk about basketball" — John Wall on why he committed to playing for Kendrick Perkins** [07:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HAjpn0pfmVg&t=434s)

John Wall: "When he came and sat and talked to my mom, my mom cold-hearted. She didn't talk about basketball, bro. He didn't promise me I'm going to have the ball 95% of the time. He said, 'Listen, you're going to leave, you're going to come out, know what you're going to do. Take some people with you, don't leave by yourself. But I want you to go make your money, make sure your family good.' He ain't trying to make you stay two to three years. Now if you sorry and you need to come back, he's going to tell your ass come back."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HAjpn0pfmVg&t=434s

**6. Kendrick Perkins — "I knew that they wanted Marcus Banks. And if anybody was familiar with Marcus Banks in college" — Kendrick Perkins on the Memphis Grizzlies trading picks to get Marcus Banks** [08:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HAjpn0pfmVg&t=491s)

Kendrick Perkins: "I knew that they wanted **Marcus Banks**. And if anybody was familiar with **Marcus Banks** in college, motherfuckers were staying up late to watch **Marcus Banks** at UNLV. I didn't realize that Danny made a deal with the Memphis Grizzlies to go ahead and say, 'You know what, we're going to swap picks.' Memphis had the 13th pick and the 27th pick, and what happened was, in between there, Danny had got with that **Sam Presti** in Seattle was going to take **Marcus Banks** in between that 13 and 16. So Danny had to maneuver with **Chris Wallace** at the time, 'Hey man, I need you to do this and I'm going to do that.'"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HAjpn0pfmVg&t=491s

**7. Kendrick Perkins — "I got drafted at 18 to the Clippers. They had three picks. They took D Miles" — Kendrick Perkins on being drafted by the Los Angeles Clippers** [10:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HAjpn0pfmVg&t=644s)

Kendrick Perkins: "I got drafted at 18 to the Clippers. They had three picks. They took D Miles, which at the time he was the highest high school pick ever to come out. They took him, D Miles didn't work out for the Clippers. They took **Keyon Dooling** in that 10, he didn't work out for the Clippers, and they took me at 18, and I never worked out for the Clippers. I was already hot by the time they got to 18. I'm watching people get picked, and you know how we are, I'm like, 'Man, stop it.' I literally had to work out against these dudes and I was like, 'Oh, so the workouts didn't mean nothing.'"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HAjpn0pfmVg&t=644s
