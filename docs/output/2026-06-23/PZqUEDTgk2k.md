**1. Chandler Parsons — "Anthony Edwards is not going anywhere. He is the face of the NBA." — Why Anthony Edwards is not a realistic trade candidate** [02:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PZqUEDTgk2k&t=135s)

Chandler Parsons: "**Anthony Edwards** is not going anywhere. He is the face of the NBA. You do not even entertain the thought of moving a player who has that kind of gravitational pull on the league. The idea that he would be the next superstar to be traded is just noise. **2. Lou Williams — "The pressure is on the front office to maximize his window right now." — The challenge of building around a young star like Anthony Edwards** [15:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PZqUEDTgk2k&t=940s)

**Lou Williams:** "The problem in Minnesota is not **Anthony Edwards**, it is the cap situation. They have so much money tied up in **Karl-Anthony Towns** and others that they cannot get him the secondary help he needs. The pressure is on the front office to maximize his window right now."

**DeMarcus Cousins:** "Exactly. If you do not surround him with the right pieces, you are wasting his prime. You cannot expect him to carry that load alone every single night in the Western Conference."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PZqUEDTgk2k&t=940s

**3. DeMarcus Cousins — "This draft class is being unfairly criticized for its lack of immediate impact." — Critique of the current NBA draft class depth and impact** [28:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PZqUEDTgk2k&t=1690s)

DeMarcus Cousins: "This draft class is being unfairly criticized for its lack of immediate impact. People want to see instant superstars, but the reality is that these guys need time to develop in a professional system. The front offices that are patient with these picks are going to be the ones that look smart in three years. It is not about who is the best player today, it is about who fits the long-term identity of your roster."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PZqUEDTgk2k&t=1690s

**4. Chandler Parsons — "If Minnesota cannot put the right pieces around Anthony Edwards, he is going to want out." — Anthony Edwards' future trade speculation and Minnesota's financial constraints** [1:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PZqUEDTgk2k&t=3600s)

**Chandler Parsons:** "If Minnesota cannot put the right pieces around **Anthony Edwards**, he is going to want out. You look at the second apron, you look at the financial situation they are in, it is going to be impossible to keep this roster together. If they do not win a championship soon, he is the next guy who is going to be looking for a way out."

**Lou Williams:** "I agree. When you have a talent like Ant, you have to maximize that window.
