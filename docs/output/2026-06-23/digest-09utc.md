**4. Carmelo Anthony — "The person that sort of determines that besides you is Darryn Peterson of Kansas." — Carmelo Anthony on the rivalry between AJ Dybantsa and Darryn Peterson** [03:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wYRCcZckiBY&t=183s)

**Carmelo Anthony:** "The person that sort of determines that besides you is **Darryn Peterson** of Kansas. For the last several years, you both have been one and two, and in a lot of people's eyes, they are not really sure if it's going to be one or two. What's that back and forth been like with **Darryn Peterson** over the years?"

**AJ Dybantsa:** "I was class of 2026 before I reclassed back up. I was light years better than everybody until I got back in that class and he gave me a run for my money. It just makes me more competitive, keeps me motivated."

**5. AJ Dybantsa — "Me never winning actually helped me because a lot of people can get complacent." — AJ Dybantsa on overcoming adversity and losing in his development** [05:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wYRCcZckiBY&t=345s)

**Monica McNutt:** "What adversities or what challenges do you think have helped you grow the most to this point in your journey?"

**AJ Dybantsa:** "Me never winning actually helped me because a lot of people can get complacent. When you win, you are used to it, so when you don't win, you don't know what to do. I have been in teams and situations where I haven't been winning. I lost in the first round last year, I didn't win Big 12, I didn't make it to Chipotle Nationals. So I have to keep finding ways to win. When I hit my peak, I will be winning, rather than me winning now and when I'm losing I don't know how to deal with losing."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wYRCcZckiBY&t=345s

**6. AJ Dybantsa — "My rate of improvement throughout the season is actually ridiculous." — AJ Dybantsa on his rate of improvement and film study** [07:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wYRCcZckiBY&t=437s)

**Carmelo Anthony:** "What are your small goals throughout the course of the season?"

**AJ Dybantsa:** "My rate of improvement throughout the season is actually ridiculous. If you watch my first game reads versus my last game reads, it's night and day, especially the film I watch. I have only played 35 games, so that's half a season. Imagine if you give me 82 games to just watch film, and I have a coach dedicated to watch film with me and unlimited gym access. Those are going to be the smaller wins for me. In the beginning of the season, you are averaging 15, five and five; at the end of the season, you are averaging 26 and seven."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wYRCcZckiBY&t=437s
