# Josh Donaldson on REJECTING $35M, Demanding $100M + Oakland A's Contract Negotiations — *Brice Butler Studios*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aM0IAnmcSbk

_Speakers identified: Josh Donaldson, Brice Butler_

**1. Josh Donaldson — "I said, 'No. We're not even close.'" — Josh Donaldson on rejecting a five-year, $35 million contract offer** [01:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aM0IAnmcSbk&t=72s)

**Josh Donaldson:** "**Bob Melvin**, our manager, goes, 'Hey, Finny,' who's **Dan Feinstein**, the assistant general manager, 'let's get JD locked up to a deal right now. JD, what do you think?' I said, 'Sure. Let's do it.' He goes, 'JD, what do you think about five years for $35 million?' I wasn't. In baseball, what's different is that you have to serve three full years until you can go into arbitration. If you do that, then you have three years of arbitration. You go to arbitration and you're not getting actually what your value is. It's a lot lower. But so he throws a five-year deal at me for $35 million and I go, 'No. We're not even close.' I said, 'We're not even close.' Our bench coach at the time, **Chip Hale**, looks at me and he's like, 'Well, what about **Jason Kipnis**'s deal? He signed a six-year deal for $52 million.' I said, 'Chip, we've already had this conversation. You know I'm not taking that deal.'"

**Brice Butler:** "At this point, are they looking at you like, 'Damn, he's confident'?"

**Josh Donaldson:** "They're just feeling me out. They're trying to see what's going on."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aM0IAnmcSbk&t=72s

**2. Josh Donaldson — "I'll sign right now, seven years, $100 million." — Josh Donaldson on his $100 million contract demand to the A's** [02:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aM0IAnmcSbk&t=165s)

**Josh Donaldson:** "I look at **Dan Feinstein** and I say, 'Hey, Dan, I'm going to save you $50 million right now. You want to hear what I got to say?' He's like, 'Yeah, JD, what do you got to say?' He's starting to beat up with sweat. I can see some beads coming down the side of his head. I go, 'All right. I said, look here, I'll sign right now, seven years, $100 million. And here's how I'm going to save you $50 million. Because if the season goes like I think it's going to go, at the end of the year, I'm asking seven for $150 million. Look, I just saved you $50 million.' He's like, 'JD, get the hell out of here.' I said, 'All right. I said, don't come to me unless you're going to be serious. Now you know what you're dealing with right now.' Literally, a week after that, **Kyle Seager**, who was a third baseman for the Seattle Mariners, signed seven for $100 million."

**Brice Butler:** "I mean, you know the market. The players know."

**Josh Donaldson:** "But I just didn't know that was going to happen. But that's what it would take for me to give up, because I was going to have to give up more of my free agent years at that time."
