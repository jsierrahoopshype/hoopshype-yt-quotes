This team's got a lot of problems, Rachel. How concerned are you about Miami?"

**2. Chris Mannix — "This team is built to be a grind-it-out, win games in the 90s, early low triple digits." — Critique of the Miami Heat's current playing style** [01:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3PLgPBqGNv8&t=97s)

Chris Mannix: "I hate how they're playing. I hate the style with which they're playing. I said all during the preseason, this team is built to be a grind-it-out, win games in the 90s, early low triple digits and be the best defensive team in the league. You've got arguably the best defensive frontcourt in basketball, two-man frontcourt, putting aside **Victor Wembanyama** in San Antonio. You've got **Damian Lillard** who is a proven defender at the point guard position, **Andrew Wiggins** who has been a decent defender over the years. This team should be grinding out wins. Instead, they are 10th in the NBA in pace right now. They are playing the high-possession game and they're losing. They're losing these high-possession games. It's crazy to me how this team is playing. They are not an up-tempo team."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3PLgPBqGNv8&t=97s

**3. Chris Mannix — "Clay Thompson, not having a bad start to the season, but he's not Clay Thompson anymore." — Assessment of Clay Thompson and Damian Lillard's roles** [02:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3PLgPBqGNv8&t=143s)

Chris Mannix: "**Clay Thompson**, not having a bad start to the season, but he's not **Clay Thompson** anymore. He's not. He's a mid-30s three-point shooter at this stage of his career. Probably better suited being a sixth man off the bench. **Damian Lillard** is not an offensive point guard. That is not his strength. It is driving me crazy."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3PLgPBqGNv8&t=143s

**4. Rachel Nichols — "I mean, I think they've given up 115 points or more per game so far, is that right?" — Miami Heat's defensive struggles and high points allowed** [04:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3PLgPBqGNv8&t=244s)

**Rachel Nichols:** "I mean, I think they've given up 115 points or more per game so far, is that right? I mean, do you have the numbers of where they've ranked defensively?"

**Chris Mannix:** "Yeah, I'll pull it up. But it's a chicken and the egg thing though. If you're playing high possession, you're giving up a lot of possession on the other end. You could be a solid defensive team in the half court, but if you're getting possession after possession, it's going to be up that high, then you're going to have problems. This team should be walking the ball up the court, running offense through Giannis and Bam in the post or the mid-post, wherever. They're just not playing the right way."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3PLgPBqGNv8&t=244s

**5. Rachel Nichols — "To me, Miami is a work-in-progress team." — Miami Heat as a work-in-progress team needing time** [06:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3PLgPBqGNv8&t=386s)

**Rachel Nichols:** "To me, Miami is a work-in-progress team. And look, you and I live in LA, we heard it from the Lakers for years when they were saying, oh, just wait, just wait till next season when we have our roster out here because then we'll really have the off-season to tinker. And to an extent, it's what happened with Luka, right? They got Luka, kind of dropped in their lap in the middle of the season and they were like, okay, this isn't the team we're going to have. And in Miami, with Giannis, obviously they went after him, he was not dropped in their lap, but they had to give away enough of the farm to get him, who was available, and at what they could get this past off-season was not a lot, which is why they ended up with a **Clay Thompson**, the exact point you're making. So they are a team to me that is going to need a season or two to get the roster around Giannis and Bam to really maximize having Giannis and Bam."

**Chris Mannix:** "I don't think it's a fit issue though. I think they're just not very good. They've got two great big men, serviceable point guard, a faded former all-NBA guard. I don't think this is the kind of group that if they grow together, they're going to get significantly better."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3PLgPBqGNv8&t=386s

**6. Chris Mannix — "Bam Adebayo looks lost out there. He's averaging 13 points per game through three games." — Bam Adebayo's poor performance and need for change** [08:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3PLgPBqGNv8&t=506s)

Chris Mannix: "**Bam Adebayo** looks lost out there. He's averaging 13 points per game through three games, shooting around 42% from the floor, low 20s from three. He looks completely lost on the offensive end. The style of play has got to change. I think they've got to be much more of a half-court focused team and slow the game down. Now, that may not be the solution because the shooting problems, as you pointed out, are still going to be there. This style of play ain't beating top teams, not consistently. It is the wrong style of play for the roster that this team currently has."
