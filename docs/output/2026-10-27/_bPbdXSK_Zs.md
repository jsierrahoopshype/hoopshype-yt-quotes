# Lou Will DOUBLES DOWN On Knicks Championship Hangover! — *Run It Back*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_bPbdXSK_Zs

_Speakers identified: Michelle Beadle, Lou Williams, Chandler Parsons_

**1. Lou Williams — "The odds are stacked against you because everybody understands your style of play." — The New York Knicks are suffering from a championship hangover** [00:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_bPbdXSK_Zs&t=37s)

Lou Williams: "They have a certain style of play that everybody was able to watch on the biggest stage. While you guys were competing for a championship, the rest of the league was at home watching and studying you. You became a marked target for what teams and players individually wanted to accomplish. When you come back into that season and you bring everybody back, you haven't tweaked a lot of things. Teams have been able to scout it way more than you've been able to scout them, and they've had success with competing against you. That's part of the hangover. It's not necessarily saying that the Knicks are going to come into the season and not take the opportunity serious to go back-to-back and try to repeat. No, it's just the odds are stacked against you because everybody understands your style of play. Everybody knows what you're bringing to the table. We know who is going to have the ball in the fourth quarter. We know that **Josh Hart** and **Mikal Bridges** are going to be the energy guys."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_bPbdXSK_Zs&t=37s

**2. Chandler Parsons — "I don't think we've seen the best version of the Knicks this season." — The New York Knicks have a history of slow starts** [02:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_bPbdXSK_Zs&t=174s)

Chandler Parsons: "They do have a history of having ups and downs throughout their season, but they've also shown that they can go run off ten straight at any moment. I think this is part of their identity. It's almost like they got to get put in the corner first to then press the gas and move forward. I don't think we've seen the best version of the Knicks this season. It could be the off-season playing a part in it. It could also be the contract extension talks that have been put at a halt. I also feel like **James Dolan** had a pretty loud message without saying anything as far as not extending the guys, not really improving the team over the off-season. It seems like they are a little content with just winning one."

**3. Michelle Beadle — "I've talked to a bunch of Knicks fans who are like, 'Well, we got our one.'" — Knicks fans feel satisfied after winning one championship** [05:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_bPbdXSK_Zs&t=342s)

Michelle Beadle: "I've talked to a bunch of Knicks fans who are like, 'Well, we got our one.' I've never heard that from a fan. As a Spurs fan, I've never said, 'Well, we got our five.' No, the next one, you want the next one and the one after that and all that. And those are just fans. I don't think the players feel this way, but I'm wondering if the pressure from the outside going in feels less because they got that one out of the way."
