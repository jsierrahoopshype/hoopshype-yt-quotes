# How LeBron Adjusts His Game With Age

Channel: Mind the Game

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kl0IpP7XDBw

_Speakers identified: Steve Nash, LeBron James_

**1. LeBron James, on team dynamics, player legacy, playoffs**
[00:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kl0IpP7XDBw&t=17s)

"I'm trying to push the limit, Steve, as much as possible. There are times out on the floor where, okay, you have to maybe scale back a little bit. You have to maybe, I don't want to say preserve any energy because definitely in the postseason, it's so much harder to do that. Now, you get off the ball more than you would when you were 28, 32. I can run 10-12 straight pick and rolls and make all the plays and pretty much not be tired at the end of that or not feel worn down in the fourth quarter. That's not probably going to be beneficial to the long term of that game or even the next game, especially in the postseason, of me running 10 straight pick and rolls or me having the ball in my hands for 10-12 straight possessions. That's just not beneficial to not only myself, and then it won't be beneficial to the team. So just kind of picking your spots, understanding when the team may need this or need that. So playing off the ball, playing on the ball, spotting up in the corner, just trying to diversify my portfolio out on the floor in order for me to have the maximum energy that I can produce still at this point in my career."

_Why it matters:_ This insight from LeBron reveals his strategic approach to energy conservation and role diversification, which is crucial for the Lakers' postseason aspirations and his continued effectiveness at an advanced age.
