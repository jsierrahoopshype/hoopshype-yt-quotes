**2. Iman Shumpert — "We're not used to seeing a small guard win at this rate." — Iman Shumpert on why Jalen Brunson is underrated** [00:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5JCL0JfiElE&t=41s)

But I can't say he's underrated around the league because you still got to put eyes on him, you still got to have a scheme for him."
