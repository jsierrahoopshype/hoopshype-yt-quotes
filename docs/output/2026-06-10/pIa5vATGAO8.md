# Vince Goodwill says the Knicks losing Game 4 would UNDO Games 1 & 2 | The Rich Eisen Show — *NBA on ESPN*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pIa5vATGAO8

_Speakers identified: Rich Eisen, Vincent Goodwill, Chris Brockman_

**1. Vincent Goodwill — "I don't think that we are at the risk of something ugly happening. This is the NBA Finals." — The impact of officiating on the Knicks and Spurs series** [00:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pIa5vATGAO8&t=18s)

**Rich Eisen:** "We just started the conversation about how the narratives from Game 3 are going to truly roost in Game 4. I think it's the officiating. And if they just spend the first six, seven minutes just letting everything go like Game 3, we're running some risks, quite frankly. What's been the conversation, do you think, on that front?"

**Vincent Goodwill:** "It's always weird from game to game because it's a different officiating crew every game. **Zach Zarba** tonight is the league's lead official. He might be the NBA's best official and the players respect him and they like him and he's always communicative with those guys. I don't think that we are at the risk of something ugly happening. This is the NBA Finals. I think there's a lot of chatter, but nobody wants to get thrown out of the game. I think what happened with **Victor Wembanyama** the other day, that was a little retaliatory for things that have been happening throughout the course of the series. But at least I don't think, unless **Charles Oakley** gets his ban lifted and comes into the Garden tonight, I don't think anything crazy is going to happen on the floor."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pIa5vATGAO8&t=18s

**2. Vincent Goodwill — "There has been a conversation that I'm sure some Knicks players have heard is that you can't allow Jalen Brunson to be shoved over in your Garden." — Protecting Jalen Brunson from physical play in the Garden** [01:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pIa5vATGAO8&t=102s)

**Rich Eisen:** "There has been a conversation that I'm sure some Knicks players have heard is that you can't allow **Jalen Brunson** to be shoved over in your Garden and be able to live to tell the tale. You shouldn't let that happen. I don't know who's going to be the guy if it happens again, or if the officials will just say we've got this. But I certainly know the Garden crowd will be hyper-aware from jump as to whistles, where they are, where they aren't."

**Vincent Goodwill:** "It's going to be a desperate atmosphere. The Knicks have so much on the line for tonight. The pressure is all on them. If this series goes back to San Antonio 2-2, you have done undid all of your great work throughout the course of this series. So as far as **Jalen Brunson** goes, there has to be someone that says we have to protect our captain, we have to protect our leader. What that looks like can be on a multitude of things. It doesn't mean you go after **Victor Wembanyama**. It means maybe I'll set a hard screen on **Stefan Castle**. Maybe I'll set a really hard screen. Maybe it's clean, maybe it's an offensive foul, but it's a physical screen that sets the tone."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pIa5vATGAO8&t=102s

**3. Vincent Goodwill — "The Knicks were by far the more physical, aggressive team. And when that happens, the refs look at it." — The Knicks must adopt a more physical tone to avoid losing momentum** [02:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pIa5vATGAO8&t=150s)

Vincent Goodwill: "The Knicks were not the more physical team the other night. The Spurs were by far the more physical, aggressive team. And when that happens, the refs look at it and they say, 'Oh, if this team is playing on this tone and you guys are not matching their physicality, don't expect us to bail you out.' So I do think the Knicks have in general to take a more physical, a more aggressive tone, but they just have to know where the line is."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pIa5vATGAO8&t=150s

**4. Vincent Goodwill — "Mitch Johnson took over for Greg Popovich, who had a stroke over a year ago, and has had to mold and meld this team into his image." — Mitch Johnson's challenge as a first-time head coach in the Finals** [07:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pIa5vATGAO8&t=420s)

Vincent Goodwill: "I am fascinated by **Mitch Johnson**, the not yet 40-year-old head coach of the San Antonio Spurs. So much of what we talk about with the Spurs revolves around this lineage: **Greg Popovich**, **Tim Duncan**, and **David Robinson**. **Mitch Johnson** took over for **Greg Popovich**, who had a stroke over a year ago, and has had to mold and meld this team into his image. And we don't know that much about him. And he's in the NBA Finals for the very first time. Can you imagine the pressure that he feels? He's a novice in this. Mike Brown, this is his third NBA Finals. He's been through this. **Mitch Johnson** has not."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pIa5vATGAO8&t=420s

**5. Vincent Goodwill — "They've had crushing losses throughout all of their championship years. Remember the Ray Allen shot in 2013." — The Spurs' resilience in the face of crushing playoff losses** [09:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pIa5vATGAO8&t=540s)

Vincent Goodwill: "Think about it from Pop's standpoint or the Spurs' standpoint. They've had crushing losses throughout all of their championship years. Remember the **Ray Allen** shot in 2013. That was in Game 6, they could have clinched the championship, and they had to go back two days later and play a Game 7, and they should have won that Game 7, and it didn't break them. So for Pop to come on after a crushing loss in Game 2, they very well could have packed it in and said, 'You know what, this is the Knicks' coronation.' They don't care, and they don't scare. And I think part of that has to come from this Spurs DNA, led by **Greg Popovich**, who clearly, of course, 75, 76, 77 years old, is not the person he once was, but still clairvoyant and still has experience and still has the players' ears."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pIa5vATGAO8&t=540s

**6. Vincent Goodwill — "I think Game 4s are usually classics. Michael Jordan's 55 against the Phoenix Suns was a Game 4." — The historical significance of Game 4 in NBA Finals series** [10:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pIa5vATGAO8&t=620s)

Vincent Goodwill: "I want to see a long series. I don't want this NBA season to end. It has gotten great. I think Game 4s are usually classics. **Michael Jordan**'s 55 against the Phoenix Suns was a Game 4. Magic's Junior Junior Sky Hook was a Game 4. **Shake Gilgeous-Alexander** gave you a great Game 4. **Stephen Curry** put the Warriors on his back in Boston a few years ago in Game 4. What I'm looking forward to seeing is either **Jalen Brunson** or **Victor Wembanyama** adding their name to NBA Finals lore, because Game 4 tells you who's winning the series and it tells you who the best team is."
