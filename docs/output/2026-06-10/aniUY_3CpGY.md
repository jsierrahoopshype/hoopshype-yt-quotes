**4. Richard Jefferson — "This Spurs team has been playing against physical guys." — The Spurs are a highly physical team** [03:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aniUY_3CpGY&t=202s)

Richard Jefferson: "When we were talking to the Spurs earlier, they said people forget, the Portland team that we played against was a very physical team. Then you look at OKC, a very, very physical team. So this Spurs team has been playing against physical guys. You have **Drew Eubanks**, **Camara**, they had physical defenders in Portland. We know what OKC has with **Caruso** and **Cason Wallace**. So they are seasoned when it comes to a physical game. The Spurs are now leading with physicality. If you're down 0-2 and you're on the road, if you're not going to be physical, you don't got much of a chance. You better show up with, I'm going to run some people over. Obviously there is a Wembanyama play, there is a Castle play. Even they were talking about Minnesota. Minnesota, even though they were banged up, they're a physical team. You got **Anne Edward**, you got **Jaden McDaniels**, you got good defenders. So when you look at their group, they've been."
