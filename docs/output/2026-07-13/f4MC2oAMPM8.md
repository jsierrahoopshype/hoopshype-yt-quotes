**6. Vincent Goodwill — "we never asked NBA owners or owners of any sport, 'Hey, sacrifice some of your profits'" — NBA players shouldn't be expected to sacrifice earnings for team building** [10:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f4MC2oAMPM8&t=603s)

Vincent Goodwill: "I don't like it. I'm all for these players, and we got to understand this, these players have a very short shelf life to be able to maximize their earnings potential. We never asked NBA owners or owners of any sport, 'Hey, sacrifice some of your profits so that you can put a better product on the floor or on the field.' It's only on the players to have this responsibility. I don't like it. It's clearly a trickle-down effect, not just from the second apron, but from the CBA that was signed in 1998-99 that instituted a max contract. All these things have been taken away from players, and I know it's hard to understand that when you see the numbers going up and up and up with a bigger pie, but it's the elements of the pie. It's the haves and the have-nots. And when you look at the max players, the fact that in every other sport, it's a free market. In the NBA, there is a max salary, which is a cap on what you can do. And when you have a second apron, you're putting this responsibility on the players to play coach and general manager. If that's the case, give me two salaries if I got to do two jobs."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f4MC2oAMPM8&t=603s

**7. Dominique Foxworth — "in basketball, you don't want the great dynasty split up as a result of some salary cap maneuvering" — Salary cap maneuvers shouldn't dictate the future of NBA dynasties** [11:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f4MC2oAMPM8&t=683s)

Dominique Foxworth: "I don't think it's a good precedent to be set, and it's a bad expectation to have of the players. I don't think people appreciate that it's probably bad for the league and for the fans going forward. In basketball, you don't want the great dynasty split up as a result of some salary cap maneuvering. You want those teams to stay together. I want to watch Wembanyama and Castle and Harper grow into some legendary threesome that wins championships, and it's compared to other teams. This is bad for us when the history of the league is going to be determined by which player is willing to give back more money, not which player is the best and has most impact on the game. So like that's the concerning part. In this situation, it's fine, Wembanyama's great, he's going to make a lot of money. Him giving up less is not going to harm anything, but if there is even better stars who are willing to take much less than that and build up a power, it reminds me a little bit, and it might be a little bit of an unfair situation, but that momentary salary cap bump that allowed **Kevin Durant** to go to the Warriors and that kind of made those seasons a unique blip in NBA history that I think kind of messed things up. And I don't want to create any more situations like that going forward, so abolish salary cap in all sports all the time."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f4MC2oAMPM8&t=683s
