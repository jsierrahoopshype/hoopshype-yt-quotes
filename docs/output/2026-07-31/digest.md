## Team Greece: Giannis and Thanasis Antetokounmpo on WINNING a medal — *Thanalysis Show*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vr5CECk0SWs

_Speakers identified: Thanasis Antetokounmpo, Giannis Antetokounmpo_

**1. Giannis Antetokounmpo — "I'm happy that we were able to give this back to the people." — Giannis and Thanasis Antetokounmpo on the significance of winning a medal for Greece** [00:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vr5CECk0SWs&t=5s)

**Giannis Antetokounmpo:** "It's something that we've been wanting to win since we were kids. I remember 2009, 2010, when we were waking up to watch them under 20. Back then you remember **Nikos Pappas**, **Kostas Papanikolaou**, those players, and they were winning. I think they beat the USA team also. I looked at them and said maybe one day, if we're fortunate enough and we work hard, we can play in the national team. Thanasis had the opportunity to play when he was younger, he was practicing with them. So always when I saw him, he came back with the national team gear, I was like, man, maybe one day I can be part of this, represent my country. In our head, playing basketball for our country is almost like going to war."

**Thanasis Antetokounmpo:** "Going to war."

**Giannis Antetokounmpo:** "I started playing for them when I was 18, 2013, and I've played in nine tournaments and always wanted to win a medal for them. And nine tournaments later, me, my brother Thanasis, and my brother Kostas were able to accomplish this together, which was an incredible experience, one of the most great memories I've had as an athlete. I'm happy that we were able to give this back to the people, because they've been by our side for many years. Even when we failed, when we didn't accomplish our goals, they were always by our side."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vr5CECk0SWs&t=5s
