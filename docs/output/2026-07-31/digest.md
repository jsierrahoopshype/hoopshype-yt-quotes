## Team Greece: Giannis and Thanasis Antetokounmpo on WINNING a medal — *Thanalysis Show*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vr5CECk0SWs

_Speakers identified: Thanasis Antetokounmpo, Giannis Antetokounmpo_

**1. Giannis Antetokounmpo — "I'm happy that we were able to give this back to the people." — Giannis and Thanasis Antetokounmpo on the significance of winning a medal for Greece** [00:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vr5CECk0SWs&t=5s)

**Giannis Antetokounmpo:** "It's something that we've been wanting to win since we were kids. I remember 2009, 2010, when we were waking up to watch them under 20. Back then you remember **Nikos Pappas**, **Kostas Papanikolaou**, those players, and they were winning. I think they beat the USA team also. I looked at them and said maybe one day, if we're fortunate enough and we work hard, we can play in the national team. Thanasis had the opportunity to play when he was younger, he was practicing with them. So always when I saw him, he came back with the national team gear, I was like, man, maybe one day I can be part of this, represent my country. In our head, playing basketball for our country is almost like going to war."

**Thanasis Antetokounmpo:** "Going to war."

**Giannis Antetokounmpo:** "I started playing for them when I was 18, 2013, and I've played in nine tournaments and always wanted to win a medal for them. And nine tournaments later, me, my brother Thanasis, and my brother Kostas were able to accomplish this together, which was an incredible experience, one of the most great memories I've had as an athlete. I'm happy that we were able to give this back to the people, because they've been by our side for many years. Even when we failed, when we didn't accomplish our goals, they were always by our side."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vr5CECk0SWs&t=5s

**2. Giannis Antetokounmpo — "The only game we lost was against Turkey in the semifinals." — Giannis Antetokounmpo on the team's loss to Turkey in the semifinals** [02:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vr5CECk0SWs&t=172s)

Giannis Antetokounmpo: "The only game we lost was against Turkey in the semifinals. You have to say that's the worst moment, but Turkey had five, six NBA players. We knew going to the game that we had to play our A-game to be even close in the game, but we did not even play our A-game. We did not even play a C-game. It was not good. And they were ready to go. So probably I'd say, losing from a stacked team against Turkey."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vr5CECk0SWs&t=172s

**3. Giannis Antetokounmpo — "I just love the anxiety, the urgency that I feel before a big game." — Giannis Antetokounmpo on the emotional intensity of playing for the national team** [04:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vr5CECk0SWs&t=288s)

Giannis Antetokounmpo: "I just love the anxiety, the urgency that I feel before a big game. The day before Finland, I was just anxious and I saw my brother Kostas talking to everybody. He went to everybody and talked specifically, 'Hey, we need to do this, we need to do this.' Everything that we did lead up to the last game, I was uncomfortable, but looking back, I love that feeling. I love that feeling when I'm uncomfortable. And also when we got the job done and we won the game, when my brother Thanasis and Kostas came and hugged me, and we were hugging in the middle of the court, it was probably one of the best moments of my career and of EuroBasket this year."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vr5CECk0SWs&t=288s

**4. Giannis Antetokounmpo — "Everything has been filmed, documented, and I tell her all the time." — Giannis Antetokounmpo on his mother's role in documenting his career** [07:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vr5CECk0SWs&t=423s)

Giannis Antetokounmpo: "When you're six, seven, eight, 11 years old and you're young and your mom comes to little tournaments and she sees you win the ping pong tournament, and then she sees you win the soccer tournament, and then she sees you do other stuff, track or whatever tournament. So she knows that we might not win immediately, but eventually we are going to win, right? Because she knows we put the work in. Behind the scenes, she filmed the whole thing. Winning the championship, she filmed the whole thing. Winning the second MVP, she filmed the whole thing. Thanasis's championship, she filmed the whole thing. Everything has been filmed, documented, and I tell her all the time. As a joke, I said, 'Mom, I want nothing. Nothing. In 100 years, when you leave this world, I just want the phone. Just put that phone on the wheel to me.'"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vr5CECk0SWs&t=423s
