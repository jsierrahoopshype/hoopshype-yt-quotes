**1. Dwyane Wade — "I have an incredible tolerance for pain, I have a high threshold for pain." — Dwyane Wade on playing through his career without a meniscus** [00:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UvXxewyQr1k&t=36s)

Dwyane Wade: "When I was 19 years old and I was at the hospital and the doctor told me that they had to take my meniscus out, and that later in life I would experience arthritis and all this stuff, you think I understood what that meant? Man, get me back on the court. I have to get to this money for my family. I don't even know what a meniscus is. I have an incredible tolerance for pain, I have a high threshold for pain. I can play through things that I know a lot of guys wouldn't play through. I played through a lot of discomfort in my career, and I figured out how to still be effective knowing I couldn't do certain things."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UvXxewyQr1k&t=36s

**2. Dwyane Wade — "the killer mentality that everyone talks about and that they wish certain people had, you're born with that." — Dwyane Wade on the nature of developing a killer mentality** [06:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UvXxewyQr1k&t=370s)

Dwyane Wade: "The killer mentality that everyone talks about and that they wish certain people had, you're born with that. You're born with that and then you have to nurture it. You can't just go find that, you can't go buy that. You're born with it and then you have to allow people to help you nurture that killer mentality because it's a fine line between having a killer mentality and being on the other side of killer mentality. You have to get the right stuff out of the killer mentality. You want to get the positives out, don't bring too many of the negatives."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UvXxewyQr1k&t=370s

**3. Dwyane Wade — "it's a no-brainer, it's 2006. I love all my championships, but that was my favorite." — Dwyane Wade on why the 2006 championship is his favorite** [09:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UvXxewyQr1k&t=558s)

Dwyane Wade: "I definitely have a favorite championship and it's a no-brainer, it's 2006. I love all my championships, but that was my favorite. It was just because of being a young kid at 24 years old. Everything just happened so fast in my life. 17 years old when I was like, all right, I'm putting everything into basketball and then 24 years old, winning a championship in the NBA. And being able to be in the position where I was the leading man to accomplish that. My other championships, I wasn't the leading guy. I just had to be the supportive guy. That was my role, played it to a T. But that one I had to lead, played that to a T. And so I was proud of myself."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UvXxewyQr1k&t=558s

**4. Dwyane Wade — "I think my career in this generation of conversation has been one of the most talked about." — Dwyane Wade on the recognition and legacy of his career** [12:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UvXxewyQr1k&t=761s)

Dwyane Wade: "I think my career in this generation of conversation has been one of the most talked about careers from the standpoint of comparison. I've been compared probably more than anybody from a career standpoint. And so I feel like here's a pro in that, is the fact that your career is continued to be talked about. A lot of people have had better careers than me and amazing careers that their career is not even talked about. But here's the other side of it, here's the con to it. I'm alive, I hear y'all. I'm here, bro. I see you talking about me. And so please don't be surprised when I pop my shit back."
