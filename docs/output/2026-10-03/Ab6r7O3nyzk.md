**2. Grant Liffmann — "I could make an argument right now that OKC should be favored above San Antonio too." — OKC and San Antonio are the top contenders in the West** [01:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ab6r7O3nyzk&t=86s)

**Grant Liffmann:** "I could make an argument right now that OKC should be favored above San Antonio too. I'm not saying it's necessarily true of 1A, 1B. I think just **Victor Wembanyama**'s growth, if he gets any better, he's the best player in the world, so of course you want to hand it to San Antonio at that point. But OKC, I would have to search for a weakness. And if anything I can think of, is their wing position depth is a little weak. That's it."

**Chris Mannix:** "Can Oklahoma City beat the Spurs when **Victor Wembanyama** does to **Chet Holmgren** what he did last series, last playoffs?"

**Grant Liffmann:** "They need more. I could not agree more. That is a massive, massive storyline. But I cannot stress enough, a healthy **Jalen Williams** can counteract the offense that Chet wasn't bringing. Chet was the number two scorer pretty much, or supposed to be, their second option when Jalen was hurt and Chet wasn't scoring against Victor. So if Victor is eliminating Chet, which is horrible for OKC, that is a huge issue. But I cannot stress enough, a healthy **Jalen Williams** playing at top level, you're talking about an All-Star."
