Draymond Green: "It was reported that I did not receive a no-trade clause. No, I didn't. I think it was something that we could have fought over, but it wasn't worth fighting over. **5. Draymond Green — "When the best player on your team takes a discount, it squeezes everyone else." — Draymond Green addresses resurfaced comments about Jalen Brunson's contract** [08:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AZxByHihEe4&t=524s)

Draymond Green: "Someone, maybe the people behind Club 520, posted an old video of me discussing **Jalen Brunson** taking a pay cut. **Josh Hart** made a comment and he said, 'Let's normalize not talking about or not counting somebody else's pockets.' I put on threads, 'Let's normalize speaking up when things are said, not when you feel like you up.' The reason I said that is because that comment was made two years ago in 2024. To respond to it two years later, I think was a little weird. I stand by what I said. When the best player on your team takes a discount, it squeezes everyone else. I said **Josh Hart** coming from a place where I come from, meaning being on a team where you are very important to that team's success. I don't think me and **Josh Hart** played the same role. I don't think we played close to the same role. I don't think we were the same in as far as our teams go. But that's not the point. The point is **Josh Hart** is not **Jalen Brunson**, just like I'm not Stephen Curry."
