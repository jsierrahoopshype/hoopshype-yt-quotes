**7. Stephen Curry — "It helped me off the dribble because it's kind of unorthodox, but it is a one piece shot." — Stephen Curry on his unorthodox off-the-dribble shot** [07:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bgSixq0dOKs&t=437s)

Stephen Curry: "It helped me off the dribble because it's kind of unorthodox, but it is a one piece shot that no matter if you're really all the way on balance or just off, or if you got it all in the right hand, left hand off the dribble, whatever it is, I kind of can get it here quick enough that I still have all of my power.
