**3. Jaylen Clark — "Having a routine is very beneficial to being successful." — Jaylen Clark explains the importance of routine for NBA players** [6:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6oiWGD1qZp4&t=405s)

Jaylen Clark: "In high school it's different. You go ahead, you pull up to the game, you throw your shoes on, you got the little layup lines. Here it's like a whole procedure from weight room to being on the court to the time you eat. And as you guys will learn, especially young people learning watching this, having a routine is very beneficial to being successful. Doing stuff at different times, your body's going to react different. I eat the same meal before every game day, every time I'm having the chicken teriyaki with white rice. I try to keep everything the exact same, so when it comes time to play and perform, I can somewhat expect the same results."
