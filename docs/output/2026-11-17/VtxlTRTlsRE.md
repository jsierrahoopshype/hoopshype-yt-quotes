**4. Steve Nash — "Footwork, first and foremost, because it gets you on balance and allows you to get lift." — Steve Nash on the importance of footwork for shooting** [07:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VtxlTRTlsRE&t=428s)

Steve Nash: "Footwork, first and foremost, because it gets you on balance and allows you to get lift. Lift, you got to create force, you got to create power to get the ball up in the air to allow the hand to be soft. Rhythm, that force then creates rhythm. One thing that I think is important is your release point. Some guys have been good at this, I think **Jaylen Brown** actually sometimes hangs or shoots almost on the way down on his middies. But I think the rule of thumb is you want to shoot as or before you get to the top, because then you're using your legs and you have the same, and it's repeatable."

**5. LeBron James — "My shooting journey is literally like watching Michael Jordan." — LeBron James on learning to shoot by watching Michael Jordan** [09:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VtxlTRTlsRE&t=542s)

LeBron James: "My shooting journey is literally like watching **Michael Jordan**. But the thing I didn't realize is how balanced **Michael Jordan** was. I saw a fadeaway. So the flick of the wrist, the head back, I saw a fadeaway. So when I started to see that I had some athletic genes, I maybe picked up some bad habits in my shooting because I can emulate that. So I was shooting fadeaways as like a freshman in high school, and I was making them, but I didn't realize that I was picking up bad habits along that time because I didn't study the fact that, okay, yeah, MJ is doing that, but you're not even seeing how on balance he is."

**9. LeBron James — "When the ball is coming towards me, I'm almost fixing it. Well, not almost, I am fixing the laces before I even get to my shot." — LeBron James on fixing his shot mechanics** [20:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VtxlTRTlsRE&t=1208s)

LeBron James: "When the ball is coming towards me, I'm almost fixing it. Well, not almost, I am fixing the laces before I even get to my shot, you know, or even off the dribble, I'm trying to fix the laces. And I didn't realize that a lot of players are like that as well. So like any team I go to or play with or guys, like if I'm new to the team, I always ask them like, how do you, how do you like to shoot the ball? And they be like, I like, you like, what do you mean? I like, do you like laces or no laces? Do you prefer? Because when I deliver the ball, I want to be able to deliver the ball right to you, so all you have to do is, and it's not going to be like perfect every time, but sometimes you got to like balance it, you got to spin it, but like I want to know how you shoot the ball. So when I deliver it to you, either if you, if you like the laces, the ball is coming to you like this, so it's already in the pocket, or if you don't, then I know to put a little spin on it too."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VtxlTRTlsRE&t=1208s
