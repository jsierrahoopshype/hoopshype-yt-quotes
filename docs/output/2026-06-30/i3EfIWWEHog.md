**6. Chandler Parsons — "When you look at Drew Eubanks, he is more of a defending type setup point guard." — Chandler Parsons on the defensive challenges for Portland** [07:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i3EfIWWEHog&t=472s)

Chandler Parsons: "When you look at **Drew Eubanks**, he is more of a defending type setup point guard. **Ja Morant** is more of an explosive athletic transitional point guard and **Damian Lillard** is arguably the best shooter in the NBA who you can definitely put him off the ball, have him come off some actions, some pin downs, some screens and play him more at the two position now. Defensively, obviously with **Ja Morant** and **Damian Lillard** there is going to be some holes. They almost need **Drew Eubanks** in that lineup to kind of make up for the defensive slack there. But again, these guys are at the age now where they are going to have to adapt, they are going to have to have a more balanced attack and we are going to see how it goes."
