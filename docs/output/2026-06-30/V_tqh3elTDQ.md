# LeBron and Steph on Teaming Up for Team USA’s 2024 Gold Medal Run — *Mind the Game*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_tqh3elTDQ

_Speakers identified: LeBron James, Stephen Curry, Steve Nash_

**1. LeBron James — "the difference between obviously the biggest difference like on the court is that your usage rate is not chopped in half" — LeBron James and Stephen Curry on the challenges of short-term team chemistry** [01:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_tqh3elTDQ&t=78s)

**Steve Nash:** "It's got to be tricky though, you guys bringing the best players in the league, come together in a short period of time. You don't even know sometimes who's getting on the plane with injuries. You guys have to find a way that the room feels right."

**LeBron James:** "The biggest difference on the court is that your usage rate is not chopped in half, it's chopped like three-fourths. We all have the ability to, if we have a bad first quarter or a bad first half, we can figure it out in the second half. The ball is going to find us, the ball is going to be in our hands."

**Stephen Curry:** "You don't always have to be in a rhythm because you look to your left and you're like, okay, he got it tonight. So what can I do that can still impact winning? That was our mindset."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_tqh3elTDQ&t=78s

**2. Stephen Curry — "I felt the panic in the third quarter when old buddy hit the and-one three" — Stephen Curry on the pressure of the Team USA semifinal game** [03:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_tqh3elTDQ&t=220s)

Stephen Curry: "I felt the panic in the third quarter when old buddy hit the and-one three. We were down 15. I'm counting the minutes because to your point, how fast the games were. You felt how fast the games were and you're like, damn, I'm down 15 in a one-game tournament. When it got under eight, under seven in the fourth, and I'm looking, I'm like, if we don't, we're down double digits to that point."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_tqh3elTDQ&t=220s

**3. Stephen Curry — "Everything was a little tough, they were playing out of their mind" — Stephen Curry on his shooting performance against Serbia** [04:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_tqh3elTDQ&t=288s)

Stephen Curry: "First quarter against Serbia in the semifinals, everything was a little tough, they were playing out of their mind. I found a couple openings. I missed my first three, we got an offensive rebound and kicked it back to the right wing and I made the second, the two-foot dollar. At that point, you start to feel the energy, maybe if I get a couple good looks I'm starting to feel it. They just came, pin downs were on time, transition, finding me in the corner, everything just kept us in it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_tqh3elTDQ&t=288s

**4. Stephen Curry — "When he threw that pass, muscle memory just took over" — Stephen Curry on the final possession against Serbia** [06:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_tqh3elTDQ&t=376s)

Stephen Curry: "There was no thought to it. I remember the conversation we had. They had just cut it to three when we hit the putback. I'm like, you get the ball, I'm coming to set a screen for you. I actually wanted him to go downhill, maybe get to the free-throw line, at least settle us down. When he threw that pass, muscle memory just took over. I couldn't even tell you what I was thinking. I saw Yabusele bite on it on the left jab, and once you see that, it's just reactionary and you make it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_tqh3elTDQ&t=376s
