**6. Stephen A. Smith, on player legacy, team dynamics**
[09:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9u5qx_I6zZo&t=573s)

**8. Stephen A. Smith, on player legacy, team dynamics**
[02:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9u5qx_I6zZo&t=128s)

"As I've said a gazillion times by now, I never went to watch Bronny play in high school because I didn't want to judge him. _Why it matters:_ Stephen A. Smith explains his deliberate decision to avoid watching or commenting on Bronny James during his high school and early college career, citing his influence and a desire to be fair to the young player.
