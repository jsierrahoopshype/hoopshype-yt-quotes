**6. Vince Carter — "He was a younger guy than me and the player said to me many, many years ago, Kevin Love." — Kevin Love's poster dunk on Vince Carter in Minnesota** [05:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JJgO6QUGXek&t=332s)

Vince Carter: "There is another player that dunked on me and I was at the left elbow, this player is at the right block. They threw him the ball. I look, he takes one dribble. I'm looking, I'm trying to be athletic and try to go get the block, just he wide open, no layups. And as I get there, he kind of two-hand just. And he was a younger guy than me and the player said to me many, many years ago, **Kevin Love**, who it was. Yeah, **Kevin Love**. Minnesota. It wasn't like terrible, but for me, it was terrible for the simple fact that he did it because, and this is so random, but when he won Gatorade Player of the Year in high school, he received the award, he looked at me and he said, oh, I'm going to be up there with you one of these days. I said, cool, I'll be there. And then we fast forward, he dunked it and when he dunked, I was like, oh my god, in Minnesota, if I'm not mistaken, and I just remember running down like, oh my god."

Tracy McGrady: "Butch used to put me on the offensive players and let your ass be free in scoring. So I had to guard Sprewell, **Allan Houston**. Man, he made a move, I'm guarding, he made a move and boom, boom, went up. I thought it was **Allan Houston**. Yeah, he had bounce."
