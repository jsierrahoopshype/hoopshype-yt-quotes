**7. Mat Ishbia — "It's a collaboration. I'm extremely engaged from every detail, every question." — How the Suns organization makes big decisions** [22:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rIes-wODyU8&t=1357s)

**Brian Windhorst:** "When you come to a big decision, what is the process you go through to make a decision like that? Whether it's to make a big trade, to make a hire, to make a firing? As the head of the organization, how do you get there?"

**Mat Ishbia:** "It's a collaboration. I'm extremely engaged from every detail, every question. We've got alignment from me to the CEO **Josh Bartelstein**, to the GM **Brian Gregory**, to the coach **Mike Budenholzer**, and then down to our players as well. But the four of us sit in a room and talk and debate everything. On the playing time and rotations, coach Bud is the final decision maker. On who we draft, **Brian Gregory** is the final decision maker. Everyone has their role, but everyone's thoughts are put into it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rIes-wODyU8&t=1357s

**10. Mat Ishbia — "I'm supportive of growing the game, so I'm pro expansion." — Mat Ishbia's support for NBA expansion** [26:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rIes-wODyU8&t=1590s)

Mat Ishbia: "I'm supportive of growing the game, so I'm pro expansion. Obviously, it depends on the situation and making sure there's the right partners and the right value. But I believe that Vegas is a market that we need to fill. I think Seattle is one. I know there's been talk about Mexico City. The way I look at it is expanding the game. NBA is a global game, and having 30 is a limited number. Can you get to 32? I'm going to go that many more. Can you grow, can you cover a big market like Vegas? I don't care that it's close to Phoenix. I'm excited. I think there's abundance, there's opportunity everywhere, and I'm supportive of it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rIes-wODyU8&t=1590s
