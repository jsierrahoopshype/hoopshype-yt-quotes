**4. Golden Tate — "I went to the Titans for half the season when AJ Brown and Julio were hurt." — Golden Tate on his time with the Tennessee Titans** [01:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sk3dXYuKm1Y&t=112s)

Golden Tate: "I went to the Titans for half the season when **AJ Brown** and Julio were hurt. They kept me on practice squad for eight weeks while they were the number one seed and chose to play some young rookie guys over me. I was like, is it me maybe? I just took that as, hey God, you put me here for clearly not to play, but you put me here for closure."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sk3dXYuKm1Y&t=112s

**5. Golden Tate — "Talk to me through the emotions that you felt getting traded a day after you had just bought out." — Brice Butler and Golden Tate on the emotional toll of trades** [02:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sk3dXYuKm1Y&t=144s)

**Brice Butler:** "Talk to me through the emotions that you felt getting traded a day after you had just bought out. Because I feel like what normal fans don't understand is what all happens after you get traded, especially at this time for you. You have a family, you have a wife, you have kids, we have a life here. What are we going to do? Are we going to stay? Talk to me about the trade."

**Golden Tate:** "Look y'all, being traded is chaotic. It is chaotic. Can you imagine working your job that you've worked in my case four and a half, five years? You have your family, you have your home, you have all your stuff. You work at Enterprise, and you show up on your off day, they call you and say, hey, thank you for your work, you've been one of our top sales people, but we are going to transfer you to Philadelphia, effective immediately. Thank you so much, goodbye. Right then and there, you realize, okay, I got to go to work tomorrow or the next day, and I got to figure out how I get my family packed up out of here to Philadelphia to start work on Monday."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sk3dXYuKm1Y&t=144s
