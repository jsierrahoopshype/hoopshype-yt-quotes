## Shams Charania confirms Jaylen Brown did NOT put in a formal trade request 👀 | SportsCenter — *NBA on ESPN*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VtdpAXuB1X0

_Speakers identified: Shams Charania, Kendrick Perkins, Monica McNutt_

**1. Shams Charania — "When you dangle Jaylen Brown in trade talks with Giannis Antetokounmpo, we know they offered Jaylen Brown for Kevin Durant" — Celtics trade Jaylen Brown to 76ers for Paul George and picks** [00:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VtdpAXuB1X0&t=41s)

**Monica McNutt:** "Everyone couldn't believe their eyes yesterday seeing that **Jaylen Brown** is now a 76er. How did this trade come to be?"

**Shams Charania:** "The Celtics had made the decision a long time ago. When you dangle **Jaylen Brown** in trade talks with **Giannis Antetokounmpo**, we know they offered **Jaylen Brown** for **Kevin Durant** in 2022. And now four years later, after winning Finals MVP, after being an MVP finalist this season, they still were willing to move on from him. They missed out on Giannis. And then after that, a strange thing happened. They continued to have trade conversations. They couldn't get a premium player like a Giannis, but they pursued a package of four, five first-round draft picks. That was not attainable on the open market. But what you have though, is when you shop a player of **Jaylen Brown**'s caliber, it comes with fallout. Him understanding and finding out that he's being dangled, the disrespect that comes with that, what he feels, how it rubs him. And so there were hard conversations that took place between the Celtics and **Jaylen Brown**. There was no formal trade request by Brown, but everyone around the league understood, given how many times he's been put out there, he would have welcomed a trade at the end of it. And so the bar continued to fall as time went on, and went from four to five firsts to what we ended up seeing, which is **Mike Gange**, the new president of basketball operations in Philadelphia, stuck with it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VtdpAXuB1X0&t=41s

**2. Shams Charania — "They clearly were not ready to give Jaylen Brown a two-year $145 million supermax contract extension come July." — Celtics traded Jaylen Brown to avoid future contract extension issues** [01:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VtdpAXuB1X0&t=108s)

Shams Charania: "**Mike Gange**, the new president of basketball operations in Philadelphia, stuck with it. They didn't exit the party like some teams did, and they were there at the end with **Paul George**, a couple first-round picks, and a couple seconds, and they got this deal done because they stuck with it. And at the end of the day, the Celtics knew there was a hard conversation coming in July. They clearly were not ready to give **Jaylen Brown** a two-year $145 million supermax contract extension come July. So this ultimately makes sure that they got rid of this dilemma, this issue, before they had to face it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VtdpAXuB1X0&t=108s

**3. Kendrick Perkins — "It was one of the dumbest trades in NBA history." — Kendrick Perkins calls the Jaylen Brown trade a historic mistake** [05:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VtdpAXuB1X0&t=319s)

Kendrick Perkins: "It was one of the dumbest trades in NBA history. And **Mike Gange**, **Jamil Nelson**, **Bob Myers**, they should be serving breakfast in bed to **Brad Stevens**. I'm talking about cut up fruit, seedless grapes, mint tea, toast with strawberry jam, because they finesse the hell out of him. And if you're going to trade a guy that's a top-10 player, you at least got to get back a guy that's a top-15, top-20 player, and **Paul George** is not that. **Paul George** hasn't been consistently good since I was skinny, and that's been a long damn time."
