**1. Brian Windhorst — "Celtics fans do not understand this trade at all." — Celtics fans and analysts question the Jaylen Brown trade logic** [01:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yIXyiIgYiv4&t=77s)

**2. Brian Windhorst — "There is no reason to feel any pressure to make this move on July 1st." — Questioning the urgency of the Jaylen Brown trade** [01:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yIXyiIgYiv4&t=111s)

Brian Windhorst: "Even if they wanted to move on from **Jaylen Brown** and even if they didn't like the offers that they had today, it doesn't make sense why this trade would have to happen now. Even if you believe that **Jaylen Brown** had played his last game for you, there is no reason to feel any pressure to make this move on July 1st. I promise you that there would have been other opportunities. And not only that, to make it to a team that is in your conference, in your division, a team that just vanquished you in the playoffs, the more you look at it, the more mystifying it gets."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yIXyiIgYiv4&t=111s

**3. Brian Windhorst — "This absolutely improved the Sixers, move them up the ranks." — Impact of Jaylen Brown and Paul George on Eastern Conference** [02:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yIXyiIgYiv4&t=169s)

Brian Windhorst: "This is definitely an upgrade for them because **Jaylen Brown** has been very healthy. **Paul George** has been not healthy there and of course this last year he got suspended. You are getting a player who can defend the perimeter. If you play the Celtics in the playoffs again, **Jaylen Brown** is going to be your defender on **Jason Tatum**. One of the big things you have to solve in the Eastern Conference is how do you guard these superior wings like **Donovan Mitchell**, like Jaylen Brunson, like **Jason Tatum**. They traded for that player. Plus your guards are ascending, **BJ Boston** and **Tyrese Maxey** are ascending. They are a power backcourt. This absolutely improved the Sixers, move them up the ranks."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yIXyiIgYiv4&t=169s
