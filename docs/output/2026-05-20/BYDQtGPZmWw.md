# The Inside Guys react to EPIC Cavs-Knicks ECF Game 1 👏 | Inside The NBA — *NBA on ESPN*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BYDQtGPZmWw

_Speakers identified: Ernie Johnson, Shaquille O'Neal, Kenny Smith, Charles Barkley, Jalen Brunson, Kenny Atkinson, Mike Brown_

**1. Jalen Brunson — Jalen Brunson explains the Knicks' improbable 22-point comeback win — "I think the common denominator was just us still believing in each other and still playing, still fighting."** [04:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BYDQtGPZmWw&t=272s)

"I still don't, but I think the common denominator was just us still believing in each other and still playing, still fighting. Just chipping away. We knew we weren't going to get it all back in one possession, so we got some stops, kept running, got a couple lucky shots to go in, but we just kept fighting."

**2. Jalen Brunson — Jalen Brunson discusses if rest or rust affected the Knicks' Game 1 performance — "I think you could say our rest and that we were still resting in the first three quarters."** [06:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BYDQtGPZmWw&t=391s)

"I noticed a lot. I think you could say our rest and that we were still resting in the first three quarters, whatever. But I mean, obviously, we got to come out better in the way we were playing in that middle second, third quarter. That wasn't us. So we got to find a way to be better than what we did, but I mean, the rest helped us definitely in the fourth and overtime."

**3. Charles Barkley — Charles Barkley criticizes the Cavaliers' stagnant offense and coaching decisions in Game 1 — "They were just going one-on-one. The shot clock was on their back every single time."** [08:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BYDQtGPZmWw&t=539s)

"They were just going one-on-one. They were the shot clock was on their back every single time. **Kenny** should have called timeout sooner. I give **Brunson** a lot of credit, he was fantastic and the other guys chipped in, but that was a damn gag job right there."

**4. Kenny Atkinson — Cavaliers coach Kenny Atkinson reacts to Knicks' tough shots and Jalen Brunson's late-game heroics — "Brunson obviously took over at the end and we started double-teaming him and try to do some different things."** [09:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BYDQtGPZmWw&t=564s)

"I thought they hit some really tough shots in that fourth quarter. Those two threes, prayer threes, end of the shot clock. We got a little unlucky, quite honestly. **Brunson** obviously took over at the end and we started double-teaming him and try to do some different things, but I'm super proud of the way our group played. We played great basketball tonight for three quarters. Unfortunately, fourth quarter, they dominated us in the fourth quarter."

**5. Kenny Smith — Kenny Smith shares his experience with losing big leads and analyzes the Cavaliers' offensive stagnation — "It only takes it out of you if you don't feel you got up the correct way."** [10:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BYDQtGPZmWw&t=623s)

"When you're in the Western Conference Finals, I wouldn't say it takes more out of you. But you realize that when you're up 22 in their gym. I can only talk from experience. The experience, the guy sitting next to me, we had two games where we were up 20 and they beat us both times, the first two games of the series. We came back and actually won the series, but the difference, and when you say it takes it out of you, it only takes it out of you if you don't feel you got up the correct way. We felt we had played the correct way and it just, we just couldn't sustain it. We just had to figure out a way to sustain it. But I look at Cleveland and Charles talked about the ball pinging, but the offense didn't initiate with **Donovan Mitchell** in the last eight minutes of the game. It initiated with **James Harden** and or **Merrill** and or anyone else. And so now there are, you don't have to double-team off the start of the play. So that's why the ball didn't move. So when he was initiating the play, they were double-teaming him off the pick and rolls, which makes the ball move."

**6. Shaquille O'Neal — Shaquille O'Neal advises the Cavaliers not to get discouraged after losing a big lead on the road — "You don't really get down on yourself when you're a good team. You're up 22, fans went crazy in New York, we let one get away."** [11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BYDQtGPZmWw&t=706s)

"You don't really get down on yourself when you're a good team. You're up 22, fans went crazy in New York, we let one get away, but the goal is to at least get one on the road. So they still have another chance to do that in Game 2."

**7. Charles Barkley — Charles Barkley argues the Cavaliers' Game 1 defeat was a 'bad loss' that will energize the Knicks — "All losses are not created the same. That was a bad loss."** [12:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BYDQtGPZmWw&t=722s)

"I disagree with both of them. This was a bad loss. Because now the **Knicks** feel like they stole one at home. They like, wait a minute guys, they should have won that game. We got away with one and now they're going to come out like, they hadn't played basketball in all those days. You know they're going to come out. That's all **Coach Brown** going to talk about the next 48 hours. If you were Cleveland, you feel deflated. Cleveland's got to be deflated. They can say anything they want to, they can put on the smooth front, but let me tell you, that next game is huge. All losses are not created the same. That was a bad loss. You're up 20 and that team hadn't played in a minute and you blow it like that? And now they still got to stay in New York and listen, **Knicks**, this going to energize the **Knicks** in my opinion. They're going to be like, man, we can't come out like that again. We hadn't played and you know they're going to be ready in Game 2. So I do, I think that was a really bad loss."

**8. Mike Brown — Mike Brown praises the Knicks' resilience and ability to secure a historic comeback victory — "I take my hat off to my group. Cleveland, they were shooting the ball well, they were turning us over."** [13:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BYDQtGPZmWw&t=826s)

"I got to give my group credit. They've been resilient all year and I don't know if I've seen that in a playoff game. I don't know if I've been a part of it, maybe I have, but to be down 18, 19, 20, whatever we were down and to find a way to come back and win. I mean, I, I take my hat off to my group. **Cleveland**, they were shooting the ball well, they were turning us over, they were getting the ball in the pocket and taking advantage of us. And we just found a way. We found a group of five guys that went out there, ended up getting stops and scoring the basketball."

**9. Shaquille O'Neal — Shaquille O'Neal and Charles Barkley discuss the Knicks' collective effort and the Cavaliers' offensive stagnation — "Once Brunson started going and the crowd started getting into the game, the others stepped up and played."** [14:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BYDQtGPZmWw&t=880s)

**Shaquille O'Neal:** "Once **Brunson** started going and the crowd started getting into the game, the others stepped up and played. They just played well. Credit to **Mike Brown**, he's a great coach. I've always said if the coach doesn't panic, the players don't panic. I was watching him the whole time. Never had a look of worry in his face. Again, **Brunson** just stepped up and took over, the others played well. Everybody was into the game. So, **Mikal Bridges**, **Larry Johnson**, **Patrick Ewing**, **Al Horford**, the **Knicks**, Madison Square Garden was on fire tonight."

**Charles Barkley:** "Yeah, but Ernie said something earlier, it was dead in there. It was dead. It was 100% dead. And then **Brunson**, and then once, once he let **Brunson** score like 12 points, then those other guys just had to make a couple of shots and it was over."

**10. Kenny Smith — Kenny Smith criticizes the Cavaliers' defense for allowing Mikal Bridges and Shamet to dominate — "You cannot let Mikal Bridges and Shamet win that battle."** [15:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BYDQtGPZmWw&t=937s)

"As much as he was targeting him, those were very difficult shots. Regardless if he was targeting him, those were difficult shots. But I also thought that if I'm looking at **Strauss** and **Merrill**, along with **Donovan**, I would have to tell them, I'm like, you cannot let **Mikal Bridges** and **Shamet** win that battle. And that's what happened. As much as they made shots, they defended at such a high level. **Shamet** did a great job of keeping **Donovan** away from the ball in the last eight minutes and **Mikal Bridges** just shut down the wings. So we can't have that happen. If we're going to be a championship team, two guys, one that comes off the bench cannot shut us down in our offensive lane. And that would be, that would be my talk."

**11. Charles Barkley — Charles Barkley reiterates the Cavaliers' offensive stagnation led to a hard and deflating loss — "They were so stagnant. They were out there with that prevent offense."** [16:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BYDQtGPZmWw&t=1011s)

"Everybody's going to complain about different things, but they lost, in my opinion, they lost this game offensively. They were so stagnant. They were out there with that prevent offense. They had no, I'm pretty sure they had no assists the last five or six minutes. They were playing, it's like when you're watching a football game when they be like, we're just going to milk the clock. Every possession started with five seconds to go. You don't even have to play defense then because the shot clock is your real defender, but this game, man, they, that's a hard loss right there."
