**1. Stephen A. Smith — Jalen Brunson's offensive explosion against James Harden — "Jalen Brunson goes ballistic and literally should be arrested for the assault that he put on James Harden."** [00:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KW-rpjD6IpA&t=38s)

"Relieved **Nicole**, absolutely relieved. I mean, they looked absolutely awful for more than three quarters of the game, and then after that, it was seven minutes left, they were down 22, and then all of a sudden **Jalen Brunson** goes ballistic and literally should be arrested for the assault that he put on **James Harden**. Just abused him at every single turn, annihilated him, kept going by him, scored like five or six straight baskets, and then it got to a point where seemingly anybody, whether it was **Mikal Bridges**, whether it was Brunson, whether it was **Landry Shamet**, anybody who attempted a shot, it was **James Harden** in the vicinity. It ain't just **Jalen Brunson** that should be arrested for assault, it's the rest of the Knicks because everybody at that point was targeting **James Harden** because he couldn't stop a cold. It was just that simple. But up before then, up before the seven-minute mark, when they were down by 22, they looked beyond rusty, they looked lethargic like they had been chilling for the last nine days, eating pizza and Lord knows what else. They did not look like the New York Knicks we had seen when seven straight playoff games, but thank goodness it didn't last the entire game."

**2. Stephen A. Smith — Cavaliers' Game 1 collapse was a demoralizing choke job — "It was unquestionably a choke job, make no mistake about it. It was a choke job."** [02:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KW-rpjD6IpA&t=131s)

"It was unquestionably a choke job, make no mistake about it. It was a choke job. You can't collapse like that. Where was **Donovan Mitchell** in the last seven minutes to stop the bleeding? He was nowhere to be found. **James Harden** was getting abused by every Knick in sight. You end up losing a 22-point lead, you end up spending the last seven minutes of regulation and overtime getting outscored 44 to 11. That is a demoralizing situation to be in, especially when you came to New York just to get one. In all likelihood, I can't imagine them getting Game 2. They're going to be in an 0-2 hole and it's going to be virtually impossible to imagine them doing what they did against **Detroit**, winning four of the last five games to win this series and get to the NBA Finals. I'd be shocked if a team this demoralized, clearly and visibly, is going to come back from a defeat of this nature. It's not just a loss, it's how you lose like that. This is a devastating loss."

**3. Kendrick Perkins — James Harden's defensive liability and Kenny Atkinson's coaching decisions — "James Harden was a defensive liability once again. The Knicks were scoring on him at will."** [05:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KW-rpjD6IpA&t=342s)

"But let me say this, it's two sides of the basketball, right? I'm not disagreeing with you, they were awful offensively, but you're up double digits in the fourth quarter, all you have to do is get stops. And **James Harden** was a defensive liability once again. The Knicks were scoring on him at will. The entire Knicks squad was nine for 15 in the fourth quarter and overtime when **James Harden** was the defender. **Jalen Brunson** was seven for eight on **James Harden**. At some point, if you're **Kenny Atkinson**, you have to see this and you have to make a business decision and get him off the damn floor. Here we are again. Different series, same story. **James Harden**, more turnovers than field goals made."
