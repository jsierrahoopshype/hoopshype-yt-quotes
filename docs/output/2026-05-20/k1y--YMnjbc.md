# Tim Legler & Kendrick Perkins react to Cavs-Knicks ECF Game 1 OT thriller 🔥 | SC with SVP — *NBA on ESPN*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k1y--YMnjbc

_Speakers identified: Donovan Mitchell, James Harden, Scott Van Pelt, Kendrick Perkins, Jalen Brunson, Tim Legler, Mike Brown_

**1. Jalen Brunson — Jalen Brunson on the Knicks' mentality during their comeback — "Keep fighting, keep chipping away, you know, we're not going to get it back in one possession."** [04:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k1y--YMnjbc&t=283s)

"Yeah, just keep fighting, keep chipping away, you know, we're not going to get it back in one one possession. Um, most importantly, sticking together. You know, no matter how that game finished, habits translate. And so they translate to the next game. So just finishing the game strong, regardless of whatever is whatever is going on, making sure we're having the right habits. So we'll be going to the next game, you know, we're we're we're doing what we were just were doing. You know, we're not just giving up, you know, we don't want to give up ever. And so, um, just having faith in each other."

**2. Kendrick Perkins — Kendrick Perkins praises Jalen Brunson's leadership during the Knicks' comeback — "Jalen Brunson said something real special right there. He said, 'We just said we got to continue to stick together.'"** [05:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k1y--YMnjbc&t=339s)

"Absolutely, you build from it. And you know, **Jalen Brunson** said something real special right there. He said, 'We just said we got to continue to stick together.' Right? And I've been in a situation like this in the 2008 Finals, right? When we were on the road, we were down double digits, **Scott**, and we had to go get that game. And we got it was a possession by possession game. And you could see and feel the energy, the spirit. I'm a big, I'm a huge body language guy. I'm always looking at body language. And at no point throughout the course of that game, did I feel like the Knicks felt like they were going to lose or that they were out of it. I didn't see any complaining. I didn't see any, you know, dropping of the head or pointing the finger. No, they just kept chipping away, making runs."

**3. Tim Legler — Jalen Brunson's superstar performance unlocks Knicks offense in Game 1 — "This was a superstar putting a team on his back, refusing to accept they were going to lose."** [07:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k1y--YMnjbc&t=434s)

"Scott, we've made so much about the Knicks offense and changing it up and putting **Karl-Anthony Towns** more at the top and facilitating, and a lot of that is true during this run they've had over the last seven games. But when push came to shove and Game 1 was on the line, they went back to **Jalen Brunson** middle of the floor, hunt who you want, and just go to work. The Cavs at that point with a big lead, started out saying, we're going to let him operate one-on-one, even if it is against **James Harden**, the matchup he would like, because we're not going to allow three-point shots because that's how you really make that type of a comeback. Problem was Brunson made like five shots in a row and now you get it to a workable margin with enough time, the decision to double team then comes, it opens three-point shooters, it opens drives for **OG Anunoby** out of the double team. So really, it all comes down to **Jalen Brunson** unlocking the Knicks offense and doing it really single-handedly and then just reading it from that point on. This was a superstar putting a team on his back, refusing to accept that they were going to lose this game."

**4. Tim Legler — Cleveland's puzzling lack of offensive initiation for Donovan Mitchell — "That's your star. I know you've got James Harden as well."** [08:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k1y--YMnjbc&t=498s)

**Scott Van Pelt:** "I'm with that, but on the other side, you have a superstar in **Donovan Mitchell** and it felt like **Cleveland** didn't look to him. I don't get that. What do you make of that seeming absence from Mitchell and more than anything, it was that he wasn't the guy that had the ball in his hands a lot?"

**Tim Legler:** "I think he got his ankle rolled up on late. I don't know if that was a factor. He was still on the court, but it was bizarre to me, if he's on the floor, that you're not initiating with him. He had an efficient night the entire night. And he's coming off of a Game 7 in which he played incredibly well. So that's where you go. That's your star. I know you've got **James Harden** as well, but **James Harden** did not have a night where he was scoring the basketball. He was turning it over a little bit. He can still break you down. But when you see that game start to get away from you, to me, it's got to be in the hands of **Donovan Mitchell** and let him go decide how this game is going to end offensively for the **Cavs**. They just didn't get there."

**5. Tim Legler — Cleveland's shooters looked tight, Knicks' aggressive mindset prevailed — "three-point shooters across the board for the Cavs look tight on some of those looks"** [08:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k1y--YMnjbc&t=498s)

"I did see some three-point shooters across the board for the **Cavs** look tight on some of those looks that they had that really could have stopped that **Knicks** run and stalled some of that momentum. They didn't look comfortable. Seemed like they wanted the clock to run out once they had that big lead and the aggressive mindset shifted to the **Knicks**. They smelled it, they went after it, they got it."

**6. Mike Brown — Mike Brown admits the Knicks intentionally targeted James Harden defensively — "There is no secret, we were attacking Harden."** [10:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k1y--YMnjbc&t=615s)

"Yeah, you know, it's sometimes you got to do what the game dictates and they were trying to do the same thing with **Jalen** and so we said, okay, you know, we feel like we could play that game. We try not to play that game much, but we feel like we have a guy that we can play that game with in **Jalen**. And so, you know, just like we have to try to figure out different ways to guard **Harden** and and **Mitchell**, they got to figure out different ways to guard **Jalen**, but we were there is no secret, we were attacking **Harden**."
