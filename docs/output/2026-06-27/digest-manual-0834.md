Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6wZwOsYD9rQ

_Speakers identified: Allie Clifton, Richard Jefferson_

**1. Richard Jefferson — "the last three years as his usage rate has gone up, his game plays has gone down" — Austin Reaves' usage rate and physical toll on his game** [01:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6wZwOsYD9rQ&t=83s)

**Richard Jefferson:** "The last three years as his usage rate has gone up, his game plays has gone down. The number of games that he's played has gone down the last three years."

**Allie Clifton:** "Which is such an interesting thing because every year his numbers have gone up too."

**Richard Jefferson:** "The usage has gone up, the games has gone down. Do you see how much it can wear people out? Some guys are physically gifted. Some guys work their tail off and are extremely skilled. Some guys push themselves to the limit. **Austin Reaves** is outstanding, deserves every dollar, but I think when you see the game plays, the numbers going up, I'd rather have less numbers, more games played."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6wZwOsYD9rQ&t=83s

**Allie Clifton:** "How much does that, do you factor in, for example, you could just look at last season in general, the totality of games missed for LeBron at the start of the season, so him and Luka, and then Luka goes out and what he had to shoulder, which all three of them had to do at some point throughout the course of last season?"

**Richard Jefferson:** "I 100% agree. I think that that has had an impact on them. But that also shows there's a difference between a monster for 82 games and a guy that might has to do it for 35 because he's got teammates around him. I love Austin. I think he's worth every single dollar. I think the Lakers did an outstanding deal by him, but you also have to find a way, if I see a player and I sign him to the biggest deal for a player in his position, but the last three years his games have gone down, that's something that internally we want to try and address."

**4. Richard Jefferson — "the Lakers deserve a guy who's also going to help them try and win a chip" — Lakers need for defensive support around Luka Doncic** [04:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6wZwOsYD9rQ&t=290s)

**Richard Jefferson:** "That's a wonderful contract, but it's also a very movable contract if it's not working. **Austin Reaves** deserves his money, but if we talk about the Lakers, the Lakers deserve a guy who's also going to help them try and win a chip when you have **Luka Doncic**. I was bought in on the young guys stuff. When I got there, he was like, yeah, yeah, it's time to get you out of there. This is a lot of dysfunction. I took the money. You got to take the money."
