**3. Cynthia Cooper — "every single year was different. I will say year three was difficult for a lot of different reasons" — Cynthia Cooper on the emotional difficulty of the 1999 season following Kim Perrot's passing** [03:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c0AEUoSaUfk&t=222s)

Cynthia Cooper: "Every single year was different. I will say year three was difficult for a lot of different reasons. That was the year **Kim Perrot** had passed two days before the playoffs started. And I will say every team that we played that season brought their A-game. So it's really difficult to watch a team on film, you do all of your work with the film and the highlight and show all of the different plays and how the tendencies of different players, and then when you actually get out on the court, their game has risen to another level because they're playing against you."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c0AEUoSaUfk&t=222s

**4. Cynthia Cooper — "I decided to turn a negative into a positive and to pay tribute to two people who were very special in my life" — Cynthia Cooper on finding the strength to play after losing her mother and Kim Perrot** [04:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c0AEUoSaUfk&t=290s)

Cynthia Cooper: "For me, in my mind, I'm like, you know what? My mom had passed away in February of that year. So February my mom passed away, Kim passed away in August. So you know, I was really reeling. My mind was in another place, and I decided to turn a negative into a positive and to pay tribute to two people who were very special in my life. And of course, our floor general **Kim Perrot** was front and center. And the only way to do that is to be able to stand on that stage, hold that jersey up, and pay tribute to all of the sacrifices that she had made, not just for the Houston Comets, but for the city of Houston."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c0AEUoSaUfk&t=290s

**5. Cynthia Cooper — "when you're able to separate things and put things in their proper place and deal with them as they come" — Cynthia Cooper on compartmentalizing grief to lead the Comets to a championship** [06:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c0AEUoSaUfk&t=400s)

Cynthia Cooper: "When you're able to separate things and put things in their proper place and deal with them as they come, don't get ahead of yourself and don't get overwhelmed by trying to deal with everything all at once, that was kind of my superpower. I didn't get overwhelmed with dealing with everything at once. When it was time to deal with this, I dealt with it. I remember at the end of the season, I was supposed to go to USA Basketball and I was like, no way. I know I'm at my limit. I know I can't do any more. I have to go and grieve. I've got to take 10 days, two weeks, two months to grieve because it took everything in me, and I know everything in my teammates, for us to pull together and win that championship."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c0AEUoSaUfk&t=400s
