## LeBron on Battling Cooper Flagg at Team USA's Olympic Training Camp — *Mind the Game*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ff1H2mgn4aE

_Speakers identified: LeBron James, Steve Nash_

**1. LeBron James — "He played extremely well, man. I've been watching Cooper for a few years." — LeBron James praises Cooper Flagg's competitive nature at training camp** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ff1H2mgn4aE&t=0s)

**Steve Nash:** "Did you play with Cooper in the summer? Were you in those practices with USA Basketball?"

**LeBron James:** "Yeah, he was on the development team, on the select team we call them. Yeah, and he played extremely well, man. I've been watching Cooper for a few years because my son played AAU basketball on the Nike EYBL circuit. Bronny, so we would be in tournaments either in Peach Jam in Georgia or Kansas City or Dallas or Indianapolis and Cooper, unlike a lot of kids, he played for his team out of Maine. A lot of these kids are going to be recruited, but him and his brother and a bunch of those guys they grew up with played on, I think they were called Maine Select or something like that, but he made them competitive versus teams that were stacking teams. I thought that was super cool because that was how I was. I played for the Northeast Ohio Shooting Stars. Me and my guys grew up playing together and having to play all the best teams in the country. I just thought that was super cool, but he's a big-time talent."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ff1H2mgn4aE&t=0s

**2. LeBron James — "It just seems like he makes winning plays. He just makes winning plays." — LeBron James evaluates Cooper Flagg's winning mentality and development** [01:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ff1H2mgn4aE&t=64s)

**Steve Nash:** "Says something about his mentality. What did you notice practicing with him? What did you notice about his mentality?"

**LeBron James:** "It just seems like he makes winning plays. He just makes winning plays. Even at his first year at Duke, he just made winning plays. Obviously, the fact that he was the freshman and player of the year this year as well, ACC player of the year, I just thought he played the game the right way. How you would hope that a player of his caliber just comes to work every day and is trying to get better, and I just think he's been getting better and better."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ff1H2mgn4aE&t=64s

**3. LeBron James — "I love to hear that his mentality is I'm happy to do whatever the team needs." — LeBron James on Cooper Flagg's potential as a primary playmaker** [01:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ff1H2mgn4aE&t=105s)

**Steve Nash:** "I'd say the one thing for such a prodigious talent that we want to wait and see what happens with is how good is he with the ball? Like he obviously is capable with the ball, we've seen him do a lot of things with the ball, but is he that primary guy? Will he be? But I'd love to hear that like, while I think he's going to get there, I'd love to hear that his mentality is I'm happy to do whatever the team needs."

**LeBron James:** "Yeah, and he has the ability to do it at this point. Especially when you have the guys as they have, he doesn't have to put too much more pressure on himself. He can put the pressure on himself to get better every single day, but as far as his rookie year or his second year, he doesn't have to put the pressure of making every play. He has guys out there that can help him with that. So, I think he could possibly be in a position where he could build into who he's going to be at his pace and not with the league expecting the number one player picked to be scoring 25 a night."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ff1H2mgn4aE&t=105s

**4. LeBron James — "I always kept the main thing the main thing though. I understood that at the end of the day, I had to work." — LeBron James on maintaining focus as a young player** [05:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ff1H2mgn4aE&t=322s)

LeBron James: "I always kept the main thing the main thing though. I understood that at the end of the day, I had to work. If I wanted to enjoy, then I needed to work. I stayed in the gym, I wanted to work, I wanted to get better and I also wanted to have fun and enjoy, but at the same time understanding what the main goal was. The main goal for me is the only reason I'm invited to the ESPYs is because I play the game of basketball. You make sure you keep the main thing the main thing. That's the focus. As long as he understands you keep the main thing the main thing, then nothing will sway me away from what I want my career to look like."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ff1H2mgn4aE&t=322s
