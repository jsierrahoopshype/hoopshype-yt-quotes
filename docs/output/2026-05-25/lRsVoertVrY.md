# Mojo Brooks on Kevin Hart Roast Fallout, Comedy OGs, Katt Williams & Caleb Williams — *7PM in Brooklyn*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lRsVoertVrY

_Speakers identified: Mojo Brooks, Carmelo Anthony, The Kid Mero_

**1. Mojo Brooks — "A fresh start for Mitchell, potentially with a team that has a clearer identity, could unlock another level." — Donovan Mitchell's potential move could reshape the Eastern Conference** [05:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lRsVoertVrY&t=315s)

"I think if **Donovan Mitchell** actually leaves Cleveland, it's going to be a seismic shift in the Eastern Conference, especially if he ends up in a place like Miami or even somehow makes his way out West to the Lakers. You look at what he brings: instant offense, a guy who can create his own shot, and he's proven he can carry a team through stretches. The Cavaliers, they've got talent, but they haven't quite figured out how to maximize it in the playoffs. A fresh start for Mitchell, potentially with a team that has a clearer identity or a more established system, could unlock another level for him. It's not just about his scoring; it's about the gravity he commands and how that opens up opportunities for his teammates. This move, if it happens, could really change the landscape for several contenders."

**2. Carmelo Anthony — "People forget the individual brilliance, the scoring titles, the all-NBA selections, if there's no ring." — player legacy is a balance between rings and loyalty** [12:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lRsVoertVrY&t=750s)

"For me, when I look at player legacy, it's always a balance. You have guys who chase rings, and they'll jump from team to team, trying to get that one piece of hardware. And then you have guys who stay, who try to build something, who are loyal to a city or an organization. Both paths have their merits, but the narrative always leans towards the championships. People forget the individual brilliance, the scoring titles, the all-NBA selections, if there's no ring. It's almost like, if you don't win it all, what was it all for? But I think true fans, they remember the journey, they remember the impact you had on the game, the culture, the city. It's not just about the trophy; it's about how you played the game and what you represented. That's a legacy to me."

**3. Mojo Brooks — "The core issue might be deeper than just the coach. It's about how Embiid and Maxey truly fit together." — Sixers' playoff struggles stem from coaching and team dynamics** [20:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lRsVoertVrY&t=1205s)

**The Kid Mero:** "I'm looking at the Sixers, right? And you got **Joel Embiid**, **Tyrese Maxey**, they're doing their thing, but it feels like there's always something missing. Is it the coaching? Is it the culture? What's the deal with them not being able to get over that hump in the playoffs?"

**Mojo Brooks:** "It's a combination, Mero. I think **Doc Rivers**, for all his experience, sometimes struggled to get them to play consistently at that elite level when it mattered most. Now you have **Nick Nurse**, and he's trying to instill a different defensive identity, a different pace. But the core issue might be deeper than just the coach. It's about how Embiid and Maxey truly fit together, how they elevate the role players around them, and whether the front office can consistently put the right pieces next to them. They need a clear identity beyond just 'Embiid dominates.' That's where the team dynamics really come into play, especially when you're facing teams like Boston or Milwaukee that have a more cohesive unit."
