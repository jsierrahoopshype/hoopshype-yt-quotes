# How Gregg Popovich's AURA is still leaving an imprint on the Spurs 😯 | SportsCenter — *NBA on ESPN*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z9dxNt2y3Kw

_Speakers identified: De'Aaron Fox, Host, Danny Green, Jamal Collier_

**1. De'Aaron Fox — "That was the first time he walked into the locker room and it was like, no, that's that's BS." — De'Aaron Fox on Gregg Popovich's rare locker room tongue-lashing** [00:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z9dxNt2y3Kw&t=32s)

"**Pop**'s been around throughout the course of the season. But that was the first time he walked into the locker room and it was like, no, that's that's BS. That's not how we play basketball. And obviously he has some choice words for it, but that was the first time all season that he came into the locker room right after a game and told us how he felt. Everybody felt that. **Mitch** was in there, **B-Right** was in there, all the players down to the staff. You know how it is after a game. That's the first time I ever seen that. Trainers, everybody's in there. We closed the doors. We don't let anybody know what's going on in here, but that's the first time he opened that door, we see **Pop** come in and it's like, oh, he's about to say something."

**2. Danny Green — "He's just one of those guys that you're fearful of, regardless of age or health." — Danny Green describes Gregg Popovich's enduring, fearful presence and respect.** [01:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z9dxNt2y3Kw&t=79s)

**Host:** "Okay Danny, you won a championship under **Pop**. You heard **De'Aaron Fox**. What are those **Pop** speeches like? How big of a presence does he still have on this franchise and this team?"

**Danny Green:** "A huge presence. It doesn't go away. He's just one of those guys that you're fearful of, regardless of age or health. I'm still scared of him to this day. So when he walks in a room, it's business and it's serious. So he came in, I'm sure he left an imprint on them. He let them know who's messing up, who needs to come to play. That was a big deal for them and for everybody else. Everybody respects him, from the top on down. **Timmy**, **David**, **Tony**. So when they see those guys come in and how much aura that he has, that presence, it leaves an impression."

**3. Danny Green — "I couldn't imagine how he would have been during my time." — Danny Green recalls Popovich's intense tongue-lashings after losses.** [01:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z9dxNt2y3Kw&t=79s)

"Those guys, it didn't take much, but I'm sure he let them have it and that's just **Pop** in his nicer days. I couldn't imagine how he would have been during my time. I've seen him hurting quite a bit of **Pop**, especially when he was younger and in our losses. I know we won one, but a lot of those tongue-lashings came after losses, so I know what they went through and I felt scared for those guys and I still feel scared for those guys."

**4. Danny Green — "Outside the defense, just Wemby. How he came out, how he attacked and kept his foot on the gas." — Danny Green on Victor Wembanyama's aggressive Game 4 performance** [02:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z9dxNt2y3Kw&t=152s)

**Host:** "What impressed you the most about the Spurs' performance last night because they held OKC to just 82 points?"

**Danny Green:** "The defense is obvious, but outside the defense, just **Wemby**. How he came out, how he attacked and kept his foot on the gas. They came out, they went up 15-0 to start Game 3, but this one, they didn't let the lead go slip away and it starts with him and ends with him. If he comes out and hoops the way he does, it kind of pours into those other guys defensively, offensively and how he rebounds and blocks shots. Game 3, I don't know if you remember, but he didn't score much and didn't have that same activity. He might have been fatigued, but they need that energy, they need activity from him so they can feed off it. Obviously, he needs help too, they need to make some shots, but it starts and ends with him and he has to be the MVP every night for them to get out of this series."

**5. Jamal Collier — "He said the answers that he got from the players in the room really reinforced the fact that he doesn't feel like these guys have given up on the season just yet." — Jamal Collier on Cavaliers' belief despite 0-3 deficit** [04:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z9dxNt2y3Kw&t=290s)

**Host:** "I guess Jamal, what are the Cavs saying about keeping hope alive and their season alive tonight?"

**Jamal Collier:** "Brian, it started yesterday during the Cavs' film session, where **Kenny Atkinson** went around the room to the players and asked them, why do you believe? He said the answers that he got from the players in the room really reinforced the fact that he doesn't feel like these guys have given up on the season just yet. Those answers ranged from the fact that they've had success against the Knicks in the past. They won three out of four last year against a similar group. The fact that they were up 22 points in Game 1 and **Jalen Tyson** said it, he feels like they still haven't actually put together a full 48 minutes, a complete game at their best. It's a team that still feels like they're missing a lot of shots, a lot of great looks. **Jalen Tyson** said as he looks to this game tonight, they know the Knicks are going to try to come in their building and try to sweep them and the Cavs have to have some pride and take that personally."

**6. Jamal Collier — "Kenny Atkinson said as he watched film, you could just see that they were almost a step or two behind." — Cavs' fatigue and inability to match Knicks' speed** [05:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z9dxNt2y3Kw&t=359s)

**Host:** "In their previous two series, **Jamal**, the **Cavs** went seven games. So what is the team seeing? Are they just tired? Is fatigue a factor in this series?"

**Jamal Collier:** "It's not just the two seven-game series. Dating back to April 29th, Game 5 of the first round against **Toronto**, they played every other day. **Kenny Atkinson** said as he watched film, you could just see that they were almost a step or two behind, slower than the **Knicks**. **New York**'s flying to the ball, getting out in transition, and it felt like the **Cavs** could not match that speed and intensity."

**7. Jamal Collier — "Donovan Mitchell said it, that this is the bed that we've made. We did this to ourselves." — Donovan Mitchell on Cavs' self-inflicted rest disadvantage** [05:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z9dxNt2y3Kw&t=359s)

"**Cleveland**'s trying to not use it as an excuse, but **Donovan Mitchell** said it, that this is the bed that we've made. We did this to ourselves by not closing out the previous two series earlier. You look at that **Toronto** series, they had a chance to win that Game 6 before that series went to seven. They were in Game 6 at home against **Detroit** and really had a no-show before that series went to seven. If they would have won that Game 6 in **Detroit**, **Brian**, you would have had three days off in between Games 2 and 3 and now the **Cavs** are at a huge rest disadvantage. They have been in this entire series against the **Knicks**. You can see that showing up especially in this past game."

**8. Danny Green — "It starts with Donovan Mitchell and it ends with Donovan Mitchell. He has to come out, be the aggressor." — Danny Green's strategy for Cavaliers to extend series against Knicks** [07:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z9dxNt2y3Kw&t=439s)

**Host:** "Okay Danny, in your opinion, how does Cleveland stay alive tonight in Game 4?"

**Danny Green:** "Going to be tough. It's going to take all hands on deck, but it's not impossible. They have to come out like they did in Game 7 versus Detroit. It starts with **Donovan Mitchell** and it ends with **Donovan Mitchell**. He has to come out, be the aggressor, be the floor general that we know, not only score but facilitate. Get guys like **Jared Allen** involved. Get **Evan Mobley** involved. **Sam Merrill**, him knocking down shots, **Max Strus**. They were all, they were clicking on all cylinders in that Game 7. **James Harden** didn't have to have a great game, but they're going to need him to play well also. So they're going to need all these guys to play well, even the bench guys, **Tyson**, **Tomlin**, whoever comes in, **Schroder**, gives them a plus because this New York team is very deep and it's hard to keep up or defeat them unless you have production from all areas."
