**4. Danny Green — "My first major injury was 13 years in the league." — Danny Green reflects on his career's remarkable lack of major injuries.** [02:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EHFIusYGAL4&t=123s)

"That was a different thing for me, man. Because I was fortunate enough, I had a pretty good body. I hadn't had any major injuries my whole life. My first major injury was 13 years in the league. I did four years of college. All through high school, nothing, maybe an ankle sprain. I might have had little broken bones here, like finger, wrist, or elbow when I was young. But all through college, I played every one of the games. I think I'm in the books, I hold the records for the most wins in Carolina history throughout a four-year tenure or they probably got somebody to beat it now because they got fifth-year seniors now. But most games played and games won, in that stretch, I played every, I didn't miss any games. And then even in the pros, in San Antonio, I had multiple seasons where I played 75 plus games and should have 80, 81, where **Pop** forced me, he rested me one or two of those games. So for me to play that long without any major injuries was great. Even the championship year, I think I had broken a wrist and sat out for four to six weeks, but nothing major where tendons or anything broke."
