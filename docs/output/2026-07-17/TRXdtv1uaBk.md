# A STORYBOOK ENDING 📖 What's the better situation for LeBron: Warriors or Cavs? 🤔 | Get Up — *NBA on ESPN*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TRXdtv1uaBk

_Speakers identified: LeBron James, Tim MacMahon, Brian Windhorst, Pat Riley, Giannis Antetokounmpo_

**1. Tim MacMahon — "LeBron is thinking of this not just as a retirement tour, but potentially as a multi-year situation." — LeBron James is considering a multi-year situation for his final career destination** [01:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TRXdtv1uaBk&t=83s)

Tim MacMahon: "When he was talking about picking his final destination, he first said for maybe the last few years of my career. Then he said maybe two, maybe one. But what that told me is LeBron is thinking of this not just as a retirement tour, but potentially as a multi-year situation. And why not? You look at the guy, if you ignore the gray and the beard, he does not look at all like a guy in his 40s. He is still one of the best athletes in the NBA, much less an All-Star caliber player. And if **Tom Brady** can play into his mid-40s, why not **LeBron James**? He has defied science, defied even the highest expectations his entire career. He looks like a guy who still has a lot of gas left in the tank."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TRXdtv1uaBk&t=83s

**2. Brian Windhorst — "The teams that have pitched him really don't have any information." — Teams recruiting LeBron James lack information on his decision-making factors** [02:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TRXdtv1uaBk&t=164s)

Brian Windhorst: "There is such a dearth of information from LeBron. Of his free agencies that he's done, this has been the least communicative he and his camp have been. The teams that have pitched him really don't have any information. No one was actually expecting him to announce a destination, but they were hoping him to articulate those factors he referenced. He referenced things that he's considering: is it style of play, is it potential teammates, would it be related to whether a team would be able to raise more than the minimum contract? None of that came out, and we're left to really parse the few scraps of information."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TRXdtv1uaBk&t=164s

**3. Tim MacMahon — "Wherever LeBron goes, that's going to be a premier team as far as the TV schedule goes." — Wherever LeBron James goes, that team will be a premier TV draw** [03:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TRXdtv1uaBk&t=222s)

Tim MacMahon: "Wherever LeBron goes, that's going to be a premier team as far as the TV schedule goes. So, you're making the schedule makers work a little bit harder. They've probably got to come up with several different alternatives. But I'll tell you a little secret: I don't think LeBron minds being a bit of a pain in the butt for the league office on this. I don't think he minds dragging it out and maximizing the spotlight that's on his decision."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TRXdtv1uaBk&t=222s

**4. Tim MacMahon — "If he's going to wear a uniform he's never worn before, of course the Warriors would be the most intriguing." — Cleveland is a sentimental destination, but Golden State is the most intriguing** [04:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TRXdtv1uaBk&t=263s)

Tim MacMahon: "One for Cleveland because I'm a sucker for being sentimental. The full circle, storybook type of ending, however long that last chapter might be, is appealing to me. Plus, you've got a team that's coming off a conference finals. He would be enhancing an existing core instead of coming in with a new core. Philadelphia, they've got to figure out the fit with **Jaylen Brown**. Miami's obviously got to figure out the fit with **Giannis Antetokounmpo**. So I do think the Cavaliers give him the best chance to win immediately. But look, if he's going to wear a uniform he's never worn before, of course the Warriors would be the most intriguing. Him and Steph are the two best players of this generation. We saw a little glimpse of them playing together in the Olympics. To get that for a full season or two or three, that would be obviously something that would be a heck of a deal to follow for basketball fans around the world."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TRXdtv1uaBk&t=263s

**5. Brian Windhorst — "He's celebrating the arrival of Giannis Antetokounmpo, but wanted to make sure that he made it clear." — Pat Riley is celebrating Giannis but still wants to land LeBron James** [06:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TRXdtv1uaBk&t=374s)

Brian Windhorst: "He's celebrating the arrival of **Giannis Antetokounmpo**, but wanted to make sure that he made it clear that he wants **LeBron James**. This is not mission accomplished, and this is what Riley has done throughout his tenure. **Alonzo Mourning** left, there were some hard feelings, they brought him back. **Dwyane Wade** left, there were some hard feelings, they brought him back. **Shaquille O'Neal** left, there were some hard feelings, they brought him back. LeBron left. Is there one last chapter in Miami?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TRXdtv1uaBk&t=374s

**6. Brian Windhorst — "He's won a championship in his second year all three times he changed teams." — Miami needs time to build, but LeBron James has won championships quickly before** [06:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TRXdtv1uaBk&t=416s)

Brian Windhorst: "The Heat would probably need a year to build themselves up. But LeBron in the places he's gone has used that repeatedly. He's won a championship in his second year all three times he changed teams. And if LeBron is thinking about a multi-year decision, it doesn't take the Heat out of it as much as you would think before. If he thought he was only playing one year, Cleveland home, it's a no-brainer. If he's thinking multiple years, the opportunity in Miami I think becomes a little bit more interesting."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TRXdtv1uaBk&t=416s

**7. Tim MacMahon — "It gives Pat Riley and that Heat front office a few more transaction cycles." — A multi-year deal in Miami gives Pat Riley more time to build around LeBron** [07:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TRXdtv1uaBk&t=456s)

Tim MacMahon: "It gives **Pat Riley** and that Heat front office a few more transaction cycles to put the kind of shooting and the kind of players around that core, that superstar core, that you need to compete for a championship. And like Brian said, as splashy as it was the first time he went to Miami, it took them a year. And it's also not just putting pieces together, but figuring out fit. It's not going to be just a smooth, easy, natural fit if it's LeBron and Giannis. They would need some time to figure that out, you know, with **Bam Adebayo** as well. But the first time in Miami, they lose in the finals that first year to the Mavericks, the next year they win the title. If it's a two-year deal in Miami, it looks a lot better than one and done."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TRXdtv1uaBk&t=456s
