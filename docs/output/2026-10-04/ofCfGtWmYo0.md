# “I Turned Down About $25 Million More” - John Smoltz — *Brice Butler Studios*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ofCfGtWmYo0

_Speakers identified: John Smoltz, Brice Butler_

**1. John Smoltz — "I already had 13 surgeries over 20 years, not all of them in season, a lot of post-career." — John Smoltz on the physical toll of his pitching career** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ofCfGtWmYo0&t=0s)

John Smoltz: "Back in our day, the reward system was a lot different. We were asked to do things to train for a marathon instead of a sprint, and we were asked to pitch from inning one to inning nine if you had it going. We had about a two-year period where the three of us threw about 750 innings between us. That will never happen again in the history of the game. Now, that's still a regression because guys before us threw 300 innings. There's been a regression in innings naturally for whatever reason because guys are throwing a little bit harder for sure. I already had 13 surgeries over 20 years, not all of them in season, a lot of post-career surgeries. No chance I make it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ofCfGtWmYo0&t=0s

**2. John Smoltz — "I was the first guy to miss a start in 10 years, I had Tommy John surgery." — John Smoltz on the physical toll of pitching through pain** [01:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ofCfGtWmYo0&t=96s)

**Brice Butler:** "Yeah, different time for sure."

**John Smoltz:** "I was the first guy to miss a start in 10 years, I had Tommy John surgery. That was at 34 years old when you should have Tommy John if you're going to log that many innings and pitch that many postseasons. If there's a time to have it, it's because of that, not what we have today and guys don't even log that many innings."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ofCfGtWmYo0&t=96s

**3. John Smoltz — "I alleviated the pain with dropping my arm and I used my athleticism to adapt." — John Smoltz on adapting his pitching motion to manage pain** [02:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ofCfGtWmYo0&t=124s)

**Brice Butler:** "You were supposed to get a Tommy John surgery, but then you actually like changed your pitching motion. So, to me that's crazy. How do you adapt your pitching motion? Was it still painful to throw?"

**John Smoltz:** "I alleviated the pain with dropping my arm and I used my athleticism to adapt to whatever my body was telling me. The blessing and the curse for me is I can feel every joint in my body. So that allows me to adapt and change, but also there are times where things feel like a sword cutting me. I knew I needed Tommy John for about a year and a half, and I figured I would pitch until I blew it out and I would retire. I dropped down through sidearm, through knuckleballs, whatever it took to get through."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ofCfGtWmYo0&t=124s

**4. John Smoltz — "Management said, no, no, no, no, you're going to be our closer." — John Smoltz on being forced to transition to a closer role** [06:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ofCfGtWmYo0&t=395s)

John Smoltz: "Management said, no, no, no, no, you're going to be our closer. That really ticked me off. I had 14 years of being a pretty good starter, darn good starter, and that was going to be all thrown away because of one month willing to help the team? I said, I don't even have a choice? They said, no. It's chicken and the egg. Would **Mariano Rivera** be **Mariano Rivera** if he didn't have the Yankees? If he was on a last-place team, he'd still be great, but would he be **Mariano Rivera**?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ofCfGtWmYo0&t=395s

**5. John Smoltz — "I stayed because my manager is the greatest manager in the world." — John Smoltz on loyalty to his manager and turning down money** [10:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ofCfGtWmYo0&t=632s)

**Brice Butler:** "Typically, a guy of your stature up until that point would be like, F this, I'm not doing it."

**John Smoltz:** "I stayed because my manager is the greatest manager in the world. I turned down about 25 million more to go somewhere else. I gave up a lot of money, I don't begrudge any of it, I don't think differently. He's the guy I want to play for because he was the best and he had my back."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ofCfGtWmYo0&t=632s
