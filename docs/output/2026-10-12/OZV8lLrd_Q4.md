Masai Ujiri: "There's two things that are really tough in our league: trading a player and when a player leaves. And if I have the ability or I have, in my job, I trade **DeMar DeRozan** or **Pascal Siakam**, it kills me. That's a part of the business I hate. Who am I to question if Kawhi leaves?
