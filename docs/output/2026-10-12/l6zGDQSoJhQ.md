# Olivia Miles On Becoming A WNBA All-Star As A Rookie & Representing Team USA — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l6zGDQSoJhQ

_Speakers identified: Tracy McGrady, Olivia Miles, Vince Carter_

**1. Olivia Miles — "It's so hard to enjoy because it's like you need to be at 50 places all at once" — Olivia Miles on the intensity of the WNBA All-Star experience** [00:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l6zGDQSoJhQ&t=19s)

**Vince Carter:** "How did Olivia feel about herself in the moment right then?"

**Olivia Miles:** "Everything was happening so fast. They were like, okay, you got to go here, you got to go there. It's so hard to enjoy because it's like you need to be at 50 places all at once. So, I had my 10 seconds where they were like, **Olivia Miles** or whatever, and I came and I don't even know what I did. I think I waved."

**Tracy McGrady:** "Yeah."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l6zGDQSoJhQ&t=19s

**2. Tracy McGrady — "You set the tone of what the WNBA All-Star game will look like every year" — Tracy McGrady on Olivia Miles setting the tone for WNBA All-Star games** [02:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l6zGDQSoJhQ&t=124s)

**Tracy McGrady:** "**Wembanyama** made it clear, he was going out there to play. If you didn't want to play, that's fine. But you set the tone of what the WNBA All-Star game will look like every year that you're in the game. Everybody will know, it's great to be in here, but once it's time to play, **Olivia Miles** is about to come out here and get busy."

**Olivia Miles:** "Hoopers know that when the ball gets thrown up, it's just hoop at the end of the day."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l6zGDQSoJhQ&t=124s

**3. Olivia Miles — "I still can't go to local shops or something without someone coming up to me" — Olivia Miles on the increased visibility and fan recognition in the WNBA** [04:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l6zGDQSoJhQ&t=284s)

Olivia Miles: "I thought people knew me, and still, I'm not as popular as Paige, Caitlin, and all them in Asia, but this is crazy. I can't go to local shops or something without someone coming up to me, or I see people wearing my jersey randomly on the road. It's crazy. I'm not **LeBron James**, and I understand that, but I still can't go to local shops or something without someone coming up to me."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l6zGDQSoJhQ&t=284s

**4. Olivia Miles — "The main thing is winning a championship" — Olivia Miles on the importance of staying grounded and winning championships** [06:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l6zGDQSoJhQ&t=393s)

Olivia Miles: "From a young age, I had to mature quickly because I was always playing with older girls. As a point guard, you have to command, you have to be confident in what you're doing. I've always been a competitor at heart, and I know awards will follow if my team is doing well. I never really worry about that stuff. It's nice to be in those conversations and to have the opportunity to do it, but that's not the main thing. The main thing is winning a championship."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l6zGDQSoJhQ&t=393s

**5. Olivia Miles — "I've won two gold medals with the USA and that feeling of winning a gold medal is surreal" — Olivia Miles on the surreal feeling of winning gold medals with Team USA** [08:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l6zGDQSoJhQ&t=510s)

Olivia Miles: "I've won two gold medals with the USA and that feeling of winning a gold medal is surreal. I don't even know what it feels like to win a World Cup or an Olympics. We won an AmeriCup and I felt on top of the world holding both medals. I love USA basketball because you go into a system where you acknowledge that you are not the one, you are together for a bigger purpose and a bigger goal. We saw a few years back with the men's USA, **Jason Tatum** didn't really play, and he just won a championship in the NBA Finals. You just understand that it's just bigger than you."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l6zGDQSoJhQ&t=510s
