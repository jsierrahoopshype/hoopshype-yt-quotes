**9. Jim Souhan — "the WNBA is shifting from a league that forever was really ultra reliant on post play" — The WNBA's evolution toward guard-centric play** [32:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FVcth6Ec2ds&t=1931s)

Jim Souhan: "The WNBA is shifting from a league that forever was really ultra reliant on post play, on bigs right around the basket doing work, the **Lisa Leslie**s of the world, the **Cynthia Cooper**s, there's a million of them, and **Cynthia Cooper**s, all these, and it is gravitating now and evolving to a league that is dictated by guard play. It's **Caitlin Clark**, it's **Paige Bueckers**, it's **Olivia Miles**, it's **Chelsea Gray**. And for me, aesthetically, I think that that is a reason that you are seeing the popularity of the game surge, is because the most fun part about basketball is a guard who can handle the ball and can do multiple things with it and you don't know what's coming. And whether that is popping a 35-footer or whether that is throwing a no-look pass or just moving the ball and relocating, it just has breathed so much energy into the game that I think fans are really connecting with this style of play even more so. And it really follows the same kind of trajectory that the NBA did. Think back to George Mikan and Bill Russell and Wilt Chamberlain and all of the Kareem and all of the big-centric basketball that we saw from the 60s, 70s, 80s, **Robert Parish** and **Moses Malone** and all these. And then it started to kind of go into **Michael Jordan**, **Magic Johnson**, **Steve Nash**, all of the guards. And that is when it got super-sized and just really kind of hit another gear. And I think the WNBA is going through that as well right now."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FVcth6Ec2ds&t=1931s
