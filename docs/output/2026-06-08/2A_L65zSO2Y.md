# Stephen A. remembers the life and legacy of Stacey King | First Take — *NBA on ESPN*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2A_L65zSO2Y

_Speakers identified: Molly Qerim, Jay Williams, Stephen A. Smith_

**1. Molly Qerim — "Stacey King, 3-time NBA champion with Bulls, has died at the age of 59" — Remembering the legacy of Bulls broadcaster and champion Stacey King** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2A_L65zSO2Y&t=0s)

Molly Qerim: "A champion with the Chicago Bulls, **Stacey King** has died at the age of 59, the team announced on Sunday. After his playing career, King spent more than two decades as a commentator on Bulls games, becoming an Emmy-winning fan favorite on their broadcast team."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2A_L65zSO2Y&t=0s

**2. Jay Williams — "Stacey has always reached out to me on an emotional level and kept it real with me" — Jay Williams on Stacey King's emotional impact and mentorship** [00:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2A_L65zSO2Y&t=58s)

Jay Williams: "One of the beautiful things about Stacey that I can recall is that obviously you go through life and for me, there is always this connectivity with the Chicago Bulls and Chicago. Stacey has always reached out to me on an emotional level and kept it real with me about, don't spend your time thinking on what could have been, spend your time focusing on what is and the opportunity you have been given. I cannot recall a **Derek Rose** iconic moment without Stacey calling it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2A_L65zSO2Y&t=58s

**3. Stephen A. Smith — "There was a Harry Caray-like effect he had on the listenership, not just the city" — Stephen A. Smith on Stacey King's unique connection to Chicago fans** [02:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2A_L65zSO2Y&t=156s)

Stephen A. Smith: "I was there yesterday in Chicago, my hometown, and it hit hard. Everybody is walking around in a daze because Stacey connected with people of a certain age and people who may have followed the Cubs nationally for those years when WGN was a superstation. There was a **Harry Caray**-like effect he had on the listenership, not just the city, but people who follow the Bulls. **Stacey King** was the player of the year in the Big Eight when **Danny Manning** was a senior in Kansas. He was chosen sixth in the draft by the Bulls, chosen ahead of **Sean Kemp**. **Stacey King** and **BJ Armstrong** were part of a draft class that was to help **Michael Jordan** get past the Pistons."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2A_L65zSO2Y&t=156s
