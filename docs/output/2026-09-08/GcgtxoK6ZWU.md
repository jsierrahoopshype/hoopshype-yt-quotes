**1. Golden Tate — "we had a heck of a quarterback that I knew, even if the world didn't know" — Golden Tate on the impact of Matthew Stafford and Calvin Johnson** [00:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GcgtxoK6ZWU&t=29s)

Golden Tate: "God knew where I needed to be and where I'd be appreciated, and he sent me where we had a heck of a quarterback that I knew, even if the world didn't know. And so I knew once I go to Detroit, Megatron is going to be getting double and triple coverage, and I'm going to be on the backside or in the slot catching one-on-ones all day long to number twos and number three cornerbacks and I am going to eat."

**2. Golden Tate — "Matthew Stafford had a huge part in me being YAC king" — Matthew Stafford's role in Golden Tate's success as a receiver** [00:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GcgtxoK6ZWU&t=56s)

Golden Tate: "I didn't understand how good **Matthew Stafford** was. **Matthew Stafford** had a huge part in me being YAC king and this is again something I also realized once I didn't have **Matthew Stafford** anymore. He would throw the ball to me still and place that joker right here. If he places that ball right here, anywhere else, it gets knocked down, picked off. He places it right here while he's on my back, dart. I catch it, I slide off me and I run for like 40 to 50 yards because he was just on time and he threw me open."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GcgtxoK6ZWU&t=56s

**3. Golden Tate — "Russell Wilson was a play extender" — Comparing the playing styles of Matthew Stafford and Russell Wilson** [03:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GcgtxoK6ZWU&t=204s)

Golden Tate: "**Russell Wilson**, when I had him, he was a play extender. And so a lot of times it was like having two plays every single time a play was called because it was a play that you ran the routes, but you always had to figure he's going to get outside the pocket and extend his play. And so then it turned into backyard football and so you had kind of double opportunities to get the ball. And he was so, so good at extending plays. But what separated Russell to me was his deep ball. His deep ball was so nice and it was so high and would just hit your fingers like soft."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GcgtxoK6ZWU&t=204s

**4. Golden Tate — "Russell was a vocal leader and Matthew was more of a doer" — Contrasting the leadership styles of Matthew Stafford and Russell Wilson** [05:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GcgtxoK6ZWU&t=346s)

Golden Tate: "Both were fantastic leaders and Russell was learning how to be a leader because he was a young boy. Russell had a lot of pressure. When they drafted him, the same year that we brought in **Matt Flynn** and paid him a 25 bag from Green Bay, **Matt Flynn** didn't touch the field. Russell came in right away, mature out of his mind, grinded, and **Matt Flynn** never saw the field. Russell was a vocal leader and Matthew was more of a doer. He knew like, hey, I'm that guy. Just give me some, O-line just give me some time, I'll make it happen."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GcgtxoK6ZWU&t=346s
