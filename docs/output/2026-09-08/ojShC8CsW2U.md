**5. Patrick Beverley — "I got double what I asked for" — Patrick Beverley on his successful tenure with the Minnesota Timberwolves** [04:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ojShC8CsW2U&t=244s)

Patrick Beverley: "I go to Minnesota. Minnesota gave me extension of $13 million. So not only I doubled it with Minnesota. I don't think people understand this. That starting five we had in Minnesota was arguably probably the best starting five offensively and defensively in the NBA that year. A historic number offensively and defensively. Me, **D'Angelo Russell**, **Anthony Edwards**, **Jarred Vanderbilt**, **Karl-Anthony Towns**. It was arguably we had the top starting five offensively and defense in the NBA for a large body of work. They saw what I've been trying to show people and what I do to organizations all the time, that I give them a little juice."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ojShC8CsW2U&t=244s

**6. Patrick Beverley — "They haven't missed the playoffs since I got there" — Patrick Beverley on the Timberwolves' playoff drought and his impact** [05:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ojShC8CsW2U&t=305s)

Patrick Beverley: "The first team we play against is the Clippers, and if we win, the Minnesota gets into the playoffs after 16 years. **Jimmy Butler** has been there, **Derrick Rose** has been there, all these different players have been there, high-level players, all-star, **Jeff Teague**, all-star caliber type players, and Minnesota has never been there. And then you inject Pat Bev in there and we're playing with a number seven seed. So not only did it was the emotional time for me, you know, I had family there, you know, and I really put on for the city of Minnesota. They haven't missed the playoffs since I got there. Two Western Conference Finals. They haven't missed the playoffs from me being there until now, they have not missed the playoffs."
