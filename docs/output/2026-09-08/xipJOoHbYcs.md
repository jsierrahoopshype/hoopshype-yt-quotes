**2. Cason Wallace — "He's one of the best at building players up in my opinion." — Cason Wallace on Coach Calipari's ability to develop players for the NBA** [02:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xipJOoHbYcs&t=145s)

Cason Wallace: "He's one of the best at building players up in my opinion. He knows your game to a T pretty much. He limits you to what you do best at first and once you're very dominant at that, then he lets you. Now he's like, okay, you get it now and he gives you a lot more, then he lets you grow. But I feel like a lot of times, we all go into college and some might not know. Going to college and it's like you feel like you have everything and then there's some things that isn't as polished and he's like, don't even worry about that right now. Focus on what you're very good at. That's what's going to get you to the next level."

**3. Cason Wallace — "He does a great job of helping those guys get to the NBA that are having family struggles." — Cason Wallace on Coach Calipari's support for players with family struggles** [05:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xipJOoHbYcs&t=330s)

Cason Wallace: "What I love about **Cal**, **Cal** does a great job of going to get those players, they're dogs. He knows they're dogs. But also the family dynamic is not structured properly and he does a great job of helping those guys get to the NBA that are having family struggles from an economical standpoint. **Cal** is really good at that and that's what I appreciate about him. Like, hey, if you are a top 10, top 15 draft pick, you ain't staying in college, man. Go take care of your family, bro. Get up out of here, go take care of your family.
