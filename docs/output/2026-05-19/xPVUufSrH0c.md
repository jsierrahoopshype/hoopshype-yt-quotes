# Hear from Wemby and SGA after the Spurs win Game 1 of the WCF in 2OT 🗣️ | SportsCenter — *NBA on ESPN*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xPVUufSrH0c

_Speakers identified: Victor Wembanyama, Shai Gilgeous-Alexander_

**1. Victor Wembanyama — Victor Wembanyama on personal motivation from Shai Gilgeous-Alexander's MVP award — "Yeah, for sure. Everything you just said."** [01:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xPVUufSrH0c&t=64s)

**Reporter:** "I'm just curious if that was in your mind at all, knowing that **Shai** had got that award and you were in the top three for it and, you know, did any part of tonight feel personal? Some of your facial expressions kind of look like you were really, really going for it."

**Victor Wembanyama:** "Yeah, for sure. Everything you just said."

**2. Victor Wembanyama — Victor Wembanyama on wanting MVP trophies many times in his career — "I still got a lot to learn and I want to get that trophy many times in my career."** [01:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xPVUufSrH0c&t=85s)

**Reporter:** "What does it feel like to see someone else get the MVP trophy right before you step into, I guess, what is basically the biggest game of your career and then to have the best performance of your career?"

**Victor Wembanyama:** "Feels like I still got a lot to learn and I want to get that trophy, you know, many times in my career."

**3. Victor Wembanyama — Victor Wembanyama on Spurs' team message of relentlessness against any opponent — "Our effort should be over anybody else's and tonight we were relentless."** [02:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xPVUufSrH0c&t=142s)

**Reporter:** "Tonight almost felt a little bit symbolic, the MVP ceremony for **SGA** before the game and then you have a big response tonight with your performance. If your performance tonight had a message behind it, what would that message be?"

**Victor Wembanyama:** "The message would be that we as a team are ready to go in any environment, in any place, against anybody, even though we still got a lot to learn. Our effort should be over anybody else's and tonight we were relentless."

**4. Victor Wembanyama — Victor Wembanyama on physical and mental toll of long games — "It takes a toll, but we will rest in July."** [03:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xPVUufSrH0c&t=207s)

**Reporter:** "You've said a couple of times now that you're tired, which makes a lot of sense. I imagine there's a mental toll during a game like that as well. Can you just explain what that game was like to be a part of with the amount of hits you took and to continue to keep going?"

**Victor Wembanyama:** "It's something, you know, the relentlessness, it's built as well. First of all, the first thing is physical ability, you know, getting stronger as the years go on. And the mental toughness, you have to have it all the time. And as you said, yes, it takes a toll, but we will rest in July."

**5. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander — Shai Gilgeous-Alexander on enjoying playoff problem-solving and self-improvement — "That's what this time of year is about, the highest level of basketball and you're going to find out exactly what type of player you are."** [07:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xPVUufSrH0c&t=461s)

**Reporter:** "You've talked before about enjoying finding ways to get better as a player, but also just throughout a series. How much do you enjoy the problem-solving aspect of finding ways to be effective against a specific team like this?"

**Shai Gilgeous-Alexander:** "Yeah, that's what it's about. That's what this time of year is about. It's the highest level of basketball and you're going to find out exactly what type of player you are, what type of competitor you are, and exactly what you need to get better at. And it's no different this time of year around."

**6. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander — Shai Gilgeous-Alexander on balancing aggression and smarts against Victor Wembanyama — "You got to be able to be aggressive but also be smart."** [09:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xPVUufSrH0c&t=584s)

**Reporter:** "How tough is it to shoot over a guy with that kind of length? And one, the things that you did find, is that something you think you can take for the rest of the series with you?"

**Shai Gilgeous-Alexander:** "The second part of your question I probably wouldn't want to say in public, just I don't want to give any answers away. It's obviously challenging. He's very tall, very long, deters a lot of things at the rim. Like I said before, you got to be smart when you go in there. Be patient, be aggressive, but also be aggressive and don't be too timid in there. I think you get caught worrying about it too much and you lose aggression and that's where they really stranglehold on the game. You got to be able to be aggressive but also be smart."
