**4. Draymond Green — "You need those people on your side. As equally important as it is for the coach to love you." — Draymond Green explains the importance of young players building relationships with coaching staff** [05:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2Er0SiZcIVY&t=353s)

Draymond Green: "I spoke about **Khalid Robinson** on the last podcast where I was just speaking about him getting up there and getting on the mic and his post-game press conference after they won the championship. The reason it's important is because I am speaking to young guys when I say this. You need to understand the layout of these organizations. I think so oftentimes people come in and they think, oh man, like if the head coach like me, I am good. And that's so far from the truth because what you have to understand in this league is there is someone or a few someones, if you will, that the coach may, if you take a weighted approach, right, and you take different opinions, there is a few people on the staff that the weight of their opinion could be a little higher. And what these young guys should understand is you need those people on your side. As equally important as it is for the coach to love you, it is as important for the guys who got his ear to love you."

Draymond Green: "Per Anthony Slater, the Naismith Basketball Hall of Fame will launch a new exhibit this weekend dedicated to the Golden State Warriors star **Steph Curry**. My thoughts on this are that this is insane. It's a reason it's never happened. It's a reason this is the first, because **Steph Curry** is just something different. The way he's changed the game of basketball, the way he's made kids dream because of the size that he is, because he did it with finesse if you will in shooting the basketball and not necessarily dunking the basketball or off athleticism, which I think the most special players we've ever seen in NBA history, a lot of them, it's built somewhat off athleticism and Steph's the complete opposite. And so, I think this is special. Congrats to 30. This is, I mean, he's not even in the Hall of Fame yet. Obviously, we know he's going, but to not be in the Hall of Fame and yet have something dedicated to you at the Hall of Fame, man, I tip my cap to that brother."
