**5. Chris Mannix — "The front line though now is not a high-efficiency three-point front line." — The 76ers' lack of high-efficiency three-point shooting** [06:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyP0sBIS2MQ&t=375s)

Chris Mannix: "The front line though now is not a high-efficiency three-point front line. **Joel Embiid** is 33% from three. He's much better suited to playing in the post even though his legs probably force him out on the perimeter a lot more than they used to. **Jaylen Brown** can shoot threes but he is more of an inside the three-point line type of player as well. **LeBron James** last year shot around 32% from three. Is that a concern with this group? When we were talking about Miami and the fit for LeBron in Miami, we said that LeBron didn't solve the Heat's three-point shooting problem because Bam Adebayo and Giannis Antetokounmpo are both non-three-point shooters. LeBron does not solve any shooting problems in Philadelphia and **Paul George** for all of his weaknesses is a pretty good three-point shooter."
