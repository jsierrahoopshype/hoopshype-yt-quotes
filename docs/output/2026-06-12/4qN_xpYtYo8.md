**1. Isiah Thomas — "Guys are jealous. They get envious. They intentionally ignore the obvious." — Isiah Thomas on why former players exclude LeBron James from top-5** [00:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4qN_xpYtYo8&t=6s)

**Chandler Parsons:** "None of them had **LeBron James** in their top-5. What is the disconnect there between LeBron and these guys?"

**Isiah Thomas:** "Jealousy. They get envious. Y'all bring them on the show, then they want to talk about what they did, or what somebody else did, and then they intentionally ignore the obvious. And when you intentionally ignore the obvious, then that has to come back to some jealousy or envy or just straight-up BS. This man got every basketball record on the books, and he ain't in your top-5, you full of shit."

**2. Isiah Thomas — "New York is on steroids because they're so starved. It's a different kind of passion." — Isiah Thomas on the unique passion of New York Knicks fans** [01:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4qN_xpYtYo8&t=101s)

**Michelle Beadle:** "What do you make of these New York Knicks fans?"

**Isiah Thomas:** "What we're watching, you got to put them as some of the best. New York is on steroids because they're so starved. It's a different kind of passion that we're seeing. The other fan bases, we do the same tricks. All fans stay at the hotel, pull the hotel alarms, and they didn't throw eggs, they threw coins. But I think what New York has, it has a hunger and a passion that no other basketball city has because it's been starved for so long."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4qN_xpYtYo8&t=101s

**3. Isiah Thomas — "Boston had all the tricks. You walk out on the floor, they're calling you every name." — Isiah Thomas on the Boston Celtics being the toughest road environment** [03:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4qN_xpYtYo8&t=207s)

Isiah Thomas: "Boston Celtics. It ain't no question. We had to bring our own water because we couldn't order room service. They was pulling the alarms. We had to walk outside 4:00 in the morning. We're going to our locker room, the air conditioning was off, it was heated up. The showers were cold. Boston had all the tricks. You walk out on the floor, they're calling you every name, mentioning everything. But it made you want to beat them and it made you so tough."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4qN_xpYtYo8&t=207s

Isiah Thomas: "When we would try to leave Boston, they would shut the airport down. Logan Airport would not let a flight take off after 10:00 if it was the Detroit Pistons. A lot of times we couldn't get out. Boston had all the tricks and they used them."

**5. Isiah Thomas — "If they're going to be 100% honest and not promote this BS. Tell the truth about the game." — Isiah Thomas on the need for honesty in NBA media coverage** [07:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4qN_xpYtYo8&t=472s)

**Michelle Beadle:** "How do you feel about the idea of bringing in more legends and having them be more involved on a more daily basis?"

**Isiah Thomas:** "Only if they're going to be 100% honest and not promote this BS. Tell the truth about the game, give our game truthfully, honestly, and factually to the fans so they can continue to learn and grow and love our game. But the way our game now is being talked about in some ways, it's very mythical. It ain't real."
