They have a bunch of big guards on Brunson. **Josh Hart** can kind of handle it. You don't want OG Anunoby or any of their other wings handling it with the type of pressure that San Antonio can. **2. Channing Frye — "They figured out what Wembanyama can't block. When you drive right at him and stop and pull." — Spurs' defensive strategy against Jalen Brunson** [02:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HCoVR49oGyM&t=152s)

Channing Frye: "I would say anybody else but Brunson. I don't care if **Jose Alvarado** shoots this. I could go to sleep at night if he makes a miraculous shot. One thing I noticed in the game, and Alvarado I think did it maybe the game before, they figured out what Wembanyama can't block. When you drive right at him and stop and pull, he can't move his feet like that. He's too big, too long and lanky. So Alvarado and Brunson have figured out that the pull-up middy, or a hard pull-up as he's backing up, he can't back up with his long self and jump and block it. So that's why I think for me, **Jalen Brunson** took that quick three at the end of the game."
