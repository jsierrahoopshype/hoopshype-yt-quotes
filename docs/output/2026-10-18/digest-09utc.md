**1. Kerry Washington — "I had this new question mark about 50 percent of my genetics." — Kerry Washington on discovering her father was a sperm donor** [00:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5cbpY0ZVg&t=9s)

Kerry Washington: "When Scandal ended, I got a call from Skip Gates, who does that show Finding Your Roots. I knew Skip, and he knew that I loved that show, but I never had time when we were doing Scandal because we were so busy. So, Skip was like, you should come do the show. I called my parents to tell them that I was going to do Finding Your Roots, and my dad started having panic attacks. Eventually, my parents confided in Skip. My parents got on the phone with Skip Gates and were like, let's say that hypothetically we used a sperm donor to conceive Kerry. Is that something that would come up in the show? And Skip was like, yes, and you should tell her. They really struggled because their plan was to never tell me that. I had this new question mark about 50 percent of my genetics."
