# Cynthia Cooper Joined the WNBA at 34 — Here's What the League Was Really Like | Cousins — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aVStQiaj0FA

_Speakers identified: Tracy McGrady, Cynthia Cooper, Vince Carter_

**1. Cynthia Cooper — "I wasn't a star when I got to the WNBA. I just happened to be picked." — Cynthia Cooper on being drafted to the WNBA at 34** [00:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aVStQiaj0FA&t=33s)

Cynthia Cooper: "It was super unique. It was super unique because guess what? I wasn't a star when I got to the WNBA. I just happened to be picked because **Sheryl Swoopes** was pregnant. Then they were like, you know what, we feel bad for them that their superstar is out on maternity leave, so we are going to give them the number one draft choice and we picked **Tina Thompson**. Now I knew from SC days, I knew from watching SC that Tina was a stretch four, so I'm thinking great, that's going to open up the lane for me to penetrate. But the one X-factor that became evident in year two and not so much in the first part of the season was nobody knew who I was. Nobody knew what I could do. Plus, I'm 34 years old."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aVStQiaj0FA&t=33s

**2. Cynthia Cooper — "I was a different player and we could compliment each other." — Cynthia Cooper on her role and chemistry with teammates** [01:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aVStQiaj0FA&t=69s)

Cynthia Cooper: "The one X-factor that became evident in year two and not so much in the first part of the season was nobody knew who I was. Nobody knew what I could do. I was a different player and we could compliment each other. And then Tina, I love it. Get off this pick and roll, you are going to pop sometimes, you are going to roll sometimes, I got you for the three-point shot. And once we all came together and understood that we could bounce off of each other and play off of each other and then no one could stop us if we did that, we had that recipe."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aVStQiaj0FA&t=69s

**3. Cynthia Cooper — "Kim Perrot was really that X-factor. She wouldn't let no point guard bring the ball up." — Cynthia Cooper on the impact of Kim Perrot and Elena Shakova** [03:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aVStQiaj0FA&t=232s)

Cynthia Cooper: "I would say the first person that came to my mind was **Kim Perrot** for our first two years because she was really that X-factor. She wouldn't let no point guard bring the ball up the floor. She was knocking down the corner three so people couldn't double team. And then I would say this unsung hero, I'm blanking on her first name, but her last name was Shakova. Shakova was from Bulgaria and I had played against Shakova when I was in Italy playing in the EuroCup. And so when her name came up, no one knew her here. She was a beast, knocking down the three, so strong, she could play with her back. She did up **Lisa Leslie** like it wasn't no nothing. She put that meat on her and then trailed the play and knocked down two huge three-pointers. I begged Coach Chancellor to draft her and he did. He brought her in and I think she is a major reason why we won that fourth championship."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aVStQiaj0FA&t=232s

**4. Cynthia Cooper — "I'm from Watts and she was from Riverside. She was from the suburbs." — Cynthia Cooper on her early rivalry with Sheryl Miller** [05:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aVStQiaj0FA&t=312s)

Cynthia Cooper: "I'm from Watts and she was from Riverside. She was from the suburbs. So I'm just trying to figure out who this kid is that they keep saying is so great. We won a state championship. I averaged 31 as a freshman. I had only been playing for three years, well three years my senior year in high school. So I'm like, who is this kid? I'm like, I'm killing when she getting here. When she getting here, I'm dragging her. And then I saw her practice once and I was like, oh my god."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aVStQiaj0FA&t=312s

**5. Cynthia Cooper — "Now young girls are dreaming of playing in the W and they're preparing sooner." — Cynthia Cooper on the growth and talent in the WNBA** [09:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aVStQiaj0FA&t=583s)

Cynthia Cooper: "I love the new CBA. I love the players taking ownership of their own legacy and their own league. I love the talent. Now young girls are dreaming of playing in the W and they're preparing sooner. They're getting that skill set and that motivation and that mindset and getting ready to prepare to play professional basketball in America. So that breeds super talent, a lot of talent, and great girls, women's basketball. And that's what you're starting to see 30 years into the W. I love the competition. Now because there's so much talent and opportunity in the W, now you're starting to see very competitive games played at a very high level."
