**4. Brian Windhorst — "Analytics is a piece of what you do to evaluate a player." — Brian Windhorst explains analytics as a complex, multi-layered decision tool** [04:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pT8NG8svc0&t=258s)

Brian Windhorst: "When you say analytics it is a code word, it's a cover and catch-all for a lot of different philosophies that sort of blend into one. Analytics is a piece of what you do to evaluate a player. You don't only look at analytics and if there's an analytics person on a team that says they evaluate **Jaylen Brown** as seventh, fine, that's his evaluation. You can criticize it and say he's wrong, he probably thinks he's right. You go look at your coaches and say what are your coaches? Well, the coaches may say Jaylen's number one and your development people may say Jaylen's number three. And you put all that together and you make your evaluation of a player."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pT8NG8svc0&t=258s
