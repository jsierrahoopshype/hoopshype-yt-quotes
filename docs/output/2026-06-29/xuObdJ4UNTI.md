# What is the bigger potential impact: Kahwi to Raptors or LeBron to Warriors? | First Take — *NBA on ESPN*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xuObdJ4UNTI

_Speakers identified: Molly Qerim, Vincent Goodwill, David Dennis Jr., Brian Windhorst_

**1. Vincent Goodwill — "for the culture, of course Golden State. For entertainment values, of course Golden State." — Why Golden State is the preferred destination for cultural and entertainment value** [00:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xuObdJ4UNTI&t=43s)

Vincent Goodwill: "For the culture, of course Golden State. For entertainment values, of course Golden State. But for basketball, you are looking at the Toronto Raptors and the conference that does not have the two monuments at the top in San Antonio and Oklahoma City. Toronto has had a history of doing this before. When they sense that there is an opening at the top, they go all in. They went all in for **Kawhi Leonard** with no guarantees in 2018 coming off of that injury that was pretty mysterious with the San Antonio Spurs."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xuObdJ4UNTI&t=43s

**2. Vincent Goodwill — "They're looking at it and saying, we have an opportunity even if Kawhi Leonard is 35 years old." — Toronto's potential to capitalize on Eastern Conference instability** [01:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xuObdJ4UNTI&t=95s)

Vincent Goodwill: "They're looking at it and saying, we have an opportunity even if **Kawhi Leonard** is 35 years old. You still have **Scottie Barnes**. You still have players that you do not have to put in the deal along with still having draft capital that you can put in subsequent deals at the trade deadline to bolster whatever you wind up having. It puts them in the driver seat, at least in theory, of the conversation. So I will say, on the floor, less competition, less certainty, Toronto."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xuObdJ4UNTI&t=95s

**3. David Dennis Jr. — "there is no way I'm going to go against two top 10 dudes joining each other." — Why betting against LeBron James and Stephen Curry is unwise** [02:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xuObdJ4UNTI&t=150s)

David Dennis Jr.: "There is no way I'm going to go against two top 10 dudes joining each other. Like this **LeBron James** and **Stephen Curry** we're talking about. And I am just not going to bet against them when it comes to them trying to win basketball games. Like you, I don't care how old they are. Explain to me who is going to stop them in a pick and roll. Who is going to have more basketball know-how on the court than **LeBron James** and **Stephen Curry**?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xuObdJ4UNTI&t=150s

**4. Brian Windhorst — "Kawhi fits in at both ends of the court and provides some level of antidote for the Knicks." — Why Kawhi Leonard provides a better defensive antidote for the Knicks** [03:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xuObdJ4UNTI&t=215s)

Brian Windhorst: "Kawhi fits in at both ends of the court and provides some level of antidote for the Knicks. His ability to play at both ends, so you also have to see like what else would the Warriors have on their roster. Let's say they don't trade **Jimmy Butler**. How **Jimmy Butler** look coming off an ACL in his mid 30s? Maybe he looks great. He gave our interview to **Anthony Slater** the other day and he said he feels great, he's recovering great. Maybe he comes back and is super strong."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xuObdJ4UNTI&t=215s

**5. Vincent Goodwill — "what we've seen in the playoffs the last few years is that sweat equity wins." — The importance of playoff experience and roster chemistry** [05:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xuObdJ4UNTI&t=343s)

Vincent Goodwill: "What we've seen in the playoffs the last few years is that sweat equity wins. You have to have some years together and go through some playoff pain. Now, **Stephen Curry** has caused **LeBron James** some playoff pain. **LeBron James** has caused **Stephen Curry** some playoff pain. **Draymond Green** has caused everybody some playoff pain in some way shape or form. But they haven't gone through it together. The Olympics is a great sample size in a small six-week sample size, completely different than playing 82 games with that level of roster."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xuObdJ4UNTI&t=343s

**6. David Dennis Jr. — "nobody knows LeBron James on the court better than Stephen Curry." — Why the Warriors' core has the highest basketball IQ** [07:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xuObdJ4UNTI&t=420s)

David Dennis Jr.: "I would say that nobody knows **LeBron James** on the court better than **Stephen Curry**. And I would say nobody knows **Stephen Curry** on the court better than **Draymond Green** and **LeBron James**, even though they have not played a lot of games together. We talk about people who have experience in these high intense things, I would say that those two players, if anybody can figure it out on the fly and learn how to play with each other. You got to remember **Stephen Curry** and **Jimmy Butler**. **Jimmy Butler** came at the trade deadline and they were off to the races very soon."
