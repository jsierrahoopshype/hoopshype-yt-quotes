# How Players TV Aims to Help Athletes in Today's Media | Kyrie Irving 24 Hour Stream — *Kyrie LIVE*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HGo4LQqGcOQ

_Speakers identified: Kyrie Irving, Colin Castellaw_

**1. Colin Castellaw — Players TV co-founded by 70+ athletes to own content and distribution**
[00:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HGo4LQqGcOQ&t=39s)

"Yeah, man, it's going great. So Players TV were basically co-founded by about 70 plus professional athletes. **Kyrie** was one of the initial guys who came on board and really saw the vision. And at the end of the day, it's really all about number one athletes being able to own their own content, own their own IP, but more importantly, own their own distribution. We saw this budding problem in the ecosystem where all of the traditional media companies were starting to jump to shard, getting to this athlete versus that athlete stuff, and really at the detriment for how athletes could connect with their fans. So we're like, yo, instead, let's cut out the middleman. Let's create a platform that allows athletes to create content, distribute content and try to change the game."

**2. Colin Castellaw — Players TV faced early skepticism from streaming networks**
[01:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HGo4LQqGcOQ&t=90s)

"I'll never forget probably really early on. There was an executive, a very well-known streaming network that we went for potential investment. It was like, hey, we got some athletes on board and they basically told us, this isn't going to work. You guys aren't going to be able to do it. It's not going to happen. So we're like, all right, well, we'll just bootstrap it ourselves and keep it rolling. It's definitely interesting because anytime I think you're trying to disrupt an industry in general, there's going to be some headwinds. But that's why it took us, I think it was like 18 months before we even launched the company from when we first started talking to you guys. So yeah, it's been fun to just try to change the game."

[02:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HGo4LQqGcOQ&t=176s)

**5. Colin Castellaw — Players TV aims for IPO and sustainable business growth**
[05:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HGo4LQqGcOQ&t=347s)

**10. Colin Castellaw — Media collaboration and 360-degree distribution for content monetization**
[13:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HGo4LQqGcOQ&t=789s)

"That's a great question. I think overall, it's really about eyeballs. Media more or less is just about attention. And so a lot of people make it like me versus you. And they're like, well, every eyeball that I might bring somebody else is an eyeball that could be taken away from me or whatever it is. And really, we found it doesn't work like that. We call it eyeballs everywhere, 360-degree distribution. So the more you can collaborate, the more you can come together, the more you can do social media collabs, the more you can do distribution collabs. A lot of people don't realize licensing content and syndication is a huge side of the business. So hey, you've got a show or a piece of content. There's no reason that you can't take somebody like Players TV interested in across the world. Monetize that same thing instead of once by throwing it up on YouTube and giving **Daddy Google** a bunch of money. You can monetize it seven, eight, nine, ten different ways if you got the distribution now."
