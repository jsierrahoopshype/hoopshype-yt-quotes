# Jalen and Josh on if they prefer home or road crowds — *Roommates Show*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yL5rWm7r9Ek

_Speakers identified: Unknown speaker, Jalen Brunson, Josh Hart_

**1. Jalen Brunson — Jalen Brunson on the unique satisfaction of silencing road crowds**
[00:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yL5rWm7r9Ek&t=22s)

"I personally love playing at the Garden, but man, something about playing on the road and it getting quiet, it's like music to my ears. At home that would've been sick too. But ever since I was in high school, playing on the road, I can't explain it. You hear everyone quiet and you hear the Knicks fans. Nothing obviously playing in the Garden, nothing beats that. But when you hear the Knicks fans on the road and they're loud in another gym, I know teams are sick."

**2. Josh Hart — Josh Hart prefers home games at Madison Square Garden**
[00:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yL5rWm7r9Ek&t=38s)

"Yeah, I would say home cause it's the Garden. I think if I was on any other team, I would probably say away, cause like **JB** said, it hits a little different when you're in, especially a close game, when the crowd's going crazy or whatever and y'all hit a big shot."
