# Jalen & Josh Talk Mental Strategy, Knicks' New-Look Offense and the KAT Factor — *Roommates Show*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cnk6RClnMog

_Speakers identified: Josh Hart, Josh Giddey_

**1. Josh Hart — CJ McCollum is a great player, but matchups made a difference**
[00:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cnk6RClnMog&t=57s)

"I don't want to say return to normalcy, **CJ**'s always been a great player. Yeah. **CJ**'s always been a player that's shown up in playoffs. He's had big, he's had big playoff games. I don't even know the last time he was in the playoffs, but he's had big-time games. I mean, you're being guarded by me and then you're being guarded by **Josh Giddey**. I think that's a clear difference."

**2. Josh Giddey — Karl-Anthony Towns' new role as playmaker changed offense**
[02:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cnk6RClnMog&t=140s)

"I feel like it's comparing apples and oranges, honestly, if you compare it to last postseason. Last postseason his job was to score. **JB**'s job were, both of their jobs were to score, obviously to get guys involved and when they get blitzed, make the right play. But, how the offense was last year was, when the five was on me, I was more in that position where, being more of that playmaking hub. Obviously this year, and especially the Atlanta series, the last four or five games, we really used his ability at the elbow and to playmake, whether that was driving to the rim at that area. Comparing it to last year, like I said, I think it's apples and oranges, but I think new year, new system, new role, he did an amazing job this series of playmaking, being that hub and obviously allowing **JB** to get off the ball and get touches more in the flow and playing off the catch to stop having to be hounded by **Dyson Daniels** or **Naji Marshall** for 94 feet."

**3. Josh Hart — OG Anunoby's fantastic play was evident throughout the series**
[04:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cnk6RClnMog&t=279s)

"He played fantastic. Made big shots. Made plays on both sides of the ball. He's just doing what **OG** does. I think we see that on a regular basis. But I think **OG** was just hot the entire, entire series. And everyone saw it. We see it all the time, but everyone really, really saw it this time."
