**2. Iman Shumpert — "If you take the ball away from Cade, Cade becomes less good and your team becomes worse" — Iman Shumpert's critique of the Detroit Pistons' offensive system** [00:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jfncjiRYThA&t=38s)

Iman Shumpert: "Basically what he said is we are going to try to find other ways to activate the defense and keep everybody from watching Cade. But that is what we all watched last year the whole time, and I just do not think that is going to change at all. That is why Cade is so good. If you take the ball away from Cade, Cade becomes less good and your team becomes worse. Who is coming into Detroit to see anything else besides **Cade Cunningham** put everything on his back, dump it off to **Jalen Duren** who is going to clean it up in the post?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jfncjiRYThA&t=38s

**3. Iman Shumpert — "if Cade is going to make 80% of the decisions on this ball club, you are going to be good" — Iman Shumpert on Cade Cunningham's role and team identity** [01:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jfncjiRYThA&t=107s)

Iman Shumpert: "Detroit's identity comes from that. If Cade is going to make 80% of the decisions on this ball club, you are going to be good because he is good at the decisions. If you take the ball out of his hands or try and make an offense where it is passing around, who knows if we like Cade in an efficient role where he gets four seconds at the end of a shot clock to make a decision?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jfncjiRYThA&t=107s

Like every game we are going to have a list on the locker room with the biggest crashers of the opponent. Tonight, it is the whole team out there, so they are all going. Can't forget about it.
