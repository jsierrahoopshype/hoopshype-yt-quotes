**1. DeMarcus Cousins — "All I saw last night was a bunch of just long shots and it got to the point where they shot themselves out of the game." — DeMarcus Cousins on the Celtics' reliance on long-range shooting** [00:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CePB6g1TOeQ&t=27s)

DeMarcus Cousins: "They've always been a team that relied on a lot of threes, but when they had **Jaylen Brown** in crunch moments, you had that guy that could get to the basket, make plays within the paint. All I saw last night was a bunch of just long shots and it got to the point where they shot themselves out of the game. When you think about your best player, you think about crunch time minutes. You want to have that guy that can obviously knock down shots from the outside, but get to the paint, create, create for others. I didn't quite see that from **Jayson Tatum**."

**2. Lou Williams — "Boston just got to find some aggression at some point. We understand that they are a three-point shooting team." — Lou Williams on the Celtics' need for more aggressive play** [01:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CePB6g1TOeQ&t=117s)

Lou Williams: "Boston just got to find some aggression at some point. We understand that they are a three-point shooting team. We understand that they are going to live and die by the three, but at some point, somebody is going to have to create, slow the game down, get to the free throw line. At some point, they are going to have to try to figure out what they are going to do in the half court and not just try to shoot themselves out of problems. That has been the identity for this team for a few years now since they have been competitive in the Eastern Conference, and the most aggressive guy that you had, you shipped him out to Philadelphia now."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CePB6g1TOeQ&t=117s

**3. Chandler Parsons — "Their style of play is just such a gift and a curse, man. They can look really good nights when they shoot 55% from three." — Chandler Parsons on the Celtics' inconsistent offensive style** [02:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CePB6g1TOeQ&t=171s)

Chandler Parsons: "Their style of play is just such a gift and a curse, man. They can look really good nights when they shoot 55% from three, and they can look really bad when we just consistently see them settle for long contested jumpers and step-back two-pointers and early threes. It is just the same thing they did last year. Now, do I think **Jayson Tatum** is going to go 6 for 23 from the field often? No. And I do like that he was playmaking; he had 11 assists last night. He was showing that more versatile side and **Paul George** was unbelievable, but defensively, I think they struggle going forward."

**4. Michelle Beadle — "I don't think that's the excuse coming into this season." — Michelle Beadle and DeMarcus Cousins on Jayson Tatum's performance** [04:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CePB6g1TOeQ&t=244s)

**Michelle Beadle:** "Lou, you think that the Tatum post-injury, can some of that be attributed to he's not 100%, or worst case, that this is the new **Jayson Tatum**?"

**DeMarcus Cousins:** "I don't think that's the excuse coming into this season. I mean, he came back last year, he played really well in the return. The excuse last year when he came back and played off, he looked like he's back, he looks great. Going into the next year, he'll be even even better. So, I think he's too far removed from the injury for it to still be an excuse or an injury. Obviously, it's always going to play a part in his career, but I think he's in a better place health-wise. I just think it was a bad performance on his part, plain and simple."

**5. DeMarcus Cousins — "He's now at the point in his career with now being the clear-cut lead guy for this Celtics group, he has to become better as an individual." — DeMarcus Cousins on Jayson Tatum's new role as the primary option** [05:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CePB6g1TOeQ&t=340s)

DeMarcus Cousins: "He's now at the point in his career with now being the clear-cut lead guy for this Celtics group, he has to become better as an individual. At one point you could get away with your game being all jump-shot based. Now, being that lead guy every night, we need more from you. You can't just go out and win games shooting all jumpers. We need you attacking the rim, we need you getting to the free throw line, we need you getting guys involved. We need you to be that three-level scorer that we know you're capable of being. But if this version of you is just all jump shots, this is going to be a very long season."
