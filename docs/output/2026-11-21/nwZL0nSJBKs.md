# Davion Mitchell Is THE Davion Mitchell Type | Chris Mannix & Evan Turner — *Sports Illustrated*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nwZL0nSJBKs

_Speakers identified: Chris Mannix, Evan Turner_

**1. Chris Mannix — "they made adjustments on the fly. They're doing a lot more dribble hand-offs, they're doing a lot more pick and roll." — Erik Spoelstra's tactical adjustments have diversified the Miami Heat's offense** [00:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nwZL0nSJBKs&t=44s)

Chris Mannix: "I'll give credit again to **Eric Spoelstra** and the coaching staff for some of the tweaks that they made. They made adjustments on the fly. They're doing a lot more dribble hand-offs, they're doing a lot more pick and roll. I think that's unlocked some things. They're not just playing downhill with Giannis anymore. They've diversified their offense, which I think has unlocked some stuff in Bam Adebayo, who had a great game 37 against Cleveland. Clay Thompson has been outstanding during this stretch. Giannis continues to be Giannis. Davion Mitchell proving everybody wrong. Anybody that thought Davion Mitchell could not be a starting point guard in the NBA, including me, I was one of them, proving people wrong."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nwZL0nSJBKs&t=44s

**2. Evan Turner — "now it seems as though everybody's stepping up, everybody's in the groove and they have a rhythm." — The Miami Heat's turnaround is driven by collective team contributions** [01:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nwZL0nSJBKs&t=94s)

Evan Turner: "I'm buying the Heat now due to the simple fact of the others. When we first started the season, it looked like it was just a one-man show. Obviously, **Bam Adebayo** was nowhere to be found, Clay was still trying to find his shot, **Wiggins** was still kind of on chill mode and you only had **Davion Mitchell** really showing up. Obviously, **Giannis** with his shots as well, but now it seems as though everybody's stepping up, everybody's in the groove and they have a rhythm."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nwZL0nSJBKs&t=94s

**3. Chris Mannix — "not many people believed for years that Davion Mitchell could be a starting point guard." — Davion Mitchell is proving he can be a starting point guard** [02:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nwZL0nSJBKs&t=161s)

Chris Mannix: "I want to key in on Mitchell for a minute. For a long time, Mitchell was like the guy you referenced when there was a college player coming out who was a little bit undersized. You said, well, he can make it because he's a **Davion Mitchell** type, which is really like a defensive bulldog who could be kind of a tweener in that backcourt, overcome a size disadvantage. Not many people believed for years that **Davion Mitchell** could be a starting point guard. But I was watching that Houston game, 16 points, 10 assist, he's playing that same brand of on-ball defense. He's just figured some stuff out and he's coming into his own as a playmaker in this league."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nwZL0nSJBKs&t=161s

**4. Evan Turner — "he was able to disrupt some of their emotional leaders." — Davion Mitchell's defensive presence disrupts opposing team's emotional leaders** [04:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nwZL0nSJBKs&t=270s)

Evan Turner: "One thing with **Davion Mitchell** that makes him so impressive, when we thought the Rockets were unbelievable, it was like, hey, when you watch that game, it was like **Giannis**'s presence was amazing, but it starts from the head of the snake and **Davion Mitchell**, even when it comes down to being able to guard **Fred VanVleet**, he was able to disrupt some of their emotional leaders. He was able to speed up and be like, hey, if I hit you right here, a gut punch, I know you guys are going to crack."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nwZL0nSJBKs&t=270s

**5. Chris Mannix — "you've got Larsen shooting 47%, you got Mitchell shooting close to 44%, you got Clay Thompson at 43%." — Miami's success is tied to their high-percentage three-point shooting** [08:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nwZL0nSJBKs&t=495s)

Chris Mannix: "I keyed in before the season on the three-point shooting with Miami. You've got **Larsen** shooting 47%, you got **Mitchell** shooting close to 44%, you got **Clay Thompson** at 43%. These are not particularly small sample size, especially with Clay who's had nine and a half threes per game. You got guys that are making three-point shots and I mentioned **Wiggins**, he's not played well this season, there's still hope I'm sure there that he can improve his play. They've figured some stuff out too as well. I think for me, just like I talked about the top with Walker Kessler being like the wildcard with the Lakers going from good to great, I think the same thing about **Davion Mitchell**. I think you know what you're going to get from **Giannis**, you know what you're going to get from **Bam**, Clay may not be Warriors Clay, but he's still a pretty good player. If **Mitchell** can like play other point guards to like a standstill, they're good."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nwZL0nSJBKs&t=495s
