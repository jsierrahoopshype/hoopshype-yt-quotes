## Walker Kessler Describes Playing With Luka Doncic — *The Old Man and The Three*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vwc1TvYTnpM

_Speakers identified: Flea, Walker Kessler, Tommy Alter, Cam Johnson_

**1. Walker Kessler — "he gets a steal and throws the ball behind his head to Kyrie" — Walker Kessler on the viral play against Luka Doncic** [02:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vwc1TvYTnpM&t=150s)

**Tommy Alter:** "When did you realize this with Luka?"

**Walker Kessler:** "When we played Dallas, there is a viral clip when I was on Utah. This is when Luka was still in Dallas, he was not in LA. He gets a steal and throws the ball behind his head to Kyrie, and Kyrie grabs the ball. I go to contest the ball and he throws a lob. That whole game, the way he talked and the swagger and confidence he had, it is just different."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vwc1TvYTnpM&t=150s

**2. Walker Kessler — "for 100 percent, because it just gets him fired up" — Advising opposing fan bases to leave Luka Doncic alone** [03:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vwc1TvYTnpM&t=209s)

**Tommy Alter:** "Do you think he is one of those people, there are certain players who, if you are advising an opposing fan base, to just leave him alone?"

**Walker Kessler:** "For 100 percent, because it just gets him fired up."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vwc1TvYTnpM&t=209s

**3. Walker Kessler — "it seems like nobody wants to try the skyhook" — Walker Kessler on why players avoid the skyhook** [09:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vwc1TvYTnpM&t=571s)

**Tommy Alter:** "Can you answer me a question? This is actually a great question. The guy scores more points than anyone in NBA history until LeBron comes along, right? And no one tries the skyhook."

**Walker Kessler:** "People got baby hooks, they got small, but it seems like nobody wants to try the skyhook. You might be 35 a game with the skyhook. If I could make it at a percentage that was good enough, yeah, but if I go out there and shoot three of those and I hit just backboard, people are going to look at me like I am crazy. You speak about fearlessness, I do not have enough fearlessness to shoot a skyhook three times."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vwc1TvYTnpM&t=571s
