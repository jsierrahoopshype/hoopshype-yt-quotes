## Walker Kessler Breaks Down Playing For JJ Redick — *The Old Man and The Three*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QTq55Vba-rc

_Speakers identified: Walker Kessler, Tommy Alter, Flea, Cam Johnson_

**1. Walker Kessler — "JJ is a good, good basketball mind. It's pretty obvious, but just how high IQ he is." — Walker Kessler on JJ Redick's high basketball IQ and coaching style** [00:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QTq55Vba-rc&t=15s)

Walker Kessler: "JJ is a good, good basketball mind. It's pretty obvious, but just how high IQ he is. And I think him being a player and being a big-time player in the past, he gets it. I feel like some coaches, and I obviously have only played for two organizations now, but some coaches, whether it's college or whatever, they try to curve messages. They don't outright say what it means or what it is. JJ is very upfront. He's like, 'Hey, we got to do this. I'm not going to spend all this time yelling at you, but this is what it is. Get your act together, let's move on.'"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QTq55Vba-rc&t=15s
