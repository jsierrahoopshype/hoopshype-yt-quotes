**2. God Shammgod — "I used to always wear ankle weights, so I could move faster. Then I put them on my wrists." — Using ankle weights on wrists to develop faster ball-handling** [02:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6A9YYAhb_kw&t=161s)

God Shammgod: "I used to always wear ankle weights, so I could move faster. Then I put them on my wrists and I started dribbling like that. Then I would take them off and my hands would be fast, fast, fast. I would dribble with no air in the ball so I could pound it. After that, it became a thing in my neighborhood. Oh, we talking about Shammgod, the one that wear the ankle weights on his wrist and be dribbling. That is how my handle got faster and faster."

**3. God Shammgod — "My father used to train boxers, so I used to box young and he gave me the discipline." — Boxing training provided the foundation for Shammgod's footwork** [04:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6A9YYAhb_kw&t=290s)

God Shammgod: "My father used to train boxers, so I used to box young and he gave me the discipline. I always knew the hand is just an illusion. The footwork and all that is the most important part of boxing. The hands and all that is just what comes with it. I felt that same way in basketball. I used to watch the tapes in slow motion because I see how people move their legs when they cross, pause, and all that stuff. The basketball and your hands, that is just all an illusion. Everything else is your body, the way your body moves with it is the thing that gets somebody."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6A9YYAhb_kw&t=290s

**4. God Shammgod — "If you know how to do a move perfectly, then you can adjust perfectly." — The importance of technical details in player development** [06:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6A9YYAhb_kw&t=384s)

God Shammgod: "Details are working with **Paulo** or anybody. I am so specific on details because the details are the biggest part to me. If you know how to do a move perfectly, then you can adjust perfectly. If you can't do something perfectly, it is hard to adjust perfectly. Even when you make a mistake, you can take that mistake and turn it into something else. I just look at people's tendencies and their cadence and think, okay, this person really loves this move. Everybody tells them don't do this, but he always do it. So I think, okay, I am not going to be the person not to tell you to do it. Let's see how we can perfect it to make it work in your favor."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6A9YYAhb_kw&t=384s

**6. God Shammgod — "Kyrie loves dribbling in traffic. Sometimes when you get at the elbow you catch it." — Teaching Kyrie Irving to dribble in traffic** [08:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6A9YYAhb_kw&t=486s)

God Shammgod: "**Kyrie Irving** loves dribbling in traffic. Sometimes when you get at the elbow you catch it, if you got **Vince Carter** on one wing and you got **Tracy McGrady** on one wing, and they are in the gaps, the greatest as you could dribble, it is hard to get. So how about back up? Because if you back up, the person got to come up, now the court opens up. Now your rear attack. That little adjustment takes that from this to this. I have always been big on detail, detail, details. Vince knows we are in the McDonald's. Everybody be like, oh, can you show me how to dribble? So it wasn't just, oh I can show a point guard how to dribble. Yeah, I can show **Tracy McGrady** how to dribble, but he don't have to dribble like the point guard. Because the person guarding him is going to be his size, so he don't have to be to the floor and dribble like Shammgod. It is just a simple drop cross move, you will get it every time because the person guarding you is 6'9". You don't have to go between the leg and all that stuff like that."

**7. God Shammgod — "You have to give them what they want to get what you want." — Developing counter-moves by building on a player's favorite move** [10:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6A9YYAhb_kw&t=603s)

God Shammgod: "You have to give them what they want to get what you want. If you show me your favorite move, I can be like, okay, cool. You show me your favorite move, I can show you 10 alter moves to that same move. So I am not telling you not to do that move. I am just telling you, by you doing this move, this what comes after that move. You might just say I do this move then I cross, and I am like, okay, so you do that move, how about you double cross? How about you cross to a spin? How about you cross to an in-and-out and keep going the same way?"
