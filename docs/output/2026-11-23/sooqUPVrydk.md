**3. Jon Krawczynski — "You can start making the case for Jaden McDaniels All-Star on a team that is winning." — Jaden McDaniels' All-Star potential while Anthony Edwards is out** [14:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sooqUPVrydk&t=860s)

Jon Krawczynski: "You can start making the case for **Jaden McDaniels** All-Star on a team that is winning at a very unexpected level given that **Anthony Edwards** has been out for most of the season. They are nine and four right now with **Anthony Edwards** out, and one of the biggest reasons that they are still right near the top of the Western Conference is **Jaden McDaniels** has been incredible on both ends of the floor. He is not always just coming from scoring. He had 25 points the other night to help them beat Memphis over the weekend, but he is doing it by almost not doing too much. It would be easy for him to say, 'Okay, **Anthony Edwards** is out, I'm taking all of the shots, and I'm just going to get 30 whenever I can.' He's not doing that. What he is doing is playing within himself and in control, highly efficient basketball, but then also has been incredible defensively."

**4. Jon Krawczynski — "I felt like the shot allocation wasn't exactly where it should be. I thought that Jaden should get more shots." — Adjusting shot distribution between Jaden McDaniels and LaMelo Ball** [16:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sooqUPVrydk&t=996s)

**Jim Souhan:** "I did feel like, even though they've had success without Anthony Edwards, I felt like the shot allocation wasn't exactly where it should be. I thought that Jaden should get more shots and LaMelo should maybe force fewer shots, and it feels like that's what they want to move toward."

**Jon Krawczynski:** "It does, and I think that it's a little bit of a challenge for **Chris Finch** to implement that exact strategy. I agree with you, Jim. I do honestly think that maybe **Chris Finch**, at the start of the post-Anthony Edwards surgery era, one of the early messages that **Chris Finch** was really adamant in putting out there is, 'Look, while Anthony Edwards is out, we need **LaMelo Ball** to be more aggressive, looking for his own shot.' And that's something that the Wolves did also with Julius Randle when he was first around, just saying, 'Julius, we need you to look for your own shot, be aggressive, don't be bashful about getting yours.' And I think in both cases, it might have gone a little bit too far to one side of it. In terms of LaMelo, he came out and was really aggressive, trying to find his own shot. The Chicago game in particular stood out as one where I think he was four for 19 from three, and it's like, 'Okay, we want you to be aggressive, but when it's not falling, let's be a little bit more of a playmaker.' And Julius became a little bit more of a playmaker as he settled into a role there."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sooqUPVrydk&t=996s
