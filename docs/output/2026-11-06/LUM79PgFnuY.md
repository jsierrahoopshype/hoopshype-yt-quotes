Those numbers have ticked down this season, so I wanted to see what was all about. I tune in, and what do I get? I get Apex Jokic. **2. Chris Mannix — "His scoring is down more than four points per game from last season." — Nikola Jokic's statistical regression in scoring and shooting efficiency** [04:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LUM79PgFnuY&t=249s)

His field goal percentage is down seven points. His three-point percentage is down 16 points. He is shooting the low 20s from three-point range this season, and his rebounding numbers across the board have fallen off."

**3. Evan Turner — "I am wondering, has that knee gotten better? Because last year he was balling out of control." — Evaluating Nikola Jokic's knee injury and potential impact on play** [04:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LUM79PgFnuY&t=288s)

Evan Turner: "I don't want to say that we have seen the end of Jokic. It has only been eight games. But I am wondering, has that knee gotten better? Because last year he was balling out of control. It was a knee hyperextension or something along those lines at the end of December. Bone bruises are killers. They bother you for a long, long time and they take a while to heal on their own. I feel as though prior to that, he was balling outside of his mind, literally out of control, and I think when he came back he is still trying to find his footing, find his grounding."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LUM79PgFnuY&t=288s

**4. Chris Mannix — "They are 24th in the league in defensive rating. That is very bad." — Denver Nuggets' defensive struggles and lack of modern defensive efficiency** [11:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LUM79PgFnuY&t=673s)

Chris Mannix: "The Denver Nuggets have the sixth-best offensive rating in the NBA, that is good, at least fine. They are 24th in the league in defensive rating. That is very bad. You are not going to win a lot of games. They are not a modern offense. They are top 10 in the NBA in three-point percentage, but they are bottom five in attempts. They have a seven-attempt disadvantage against their opponents this season. Their defensive rebounding numbers have been completely off from last season. Last season they pulled down 75% of their defensive rebounds, that was in the top five. This year it is right around 68%, which puts them in the bottom third. It began with **Caldwell-Pope** and **Bruce Brown**, it continued over the years, **Peyton Watson** is the most recent loss that they have suffered, **Tim Hardaway Jr.**, which just cost their bench."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LUM79PgFnuY&t=673s

There is some flashing red warning signs with the Denver Nuggets right now."

**9. Evan Turner — "The team that I am most disappointed in, that you probably aren't shocked about, is the Miami Heat." — Miami Heat's disappointing performance and loss of team identity** [32:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LUM79PgFnuY&t=1953s)

I remember having conversations you and I being like, nah, I am going to give them the benefit of the doubt. I think sometimes when it comes down to it, the successor to the presence of Giannis, I feel as though has withdrawn from the Heat culture and what the Heat had on, which is great ball movement and quote-unquote defense.
