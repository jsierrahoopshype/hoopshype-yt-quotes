**1. Carmelo Anthony — "the off-seasons are not the same no more. I think it's a lot more going into the off-season" — Carmelo Anthony on how modern NBA off-seasons have changed** [00:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k0ns1p-xvhE&t=4s)

Carmelo Anthony: "I mean, I just think that the off-seasons are not the same no more. I think it's a lot more going into the off-season, from a player side. You are working on your game, you are working on your body, you are working on your brain. It's like you are working on all of these things in the off-season. So by the time you go from working out in the gym, individual workouts, and the training that you do to just jump-start you, that training is just to jump-start you to the season. That training is to get you going, like first week, first month, first two months. That's what them off-season workouts for. Then you got to keep up on them workouts. Because if you just relying on your off-season workouts in December, in January, and February, nah. What are the work that you are doing on a day-to-day basis to keep your body up? So the off-seasons is a lot different today."
