**2. Kendrick Perkins — "Cleveland is missing something that LeBron is bringing, one of the things that he is bringing, leadership." — LeBron James brings essential leadership to the 76ers** [05:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mpkTWsnp42Q&t=311s)

Kendrick Perkins: "Cleveland is missing something that LeBron is bringing, one of the things that he is bringing, leadership. We can look at Cleveland right now and ask, it is almost like it is Christmas Day as a kid. You go there, you see this big pretty box, it is a bow on top, and you are like, man, I know it is good up in there. I know my parents, grandparents took good care of me. And all of a sudden you open it, and it is some long johns and some Kleenex. I am expecting a PlayStation. That is the Cleveland Cavaliers, right? They got all the pieces, but for some reason they cannot put it together because there is not a leader in there."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mpkTWsnp42Q&t=311s

Maxey took a step forward in his confidence level and leadership."

**4. Kendrick Perkins — "When are we going to see him take that Anthony Davis type leap?" — Evan Mobley needs to be unlocked as a primary option** [07:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mpkTWsnp42Q&t=474s)
