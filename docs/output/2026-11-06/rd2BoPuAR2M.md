# Olivia Miles Describes How It Feels To Guard Caitlin Clark — *The Old Man and The Three*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rd2BoPuAR2M

_Speakers identified: Olivia Miles, JJ Redick_

**1. Olivia Miles — "she can't be guarded" — Olivia Miles on the difficulty of guarding Caitlin Clark** [00:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rd2BoPuAR2M&t=3s)

Olivia Miles: "We played the Fever the first time and **Caitlin Clark** was just killing us. She was killing us and I was like, she can't be guarded. That definitely was a welcome to the league moment where I was like, this is legit."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rd2BoPuAR2M&t=3s

**2. Olivia Miles — "It's hard to guard an efficient player because they're going to make tough shots" — Olivia Miles on the challenge of guarding efficient players** [00:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rd2BoPuAR2M&t=29s)

Olivia Miles: "It's hard to guard an efficient player because they're going to make tough shots. Having counters as a basketball player is the best thing you can do. I see a little bit of that in **Caitlin Clark** and also the quickness and speed and craftiness. The blend is just crazy."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rd2BoPuAR2M&t=29s

**3. Olivia Miles — "It is annoying to play against her, right? You got to guard her" — Olivia Miles on the playmaking abilities of Caitlin Clark and Paige Bueckers** [01:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rd2BoPuAR2M&t=75s)

Olivia Miles: "Obviously **Caitlin Clark** with the shooting and her playmaking ability. She's so good with her right hand attacking downhill and creating for others and obviously in transition she's deadly. It is annoying to play against her, right? You got to guard her and you got to make sure that you have bodies on her at all times and making it hard. And same for **Paige Bueckers**. Paige is great to her right hand and great posting up in the short corner and facilitating for her teammates as well."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rd2BoPuAR2M&t=75s

**4. Olivia Miles — "She's a different type of player in the sense of how surgical she is" — Olivia Miles on the surgical playmaking style of Chelsea Gray** [02:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rd2BoPuAR2M&t=132s)

Olivia Miles: "She's a different type of player in the sense of how surgical she is and how the timing and her knowing where her teammates are going to be before they do. I feel like we have a lot of similarities in that way, our processing. Even though she may not be the most athletic on the floor or the quickest at her age now, no disrespect, she's still so surgical in her movements."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rd2BoPuAR2M&t=132s

**5. Olivia Miles — "To have the longevity that they have and to win as well is so unheard of" — Olivia Miles on the longevity and success of Diana Taurasi** [03:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rd2BoPuAR2M&t=190s)

Olivia Miles: "She is multi-time champion, Olympics, World Cup. To have the longevity that they have and to win as well is so unheard of and it's so aspiring. I don't think I want to play for that long, but basketball is such a love of mine that I want to give it all I have and learn from the great. We talk about this with **LeBron James** as well."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rd2BoPuAR2M&t=190s

**6. Olivia Miles — "Growing up I definitely watched like the Maya Moore, Sue Bird, Stewie" — Olivia Miles on the players she watched growing up** [03:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rd2BoPuAR2M&t=227s)

I really watched the NBA and I watched **Jason Williams**, the **Jason Kidd**s, **Steve Nash**, **Stephen Curry**, all those guys. Kyrie was a big influence, obviously Jersey kid."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rd2BoPuAR2M&t=227s

**7. Olivia Miles — "Kyrie was a big influence, obviously Jersey kid" — Olivia Miles on the influence of Kyrie Irving and Steve Nash** [04:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rd2BoPuAR2M&t=287s)

Olivia Miles: "I watch the game because I love the game and I forgot to mention Kyrie. Kyrie was a big influence, obviously Jersey kid. Watching what they do, wanting to do crazy layups like that and handles and obviously **Steve Nash**'s passing. As a student of the game, you just love to learn things and pick up different things and I feel like I have a little bit of everything in my game from people that I've watched and then kind of put your own unique turn on it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rd2BoPuAR2M&t=287s
