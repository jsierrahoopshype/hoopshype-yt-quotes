**1. Josh Donaldson — "I had such a will to want to beat whoever was in front of me" — Josh Donaldson on his intense, competitive mindset during his youth** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XCKwI9HLSTU&t=0s)

Josh Donaldson: "I think from when I was younger, I always played intense. I had such a will to want to beat whoever was in front of me that I didn't care. Whatever it took, I was going to do. When you're working out, I was that guy that was doing, everybody's going to do 20, now I'm going to do 40. It just didn't matter. I was going to make sure that I was in better shape than everybody. There wasn't anybody that was going to outwork me."

**2. Josh Donaldson — "I would sit there and go in and hit 500 balls right-handed, 500 balls left-handed" — Josh Donaldson on his relentless training routine as a young athlete** [00:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XCKwI9HLSTU&t=38s)

Josh Donaldson: "During the summer when I was playing baseball, I had configured this net in my backyard. It was probably eight foot tall, probably five feet wide. I would sit there and go in and hit 500 balls right-handed, 500 balls left-handed. I'd go in the pool, I was doing laps in the pool. I was doing sit-ups because you hear **Herschel Walker**, those guys, they were doing 1,000 sit-ups a day and push-ups. I was doing all of that. And I'd go inside and I'd tell my mom, I just did 500 swings left-handed and right-handed. She's like, that's good. She's like, I bet there was somebody that did 501. I just wasn't satisfied with doing enough."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XCKwI9HLSTU&t=38s

**3. Josh Donaldson — "I never felt like what I had was enough and I felt like I needed more to get that" — Josh Donaldson on his insecurity and need to win** [01:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XCKwI9HLSTU&t=106s)

Josh Donaldson: "I never felt like what I had was enough and I felt like I needed more to get that. And it came across that way because I played with a certain intensity because I wanted to win. I always loved the **Michael Jordan** 'The Last Dance' documentary. Somebody said I took that personal. Before that was a thing, I was doing that. I tried to find something about the opposition and I made it very easy for them also to find something about me."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XCKwI9HLSTU&t=106s

**4. Josh Donaldson — "When you play as many games in baseball as you do, it gets monotonous" — Josh Donaldson on avoiding complacency in a long baseball season** [02:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XCKwI9HLSTU&t=141s)

Josh Donaldson: "When you play as many games in baseball as you do, it gets monotonous. It's very easy just to go into this almost like coma. You go through the motions is what we would call it. For me to stay away from that, I had to find something. I tried to find something about the opposition and I made it very easy for them also to find something about me."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XCKwI9HLSTU&t=141s

**5. Josh Donaldson — "They say that you can't flip a switch, but I had a switch for sure" — Josh Donaldson on the ability to flip a switch before games** [03:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XCKwI9HLSTU&t=204s)

Josh Donaldson: "Before the game, it was just you're trying to, they say that you can't flip a switch, but I had a switch for sure. Especially early in my career, I could get away with it more than in the end because everybody kind of knew me at the end of my career. They knew that I was a fun guy. We're over here razing each other, we're talking crap to each other. Early on, it was easy for me to be that fun-loving teammate, camaraderie guy, and then once the game showed up, switch. I don't care who is out there. I'm here to let you know that I'm here and I'm winning."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XCKwI9HLSTU&t=204s

**6. Josh Donaldson — "Johnny Gomes was one of my teammates and he was somewhat of a mentor to me" — Josh Donaldson on Johnny Gomes' mentorship and perspective** [05:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XCKwI9HLSTU&t=337s)

Josh Donaldson: "The last time I got recalled, **Johnny Gomes** was one of my teammates and he was somewhat of a mentor to me. I don't know if he was trying to be, but I kind of gravitated towards him a little bit. I remember I'd hit a home run or something like that and I was like, dude, did I pimp that too hard? I don't want to piss anybody off. He looked me in the eye and he said, you know how hard you worked to hit a home run in the big leagues? I said, yeah, actually I do. And he's like, it's okay for you to enjoy it. If you go excessive on it, I'll let you know."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XCKwI9HLSTU&t=337s
