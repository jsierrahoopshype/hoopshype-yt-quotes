# What do the Cavs NEED TO DO to take Game 3? 👀 Windy breaks it down... | NBA Today — *NBA on ESPN*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H7L_CMkV5cA

_Speakers identified: Brian Windhorst, Kendrick Perkins_

**1. "During their nine-game winning streak, they've outscored opponents by 212 points." — Knicks' historic scoring differential in 9-game win streak** [00:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H7L_CMkV5cA&t=21s)

"MSG has been rocking this postseason. The **Knicks**, they haven't given the New York faithful a lot of reason to slow down here, right? During their nine-game winning streak, they've outscored opponents by 212 points. It's the most in any nine-game stretch in NBA postseason history."

**2. Brian Windhorst — "Their defense isn't good enough to win a game that's in the 90s or the low 100s." — Cavaliers need offense and shooting to win Game 3** [02:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H7L_CMkV5cA&t=163s)

"They need some offense. They need some shooting. They're shooting about 30% from three points in this series. And look, I understand that you should be able to survive a bad shooting night, but the reality is they can't. Their defense isn't good enough to win a game that's in the 90s or the low 100s. They need to get up into the 100 teens, ideally the 120s. And I know everybody's all focused on **Harden** versus **Brunson**, and I agree. It hasn't looked great defensively, but **Harden**, the bargain with **Harden** is what he can do at the other end. He's got to start living up to that at the balance at the other end."

**3. Kendrick Perkins — "If you give a damn, if you're trying to really get somewhere, if you got personal pride." — Kendrick Perkins recounts personal 0-2 playoff experience and advice.** [03:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H7L_CMkV5cA&t=194s)

"You got to have the conversation in your head and in your heart. You got to care first. We was down 0-2 against San Antonio Spurs 2012, and I remember I was getting cooked in the pick and roll by **Tony Parker** and **Ginobili**. We was in the drop coverage. And I used to always go back home and watch the games when we land because I wanted to hear what was said about me. At the time it was **Steve Kerr** who was doing the play-by-play analyst, and I remember him saying, 'Oh, they need to bench **Perk** and go small with **Serge Ibaka** and switch all the pick and rolls.' The junk pissed me off. I took it personal. We came out, we switched in Game 3, I locked up **Tony Parker** and **Ginobili** on the switch in the first quarter, and then I looked over there and **Steve Kerr** in the timeout and was talking noise to him and **Reggie Miller**, and he said they was talking about it because that's what's supposed to fuel you. If you give a damn, if you're trying to really get somewhere, if you got personal pride, then come with it. That's where it starts. I ain't got no blueprint for them. Act like you care first."
