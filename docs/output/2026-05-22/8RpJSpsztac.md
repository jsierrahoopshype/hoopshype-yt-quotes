**4. Brian Windhorst — "This is what really good teams do, beat you in different ways." — Brian Windhorst on Knicks' versatility and Josh Hart's Game 2 impact** [07:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8RpJSpsztac&t=476s)

"So this Knicks team really does have a lot going for it. They are not a perfect team, they were not the best team in the NBA during the regular season, but they have a lot going for it. And one of the things that they can do is play different ways. They have different players who can do different things. And so **Jalen Brunson** crushes the Cavs in Game 1, so the Cavs come back naturally and try to clamp down on him. They totally don't guard **Josh Hart**. And this was one of the reasons why he got benched at the end of Game 1 because the Knicks weren't surviving well when they were going four on five. This game, they go to him and he crushes the Cavs with an all-time terrific performance from him. Not only with the 26 points, but also with seven assists. So not only did Brunson end up with 14 assists, one of his best assist games ever, but he got a bunch of hockey assists because he was kicking it to Hart. **Mikal** would scramble to him and he would kick it onward. This is what really good teams do, what championship teams do, beat you in different ways."

**6. Brian Windhorst — "what's happening now is going to be celebrated for a long time." — Brian Windhorst on Knicks' multi-year team building and fan connection** [10:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8RpJSpsztac&t=623s)

"So this isn't just about this year. This fanbase started falling in love with this team two years ago when they made that first playoff run. And then last year when they upset the Celtics. And then really this is the culmination of a multi-year stage to get close to it. And the city loves the way they play. This is not a mercenary team. They have built this team methodically. They have their 10,000 hours in with this group. They've watched them have a bunch of comeback victories. They've got four 20-point comeback wins in the playoffs in the last two years. All of that has built this incredible faith in this roster. You feel the connection in the building, you feel the connection in the city. This is something look, around here they talk about 1994 and grown men get tears in their eyes and look up to the ceiling. That's building with this unit. I don't know if they're going to win it, but you can feel that what's happening now is going to be celebrated for a long time. **Josh Hart** and **Jalen Brunson** and **Mikal Bridges** are going to be sitting courtside here in 20 years, no matter how this season ends up."
