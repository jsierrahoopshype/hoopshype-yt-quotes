# Josh Smith & Lou Williams Talk Hawks, Atlanta Basketball, & ATL Mt. Rushmore — *Run It Back*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TVnxOhcZ2_8

_Speakers identified: Michelle Beadle, Lou Williams, Josh Smith, Chandler Parsons_

**1. Josh Smith — "Just being able to like have a Christmas where your mom, your dad, everybody's opening gifts and stuff like that." — Josh Smith's emotional 'Welcome to the NBA' moment** [18:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TVnxOhcZ2_8&t=1083s)

"Seeing a Christmas tree in my house for the first time. You know, when I was a kid, we knew Santa wasn't real. Presents wasn't on the tree. No tree, no presents. So, just being able to like have a Christmas where your mom, your dad, everybody's opening gifts and stuff like that, that was probably my welcome to the NBA moment. When I was able to do things like that, you know what I mean? Really experience a Christmas."

**2. Josh Smith — "I don't know how to like obsess that scenario. You know what I mean? Because it starts to kick in after a while." — Josh Smith on getting snubbed from All-Star games** [16:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TVnxOhcZ2_8&t=993s)

"Absolutely. There's guys that were averaging 10, 12 points that got All-Star nods, but one year I think that kind of stood out the most was like after the lockout year. Joe got hurt and Al got hurt that year and I helped lead the team to like third in the East and I still didn't get an All-Star nod. That's when I was like, man, I don't know what's going on. I don't know what more I could do. I don't know how to like obsess that scenario. You know what I mean? Because it starts to kick in after a while. You know what I mean, when you have eight years of all that, like how? A lot of people say this and that and the third about the NBA, the NFL, MLB, but when it comes to like individual accolades, I kind of feel like they kind of like guide people's careers when it comes to those type of things."

**3. Josh Smith — "James and Dwight were on that Shaq and Kobe type shit. We had to navigate that." — Josh Smith on Rockets' dynamic with Dwight Howard and James Harden** [20:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TVnxOhcZ2_8&t=1255s)

"I did, man. I really thought that that was the shot to do it. When I came there, I think James and Dwight were on that Shaq and Kobe type shit. You know, so we had to like navigate that. You know what I mean? **Jason Terry** had **James Harden** and I had Dwight, so we were like doctor filling the situation. I think what really happened when **Patrick Beverley** got injured, it kind of hurt us a lot too because he was a younger point guard, great defensive point guard and he was a guy that really kind of like got us in our sets too as well. When he got hurt, no pun intended to **Pablo Prigioni** and **Jason Terry**, but they were like 40 years old. We were going into the Western Conference and asking them to play super big minutes against **Stephen Curry** and all those guys and he was just running around with it, six, seven miles a game, just running out pick and rolls. That was probably the main reason why we kind of struggled in that Western Conference Finals."

**4. Josh Smith — "We have so much time in the NBA, what are you working on?" — NBA players must work on game, question trainer effectiveness** [14:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TVnxOhcZ2_8&t=855s)

"My whole thing is, we have so much time in the NBA, what are you working on? You came into the league and we knew that you could be super athletic, you were high-level defenders. What are you working on? This is what I tell my players that I coach. It's like, you all just be paying these trainers, what are they helping you all really do? Are they helping you all get your game better or not?"

**5. Josh Smith — "These guys are getting paid so much and to average five points is crazy." — Players must be realistic about development, averaging five points is crazy** [14:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TVnxOhcZ2_8&t=855s)

"I don't mind the shooting missing threes because it makes my percentage look better, so keep shooting those threes. I'm not in the conversation anymore, so keep shooting those threes. In all seriousness though, they can develop their games. They just got to be realistic with what they want to accomplish because it's going to be hard. These guys are getting paid so much and to average five points is crazy."

**6. Josh Smith — "A wing player, you're a wing player and they're guarding you inside the paint." — Wing players must develop to avoid clogging paint for guards** [14:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TVnxOhcZ2_8&t=855s)

"You got to see, as far as an individual standpoint, you got to be real with yourself about what you need to work on. A wing player, you're a wing player and they're guarding you inside the paint. How is that going to be? It's hard for the other guards to be able to make great things happen for the floor if somebody is just in the paint the whole time on the weak side."

**7. Josh Smith — "Him being able to beat a team like that who has probably five, six guys that were going Division 1." — Josh Smith on Lou Williams' high school talent** [2:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TVnxOhcZ2_8&t=142s)

"He beat Oak Hill when he was in high school his senior year. He was at a regular high school and you know how Oak Hill is, they do a lot of recruiting and guys come up there and basically begging to come up there sometimes back in the day. He went up there and took care of that business. So, him being able to beat a team like that who has probably five, six guys that were going Division 1, he was probably the only one, him and **Mike Mercer**, that was going Division 1 and they, them beating prep schools lets you know how cold he was."

**8. Josh Smith — "I can't stunt my growth at this school, so I had to go to Oak Hill." — Josh Smith on his decision to go to Oak Hill** [6:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TVnxOhcZ2_8&t=365s)

"You know, we coming back to school, like you just said, you come back to school, you went to LA, you went to all these different places in the summer. It's ranking you, so you don't want to lose that momentum of the summer. You get what I'm saying? My coach wasn't really seeing eye to eye on my development process and I was like top five in the country. We were getting Adidas shoes, we were going to showcase games my junior year and I knew it was all because of me, but I still was humble in how I acted and handled it. He was just fighting with me on the position wise and then I was like, man, I can't stunt my growth at this school, so I had to go to Oak Hill where we were playing everybody. We were going to Barcelona, Spain, we were going to LA, we were going to New York, we were going to New Jersey. We were playing a lot of teams that were outside of the country and outside of the state of Virginia to give me what I needed in order to make it, in order to be ready for that next level."
