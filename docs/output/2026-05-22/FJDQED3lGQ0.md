_Speakers identified: Jay Williams, Alan Hahn, Mike Greenberg, Brian Windhorst, Joe Fortenbaugh, Josh Hart, Karl-Anthony Towns_

**1. Brian Windhorst — "They are a multi-faceted beast. And this is what you have to have to have a championship team." — Knicks are a very well-rounded, confident, and strong team** [3:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FJDQED3lGQ0&t=223s)

"This is a very well-rounded, very confident, very strong team. You watch how they've gone through these playoffs, you continue to be impressed. They've played 12 games, they've won 10 of them. The two they lost were by a single point that came down to the final possession. You've seen them win with **Karl Towns** running their offense, you've seen them win with **Jalen Brunson** running their offense. You've seen in this series them win taking **Josh Hart** off the floor because he can't make shots. In this game, they leave him on the floor and he crushes the Cavs with his shot-making. They are a multi-faceted beast. And this is what you have to have to have a championship team."

**2. Alan Hahn — "This is not something people are used to here. Normally, May is reserved for the Knicks trying to win the lottery." — Jalen Brunson has elevated the Knicks' standard of play** [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FJDQED3lGQ0&t=300s)

"This is not something people are used to here. Normally, May is reserved for the Knicks trying to win the lottery. That had been the thing and by the way, they never won that either. So that has been sort of the story of this franchise. Since **Jalen Brunson** got here, the standard of play, the standard of expectations has gone up year after year. It has built. They have had to go through the calisthenics of losing in the playoffs. But when you start with a guy who's got a winning pedigree, what he did at Villanova, two-time champion, player of the year, Josh Hart also, Mikal Bridges also. That culture has permeated this franchise to a point now where they are two wins away from the NBA Finals for the first time since 1999."

**3. Jay Williams — "They're a championship contender. Mikal Bridges carried the load in the first half as well." — Josh Hart's versatility makes the Knicks championship contenders** [5:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FJDQED3lGQ0&t=349s)

"They're a championship contender. **Mikal Bridges** carried the load in the first half as well. I mean, look, if you're a Knick fan, you're saying let's go to Cleveland and get one. Like we get one, we come back, let's close it out at home, right? And if you think about the way this is lining up, let's say Spurs and OKC go to six, they go to seven, and they beat the hell out of each other. And then you get a scenario in which you're playing in the NBA Championship, a team is tired, you have a fighter's chance, right?"

**4. Jay Williams — "I not only think that Jalen Brunson has been the best player in this playoffs because he's 6'2"..." — Jay Williams believes Jalen Brunson could be greatest Knick ever.** [06:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FJDQED3lGQ0&t=377s)

"I will go back to saying this because I said it a couple weeks ago, it was the first time I ever said in my time here at ESPN, I believe the Knicks can win a championship. Two years ago, Alan, you and I were on this show and I said to you when they were playing, I was like, hey man, it's turning in this direction. I know that you have to win a championship to do so, but I not only think that **Jalen Brunson** has been the best player in this playoffs because he's 6'2" and the difficulty in doing that at that size and that frame compared to the 7'4" alien on the Spurs. But if he won a championship, I said to you, I think he has a chance to be the greatest Knick ever. It's not only the way he does, it's his leadership, it's everything. 14 assists last night, 28 plus points the night before, he just has a variety of different ways of showing up and his toughness speaks for itself. He has a chance to trend in that direction."

**5. Alan Hahn — "That one cup had greater value than the previous three that the franchise had won back when it was a six-team league." — Alan Hahn on Brunson's legacy and a Knicks championship's historical value.** [06:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FJDQED3lGQ0&t=377s)

"He's played his way into the conversation already without a ring on his finger, but to surpass the first two names, you've got to get one because, honestly, in context, that one, 53 years we're talking about here. That one in the same building **Mark Messier** did this. **Mark Messier** walked in, 54 years, ended it, right? That one cup had greater value than the previous three that the franchise had won back when it was a six-team league. So the one can be the difference maker for anybody because of the context of the history of how long it took and what it would mean. So I do think it definitely becomes part of that conversation, but you still got to do it."

**6. Brian Windhorst — "Mike Brown has done his best coaching as an adjuster within playoff series." — Mike Brown's playoff adjustment coaching and experience with winning streaks.** [8:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FJDQED3lGQ0&t=490s)

"I think one of the things that **Mike Brown** has done in his career, he has done his best coaching as an adjuster within playoff series. He is, and I know he's been fired quite a few times, but you go back and look at the playoff series that he's been involved in, either as a head coach or an assistant coach, there have been series where you've seen stuff like this happen before. He said something last night that made me sit back in my seat. He was like, earlier in my career when I was head coaching a team that won 10 straight playoff games. Don't forget, he coached the **Warriors** when **Steve Kerr** was out with the back issue and he coached them to 11 straight wins. So he's actually a guy who's dealt with, he can actually say, listen, when you've won 10 games in a row in the playoffs, you have to do A, B and C. He was saying that to the team last night."

**7. Brian Windhorst — "He went in there and said, guys, we should be down 0-1." — Mike Brown's pre-game message to the team and Knicks' improved play.** [8:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FJDQED3lGQ0&t=490s)

"They had a meeting before the game. They all got together, they gave him some time off, they had a shoot around like two hours before the game. He went in there and said, guys, we should be down 0-1. We should be down 0-1, we got away with it. We are not going to be able to come out and give the same effort tonight. That was what got the **Knicks** into a much smoother rhythm. I thought they played a much cleaner game last night."

**8. Joe Fortenbaugh — "Cleveland on the road this postseason has been an abject disaster. But at home, they've been fantastic." — Cleveland Cavaliers' home court advantage and betting outlook for Game 3** [09:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FJDQED3lGQ0&t=587s)

"Odd to say everything we just said and then say **Cavs** minus 2.5. I would be playing the **Cavs** here. We saw this before. I think you can play them in the first half. I think you play them on the game line and then that team total of 107.5. They shot 39% last night. **Knicks** shot 52%. I think one comes down, I think one goes up and that's the difference."
