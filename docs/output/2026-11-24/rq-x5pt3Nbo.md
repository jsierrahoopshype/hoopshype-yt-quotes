**5. Lionel Hollins — "I yell, but also hug. I also talk." — Lionel Hollins on his coaching style and communication** [04:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rq-x5pt3Nbo&t=248s)

Lionel Hollins: "I yell, but also hug. I also talk. You and Zach were going through the jogging up and down at start of practice and I just said, yo, is that the fastest you can do that? Zach said, nah. And so y'all started running. I didn't yell at him and I didn't yell at you because if I yell, y'all would be all over me. But what I told Zach one time, he may not remember, I said, Zach, I know there's days you don't want to practice. I don't know why, but I know there's days that you don't want to practice. And I said, if you don't want to practice, just tell me. Because Z-Bo was way up here. I could give Z-Bo a day off and not feel bad about it. And so, I said, but if you practice, you got to practice hard. You don't mess up my practice."
