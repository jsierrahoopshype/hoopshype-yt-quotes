**1. Davion Mitchell — "we're going to call you Off Night" — Davion Mitchell explains the origin of his 'Off Night' nickname** [00:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evPnIuTlJ2k&t=35s)

Davion Mitchell: "Shout out to three of my closest friends. They were GAs with me at Baylor: Rim, Peyton, and Matt Gray. Rim is at Texas Tech, Peyton is a coach there, and Matt Gray is working with the G League in Denver. They came up with the name Off Night, which is me guarding the top guards on our lead, and they weren't having the usual nights that they have every day. I was in the film room with them, and we were just chatting, and he was like, 'Yo, we're going to call you Off Night.' It just ran with it, and it's crazy that that simple conversation made the nickname into now the NBA and everyone knows it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evPnIuTlJ2k&t=35s

**2. Davion Mitchell — "playing with someone great like that, everything is going to change" — Davion Mitchell on the challenge of playing with Jimmy Butler** [02:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evPnIuTlJ2k&t=169s)

Davion Mitchell: "Playing with someone great like that, everything is going to change. Especially with a guy that can do everything on the floor, who can post up, who can play point guard, who can play the pick and roll, who can have the ball playing the pick and roll. He can do everything. Sometimes I got stuck just watching, being like, 'Oh snap, he can do this, he can do this.' You kind of let him do everything and then not realizing that you don't get a rhythm like that. You don't get a rhythm just watching. You get a rhythm just swinging the ball, just at least initiating the offense. At first, I think we had to realize that for us to be good, we all got to have this rhythm."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evPnIuTlJ2k&t=169s

**3. Kendrick Perkins — "it was the change of culture, right? It was the organization in Oklahoma City" — Kendrick Perkins on the cultural shift after being traded to OKC** [04:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evPnIuTlJ2k&t=267s)

Kendrick Perkins: "When I got traded from Boston to OKC, we went from chocolate chip cookies and ice cream dessert on the plane to it was nothing but bananas and apples and oranges. It was the change of culture, right? It was the organization in Oklahoma City. I have never taken a body fat test where they actually put you under a scanner and it takes an hour. It scans your organs and everything, so it wasn't the squeeze. So it was the change of culture."

**5. Davion Mitchell — "I would say like the person that I previously played, I would probably say Jrue Holiday" — Davion Mitchell names Jrue Holiday as the toughest defender he's faced** [08:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evPnIuTlJ2k&t=506s)

Davion Mitchell: "I would say like the person that I previously played, I would probably say **Jrue Holiday**. He's like one of those guys where, when I was bringing the ball up, usually I don't have a problem bringing the ball up. I'm like, 'All right, this dude ain't about to put pressure on me, I'm going to just run by him, I'm faster than him.' But with Jrue, like he's like such a smart defender. He jump into the move before I am even thinking about it. It's to a point where I'm like, 'Bro, let me get off this.' I've never been a guy like, 'All right, let me get off this.'"

**6. Davion Mitchell — "I said in the summer, like, I think we're already champions" — Davion Mitchell on the Heat's championship mentality for the NBA Cup** [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evPnIuTlJ2k&t=810s)

Davion Mitchell: "I mean, our goal is to get better each game. I don't know if we look at the cup as like a different game. I think we just, I mean, obviously we just try to win every game. And obviously towards the end of the game if we're up, we try to keep putting points on the board. But I think it's for us, like, not to kind of like look down on the cup, but we're trying to look at something bigger. And we know what version we have to get to to get there. So I think it's game is like, 'All right, we're going to get 1% better each game.' I said in the summer, like, I think we're already champions. We already have what we need."
