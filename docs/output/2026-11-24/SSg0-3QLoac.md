# Celtics BAD Road Trip // Sixers Check-In // Injured Wolves Keep Winning | Chris Mannix & Evan Turner — *Sports Illustrated*

_Speakers identified: Chris Mannix, Evan Turner_

**1. Chris Mannix — "The offense has completely cratered around Jason Tatum." — Celtics' offensive struggles during their four-game West Coast road trip** [02:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SSg0-3QLoac&t=144s)

Chris Mannix: "The Celtics have fallen off a cliff during this four-game West Coast road trip. They are 0-4, with losses to Golden State without Steph Curry, and Sacramento, which is generally bad. The offense has completely cratered around **Jason Tatum**. **Paul George**'s numbers have dipped, **Payton Pritchard**'s numbers have fallen off, and **Derrick White** still cannot buy a bucket from beyond the three-point line."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SSg0-3QLoac&t=144s

**2. Evan Turner — "You live by the three, you die by the three, and I think that's the philosophy." — Celtics' reliance on three-point shooting despite recent poor performance** [02:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SSg0-3QLoac&t=178s)

**Evan Turner:** "They are aware, but I don't think they are really concerned because it goes back to that age-old adage of, you live by the three, you die by the three, and I think that's the philosophy for the Boston Celtics. You look at the past two games, they have only lost by a total of two points. Of course, you want to win in Denver, and Denver has been playing at a high level. And then you caught a hot Magic team. **Franz Wagner** is playing unbelievable. But I think one thing that occurs is you pay attention to their defensive rating, and they are still doing enough to stop guys. They are still doing enough to stop teams and doing enough to win, but it is all about, do you want to be more versatile offensively?"

**Chris Mannix:** "It has always been a complaint of guys shooting too many threes. I think they are hoisting 51 threes per game during that four-game skid, and shooting probably around 28%. So, I think if you went into that locker room, you would bet on your guys stepping up and making shots as opposed to them not making shots. **Payton Pritchard** did not have a good few games. You mentioned Paul George kind of dipping, and they are relying so heavily on **Jason Tatum** to playmake. But at the same time, I believe those other two guys will show up, but definitely need to see something more than **Derrick White**. What do you think? Do you think it is the team's situation, or is it just, hey, live by the three, die by the three? And would you change it?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SSg0-3QLoac&t=178s

**3. Chris Mannix — "Derrick White's shooting is not a short-term thing. He has been in steady decline over the last year." — Concerns regarding Derrick White's shooting slump and its impact on the Celtics** [05:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SSg0-3QLoac&t=326s)

Chris Mannix: "I am concerned about **Derrick White**. **Derrick White**'s shooting is not a short-term thing. He has been in steady decline over the last year and a half. That is concerning to me. I think **Payton Pritchard** will figure it out, and they need him to figure it out because he is a shot creator for that team. But **Derrick White**'s shooting numbers, that is a real problem for this team because as good as he is defensively, and as solid as he is as a playmaker, he has to make open shots. If he is open beyond the three-point line and he cannot be shooting sub-30%, that to me is a real concern."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SSg0-3QLoac&t=326s

**4. Evan Turner — "His confidence has been affected. He had at least two or three seconds to drive that ball." — Impact of Paul George's shooting slump on the Celtics' offensive rhythm** [06:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SSg0-3QLoac&t=363s)

Evan Turner: "I think his confidence has been affected. You look at the Sacramento game, he had at least two or three seconds to drive that ball, attack, and try to make a play. I feel as though, ever since last season, he has been trying to find his shot and kind of been inside his head. But it is so necessary because, like we said prior, you cannot rely on a 36, 37-year-old **Paul George** to be shooting and playing out of his mind, averaging mid-20s. He has been a great 3-and-D guy. He will be a great third option, but I think **Derrick White** definitely has to step up in order for this team to be anything, space the floor, but at the same time, take pressure off **Jason Tatum**."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SSg0-3QLoac&t=363s

**5. Chris Mannix — "Teams are going to continue to do this to Tatum. They are going to continue to pressure him." — Opposing teams' defensive strategies to pressure Jayson Tatum** [07:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SSg0-3QLoac&t=424s)

Chris Mannix: "Teams are going to continue to do this to Tatum. They are going to continue to pressure him, to blitz him, to send double teams at him. And if you look at some of the numbers, it is having an effect. In his first 12 games, Tatum averaged 7.3 assists per game, a very good number. In the last three, he is down to 4.3. Turnovers, first 12 games, 2.7. Last three, five. Assist-to-turnover ratio, obviously not as good. 2.7 the first 12 games, less than one, 0.86 over the last three. Teams are just going to try everything they can to take the ball out of Tatum's hands. And if **Derrick White** is not making shots, if Pritchard is not making shots, if **Paul George** is not making shots, if **Sam Hauser** is not making shots, they are going to keep doing it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SSg0-3QLoac&t=424s

**6. Chris Mannix — "Joel Embiid, not the same guy overall in terms of offense, but defensively he's getting the job done." — State of the 76ers and Joel Embiid's defensive impact** [12:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SSg0-3QLoac&t=764s)

Chris Mannix: "Let's do a state of the 76ers check-in here. Right now, they are 14-4. If you asked anybody in the 76ers front office if they could be 14-4 around Thanksgiving, they would be pretty ecstatic. **Joel Embiid**, not the same guy overall in terms of offense, but defensively he is getting the job done. He has been a stopper for them. Their defensive rating with Embiid is around 98. With Embiid off the floor, it is about 112. So, he has been a difference-maker for that team."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SSg0-3QLoac&t=764s

**7. Chris Mannix — "They are 10-0 in clutch games this season. So, that's been good." — 76ers' success in clutch situations and their offensive/defensive ratings** [13:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SSg0-3QLoac&t=807s)

Chris Mannix: "For all the talk about who is going to take the last shot, would there be dysfunction there, they are 10-0 in clutch games this season. So, that's been good. The analytic numbers, the deeper dive into them, not necessarily good. They are 10th in the NBA in offensive rating, that's good but not great. They are 10th in the NBA in defensive rating, good but not great. 12th in the NBA in net rating, good but not great."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SSg0-3QLoac&t=807s

**8. Evan Turner — "I like everything they're doing to be completely honest with you. I like how they're aware of each other." — Evan Turner's positive assessment of the 76ers' team chemistry and play** [14:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SSg0-3QLoac&t=882s)

**Evan Turner:** "I see glass half full. I actually like everything they're doing to be completely honest with you. I like how they're aware of each other. They're trying to make it work. There's been certain games where you'll sit there and it's not always beautiful, but you're like, bro, they're moving the ball around beautifully. They're making sure everybody's getting touches. I feel as though even how **V.J. Edgecombe** is playing. I feel like that that camaraderie is kind of moving towards togetherness. There was a moment where **Joel Embiid** was kind of like, I don't know what's going on here, there, but I think that kind of got cleaned up. And I like where they're at personally."

**Chris Mannix:** "I mentioned the decline of Derrick White to keep it focused on him for a second. 23-24, the year the Celtics won the championship, he shot 46% from the field, close to 40% from three. That was like a high-water mark for Derrick White. That's when it's like, all right, this guy is the best third or fourth option in the NBA. The following season, 44%, 38%. Still good. Still good numbers. Last season, 39.4 from the field, 32.7 from three. This year, so far, 41% from the field, 28.9% from three. So this has basically been a steady decline for four years now."
