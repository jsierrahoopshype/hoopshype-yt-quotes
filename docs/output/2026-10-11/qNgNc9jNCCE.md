# Lou Williams On Entering The NBA Draft Unprepared & Adjusting To Life As A Rookie — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qNgNc9jNCCE

_Speakers identified: Tracy McGrady, Lou Williams, Vince Carter_

**1. Lou Williams — "that was the first time I thought to myself, oh shit, I can do this." — Lou Williams on realizing he could be a pro prospect** [00:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qNgNc9jNCCE&t=56s)

Lou Williams: "We were at USA in Colorado training for Junior Olympics or World Games. I, Monta, CJ, Blatche, Martell Webster, Gerald Green were all the same class. We were watching the draft together and I remember it, at 13, **Sebastian Telfair** went. That was the first time I thought to myself, oh shit, I can do this. At that time, it was guys like you, Mac, that were already physically there. You are 6'7, 6'8, 6'9, y'all are built like men. That was the first time I saw a guy my size have that opportunity and I'm like, man, if the door is open for guys at this size and I think I am who I am, I'm out of here. I'm going."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qNgNc9jNCCE&t=56s

**Vince Carter:** "What information did you get from outside?"

**Lou Williams:** "Ain't nobody tell me nothing. I was very clear, T-Mac. Ain't nobody tell me nothing."

**3. Lou Williams — "I got picked 45 because I wasn't in shape, I wasn't ready, I didn't take the workouts serious." — Lou Williams on being drafted 45th due to lack of preparation** [03:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qNgNc9jNCCE&t=221s)

Lou Williams: "That's why I got picked 45 because I wasn't in shape, I wasn't ready, I didn't take the workouts serious. I just was going on being naive to think, hey look, if these guys can do it and I'm in the conversation of guys going, I want to be one of them too. I don't care where I get drafted, I'm going to the league. I don't want to do with school."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qNgNc9jNCCE&t=221s

**4. Lou Williams — "I'm going to look at this like a job and this is a career." — Lou Williams on treating his rookie season like an internship** [05:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qNgNc9jNCCE&t=310s)

Lou Williams: "The work that it takes, the discipline that it takes, some of the sacrifices, knowing that you can go play at the next level but this is a necessary step I got to take to get there, I didn't have none of that in my system. So I figured, look, I'm going to look at this like a job and this is a career. If I get drafted anywhere, if I get a foot in the door, it becomes an internship to me now."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qNgNc9jNCCE&t=310s

**5. Lou Williams — "The most challenging thing for me my rookie year was just to be able to find a role." — Lou Williams on the challenge of finding a role as a rookie** [08:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qNgNc9jNCCE&t=495s)

Lou Williams: "The most challenging thing for me my rookie year was just to be able to find a role, be able to find my place in being an 18-year-old kid, not knowing you're going to play, but knowing you're expected to show up, knowing you're expected to do all of the extra things. How do you stay motivated and how do I stick if I'm not going to play being so young?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qNgNc9jNCCE&t=495s

**6. Lou Williams — "You better get that ball to Kyle Korver, you better get that ball to Willie Green." — Lou Williams on Mo Cheeks' advice during his rookie season** [08:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qNgNc9jNCCE&t=538s)

Lou Williams: "**Mo Cheeks** was like, hey man, in practice, you better get that ball to **Kyle Korver**, you better get that ball to **Willie Green** and **John Salmons** and you better make sure them guys get shots. You stay out the way, young fella. He was training me to be another version of **Mo Cheeks**."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qNgNc9jNCCE&t=538s

**7. Lou Williams — "He said, man, I've been coaching you the wrong way this whole time." — Lou Williams on Mo Cheeks admitting he coached him wrong** [10:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qNgNc9jNCCE&t=614s)

Lou Williams: "Mo had called me to the back of the plane and I sit down with him and he's sitting there watching my high school highlights. He said, man, I've been coaching you the wrong way this whole time. From now on out, you go out there and you be yourself. Play your game."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qNgNc9jNCCE&t=614s
