**1. Jon Krawczynski — "the Timberwolves went into these last couple of weeks really needing Jonathan Kuminga" — Jonathan Kuminga's signing with the Timberwolves in free agency** [01:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fj1uM9S4f1w&t=109s)

**Jim Souhan:** "Do you want to start with the ownership, Kuminga, or is it all one big package?"

**Jon Krawczynski:** "It is all one big package that I think we can get to, but I just think that just to start, in one of the newsiest Augusts I can ever remember, for the Timberwolves to get a player like **Jonathan Kuminga** at a position of need while offering half as much money and one less year as the Los Angeles Lakers did in free agency, I just think that we cannot overstate how big that is. **Jonathan Kuminga** is not **LeBron James**, he is not an all-star level player at this point in his career, he has got a lot of questions to answer and things to prove, but ultimately what happened is the Timberwolves went into these last couple of weeks really needing **Jonathan Kuminga** because they did not have another option out there in free agency to really address their thinnest spot on the roster. They had to get into a bidding war, a recruiting battle with the Los Angeles Lakers for Kuminga's services. The Lakers were offering a possible starting spot, the Wolves were offering a possible starting spot, the Lakers were offering more than $12 million per season, the Wolves could only get to six, and yet with a huge recruiting push from **Mark Stod**, **Mark Lore**, and **Alex Rodriguez**, and **Tim Connelly**, and **Anthony Edwards**, and a bunch of Timberwolves teammates, they were the ones that outmaneuvered the Lakers in free agency for a player both teams coveted."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fj1uM9S4f1w&t=109s

**2. Jon Krawczynski — "Jonathan Kuminga has always believed that he is a big money player" — Jonathan Kuminga's motivation for joining the Timberwolves** [04:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fj1uM9S4f1w&t=255s)

**Jim Souhan:** "Is that because Kuminga was looking to make a purely basketball decision or because he thinks this will regenerate his career, or does he have a personal connection? Why do you think he made the decision to come here? He could have signed with the Bulls too for a lot of money, I think."

**Jon Krawczynski:** "The Bulls were in, Cleveland was in before they traded for Peyton Watson, there were a couple other teams that were sniffing around as well. But from what I understand in talking to people all around the process, the push for the Wolves that ownership got involved in, that **Anthony Edwards** was really involved in, that **Tim Connelly** went down and met face-to-face with **Jonathan Kuminga** in Miami, **Chris Finch** talked to him on the phone several times, and really let **Jonathan Kuminga** know exactly what role was available for him and how they would use him. And ultimately, I think what it came down to is **Jonathan Kuminga** has always believed that he is a big money player, that whether he was with Golden State or Atlanta, he thinks that he deserves a contract north of $20 million a year. That market has not been there for him yet. And so he looked at this opportunity, coming to Minnesota, to a team that expects to play deep into the playoffs, on a team with really high-caliber starting lineup, he looked at it as an ability for him to sort of prove himself to the league and really kind of repair the value that he has."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fj1uM9S4f1w&t=255s

**3. Jon Krawczynski — "the power forward position that's in the starting lineup is wide open for him" — Jonathan Kuminga's role in the Timberwolves starting lineup** [08:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fj1uM9S4f1w&t=502s)

The Wolves do also have depth though, they have **Terrence Shannon Jr.**, they have **Jaden McDaniels** could slide down to the four if you need it, they have **Jaylen Clark**, they have **Trey Lyles**. And so they will have other options to look at, but I do believe that throughout this process, the Wolves were telling **Jonathan Kuminga**, telling Aaron Turner his agent, that look, we have an opening in the starting lineup here, come and get it basically."

**4. Jon Krawczynski — "Mark Lore is essentially just stepping down the ladder in the investment group from first to number three" — Mark Lore's transition to majority owner of the Timberwolves** [14:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fj1uM9S4f1w&t=855s)

**Jim Souhan:** "Take us through everything involving the ownership change."

**Jon Krawczynski:** "We'll start with the news of the day because as we've seen over the last year, but really even earlier this summer, you're starting to see a little bit of volatility in the ownership ranks, especially in the NBA. The Los Angeles Lakers just abruptly sold kind of out of nowhere from Mark Walter to **Josh Kushner** and **Bob Iger**, and that was kind of more it looks like brought on under duress because Mark Walter is the subject of a federal probe into his businesses and it looks like he's kind of unloading things because he really has to to address some issues that he has in his own business and personal world. But in this case, you have **Mark Lore** essentially just stepping down the ladder in the investment group from first, from number one to number three, and selling controlling interest to **Mark Stod** and his wife **Alisa Stod**. And this constitutes not a wholesale change in the ownership ranks like the Lakers, so we don't have a situation where a brand new owner is coming in and it's going to take the entire organization getting to know that person and trying to find out the vision and probably making major, major changes to staffing and to the way things are done because they have a different kind of view of how things should work, whereas **Mark Stod** was the number three investor with **Mark Lore** and Alex Rodriguez in this ownership group. He's been very quiet and under the radar for his tenure with the Wolves."

**9. Jon Krawczynski — "I don't necessarily need to average 20 and 10 on this team to get that big deal." — Jonathan Kuminga's offensive fit and role with the Timberwolves** [33:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fj1uM9S4f1w&t=2010s)

**Jim Souhan:** "I want to hear how you think Kuminga is going to fit in offensively with that group, assuming he's in the starting lineup."

**Jon Krawczynski:** "I think the biggest point to make on **Jonathan Kuminga**'s arrival is how he is going to approach this role because he did take much less money for what he sees as a better basketball fit. One of the crucial components of whether this will work or not is, does **Jonathan Kuminga** come in here and say, I've took this essentially one-year deal to up my value, and so I'm going to be super aggressive offensively, I'm going to be taking as many shots as possible, I'm going to get my stats up as much as possible, so I get that big deal down the road? In reality, I think the best case scenario for him is to look at it as, I don't necessarily need to average 20 and 10 on this team to get that big deal. What I need to show the rest of the league is that I can do all the dirty work, that I can be a really high-level role player on this team and help **Anthony Edwards** and **LaMelo Ball** and **Jane McDaniels** and **Rudy Gobert** take this team to another level from an overall team winning aspect."
