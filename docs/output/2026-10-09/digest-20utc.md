## Bob Myers On Returning To An NBA Front Office & Making Tough Roster Decisions — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mFgQjJBthic

_Speakers identified: Vince Carter, Tracy McGrady, Bob Myers_

**1. Bob Myers — "There's no industry where you're all trying to win this trophy that only one team gets." — Bob Myers on the competitive drive behind front office work** [00:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mFgQjJBthic&t=11s)

Bob Myers: "It's hard to find competition. I didn't play in the NBA, but that's why I didn't love the agency thing because I wasn't competing. I love getting to know you, I love getting to know players, I love being around the NBA. I never thought I'd be around the NBA that close. But then when I realized there might be another opportunity to compete for someone like me, you either have a choice to coach or be an executive if you can't play. There's no industry where you're all trying to win this trophy that only one team gets, and you're all killing yourself to get it. That doesn't exist. You win or you lose 82 times a year and in the playoffs. I missed that fight and that competitiveness, and so trying to figure out how to feel that again was the motivation."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mFgQjJBthic&t=11s

**2. Bob Myers — "I have to believe that we're going to fix whatever we need to fix to win." — Bob Myers on the GM's responsibility to fix roster issues** [02:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mFgQjJBthic&t=130s)

Bob Myers: "I have to believe that we're going to fix whatever we need to fix to win. I have to believe that I can't play, so my skill has to be putting something together that can succeed. I have to believe that. Just like if I say to you guys, can you give me 20 a night? You go, yeah. Can you be 22 next year? You go, yeah, I can do that for you. I have to believe it. But I have to know, the hard part about putting a team together is you can trade five first-round picks, you can trade two, you can never trade them, you can sign one of you two, you can sign a mid-level guy, you can draft a young guy, you can draft a guy, he was more ready to go than you in high school. I can take a chance on a **Tracy McGrady** and not know, and then you hit a home run."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mFgQjJBthic&t=130s

**3. Bob Myers — "Had I stayed in Golden State, it might have. Had I had to move off a Clay." — Bob Myers on the emotional difficulty of moving on from players** [04:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mFgQjJBthic&t=245s)

Bob Myers: "It never did, but had I stayed in Golden State, it might have. Had I had to move off a Clay, I might have had a hard time with that. If I had been there and I had to do something with Draymond or Steph, something like that, that would have been personal because of what we went through together. Guys like **Andre Iguodala**, **Shaun Livingston**, guys that won multiple championships, if I don't feel a personal, then what the hell was I doing? If I don't have a relationship with those guys, there's not 10 of them, but there's a few guys where I would have a hard time. I think I'd still do the right thing for the organization, but it would be really hard to tell a guy all that that guy did for my life, my life, personally, forget about the organization."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mFgQjJBthic&t=245s

**4. Bob Myers — "You're in trade rumors to the point where like everybody knows you're in a trade rumor." — Bob Myers on how to handle players asking to be traded** [06:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mFgQjJBthic&t=393s)

Bob Myers: "This happened. I'll tell you what happened with the player. You're in trade rumors to the point where like everybody knows you're in a trade rumor. Now you haven't been traded, nobody's said anything to the organization. You walk in one day and you go, F this, I'm going to find out what the hell is going on around here because you're sick of it, because you're a human being and you're tired of it. So you come in and you go, hey, you got a minute? Yeah, what's up? You can trade me? What should I say? And I did, I'll tell you what I said, but what should I say?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mFgQjJBthic&t=393s

**5. Bob Myers — "I haven't traded you yet, nothing I've said yes to, you're on this team." — Bob Myers on being honest with players about trade rumors** [08:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mFgQjJBthic&t=493s)

Bob Myers: "I can say what you guys said, which is I haven't traded you yet, nothing I've said yes to, you're on this team. I can do that. I could say no and lie, no I'm not trading you. I'm obviously not going to say yes I am trading you if I don't have a trade. So I said, I don't know, maybe. But I said, here's the point, there's 14 other guys on the team that would come in here and ask me the same thing, and I would give them the same answer. I go, there's only one guy on this team that I'm telling him he's not getting traded, there's one guy. And so I said, just so you know, we can do a line of these guys, you're going to line everybody up, I can say maybe. And I say, if we don't win and you're not playing well, what is my job? What am I supposed to do? That is my job."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mFgQjJBthic&t=493s

**6. Bob Myers — "Getting to know a player, really trying to get to know them, you develop a trust." — Bob Myers on the importance of building trust with players** [10:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mFgQjJBthic&t=640s)

Bob Myers: "You get so much closer to someone as an agent, I think. You and I's relationship, think of all the stuff we went through together. But I got to know you and what I realized was getting to know a player, really trying to get to know them, you develop a trust. And if you can do that as an executive, then you got a real shot at winning because we're here to win. And that requires now, and maybe more so now, every day that kind of constant discussion and environment and culture that is really hard to do.
