# One-on-One With New Orlando Magic Head Coach Sean Sweeney | Open Floor w/ Chris Mannix — *Sports Illustrated*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9i6BJNwOGe0

_Speakers identified: Chris Mannix, Sean Sweeney_

**1. Sean Sweeney — "Your job is to help the players regardless of who it is, be as efficient and impactful." — Jason Kidd's coaching philosophy of serving players** [09:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9i6BJNwOGe0&t=567s)

Sean Sweeney: "**Jason Kidd** was a great listener. He made sure that everybody knew you're here to service the players. Your job is to help the players regardless of who it is, be as efficient and impactful as you can possibly be. As coaches, your job is to serve. The thing about Jason and it was the same way he was as a player, he leads as a guide, not as a hero. He's serving leadership from that standpoint, not only as a player and how he set up his teammates, but as a head coach, he did the same thing. He didn't take credit for anything and really wanted to see guys do well."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9i6BJNwOGe0&t=567s

**2. Sean Sweeney — "Our job is to be direct communicators, to know what we're talking about, to be really prepared." — Sean Sweeney's coaching philosophy of direct communication** [10:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9i6BJNwOGe0&t=636s)

Sean Sweeney: "Our job is to be direct communicators, to know what we're talking about, to be really prepared, to be really consistent, and then at the end of the day, our leadership is intention-based and our attention is active, because our job is to help the players get as much as they can out of themselves and ultimately the team. We want them to have individual success as well as team success, and the best job they do with their individual execution means the best job we're going to do with our team execution."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9i6BJNwOGe0&t=636s

**3. Sean Sweeney — "His drive was incredible and I think his mind was stronger than people probably understood." — Giannis Antetokounmpo's drive and competitiveness** [11:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9i6BJNwOGe0&t=691s)

Sean Sweeney: "You could see internally **Giannis Antetokounmpo** had way more than people grasped on the outside. His drive was incredible and I think his mind was stronger than people probably understood. You're a young player coming from overseas trying to learn the language, trying to figure out what to do, and so you can make some mistakes early on, but you could tell he was a smart player, he was a driven player, and he had a level of competitiveness that was singular."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9i6BJNwOGe0&t=691s

**4. Sean Sweeney — "He'll compete in anything, cards, checkers, tidlywinks, guessing numbers, he doesn't care, he wants to compete." — Luka Doncic's singular drive to win** [12:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9i6BJNwOGe0&t=745s)

Sean Sweeney: "**Luka Doncic** will compete in anything, cards, checkers, tidlywinks, guessing numbers, he doesn't care, he wants to compete. You put a scoreboard on and he's trying to win. But that's all those guys, they all have that desire to compete and the hate of losing and the love of winning. They're willing to do every day what most are not. Their consistency day in and day out, whether the lights are on or not, it's different than most."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9i6BJNwOGe0&t=745s

**5. Sean Sweeney — "Defensively, we want to be a team that dominates the ball, we want to punish our opponent." — Sean Sweeney's defensive vision for the Magic** [23:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9i6BJNwOGe0&t=1382s)

Sean Sweeney: "Defensively, we want to be a team that dominates the ball, we want to punish our opponent with some physicality and what I would term as appropriate violence, which we've got to get a little better at here because we've been fouling too much in our first couple preseason games. We want to do a great job with our physicality and then we want to be ready to attack the play."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9i6BJNwOGe0&t=1382s

**6. Sean Sweeney — "It starts with being aggressive with balance. We really want to attack, but we want to have balance." — Sean Sweeney's offensive vision for the Magic** [23:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9i6BJNwOGe0&t=1408s)

Sean Sweeney: "Offensively, it starts with being aggressive with balance. We really want to attack, but we want to have balance and control while we do it. We want to do a great job of creating together, that it's us together against the other team and making sure that we make the right play when the right play presents itself. And then the third thing is we want to weaponize our screening. We've got guys that are versatile, so we want to put them in different spots that are advantageous for their skill set."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9i6BJNwOGe0&t=1408s

**7. Sean Sweeney — "He has a level of competitiveness and positive energy that I think that's what you're referring to." — Jalen Suggs' skill set and leadership** [25:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9i6BJNwOGe0&t=1550s)

Sean Sweeney: "**Jalen Suggs** has a great skill set and just in general, who he is as a person, he has a level of competitiveness and positive energy that I think that's what you're referring to when you talk about the team winning when he's on the floor. He can really uplift those around him. He's a guy who can dribble, he can pass, he can shoot, he can guard at a high level, and we need him to be 100% of himself."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9i6BJNwOGe0&t=1550s
