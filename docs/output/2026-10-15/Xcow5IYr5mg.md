Kendrick Perkins: "My brother, the great **Kevin Garnett**, told me a lot, said a lot. But the one thing he always said was, always watch young players to see how they are going to perform after they get their bread. **Jalen Duren** got his lettuce. And $200 million is, we are going to play through you type lettuce. I don't want to just see drop-offs. I don't want to see pick-and-rolls. I want to see **JB Bickerstaff** highlight this young man in isolation because last year in the postseason, Tobias Harris was their second option. He is not there no more. So, who is going to take that pressure off of Cade? Meaning a guy that you can actually run a play for and for him to go get a bucket. It is time for **Jalen Duren**. JB has to use this regular season to highlight that even more so that when they get to postseason, it is not foreign to them."

Brian Windhorst: "**Jalen Duren** looked like a $200 million player in this game. I really liked how they put the ball in his hand. We saw in some of those highlights that he was having the ball and creating. He can do a lot more than just finish lobs. He has got a lot of talent, he is a super athlete, and you know he is going to do it on the defensive end."
