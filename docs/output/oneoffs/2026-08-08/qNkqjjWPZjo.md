**1. Mikel Brown Jr. — "I love competition and I love to compete and I love challenges." — Mikel Brown Jr. on the challenges of playing up in age** [03:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qNkqjjWPZjo&t=221s)

Mikel Brown Jr.: "I've always been used to playing up, even dating back to when I first started playing in a rec league when I was five, six years old. I would always play with the seven and eight-year-olds, moving up. Now I'm 10 years old, I'll be playing with the 13 and 14-year-olds. I think playing up for me has always been just natural, just because I love competition and I love to compete and I love challenges. Playing up is obviously a challenge. It's not easy. The game is faster, reads are quicker, more physical. I think for me playing up from a young age has kind of already built that foundation of just used to playing against older guys."
