# Jeremy Lin revisits 2019 title, fond Toronto moments, and Kawhi's sensational return — *Hello and Welcome*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pIiYfS2ctz8

_Speakers identified: William Lou, Jeremy Lin_

**1. Jeremy Lin — "I took 13 three-pointers from the dead center of the top center of the three-point line." — Jeremy Lin on hitting a game-winner for the Toronto Raptors** [07:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pIiYfS2ctz8&t=420s)

Jeremy Lin: "A funny story is I was on vacation in Hawaii and I ran into in the hotel weight room, I ran into **Dwayne Casey**. He was just so happened to be who was a head coach of the Raptors at that time and he brings up that moment. He was like, Jose did exactly what we wanted him to do. We wanted you to be able to shoot that shot because no, he was right, right? Actually, something that people don't know is that year, I took 13 three-pointers from the dead center of the top center of the three-point line. I was one for 13 and that was the one I made. So statistically, they had it figured out what they felt like was the best shot, but I just happened to hit that one."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pIiYfS2ctz8&t=420s

**2. Jeremy Lin — "Lowry was actively recruiting me in one of the games where we were playing against them." — Jeremy Lin on being recruited by Kyle Lowry to join the Raptors** [10:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pIiYfS2ctz8&t=633s)

**William Lou:** "Lowry was kind of actively recruiting me in one of the games where we were playing against them. And so we're playing in Atlanta and Lowry is like, so what you thinking? I'm like, what? Thinking about what? And he's like, what you mean? I'm like, what do you mean? And he's like, you joining us or what?"

**Jeremy Lin:** "I was like, you for real? Because I was trying to, at that time I was already telling my agent, hey, it would be nice if I could get a buyout and go somewhere else. So then after that, I met with **Nick Nurse** in the in the visitor's locker room. And so I'm playing, imagine like I play a game against the Raptors in Atlanta and then I go from the home locker room to the visitor's locker room, I meet with them privately. So we talk for a little bit. Next thing you know, I joined the team."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pIiYfS2ctz8&t=633s

**3. Jeremy Lin — "Missing two years in my prime to injury really affected me mentally more than I realized." — Jeremy Lin on the mental impact of injuries during his prime** [13:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pIiYfS2ctz8&t=808s)

Jeremy Lin: "Missing two years in my prime to injury really affected me mentally more than I realized. And so it was kind of after that moment where playing for the Hawks there was no pressure, everyone expected us to lose, but now being thrust into the position where it's like, yo, this team is legit and it's kind of championship or bust, mentally, I probably would have been fine earlier in my career, but coming off of those two straight years of injury where I was really starting to second-guess certain things and I was not trusting my body, I wasn't built for that moment."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pIiYfS2ctz8&t=808s

**4. Jeremy Lin — "The team is better suited than people are realizing. The potential to be a huge surprise is high." — Jeremy Lin on the Toronto Raptors' potential in the East** [19:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pIiYfS2ctz8&t=1161s)

Jeremy Lin: "I think the team is better suited than people are realizing. When people think about the East, they're talking Knicks, they're talking Sixers. I think the Raptors are a huge the potential to be a huge surprise is very, very high. Kawhi is literally that game-changing. He is magnetic in the sense of when he is on the floor, the attention that he demands, it will open everybody up. It will open Scottie, it will open RJ, it will open the shooters. Defensively, there's going to be problems. The Raptors create lots of mismatch problems defensively. They have a lot of versatility."
