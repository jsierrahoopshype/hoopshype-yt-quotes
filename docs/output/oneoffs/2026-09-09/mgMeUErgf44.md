# Richard Jefferson joins the Aussie Hoops Hour! | Ben Simmons, Matthew Dellavedova, & TENNIS CROSSES? — *ESPN Australia*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mgMeUErgf44

_Speakers identified: Olgun Uluc, Richard Jefferson_

**1. Richard Jefferson — "Australian basketball, there's a grit and a toughness to their players that stands out amongst other regions." — Richard Jefferson on the unique grit of Australian basketball players** [01:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mgMeUErgf44&t=83s)

**Olgun Uluc:** "When you think Australian basketball, what's the first thing you immediately think of?"

**Richard Jefferson:** "Gritty. I've played with enough, I've been around enough, whether it's **Patty Mills**, **Joe Ingles**, **Andrew Bogut**, I was teammates with, obviously Dellavedova. When you see those style, every culture, every region has a style of play. Australian basketball, there's a grit and a toughness to their players that stands out amongst other regions."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mgMeUErgf44&t=83s

**2. Richard Jefferson — "I watched that 2015 final where Dellavedova almost died chasing around Steph. That was really my first introduction." — Richard Jefferson on Matthew Dellavedova's impact as a teammate** [02:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mgMeUErgf44&t=140s)

**Olgun Uluc:** "What was your first impression of Dellavedova? He comes into your locker room with the Cavs, and he sort of works his way up into the rotation that year."

**Richard Jefferson:** "I wasn't on the team in 2015. I was on the team in 2016-17. My best friend and roommate **Luke Walton** was an assistant coach for the Golden State Warriors. **Steve Kerr** was the head coach. We were all Arizona Wildcats. So I actually watched that 2015 final where Dellavedova almost died chasing around Steph. That was really my first introduction to Dellavedova, probably like the rest of the basketball world that weren't familiar with college or Australia. But the basketball world outside of that, that was Dellavedova's kind of, 'Hey, here I am.' And so I was very familiar with what he did in that finals and I watched him. But then I got on his team and I'm like, 'Oh, this is him every day.' He is the ultimate pro. He is a guy that, I always say athletes have their 15-man roster. If you had to put together a 15-man team of all the guys that you played with, Dellavedova would be on my roster."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mgMeUErgf44&t=140s

**3. Richard Jefferson — "Ben has the talent to be an All-Star this year." — Richard Jefferson on Ben Simmons' potential as an All-Star** [07:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mgMeUErgf44&t=455s)

**Olgun Uluc:** "What should our expectations be for **Ben Simmons**?"

**Richard Jefferson:** "I think you should have no expectations. There are certain basketball, just like many other sports, as much as it is physical, it is mental. Ben's always have the talent. Ben has the talent to be an All-Star this year. If Ben goes and has the best season, he's healthy, his back is right, he has the talent to be an all-defensive player, to be a defensive player of the year level talent because he can guard one through five positions."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mgMeUErgf44&t=455s

**4. Richard Jefferson — "He's a grumpy dude, so I 100% think he is built to coach." — Richard Jefferson on Andrew Bogut's coaching potential** [17:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mgMeUErgf44&t=1041s)

**Olgun Uluc:** "What do you think of **Andrew Bogut** as a coach?"

**Richard Jefferson:** "He's a grumpy dude, so I 100% think he is built to coach. No, but the thing I like about Bogut is he has a respect for the game of basketball. He has an appreciation and a respect. Now his grumpiness is part of that. I think most great players and talented players have a grumpiness when it comes to the sport. And I think he's going to be outstanding. I think he's going to demand high amounts of focus from his teammates and from his players. And he also carries a tremendous amount of respect."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mgMeUErgf44&t=1041s

**5. Richard Jefferson — "Effort is a skill and there's certain guys that have the ability to play at that effort level." — Richard Jefferson on the importance of effort as a skill** [24:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mgMeUErgf44&t=1440s)

**Olgun Uluc:** "How did **TJ McConnell** make it in the NBA with limited athleticism and ability?"

**Richard Jefferson:** "I watched **TJ McConnell** do outstanding against **Oklahoma City** with the Indiana Pacers. When I watch him play and the things that he did, it is very reminiscent. It's an energy style, it's a focus style, it's a grit style. And there are lots of players like that. Effort is a skill and there's certain guys that have the ability to play at that effort level that is just unique and special."
