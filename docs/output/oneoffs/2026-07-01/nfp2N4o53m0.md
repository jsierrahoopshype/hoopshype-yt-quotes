# Dusty May Interview with Gina Miller — *Dallas Mavericks*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nfp2N4o53m0

_Speakers identified: Dusty May, Gina Miller_

**1. Dusty May — "everyone is committed to working together and building a championship contender day by day, brick by brick" — Dusty May on the collaborative culture of the Dallas Mavericks** [00:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nfp2N4o53m0&t=27s)

**Gina Miller:** "What was it about this opportunity with the Dallas Mavericks at this time, with this organization, this roster, these players, these staff members? What was it that made it the right fit for you to leave what objectively was a really nice opportunity at Michigan?"

**Dusty May:** "Each of us in life have our own boxes to check as far as what's important to us, and Dallas checked every box. It started with the alignment between the ownership group, the front office, and also the coaching staff on down to the players. Everyone is committed to working together and building a championship contender day by day, brick by brick."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nfp2N4o53m0&t=27s

**2. Dusty May — "the opportunity to coach the players on our roster now, Cooper Flagg obviously, Kyrie, one of the best guards ever" — Dusty May on coaching Luka Doncic and Kyrie Irving** [01:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nfp2N4o53m0&t=60s)

Dusty May: "The second is just the opportunity to coach the players on our roster now, **Cooper Flagg** obviously, Kyrie, one of the best guards ever to play our game, and some veterans who have done it at a high level with some inexperienced guys that are on the verge of doing it at an even higher level."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nfp2N4o53m0&t=60s

**3. Dusty May — "I want us to be the best passing team in the NBA" — Dusty May's tactical vision for the Mavericks' offense and defense** [01:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nfp2N4o53m0&t=106s)

Dusty May: "On both sides of basketball, we are going to try to make sure that everything we do fits our players' strengths and hides their weaknesses. As far as offense, the unselfish piece, I want us to be the best passing team in the NBA. I want us to play with top-five pace and effort to get it up the court quickly before the defense gets set. And then defensively, to be incredibly competitive. A team that competes every second of every game."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nfp2N4o53m0&t=106s

**4. Dusty May — "they'll feel like there's a championship on the horizon, that the foundation has been laid" — Dusty May on the Mavericks' championship window** [03:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nfp2N4o53m0&t=216s)

Dusty May: "They'll feel like there's a championship on the horizon, that the foundation has been laid from the ownership group, from the front office to the team, and we're not far off. And I think if we go into the next offseason feeling like we're one step closer to that championship puzzle, then we'll all feel great about where we are."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nfp2N4o53m0&t=216s
