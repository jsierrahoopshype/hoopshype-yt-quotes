## Mario Chalmers is brutally honest about Standing Up To LeBron — *Bovada*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=obTU7fQ41FU

_Speakers identified: Vernon Maxwell, Mario Chalmers_

**1. Mario Chalmers — "I earned my respect and they knew whatever they said to me it wasn't going to affect me." — Mario Chalmers on earning respect from LeBron, Dwyane Wade, and Chris Bosh** [00:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=obTU7fQ41FU&t=24s)

**Vernon Maxwell:** "Talk to me about your conversations with these guys. I know these guys were big in your life when it comes to basketball."

**Mario Chalmers:** "Especially guys I looked up to. But for me, I got to earn your respect and that's what I did for each one of them. I earned my respect and they knew whatever they said to me it wasn't going to affect me. I'm still going to show up, I'm still going to do my job, I'm still going to play the game of basketball. So it was more of them trying to bring the best out of me. We all want the same thing."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=obTU7fQ41FU&t=24s

**2. Mario Chalmers — "I got into it with Dwyane Wade in Utah. I got into it with LeBron James on the bench." — Mario Chalmers on his confrontations with Dwyane Wade and LeBron James** [00:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=obTU7fQ41FU&t=58s)

**Vernon Maxwell:** "Talk to me about the switch in the jerseys."

**Mario Chalmers:** "I got into it with **Dwyane Wade** in Utah. I got into it with **LeBron James** on the bench. Me and Chris Bosh never really got into it just because that's not really his personality. Me and **Dwyane Wade** got into it in Chicago. So it was kind of like, yeah I get you, but I'm still a man at the end of the day."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=obTU7fQ41FU&t=58s

**3. Mario Chalmers — "The year before LeBron James came, we had a guy named Mark Blount on our team." — Mario Chalmers explains why he switched back to number 15** [02:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=obTU7fQ41FU&t=121s)

Mario Chalmers: "Everyone thinks that he took the number from me. That's not what happened. The year before **LeBron James** came, we had a guy named **Mark Blount** on our team. **Mark Blount** barely played but he wore number 15. That was his last year with us on contract. So the season before going into before **LeBron James** came, I had told them I want to switch back to 15. That was my college number, my number I won all the championships in, and more importantly, that's the number my mom wore when she played. So that's the main reason I wore 15 anyway. So after that season, I was switching back to 15 anyway and it just happened that **LeBron James** was coming to the Heat."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=obTU7fQ41FU&t=121s

**4. Mario Chalmers — "I didn't think it was that tough just because of who I had on my team." — Mario Chalmers on the ease of the 2012 NBA Finals** [03:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=obTU7fQ41FU&t=189s)

**Vernon Maxwell:** "Tell me about your memories about your first just getting to the finals, how tough it is."

**Mario Chalmers:** "I'm not going to lie, I didn't think it was that tough just because of who I had on my team. **Dwyane Wade**, **LeBron James**, **Chris Bosh**. So it was kind of more like everybody hated us when the team came together. When those three came to the Heat, everybody hated our team. So it was kind of like, oh yeah, I like being the villain. I like when people don't like me and bring out the best in me. So I felt like that year was easier just because of the hate that we were entitled to."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=obTU7fQ41FU&t=189s

**5. Mario Chalmers — "It's a toss up between the second NBA ring and my college ring." — Mario Chalmers on his most meaningful basketball rings** [05:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=obTU7fQ41FU&t=346s)

Mario Chalmers: "It's a toss up between the second NBA ring and my college ring just because college on a selfish note, college was because of me. Just because I hit the shot, I sent it into overtime and we won as a team. But if I didn't hit that shot, then there's no Mario Chalmers, there's no Mr. Clutch. And then the last one just because I ain't going to lie, that's the hardest championship I ever had to win. Game six was probably the hardest game I ever played in my NBA career."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=obTU7fQ41FU&t=346s

**6. Mario Chalmers — "They need more of a guidance that's not trying to be a father figure." — Mario Chalmers on the need for veteran guidance for young players** [07:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=obTU7fQ41FU&t=437s)

**Vernon Maxwell:** "Is it big you think for these young guys nowadays to have these old veterans on the team?"

**Mario Chalmers:** "I think so just because it's easy to talk to somebody that's been there before or that's went through it. Instead of somebody that's like, yo real, let me highlight you, bro. We about to go out tonight, have a couple drinks, but look, 1:00, don't drink no more, we going back to the hotel by 1:30. Whatever you do, be sleep by 3:00, boom, be ready to hoop tomorrow."
