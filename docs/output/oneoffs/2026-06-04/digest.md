## What Gregg Popovich Told Danny Green About Dylan Harper — *Danny Green x Inside the Green Room*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xoCSpHOvzO0

_Speakers identified: Harrison Sanford, Danny Green_

**1. Danny Green — "If he stays hot, he can really shift this series." — Julian Champagnie's impact and potential for the Spurs** [01:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xoCSpHOvzO0&t=75s)

**Harrison Sanford:** "In the first half, **Julian Champagnie** was doing his Danny Green impression. He was five of six from the three-point line, five threes in the first half."

**Danny Green:** "Somebody asked me before the game, who is the guy that can be on the Spurs? I said **Julian Champagnie**. If he stays hot, he can really shift this series. He started making me look good. He's about to have a series where people are going to remember his name, he's going to become a household name."

**2. Danny Green — "That Dylan Harper kid is amazing. I don't know how he does it, he's incredible." — Gregg Popovich's praise for Dylan Harper's rookie performance** [04:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xoCSpHOvzO0&t=248s)

Danny Green: "We started talking some of the game and the first thing **Gregg Popovich** said, that **Dylan Harper** kid is amazing. I don't know how he does it, he's incredible. Big body, strong, finished right hand, left hand at the rim, just poise. He's incredible. I had a chance to watch **Dylan Harper** since he was in high school and he's still doing the same thing. He looks very polished for a rookie."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xoCSpHOvzO0&t=248s

**3. Danny Green — "I said the Knicks are going to win the NBA Finals, not just because they're talented." — Danny Green's Western Conference Finals prediction for the Knicks** [05:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xoCSpHOvzO0&t=327s)

Danny Green: "Around Game 5 of the Western Conference Finals, I said that the Knicks are going to win the NBA Finals, not just because they're talented but because the wear and tear of the teams in the Western Conference Finals. It's hard to deny great basketball when you see it, the defending champs in OKC and then also the Knicks when they're having a historical run and the stars are aligning for them to have a good series."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xoCSpHOvzO0&t=327s
