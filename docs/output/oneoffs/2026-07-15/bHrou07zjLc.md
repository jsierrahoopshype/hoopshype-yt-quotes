# Bam Adebayo reflects on his 83-point game, teaming up with Giannis | ESPYS Preview Show — *NBA on ESPN*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bHrou07zjLc

_Speakers identified: Bam Adebayo, Kevin Negandhi, Elle Duncan_

**1. Bam Adebayo — "Most people don't talk about how exhausted I was. We had guys out, three out of five starters." — Bam Adebayo on the fatigue and context of his 83-point game** [00:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bHrou07zjLc&t=44s)

**Elle Duncan:** "What do you remember from that night?"

**Bam Adebayo:** "Most people don't talk about how exhausted I was. We had guys out, three out of five starters. We were in this funk. I don't know what it was, we were just in this funk. Obviously **Dwyane Wade** didn't add by saying we had no buzz in the city. Love you **Dwyane Wade**. I just got going early and it went from there."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bHrou07zjLc&t=44s

**2. Bam Adebayo — "The biggest thing is we want to win. You can always be in line with somebody." — Bam Adebayo on his shared goal with new teammate Giannis Antetokounmpo** [01:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bHrou07zjLc&t=82s)

**Kevin Negandhi:** "What's going through your mind now that **Giannis Antetokounmpo** is going to be a teammate of yours?"

**Bam Adebayo:** "The biggest thing is we want to win. You can always be in line with somebody who's about the right stuff and willing to win."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bHrou07zjLc&t=82s

**3. Bam Adebayo — "When everybody gets involved when it's Team USA, it's about winning. The standard is gold." — Bam Adebayo on the high standard of playing for Team USA** [02:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bHrou07zjLc&t=146s)

Bam Adebayo: "To be a part of something special like that, obviously when everybody gets involved when it's Team USA, it's about winning. The standard is gold. To be a part of that, to be a part of that run, obviously we had the doubt in our mind down 15 going into the fourth and **Stephen Curry** became **Stephen Curry**. To be a part of those type of moments and watch somebody be at the highest stage, excel like that is pretty exciting."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bHrou07zjLc&t=146s

**4. Bam Adebayo — "I feel like everybody is a fan of Kobe and to have my name cemented next to him." — Bam Adebayo on the honor of being compared to Kobe Bryant** [03:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bHrou07zjLc&t=182s)

Bam Adebayo: "I feel like everybody is a fan of Kobe and to have my name cemented next to him, doing something that obviously not a lot of people can do, having the opportunity to be a part of that was truly special. And I mean it's 83 points in an NBA game."
