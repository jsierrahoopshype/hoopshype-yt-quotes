# 'It's weird' - Jayson Tatum on how it's felt after the Jaylen Brown trade | ESPYS Preview Show — *ESPN*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YDrrnUFjC74

_Speakers identified: Jayson Tatum, Marcello Hernández, Christine_

**1. Jayson Tatum — "If I'm being transparent, it's weird. I've been on the Celtics for nine years and he was my teammate." — Jayson Tatum on the weirdness of the Jaylen Brown trade** [01:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YDrrnUFjC74&t=103s)

**Marcello Hernández:** "How would you describe this off-season? Obviously the big story is your teammate, ex-teammate **Jaylen Brown** trade. What's it been like for you as this team, you guys grew up together in Boston, won a championship. What's it been like?"

**Jayson Tatum:** "If I'm being transparent, it's weird. I've been on the Celtics for nine years and he was my teammate every single one of those. You understand throughout the course of a career that the NBA is a business and people change teams and change coaches and front office, but it doesn't make it any easier. There's still a human element of it that you feel those emotions, the city feels those emotions. But there's also a side of like we have to welcome our new teammates in with open arms and we still have to attack the new season. So there's a balance, but it's weird."
