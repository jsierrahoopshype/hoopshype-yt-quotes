**2. Dwyane Wade — "I was good enough, but I was not elite yet, and so I worked to be elite." — Dwyane Wade on his path to becoming an elite NBA player** [10:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6-8xh353sB4&t=604s)

**Cameron Rogers:** "When did you realize that you were much better than everyone else? Was there a turning point when you were playing with your friends and your cousins and you were like, yeah, we are all good, we are all playing, but like, I am going to be in the NBA?"

**Dwyane Wade:** "No, it did not happen that way. I wish it was that easy. Some people have that story, but that is not really mine. It did take me a while to get better than my friends. I did see the moment I was better than my friend group. At first, we were all about the same, and then it was a summer I was like, oh, y'all too small. I felt that, but it took me a while to develop my game, and so I was one of those guys that just slowly every year, I added something, I got better, I got better. I was not somebody you would look at and be like, he is going to go into the NBA, probably until the year before I went into the NBA. I do not think a lot of people would have deemed me as a pro, and there were probably a lot of question marks on me. If you think about all the things, whether it was my personal life, what I was dealt, or it was my size, or it was just my game, I had to get better. I was good enough, but I was not elite yet, and so I worked to be elite. This was not as natural as I made it look for me to become really good at basketball."
