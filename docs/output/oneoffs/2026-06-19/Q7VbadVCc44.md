**1. Carlos Boozer — "Traditionally, they like to paint it in a negative light, but lately that's shifted." — Changing perceptions of Black fathers in the NBA** [00:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q7VbadVCc44&t=50s)

**Marc J. Spears:** "What do you think the perception has been about Black fathers?"

**Carlos Boozer:** "Traditionally, they like to paint it in a negative light, but lately that's shifted. You see a lot more Black fathers standing up with their sons or standing up with their daughters and being there, showing up, shining light on the positive impact so that we can inspire the next generation of fathers."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q7VbadVCc44&t=50s

**2. Dee Flemings — "I'm a present Black father and I want them to see that." — Dee Flemings on being a present Black father** [01:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q7VbadVCc44&t=66s)

**Marc J. Spears:** "What do you think the perception has been about Black fathers?"

**Dee Flemings:** "There's the age-old stereotype that we're not present, so I think that was something that like I'm always going to be here. I'm a firefighter, my wife's a nurse, so like community is really important to us. I'm a present Black father and I want them to see that."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q7VbadVCc44&t=66s

**3. Carlos Boozer — "I always told him if we're going to do this, we're going to do it the right way." — Carlos Boozer on his tough love coaching style** [02:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q7VbadVCc44&t=140s)

**Marc J. Spears:** "Were you tough love with your son in basketball?"

**Carlos Boozer:** "Always. Always. It was me just introducing him to the game and I always told him if we're going to do this, we're going to do it the right way. There's not going to be any shortcuts or anything, so I was always tough on him for sure."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q7VbadVCc44&t=140s

**4. Carlos Boozer — "I knew that this one was really special probably around like sixth grade." — Carlos Boozer on Cam Boozer's early potential** [06:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q7VbadVCc44&t=371s)

**Marc J. Spears:** "At what point did you say, when I'm seeing is something that could be on the highest level?"

**Carlos Boozer:** "I knew that this one was really special probably around like sixth grade. We had him with different trainers that were really good with developing skill sets in the backyard, and he was picking it up faster than everybody else. And then I think the light bulb went off like seventh, eighth grade and he just took off from there."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q7VbadVCc44&t=371s

**5. Carlos Boozer — "He doesn't cheat the game. He shows that every single day." — Cam Boozer's leadership and work ethic** [06:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q7VbadVCc44&t=394s)

**Marc J. Spears:** "What separates Cam from everyone else in the draft?"

**Carlos Boozer:** "He's a guy that the rest of the team will galvanize around because he doesn't cheat the game. He shows that every single day, he studies the game, he puts the work into it every single day. He's as dedicated as you're ever going to find a player to his craft. And so because of that, the team will always follow him, because he's the natural leader."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q7VbadVCc44&t=394s

**6. Dee Flemings — "That'd be a great culmination to everything we've done." — Dee Flemings on his son's draft journey** [07:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q7VbadVCc44&t=427s)

**Marc J. Spears:** "When you notice that Father's Day was two days before the draft, what came to mind?"

**Dee Flemings:** "That'd be a great culmination to everything we've done. Look, we've been doing this since, like I say, three or four. Like he finally reached this goal, this was always his goal. And so to see him achieve that and know that I was able to actually help him achieve that, like I did my job."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q7VbadVCc44&t=427s

**7. Dee Flemings — "It costs you nothing to be a good person, but it can cost you everything if you're not." — Dee Flemings on raising a good human** [08:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q7VbadVCc44&t=532s)

**Marc J. Spears:** "What life lessons stick with you daily that he's bestowed on you?"

**Dee Flemings:** "One of my favorite sayings: it costs you nothing to be a good person, but it can cost you everything if you're not. Be a good dude, stand up dude, honest dude, and everything else will take care of itself because you're going to be a professional at life longer than you're going to be at the game of basketball."
