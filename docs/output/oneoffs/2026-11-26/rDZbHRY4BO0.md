**1. Marc Stein — "They are either going to trade someone away to open a spot or they have to do the outright wave." — Mavericks' two potential paths for roster flexibility with Walter Clayton Jr.** [00:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rDZbHRY4BO0&t=10s)

Marc Stein: "There are clearly two pretty clear pathways. They are either going to trade someone away to open a spot or they have to do the outright wave. They naturally would rather trade someone away. You don't want to just wave someone and eat guaranteed money. The two most clear-cut names for that would be **Moussa Cisse**, who last time I checked had logged only 36 minutes this season. I know he got in last night, but he couldn't have gotten too much time. So I think he is still sub-40 minutes for the season, and he has a very tradeable contract. But if **Bobby Karalla** does not allow that to happen and storms the Mavs offices to say I refuse to let you trade **Moussa Cisse**, I suppose they could try the same with Marcus Sasser, who is on basically a $5 million expiring deal."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rDZbHRY4BO0&t=10s

**2. Marc Stein — "In the luxury tax aprons era, every dollar matters to pretty much every team." — The impact of the luxury tax apron on roster flexibility** [01:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rDZbHRY4BO0&t=73s)

Marc Stein: "In the luxury tax aprons era, every dollar matters to pretty much every team. We will have to see. **Moussa Cisse**'s contract in theory should be ultra-tradeable because it is only $2.2 million. He has shown some great flashes as a very active big man. It is only guaranteed this season, not next season. I initially thought, and I think the Mavs probably felt this way, that that would be an easy deal to move if they needed to. It certainly made sense when you are making the decision between keeping Cisse or **Dwight Powell**."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rDZbHRY4BO0&t=73s

**3. Marc Stein — "It would have been foolish to just wave Cisse if you know teams are lining up." — The risk of waiving Walter Clayton Jr. and losing him to waivers** [01:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rDZbHRY4BO0&t=111s)

Marc Stein: "It would have been foolish to just wave Cisse if you know teams are lining up. There was a school of thought that had the Mavs chosen that route, he wouldn't have even cleared waivers. There were some rumbles that Chicago had interest of just claiming him. If you know you are going to let someone go and they are going to be instantly claimed, or there is a line of teams forming to get him as a free agent, if nothing else, you keep that player because you hope you can trade him down the road, where obviously there would have been no such trade market for **Dwight Powell**."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rDZbHRY4BO0&t=111s

**4. Marc Stein — "Does someone come after a Mavs vet in a trade?" — Potential trade scenarios involving Mavericks veterans** [02:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rDZbHRY4BO0&t=155s)

Marc Stein: "There also of course is a third scenario where does someone come after a Mavs vet in a trade? But also, let's just say they trade away a higher salary player. Unless they are trading into someone's trade exception, salary is coming back, so that really wouldn't help the Mavs. The theory is Moussa Cisse making only $2.2 million, theoretically you can trade him somewhere and someone would just take that salary into a trade exception of some sort and then the Mavs don't have to take a player back and now they have easily created a spot for **Walter Clayton Jr.**"
