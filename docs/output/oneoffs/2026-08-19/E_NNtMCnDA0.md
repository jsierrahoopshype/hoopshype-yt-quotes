# NBA pushes back on report about Kawhi Leonard, LA Clippers investigation | NBA on NBC — *NBA on NBC*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_NNtMCnDA0

**1. "the NBA has found no evidence that the Clippers and owner Steve Ballmer funneled money to Kawhi Leonard" — NBA denies evidence of Clippers funneling money to Kawhi Leonard** [00:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_NNtMCnDA0&t=1s)

"Let's dive into the latest reporting on the now 11-month saga of the alleged cap circumvention case involving the Los Angeles Clippers and **Kawhi Leonard**. This week, ESPN came out with a report that said the NBA has found no evidence that the Clippers and owner **Steve Ballmer** funneled money to **Kawhi Leonard**. This is in connection to the allegations that the Clippers funneled $28 million to Leonard through a now defunct Green Bank and deals with two companies, Dactronics and Boingo Wireless, all of which the Clippers have denied."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_NNtMCnDA0&t=1s

**2. "the NBA is reportedly focused on whether or not the team introduced Leonard to team sponsors" — NBA investigating if Clippers introduced Kawhi Leonard to team sponsors** [00:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_NNtMCnDA0&t=38s)

"The NBA is reportedly focused on whether or not the team introduced Leonard to team sponsors and whether that constituted a violation of the league's rules prohibiting cap circumvention. The NBA, they turned around and responded with a statement that said, quote, ESPN's article regarding the LA Clippers investigation, for which the NBA declined to cooperate, contains numerous and significant inaccuracies. The result in this matter we make clear once the investigation is concluded."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_NNtMCnDA0&t=38s

**3. "this is as strongly worded a statement that I have seen from the league" — NBA's strong response to ESPN report on Clippers investigation** [01:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_NNtMCnDA0&t=68s)

"I have been asking the NBA for comment on stories for two decades. This is as strongly worded a statement that I have seen from the league. This is the NBA saying that the story is wrong. What it does not say is how the story is wrong. I have talked to people in the league office over the last couple of days, and the message that I have been getting is that they are not going to be rushed into any public statements or tied to any public statements until this investigation is complete. **Adam Silver** made it clear at Summer League that the investigation needs to wrap up, and he was optimistic that it could happen this summer."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_NNtMCnDA0&t=68s

**4. "there have been discussions between lawyers for the Clippers and the league about finding a resolution" — Clippers and NBA discussing resolution to cap circumvention investigation** [02:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_NNtMCnDA0&t=164s)

"The report says that the league did present its initial findings to the Clippers in late July and that there have been discussions between lawyers for the Clippers and the league about finding a resolution. An agreement between both sides is widely regarded as the best case scenario here. No lawsuits, no arbitration, no mess that could play out for months, even years. The league does not want to devote even more time to fighting one of the richest men in the world and the richest owner in the NBA."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_NNtMCnDA0&t=164s

**5. "if the worst punishment you get for cap circumvention is a fine and maybe docked a couple" — Potential punishment for Clippers in cap circumvention case** [04:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_NNtMCnDA0&t=241s)

"The question is, is there a punishment severe enough that the Clippers will accept, that the rest of the league will accept? I am talking about the 29 other teams right now because they are the audience on any punishment for the NBA. I have had several high-ranking team officials tell me throughout this process that if the Clippers get what amounts to a slap on the wrist, it is probably going to be open season on stuff like this. If the worst punishment you get for cap circumvention is a fine and maybe docked a couple of second-round picks, then the risk is worth the reward of potentially landing a marquee player like **Kawhi Leonard**."
