**1. Sasha Vujacic — "he's showing the leadership. I'm very excited about this year" — Luka Doncic's leadership and impact on the Lakers** [02:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9o7c4k3wjjk&t=122s)

**Host 1:** "What is it like for you seeing the awareness of Slovenia rising with **Luka** being with the Lakers? There has been so much coverage of him taking his teammates to Slovenia. As someone from a small country, what is it like for you seeing the awareness of Slovenia rising?"

**Sasha Vujacic:** "It has been there and what **Luka** has been doing, he's showing the leadership. I'm very excited about this year. Finally he's a Laker, Laker. And him and **AR** and the team that we have, I can't wait to watch it. And for him to bring the team to Slovenia, to show them where he comes from, to show everybody what Slovenia is all about, I think they have a better understanding of what it means for him to be in LA and through his journey how important it is to win. So that's always great to see."

**2. Sasha Vujacic — "we will not know what the team's identity is going to be until sometime in Thanksgiving" — Lakers team identity and expectations for the upcoming season** [02:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9o7c4k3wjjk&t=170s)

**Host 1:** "We were just talking to **Robert Horry** and one of the things we talked about is I've heard people say 50 wins, 46 wins, 56 wins. Nobody really knows at this point, right?"

**Sasha Vujacic:** "As long as you win the last game of the season, actually the last game in the playoffs, that's all that matters. I think we have a great team at the moment, we have good coaching staff. And as **PJ** always said, he said, we will not know what the team's identity is going to be until sometime in Thanksgiving or after Thanksgiving. So we got to give it some time, we got to be patient. And I think we've done great moves off-season by getting every position filled and I'm really excited to see this team perform. So it's going to be a fun season for all the Laker nation."
