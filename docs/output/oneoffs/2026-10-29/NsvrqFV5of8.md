**2. Norman Powell — "I don't eat breakfast in the morning on game days." — Norman Powell shares his simple game-day routine** [04:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NsvrqFV5of8&t=250s)

Norman Powell: "Game day routine is actually pretty simple. Oddly enough, I don't eat breakfast in the morning on game days. I usually get up and work out in the morning, and then I do my team shootaround, and then when I come back, I'll eat brunch. I'll probably get on the game, which is Call of Duty, for an hour, and then I'll take a two-hour nap, wake up, get ready for the game."

**4. Norman Powell — "When you prioritize a big goal, a big dream, you got to put that stuff to the side." — Norman Powell on the sacrifices required for an NBA career** [09:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NsvrqFV5of8&t=575s)

Norman Powell: "When you prioritize a big goal, a big dream, you got to put that stuff to the side, the fun stuff, and put the work in. I never played basketball just because of what it could provide. I played basketball because of my love for the game.
