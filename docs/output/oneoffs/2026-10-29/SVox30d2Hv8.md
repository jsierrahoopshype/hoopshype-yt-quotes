**2. Brian Scalabrine — "It technically did not demand to be traded." — Brian Scalabrine walks back his report on Jaylen Brown's demands** [00:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVox30d2Hv8&t=32s)

Brian Scalabrine: "To be fair, after the 48 calls I fended today, listen, it technically did not demand to be traded. I just summed it all up. I summed up the entire word salad and kept it simple, but I think that's what kind of got me in trouble there. I don't know what the ultimatum was or the demands or whatever it was, but so, it technically did not demand to be traded. It was, do these things right here or else."

**3. Jaylen Brown — "I never demanded a trade, never made a list of ultimatums, none of the sort." — Jaylen Brown denies requesting a trade from the Boston Celtics** [01:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVox30d2Hv8&t=65s)

**Reporter:** "There was a report a couple of days ago that you requested a trade from the Boston Celtics and that that report was walked back on, kind of walked back on live television. So, two questions: did you request a trade? And if you did, how frustrating is this that this kind of stuff just keeps coming up a little bit?"

**Jaylen Brown:** "Yeah, I mean, I guess it is what it is. I saw that. I saw Scal's made some comments. Never had any issues with Scal. I love Scal. But I guess he's doing what he what he has to do. I understand why he feels the need to do that. But I have no interest of letting any false allegations follow me into my next chapter here in Philadelphia. I never demanded a trade, never made a list of ultimatums, none of the sort. And if that is true, but as of now, I'm going to just stay focused on what's in front of me here in Philadelphia. I got a tremendous opportunity and I'm looking forward to that."
