**1. Brian Windhorst — "It is not his third free agency, it is his fourth and it is fast becoming the longer" — LeBron James' fourth free agency is becoming his longest yet** [01:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W83QUzXXx-M&t=117s)

Brian Windhorst: "It is not his third free agency, it is his fourth and it is fast becoming the longer than all of the previous free agencies put together. There had been some hope that today, there was not an expectation within the league that LeBron would reveal where he was going today. But what teams, and when I say teams, I am not talking about just your average fan here, because when I was in Las Vegas this week at Summer League, the teams that are trying to get him are talking to each other, they are talking to me, they are saying what are you hearing. There has been no information coming from LeBron or **Rich Paul** about what he is thinking and not so much about what he is picking, but what is important to him."
