**3. Maxime Raynaud — "I try to lead by example. I try to talk to the guys a little bit." — Maxime Raynaud on his role as a veteran mentor for the team** [04:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EM4y4vxnkSM&t=283s)

Maxime Raynaud: "I try to lead by example. I try to talk to the guys a little bit. I was trying to spend some time one-on-one with some of the rookies at some time of the game, trying to figure out, okay, right now you're struggling, do you like to be left alone? Do you like to have someone come talk to you? Right now you're doing great, how can we get you going even more? So, just trying to figure this out. I understand it's really hard to do in a one-week window, but hopefully I can learn a little bit from that and already be a little bit more of a mentor when we come in around September and October."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EM4y4vxnkSM&t=283s

**4. Maxime Raynaud — "You've got to take it one day at a time, expect the expected." — Maxime Raynaud on adapting to the challenges of an NBA season** [06:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EM4y4vxnkSM&t=406s)

Maxime Raynaud: "You've got to take it one day at a time, expect the expected. Nothing is going to go well all the time. You're always going to play at 70%, 60%. You're always going to be half injured. You're always going to scout a game and they turn out they're going to do something completely different that day. So you just got to be able to adapt and find solutions on your own, and that's why we prepare so much over the summer."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EM4y4vxnkSM&t=406s

**5. Maxime Raynaud — "Being able to play with some guys like Rus, Demar, veterans that have been here for 15+ years." — Maxime Raynaud on the impact of veteran teammates on his development** [07:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EM4y4vxnkSM&t=466s)

Maxime Raynaud: "Being able to play with some guys like **Rus**, **Demar**, veterans that have been here for 15+ years in the league that you were watching, that was absolutely surreal. I strongly believe that **Rus** gave me that kind of confidence, mentorship that I needed to really play at my full potential."

**6. Maxime Raynaud — "Being able to play the four has opened up more threes for me." — Maxime Raynaud on his comfort playing both power forward and center** [10:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EM4y4vxnkSM&t=625s)

Maxime Raynaud: "Being able to play the four has opened up more threes for me. So being able to knock those down and some post-up as well, because whether I play the four or the five, I need to be able to play those. But especially at the four, I have way more mismatch in terms of my size."
