Darius Acuff Jr.: "For me growing up, **Derrick Rose** for sure. It's funny because I used to tell people when I went to school elementary that he was my cousin. Everybody used to call me on it so much, like people I knew. I played like him, so the people I didn't really know, I was like, 'That's my cousin, **Derrick Rose**, that's my cousin.' I'm lying though. Actually, when we landed in Sacramento, that was the first time he called me. I was telling Coach Cal, I almost cried. It was crazy."
