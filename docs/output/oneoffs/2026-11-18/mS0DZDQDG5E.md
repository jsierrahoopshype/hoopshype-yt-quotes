# Blake Griffin on Becoming the John Oliver of Sports and Wanting to Join the Strike Force Five — *Late Night with Seth Meyers*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mS0DZDQDG5E

_Speakers identified: Seth Meyers, Blake Griffin_

**1. Blake Griffin — "I honestly didn't want to, mostly just because I felt like guys are so critical of players." — Blake Griffin on the challenges of being a critical NBA analyst** [02:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mS0DZDQDG5E&t=138s)

Blake Griffin: "I honestly didn't want to, mostly just because I felt like guys are so critical of players. As a player, you go play as hard as you possibly can, and then critics are like, 'This guy sucks.' I was trying. So when I signed on to do it, I wanted to try to do it a different way. Be a little bit uplifting, still give analysis, but teach the game."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mS0DZDQDG5E&t=138s

**2. Blake Griffin — "It's like if your friend messed up and you had to go on national TV." — Blake Griffin on the difficulty of criticizing former teammates** [02:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mS0DZDQDG5E&t=170s)

Blake Griffin: "It's tough. It's like if your friend messed up and you had to go on national TV and be like, 'This guy sucks.' No, there's a way to do it. I definitely get some texts, I get some calls. There are open lines of communication."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mS0DZDQDG5E&t=170s

**3. Blake Griffin — "John Oliver just got to read from a teleprompter for 30 minutes. And now I do that." — Blake Griffin on the challenges of reading a teleprompter** [03:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mS0DZDQDG5E&t=230s)

Blake Griffin: "**John Oliver** just got to read from a teleprompter for 30 minutes. And now I do that, but I played basketball, and that's not basketball player strength, reading. I don't know what made me think I could do this, but it's been a blast. I grew up watching late night and love **John Oliver**, love comedy in general."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mS0DZDQDG5E&t=230s

**4. Blake Griffin — "Sports is so reactionary because there's games all the time." — Blake Griffin on the reactionary nature of sports media** [09:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mS0DZDQDG5E&t=570s)

Blake Griffin: "Sports is so reactionary because there's games all the time. So the next morning you have to go on and you update people on what happened and who played well. The reason it's named The Step Back is because we're literally taking a step back and not being just rushing to judgment on these issues, doing our research, putting together a script, and trying to make people laugh."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mS0DZDQDG5E&t=570s

**5. Blake Griffin — "I was playing on the trampoline with my kids and I look at my phone." — Blake Griffin on finding out about his trade via Twitter** [10:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mS0DZDQDG5E&t=639s)

Blake Griffin: "I was playing on the trampoline with my kids and I had an idea that it might go down. I look at my phone and it was, 'Blake Griffin has been traded from the Los Angeles Clippers to the Detroit Pistons.' I was like, 'All right, Daddy's turn. Let's go.'"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mS0DZDQDG5E&t=639s

**6. Blake Griffin — "Kids don't listen, even when you played professionally." — Blake Griffin on the challenges of coaching his own children** [11:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mS0DZDQDG5E&t=672s)

Blake Griffin: "I haven't done games, but I have helped out in practice, and man, kids don't listen, even when you played professionally. They're very respectful, but I think sports and life in general, you have to learn through mistakes. You have to go through them. If I tell them something, it's not really going to click until you experience it. So figuring out how to coach, it's been brutal."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mS0DZDQDG5E&t=672s

**7. Blake Griffin — "I had a basketball camp that I had to officiate a game for." — Blake Griffin on the importance of parents officiating youth games** [12:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mS0DZDQDG5E&t=732s)

Blake Griffin: "I had a basketball camp that I had to officiate a game for. So all the kids are at the Blake Griffin Basketball Camp, and these kids hated me. And I was like, 'You know what? I got to be nicer to refs. This is happening, I'm turning over a new leaf.' And it didn't happen. I don't know if you saw my career, but a lot of technical fouls."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mS0DZDQDG5E&t=732s
