## NIKOLA JOKIĆ BEZ FILTERA | Ekskluzivni razgovor sa Miletom Ilićem — *Jao Mile TV*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ImCpWX9_zzI

_Speakers identified: Mile Ilić, Nikola Jokić_

**1. Nikola Jokić — "I was involved in the design. The first logo was on the first shoe. We agreed to change it." — Nikola Jokić on his involvement in the design of his 361° signature shoes** [00:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ImCpWX9_zzI&t=36s)

**Mile Ilić:** "The new shoes are here. They look good, and you told me they are comfortable."

**Nikola Jokić:** "Yes, they are. They are very comfortable. I was involved in the design. The first logo was on the first shoe. We agreed to change it. On the third one, we agreed that on every new shoe, there should be something new, something different compared to the other shoes. I was involved in the design, and I really like how it looks."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ImCpWX9_zzI&t=36s

**2. Nikola Jokić — "Aaron Gordon, who has been playing with me for a few years, was a connection." — Nikola Jokić on why he chose to partner with 361°** [01:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ImCpWX9_zzI&t=117s)

**Mile Ilić:** "When 361° reached out, what made you decide to sign with them?"

**Nikola Jokić:** "**Aaron Gordon**, who has been playing with me for a few years, was a connection. He was the first person I saw wearing them. He told me about them, and I started looking into it. I even have his first shoes, the **Aaron Gordon** 1. They had a really good shape, and I liked it. When I got the contract offer, I was looking for what I could get, and **Aaron Gordon** told me what he could. A connection was made, and they were really professional. The communication and the relationship were great. They respected me in a way, and I am really grateful for the cooperation."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ImCpWX9_zzI&t=117s

**3. Nikola Jokić — "I often stay alone in the locker room because I have my own routine." — Nikola Jokić on his 'Joker's done, let's go home' quote after winning the title** [03:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ImCpWX9_zzI&t=227s)

**Mile Ilić:** "What was going through your head when you were left alone in the locker room with the trophy?"

**Nikola Jokić:** "I often stay alone in the locker room because I have my own routine. I do some work in the training room, some stretching, and I have a cold tub. So I really need time, and when we are on the road, everyone is waiting for me. But I often say that it is one of the most beautiful feelings when you stay alone in the locker room when all the noise is gone and when everyone is gone, and you stay somehow alone for two, three, five, 10, 15, 20 minutes. Some feelings and some emotions somehow gather and it becomes a really beautiful moment. But I also had that feeling then. The job is done, somehow the relief is greater than the euphoria. I felt more relief than euphoria. I won, I said, 'Ugh, we won.' I felt like I took a burden off my back."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ImCpWX9_zzI&t=227s
