Will Hardy: "Every off-season is spent in two pathways. You're looking back and trying to self-assess everything. It's the individual players, it's the team, it's programmatic things, it's myself, my staff, things we can do better based on what has already happened. And then there's the pivot point in the summer, which is the draft, where somebody new is joining your program. And now things start to shake out from a roster standpoint through free agency, and you have a clearer picture of what may lie ahead. And so then all of your thinking goes into, okay, how can we help this team? And how can we help these individual players? What are the priorities for everybody? Prioritizing is probably my biggest responsibility. There's always a laundry list of things that need to be done, both as a team and individually. Most people who love to give me their opinions on what should happen, usually my response is, yeah, you're right, but we can't do everything in one day. And so I feel like a lot of my responsibility throughout the summer is trying to prioritize those things so that we come into camp and we've got a pretty clear vision and the communication is organized."

Each matchup that they draw could lead us to do different things offensively, and I think it's going to serve both of them well. My communication with them is to try to continue to have them not focus on what it all means. There's always this question of, well, what does it mean? Will Hardy: "My job as a coach is to come home, open the fridge, see what's in there and make dinner. And this is who's on our team.
