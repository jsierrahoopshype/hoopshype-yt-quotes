**1. Jon Macri — "Everything seems more cohesive when he is going right." — Mikal Bridges' impact on the Knicks' team cohesion** [01:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qQIE2g410jM&t=98s)

Jon Macri: "With Mikal, it is tougher with him than with other players because, for instance with **Josh**, who I know we are going to talk about in a bit, it is more obvious because when he is going right, it is so loud and often times the shot is falling as well. With Mikal, it is more subtle because he is such a connector for this team, and not just in the traditional way of literally connecting the ball on offense, but everything seems more cohesive when he is going right. When **Mike Brown** is playing him 13, 14, 17 minutes, it tells you that part of his game is not really where it usually is."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qQIE2g410jM&t=98s

Jon Macri: "This off-season was unlike any. The way I have been saying it is, it is not only an off-season unlike any of them have ever had, I think this is an off-season unlike any team in NBA history has ever had. **4. Jon Macri — "My faith in this starting lineup continuing to go out there is more trust in Mike Brown." — Trust in Mike Brown's starting lineup decisions** [08:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qQIE2g410jM&t=516s)

Jon Macri: "My faith in this starting lineup continuing to go out there is more trust in **Mike Brown** than trust in any of the individual players, because I think this guy has as good a read on his team and his locker room as probably anybody in the NBA. **5. Jon Macri — "He is as under control as we have ever seen him over the course of his career." — Karl-Anthony Towns' improved decision-making and control** [10:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qQIE2g410jM&t=624s)

Jon Macri: "He is as under control as we have ever seen him over the course of his career. And I mean that in the sense that over the last two years, how often would we see him with kind of a somewhat flailing drive and wind up on the ground with not like a great shot attempt? Some of the decision-making was not always where you wanted it to be. It feels like you watch him now night after night, and every decision is the correct decision. And that is not only on offense, that is on defense too."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qQIE2g410jM&t=624s

**6. Jon Macri — "The Knicks right now without KAT on the floor, they are scoring 102.3 points per 100 possessions." — The Knicks' offensive struggles when Karl-Anthony Towns is off the floor** [14:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qQIE2g410jM&t=853s)

Jon Macri: "The Knicks right now without KAT on the floor, they are scoring 102.3 points per 100 possessions. Now for anybody who needs a little context, that would be worse than the worst offense in the league, and that is the Charlotte Hornets who are in their own tier of bad. And a lot of those minutes are coming with **Jaylen Brunson** on the floor, and that is not to disparage **Jaylen Brunson**. **Jaylen Brunson** has had no case start to the season, not typical **Jaylen Brunson**, but you look at what the offense turns into when KAT is not out there."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qQIE2g410jM&t=853s

**7. Jon Macri — "Mike Brown has established himself pretty firmly as the most underrated coach in basketball." — Mike Brown's underrated coaching performance** [19:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qQIE2g410jM&t=1150s)

Jon Macri: "**Mike Brown** has established himself pretty firmly as the most underrated coach in basketball. And to me, it speaks volumes how low the volume has been on the credit for him for this championship because coming off of the win, you heard all the talk about Brunson, all the talk about KAT, all the talk about OG, all the talk about the starting five as a whole, all the talk about **Leon Rose** and the great job he did to get your Duse and McBride on a 4 million a year contract and **Landry Shamet** on the minimum. And then it was kind of like **Mike Brown**, the steady hand, and he was a change from **Tom Thibodeau** when they needed to make a change. No, this is so much more than that."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qQIE2g410jM&t=1150s

**9. Jon Macri — "I feel like the concept of showcasing is maybe sometimes a bit overblown." — The Knicks' strategy for developing young players like Dadie** [28:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qQIE2g410jM&t=1725s)
