**1. Klay Thompson — "Miami was always on my mind. My agent and I approached Dallas with the buyout because things change." — Klay Thompson on his decision to join the Miami Heat** [00:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aEduVbwq6Gc&t=13s)

**Host:** "Can you take us a little into the whole situation? Was it a stressful thing in the summer? Was it a process of teams or was it always Miami on your mind?"

**Klay Thompson:** "Miami was always on my mind. My agent and I approached **Dallas** with the buyout because things change a lot when I signed there in 2024. They were great in honoring our request and understanding that those things did happen and that I'm on the back end of things. They understood I wanted a chance to really make a deep postseason run. I'm not saying they won't do that, but I think just Miami was way better position for that. It wasn't too stressful though. I knew I always had a chance to end up here and I'm just happy it all worked out."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aEduVbwq6Gc&t=13s

**2. Klay Thompson — "I've never been this close in proximity to my Bahamian family. To be this close is super special." — Klay Thompson on his connection to his Bahamian family** [01:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aEduVbwq6Gc&t=98s)

Klay Thompson: "I've never been this close in proximity to my Bahamian family. I grew up on the West Coast, I went to college there, I played professionally there, so to be this close to them is super special. I get a chance to play in front of them on a consistent basis. It feels like a second home. In **Dallas** at times it felt like I was kind of out there by myself a lot, but now it feels like I'm right at home. The Bahamas is right across the water and my family will be here all the time. It gives me new life to make them proud and wear that Thompson on the back of my jersey with great pride."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aEduVbwq6Gc&t=98s

**3. Klay Thompson — "I just missed out on the postseason and I hate watching from home. You just get FOMO." — Klay Thompson on his motivation for a redemption season** [03:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aEduVbwq6Gc&t=188s)

Klay Thompson: "I thought I didn't play up to my standards last season. I thought in 2025 I played decent, but I just missed out on the postseason and I hate watching from home. You just get FOMO watching all your coworkers or your peers just getting to chase their dreams and it's not fun not making the playoffs. That's all I want to have to do these next couple years, have a chance to make a deep postseason run. It's so exciting to be in Miami. They are a very demanding organization, but I love being in that ultra-competitive atmosphere and this was just a match made in heaven."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aEduVbwq6Gc&t=188s

**4. Klay Thompson — "All the individual accolades, all the notoriety you get from playing this game doesn't hit the same." — Klay Thompson on the common threads of winning organizations** [04:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aEduVbwq6Gc&t=242s)

Klay Thompson: "I would say the practices and everyone being on the same page as far as training staff, front office, coaching staff, and the players. Obviously you have to have a healthy ego as an NBA player, but when everyone lets their guard down and understands that winning is the best thing in the world, all the individual accolades, all the notoriety you get from playing this game doesn't hit the same as chasing a championship. I know Pat and Spo know all about that, so does Bam, so does Giannis, so does Wiggins. These young guys are all hungry to make their mark in this league and that fire still burns within me."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aEduVbwq6Gc&t=242s
