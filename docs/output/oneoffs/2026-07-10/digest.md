**Pablo S. Torre:** "In some ways, you're talking to the worst possible person, but also, I guess, the ideal. And yet the question I have, Tony, is why did this particular development take nine days to become public after the trade was made public on June 30th? That's what's confusing to me. It doesn't really make a ton of sense. It seems like, if nothing else, a giant PR disaster that could have been avoided through some coordination if that's what did not happen. But really, I am wondering, did they find something that might align with the independent journalism that I've done, or is this just a smokescreen of a different kind?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EfQ3e5Tq9Z0&t=97s
