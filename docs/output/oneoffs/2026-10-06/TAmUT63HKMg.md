**2. Monica McNutt — "It behooves us to look out for ourselves, right? And so I think this is common." — Monica McNutt on the business of NBA contract extensions** [04:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TAmUT63HKMg&t=252s)

Monica McNutt: "The timing of this for me, as I just went through my own version of some of the stuff, I am reminded that we all offer a service for an exchange, right? And it behooves us to look out for ourselves, right? And so I think this is common. I think both sides are doing what gets done in a negotiation. Having been around this group, I think guys want to be here, but I also understand this very short saturated window of time that they have to maximize their earning. I am not one to count anybody's pockets even though their pockets are pockets I can only dream of. I will add though for consideration that there is no market like New York. And while it may not be on the contract, what is there by virtue of being in the market and being an NBA champion? And I will allow both sides to do what they need to do, but I understand the nature of these things. They can get terse, that's just how it goes."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TAmUT63HKMg&t=252s

**4. Monica McNutt — "You've got a coach that has been a part of a staff that has done it." — Monica McNutt on the Knicks' potential to repeat as champions** [14:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TAmUT63HKMg&t=870s)

Monica McNutt: "You've got a coach that has been a part of a staff that has done it. And I think that is an X-factor that maybe is a little bit underrated in how to repeat the conversation. I also think you've got a leader in **Jalen Brunson** who probably is of that same ilk of guys that are as unique as **Stephen Curry**, who was obviously the centerpiece of the Warriors when they were able to do it eight years ago. I think beyond that, you maintain a lot of continuity and this group now has taken an exponential leap in their confidence and their equity one to another, which means they also can raise the bar in terms of the accountability that will be required to achieve such a feat."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TAmUT63HKMg&t=870s

**5. Ian Begley — "I don't think you table the talks once the season starts." — Ian Begley and Matt Spendley on the timeline for a KAT extension** [17:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TAmUT63HKMg&t=1051s)

**Matt Spendley:** "If a KAT extension doesn't happen before the start of the season, does the front office kick the can down the road or will the focus be to get it done sometime during the year?"

**Ian Begley:** "I don't think you table the talks once the season starts. And I say that because if you get to January, mid-January, late January, and you've tabled talks, how do you know how to proceed for your future there? It's a tough place to be I think for both sides. But from a Nick perspective, you don't have anything done there. **Karl-Anthony Towns**, he can opt out and he can be a free agent this summer. And the worst-case scenario here for New York, they gave up a lot to get **Karl-Anthony Towns**. The worst-case scenario is he signs somewhere else as a free agent and the Knicks get nothing in return."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TAmUT63HKMg&t=1051s

**6. Ian Begley — "I think that the safest play is Drew Eubanks." — Ian Begley on the Knicks' options for the final roster spot** [24:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TAmUT63HKMg&t=1479s)

**Matt Spendley:** "Who do you think has the best chance to grab the final roster spot?"

**Ian Begley:** "I think that the safest play is **Drew Eubanks**. Going back to before **John Konchar** left the team for personal reasons, they agreed to mutually agree to have him waive there. Before that happened, they were looking at a wing and looking at a big. And if in order to have a wing and a big, if you're still looking that way, you got to make a trade. Who is it? Is it **Pacome Dadiet**? Is it **Miles McBride**? Is it **Bruce Brown**? Is it anybody? To try to open up this spot. But you can't get there to keeping two without trading somebody. So let's assume that that trade doesn't happen, you have to go with a big. And Eubanks would be the safer play. But Wiseman, definitely, **Mike Brown** spoke highly of him. There are people who watched him last week who felt like he looked good. And so, wouldn't be stunned if they went that route, but if you're being conservative and you're the Knicks, you probably go **Drew Eubanks**."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TAmUT63HKMg&t=1479s
