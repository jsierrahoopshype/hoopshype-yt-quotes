## Everyone Thought Dr. J Made $6 Million. They Were Wrong. — *Scripps Sports*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OEa7zrU_6-8

_Speakers identified: Sanya Richards-Ross, Julius Erving_

**1. Julius Erving — "I got a five-year contract for $3 million. I don't know why they call me the $6 million man." — Julius Erving clarifies the reality of his $6 million contract** [01:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OEa7zrU_6-8&t=102s)

**Julius Erving:** "They have so far surpassed economically the numbers we were negotiating for. They go through like a four-second routine, then they step three steps backwards and they take a shot. There is a lot of contact and a lot of extra steps that go on that they could blow the whistle on, and they would have maybe back in the day, but they try to keep it flowing."

## Steve Kerr reveals why he returned to coach Warriors | Dubs Talk | NBC Sports Bay Area — *NBC Sports Bay Area & California*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OTYJRxWCbxQ

_Speakers identified: Kerith Burke, Steve Kerr_

**1. Steve Kerr — "every organization needs alignment and a shared vision" — Steve Kerr on the Warriors' need for alignment and shared vision** [00:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OTYJRxWCbxQ&t=13s)

Steve Kerr: "I have been around long enough to know that every organization needs alignment and a shared vision. Where we have been in the last couple of years has been really interesting. Obviously, we are trying to keep this thing going. The Jimmy trade was brilliant. From the time we got Jimmy to the time he got hurt, we had the fourth-best record in the league. We felt like we can do some real damage here. Then with the injuries and the way last season ended, it felt different. It just felt like we need to examine this and reassess. That is what we did. That is why we took several weeks."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OTYJRxWCbxQ&t=13s

**2. Steve Kerr — "we have to reimagine our way of operating both on and off the court" — Steve Kerr on the need to reimagine the Warriors' operations** [01:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OTYJRxWCbxQ&t=91s)

Steve Kerr: "We have to reimagine our way of operating both on and off the court. We have had a formula here that has been wildly successful. Some of those values we have to get back to. I think we have to tighten the ship a little bit. But I think we also have to change some things on the court. The game has changed quite a bit. It has gotten faster, it has gotten more spread out. How we practice and how we teach the players, film sessions, walkthroughs, detail, we are undergoing some major changes in that regard."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OTYJRxWCbxQ&t=91s

**3. Steve Kerr — "we are no longer in a position to be the wild chaotic team" — Steve Kerr on ending the Warriors' high-turnover style of play** [03:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OTYJRxWCbxQ&t=193s)

Steve Kerr: "One thing that I have always done here is I have told Draymond and Steph in front of the whole team, you guys are allowed to turn it over five, six times a game combined because you are making all the plays and the ratio frankly was worth it. They made so many good plays that if they made five or six turnovers, it was fine. I am not going to do that anymore because we are no longer in a position to be the wild chaotic team. So for Draymond and Steph, they need to understand that it starts with them. We cannot be that team anymore. The league is different, our team is different."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OTYJRxWCbxQ&t=193s

**4. Steve Kerr — "I cannot even imagine a coach being luckier ever in the history of the game than I am" — Steve Kerr on the unique experience of coaching Stephen Curry** [04:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OTYJRxWCbxQ&t=287s)

Steve Kerr: "I cannot even imagine a coach being luckier ever in the history of the game than I am to coach **Stephen Curry**, not just the player but the human being, the person. It is so fun seeing him every day. He brings light to every room that he is in, even in the dark times, and that is his magic power. I think he is someone who just brings hope and brings light and brings energy, and the way that sustains a team's journey, it just makes my job so much easier."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OTYJRxWCbxQ&t=287s

**5. Steve Kerr — "Steph will always be the Warriors" — Steve Kerr on Stephen Curry's commitment to the Warriors** [06:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OTYJRxWCbxQ&t=373s)

Steve Kerr: "When we talked the end of last year, I asked him if that was a consideration and the first thing he said was I am not moving my family. My family is so important to me, they are happy here and he said I do not want to go anywhere. He understands the power and the value of being the face of the franchise not only while he is playing but even beyond. He is going to be Kobe for the Lakers, **Tim Duncan** for the Spurs. Steph will always be the Warriors and that is pretty cool."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OTYJRxWCbxQ&t=373s

**6. Steve Kerr — "I want Draymond shooting more threes" — Steve Kerr on Draymond Green's need to shoot more threes** [07:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OTYJRxWCbxQ&t=469s)

Steve Kerr: "Draymond and I have talked a lot about his role, about reimagining who he is as a player. I think every player as he gets older has to rethink how he goes about his business. But it is especially important for Draymond because our team stylistically has to change. The two-man game with Steph and Draymond is always going to be a huge part of our identity. But I want Draymond shooting more threes. When he is open and he shoots it, good things happen for us. The offense keeps going."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OTYJRxWCbxQ&t=469s

**7. Steve Kerr — "when Draymond shoots a three, the possession goes really well for us" — Steve Kerr on the Warriors' high points per possession with Draymond** [09:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OTYJRxWCbxQ&t=568s)

Steve Kerr: "One of the things that our analytics department brought me last year is when Draymond shoots a three, the possession goes really well for us. We have a really high points per possession for a lot of the reasons I just mentioned. Part of the way we are playing now is five out. We did not do that for most of my era, my run here. Two bigs who are more playing off the elbows. Now we are going to be playing more five out on the perimeter. So yeah, we need Draymond shooting threes and playing out on the perimeter in the corners more than he ever has before."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OTYJRxWCbxQ&t=568s

**8. Steve Kerr — "it is a setback for our team, it is a setback for Kristaps" — Steve Kerr on Kristaps Porzingis' injury and training camp absence** [11:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OTYJRxWCbxQ&t=674s)

Steve Kerr: "It is a setback for our team, it is a setback for Kristaps. I feel bad for him and I hope he is back soon. Obviously, anytime you get injuries, health matters. Your first concern is just let us help him get better and so that is definitely the case our training staff is already on that and we will welcome him back whenever he gets back. He is a veteran player, he knows this league so well, so I am not too concerned with him missing things in camp. We would just like to get him back as soon as we can because he is a really good player."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OTYJRxWCbxQ&t=674s
