# Gregg Popovich speaks at Don Nelson's celebration of life in Dallas — *KENS 5: Your San Antonio News Source*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CXElb2kmmxQ

_Speakers identified: Gregg Popovich, Don Nelson_

**1. Gregg Popovich — "most of the stuff that I ran in San Antonio, I stole from you" — Gregg Popovich on his coaching debt to Don Nelson** [00:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CXElb2kmmxQ&t=16s)

Gregg Popovich: "For that coach, I apologize because I didn't deserve it. Most of the stuff that I ran in San Antonio, I stole from you and I just tweaked it because I had different players. And that's the truth. I'm trying to recover. I got a little PTSD here looking around at all these people that were opponents mostly."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CXElb2kmmxQ&t=16s

**2. Gregg Popovich — "it's emblematic of what Don Nelson did for almost all of us in the room" — Don Nelson's lasting impact on his former players and coaches** [01:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CXElb2kmmxQ&t=65s)

Gregg Popovich: "It's obviously sad for everyone that it has to be an event like this for everybody to get together, but it's emblematic of what **Don Nelson** did for almost all of us in the room. He had some effect on all of us to help us along the way and do some things. That's who he was. He's a special guy in that respect."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CXElb2kmmxQ&t=65s

**3. Gregg Popovich — "on the financial side, we all know that he's a wheeler dealer" — Gregg Popovich on Don Nelson's financial and contract negotiations** [03:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CXElb2kmmxQ&t=210s)

Gregg Popovich: "On the financial side, we all know that he's a wheeler dealer. And so when I went back to San Antonio and they hired me there, I got a five-year contract and it went up 50,000 each year. I said, well, I got to go home and talk to my wife and I got a couple calls to make. And of course, **Don Nelson** was the call. I told him the deal and he said, you're a big dummy, you're going back there, and this is how you're going to do it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CXElb2kmmxQ&t=210s

**4. Gregg Popovich — "I didn't really know Don Nelson except for passing by him once in 1988" — Gregg Popovich's first meeting with Don Nelson in 1988** [05:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CXElb2kmmxQ&t=335s)

Gregg Popovich: "I didn't really know **Don Nelson** except for passing by him once in 1988. I think it was in Cologne, Germany at the final four for Europe. I had just gotten to San Antonio with **Larry Brown** as an assistant and I begged our general manager **Bob Bass** to let me go to Europe. I guaranteed him there was a million players over there as good or better than a lot of the NBA guys. So they let me go and there was this one lunch room where everybody would meet. There weren't many from the NBA, but **Don Nelson** was there. He was looking at everybody and I knew I was in the right place."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CXElb2kmmxQ&t=335s

**5. Gregg Popovich — "he goes, young fella, you got no shot" — Don Nelson's blunt assessment of Gregg Popovich's coaching prospects** [06:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CXElb2kmmxQ&t=417s)

Gregg Popovich: "We just walked by each other and he bent down and he goes, young fella, you got no shot. And I took me a while and I remembered, I figured out what he was talking about. I went back to **Don Nelson** and he said, yeah, he goes, I'm going to Golden State, he says I'm all locked up, that's where I'm going."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CXElb2kmmxQ&t=417s

**6. Gregg Popovich — "he offered me the job to be with Don Nelson and Paul Pressey there at Golden State" — Gregg Popovich on joining Don Nelson's coaching staff** [08:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CXElb2kmmxQ&t=500s)

Gregg Popovich: "He dragged me in his office the next day, put on the film, and he said, tell me what you see here, tell me what's going on, tell me the good and the bad. So we did the film for a couple hours and he offered me the job to be with **Don Nelson** and **Paul Pressey** there at Golden State. And those were my two happiest years in the NBA."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CXElb2kmmxQ&t=500s

**7. Gregg Popovich — "it was hard to lose with Tim and Tony and Manu and David in his twilight" — Gregg Popovich on the difficulty of coaching against Don Nelson** [09:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CXElb2kmmxQ&t=540s)

Gregg Popovich: "It was hard to lose with Tim and Tony and Manu and David in his twilight. It's hard to lose those games. So if I was such a good coach, I should have been undefeated during all that time. But I thank **Don Nelson** for all that. And again, like he said, he does those things for everybody because his heart is bigger than the moon and it's as soft as marshmallows when you get to know him. That's who he was to me."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CXElb2kmmxQ&t=540s
