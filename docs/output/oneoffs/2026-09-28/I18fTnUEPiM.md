**2. Luka Doncic — "I play a lot of games against him. He's a great rim protector." — Luka Doncic on the value of adding Walker Kessler** [00:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I18fTnUEPiM&t=37s)

Luka Doncic: "I play a lot of games against him. It's very tough to play against him. Obviously we know he's a great rim protector, but what I saw this year, how he improved his game offensively, how he can pick and pop also, and then obviously the pick and roll with me and him, I think that will be amazing, so I really wanted him."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I18fTnUEPiM&t=37s

**3. Luka Doncic — "I would say championship. I think we got to look at that." — Luka Doncic sets championship expectations for the Lakers** [02:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I18fTnUEPiM&t=158s)

Luka Doncic: "I would say championship. I think we got to look at that. I feel like I say this stuff a lot, but if you don't think we can win a championship, then you shouldn't be here. So, honestly, I think we have a great group, we just got to show up every day and give everything we got."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I18fTnUEPiM&t=158s

**4. Austin Reaves — "You never want to see guys that you've been with for multiple years leave." — Austin Reaves on the departure of longtime Lakers teammates** [03:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I18fTnUEPiM&t=217s)

Austin Reaves: "You never want to see guys that you've been with for multiple years leave or be on a different team. You got **LeBron James**, **Rui Hachimura**, **Jackson Hayes**, you go down the list of guys that I've been with for multiple years that have real relationships with, on and off the court, but that's kind of how the business works."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I18fTnUEPiM&t=217s

**5. Austin Reaves — "It's what comes with it, but at the same time, if I go out there and win." — Austin Reaves discusses the expectations of being a max player** [04:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I18fTnUEPiM&t=254s)

Austin Reaves: "It's what comes with it, but at the same time, if I go out there and take care of my business and play the way that I know I can play, nothing really is to be talked about. And along those lines is winning. If you go out and win, it cures everything."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I18fTnUEPiM&t=254s

**6. Austin Reaves — "Just not even on court, all off court stuff, I enjoy being around him." — Austin Reaves on his close relationship with Luka Doncic** [05:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I18fTnUEPiM&t=311s)

Austin Reaves: "Just not even on court, all off court stuff, I enjoy being around him. Enjoy bowling at his house, playing some golf together, which he did beat me a couple days ago, which was a little crazy. He's someone that I'll be friends with for as long as I live. I respect what he's just done for basketball, how he carries himself, and on court, he makes my life easy."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I18fTnUEPiM&t=311s

Walker Kessler: "I didn't know what to expect, honestly. I'm a believer in whatever happens is meant to happen, and so I didn't really know. And so to be here now, I'm just so grateful and excited."

**8. Walker Kessler — "Having one of the best point guards to play, voicing that belief in you." — Walker Kessler on the impact of Luka Doncic's belief** [07:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I18fTnUEPiM&t=456s)
