**1. Dylan — "It's my first year and you can't keep moving forward if you don't have a positive attitude." — Dylan on his first NBA playoff experience and future growth** [00:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V-5ME6MRTxA&t=12s)

**3. Julian — "I don't think anybody pegged us to be in the championship come November when the game started." — Julian on the team exceeding expectations during the season** [04:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V-5ME6MRTxA&t=269s)
