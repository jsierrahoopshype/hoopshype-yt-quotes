**5. Doc Rivers — "the first time I saw Mike Fratello was my first head coach. Mike's like 5'5" — Doc Rivers on the evolution of player respect for coaches** [11:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6CoRQ2fHbhE&t=696s)

Doc Rivers: "The first time I saw **Mike Fratello** was my first head coach. Mike's like 5'5, and he stands up and he's the head coach, and the respect was given.
