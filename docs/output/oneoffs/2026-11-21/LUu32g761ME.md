**2. Dwight Howard — "It can be difficult when your ego can be bigger than what you're trying to accomplish." — Dwight Howard on the emotional difficulty of transitioning from superstar to role player** [02:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LUu32g761ME&t=138s)

Dwight Howard: "It was very difficult coming from a place where I was the number one guy as far as shots, getting touches on the floor, getting minutes, starting. To go from that to maybe not playing some games, to basically sometimes being a cheerleader on the bench. It can be difficult when your ego can be bigger than what you're trying to accomplish. And what I was trying to accomplish was to win a championship. So the ego had to be put to the side, and it was a great experience. I feel like I did so much less, but more."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LUu32g761ME&t=138s

**3. Dwight Howard — "The night before the game, I would literally be playing the game in my head while I'm sleep." — Dwight Howard on his pre-game visualization routine and superstition** [06:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LUu32g761ME&t=361s)

Dwight Howard: "The night before the game, I would literally be playing the game in my head while I'm sleep. I would wake up drenched in sweat because I could see the game that's about to happen. Usually before the game, I would know if we're going to win or lose just by the energy that I'm feeling when we're warming up, watching the other team. I do stuff like I put my left shoe on first before the game. I used to have to, before every game, I tried to take a dump. That was my little superstitious thing, take a dump before all the games."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LUu32g761ME&t=361s

The head coach at the time was Kevin McHale. So for me, I'm looking at it from a standpoint of this is a chance for me to maybe grow as a player. So I'm thinking, oh, this is perfect. Jeremy Lin is here, Chandler Parsons is here, we got shooters, we got defenders. I mean, we made it to the Western Conference Finals.
