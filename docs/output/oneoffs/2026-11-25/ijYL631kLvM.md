**2. Jared McCain — "I said I'm going to be in that green room next year." — Jared McCain on manifesting his NBA draft selection** [04:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ijYL631kLvM&t=252s)

Jared McCain: "I like to write notes when significant things happen in my life, whether it's good or bad. This one is probably my favorite one. It was my freshman year of college and we were all watching the draft and I got back from the dorm. This is the day of the draft, so it was 11:00 p.m., June 22nd, 2023, and I said I'm going to be in that green room next year. The green room is at the draft, they invite the top 20, 30 picks, and I'm going to hear my name be called. I know I am. I'm going to pick my suit and hug my mom, dad, and brother so tight. I'm going to cry, it's going to be euphoric. Keep working Jared and it's going to happen."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ijYL631kLvM&t=252s
