Especially with players like **James Harden** that can do more than just shoot threes. I don't know why they was so stuck on shooting threes, but I'm happy. And without **Chris Paul**, I think that's why, because **Chris Paul** is more of a leader. He would have got fouled, tried to do the post-up mid-range like he do.
