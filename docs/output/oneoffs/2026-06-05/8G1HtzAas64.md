**5. Jeremy Lin — "Score-first point guards can win championships and have proven to do that and have won many championships." — Jeremy Lin on why score-first point guards can win championships** [10:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8G1HtzAas64&t=645s)

Jeremy Lin: "As the game has evolved, I think that's more and more where things are trending, whether it is a **Steph** or a **Kyrie**, these types of score-first point guards. And I think it's all about who is next to them, who they're being paired with. **Steph** worked great because of Klay and Draymond and even **KD** for two of the championships. **Steph** is a Hall of Famer and one of the best point guards ever to play the game. And same with **Kyrie**. And so with Brunson, they let Brunson do what Brunson is elite at, which is scoring, which is taking over games and putting the ball in the hoop."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8G1HtzAas64&t=645s
