**1. Bobby Marks — "If you sign LeBron James to a $15 million contract, you don't have room to fill out the roster." — Golden State's salary cap constraints limit their ability to sign LeBron and AD** [01:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qEe3e8kBzGU&t=84s)

Bobby Marks: "Why it's complicated is where Golden State is with salary right now. With the **Kristaps Porzingis** contract, they're at $177 million in salary. The first apron hard cap is $209 million. That basically leaves them $32 million below that number. Remember, **Draymond Green** opted out of that $27 million number. You're expecting that it's probably going to come in somewhere between that $20 million range. If you sign **LeBron James** to a $15 million contract, you don't have room to fill out the rest of your roster here. So, you will have to go out and make a trade and clear out some room to do so."
