**10. Draymond Green — "My thoughts on James Dolan refusing to go into the second apron this off-season despite winning a championship." — Draymond Green explains why avoiding the second apron is crucial** [33:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F7PXwNRGRLc&t=2038s)

Draymond Green: "My thoughts on **James Dolan** refusing to go into the second apron this off-season despite winning a championship is probably different than most people. I am sure everybody is like, oh man, he just won a championship and you are in New York City, you should go into the second apron. I disagree. The second apron is debilitating. I said this before the deal was signed, I said it after the deal is signed, I am going to say it again. Going into the second apron is debilitating. If you go into the second apron, everybody is like, oh man, he should just be willing to go into the second apron. No, he should not. The second apron is a hard cap and once you hit that hard cap, it is debilitating. You lose draft picks, you lose exceptions, you lose roster spots, you lose everything. And guess what, you no longer can compete for a championship. That is why you keep seeing everybody duck under the second apron. The second apron has hurt everyone. It has hurt the NBA as a whole. As an entirety, the second apron has been awful. It will be interesting to see if the league and the NBPA will figure this out and get rid of the second apron. It has unintended consequences like I said from the very beginning. It is awful, it is not good for anyone."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F7PXwNRGRLc&t=2038s
