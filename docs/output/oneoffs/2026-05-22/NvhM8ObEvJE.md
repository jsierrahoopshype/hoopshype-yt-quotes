# The Second Act: Kevin Love reflects on the biggest moments from his NBA career — *Utah Jazz*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvhM8ObEvJE

_Speakers identified: KEVIN LOVE_

**1. KEVIN LOVE — "lessons I had to learn the hard way, but also lessons I've learned that helped me in a major way" — Kevin Love reflects on lessons learned throughout his 18-year NBA career** [01:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvhM8ObEvJE&t=93s)

"A lot of good, truly a lot of good and still being here after 18 years, but also there's things that I just had to learn. I had to go through the process, both on and off the floor, away from it, and a lot of moments that I'm truly grateful for and lessons I had to learn the hard way, but also lessons I've learned that helped me in a major way from this point on."

**2. KEVIN LOVE — "playing Tim Duncan, the greatest power forward of all time at 20 years old where he for so many more years had so much more to give was really special." — Kevin Love on playing against Tim Duncan as a 20-year-old rookie** [02:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvhM8ObEvJE&t=125s)

"I played my first game. Drafted at 19, played my first game at 20, playing **Tim Duncan**, the greatest power forward of all time at 20 years old where he for so many more years had so much more to give was really special. But it was a learning experience for sure."

**3. KEVIN LOVE — "This was a breakout year for me. I had a 30-30 game. Coach Rambis at the time said, 'You're not coming out of this game.'" — Kevin Love discusses his breakout 2010-11 season, including a 30-30 game and first All-Star selection** [02:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvhM8ObEvJE&t=168s)

"I got my body had gone in a lot better shape. This was a breakout year for me. I had a 30-30 game. **Coach Rambis** at the time said, 'You're not coming out of this game.' Life has a funny way and basketball gods have a funny way of allowing things to unfold as they should. 53 straight double-doubles, had made my first All-Star team."

**4. KEVIN LOVE — "I just got to spend the time with Kobe like tipping champagne, but I look at that and I look at this photo and I think naturally, I think most guys would gravitate towards Kobe in the middle." — Kevin Love cherishes his London Olympics experience and bond with Kobe Bryant** [04:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvhM8ObEvJE&t=248s)

"I just got to spend the time with **Kobe** like tipping champagne, but I look at that and I look at this photo and I think naturally, I think most guys would gravitate towards **Kobe** in the middle. So those pictures are extremely special and that relationship forged with him."

**5. KEVIN LOVE — "everybody's going to go through it in their different way and heal from it in a different way." — Kevin Love emphasizes understanding teammates' backgrounds and individual mental health journeys** [06:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvhM8ObEvJE&t=367s)

"One of the things I like to do is understand my teammates, where they come from, what it's like where they're from, what it's like at home. And while those stories, none of these stories are all the same and you can't say I'm going to coach this guy or treat this guy, even with mental health, you can't put somebody in a box and say, 'Okay, this is how we're going to work with them,' because everybody moves through life differently. You might suffer from the same affliction, you might suffer from the same thing, but everybody's going to go through it in their different way and heal from it in a different way."

**6. KEVIN LOVE — "I still have this act. I feel like this is my second act. It's not done yet." — Kevin Love describes his current phase as his 'second act' focused on intentionality and doing good** [08:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvhM8ObEvJE&t=496s)

"I still have this act. I feel like this is my second act. It's not done yet. I know the gap and I know everything's closing in, but I do try to be as intentional as I possibly can be in again, setting a good foundation, acting on it and just overall just trying to be good and do good. That's what I got."
