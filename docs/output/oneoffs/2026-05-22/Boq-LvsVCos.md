# Euroleague coaches slam NBA Europe as being a soccer project  — *BallinEurope*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Boq-LvsVCos

**1. "The biggest sports brands in the world don't pay to play. They get paid to play." — FC Barcelona and Real Madrid won't pay NBA license fees** [01:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Boq-LvsVCos&t=98s)

"I also said from the very beginning, I think it's absurd to believe that the biggest sports brands in the world like **FC Barcelona** or **Real Madrid** would actually be ready to pay a license fee. The biggest sports brands in the world don't pay to play. They get paid to play. This is very important to understand."

**2. "I don't believe anything until I see a business plan presented or until I see the shareholders raise their hand." — Skepticism about business plans until official announcements** [02:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Boq-LvsVCos&t=125s)

"The next thing is I always say in any kind of business development, I don't believe anything until I see a business plan presented or until I see the shareholders raise their hand in a board meeting and give out a press release. Until this moment, I never believed anything."

**3. "Since the NBA presented their business plan, we can say that the interest declined from all stakeholders." — Interest in NBA Europe declined after business plan** [02:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Boq-LvsVCos&t=150s)

"So now since the NBA presented their business plan, we can say that the interest declined from all stakeholders. Everyone was hyped, starting from you reporters, starting from EuroLeague clubs, starting from fans, starting any stakeholder group was so anxious, what will happen? Suddenly, the interest declined."

**4. "The most enthusiastic, outspoken person of the NBA project was Tony Parker. We heard that he just is ready to re-sign with the EuroLeague." — Tony Parker's shift from NBA project to EuroLeague** [03:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Boq-LvsVCos&t=180s)

"And I want to give the example of the most enthusiastic outspoken person of the NBA project was **Tony Parker**. We heard that he just is ready to re-sign with the EuroLeague. So I know a lot of potential investors and many stakeholders, like I said, were very enthusiastic, that suddenly became very pessimistic."

**5. "I think UEFA knows best how to maximize the European sport market and that they are always the example we should meet." — UEFA knows best how to maximize European sport market** [03:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Boq-LvsVCos&t=205s)

"Instead of expecting miracles from the NBA, we should look to learn from those football clubs that the NBA tried to lure into business. And I think UEFA knows best how to maximize the European sport market and that they are always the example we should meet."

**6. "Let's sit down on the same table and exchange our thoughts and come up with a joint idea." — Call for collaboration among all basketball stakeholders** [03:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Boq-LvsVCos&t=226s)

"Still, I believe that an honest collaboration between everyone, the NBA, the EuroLeague, FIBA, UEFA, any potential investors, anyone, is good for the benefit of basketball. But I always say, let's sit down on the same table and exchange our thoughts and come up with a joint idea."
