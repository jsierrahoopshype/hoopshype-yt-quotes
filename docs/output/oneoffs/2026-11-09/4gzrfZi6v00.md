# Taylor Jenkins on Building the Next Great Era | Behind the Bucks presented by Gallagher — *Milwaukee Bucks*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4gzrfZi6v00

_Speakers identified: Dave Koehn, Taylor Jenkins_

**1. Taylor Jenkins — "I come in knowing there's still so much work to be done in a lot of areas." — Taylor Jenkins on building a new culture for the Milwaukee Bucks** [01:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4gzrfZi6v00&t=72s)

Taylor Jenkins: "For me, it still feels like day one. Every day I try to come in just with a renewed freshness with my mentality, my spirit. Five months it flies by, especially in an off-season with a lot of work done in a lot of areas from roster construction to developing a culture and identity, our processes of how we're going to collaborate every single day just amongst our staff, building relationships with our players. So, I come in knowing there's still so much work to be done in a lot of areas. So, to think that it's been five months is crazy and we're already a few games into the season as well, but I was coming with a day one mentality. There's always something to establish, always something to improve on, that get better mentality."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4gzrfZi6v00&t=72s

**2. Taylor Jenkins — "the three pillars that matter the most: our compete, together, and get better." — The three core pillars defining the Milwaukee Bucks' new identity** [02:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4gzrfZi6v00&t=142s)

Taylor Jenkins: "Day one of training camp we sat down and before we even talked to X's and O's and how we're going to try to create advantages on offense and take away advantages on defense, my whole message was who we are. We've got to build to get there. We're not going to be that iteration of who the Bucks are going to be day one, but really establishing who we're going to be about, what's going to be our day-in and day-out process, what's going to be our mentality, what are the things that we're going to be able to fall back on. I talked about a number of different things, but always came back to the three pillars that matter the most: our compete, together, and get better. Every facet of what we do here on and off the floor always comes back to how competitive are we."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4gzrfZi6v00&t=142s

**3. Taylor Jenkins — "the emblems and the logos, we just kind of place it in different spots that hopefully organically" — Using team branding to reinforce the Bucks' core values** [05:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4gzrfZi6v00&t=352s)

Taylor Jenkins: "Sometimes it's very forward and direct and sometimes it's subliminal. Messaging that we have on the walls or on our schedule and the emblems and the logos, we just kind of place it in different spots that hopefully organically it's brought up in conversation. In the middle of a game, in a heated moment, we can talk about our togetherness. It's a talk at halftime or pregame, we can talk about are we truly getting better? Are we trying to build from what we experienced in the first half to the second half? Every opportunity that presents itself, we can always draw back to compete, together, get better and try to continue to always re-educate ourselves and remind ourselves, that's the stuff that matters at the end of the day."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4gzrfZi6v00&t=352s

**4. Taylor Jenkins — "I'm not one to talk about the wins and the losses. We want to build championship habits." — Taylor Jenkins prioritizes building championship habits over wins and losses** [08:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4gzrfZi6v00&t=491s)

Taylor Jenkins: "I'm definitely a big picture thinker. I try to think about the future. Sometimes I think about it probably way too much. I'm already thinking about next off-season and next season, what we've got to be planning for. But I want everyone thinking about that way, that if we're truly building something, our day-to-day process is going to be that springboard to accomplish the things that we're thinking about the future, our North Star, the goals that we're going to set for ourselves. But I sit here and I'm not one to talk about the wins and the losses. We want to build championship habits. Hopefully that brings more championships back to Milwaukee."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4gzrfZi6v00&t=491s

**5. Taylor Jenkins — "there's going to be failure. I mean, you can always bet on that on any given night." — Taylor Jenkins on using adversity to build team character** [15:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4gzrfZi6v00&t=922s)

Taylor Jenkins: "I would say probably one of the guarantees in life, let alone in basketball, there's going to be failure. I mean, you can always bet on that on any given night. There's going to be a turnover, there's going to be a miss shot, there's going to be a bad run, there's going to be a loss, there's going to be a call that doesn't go your way, there's going to be an atmosphere that, you know, isn't the most uplifting. In life you're going to face adversity, you're going to face failure. How do you harness the ability to have that strength of character? I think it reveals your character, what you're made of."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4gzrfZi6v00&t=922s

**6. Taylor Jenkins — "Don't wait until you get your second contract. Don't wait until you're hitting your prime." — Taylor Jenkins' development philosophy for young Milwaukee Bucks players** [18:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4gzrfZi6v00&t=1126s)

Taylor Jenkins: "Especially in a rookie season, I press upon these guys establish your foundation now. Don't wait until you get your second contract. Don't wait until you're hitting your prime. Don't don't fall prey to that or subject to that because then you're going to be so delayed in what gifts and great opportunities you can have right now, early in your career, as a rookie, second year player, whatever it is. It's how yes, you're trying to fit into our basketball system, but what's just your daily approach? What's your mentality going to be to nutrition, to recovery, to your sleep, to your preparation for your opponent, watching film of yourself? How are you coming in with a worker mentality?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4gzrfZi6v00&t=1126s
