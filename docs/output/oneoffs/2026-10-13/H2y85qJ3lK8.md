**1. Josh Giddey — "a lot of teams are playing faster and up tempo where they're trying to get shots" — The evolution of NBA transition play and pace** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H2y85qJ3lK8&t=0s)

Josh Giddey: "The NBA is transitioning now where a lot of teams are playing faster and up tempo where they're trying to get shots in the first six to eight seconds of the shot clock, whether it's layups or threes. Ideally, you want to get good shots. You don't just want to come down and start bombing up shots for the sake of it, but if you can get good shots in the first eight seconds of the shot clock, a lot of teams have started to move that way and the game is trending in that direction."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H2y85qJ3lK8&t=0s

**2. Josh Giddey — "my job as a point guard is to come back and get the ball" — Point guard decision-making in semi-transition offense** [01:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H2y85qJ3lK8&t=90s)

Josh Giddey: "My job as a point guard is to come back and get the ball so I can orchestrate the offense and get us into our set. It's semi-transition because the defense is set, but there's a lot of space between the slot towards the baseline or the middle. I'm looking at **Jock Landale** who is our five trailing the play. In an ideal world, he'd be sitting at the top of the key to show presentation and plug up the middle of the floor, but I see he's a little late. I hit this guy in and out as my first move, and if the big is a half a step down the floor, that lane for me to go isn't there. But I see he's a little hard up the floor and worried about Jock who can shoot the three, so as I start to get downhill, there's an open gap and I'm able to get to the rim."

**4. Josh Giddey — "you want to alert the bigs. When they get a rebound, they don't always know where the point guard is." — Point guard responsibilities after a defensive rebound** [03:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H2y85qJ3lK8&t=208s)

Josh Giddey: "First job as a point guard is to alert the bigs. When they get a rebound, they don't always know where the point guard is, so you want to let them know where you are. **Jack McVeigh** does a good job here, he gets it and you can see I'm showing my hands and ready to go. This is a real transition, this is the fast break here because no one is set. Everyone is still running down the floor. I'm looking, there's a lot of options to attack here because no one is set. Everyone is worried about getting a matchup, and in transition, a lot of the time defenses will teach you, you don't have a man specifically, you just got to get a man. That means find the nearest guy and matchup. The idea in transition is to try to stop the ball."

**7. Josh Giddey — "I've played with a lot of smart bigs who, when they rebound, their first instinct is not to port the ball" — The importance of passing to teammates in transition** [06:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H2y85qJ3lK8&t=386s)
