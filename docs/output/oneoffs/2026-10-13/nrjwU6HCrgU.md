**2. John Collins — "Most other players are just trying to get used to the Pistons culture and what we require" — John Collins on adjusting to the Detroit Pistons' team culture** [01:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nrjwU6HCrgU&t=63s)

John Collins: "The heart, the intensity, the grit, just the identity of who we are as Pistons is different historically. Even to now with JB, what he wants us to do and how he wants us to play. So, again, just trying to fit into the culture and bring what I can."
