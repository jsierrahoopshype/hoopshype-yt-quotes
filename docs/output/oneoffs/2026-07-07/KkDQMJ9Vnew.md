# How does Donovan Mitchell’s max extension impact the Cavs' pursuit of LeBron James? | SportsCenter — *NBA on ESPN*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KkDQMJ9Vnew

_Speakers identified: Kevin Negandhi, Shams Charania_

**1. Shams Charania — "Donovan Mitchell has been hands-on with his approach to trying to help the Cavs" — Donovan Mitchell's extension signals commitment to the Cavaliers' roster upgrades** [00:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KkDQMJ9Vnew&t=20s)

**Kevin Negandhi:** "We know the Cavs are among the leaders to land **LeBron James**. How does Mitchell's extension impact that decision?"

**Shams Charania:** "It certainly helps because here you have a franchise cornerstone in Cleveland now doubling down on the franchise. This is the second extension **Donovan Mitchell** has signed since joining the Cavs in 2022. I am told **Donovan Mitchell** has been hands-on with his approach to trying to help the Cavs and work with the Cavs on roster upgrades after their run to the Eastern Conference Finals, and that does include the pursuit of **LeBron James**. **Donovan Mitchell** has made it clear behind the scenes that he would embrace **LeBron James** coming and joining him in Cleveland."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KkDQMJ9Vnew&t=20s

**2. Shams Charania — "James Harden will be going back to the Cavaliers just depending on how this shakes out" — James Harden's future with the Cavaliers depends on roster upgrades** [01:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KkDQMJ9Vnew&t=93s)

**Kevin Negandhi:** "Let's get to one guy that played with the Cavs this past season, that is **James Harden**. He opted out of his deal with Cleveland. What can you tell us about his future with the Cavs?"

**Shams Charania:** "**James Harden** will be going back to the Cavaliers just depending on how this shakes out with **LeBron James** and other upgrades the Cavs are able to make. He will be back, my understanding is on a multi-year deal. Whether that is a two-year deal, a three-year deal, a lot of that shakes out after the Cavs' offseason moves. But I am told **James Harden**, his reps are on the same page with the Cavs. They want to give the organization as much flexibility as possible to see what upgrades they can make, because they know there needs to be some level of upgrade on the roster, especially on the wings. So whether it is **LeBron James**, a guy like **Jonathan Kuminga**, this is a Cavs group led by **Kobe Altman**, president of basketball operations, that is being aggressive here in the market, and that starts with **LeBron James**."
