**1. Norman Powell — "the best thing besides doing something that's never been done for a country was just the brotherhood" — Norman Powell on the brotherhood and resilience of the 2019 Raptors championship team** [00:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZb9IDFsjNI&t=59s)

Norman Powell: "The best thing besides doing something that's never been done for a country was just the brotherhood. When you are in the trenches and you are going through the series, you are going through the battles and you are trying to figure out how to bounce back, those moments and those memories that you can reconnect with now that we have all left and moved on, that's the true beauty of it."

**2. Klay Thompson — "being on that team with those guys was like before we all became who we were" — Klay Thompson on the camaraderie of the 2014 Team USA World Cup roster** [03:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZb9IDFsjNI&t=182s)

Klay Thompson: "Being on that team with those guys was like before we all became who we were as players in the league, whether it be **Kyrie Irving** or **James Harden**, Steph Curry, Boogie Cousins. **Kenneth Faried** was killing it, **Anthony Davis**, **Rudy Gay** was our vet, even **DeMar DeRozan** was like a dunk contest every day. I was talking to Mike who is out here helping me train. I was like, **Derrick Rose** was the most famous player on our team because he was coming off his MVP season. I was out one night in Barcelona and I saw Derrick in the streets. I was like, man, come to the club with me. It would be a great time and he couldn't even move around because the locals were on him. It was crazy."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZb9IDFsjNI&t=182s

**3. Norman Powell — "I saw it as an opportunity to connect with my roots" — Norman Powell on his motivation for playing for the Jamaican national basketball team** [05:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZb9IDFsjNI&t=305s)

Norman Powell: "Playing for Team Jamaica and trying to revive the basketball culture and scene there has been everything. The crazy thing about it was my dad was born in Kingston, but I don't really know much about my Jamaican side. I have always wanted to know where I come from, know that side of the family, speak a little Patois. I saw it as a moment when Coach Nurse reached out about him trying to restart the national team after 15 years of not having a team, I jumped on it because I saw it as an opportunity to connect with my roots."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZb9IDFsjNI&t=305s

**4. Norman Powell — "I'm going to go with Russell Westbrook, Baron Davis, Aaron Holiday, Bill Walton at the four, and Kareem" — Norman Powell selects his all-time starting five of UCLA Bruins** [07:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZb9IDFsjNI&t=453s)

Norman Powell: "I'm going to go with **Russell Westbrook**, **Baron Davis**, **Aaron Holiday**, **Bill Walton** at the four, and **Kareem Abdul-Jabbar** at the five. I have to take **Kevin Love** off, not because I don't like **Kevin Love**, but I have to put **Bill Walton** at the four, rest in peace, and Kareem at the five."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZb9IDFsjNI&t=453s

**5. Klay Thompson — "I'm going to go with Craig Ehlo at the three. Craig is a legendary Coug" — Klay Thompson selects his all-time starting five of Washington State Cougars** [09:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZb9IDFsjNI&t=543s)

Klay Thompson: "I'm going to go with **Craig Ehlo** at the three. Craig is a legendary Coug. I know he is known for getting jaded on by MJ, but the man was guarding for a reason. I'm sorry Ron, I'm sorry Mr. Harper, but Craig had a lot of heart. He had to put Craig at the three. And then at the one, probably our all-time leading scorer, **Isaac Fontaine**. He is from Sacramento, he got us to the tournament back-to-back years. At the five, I'm going with my guy **Aron Baynes** just because he was my vet, low key, as a freshman. He made the league on a conventional route, went overseas then the D-League at the time, before the G, and played a nice 10-12 years in the league. At the four, I got to give one of these young bucks, probably **Mark Hendrickson**. Crazy pull, played in the NBA and in the MLB."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZb9IDFsjNI&t=543s
