**4. Jonathan Isaac — "I felt like I had this desire to do everything that the NBA has to offer, which I did, and finding that, oh man, I still feel as unfulfilled as I felt chasing that thing." — Jonathan Isaac on his faith journey and finding identity** [56:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L4dKFE4gcu0&t=3397s)

**Cameron Stang:** "You mentioned your faith. For those listening or watching that don't know, tell us a little bit about your faith story and how you came to Lord and all that."

**Jonathan Isaac:** "I grew up in church. I kind of have the same story as a lot of people where you're introduced to faith at a young age, but for it's really just a traditional thing. Your family takes you, you learn the songs, you go to the youth group, but it's not something that you actually own or that you really understand. It wasn't until basketball became what I would say is my god, from middle school, high school, college, I'm focused on I want to be like these guys, I'm going to play in the NBA, I want to do all these things. It wasn't until I was able to experience it all at the highest level in the NBA and feeling like I had this desire to do everything that the NBA has to offer, which I did, and finding that, oh man, I still feel as unfulfilled as I felt chasing that thing. That for me made me take a step back and go, you know what, maybe this faith stuff that I learned as a kid, maybe that really is the answer."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L4dKFE4gcu0&t=3397s
