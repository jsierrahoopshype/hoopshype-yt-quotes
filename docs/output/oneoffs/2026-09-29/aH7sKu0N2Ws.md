**1. Doug Christie — "He is an ultimate professional. His attention to detail is extremely high. He is a coach." — Mike Woodson's coaching style and attention to detail** [00:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aH7sKu0N2Ws&t=32s)

Doug Christie: "He is an ultimate professional. His attention to detail is extremely high. He is a coach. He is not an offensive or defensive coach. He is a coach. He has seen so much that it would be really dumb not to listen to the things that he has to say."

That means we have to care for our teammate enough to make really good passes where we say on time, on target, and take advantage of the situation when it presents itself."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aH7sKu0N2Ws&t=73s

He plays on the ball, off the ball, so there is a lot of things to be happy about with **DeMar DeRozan**."

**6. Doug Christie — "DeAndre is a two-way player. He plays defense, three and D, shoots the ball way better." — DeAndre Hunter's versatility and impact as a two-way player** [05:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aH7sKu0N2Ws&t=352s)
