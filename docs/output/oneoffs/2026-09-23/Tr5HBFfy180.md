**Juancho Hernangomez:** "It was a difficult summer. There were offers from Europe. Maccabi, Fener, all clubs were interested. It was a very personal decision. Thanks to God, I have the position that I have, and I have all the offers. If I did not have offers, it would be easier, but it was not easy to make that decision."

**Javi Gancedo:** "You had several offers, I do not think Panathinaikos was the only one."

**Juancho Hernangomez:** "My brother signed in Barcelona, he signed a great contract. He also wanted to pull me to Barcelona, he called me every day to sign in Barcelona.
