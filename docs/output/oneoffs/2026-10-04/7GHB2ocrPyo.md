# Q&A Pt.2 With Brandin Podziemski — *Brandin Podziemski*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7GHB2ocrPyo

_Speakers identified: Brandin Podziemski_

**1. Brandin Podziemski — "If you're always comparing yourself to somebody else, in your mind you'll never be satisfied enough." — Brandin Podziemski on avoiding the trap of comparing himself to others** [01:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7GHB2ocrPyo&t=62s)

Brandin Podziemski: "A lot of it would come down to comparison, being a thief of joy. If you're always comparing yourself to somebody else, in your mind you'll never be satisfied enough. Everything from that lens is about perspective, because if you're never good enough, how do you ever feel joy, or how do you ever have gratitude for the experiences that you've had? So for me, it's just never comparing myself to others."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7GHB2ocrPyo&t=62s

**2. Brandin Podziemski — "The hardest player I've ever had to guard, I would say would be Kyrie Irving." — Brandin Podziemski names Kyrie Irving as his toughest defensive assignment** [01:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7GHB2ocrPyo&t=97s)

Brandin Podziemski: "The hardest player I've ever had to guard, I would say would be **Kyrie Irving** because he's really good at everything. It's hard to guard players like that. Super fast, shifty, can shoot, can drive, can make plays for others. So him being an all-around player like that, it's probably the hardest player I've ever had to guard."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7GHB2ocrPyo&t=97s

**3. Brandin Podziemski — "Having to guard him and Klay were probably the hardest, too." — Brandin Podziemski on the challenge of guarding Steph Curry and Klay Thompson** [03:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7GHB2ocrPyo&t=190s)

Brandin Podziemski: "Definitely my first training camp, having to guard Steph and chase him around. Just having to guard him, my first training camp and preseason. Still to this day, it's pretty challenging, but I've learned and tried to grow from those moments. So it's always a learning experience when you first come to the league, but having to guard him and Klay were probably the hardest, too."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7GHB2ocrPyo&t=190s
