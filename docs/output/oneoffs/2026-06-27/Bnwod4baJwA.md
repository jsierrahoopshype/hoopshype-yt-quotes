**1. Sam Quinn — "We have to at least have the conversation about his future because he can become a free agent" — Nikola Jokic's future and the possibility of a trade** [02:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bnwod4baJwA&t=123s)

Sam Quinn: "For years and years, what we heard from Bucks fans was it's so unfair that you do this with Giannis and you fake trade him all the time and you say he has to leave Milwaukee. Sam Quinn: "I look at **Nikola Jokic** as a caliber of player that is just very, very, very rarely in this situation. The Thunder could have traded **Kevin Durant** in 2016, they did not. The Warriors could have traded **Kevin Durant** in 2019, they did not.
