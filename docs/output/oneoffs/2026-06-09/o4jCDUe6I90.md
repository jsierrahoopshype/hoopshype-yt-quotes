**3. Karim Lopez — "It was an opportunity to just follow my dreams. It's an opportunity to just get better." — Karim Lopez on the challenge of moving to Spain at 14** [03:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o4jCDUe6I90&t=200s)

Karim Lopez: "It was a challenge for sure. It was not just for me, but also for my family, especially my mom. She was the one that was the most hesitant. She didn't want to let her baby just go at 14 years old to a whole different continent by himself. But it was an opportunity to just follow my dreams. It's an opportunity to just get better, and it's something that I really wanted to do. My dad understood that, and my mom ended up understanding that. It was a process, but at the end, they figured it's what I wanted to do and it's what I needed to do."

**4. Karim Lopez — "It made me mature way quicker. It made me see the real world." — How the European basketball system accelerated Karim Lopez's development** [05:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o4jCDUe6I90&t=310s)

Karim Lopez: "It helped a lot in lots of different ways. It made me mature way quicker. It made me see the real world, see what it is about, and live things that many 14-year-olds don't live. The European style is different to the NBA, more tactical, more of a half-court game, more set plays. It's about expanding, adapting, learning. I learned a lot about basketball, learned a lot about myself, and I feel like I grew in lots of different ways. My IQ improved, which is a big part of my game."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o4jCDUe6I90&t=310s

Karim Lopez: "Going into Year 1 with the Breakers, I didn't necessarily know what to expect. You're going into a whole new environment as a 17-year-old kid. I was super skinny, going against people that are 30, people that have been playing the game for 10-plus years. Coming into Year 2, I knew the team, I knew the league, I knew the pro life. I was also much more confident, having a season under my belt. I was way better, I was faster, I was stronger, I was bigger. I was just better overall."

**6. Karim Lopez — "I'm a really confident shooter, but I had stretches where I was really good and not so good." — Karim Lopez identifies shot consistency as his biggest area for improvement** [11:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o4jCDUe6I90&t=670s)

Karim Lopez: "I'm working on everything in this pre-draft process, but I'll say my shot consistency. I'm a really confident shooter, and I think if you watch the film, you can see it. The shots I was taking, I was not hesitant at all. I was letting it fly even from behind the arc, a couple feet back, or even with a contest. I'm really confident as a shooter, but I had stretches where I was really good and I had stretches where I was not so good. So, getting that consistency up, I feel like it will elevate my game for sure."
