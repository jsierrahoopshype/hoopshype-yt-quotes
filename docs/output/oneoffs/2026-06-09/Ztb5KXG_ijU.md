**1. JR Smith — "that game and that moment actually kind of saved my life" — JR Smith on how his 2004 draft experience changed his life** [01:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ztb5KXG_ijU&t=80s)

JR Smith: "That game and that moment actually kind of saved my life if you want to say it. It was crazy because I had a lot of stuff going on at that time. Everybody was under the assumption I was going to Carolina and they didn't really know the road I would have to take to get to Carolina and that my academics were nowhere near of par."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ztb5KXG_ijU&t=80s

**2. JR Smith — "I probably only heard that a handful of times. It takes me back to where I was" — JR Smith reflects on the pressure of his 2004 NBA Draft night** [02:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ztb5KXG_ijU&t=131s)

JR Smith: "I probably only heard that a handful of times. It takes me back to where I was. I was at home in Jersey, in my parents' living room, and I had a ton of family, local reporters, star ledgers, and all of that. Obviously, I'm sitting right there watching the draft with everybody and I can hear everybody around me talking. If this person goes here, then this team is going to pick you, and if this person goes here, then this team is going to pick you. I get up and I just storm out the room and I go to my parents' bedroom and I hear my name get called while I'm laying across my parents' bed."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ztb5KXG_ijU&t=131s

**3. JR Smith — "Anybody knows who Byron Scott as a coach, probably second to none on you're going to be in shape" — JR Smith on Byron Scott's intense training camp drills** [05:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ztb5KXG_ijU&t=305s)

JR Smith: "Anybody knows who **Byron Scott** as a coach, probably second to none on you're going to be in shape where they're going to make you quit. And we did this drill that lasted, first 35 minutes of practice, and it was all running. And I literally thought to myself for sure I should have went to college."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ztb5KXG_ijU&t=305s

**4. JR Smith — "It's about the name on the back, like not the name on the front" — Chauncey Billups' advice on the importance of your legacy** [08:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ztb5KXG_ijU&t=496s)

JR Smith: "**Chauncey Billups** used to always say, it's about the name on the back, like not the name on the front. You can change the city and you can change wherever you play, but the name on the back, where you come from, make sure you take care of that."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ztb5KXG_ijU&t=496s

**5. JR Smith — "I did not grow up a Knicks fan. I hated the Knicks. I did not like Patrick Ewing" — JR Smith on his childhood dislike for the New York Knicks** [09:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ztb5KXG_ijU&t=561s)

JR Smith: "I did not grow up a Knicks fan. I hated the Knicks. I did not like **Patrick Ewing**. I did not like **John Starks**. I didn't like anything about the Knicks. I was a Bulls fan. I was a **Michael Jordan** fan. **Michael Jordan** is my goat."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ztb5KXG_ijU&t=561s

**6. JR Smith — "I was kind of pissed off because the start of the season I was supposed to start" — JR Smith on his frustration with Mike Woodson's rotation** [12:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ztb5KXG_ijU&t=725s)

JR Smith: "The 2013 season, for me, I was kind of pissed off because the start of the season I was supposed to start and **Mike Woodson** came to me, literally the first day of training camp and told me I wasn't going to start. Like at that point in time, I just felt like I was continuously proving myself."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ztb5KXG_ijU&t=725s

**7. JR Smith — "It kind of felt like a slap in the face to me honestly because I felt like" — JR Smith on feeling slighted by the Sixth Man award** [12:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ztb5KXG_ijU&t=760s)

JR Smith: "It kind of felt like a slap in the face to me honestly because I felt like I should have been an All-Star. I should have been one of the first All-Stars to come off the bench. And we get to the end of the year, you give me this trophy to where it's like, you know, be satisfied, and I was just so upset because I wasn't even a thought."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ztb5KXG_ijU&t=760s

**8. JR Smith — "While I was here, we brought in a person who lied to me. Lied to me to my" — JR Smith on being betrayed by a teammate in New York** [13:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ztb5KXG_ijU&t=815s)

JR Smith: "While I was here, we brought in a person who lied to me. Lied to me to my face multiple times, told things that I told him in secret publicly. And I felt some type of way about it, obviously, which I think most people would. I was really treated like a pawn onto this bigger chess game that they were playing and I didn't appreciate that."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ztb5KXG_ijU&t=815s

**9. JR Smith — "I was in consistent pain and I turned to pills. Took a lot of pills" — JR Smith on his struggle with painkiller addiction** [14:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ztb5KXG_ijU&t=845s)

JR Smith: "I was in consistent pain and I turned to pills. Took a lot of pills, took a lot of painkillers and I got into a bad situation. I let this person know that I was also going through a breakup. I also let this person know that the information was misused to a point to where it was meant to make me look bad opposed to caring about me as a person."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ztb5KXG_ijU&t=845s

**10. JR Smith — "It felt great. It just felt weird in the bubble. It wasn't the same" — JR Smith on the surreal experience of winning in the bubble** [19:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ztb5KXG_ijU&t=1190s)

JR Smith: "It felt great. It just felt weird in the bubble. It wasn't the same. There's no parade, no fans, people wearing masks, you get the echoes coming through the thing. It was kind of weird, but winning a chip in general is just amazing, so I'm I'm I'm very fortunate to have two."
