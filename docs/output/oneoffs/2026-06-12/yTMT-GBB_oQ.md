**3. Jeff Peterson — "To make a trade is very challenging. The fans think you pick up the phone, but it's nuanced." — Jeff Peterson on the nuance and difficulty of making NBA trades** [06:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yTMT-GBB_oQ&t=368s)

Jeff Peterson: "To make a trade is very challenging. I think earlier, sometimes the fans think it's just you pick up the phone, hey, we want to get a deal done, but it's far more nuanced than that as you could imagine. Relationships are the foundation of everything we do, but especially if you want to try to get a deal done."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yTMT-GBB_oQ&t=368s

**4. Jeff Peterson — "The league did a tremendous job in terms of when they wanted to implement these rules." — Jeff Peterson on how new CBA rules impact roster building** [07:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yTMT-GBB_oQ&t=460s)

Jeff Peterson: "The league did a tremendous job in terms of when they wanted to implement these rules to try to create a little bit more parity amongst the league. It's just very restrictive. There's different constraints in terms of how much money you can take back. If you're in the second apron, your pick may be frozen, which in theory just gives teams a little bit more hesitation or extra pause in terms of if we go do this deal, the ramifications are going to be probably much more punitive than they would have been previously. I give credit to Boston and Brad and those guys for the job that they did. They had some constraints last year and they were able to spin out of it and they still had a tremendous season."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yTMT-GBB_oQ&t=460s

**5. Jeff Peterson — "The first thing is embracing the market that you're in and embracing the reality of the situation." — Jeff Peterson on the importance of embracing your market's reality** [10:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yTMT-GBB_oQ&t=607s)

Jeff Peterson: "The first thing is embracing the market that you're in and embracing the reality of the situation. If all is equal, there's a larger group of people or players that would want to be in New York, not just the Knicks, but just want to be in New York versus Charlotte. But then there's also some guys who may like the Charlotte lifestyle and it's a little bit slower. I'm biased now, but it's a tremendous place to be and to raise a family and got everything you need here."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yTMT-GBB_oQ&t=607s

**6. Jeff Peterson — "With these new lottery reform rules, it's going to make it a little bit more challenging." — Jeff Peterson on how lottery reform makes drafting more challenging** [11:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yTMT-GBB_oQ&t=698s)

Jeff Peterson: "With these new lottery reform rules, it's going to make it a little bit more challenging to build through the draft. We're looking at three or four years down the road in terms of just the consequences of what was not the reality in the past. But now that these rules have changed, we got to figure out how to adapt and there's no question that we will."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yTMT-GBB_oQ&t=698s

**7. Jeff Peterson — "In a vacuum, it does make it a bit more challenging for these smaller market teams." — Jeff Peterson on the difficulty of acquiring stars in smaller markets** [16:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yTMT-GBB_oQ&t=977s)

Jeff Peterson: "In a vacuum, it does make it a bit more challenging. It's hard to bottom out because of the new rules. So, I think what you'll see is, if you're in a smaller market, you may have to trade for a star player, and you're making a bet and you're going to give up picks and maybe a player or two, and you just want that star player who wants to be in your market and is going to embrace it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yTMT-GBB_oQ&t=977s

**9. Jeff Peterson — "The game is more read and react. You need guys that can think the game in real time." — Jeff Peterson on what 'processing the game well' means for players** [21:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yTMT-GBB_oQ&t=1303s)

Jeff Peterson: "In today's age, the game is more read and react. It's no longer I'm going to come down the court and run punch five and I'm going to throw it into the post and this guy is going to back down. You need guys that can think the game in real time and read and react and process the game. I think the better off you are. We're constantly trying to evaluate, is the player simply making the right read for the team, not just for himself, but for his team."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yTMT-GBB_oQ&t=1303s
