# How the Jaylen Brown trade changed LeBron James and Rich Paul's view of the 76ers | SportsCenter — *NBA on ESPN*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3BTVVZcgMx0

_Speakers identified: Scott Van Pelt, Ohm Youngmisuk_

**1. Ohm Youngmisuk — "it was Philadelphia that made the move for Jaylen Brown that really opened their eyes" — LeBron James's agonizing decision process before choosing the 76ers** [01:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3BTVVZcgMx0&t=114s)

**Scott Van Pelt:** "What can you tell us about the final days leading up to his last decision to join Philly?"

**Ohm Youngmisuk:** "Rich Paul detailed on his podcast with **Max Kellerman** that this was an agonizing decision for **LeBron James**. He did not make a decision until 1:00 a.m. on Friday morning. It was confirmed it was the four teams that **Shams Charania** had reported: Golden State, Cleveland, Miami, and Philadelphia. While LeBron felt the pull to return back to Cleveland or Miami, and he certainly felt the lure of **Steph Curry** and **Draymond Green** in Golden State, it was Philadelphia that made the move for **Jaylen Brown** that really opened their eyes. The Brown move changed how they viewed Philadelphia, and that is what got LeBron thinking about Philadelphia and making this basketball decision to go after a fifth championship ring."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3BTVVZcgMx0&t=114s

**2. Ohm Youngmisuk — "LeBron James is like a solar system on himself, he has his own orbit" — Why LeBron James and Tyrese Maxey are a strong on-court fit** [03:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3BTVVZcgMx0&t=189s)

**Scott Van Pelt:** "What do LeBron and his camp like so much about the on-court fit with Maxey?"

**Ohm Youngmisuk:** "If you look at some of his best success that came in Cleveland with **Kyrie Irving**, you could see the similarities with **Tyrese Maxey**. They have an established relationship. **Tyrese Maxey** is also a clutch client. They have known each other for five years. They have worked out together, and this is where they feel this will be a smoother transition than going to another team. When LeBron goes somewhere else, any other superstar on that team has to adjust and adapt to playing with **LeBron James**. **LeBron James** is like a solar system on himself, he has his own orbit. But with **Tyrese Maxey**, they feel that this will be a smoother transition. They know each other very well and they can play pick-and-roll. Rich Paul even said, perhaps **Nick Nurse** will pull a page out of **Ty Lue**'s book and use Maxey as a screener for **LeBron James**, and **LeBron James** will be a secondary playmaker, the best secondary playmaker in the league."
