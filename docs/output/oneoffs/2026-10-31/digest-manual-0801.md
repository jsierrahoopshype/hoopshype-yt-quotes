## LeBron James Defends the Knicks to Steve Nash: “Every Team Is Chasing Them” | NBA on Prime — *Sports On Prime*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7sQ80Qt5psI

_Speakers identified: LeBron James, Steve Nash_

**1. Steve Nash — "You don't lose the championship DNA overnight. That's there for forever" — LeBron James on the New York Knicks' championship potential** [01:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7sQ80Qt5psI&t=62s)

**Steve Nash:** "You said on our podcast, Mind the Game, you didn't see the Knicks as entitled. They had a slow start, they're starting to come on. You still feel like they're a contender and going to be there?"

**LeBron James:** "Oh, absolutely. You don't lose the championship DNA overnight. That's there for forever and I think they still have that championship pedigree and we know that. I think every team in the NBA, all the 29 teams, is trying to chase what they accomplished last year. So, we look forward to the challenge. We know that we got them on opening night and so they're going to be trying to get some get-back, so we just got to understand that."
