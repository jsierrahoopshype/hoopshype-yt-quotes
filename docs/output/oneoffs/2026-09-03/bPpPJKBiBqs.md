**6. Chris Paul — "In the NBA, you actually do a jump ball." — Chris Paul on the jump ball rule in the NBA** [06:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bPpPJKBiBqs&t=390s)

Chris Paul: "If you think about the jump ball, in grassroots basketball like high school, college, all that, if there's a jump ball, there's a possession arrow. So the ball goes right or the ball goes left. In the NBA, you actually do a jump ball. So if two guys hold on to the ball, you go and they throw the ball up to see who's going to win the tip to get possession. So with the jump ball, there'll be times where, like I always say, I'm six feet, maybe 6'1" on a good day. I may have to do a jump ball against a guy who's 6'7". So when we line up and we're about to jump for the jump ball, guys usually put their hand like this. So if his hand is here, I just try to make sure my hand is just a little bit over his, just a little higher. So now when the ref throws the ball up, if I just get my hand over top of his and we jump, I'm going to win the tip. And when you think about it, most referees, officials, they're not going to call that a foul because it happens so quick and it's so fast."
