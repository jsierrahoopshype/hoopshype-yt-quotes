# Wrapping the Wolves | The Jon Krawczynski Show — *The Jon Krawczynski Show - Timberwolves Podcast*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wMcR44NfhEU

_Speakers identified: Jim Souhan, Jon Krawczynski_

**1. Jon Krawczynski — Timberwolves' season was enjoyable despite falling short of expectations — "I still think it was largely an enjoyable season and especially an enjoyable playoffs."** [01:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wMcR44NfhEU&t=69s)

"I do think it's sort of two fold right now. One is I think that in the immediate aftermath of any season ending before you want it to, certainly when you end with a 30-point loss at home in the Game 6, it's very easy to say everything is bad, everything needs to be changed. This is a failure. And when I think back on this season, I think it fell short of expectations, but I still think it was largely an enjoyable season and especially an enjoyable playoffs to see them beat **Denver** in six games when they're so short-handed, to really take a great **San Antonio** team to six games when they were short-handed. And so I liked the way that it ended over the last couple of weeks before the Game 6 loss to **San Antonio**."

**2. Jon Krawczynski — Timberwolves need changes to close gap on San Antonio, Oklahoma City — "The overwhelming feeling to me right now is they're just not good enough."** [01:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wMcR44NfhEU&t=69s)

"That said, the overwhelming feeling to me right now is they're just not good enough. When you look at what **San Antonio** and **Oklahoma City** have built and the staying power that they are expected to have over the next several years, this **Timberwolves** team probably went as far as it was going to go and now needs some changes to close the gap on what **San Antonio** and **Oklahoma City** have become. And it is a big challenge and it is an obvious challenge because the simple fact is what **Rudy Gobert** said: the better team won, and **San Antonio** was better by a significant margin."

**3. Jon Krawczynski — Exploring Giannis, Kyrie Irving, Ja Morant trade possibilities — "Getting Giannis to pair with Anthony Edwards would be a significant upgrade."** [03:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wMcR44NfhEU&t=193s)

**Jim Souhan:** "Is that a **Giannis** trade? If it's not a **Giannis** trade, is there another star you could possibly get? And if you can't get a star, how do you improve?"

**Jon Krawczynski:** "Yeah, it's a couple of things. It's possibly a **Giannis** trade. I think it would be very hard to pull off because there will be other teams that get involved in this bidding that probably have a few more draft picks, that maybe have a young player that is a little more attractive to the **Bucks**. Getting **Giannis** to pair with **Anthony Edwards** would be a significant upgrade. If you cannot pull off a **Giannis** deal, there are other options out there. Maybe it's **Kyrie Irving**. I don't know that **Ja Morant** is the dice you want to roll..."

**4. Jon Krawczynski — Empowering Jaden McDaniels as a bonafide number two scorer — "Maybe then you make Jaden McDaniels your number two. If he is a little more empowered offensively..."** [03:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wMcR44NfhEU&t=193s)

"But the other avenue that you can explore is if you do move like a **Julius Randle** or **Rudy Gobert** or something like that, sort of reconfiguring the hierarchy of this team. Maybe then you make **Jaden McDaniels** your number two. If he is a little more empowered offensively, maybe you get a little more consistency from him that way. Jaden has improved every summer for the last five years, and if he were to come into this next season knowing that it's going to be **Ant** at the number one spot and Jaden at the number two spot, maybe you can get him to take one more leap. That kind of changes the way that everything looks and significantly bolsters their chances of being a team that can be more difficult to deal with offensively than they have been. You make a big swing for a **Giannis**, for a **Kyrie**, or you make a different kind of lesser impact move where you're not getting as much back in return, but you are clearing a path to see if **Jaden McDaniels** is ready to really be a bonafide number two scorer, two-way player, all of those things."
