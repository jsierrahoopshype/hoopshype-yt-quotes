## What Does Mike Conley Bring to the Celtics? | Celtics Daily — *Celtics on CLNS*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A55kn-nKCok

_Speakers identified: Bob Manning, Jon Krawczynski, Payton Pritchard_

**1. Jon Krawczynski — "Mike Conley is one of the saving graces of the last four years for the Timberwolves." — Mike Conley's leadership and impact on the Timberwolves** [01:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A55kn-nKCok&t=76s)

Jon Krawczynski: "**Mike Conley** is one of the saving graces of the last four years for the Timberwolves. He came to Minnesota at a time when the acclimation of **Rudy Gobert** to this team was rocky. It was not working great at the start of his tenure with Minnesota, and they needed a point guard who could show everyone how to go about incorporating him into the offense, how to play off of him defensively. Mike had a lot of experience playing with Rudy in Utah. They traded for **Mike Conley** midway through Rudy's second year in Minnesota. Not only did he bring Rudy along and get everyone on the same page there, but he is one of the very best leaders, communicators, and one of the smartest basketball players you are ever going to be around."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A55kn-nKCok&t=76s

**2. Jon Krawczynski — "Some of his statistics last year were muted by the way the whole season started." — How Minnesota's early season struggles impacted Mike Conley's statistics** [04:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A55kn-nKCok&t=247s)

Jon Krawczynski: "Some of his statistics last year were muted by the way the whole season started. When the Timberwolves started the year, they had **Rob Dillingham** on the roster, a first-round draft pick. They traded a future first to go up and get him, and they really wanted to give Dillingham a bunch of minutes early to see if he could eventually be our point guard of the future. **Mike Conley** did not play a lot early on in the season, and I think that threw everything off with his rhythm early. Once they moved Dillingham out of the rotation and eventually traded him, and gave **Mike Conley** more consistent minutes, he still played well. He is capable of more than just being an end-of-the-bench veteran guy who is there for vibes and can't help a team. In a limited role, with the way that **Joe Mazzulla** maximizes all of his guys, I would expect that you will see stretches of the season where **Mike Conley** is playing regular minutes and helping the team."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A55kn-nKCok&t=247s

**3. Jon Krawczynski — "He ingratiates so much faith and trust from his teammates because of the way he goes about it." — Mike Conley's unique ability to communicate and build trust** [06:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A55kn-nKCok&t=371s)

Jon Krawczynski: "He ingratiates so much faith and trust from his teammates because of the way he goes about it and because of all that he's seen in this league. **Anthony Edwards**, I swear every game you would walk into the Timberwolves locker room after the game, Ant is sitting right next to **Mike Conley** and they're going over everything that happened and what Mike saw that Ant could have done better or what he liked. He is a guy who just really has a perfect way of delivering messages that help raise a player's performance, whether Mike is on the floor helping him do that or just on the bench or in the locker room steering him in the right direction as well."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A55kn-nKCok&t=371s

**4. Jon Krawczynski — "The Timberwolves traded him in February because they wanted to save some money." — Why the Timberwolves traded Mike Conley and brought him back** [14:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A55kn-nKCok&t=881s)

Jon Krawczynski: "The Timberwolves traded him in February because they wanted to save some money and get out of the second apron and free themselves up financially a little bit more, and they were bringing in **Ike Diogu**, so there wasn't going to be as many minutes for **Mike Conley**, and so they did it. There was a hope in that moment that when they made the trade that there was going to be a way for **Mike Conley** to get traded again and then come back here. In the initial hours after the deal, it did look like that wasn't going to work out for whatever reason. I am telling you when the players and the coaches had to grapple with the idea of **Mike Conley** not being here, everyone was crestfallen. They just loved him so much. And then he gets traded to Charlotte and bought out by the Hornets, and because that happened, because he was traded again after the Wolves traded him, the Wolves could bring him back for a vet minimum salary for the rest of the season."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A55kn-nKCok&t=881s

**5. Jon Krawczynski — "Mike really wanted to at least spend one year playing for a flagship franchise." — Mike Conley's desire to play for a flagship franchise** [16:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A55kn-nKCok&t=998s)

Jon Krawczynski: "Mike really wanted to at least spend one year playing for a flagship franchise. This is nothing against the Grizzlies or the Jazz or the Timberwolves, but none of those teams are in royalty with the NBA. The Boston Celtics are historic. They have a history and a gravitas about them that Conley wanted to experience what it was like playing for that kind of a franchise with that kind of a fan base with that kind of a lore about them."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A55kn-nKCok&t=998s
