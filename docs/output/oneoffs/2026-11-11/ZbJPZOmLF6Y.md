# Josiah Johnson Explains Why Athletes Don't Need ESPN — *Get To The Point Podcast*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZbJPZOmLF6Y

_Speakers identified: Chris Spencer, Terrell Owens, Josiah Johnson_

**1. Josiah Johnson — "athletes being able to control their narrative, right? Being able to control and right or wrong" — Athletes now control their own narratives through independent podcasting platforms** [00:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZbJPZOmLF6Y&t=12s)

**Chris Spencer:** "What is this emergence of athletes' podcasts? We really want to hear from them. Why do you think it's gotten so big?"

**Josiah Johnson:** "When you look at the space, I think athletes being able to control their narrative, right? Being able to control and right or wrong whatever they're saying, I think it's really exploded over the past five, ten years. It's just giving them a lane where you're not bound by a press conference. If I'm a journalist, I'm going to try and twist you into saying whatever I want you to say to craft the narrative around the story that I'm creating. Now, you can just turn the camera on, address everything you want to address, give your opinion, give your take."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZbJPZOmLF6Y&t=12s

**2. Terrell Owens — "I wish I had the platforms then that we have now. Because there was a lot" — Terrell Owens on the media's past misrepresentation of his career** [00:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZbJPZOmLF6Y&t=40s)

**Chris Spencer:** "Why do you think it's gotten so big?"

**Terrell Owens:** "I wish I had the platforms then that we have now. Because there was a lot of things that were being said about me and I was smart enough to know that I couldn't contend or combat with all the media outlets that were saying things about me. They would get in a press conference and ask me the same question five different ways trying to get an answer out of me. Those things start to really just eat at me and it snowball to the point to where people thought I was just a bad teammate or just didn't have no home training and just wasn't professional at all."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZbJPZOmLF6Y&t=40s

**3. Josiah Johnson — "you'd have never been able to get on ESPN. You did it a little bit, but ruffle feathers" — Why athletes prefer independent podcasts over traditional studio shows** [02:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZbJPZOmLF6Y&t=176s)

**Chris Spencer:** "What is your take on athletes now with their own podcasts and traditional analysts' work and voices versus athletes in their own voices using podcasts?"

**Josiah Johnson:** "I came up obviously in a studio show world working at NFL Network, Total Access, SportsCenter. Those are fine, but there's a whole world I think you're going to see with a lot of these athletes that would, I joke with Gil about this, you'd have never been able to get on ESPN. You did it a little bit, but ruffle feathers there and they would have never rocked with you in the way that you wanted. But now you launch your own platform, your own podcast, and we literally can go three to four times as hard as they can on any single topic. We're not bound by commercial breaks or any of these situations. We turn the cameras on and we go."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZbJPZOmLF6Y&t=176s

**4. Josiah Johnson — "brought in Skip Bayless, tried to launch a football show, but launch a football show on the same" — The internal conflict behind the end of the Gil's Arena show** [05:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZbJPZOmLF6Y&t=330s)

**Chris Spencer:** "Tell us exactly, because we've heard they took the name and they didn't take the show."

**Josiah Johnson:** "Everything that was public facing, yes. So, I think brought in **Skip Bayless**, tried to launch a football show, but launch a football show on the same channel that we built, ended up changing the name of the channel from Gil's Arena to The Arena. That show was called The Arena with **Skip Bayless**. So, I think people were kind of seeing that and we were going four days a week previously, then they cut us down to two days a week to start our fourth season, eventually expanded to three days. Writing was kind of on the wall."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZbJPZOmLF6Y&t=330s
