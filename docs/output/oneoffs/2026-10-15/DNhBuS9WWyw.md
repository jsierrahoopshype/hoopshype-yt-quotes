# Kings' Domantas Sabonis 'shocked' to hear trade speculation | NBC Sports California — *NBC Sports Bay Area & California*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DNhBuS9WWyw

_Speakers identified: Deuce Mason, Morgan Ragan, Domantas Sabonis_

**1. Domantas Sabonis — "I don't know where people make these things up and put them out there." — Domantas Sabonis dismisses trade rumors and negative team projections** [05:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DNhBuS9WWyw&t=350s)

**Deuce Mason:** "A lot of publications, and I'm sure you see this too. All players say they don't see this, but we know they do. They're saying the Kings are going to be the worst team this year, that you guys are going to win 20 games, it's going to be ugly. Does that type of stuff feel you at all?"

**Domantas Sabonis:** "I don't know. It's 11 years in, you've had so many predictions, so many people are still wrong. You can go through draft night, trade rumors, I don't know where people make these things up and put them out there. As a team, obviously you see that. If anything, it helps to find some kind of fire in there. I know it's driving Vivek and Doug crazy, which is good. We want to go back to where we were in those beam team years and we're excited."
