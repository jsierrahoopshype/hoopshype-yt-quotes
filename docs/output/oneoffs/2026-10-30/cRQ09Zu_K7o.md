**1. Jaylen Brown — "a lot of times players wrap their identities around their performances and you have to learn" — Jaylen Brown on managing performance anxiety and public criticism** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cRQ09Zu_K7o&t=0s)

Jaylen Brown: "I definitely think I still do. Anxiety is something I have openly talked about for a large part of my career, especially around your performance because you play a lot in the NBA. You have three, four games a week and if you have one bad game, nobody can they forget about the good ones, they remember the bad game and they use that to give you criticism. A lot of times players wrap their identities around their performances and you have to learn at an early age to separate the two or you will literally go crazy because you will be so focused on what people think or what people feel about you, you will lose how you actually feel about yourself."

**3. Jaylen Brown — "my anxiety often leads to forms of greatness where because I realize I had anxiety about something" — Jaylen Brown on how anxiety fuels his drive for greatness** [02:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cRQ09Zu_K7o&t=124s)

Jaylen Brown: "A lot of times anxiety means that you either care or that you are processing information that you know that you need to be better at. I get anxiety when I could have prepared better for something or I could be better at something that maybe I'm not. That turns me in my drive mode into, okay, now I have to obsess about this thing until I'm the best at it. My anxiety often leads to forms of greatness where because I realize I had anxiety about something, now it makes me go practice it a million times over until that anxiety goes away."

**4. Jaylen Brown — "I never walk into a meeting not being prepared" — Barack Obama's advice on the importance of being prepared** [03:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cRQ09Zu_K7o&t=198s)

Jaylen Brown: "I actually learned that from **Barack Obama** when I had a meeting with him in 2020. He knew so many things about me and I asked him, you are the president, how in the hell do you know so much stuff about me? And he was like, I never walk into a meeting not being prepared. **5. Jaylen Brown — "by not giving a fuck" — Jaylen Brown on ignoring media narratives and outside noise** [04:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cRQ09Zu_K7o&t=276s)

Jaylen Brown: "By not giving a fuck. It is the art form of just not giving a fuck. You can practice it, you can work on it, and you can develop it and get better at it. But they move the bar constantly in industries, so you are chasing a bar that doesn't exist. Who is the best player in the world? Who is the MVP this year? All these different type of things, it is subjective. The media, the people that you need to vote for you to win these awards, sometimes they vote for their favorites or they vote for people that the storyline they want to win has nothing to do with it. It is marketing. It moves and if I am chasing what moves, then I am never going to be content. I am never going to be happy."

**6. Daniel Radcliffe — "the biggest difference is that you have an editor who will take out all your shitty acting" — Daniel Radcliffe on the difference between film and stage acting** [07:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cRQ09Zu_K7o&t=469s)

Daniel Radcliffe: "The biggest difference is that you have an editor who will take out all your shitty acting. Every year the Oscar for editing goes to the editor who edited a really brilliant film, which I always have a problem with because actually the best editing job that year wasn't on an amazing film. There is an editor out there that took a truly terrible film and made it mediocre and that is actually an amazing achievement but nobody talks about that. Getting on stage was something that gave me a huge amount of confidence. I was on Harry Potter and I loved it, but I could never be sure that I was meant to be an actor from when I was born. I loved being on set and I loved doing it, but I don't have that inborn confidence that this is the thing I'm meant to do."
