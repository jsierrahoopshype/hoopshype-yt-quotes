# Ian Begley on Knicks draft moves, problems with paying Mitchell Robinson and Landry Shamet — *SNY*

**1. Ian Begley — "it's just more evidence that the Knicks will operate under that second apron" — Knicks' strategy of operating under the second apron** [00:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f6uIyv7F374&t=6s)

**Chelsea Sherrod:** "The Knicks essentially trade their way out of round one, now holding five second-round picks and receiving cash for those trades. What is the significance of them doing that?"

**Ian Begley:** "What you're looking at there is a team that is going to stand under the second apron. At least that's how they're operating right now. That leaves them with not a ton of salary to work with between where they are right now and where that second apron sits. It's just more evidence that the Knicks will operate under that second apron. **James Dolan** was saying on WFAN about a week ago, the Knicks are sticking with that plan, and that's why you saw what you saw on draft night with those multiple trades trading out of the first round."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f6uIyv7F374&t=6s

**2. Ian Begley — "you wouldn't be able to sign both of those players unless you make major trades" — Financial challenges of retaining Mitchell Robinson and Landry Shamet** [00:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f6uIyv7F374&t=46s)

**Chelsea Sherrod:** "When they operate in the second apron, what does that mean for **Mitchell Robinson** and **Landry Shamet**? Obviously, they have multiple role players who are free agents."

**Ian Begley:** "With Robinson or Shamet, you would have to trade salary to create enough room under that second apron to be able to make competitive offers to either of those players. You wouldn't be able to sign both of those players unless you make major trades and shed major money from your team salary. It seems like it would be one or the other, depending on what some subsequent moves are here for New York. It's an interesting strategy here because a team coming off of an NBA championship have an opportunity to bring everybody back if ownership and James Dolan goes into that second apron. Choosing not to do that, now the Knicks are going to be busy trying to figure out whether it's Robinson or Shamet, and then what do you do with the **McBride** potentially extension? So, a lot more moving parts here now with the Knicks sticking under that second apron."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f6uIyv7F374&t=46s
