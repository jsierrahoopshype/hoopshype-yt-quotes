## Draymond Green On His Warriors Future And The Business Of The NBA — *CNBC Television*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gGkPoF1_mus

_Speakers identified: Alex Sherman, Draymond Green_

**1. Draymond Green — "The salary structure has changed a lot of teams. It has changed the NBA." — Draymond Green on how salary structures have negatively impacted NBA parity** [01:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gGkPoF1_mus&t=81s)

Draymond Green: "The salary structure has changed a lot of teams. It has changed the NBA. You have always heard the word parity thrown around. Over the last six years, there have been six different champions. That has not had the benefit of having dynasties. All fans love to see dynasties, even the fans that hate to see dynasties. They really tune in for the dynasties and we are not getting that anymore due to salary structures. It is a reason why **Michael Jordan** dominated for a period, the Spurs dominated for a period, LeBron dominated for a period. We dominated for a period. Now it is all over the place and as a basketball fan, I do not love that."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gGkPoF1_mus&t=81s

**2. Draymond Green — "The only person on that team that has won a championship is LeBron James." — Draymond Green on why the 76ers roster is built for a championship** [04:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gGkPoF1_mus&t=258s)

Draymond Green: "Anytime someone has done something, you are going to have a better opportunity with them. When I look at the 76ers roster, the only person on that team that has won a championship is **LeBron James**. Having a team where multiple people know what it takes to win a championship, I think that is always going to put you in a better position. Now in saying that, when you look at that roster, you cannot deny the talent. **Jaylen Brown** is a champion. They are all trying to put it together. **Joel Embiid** has never won a championship. I know he has that hunger to win a championship. **Tyrese Maxey** has that hunger to win a championship. You need so many things to go right to win a championship. You have to have a great roster for sure, but that roster needs to be healthy. You have to build the chemistry. A lot of things have to fall in your favor."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gGkPoF1_mus&t=258s

**3. Draymond Green — "I would be crazy to not think that that is a possibility." — Draymond Green on the possibility of retiring as a Golden State Warrior** [07:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gGkPoF1_mus&t=458s)

Draymond Green: "I would be crazy to not think that that is a possibility. Now in saying that, I do not approach every day like this is my last year here. To say that that is not a possibility, I would be fooling myself. If 14 and a half years ago, you would have handed me a sheet of paper that said, not only are you going to play 15 years in the NBA, but here is a sheet of paper, you are going to play 15 years with one team, I would have taken that sheet of paper so fast and signed me up as fast as you can. So I am not going to get to year 15 in this place and have any ill will or any ill feelings towards this may be my last year. I understand the business. The organization is going to make the best decision from a business standpoint. If I fit into that equation, great. If I do not, great. But I am extremely grateful for the journey that I have been on here."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gGkPoF1_mus&t=458s

**4. Draymond Green — "Charles Barkley in television is the equivalent of a generational talent in basketball." — Draymond Green on Charles Barkley's status as a generational talent** [12:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gGkPoF1_mus&t=726s)

Draymond Green: "**Charles Barkley** in television is the equivalent of a generational talent in basketball, a generational talent in football. When you have generational talents like a **Steph Curry**, like a **LeBron James**, you never replace those guys. The Chicago Bulls, 30 years later, is still trying to replace **Michael Jordan**. You do not replace generational talents. So to think that you are just going to go replace a generational talent when it comes to broadcasting like **Charles Barkley**, whoever is thinking that is fooling themselves."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gGkPoF1_mus&t=726s

**5. Draymond Green — "Being a better person is 1,000% going to make you a better player." — Draymond Green on how therapy sessions improve his on-court performance** [17:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gGkPoF1_mus&t=1043s)

Draymond Green: "It does because ultimately it helps your temperament. It helps you in decision making. It helps you in patience. It helps you have grace. And so I think ultimately it does because it makes you a better person. And being a better person is 1,000% going to make you a better player because it affects all the other things that you do. It affects your whole being."
