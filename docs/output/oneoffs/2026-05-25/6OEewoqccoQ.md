# LeBron, Luka & The Lakers' 5 Biggest Offseason Questions — *Jovan Buha*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6OEewoqccoQ

_Speakers identified: Jovan Buha_

**1. Jovan Buha — "The Lakers can go in a variety of ways with their team building and roster building strategy here." — Lakers' offseason strategic directions for roster building** [0:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6OEewoqccoQ&t=30s)

"Question number one is what are the Lakers trying to build. And I'm taking a macro view with this question because the Lakers can go in a variety of ways with their team building and roster building strategy here. Do they want to mimic the 2024 Dallas Mavericks build around **Luka Doncic** and the focus is going to be finding a better rim-running center or more 3&D wings, more athleticism, more defense? Do they go that route? Do they view themselves as contenders needing some tweaking with the roster and it's like, hey, if we had Luka available with the way that **LeBron** was playing, with the way that **Austin** was starting to play in that **OKC** series, maybe we can push **OKC** or **San Antonio** and we can be a conference finals threat and we're right there as the third best team in the West, we just need some maybe an upgrade at one of the starting spots, maybe a couple of new bench pieces and we are right there. Do they feel like they need a drastic overhaul and it's Luka and **Austin** and nothing else or Luka, **Austin**, **LeBron**, nothing else, maybe even just Luka and nothing else and entire new cast of characters."

**2. Jovan Buha — "The only guy that Rob Pelinka spoke glowingly about in terms of returning was Austin Reaves." — Lakers' front office's unclear vision for roster changes** [1:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6OEewoqccoQ&t=114s)

"I don't think we got clarity at the exit interviews with **Rob Pelinka** and **JJ Reddick** in terms of some of the player traits, characteristics, some of the things they want, they want more youth, more defense, more athleticism, but we didn't get clarity as to is that big change to the roster, like how do they feel about some of their free agents? The only guy that **Rob Pelinka** spoke glowingly about in terms of returning was **Austin Reaves**, so I think there's a lot of open-ended questions with **LeBron** and what he wants, his future, but you also have **Rui** and **Marcus** and **Luke Kennard** and **Jackson Hayes** and what happens with **Deandre Ayton** and his $8 million player option."

**3. Jovan Buha — "If they just run it back, that is a big vote of confidence for the current group." — Lakers' confidence in current roster and future direction** [2:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6OEewoqccoQ&t=160s)

"But I'm just curious to know how close do the Lakers feel they are because all of the questions I have from this point on to some degree stem from this big picture question. I think they will try to keep **Austin**, they will try to keep **LeBron** and they'll try to keep parts of their supporting cast and then also upgrade it. I think that middle pathway is maybe the most likely, but if they just run it back, that is a big vote of confidence for the current group and saying that they felt like they were closer than it might have looked in that second round series. Or if they overhaul it, obviously that shows to some degree they did not feel like they were that close and maybe there's some pressure either from the new ownership with **Mark Walter** or with **Luka** and his contract extension situation looming."
