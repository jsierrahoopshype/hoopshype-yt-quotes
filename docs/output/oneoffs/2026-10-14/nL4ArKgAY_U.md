# Raptors GM Webster dives into the details on bringing back Kawhi — *TSN*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nL4ArKgAY_U

_Speakers identified: Kate Beirness, Bobby Webster_

**1. Bobby Webster — "We had sort of kept in touch over the years obviously and I always tell people you keep tabs on the top players." — Bobby Webster on the Raptors' process for re-signing Kawhi Leonard** [00:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nL4ArKgAY_U&t=28s)

Bobby Webster: "It was the Sunday before the draft. I may have been out with the kids and I got a call from the Clippers GM and I was like, okay, this is... We had sort of kept in touch over the years obviously and I always tell people you keep tabs on the top players, the best players in the league and their status and you are always just checking in on them. So Kawhi was always someone that we had on our list as far as players we were monitoring. A couple of things probably happened is the Clippers decided and obviously when they had the number five pick that they were going to go in that direction and so very quietly they made the call and said, hey, this is a player you guys have asked about a number of times over the years but we are sort of willing to listen now."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nL4ArKgAY_U&t=28s

**2. Bobby Webster — "You are talking to people around the league, you are talking to people at the league, you are talking to people who know Kawhi." — Bobby Webster on the due diligence required to acquire Kawhi Leonard** [01:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nL4ArKgAY_U&t=87s)

Bobby Webster: "I was confident. I wouldn't have engaged as much with the Clippers if I didn't feel like we could get to the finish line. You do all your due diligence. I've said this a couple of times is you are talking to people around the league, you are talking to people at the league, you are talking to people who know Kawhi, you are talking to the Clippers. And so you have to do that in any deal. It's no different than when you trade for **Brandon Ingram** or you even sign a player. All of that is done. We have an entire staff that goes into looking at the background of any player."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nL4ArKgAY_U&t=87s

**3. Bobby Webster — "He signed the extension and I think listen we all knew last time coming in it was in many ways a one-shot deal." — Bobby Webster on why the second Kawhi Leonard trade window is different** [03:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nL4ArKgAY_U&t=180s)

Bobby Webster: "There is going to be so many comparisons to 2019 and for obvious reasons. I think for a couple of important reasons it is a little different is he signed the extension and I think listen we all knew last time coming in it was in many ways a one-shot deal and I think this time we feel like we have a bit of a bigger window. At the same time people will talk to Kawhi and he is matured and so I think he has a different perspective and I think he is coming in with a 'I want to help and integrate into what we have already established here' mindset."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nL4ArKgAY_U&t=180s

**4. Bobby Webster — "We honestly will take it day by day and then we will take it week by week and then we will figure it out over the course of the season." — Bobby Webster on managing Kawhi Leonard's health and workload** [04:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nL4ArKgAY_U&t=272s)

Bobby Webster: "Not just for this, we honestly will take it day by day and then we will take it week by week and then we will figure it out over the course of the season. And I think that's the only way to proceed intelligently but also not create expectations for him, not create expectations publicly, but keep that really secure and have him trust the staff. I think that's the only way to to sort of proceed."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nL4ArKgAY_U&t=272s

**5. Bobby Webster — "That progression was just as big of a vote of confidence for us to do the Kawhi deal." — Bobby Webster on the Raptors' growth and playoff performance** [05:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nL4ArKgAY_U&t=322s)

Bobby Webster: "That progression was just as big of a vote of confidence for us to do the Kawhi deal. That group going from 30 wins to 46 wins and then even in the playoffs we go down 0-2 against Cleveland, we come back, we fight back, we take it to seven games. If you ask any of the players they look back on that Game 5 or Game 6 or Game 7 and feel like those were golden opportunities. Incredible growth doesn't mean that there is a lot more for them to go but I think Scottie emerging, I think a lot of the young players, Colin in that playoffs, Jamal, Jakob, playing huge minutes in a Game 7 and so that experience for me, Jameson battling off the bench, and so adding Kawhi to that group to us was really exciting."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nL4ArKgAY_U&t=322s

**6. Bobby Webster — "He comes from a really really good basketball family." — Bobby Webster on the basketball pedigree of Allen Graves** [06:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nL4ArKgAY_U&t=391s)

Bobby Webster: "He comes from a really really good basketball family. His brother played basketball at LSU, his sister played basketball at Auburn, the family's life is by and large around basketball. And I think that was a huge one. You go of course the numbers pop but I think going to watch him, maybe he was hidden a bit in Santa Clara and he came off the bench so maybe there is a little bit of upside that was untapped there. But most of them is I think when people meet him, he's got a great positive energy, he's got a great outlook on life, he's stable, he loves basketball and so those things combined with watching him play sort of made him a really appealing package."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nL4ArKgAY_U&t=391s
