**4. Kyrie Irving — "You can't fake being an NBA player. You have to go get shots up." — Kyrie Irving on the hard work required to remain an elite player** [11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MZ3p5l1QhvQ&t=685s)

**Telfair:** "What has been changed the most about your on-court performance?"

**Kyrie Irving:** "I just want to be a complete player, be able to do it on both ends of the floor, be in the best condition that I can be in, and have fun doing what I love to do. This is the 0.1% or 0.01% of the world, and it is a lot of hard work. You cannot fake being an NBA player. You have to go get shots up. You have to sacrifice a lot of your time to be in the gym. You have to do the little things that are not the prettiest. A lot of unseen hours is really what I am referring to. You have to train your body, you have to train your mind, and you are making so many quick decisions out there. We live in a very analytical world right now where people are just basing things off the stats, so you have to just be aware of how the NBA game has grown and I feel like I have grown with it."
