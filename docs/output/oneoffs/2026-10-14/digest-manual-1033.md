**1. Kevin Garnett — "I wouldn't dare try to back Shaq down. Shaq's bigger than me." — Kevin Garnett on his defensive approach against Shaquille O'Neal** [12:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hEW2GMxxxjQ&t=740s)

Kevin Garnett: "Everybody has a profile. If you're going to play Shaq, you probably want to play him the same way you play him. I wouldn't dare try to back Shaq down. Shaq's bigger than me. Shaq's bigger than everybody in here, though. You understand? That's the reality part of this. I would try to face him and I would use more guard skills against Shaq, getting him to move laterally, and I would use that as a pump fake."

**2. Kevin Garnett — "Cliff Robinson was probably one of the best defenders I've ever had." — Kevin Garnett on the defensive prowess of Cliff Robinson** [13:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hEW2GMxxxjQ&t=811s)

Kevin Garnett: "I wouldn't say I couldn't do anything, but it was difficult. A guy by the name of **Cliff Robinson**. **Cliff Robinson** was probably one of the best defenders I've ever had. He was gritty, he could move laterally. You remember when the famous **Michael Jordan** when he goes like this in the game because he hit six threes? **Cliff Robinson** was guarding him. **Cliff Robinson** had a red headband around his head. He kind of looked the same when Michael said, 'What the fuck, I can't stop this motherfucker.' But **Cliff Robinson** was probably one of the best defenders I've ever had."
