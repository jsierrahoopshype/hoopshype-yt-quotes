**3. Jon Krawczynski — "Anthony Edwards was put into this point guard role last year with Mike Conley getting older." — Anthony Edwards is adjusting to a point guard role** [07:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YPp8krggFCc&t=425s)

Jon Krawczynski: "**Anthony Edwards** was put into this point guard role last year with **Mike Conley** getting older. They wanted to get **Dante DiVincenzo** in the starting lineup and give themselves a little more defense, a little more size. And so Ant took that on, but it certainly wasn't natural for him. It was good overall for him to teach him to maybe see the floor a little bit more, get some teammates involved, but his assist numbers dropped last season even though he was the de facto point guard. Now in these preseason games that he's played with **LaMelo Ball**, you can see how much more comfortable he is with just an attack mentality. He is one of the best catch and shoot shooters in the league, but his opportunities for those sequences were much lower than a player of his stature should have, just given the lack of real natural playmaking around him last year."
