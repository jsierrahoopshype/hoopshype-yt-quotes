# Ben Simmons discusses why he decided to join Kings in NBA free agency | NBC Sports California — *NBC Sports Bay Area & California*

**1. Ben Simmons — "I learned a bunch about myself away from the game, just as a person." — Ben Simmons on his personal growth and resilience during his year away** [00:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vf-NnbvL5jU&t=23s)

Ben Simmons: "I learned a bunch about myself away from the game, just as a person. I can say for me, just being resilient, I can really get through anything when I put my mind to it. I think that's just one thing I took into that summer. It was a weird, it wasn't even a summer, it was just a whole year. And I needed it. It was a scary thing to do and make that decision, even though I had people trying to get me to go back that year, but I think I did the right thing for myself."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vf-NnbvL5jU&t=23s

**2. Ben Simmons — "I needed to get away from the game, I needed to get my back right." — Ben Simmons on the necessity of stepping away from the game** [01:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vf-NnbvL5jU&t=68s)

Ben Simmons: "I just think for myself, I needed it. People can say what they want about not wanting to play this and that and the third, but I needed to get away from the game, I needed to get my back right to be able to play this game at a high level. The one thing people kept saying to me was it's going to be hard to get back. And I was fine with that, but I also know what I'm able to give the game. So taking that year off was the best thing I've done in my career."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vf-NnbvL5jU&t=68s

**3. Ben Simmons — "I knew for the first team, whoever came to see me, they were going to sign me." — Ben Simmons on why he chose to sign with the Kings** [02:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vf-NnbvL5jU&t=131s)

Ben Simmons: "For me, it's not a knock on anyone or anything. For me, I knew for the first team, whoever came to see me and to see where I was at physically and on the court, they were going to sign me. And that's how I felt from the moment **Scott** came down to see me. I had a bunch of teams that wanted me to come to camps and play for a spot and things like that, which is fine. I think they would have had different decisions if they would have seen me. But I think I'm right where I need to be, the right time."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vf-NnbvL5jU&t=131s

**4. Ben Simmons — "I'm excited to get going and to be able to do it healthy." — Ben Simmons on his excitement to return to healthy play** [03:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vf-NnbvL5jU&t=186s)

Ben Simmons: "I'm excited. I'm excited to get going and to be able to do it healthy, with nothing in the back of my mind where I'm hesitant with certain things or anything like that. I'm just ready to go. I've taken a few hits already in practice and it felt great. That's a good sign, exactly, and I feel great. I'm ready to go."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vf-NnbvL5jU&t=186s

**5. Ben Simmons — "I didn't watch a game until the playoffs. Not one game I didn't pick up a ball." — Ben Simmons on his intentional break from watching basketball** [03:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vf-NnbvL5jU&t=212s)

Ben Simmons: "I took that year away from the game and I didn't watch a game until the playoffs. Not one game I didn't pick up a ball until the start of summer. And that was on purpose. I needed to get my body right and that was the main thing. I was never worried about the basketball aspect, it was more so, am I able to compete? Can I play at a high level still?"
