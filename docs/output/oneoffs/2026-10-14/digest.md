## 10 Questions with Chet Holmgren #thunderup — *Down to Dunk - OKC Thunder Podcast*

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9E7hNZaIi_8&t=53s

**2. Chet Holmgren — "you don't want to completely try to reinvent yourself, but you do want to try and build" — Chet Holmgren on building on the team's existing foundation** [01:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9E7hNZaIi_8&t=90s)

**Andrew Schlecht:** "Was there anything specific you feel like you worked on to improve just from that specific scenario?"

**Chet Holmgren:** "When you go into the summer, hopefully you have a foundation built and you don't want to tear down the foundation and start from scratch because then you're actually going backwards. You don't want to completely try to reinvent yourself, but you do want to try and build on top of that foundation and figure out what do I need to add on top of this to get to the next floor. You definitely put a different amount of weight into every scenario, but I try to take that and then continue on the foundation that I've already built. Continue to work on my body, continue to work on my shot, my handle, understanding different situations better, and it's no different."
