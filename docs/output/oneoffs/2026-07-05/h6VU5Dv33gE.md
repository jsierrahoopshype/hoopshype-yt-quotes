**3. Joe Vardon — "We can't say that family is a decision here anymore because he's probably going to be living apart." — Family may no longer be the deciding factor for LeBron James** [04:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h6VU5Dv33gE&t=243s)

Joe Vardon: "When LeBron changed his mind and decided not to play for the Lakers, I think all of that is now sort of on the table. **4. Joe Vardon — "He's not that player anymore. He's 41, he's going to be 42. He doesn't move as well." — LeBron James is no longer the same player he once was** [08:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h6VU5Dv33gE&t=485s)

Joe Vardon: "I want everybody to keep in mind that while the excitement and the anticipation that is building around this is equal in some cases to his past free agencies, he's not that player anymore. He's 41, he's going to be 42. He doesn't move as well as he used to. He doesn't play nearly as often as he used to when he was last here. He shot 31 percent from three-point range last year, his lowest percentage in years. Now, he did play a little bit more defense, especially once they took the ball out of his hands."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h6VU5Dv33gE&t=485s

**5. Joe Vardon — "James is a minus defender, Donovan is okay, Evan is elite and Jarrett is elite." — LeBron James would benefit from Cleveland's current roster** [09:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h6VU5Dv33gE&t=549s)

Joe Vardon: "**James** is a minus defender, Donovan is okay, Evan is elite and Jarrett is elite. And so LeBron is going to have a nice mix of offensive talent to certainly relieve him of the scoring burden and then also some help defensively that he didn't really have behind him in Los Angeles. Now, to completely answer your question, how does he help the Cavs? Well, he has a certain veteran know-how and way of doing things when it matters that I do think is lacking. The killer instinct still is there for LeBron and knowing how to close out series is still there for LeBron."

**7. Joe Vardon — "They've been trying to move Max Strus. Now, why would they do that? Because if they can move Max." — Cavs are trying to move Max Strus to clear cap space** [13:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h6VU5Dv33gE&t=815s)
