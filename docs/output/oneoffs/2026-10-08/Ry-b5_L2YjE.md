**1. Pierce Marchant — "we think about exciting players, ascending players, stars obviously and like LaMelo is all those things" — Amazon's strategy for NBA on Prime player marketing** [01:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ry-b5_L2YjE&t=69s)

Pierce Marchant: "We are here in Culver City. We are doing our shoot for the NBA on Prime this coming season. We are shooting a bunch of players here on site, capturing them for our upcoming commercials for the NBA on Prime this season. Having LaMelo is huge for us. When we think about the NBA on Prime, we think about exciting players, ascending players, stars obviously and like LaMelo is all those things and we can't wait to have him in the spot, we can't wait to have him for the season and so he was an obvious pick for us."
