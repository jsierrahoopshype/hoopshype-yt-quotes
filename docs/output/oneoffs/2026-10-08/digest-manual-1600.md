What have you learned in this journey?"

**Craig Porter Jr.:** "I feel like, because I mean, again, when I came out of college and I was doing a lot of my pre-draft workouts, like coaches honestly, they told me straight up like, everybody in the NBA is getting looked at because they can't score or their offense. So the only way you're going to get on the court is defense. So I understood that a lot of the things that would get me on the court are the little things, hustling, playing defense and rebounding too. That's one of the things I'm good at at my size and just understanding that just plays a big role into it and it'll allow you to, honestly, pick your spots and you'll be able to do those little things while also getting a bigger role.
