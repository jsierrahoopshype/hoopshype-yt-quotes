**1. Max Kellerman — "How do you expect them to be competitive? The point of the salary cap is to stop ourselves." — The Clippers' loss of five first-round picks for salary cap circumvention** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxuXfsARTrU&t=0s)

Max Kellerman: "You're taking five first-round picks away from them. Five first-round picks. How do you expect them to be competitive? The point of the salary cap is to stop ourselves from paying the players what they're worth. Meaning, if we took the handcuffs off and we could all bid on the players, we know ourselves, and some of us would bid the price higher. And so in order to stop some of us who are competitive and maybe deeper pocketed from bidding the price higher, we curb our ability to even do that with a rule that says we can't do that. That's all a salary cap is."
