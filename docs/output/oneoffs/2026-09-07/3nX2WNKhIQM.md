**1. Dirk Nowitzki — "I find it pretty hard to analyze and really bring over what I see in terms that fans can understand." — Dirk Nowitzki on the challenges of his second year as a studio analyst** [01:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3nX2WNKhIQM&t=103s)

Dirk Nowitzki: "I'm a little bit more relaxed in year two than I was at the beginning of last season. I was very nervous, didn't really know how this is going to pan out, and I think I got more comfortable as the year got along. It's a piece of work. I want to do a good job. I find it pretty hard to analyze and really bring over what I see in terms that fans can understand. It was a challenge, but I enjoy a good challenge and hopefully I can get better in year two and learn even more. The crew has been great from the studio guys to the bosses who always support us and have us try new things, so it's been wonderful for year one."

**2. Dirk Nowitzki — "He meant so much to me in my career. He's helped me with his system." — Dirk Nowitzki reflects on the passing of Don Nelson** [03:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3nX2WNKhIQM&t=234s)

Dirk Nowitzki: "That hit me hard that he went downhill so fast now. Every time something like this happens, you go down memory lane a little bit and look at old photos and watch old films with him. He meant so much to me in my career. He's helped me with his system that when I came, it was perfect for me and he always gave me the confidence to play my game. He just saw the game a little different than most coaches and he really was a vital key piece for me to find my confidence, find my game, let me shoot threes, let me bring the ball up. It was perfect."

**4. Dirk Nowitzki — "I think he's going to raise his shooting percentage from three and from the outside more." — Dirk Nowitzki's expectations for Dereck Lively II's development** [08:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3nX2WNKhIQM&t=534s)

Dirk Nowitzki: "I think he's going to raise his shooting percentage from three and from the outside more as he gets a little bit more comfortable. He's going to keep stepping into those shots. During the season last year, he was in the 20s from three. Everybody expects that to climb every year and get more comfortable from the outside. He's shown pretty much everything else. He's unbelievable in transition, he's got good mid-range, he can post, he can run pick-and-rolls now. His decision-making got better and quicker. He's going to be an unbelievable all-around talent that can really do it all on both ends of the floor."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3nX2WNKhIQM&t=534s

**5. Dirk Nowitzki — "Nico Harrison wants me to be a part of the Mavericks." — Dirk Nowitzki on his current relationship with the Mavericks organization** [11:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3nX2WNKhIQM&t=666s)

There's nothing really imminent here in the near future, but I'll always, as we know, a Maverick for life."

**6. Dirk Nowitzki — "I got to say I was skeptic when he went to New York as a franchise player." — Dirk Nowitzki on Jalen Brunson's evolution with the Knicks** [12:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3nX2WNKhIQM&t=764s)

Dirk Nowitzki: "I love the forum, loved watching his evolution. I got to say I was skeptic when he went to New York as a franchise player there in that market. He's a quiet guy, shy guy to go to that market, in that media market. I was a little worried, but man, has he been unbelievable on and off the floor and really carrying this franchise and so well-deserved to win the championship, bring the championship back there after 50-something years. Finals MVP, it's been an incredible run. I didn't see that development coming from him. I knew he was super solid already in his rookie year, he's smart, he knows how to get to his spots, but I didn't think he's going to have a franchise player type ceiling in him."
