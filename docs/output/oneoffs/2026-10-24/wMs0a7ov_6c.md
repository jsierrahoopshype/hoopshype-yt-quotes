**1. Fred VanVleet — "between Alperen and I, those guys got to be able to take advantage if they're getting single coverage." — Fred VanVleet on Rockets' late-game execution against the Spurs** [00:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wMs0a7ov_6c&t=22s)

**Taylor Rooks:** "What did you all emphasize down the stretch?"

**Fred VanVleet:** "It was pretty simple there at the end of the fourth quarter. We wanted to execute. They kept making a run, and we just kept giving Alperen the ball. They weren't sending much help, so they were locked in on Kevin, they weren't really helping much off me, and between Alperen and I, those guys got to be able to take advantage if they're getting single coverage. So, just keep feeding the big fella, and he took us home."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wMs0a7ov_6c&t=22s

**2. Fred VanVleet — "there's not many people on the planet making that play on Wembanyama" — Fred VanVleet on Alperen Sengun's development and growth** [01:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wMs0a7ov_6c&t=72s)

**Blake Griffin:** "Talk to me about **Alperen Sengun**, not just the block, but his growth and the way he affects the game."

**Fred VanVleet:** "He got thrown into the point guard position last year, so he had an incredible year of development and learning. That block there, there's not many people on the planet making that play on Wembanyama, which is why he is the special player that he is. But there was another play where we were trying to feed Alperen in the post and we hit him on the high-low, he threw it over the top and got the and-one. Those are just plays that he wasn't making when he first got to the league, and he continues to grow. One of the hardest workers that I've seen, one of the best kids, so I'm incredibly happy for him."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wMs0a7ov_6c&t=72s

**3. Fred VanVleet — "I definitely learned that I don't want to be a coach last year." — Fred VanVleet on the difficulty of sitting out with injury** [02:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wMs0a7ov_6c&t=127s)

**Steve Nash:** "How tough was it for you last year to sit there all year knowing the team needed your leadership and your assists?"

**Fred VanVleet:** "It's hard. Your voice only goes so far. I felt like a coach. I definitely learned that I don't want to be a coach last year. I was having migraines, I was going home more angry and hurt without playing than I was from playing. So, I learned I didn't want to be a coach last year, but those guys got incredible experience. Even though it didn't look pretty at times, I think that those hard times last year are going to bode well for us as we continue out through this season."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wMs0a7ov_6c&t=127s

**4. Fred VanVleet — "he's just an angry bull out there, man, but that's why I'm out there to be the bridge." — Fred VanVleet on Alperen Sengun's demeanor and competitiveness** [03:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wMs0a7ov_6c&t=230s)

**Stephen Jackson:** "Is Alperen ever happy? Does he smile?"

**Fred VanVleet:** "He's actually yes, but not during the game. All the rest of the 21 hours of the day he's pretty unserious, he's actually very funny, joking all the time. But during that game, no, it's hard to get him to smile. I always try to make jokes with him and loosen him up a little bit during the game because no matter if he's mad at us, he's mad at the refs, he's mad at the other team, he's just an angry bull out there, man, but that's why I'm out there to be the bridge between him and our team."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wMs0a7ov_6c&t=230s

**5. Fred VanVleet — "I just try to raise the level everywhere I go, whatever room I'm in." — Fred VanVleet on his career as an undrafted player** [04:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wMs0a7ov_6c&t=267s)

**Taylor Rooks:** "What do you define your career by now?"

**Fred VanVleet:** "Just the impact that I can have on my teammates and in this organization. I did it when I was in Toronto, I'm doing it here in Houston, and I just try to raise the level everywhere I go, whatever room I'm in, whatever team I'm on, whatever building I'm in, franchise. I just try to raise the level, continue to get better, make people around me better, and that'll be my calling card for the rest of my time is how I can help the people around me."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wMs0a7ov_6c&t=267s
