# Caleb Wilson says being at the Finals is INSPIRING HIM to get his team there one day 🤩 | NBA Today — *SportsCenter NEXT*

**1. Caleb Wilson — "I want to be in the finals one day, so having an opportunity to be here is a blessing." — Caleb Wilson on the motivation of being at the NBA Finals** [01:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gArLIm1iSrE&t=87s)

**Malika Andrews:** "How do you digest all of this, knowing the draft is around the corner?"

**2. Kendrick Perkins — "Make a campaign for yourself on why you should be the number one pick." — Kendrick Perkins challenges Caleb Wilson on his draft stock** [02:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gArLIm1iSrE&t=131s)

**Kendrick Perkins:** "I have been seeing over the last few weeks our experts got you going number four. I have been seeing **AJ Dybantsa** going on this media run and every time I listen to him, he is talking about why he should be the number one pick. Make a campaign for yourself on why you should be the number one pick. Do you believe you should be the number one pick?"

**Caleb Wilson:** "I definitely do. I played against everybody else and I won and I played better. It is just that simple for me."

**3. Harrison Barnes — "If you were to get to an organization like that and it was to be in a bigger market, how do you feel like you can step in and help?" — Harrison Barnes asks Caleb Wilson about potentially joining the Chicago Bulls** [04:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gArLIm1iSrE&t=245s)

**Harrison Barnes:** "Within the projection, you could end up going to Chicago. As a Chicago guy, I know you have seen a lot of the stuff that has been happening with the Bulls. If you were to get to an organization like that and it was to be in a bigger market, how do you feel like you can step in and help?"

**Caleb Wilson:** "I am a winner. I am going to change that culture and I am going to bring it back to what it was for sure. It is a great pipeline, North Carolina to Chicago."
