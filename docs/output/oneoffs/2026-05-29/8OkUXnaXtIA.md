# ESPN’s Ramona Shelburne Talks Knicks, Spurs-OKC, Giannis & More | Full Interview | Rich Eisen Show — *The Rich Eisen Show*

**1. Ramona Shelburne — "They're in such a great mood, but it also doesn't mean anything because they still have to win." — Knicks' current momentum and the challenge of the NBA Finals** [01:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8OkUXnaXtIA&t=93s)

Ramona Shelburne: "I went to the Eastern Conference Finals in Cleveland and everybody was in the most amazing mood. When a team has won 11 in a row and everything is going great, people are so happy. I went up to **Spike Lee** and I was like, how are you doing, Spike? I wanted to see what rings he was wearing around his chest because he always wears championship rings. He looked at me and he goes, are you going to atone? I said, what do you mean atone? For what you said about **Jalen Brunson**. I said, what did I say about **Jalen Brunson**? He goes that he has to be taller, they can't win with a short point guard. I go, oh, you think I'm **Becky Hammon**. They're in such a great mood, but it also doesn't mean anything because they still have to win. You have to sit around and wait to see who comes out of this seven-game series."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8OkUXnaXtIA&t=93s

**2. Ramona Shelburne — "The one guy who's brought them back is this 6-foot-2 point guard who was a second-round pick." — Jalen Brunson's unexpected role as the savior of the Knicks** [04:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8OkUXnaXtIA&t=262s)

Ramona Shelburne: "Of all the people who wanted to be the Knicks savior, **Phil Jackson** came to New York saying I'm going to do this. **Carmelo Anthony**, **Jeremy Lin**, **Mike D'Antoni** tried to coach. Everybody has come to New York trying to win and the one guy who's brought them back is this 6-foot-2 point guard who was a second-round pick out of Villanova. How did that happen? I think the answer is that he never sought out to be the savior of the Knicks. He just shows up and he works and he plays and he's solid. He's got that Derek Jeter kind of thing where he doesn't get too high, too low. He's super poised in all these big moments."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8OkUXnaXtIA&t=262s

**3. Ramona Shelburne — "This postseason has completely reframed the trade discussions for Giannis because of how good Victor Wembanyama is." — Giannis Antetokounmpo's future and the impact of the Spurs' success** [10:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8OkUXnaXtIA&t=633s)

Ramona Shelburne: "I think as much grief as the Milwaukee Bucks took for not trading **Giannis Antetokounmpo** the last year and a half, this postseason has completely reframed the trade discussions for Giannis because of how good **Victor Wembanyama** and the Spurs are. Every single person watching these playoffs says, maybe we better get Giannis. Maybe we need that guy. I don't know how we're going to compete with the Spurs for the next five to 10 years if we don't have another star like Giannis. There are a lot more teams that have motivation to go trade for a **Giannis Antetokounmpo** to try to compete with the San Antonio Spurs and this looming giant who is going to dominate the league for the next 10 years."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8OkUXnaXtIA&t=633s

**4. Ramona Shelburne — "Essentially what they're trying to do is incentivize teams to actually compete during the season." — NBA's efforts to curb tanking and improve competitive balance** [17:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8OkUXnaXtIA&t=1022s)

Ramona Shelburne: "Essentially what they're trying to do is incentivize teams to actually compete during the season because the last third of this season kind of sucked. You literally could predict who was going to win each night based on who was tanking. There's so many teams that were incentivized to tank and so the odds are flattened for all the lottery teams. And then also there's not a huge incentive to be one of the worst teams. **Adam Silver** said, because everyone was tanking, you're not sure that the worst team actually gets the worst record because they might just be the best tankers. They might just be the best at getting dudes out of the G League or the Philippines to come play on a 10-day and call it an NBA team for a night."
