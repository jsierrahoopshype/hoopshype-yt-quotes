# Joe Dumars 1-on-1 interview w/ Antonio Daniels | New Orleans Pelicans — *New Orleans Pelicans*

_Speakers identified: Antonio Daniels, Joe Dumars_

**1. Joe Dumars — "You can't put signs on the wall, you can't just talk about it. You have to come in and live it every day." — Joe Dumars on the necessity of living a clear organizational vision** [00:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VmvzoNs78Sw&t=20s)

Joe Dumars: "First and foremost, you have to have a clear vision of how you want things done. You have to have a clear vision of what you expect from everyone, and then you have to live it every day. You can't put signs on the wall, you can't just talk about it. You have to come in and live it every day. And that's really what culture is. I didn't want to get caught in status quo. Let's just stay with status quo and we won't take any risk and we'll all be safe. That has never appealed to me. Sometimes when you're trying to reset and reestablish the culture that you really want, you have to make tough decisions. I made some tough decisions towards the end of the season, but I'm completely comfortable with that. I would have been less comfortable just staying with the status quo."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VmvzoNs78Sw&t=20s

**2. Joe Dumars — "We found more alignment with Jamal than anyone else." — The process behind hiring Jamal Mosley as head coach** [01:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VmvzoNs78Sw&t=77s)

**Antonio Daniels:** "With your comfort level, going out and getting a coach like **Jamal Mosley**? First, just tell us, there's a process that has to take place. The interviewing process, bringing in different people. Take us through the process of what it looked like in coming to **Jamal Mosley**, and then the second part of the question, why coach Mosley?"

**Joe Dumars:** "We made up a list of about 10 guys that we felt like we wanted to talk to. Of those 10 guys, there were going to be different levels in which we talked to maybe half the guys. We felt it was imperative to sit in person and talk with those particular guys. The others we did on Zoom and then it may have turned into an in-person after that. So first of all, you establish who's your list of 10 guys. And then secondly, engaging those guys and getting a feel for who they were, how they see the game. It's an exhausting process because you're talking for hours and hours with people, but it's necessary. You have to do it. The other thing I would say is that what you're trying to find more than anything else is alignment. You have to be aligned. We found more alignment with Jamal than anyone else."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VmvzoNs78Sw&t=77s

**3. Joe Dumars — "There's a human element of this sport and of this business that you cannot get around." — The importance of character and the human element in coaching** [05:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VmvzoNs78Sw&t=339s)

Joe Dumars: "It's too tough in this business to have people in those critical positions that don't get the human element of this. There's a human element of this sport and of this business that you cannot get around. Eventually, it will catch up with you if you don't understand the human element, how to treat people, how we do things. Jamal being such a high-character guy, I've had friends of mine who called and said, 'What do you think?' and I said, 'Good coach, good person.' And I said, 'That sounds really simple, good coach, good person,' but it's not. It's not as simple as people think it is because every day you're interacting with people. That head coach basically becomes the face, and if that's not a high-character person, it creates real internal issues when that head coach can't carry that the right way. He is a super high-character guy and knows how to treat people."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VmvzoNs78Sw&t=339s

**4. Joe Dumars — "If you're trying to win and win at a high level, you better be able to defend as well." — Prioritizing defensive identity as a foundation for winning** [07:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VmvzoNs78Sw&t=426s)

Joe Dumars: "We talked a lot about defending. We're going to score points. We have talented guys here. We're going to put points up. But he and I both believe wholeheartedly, if you're trying to win and win at a high level, you better be able to defend as well. So I think what you're going to see is an emphasis on we have to defend first. We're going to score, but we have to defend first. Can we build an identity of yes, we can score, but that team will compete on defense as well? I think that's what you're going to see from his teams."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VmvzoNs78Sw&t=426s

**5. Joe Dumars — "The day that you don't hold your best players accountable, you may lose the rest of the guys." — The necessity of accountability for star players and team success** [08:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VmvzoNs78Sw&t=496s)

Joe Dumars: "It's easy for a player to say, 'I want to be held accountable,' until they're held accountable. And then you'll find out real quickly if they mean that or not. If you just look at the playoffs right now, these teams that are going deep right now, their best players are being held accountable. Their best players are stepping up and doing whatever it takes to win. That's what it looks like. If you're serious about winning, you have to hold not only a young player here and there, but you got to hold your star players accountable as well. The day that you don't hold your best players accountable, you may lose the rest of the guys if you don't."
