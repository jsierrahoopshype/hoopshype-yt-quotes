**1. Rob Pelinka — "I think we can all close our eyes and hear his voice in our heads" — Rob Pelinka on the lasting impact of Lawrence Tanter's voice** [01:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sdS2bDXNMyM&t=113s)

Rob Pelinka: "We had a moment of blessing and silence before the press conference today to honor him and his family. I think we can all close our eyes and hear his voice in our heads, hear him announcing a **Kobe Bryant** three-pointer or a **LeBron James** dunk or a **Luka Dončić** alley-oop. It just gives us goosebumps and I think we will forever be grateful for what he provided in spirit to this incredible franchise."

**2. Rob Pelinka — "We really wanted to do three things: keep Austin Reaves, acquire a franchise center, and retrofit the team" — Pelinka outlines the Lakers' three-part offseason strategy** [03:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sdS2bDXNMyM&t=187s)

Rob Pelinka: "It gave JJ and I a chance to really hone in on what our fundamental thesis was to accomplish this offseason. We really wanted to do three things: keep **Austin Reaves**, we wanted to acquire a franchise center, and then we wanted to retrofit the team with depth and youth of dribble-pass-and-shoot players in free agency to complement **Luka Dončić** as our leader. We were able to accomplish those things."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sdS2bDXNMyM&t=187s

**3. Rob Pelinka — "He is a lob threat, he is a rim protector and really has a chance to be" — Pelinka explains the value of acquiring Walker Kessler** [03:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sdS2bDXNMyM&t=214s)

Rob Pelinka: "**Walker Kessler** is a 25-year-old two-way center that those guys just don't exist in the draft, they don't exist in free agency. So the chance to get him, he is a lob threat, he is a rim protector and really has a chance to be a Lakers franchise piece for a long time."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sdS2bDXNMyM&t=214s

**4. Rob Pelinka — "I feel like we are co-creators with him, because he is a very creative basketball mind" — Pelinka describes his co-creator relationship with Luka Dončić** [04:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sdS2bDXNMyM&t=270s)

Rob Pelinka: "I feel like we are co-creators with him, because he is a very creative basketball mind. Ultimately, I have the final responsibility to make calls on the roster and Luka is very clear like, 'I want you to do your job. I don't want to be the GM, I don't want to be the head coach. **JJ**, you are the coach, Rob, you are the GM and president. Do your jobs.' But we can co-create. Let's share ideas, let's break down pros and cons and look at all aspects of a potential decision point before we make it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sdS2bDXNMyM&t=270s

**5. Rob Pelinka — "Knowing that there is open competition for starting spots, seeing the wings compete" — Pelinka emphasizes open competition for starting spots** [05:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sdS2bDXNMyM&t=337s)

Rob Pelinka: "Knowing that there is open competition for starting spots, seeing the wings compete. JJ and I were talking today about the growth we have seen in **Adu Thiero**. **Cam Carr** is a tremendous draft pick, shooter, athlete. Watching **Ziaire Williams**, who is from California, who was a lottery pick, seeing which wings emerge is going to be really, really interesting."
