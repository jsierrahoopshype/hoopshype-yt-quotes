If there was ever a place to reclaim his love for basketball and his career, we have done this through the years here. I know you are a huge fan of guys having an ebb and flow in their career and turning it around. Could you take us through when you first started having him on your radar?"

**2. Scott Perry — "He said, I just miss playing basketball. I miss competing, I miss being part of a team." — Scott Perry on Ben Simmons' health and motivation to return** [01:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2q5JGSKhbgU&t=85s)

Scott Perry: "Ben is healthy now and he is really looking to make a comeback in the game. It is really interesting, just looking for the right place. When I received that call, I was home in Florida and next thing I know, we get connected with Ben. He reaches out, I reach out to him, so we decide to drive down to Miami and watch him work out. This is important, watching him work out, getting a chance to spend an hour or so with him just talking and hearing from him about his journey, also asking him why, what is his why now? The guy had made a lot of money in the sport, he had done well, and why was he interested in coming back? A couple of things that came out right away from him is that, hey, Scott, this is the first time I have been healthy in two years. And secondly, and probably most importantly, obviously the health being very important, but his mental, he said, I just miss playing basketball. I miss competing, I miss being part of a team and I just want to be able to contribute to a team and get back to playing basketball."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2q5JGSKhbgU&t=85s

Scott Perry: "When he was healthy and when he was **Ben Simmons**, he was one of the better defenders in the league. Hopefully that's something that he'll be able to regain when he comes ready to play here this year."

**4. Scott Perry — "We may be a team that does that by committee, or there may be that one person that emerges." — Scott Perry on the Kings' team-based approach to development** [09:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2q5JGSKhbgU&t=544s)

**5. Scott Perry — "I think Doug learned a ton last year. He's a hard worker, very hard worker." — Scott Perry on Doug Christie's growth as a head coach** [11:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2q5JGSKhbgU&t=705s)

Scott Perry: "Growth in the position. Last year was his first year as a full-time head coach in this league. The interim tag was gone, and I think Doug learned a ton last year. But the one thing that I've consistently said about him, as I observe him and watch him during this growth path, is he's a hard worker, very hard worker. He's open to trying to figure things out. He's not a guy that thinks that he has all the answers, but he's always in search of the right answers."

**6. Scott Perry — "He loves basketball and he wants to become a great player." — Scott Perry on the development of rookie Darius Acker** [13:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2q5JGSKhbgU&t=826s)

Scott Perry: "First thing I would start, he's 19 years old, but the reason why we drafted him, we see a lot of talent in him. Probably the biggest thing with Darius is he loves basketball and he wants to become a great player. Is he going to have bumps and bruises along the road like every other young point guard coming into this league, no matter how good they were or are? Of course. But I think he has the right mindset to persevere and push through those difficult moments."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2q5JGSKhbgU&t=826s

**7. Scott Perry — "They've got to go a lap around the track. What is a lap around the track?" — Scott Perry on the importance of rookies experiencing the league** [16:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2q5JGSKhbgU&t=1006s)

Scott Perry: "I've said this to all the rookies since I've been in the league, they've got to go a lap around the track. What is a lap around the track? That is to see the entire league your rookie season, to see the different players that you've never played against before, to experience the length and quickness that you talk about, to study things that defenders would do against him that maybe he's not used to. But again, that's the beauty of the journey for any young player, and again, Darius's mindset is he wants to be a big-time player in this league."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2q5JGSKhbgU&t=1006s
