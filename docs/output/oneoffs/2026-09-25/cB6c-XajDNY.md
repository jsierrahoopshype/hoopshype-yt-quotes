# Karl-Anthony Towns Still Can’t Believe He’s an NBA Champion, Talks Teammates Attending His Wedding — *The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cB6c-XajDNY

_Speakers identified: Karl-Anthony Towns, Jimmy Fallon, Karl Towns Sr._

**1. Karl-Anthony Towns — "the player in me is super excited to be part of this moment." — Karl-Anthony Towns on the surreal feeling of being an NBA champion** [00:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cB6c-XajDNY&t=15s)

**Jimmy Fallon:** "Are you used to hearing NBA champion connected to your name?"

**Karl-Anthony Towns:** "It is special. The Knicks fan in me cannot believe I am still hearing that the Knicks won a championship, but the player in me is super excited to be part of this moment. I knew I would get here one day."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cB6c-XajDNY&t=15s

**2. Karl-Anthony Towns — "my first card I will never forget actually got me beats by Dr. Dre headphones." — Karl-Anthony Towns on his early career selling sports cards** [02:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cB6c-XajDNY&t=133s)

**Jimmy Fallon:** "Was it collecting cards or dealing or selling them?"

**Karl-Anthony Towns:** "Selling cards, getting a shop ready of Spencer sports cards. I had become proficient at this new thing at the time. It was called eBay. It was a childhood passion I had. It was my first job before I got this gig as an NBA player. My first card I will never forget actually got me beats by **Dr. Dre** headphones. So shout out to **Tim Lincecum**. I actually pulled one of a **Tim Lincecum** card during a Cy Young year."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cB6c-XajDNY&t=133s

**3. Karl-Anthony Towns — "Jaylen Brown did not want to be seen. It is crazy you win a championship with a guy." — Karl-Anthony Towns on Jaylen Brown's singing at his wedding** [05:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cB6c-XajDNY&t=333s)

**Jimmy Fallon:** "Last time you were here the whole team came and **Mike Brown** was here, your coach, and he started singing Who Let the Dogs Out. And I know this is you and Jaylen's reaction."

**Karl-Anthony Towns:** "**Jaylen Brown** did not want to be seen. It is crazy you win a championship with a guy, you do not want to be seen with the guy."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cB6c-XajDNY&t=333s

**4. Karl-Anthony Towns — "this season is about us just going out there and starting again at 0-0." — Karl-Anthony Towns on the team's mindset for the new season** [06:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cB6c-XajDNY&t=382s)

**Jimmy Fallon:** "Tell me about the mindset coming into this season."

**Karl-Anthony Towns:** "Is it a cliche if I say it is 0-0 again? I was kind of the same thing we said every playoff game and when we won and we kept winning, it was great. But this season is about us just going out there and starting again at 0-0. Being that same hungry team, that team that is hungry to prove everyone wrong."
