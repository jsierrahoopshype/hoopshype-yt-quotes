# Damian Lillard Walkoff Interview | Portland Trail Blazers 118, OKC Thunder 108 | Nov. 11, 2026 — *Portland Trail Blazers*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMEmP5t1284

_Speakers identified: Damian Lillard, Brooke Olzendam_

**1. Damian Lillard — "It came down to who was going to get stops and who was going to execute at the end." — Damian Lillard on the Trail Blazers' mindset during their 13-3 run** [00:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMEmP5t1284&t=8s)

**Brooke Olzendam:** "Dame, this was anyone's ballgame until you guys went on that 13-3 run, led by you. What was your mindset during that stretch?"

**Damian Lillard:** "We knew it was going to be a harder game tonight. Anytime you play a team two times in a row it is going to be tough, especially a championship team. It came down to who was going to get stops and who was going to execute at the end. I just wanted to be a little more assertive on both sides of the ball, get into the ball, and attack the paint. I knew they would be playing the three-pointers, and that is what I did. I thought our team just executed, we got timely stops, and was able to get another one."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMEmP5t1284&t=8s

**2. Damian Lillard — "It is just the energy and the fight, you know. We need that from him." — Damian Lillard praises the unique energy and hustle of Cidi Sissoko** [00:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMEmP5t1284&t=42s)

**Brooke Olzendam:** "After the game last night, you said **Cidi Sissoko** was the most unique player on this team. What did you see from him tonight?"

**Damian Lillard:** "Just the energy. A guy like that, sometimes he might have a foul here, like he did at the end. Guys chase him down, he is going to grab a guy before he lets him get the ball. It is just the energy and the fight, you know. We need that from him, and he brought it against tonight."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMEmP5t1284&t=42s

**3. Damian Lillard — "We got a long way to go. I think the fact that we have been able to compete." — Damian Lillard on the Trail Blazers' potential and areas for improvement** [01:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMEmP5t1284&t=61s)

**Brooke Olzendam:** "You guys are the talk of the NBA right now. You just beat OKC back-to-back. What is this team capable of, Dame?"

**Damian Lillard:** "We got a long way to go. I think the fact that we have been able to compete at the level that we have and win some of the games that we have, I think it shows the potential that we have as a group. We got to keep addressing the things that we are struggling at, you know, the rebounding, the turnovers, being able to execute when teams apply pressure to us. The better we get at that, we will be able to sustain it against any team on any night, and that is our goal. So we just got to keep going and understand the foundation that we have laid has been great, but it is not the end."
