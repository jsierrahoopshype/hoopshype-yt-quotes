_Speakers identified: Rich Kleiman, Shaquille O'Neal, Jamie Salter_

**1. Jamie Salter — "Can we buy brands and make sure that they last forever? And that was the important part." — Authentic Brands Group business model of lifestyle and entertainment** [01:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QZQCdLoSAgI&t=93s)

**Jamie Salter:** "When we started Authentic 17 years ago, there was other companies that were in the licensing business and we sort of looked at them and we said, how can we do it better? I always admire **Steve Jobs** and he didn't invent the iPod, but Sony did, but he made it a lot better. And I think what was really, really important was, can we build a model that has duration and can it last a long time? And I think that that was the most important part, which means can we buy brands and make sure that they last forever? And that was the important part."

**Shaquille O'Neal:** "We had two parts of the business. We had lifestyle and we had entertainment."

**Jamie Salter:** "And entertainment was really important because when we looked at Disney, Disney is a company that really has content and then that content drives you to sort of the parks and then from the parks you actually buy a lot of merchandise. So content drives commerce. And I think that's how we really built the business."

**2. Shaquille O'Neal — "I attended the conference with Magic Johnson and he tells me, make sure you focus on business." — Shaq on learning to focus on business from Magic Johnson** [03:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QZQCdLoSAgI&t=195s)

Shaquille O'Neal: "I attended the conference with **Magic Johnson** and he tells me, make sure you focus on business. I had no idea what that was. So I started to focus on business. I can remember going to the Barnes & Noble and buying the Dummies Guide to starting your own business. It didn't take me back to my professor in marketing class. He gets on the board and he writes, he starts talking about branding. He says, there's good brands, there's really good brands, and then there's great brands, and then there's brands that live forever. I always wanted to live forever."

**3. Jamie Salter — "We really looked at the social media and we said, wow, there's a lot of fans with this brand." — Using social media to build business around celebrity partners** [07:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QZQCdLoSAgI&t=423s)

**Jamie Salter:** "**Marilyn Monroe** is actually a good start into the entertainment business because what it taught us was social media. And a guy came into my office, his name was Adam Kronengold, and he says to me, by the way, do you know that **Marilyn Monroe** has 250,000 followers? And of course, back then I said, what's a follower? And he explained to me, you can advertise on Facebook or on Instagram for free, and you can send messages to all your fans. And so I said, well, that's kind of neat. We should build a business sort of around this. Every time we buy a brand, we should focus on social media. And what happened when we had the opportunity to partner with Shaq, we really looked at the social media and we said, wow, there's a lot of fans with this brand. And more importantly, if we actually take those fans and we get Shaq behind not just the Shaq brand, but get Shaq behind other brands that we own and that we're going to buy, oh my god, we can move the needle."

**Shaquille O'Neal:** "It worked."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QZQCdLoSAgI&t=423s

**4. Jamie Salter — "Those are basically 50/50 joint ventures, partnerships, where we are partners together." — Structuring joint venture partnerships with celebrity investors** [09:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QZQCdLoSAgI&t=540s)

**Jamie Salter:** "If we buy something like Reebok, Authentic would buy 100% of Reebok. Obviously, Shaq's the second largest independent investor behind me in Authentic Brands. But something like **David Beckham** or **Shaquille O'Neal** or **Kevin Hart**, those are basically 50/50 joint ventures, partnerships, where we are partners together. And you're not going to tell Shaq what to do. By the way, Shaq's not going to tell us what to do either. So we have to agree on what we're going to do."

**Shaquille O'Neal:** "If Shaq doesn't want to do eyewear with a particular eyewear company, we're just not going to do that."

**Jamie Salter:** "Vice versa, if Shaq wants to go into a crypto deal, we're going to say, Shaq, we don't like crypto deals. We don't think that's good for the business. We don't think that's good for you to put your name behind something like crypto, then we're not going to do that either. So it really is truly a partnership between both sides."
