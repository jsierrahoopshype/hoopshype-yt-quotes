**Jamie Salter:** "When we started Authentic 17 years ago, there was other companies that were in the licensing business and we sort of looked at them and we said, how can we do it better? And I think what was really, really important was, can we build a model that has duration and can it last a long time? And I think that that was the most important part, which means can we buy brands and make sure that they last forever? And that was the important part."

**Shaquille O'Neal:** "We had two parts of the business. We had lifestyle and we had entertainment."

**Jamie Salter:** "And entertainment was really important because when we looked at Disney, Disney is a company that really has content and then that content drives you to sort of the parks and then from the parks you actually buy a lot of merchandise. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QZQCdLoSAgI&t=195s
