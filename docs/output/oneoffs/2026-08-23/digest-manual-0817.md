## Inside AJ Dybantsa’s Trip To Jamaica — *AJ Dybantsa*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7shsRKr5eSE

**1. AJ Dybantsa — "I don't get starstruck. I played in front of plenty of people." — AJ Dybantsa on his approach to basketball and avoiding burnout** [00:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7shsRKr5eSE&t=39s)

You don't have to be on the biggest and best basketball court to be a better basketball player. So stay the course and you'll get noticed."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7shsRKr5eSE&t=459s
