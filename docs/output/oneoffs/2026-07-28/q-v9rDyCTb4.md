**1. Antonio Davis — "I get drafted, excited about that, second round, Bill Duffy's my agent and we're plugging along." — Antonio Davis on his draft and subsequent move to Europe** [02:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q-v9rDyCTb4&t=132s)

Antonio Davis: "I get drafted, excited about that, second round, **Bill Duffy**'s my agent and we're plugging along. All of a sudden I get a call from him and this is why I love Bill to this day. In all the excitement he was like, 'Are you ready?' and I'm like, 'What do you mean? If I'm not, it's too late now, it's time to go.' He said, 'We've had these conversations and I have an offer on the table for you to go overseas. I think it's a great situation, you would be forced to be in a position where you have to learn how to play a different style and learn what being a professional is all about. What do you think about it?' I'm like, 'Okay, let's try it, where?'"

**4. Antonio Davis — "I had a search committee that really put me through the ringer." — Antonio Davis on the rebranding of the retired players association** [07:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q-v9rDyCTb4&t=432s)

Antonio Davis: "I had a search committee that really put me through the ringer. It had me questioning myself, 'Am I ready for this?' But as it so happened, I was chosen, immediately thrown into NBA All-Star in the Bay Area where I'm from. So it was actually a great celebration. I quickly, and I even knew this before I even got in, it was like, 'National Basketball Retired Players Association' always kind of hit me a little bit. I just kept thinking and asking a lot of our members, stakeholders, even sponsors, 'What does that mean?' You finally start realizing that retirement kind of is the end point. People retire and then it's like they're done. We're just transitioning into what we're going to do next."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q-v9rDyCTb4&t=432s

**5. Antonio Davis — "We wanted to be inclusive of everybody." — Antonio Davis on the inclusivity of the Legends of Basketball** [08:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q-v9rDyCTb4&t=495s)

Antonio Davis: "We wanted to be inclusive of everybody. I feel that anybody who's played professional basketball, it's not like other sports. Other sports, when you look at NFL, NHL, soccer, whatever, Major League Baseball, there's 20,000 plus who've ever played. In basketball, it's such a small number. There may be 6,000 NBA, WNBA there may be a thousand, Globetrotter maybe close to a thousand, ABA we know is not that many. So I feel like we're all legends. We contribute to the growth of the game, building this game into what it is today, and that's why I felt Legends of Basketball so branded us the right way because we're here as pioneers, as ambassadors, as lovers of the game, and we want to continue to be that."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q-v9rDyCTb4&t=495s

**6. Antonio Davis — "I wake up and I'm grateful, grateful for this opportunity." — Antonio Davis on his transition from player to executive** [13:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q-v9rDyCTb4&t=793s)

Antonio Davis: "I wake up and I'm grateful, grateful for this opportunity. I feel like God prepared me for this. Once I finished playing, I went immediately to work for the National Basketball Players Association, which I'd been a part of my whole career. They allowed me to get some admin skills, writing reports and all those different things. Then I worked for ESPN for five years and NBA TV for four years, so that allowed me to do what I'm doing now. Then I became an entrepreneur for six years, I did some things there. You learn really quick that entrepreneurship is very difficult, very tough, but again, I think it prepared me. Then I went back to the Players Association and luckily was blessed with this."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q-v9rDyCTb4&t=793s

**7. Antonio Davis — "It's different for women. If you think about a woman's season, they play in the WNBA." — Antonio Davis on the different career paths for WNBA players** [17:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q-v9rDyCTb4&t=1051s)

But yeah, just want to be inclusive of all these different leagues and we're special in that way. Football doesn't have that, baseball not like we have it, so I'm excited to be representing all of our members."

**8. Antonio Davis — "Great point guards sometimes can be awful for big guys." — Antonio Davis on the difficulty of guarding elite point guards** [19:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q-v9rDyCTb4&t=1191s)

Antonio Davis: "Great point guards sometimes can be awful for big guys. I had **Tim Hardaway** for three years. He was so incredible, crossover, crossover, 'Here Antonio, lay that up.' Here Antonio, lay that up.' And then he leaves and I'm like, 'What do I do now with Tim?' So you have to learn how to play the game. I had some phenomenal coaches, the legendary **Don Haskins** was my coach who always told me if I learn how to play defense, I can play this game for as long as I want, and I did."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q-v9rDyCTb4&t=1191s

Antonio Davis: "It's an opportunity for us to do like you said, we have our five pillars over here of camaraderie, health, financial stability, community and family, and during our annual meeting it gives us an opportunity to serve that purpose. I think the next next for us is continue to come together, create value, make sure that I'm doing what our founders set out for us to do, which is basically take care of one another."
