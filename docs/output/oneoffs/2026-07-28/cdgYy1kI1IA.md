**2. Dwyane Wade — "We obviously talked about it. We had a handshake like, okay, we're going to do this." — Dwyane Wade reflects on the initial Miami Heat recruitment handshake** [01:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cdgYy1kI1IA&t=90s)

Dwyane Wade: "We obviously talked about it. We had a handshake like, okay, we're going to do this. But then a couple weeks passed and I didn't hear from him. So I didn't know. I know it was a tough decision by the way, too. I was watching it. I did a watch party and everybody thought I was like, oh, he knew. No, we had an agreement, but also too, things change."
