# NBA insider shares new insight into Jaylen Brown and Jayson Tatum's relationship — *NBC Sports Boston*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NVdcOt7tZ0M

_Speakers identified: Jaylen Brown, Chantal McCabe, Tom Giles, Chris Forsberg, Gary Washburn_

**1. Jaylen Brown — "Obviously there's ups and downs in any type of relationship, for sure, but on my end, it's nothing but respect." — Jaylen Brown on his relationship with Jayson Tatum after trade** [00:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NVdcOt7tZ0M&t=30s)

Jaylen Brown: "We won a championship together. So, anybody that you win a championship with, you would think that's what a brotherhood or a lifelong legacy would entail. Obviously, there's ups and downs in any type of relationship, for sure, but on my end, it's nothing but respect. Besides when he plays the Sixers, I hope he does well. But other than that, everything for me is just based in respect."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NVdcOt7tZ0M&t=30s

**2. Gary Washburn — "It sounds like he let the organization handle things as they wanted to handle them." — Gary Washburn on Jayson Tatum's lack of action during trade** [02:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NVdcOt7tZ0M&t=165s)

Gary Washburn: "I do think things might have been icy toward the final few weeks when Jaylen was on the block. The question that we have to ask is what was Jayson's actions during this time? It sounds like not much. It sounds like he let the organization handle things as they wanted to handle them. And instead of going into **Brad Stevens**' office and slamming his fist on the desk and saying, 'Don't trade my partner in crime,' he said, 'Well, if you trade him, that's best for the team, I guess, and I'm just going to stay out of it.'"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NVdcOt7tZ0M&t=165s

**3. Chris Forsberg — "That doesn't mean that Jayson couldn't have made a public appeal." — Chris Forsberg on Jayson Tatum's lack of public support for Jaylen Brown** [04:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NVdcOt7tZ0M&t=243s)

Chris Forsberg: "Brad tried to get ahead of this when he came out during his own press conference and said Jayson had no input on this. But that doesn't mean that Jayson couldn't have made a public appeal. Jayson could have come out at some point and said, 'Hey, I want this guy on the team.' I wonder if part of why Jaylen is hurt by this process is the fact that from Brad to Jayson to everybody in that organization, it didn't feel like there was this, 'Hey, we need this guy, we want this guy,' and you're left a little stung by what you gave to the franchise."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NVdcOt7tZ0M&t=243s

**4. Gary Washburn — "This season, everything changed. Jayson got hurt, Jaylen got the number one role." — Gary Washburn on the turning point in the Celtics' relationship** [04:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NVdcOt7tZ0M&t=293s)

Gary Washburn: "They have a healthy respect for each other. They know how important each other was to the other's success. I think everything was great for a while. And I think this season, everything changed. Jayson got hurt, Jaylen got the number one role. Jayson came back and suddenly reassumed the guy role, the number one role. And then he misses Game 7, so everything is on Jaylen's shoulders. I don't know how Jaylen felt about that, whether Jayson could have played in that Game 7, because that was probably one of the more important games in this team's recent history."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NVdcOt7tZ0M&t=293s

Jaylen Brown: "Obviously, it got to a threshold this offseason that was a little bit higher than it's been in the past, right? Where I think people attack my character. Someone even went as far as to say I had a disease. So, it's been a crazy transition."
