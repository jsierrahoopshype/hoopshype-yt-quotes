**1. Brandon Knight — "I've heard that it's the best fans in the world, so I'm excited about it." — Brandon Knight on his expectations for the Perth Wildcats** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S6e1iGjSRzc&t=0s)

**Host:** "Brandon, congratulations. Great to have you on board the Perth Wildcats. Tell us what you know about the team and also the town."

So, I've heard nothing but great things and I've had a great experience myself. So, just looking forward to getting in there and connecting with the fans, connecting with the city, and just making the most of it."

**2. Brandon Knight — "A franchise that expects to win, a franchise that expects to compete." — Brandon Knight on the Perth Wildcats' championship expectations** [00:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S6e1iGjSRzc&t=47s)

This will be my first year playing in Australia, so I'm learning as I go, learning on the fly, but I'm doing my research now so I can get up to speed and understand what I'm getting myself into. But I've heard that it's a franchise that expects to win, a franchise that expects to compete. A franchise that has won championships before, so they're no stranger to that. And that's the goal, to continue to win championships and put a product on the floor that the fans can be proud of."

**3. Brandon Knight — "I'm not resting on what I've done before. This is a new league with new players." — Brandon Knight on transitioning from the NBA to the NBL** [01:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S6e1iGjSRzc&t=97s)

**Host:** "You've been to Greece, you played in Puerto Rico since your NBA days. You might be the player that's played the most NBA games that's ever played for the Perth Wildcats. What does that do for you to come to this competition and try and put on a show?"

**Brandon Knight:** "I think that's a great question. I think experience always helps, but at the end of the day, I'm not resting on what I've done before. This is a new league with new players, different experiences. I'm thankful for my time in the NBA, my ten years in the NBA, but this is a completely different league. I think playing in Greece, Puerto Rico, and the NBA will help my adjustment to the NBL. A lot of guys would take that and say I'm bigger or better than because I played in the NBA. I take it as this is a new challenge, it's something completely different. A lot of great players here in the NBL. The level of basketball is really high. I almost like to come in with a clean slate. I know I played in the NBA, but I've got to come in with a mentality of nobody cares about that. It's a new season and it's time to build something new here in Australia."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S6e1iGjSRzc&t=97s

**4. Brandon Knight — "My goal is to come in and try to elevate those around me." — Brandon Knight on his role as a point guard** [03:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S6e1iGjSRzc&t=198s)

**Host:** "The Perth Wildcats fans have been craving for a legit point guard. Are you their man? Can you guide them back to the top?"

**Brandon Knight:** "I'm sure the fans want to win every year. I'm in the same boat. If I'm there, the goal is to win a championship. As long as I'm there, I'm going to compete, I'm going to play hard, and the goal is to win a championship. Danny and really have made that clear to me that they think that this is a championship-level team. So my goal is to come in and try to elevate those around me, help with the guys that are already there, that have been there, **Dylan**, **Doolittle**, and just get the most out of what we have. It's not about me. It takes five to win, or really more than that. It takes an entire team, an entire city. How can I come in and fit into Perth, into the culture, into the coaching staff, into the strategies? Whatever it takes to win, I'm not above anything. I'll do whatever it takes."

**5. Brandon Knight — Brandon Knight on the competitiveness of the NBL** [05:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S6e1iGjSRzc&t=330s)

**Host:** "You said this league's pretty legit. There's names like **Cole Anthony**, **Bryce Cotton**, a young gun in **Kendrick Davis**. Do you know much about those boys?"

**Brandon Knight:** "Of course, I follow Cole and his career, so I'm very familiar with him and his time in the NBA. I know Kendrick just was in the BSN for a couple games, so we got the chance to play against each other a couple times recently. Bryce, same thing, I played and competed against Bryce before my career. So very familiar with the guards and the players that are in the league. Dell, me and Dell were teammates for a little bit in Cleveland. I have a lot of guys that I'm very familiar with that are in the league. That's the best thing as a player, you want to be in leagues where you can go against the best of the best. I think that's when you continue to get better as a player and you can bring the best out of yourself. I absolutely agree that all the guys that you just mentioned and even the ones that haven't been mentioned, there's a lot of really good players in this league and really talented. A lot of guys that have a lot of grit and play hard, so I love challenges, I love being in those environments."

**6. Brandon Knight — "Anytime any team I play on, I believe we have a chance to win." — Brandon Knight believes his new team can compete for a championship** [06:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S6e1iGjSRzc&t=411s)

**Host:** "You mentioned **Dylan Windler**, you mentioned **Christian Doolittle**, we also got **Jo Lual-Acuil**. This is a legit team but last year probably just needed that point guard. So now that they have this point guard, you think this is the team that can challenge the likes of **Joe Ingles** and **Cole Anthony** at Melbourne United and **Bryce Cotton** at the Adelaide 36ers and **Kendrick Davis** at Sydney?"

**Brandon Knight:** "Absolutely. I'm definitely not going to get on here and say no. I'm all about speaking life over our team and making sure that our words have meaning. As long as we do what we're supposed to do, what I've seen from the guys that are currently there, and as long as I come in and do my part and everybody come in and believe and play for one another and trust what coach has in place, I absolutely believe that we'll be in the hunt for the championship and have a chance to do some special things. But once again, it's no disrespect to the guys that have been there, have been playing, and have been competing, the **Bryce Cotton**s, the **Kendrick Davis**es, the **Jo Lual-Acuil**s, the guys that have been there and really putting their stamp on it. I know they put a lot into it, so they're probably maybe saying who is this guy to come in and say they could win a championship. But anytime any team I play on, I believe we have a chance to win. So a lot of great players, a lot of great teams, but if I'm on a team and they're bringing me in and they're believing that we have a championship-caliber team, I'm in agreement and I'm excited about it and I believe we can compete with any team in the NBL."
