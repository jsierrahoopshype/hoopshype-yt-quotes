# NBA Commissioner Adam Silver on the 2026 China Games, NBA Europe plans and prediction markets — *CNBC Television*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0188x3qhEfA

_Speakers identified: Joe Kernen, Contessa Brewer, Adam Silver_

**1. Adam Silver — "It's like any negotiation, it's ongoing. I think it's too extreme to say it's a rough patch." — Adam Silver on the status of NBA Europe expansion negotiations** [01:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0188x3qhEfA&t=114s)

**Contessa Brewer:** "It's been reported that negotiations with the EuroLeague hit a rough patch. Where do things stand right now?"

**Adam Silver:** "It's like any negotiation, it's ongoing. I think it's too extreme to say it's a rough patch. We've been going back and forth with the EuroLeague. They're the existing pan-regional league right now in Europe. On top of that, there's leagues in every country. It's hard to believe there are 1,200 professional clubs in Europe. So EuroLeague is the top-tier competition right now. We're going forward with what we're calling NBA Europe and we're hoping we'll work something out with the EuroLeague. I think we will."

**2. Adam Silver — "They're coming in from a combination of existing basketball clubs, some of the big soccer brands in Europe." — Adam Silver on receiving bids for 12 new international franchise teams** [02:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0188x3qhEfA&t=150s)

**Contessa Brewer:** "It's also been reported that you've received multiple bids for the 12 franchise teams that you've said that you want. Where are those bids coming in? What does it say about valuations?"

**Adam Silver:** "They're coming in from a combination of existing basketball clubs, some of the big soccer brands in Europe, and then in other markets, people are starting clubs from scratch. It's a combination of all those things, very positive in terms of valuation."

**3. Adam Silver — "Whether it's called a prediction market, whether it's sports betting, it's the same issues for our league." — Adam Silver on the NBA's stance against prediction markets** [03:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0188x3qhEfA&t=195s)

**Contessa Brewer:** "Yesterday I broke the news that the NFL had filed an amicus brief with the Supreme Court against prediction markets saying that the states are better positioned to regulate and they'd like a speedy decision from the Supreme Court. You had also taken a cautious stance against prediction markets. Where do you stand on it today?"

**Adam Silver:** "For us, whether it's called a prediction market, whether it's sports betting, it's the same issues for our league and I think for all the leagues. There's issues around integrity first and foremost. Then for us, it's a desire to capture data so that we can regulate those markets. We see operational behavior, just like with any market, something that's whether inside information, whatever else, we can detect it. So we want access to that data. We also want control over the markets. That's particularly relevant in prediction markets where currently they're taking trades on when a coach might get fired, on odd behavior that you don't necessarily want people placing contracts on or betting on. And so I understand the NFL's position. Putting aside what the right litigation strategy is, ultimately we have the same interest. We want to have control over our intellectual property. We want to make sure we're protecting the integrity of the competition."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0188x3qhEfA&t=195s

**4. Adam Silver — "To have a consistent policy nationally, I think would be in our best interest." — Adam Silver on the need for federal sports betting regulation** [04:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0188x3qhEfA&t=270s)

**Contessa Brewer:** "Does the age limit matter to you that right now 18 to 20-year-olds can go on and make trades on Calci and Poly markets?"

**Adam Silver:** "It absolutely does, but I think it's a separate issue whether it should be federal or state. I take the position that there should be federal regulation. Frankly, from at least the NBA standpoint, we have roughly 40 different jurisdictions regulating us right now. To have a consistent policy nationally, I think would be in our best interest. And by the way, just as a reminder, this is a global issue. Macao is where we are right now since 2020. Hong Kong just passed legislation to now allow sports betting, and there are roughly 80 other international jurisdictions that place allow bets on NBA games. So putting aside what happens between the state and federal government in the United States, this is a huge global issue for the NBA and first and foremost, we have to be the position to protect the integrity of our competition."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0188x3qhEfA&t=270s

**5. Adam Silver — "We are interested in having the women come here. We hope to make that happen fairly quickly." — Adam Silver on the WNBA commissioner search and international expansion** [05:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0188x3qhEfA&t=334s)

**Contessa Brewer:** "How is the search going for a new commissioner for the WNBA? Is the WNBA going to come to Macao, are you interested in having the women playing here?"

**Adam Silver:** "It's just started. We hired a search firm. Tremendous interest in the job. We are interested in having the women come here. In fact, you were at an event this afternoon and again Patrick Dumont who oversees the property here said, let's get a deal done. So I think I know there's many WNBA teams that would love to play internationally and this is a beautiful arena, it's a great opportunity here so we hope to make that happen fairly quickly."
