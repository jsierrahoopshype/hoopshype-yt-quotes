# EXCLUSIVE interview with Bulls head coach Tiago Splitter | By The Horns — *Chicago Sports Network*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oOmrJ8sa5JM

_Speakers identified: Cam Smith, K.C. Johnson, Tiago Splitter_

**1. Tiago Splitter — "I had a bigger chance to be that guy if I was in a structure like in Spain." — Tiago Splitter on his early professional basketball career in Spain** [01:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oOmrJ8sa5JM&t=104s)

Tiago Splitter: "At that age I was pretty tall already and a skilled basketball player. Everybody was talking about me. I was getting into the Brazil national team where the Spanish teams scouted me there and they invited me to go to Spain to just check it out. I went to Spain with my parents and we did a visit there. I practiced for a couple of days there and they offered me a 10-year deal as a professional. It is crazy how it sounds right now. I would never let my son do the same thing, but my parents knew that was my dream to become a professional basketball player. I would have a bigger chance to be that guy if I was in a structure like in Spain. It was better than what I had in my hometown. The competition was better, the plan, the structure, so I decided to go to Spain."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oOmrJ8sa5JM&t=104s

**2. Tiago Splitter — "I learned how to play basketball over there in Spain and how to be a professional." — Tiago Splitter on his development as a young player in Europe** [03:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oOmrJ8sa5JM&t=234s)

Tiago Splitter: "As a 15-year-old, because that is when I moved there, I was tall and not very skilled yet. I learned how to play basketball over there in Spain and how to be a professional. When you are at that age, 15, you are still very raw as a person as well. You are just developing and I got to know the Spanish culture and also to grow in a different culture, learn a different language, make new friends and understand a new basketball way of practice, of playing. So it was a lot of growth for me pretty early."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oOmrJ8sa5JM&t=234s

**3. Tiago Splitter — "The NBA is a different league, the athleticism is different, the quality of the players is different." — Tiago Splitter on his transition from EuroLeague to the NBA** [06:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oOmrJ8sa5JM&t=391s)

Tiago Splitter: "I signed a 10-year deal as a professional. I had to finish that contract over there and that is why I stayed three more years after I got drafted at 22. That contract was great at 15 years old, but at 22 it was not that good for me. I had to stay there and I had a good career. I am very grateful for everything I had in Spain. But at 25 I was kind of on the older side of a rookie. I was a little bit more experienced than the regular rookie, but also I was in a team like the San Antonio Spurs which was already competing for a championship. From the first day in the NBA I was already high performance, we got to win now. It is a different league, the athleticism is different, the quality of the players is different. It is a different game as well. I think the NBA because there is such quality here, the game becomes way more one-on-one, way more ISO. I had to adapt a little bit of the speed, the size of the players, the 82 games, the traveling."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oOmrJ8sa5JM&t=391s

**4. Tiago Splitter — "2014 was so tough, devastating for that group that made 2014 way more special." — Tiago Splitter on the 2014 NBA championship with the Spurs** [08:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oOmrJ8sa5JM&t=525s)

Tiago Splitter: "You cannot mention 2013. 2014 was so tough, devastating for that group that made 2014 way more special. We had a goal from the first day to win that championship. We were so focused. For 82 games and then getting into the playoffs, our whole goal was we are getting in there, we are going to get to the finals and whoever is there, we are going to beat them. It was an impressive group of players and sometimes it happens in basketball where everything just clicks. You know when you drive where your teammate is going to be, you do not even have to look. The confidence just goes to the roof and you are making shots. It was a special group."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oOmrJ8sa5JM&t=525s

**5. Tiago Splitter — "I was the first player to do it in NBA and it was a little bit of challenge." — Tiago Splitter on his hip surgery and final season in Philadelphia** [10:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oOmrJ8sa5JM&t=643s)

Tiago Splitter: "I was the first player to do it in NBA and it was a little bit of challenge myself. They told me that I could not play basketball anymore because I was bone-to-bone. They told me well there was an indoor football player that did, there was a hockey player that did. Well if they could play hockey or indoor football, I can play basketball. I will figure it out. So I did the hip resurfacing and worked through and ended up playing I think eight or 10 games for Philly. That was my last season."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oOmrJ8sa5JM&t=643s

**6. Tiago Splitter — "I got a chance to coach and after two, three minutes there I am coaching there I am feeling." — Tiago Splitter on his decision to pursue coaching** [12:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oOmrJ8sa5JM&t=732s)

Tiago Splitter: "I was at home and I was like no, this is not me. I got to compete, I got to be part of something. At the beginning I was not sure what it was. Should I go coaching, front office? Luckily I was in a basketball camp and got a chance to coach and after two, three minutes there I am coaching there I am feeling those butterflies again. I was like this is what I want to do. I got it. This is what I want to do, I want to coach. I started to talk to people and finally I got a chance to go to Brooklyn and kind of when I got there Sean Marks gave me the chance to be in the front office and also coaching. He told me listen, take a year, make your decision and after that one year you figured out what you want to do. You want to be a front office or coach. And then after that year it was 100% clear to me. Listen Sean, I want to coach."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oOmrJ8sa5JM&t=732s

**7. Tiago Splitter — "You got to create those connections. I know I said that a lot today but I really mean it." — Tiago Splitter on his coaching philosophy and building team connections** [14:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oOmrJ8sa5JM&t=847s)

Tiago Splitter: "Everything in life is about experience and if you have been in that position, sometimes it does not matter the level of it. You got to lead a group, you got to coach them, you got to be there in the day-to-day and it does not matter if you are in Europe or in the NBA. So that was my goal. I have the chance to lead a team in the EuroLeague, the second best competition in the world, so why not? Let us do it. You got to create those connections. I know I said that a lot today but I really mean it. If you do not do that, it is really hard to coach a team. You got to invest time, you got to be authentic and the players they got to feel it. But not just your player, your staff. Like they are the ones that are more time than me with the players. There is traveling, there is pre-game, there is practice, walk-throughs, there is a lot of time that the staff is connected with the players so they got to be able to do that as well."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oOmrJ8sa5JM&t=847s

**8. Tiago Splitter — "He is screaming at me during a game, but he is just being honest to me." — Tiago Splitter on the mentorship of Gregg Popovich** [17:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oOmrJ8sa5JM&t=1029s)

**9. Tiago Splitter — "I put that offensive player in the box and I wanted to create something different than the belt." — Tiago Splitter on the defensive box drill in Portland** [18:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oOmrJ8sa5JM&t=1102s)

Tiago Splitter: "One of our best defenders there was **Toumani Camara**, right? And whenever he got like a big stop, he would do the box sign and basically saying I put that offensive player in the box and I wanted to create something different than the belt or the chain and I why not? You are going to put their whole squad in the box and whoever is the best defender. Something of course to create a bond and the guys had fun with it. Still do not have a great idea for the Bulls, I have time, but we will figure out something."

**10. Tiago Splitter — "It is all small steps. Once we have the group of guys that we want, we are going to." — Tiago Splitter on his coaching approach for the Chicago Bulls** [21:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oOmrJ8sa5JM&t=1298s)

Tiago Splitter: "It is all small steps. Now we got to finish our staff. We are going to have the draft coming up, a free agent coming up. Once we have the group of guys that we want, we are going to start the plans and day-by-day, small steps, we are going to get better. It will be very not smart by me saying we are going to win this, we are going to do that, we are going to shoot 50% from three-point. No, that is not that is not it. It is step-by-step, smart in every step, doing everything with excellence, high standards and create this culture that is needed."
