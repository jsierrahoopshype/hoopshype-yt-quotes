**6. Howard Beck — "he came into the league as quote more magic than Michael" — Howard Beck on LeBron James' evolution into a primary playmaker** [25:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sWX6VYbIMcY&t=1507s)

Howard Beck: "He came into the league as quote more magic than Michael. People laugh at that now and they say, what in the world are you talking about? At that size with those skills, of course he was going to be an all-time great scorer. But nobody ever thought he was going to get the all-time scoring record. In fact, 10 years ago, we were saying, was **Kevin Durant** going to be the one to threaten **Kareem**? And LeBron by that time had established himself as a pretty great scorer, but he was wired as a playmaker and a passer. That was his gift coming out of high school, and he was not a complete scorer yet. He did not have the three-point shot, he did not have the post game. That stuff came later. He still scored a lot anyway, just because he is as skilled and as smart and as physical as he is. But the guy can control the game in so many different ways with his playmaking, with his court vision."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sWX6VYbIMcY&t=1507s
