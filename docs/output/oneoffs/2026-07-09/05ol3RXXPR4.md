# Shams EXPOSES the Truth About Jaylen Brown & Jayson Tatum — *Stephen A. Smith Sports*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=05ol3RXXPR4

_Speakers identified: Stephen A. Smith, Shams Charania_

**1. Stephen A. Smith — "Could not let two players take up 70% of the cap any longer." — Stephen A. Smith questions the Celtics' decision to trade Jaylen Brown** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=05ol3RXXPR4&t=0s)

**Stephen A. Smith:** "Let me ask you this, Shams, let's switch to **Jaylen Brown**. You saw what **Brad Stevens** had to say about trading him. Couldn't let two players take up 70% of the cap any longer. The high usage rate of two players spent on just two players, considering the power shifting in the Eastern Conference. Toronto, Philadelphia, the New York Knicks are champions, etc. Detroit as well. Can't dismiss that. But I'm not buying it. I'm not saying those reasons are illegitimate, Shams. I know they are legitimate, but I'm not buying it because my attitude is when you have a guy that's an MVP candidate like **Jaylen Brown**, who gave you 28 a night as a number one option without the services of a **Jayson Tatum**, you know what, chances are you could have gotten a better deal."

**Shams Charania:** "I think there's a ton of layers to this. I think the biggest thing is the Celtics, they've shopped **Jaylen Brown** at multiple points, right? 2022 Kevin Durant, this summer for Giannis. So the two-pates, especially once you have already shopped him once before, then he wins Finals MVP, then he's a finalist for MVP during the regular season in back-to-back years basically, and then you shop him again, I think that there's a disrespect level that's already there."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=05ol3RXXPR4&t=0s

**2. Shams Charania — "They had a decision to offer Jaylen Brown a two-year $145 million max extension." — Celtics faced a critical decision regarding Jaylen Brown's max extension** [02:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=05ol3RXXPR4&t=124s)

Shams Charania: "They knew as well as I knew that what was coming down the line here is late July, they had a decision to offer **Jaylen Brown** a two-year $145 million max extension. And before it got to that point where if that time came and they didn't offer him the extension, this could have gotten even uglier between these two sides. And I think before they were going to be out of that **Jaylen Brown** business, maybe not for the next year or two years, but eventually. But if a player like **Jaylen Brown** is coming up for an extension and you don't offer him the extension after already potentially disrespecting him by even including him in offers, and then shopping him, that's going to be a problem."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=05ol3RXXPR4&t=124s

**3. Stephen A. Smith — "The man got traded within the division, not even the conference." — Stephen A. Smith questions trading Jaylen Brown within the division** [04:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=05ol3RXXPR4&t=240s)

**Stephen A. Smith:** "Why are we not discussing the biggest elephant in the room? The man got traded within the division, not even the conference. They traded him to the Philadelphia 76ers. A star and MVP candidate fresh off of 28 a night. It's almost like they're saying, let somebody in the division take him and see how he works inside that locker room. It almost comes across like that."

**Shams Charania:** "I honestly feel like people in Boston believe this was the best offer they were going to get. They were going to get Paul George, a player that can help them win now, multiple picks, multiple seconds, and that's the route they went with. They talked to 8 to 10 teams. There were moments, Stephen A., they were doubling back to teams over and over. What's your best offer? Can you give us your best offer? We need your best offer. We're going to move him. We need your like and, you know, when you do it once, you do it twice, okay, that's kind of strange. You do it three, four, five times and it gets to the point where teams around the league believe you're kind of desperate to move off this guy, and I do think that hurts the value."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=05ol3RXXPR4&t=240s

**4. Stephen A. Smith — "Could you compare the Jayson Tatum brand to the Jaylen Brown brand?" — Comparing the brands of Jayson Tatum and Jaylen Brown** [05:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=05ol3RXXPR4&t=347s)

**Stephen A. Smith:** "Could you compare the **Jayson Tatum** brand to the **Jaylen Brown** brand? When I try to highlight the dichotomy, the schism that exists between the two, you understand what I'm saying? The endorsement deals, the sneaker deals, I mean everything. We don't even get me started with Team USA, which **Jaylen Brown** should have been on, but wasn't. Not just in favor of **Jayson Tatum**, but two teammates that spent 82 games a year plus the playoffs deferring to him in **Derrick White** and **Jrue Holiday** at the time. Forget all of that. At the end of the day, could you, as an insider, just highlight factually the difference between the **Jaylen Brown** brand that Boston didn't want to let go of and the **Jayson Tatum** brand that Boston didn't want to let go of?"

**Shams Charania:** "Over the last three, four weeks, while this **Jaylen Brown** stuff was going on, teams were calling the Celtics on **Jayson Tatum**. Their answer was hard stop, no. We're not trading **Jayson Tatum**. He's untouchable, he's not on the table. **Jaylen Brown**, different story. Open for business, ready to trade him, give us your best offer. So that's the dichotomy of both of those situations. Just so we know from a reporting perspective, they treated **Jaylen Brown** different than they treated **Jayson Tatum**."
