## Shams EXPOSES the Truth About Jaylen Brown & Jayson Tatum — *Stephen A. Smith Sports*

Toronto, Philadelphia, the New York Knicks are champions, etc. Detroit as well. Can't dismiss that. But I'm not buying it. I'm not saying those reasons are illegitimate, Shams. I know they are legitimate, but I'm not buying it because my attitude is when you have a guy that's an MVP candidate like **Jaylen Brown**, who gave you 28 a night as a number one option without the services of a **Jayson Tatum**, you know what, chances are you could have gotten a better deal."

**Shams Charania:** "I think there's a ton of layers to this.
