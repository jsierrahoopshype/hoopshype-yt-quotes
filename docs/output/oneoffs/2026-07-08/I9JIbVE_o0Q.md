**9. Jim Souhan — "the Wolves were up against those apron issues a lot sooner than a lot of teams were" — Timberwolves' strategy behind the Rudy Gobert trade** [25:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I9JIbVE_o0Q&t=1522s)

Jim Souhan: "Right after the Wolves traded **Rudy Gobert** for **D'Angelo Russell**, Jaden McDaniels and the pick, of course there was a lot of hand-wringing about the deal, about the decision, especially coming off a Western Conference Finals run. And what was clear at that moment is that we were sort of ushering into a new financial landscape in the NBA that was going to be designed to distribute the talent around the league in a much more aggressive way. And because the new collective bargaining agreement was adopted right after the Wolves made the **Rudy Gobert** trade, the Wolves were up against those apron issues a lot sooner than a lot of teams were. And so they made their trade for the Karl-Anthony Towns trade on the front end of that. And of course it looks pretty bad once KAT goes to New York and wins a championship, but what I said at the time and what we are starting to see more and more of is that teams are starting to make real tough decisions about the top part of their roster."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I9JIbVE_o0Q&t=1522s

**10. Jim Souhan — "Jaylen Brown had an unbelievable season last year, was I think top six in the MVP voting." — Jaylen Brown's impact and the Celtics' salary cap constraints** [26:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I9JIbVE_o0Q&t=1605s)

Jim Souhan: "We saw it this time with **Jayson Tatum** and **Jaylen Brown**. They're both max contract guys. **Jaylen Brown** had an unbelievable season last year, was I think top six in the MVP voting, helped a team that was incredibly undermanned without **Jayson Tatum**, still winning 56 games and get to the playoffs and in a year when a lot of people expected them to miss it entirely without **Jayson Tatum**, **Jaylen Brown** played amazing. I don't want to hear any of the analytic arguments, like he wins, he wins at a high level. But with **Jayson Tatum** coming back fully healthy and with the Celtics pushing up into these the second apron territory, they had a decision to make. And they were parting with **Jaylen Brown** in part to reconfigure their salary cap sheet years down the road so they didn't continually come up against these luxury tax penalties, against these restrictions on deal making that are for teams above the first and second aprons. It is just designed to be a hard salary cap."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I9JIbVE_o0Q&t=1605s

**11. Jim Souhan — "It is all designed now to be more like the NFL where you have basically the quarterback." — The NBA's new era of roster turnover and salary restrictions** [26:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I9JIbVE_o0Q&t=1605s)

Jim Souhan: "I know that **Paul George** still makes a lot of money too. I understand that, but his contract is shorter. And so they made this move, even though I don't agree with the trade at all and I think it was a bad trade, like they made a move in large part because they are being forced to make that move. And the Wolves made a move because they were forced to make a move. Now I think they got a lot more from the Knicks than the Celtics got from the Sixers, certainly, but you're going to see more and more of these teams make these decisions. **Kawhi Leonard** going to Toronto, I think Cleveland will have to make some decisions soon with **Donovan Mitchell** and **Evan Mobley**.
