# Inside the Minnesota Timberwolves' pursuit of LaMelo Ball and Jonathan Kuminga — *Star Tribune*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oti1NhnpEfE

_Speakers identified: RandBall, Matt Lloyd_

**1. Matt Lloyd — "This team has really transformed itself in an off-season span where you were a more veteran team." — Timberwolves' roster transformation and focus on younger players** [14:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oti1NhnpEfE&t=888s)

Matt Lloyd: "This team has really transformed itself in an off-season span where you were a more veteran team. The pace was probably different, especially with different personnel. Now you have all these under-26 guys that you are trying to lean into and build around. It is a product of a lot of phone calls over several, several years. It is not something that just comes up. It is constant communication with Jeff Peterson, the president of basketball operations in Charlotte, who is a good contact, a good friend, and those discussions started to heat up around the draft. It started to trickle out that there was some interest in the market of other opportunities that we could have pursued, but this was one that we thought there was a really good synergy with Anthony Edwards."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oti1NhnpEfE&t=888s

**2. Matt Lloyd — "We had identified that that team had an identity of a defensive first team anchored by Rudy Gobert." — Timberwolves' defensive identity and roster adjustments** [16:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oti1NhnpEfE&t=996s)

Matt Lloyd: "We had identified that that team had an identity of a defensive first team anchored by **Rudy Gobert**, arguably one of the seven, eight best defensive players of all time. Is there a way to shift around that and enhance our shot creation, enhance our three-point shooting, our size in the backcourt, get a little bit more switchable, get a little bit more athletic, and have more options to throw at these two teams in the conference that we are going to be competing with for several years? The opportunity to acquire LaMelo Ball was the product of a lot of phone calls over several, several years. It is not something that just comes up."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oti1NhnpEfE&t=996s

**3. Matt Lloyd — "The opportunity came up to sign Jonathan Kuminga, which again was the product of many, many phone calls." — The process behind acquiring Jonathan Kuminga** [18:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oti1NhnpEfE&t=1092s)

Matt Lloyd: "The opportunity came up to sign **Jonathan Kuminga**, which again was the product of many, many phone calls and a lot of time investment. My wife asked me at one point, how many times has he called you? She was referencing **Jonathan Kuminga**'s agent. How many times have you been on the phone with him since the middle of July? So I said, okay, I will look, and we went through my phone and we had 94 phone calls between July 15th to the day we signed Jonathan. Anthony Edwards was really instrumental in recruiting him to come here. We had several conversations with him, his representatives, and from **Chris Finch** to **Tim Connelly** to **Alonzo Gee**, who is on our staff, everything just came together."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oti1NhnpEfE&t=1092s

**4. Matt Lloyd — "The thing that people should be most proud of is watching him evolve as a leader." — Anthony Edwards' growth as a leader and winner** [25:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oti1NhnpEfE&t=1516s)

Matt Lloyd: "The thing that people should be most proud of is watching him evolve as a leader and watching him grow as a leader. He has been nothing but the driving force of this ramp-up to training camp in the gym. He has been constant presence. He has rallied people to take a team trip together. He has really been and he has learned all this of his own volition. This is not something that has been it is his own intrinsic wiring coming out by itself. He is going to be one of the best players of the last 20 years and maybe one of the best players of all time. It will be a product of his work, his willingness to be coached, his willingness to be a great teammate, and his intense competitiveness in every situation."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oti1NhnpEfE&t=1516s
