**1. Chris Washburn — "Chris Washburn was the Shaq before there was a Shaq." — Chris Washburn reflects on his high school basketball dominance** [19:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7EuoJDU0ACo&t=1161s)

Chris Washburn: "**Chris Washburn** was the Shaq before there was a Shaq. I grew up a North Carolina basketball fan, just watching North Carolina because Dean Smith and we would get those games. But when Jim Valvano ran this monster out there, they upset the national title that year with Whittenburg and them guys. Then they said they were bringing the number one high school basketball player in the country, and **Chris Washburn**, the things he was doing as a big man, the ACC hadn't seen that yet. Not athletic, because it was about big men who could post up, play defense, but they struggled to get up and down the court. **Chris Washburn** came in and introduced a whole new brand of basketball."

**2. Chris Washburn — "What school did you want other than NC State, and why did you choose NC State?" — Chris Washburn on his recruitment to NC State** [22:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7EuoJDU0ACo&t=1345s)

**Brentson Buckner:** "What school did you want other than NC State, and why did you choose NC State?"

**Chris Washburn:** "What school did it more when they tried to be the easiest choice? What school did more? I can't tell you because at that time I was the last person to know that I was that good. A lot of families had brothers and sisters and stuff, so a lot of those families were chasing me home. The families didn't like big Chris. I understand after I walked out of the gym, I see the kids standing on the corner, waiting on me to get to the corner so they can beat me up. So I would take off across the street, across the field, and they behind me, so I'm running through the paths and all that kind of stuff. So I could trip them, my speed from them, but not again, I could fight them, but due to my size, my mom always told me don't fight them. So I listened to my mom, so I would run home. Sometimes they would corner me off, and they would hit me, their punches wouldn't hurt. I would be faking at them, like I can hit you in your face, I can hit you in your face, I'll go to their face with a closed fist, but I would never hit them, you know what I'm saying? My mom said don't fight them."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7EuoJDU0ACo&t=1345s

**3. Chris Washburn — "Who was the first person to put a basketball in your hand?" — Chris Washburn on his early basketball influences** [24:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7EuoJDU0ACo&t=1462s)

**No Limit Larry:** "Who was the first person to put a basketball in your hand?"

**Chris Washburn:** "There was a cat named **Jerry Young**. A friend of mine whose name was **Al Young**. Al played for Virginia Tech in basketball, but his brother had a little elementary team, and it's like fourth grade and stuff like that. So he asked my mom, could I play? Because my thing was football and race car driving. I want to be a damn race car driver. That ain't bad, now. See, black kids, we didn't have no damn race car. So basketball was my next outlet. So he got me started. Then again, after I got to like fifth, sixth grade, the white guys started to, the white coaches came in. So all the white coaches know, so for my whole career, most of the guys think white coaches started me off. But no, it was just the neighborhood black guys that told me, Chris, you can do this thing. That's where I started from."
