**2. Jalen Brunson — "It wasn't like he forced me to play basketball, he forced me to work hard." — Jalen Brunson on his dad's impact on his career path** [02:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zRhCCrh8wnU&t=124s)

**3. Rick Brunson — "I'm on the coaching staff. We have to go down there and try to diffuse things." — Rick Brunson on his heated exchange with De'Aaron Fox** [04:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zRhCCrh8wnU&t=243s)

**Tamron Hall:** "There was a heated exchange between Jalen and **De'Aaron Fox** and you stepped in. What was going on?"

**Rick Brunson:** "First and foremost, **De'Aaron Fox**, who we love as a player, they played against each other in high school so I've known him for a long time. He's trying to win a championship, we're trying to win a championship in that moment. All I saw was Jalen in front of their bench with a bunch of black uniforms around him. Us as a coach, I'm on the coaching staff. We have to go down there and try to diffuse things."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zRhCCrh8wnU&t=243s
