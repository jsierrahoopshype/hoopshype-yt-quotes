# Damian Lillard discusses 'unselfish' dynamic of Portland Trail Blazers | NBA Showtime — *NBA on NBC*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oH3JU5gB0a0

_Speakers identified: Ashley Shahahmadi, Damian Lillard, Ahmed Fareed, Carmelo Anthony, Michael Finley_

**1. Damian Lillard — "when you spend a year away from the game you played your whole life it's a little weird" — Damian Lillard on returning to Portland after a year away** [00:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oH3JU5gB0a0&t=4s)

Damian Lillard: "It's been great. When you spend a year away from the game you played your whole life it's a little weird. It's a little different. But for me, it came at the right time in my life where I was mature and old enough to handle it the right way and take advantage of that time. I thought I did well with that. But just being back in Portland and back playing, there's no better feeling."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oH3JU5gB0a0&t=4s

**2. Damian Lillard — "I've been able to see that our team is really selfless and everybody's committed to sacrificing" — Damian Lillard on the unselfish nature of the Trail Blazers** [00:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oH3JU5gB0a0&t=37s)

Damian Lillard: "I think our biggest strengths are how unselfish we are. I know everybody comes into the year saying it, but I've played on my fair share of teams and I feel like I can see when it's hitting agendas and people got their own idea of what things are supposed to look like. From September up until now, I've been able to see that our team is really selfless and everybody's committed to sacrificing or doing whatever we need to do for the team to be successful."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oH3JU5gB0a0&t=37s

**3. Damian Lillard — "just show my true character. I think what I've always done has been the right thing" — Damian Lillard on his leadership approach with young players** [01:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oH3JU5gB0a0&t=84s)

Damian Lillard: "I think just to show my true character. I think what I've always done has been the right thing. It might sound simple, but that's just being on time, being coachable, being humble, knowing how to handle success, knowing how to handle failure, and just how you show up every day. Because of my experience and how long I've made a career off of doing those things as a foundation, any young player could take a lot away from that."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oH3JU5gB0a0&t=84s

**4. Carmelo Anthony — "when you hit a certain age your maturity level kicks in to a level that most people" — Carmelo Anthony on Damian Lillard's maturity and handling adversity** [03:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oH3JU5gB0a0&t=203s)

Carmelo Anthony: "When you hit a certain age your maturity level kicks in to a level that most people may not be able to understand. He said he took his time with the Achilles injury and he had to really be mature about the situation and how am I going to get through this. A younger player probably would have went crazy trying to rush it back and getting to the gym, but he took his time and understood his body and got his mind right."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oH3JU5gB0a0&t=203s

**5. Michael Finley — "he actually comes in and gets to be a mentor to someone like Ja" — Michael Finley on Damian Lillard's role as a mentor** [04:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oH3JU5gB0a0&t=242s)

Michael Finley: "I like this situation Damian is in, back in his career, but he actually comes in and gets to be a mentor to someone like Ja. We spoke about this, all the stuff that Ja has gone through, and when you're trying to get your game and your life back on track, what better mentor to have than **Drew Holiday** and to have a guy like **Damian Lillard** who are great ambassadors for our game."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oH3JU5gB0a0&t=242s

**6. Michael Finley — "as a mentor as an older guy sometimes you need that and that kind of helps" — Michael Finley on the value of veteran mentorship for Ja Morant** [05:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oH3JU5gB0a0&t=302s)

Michael Finley: "As a mentor as an older guy sometimes you need that and that kind of helps put everything in perspective and not really worry about the injury. Because sometimes when we bounce back from an injury, that plays in our mind sometimes. But now I got to be that vet for him. And for Ja, it's on the other side of it, you can go out there and play knowing that you have a guy that's been through everything."
