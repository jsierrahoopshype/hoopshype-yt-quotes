## Muggsy Bogues Pushes Back on the NBA GOAT Debate — *Bovada*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9_09-kEMYfk

_Speakers identified: Vernon Maxwell, Muggsy Bogues_

**1. Vernon Maxwell — "The game has changed. The game definitely has changed." — Vernon Maxwell and Muggsy Bogues discuss the evolution of NBA play** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9_09-kEMYfk&t=0s)

**Vernon Maxwell:** "The game has changed a lot, though. You can't touch these guys no more. The professional, the lack of, it's just, talk about it, man. The players today, just talk about the game today."

**Muggsy Bogues:** "The game has changed. The game definitely has changed. But you know what? That's with every era. We look at it and talk about it. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9_09-kEMYfk&t=0s

**2. Muggsy Bogues — "The game is definitely changing the skill set because of the entertainment aspect of it." — Muggsy Bogues on how modern NBA skill sets have shifted** [00:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9_09-kEMYfk&t=50s)

Muggsy Bogues: "The game is definitely changing the skill set because of the entertainment aspect of it. We got guys back in our era, as we said earlier, they played with their back towards the basket, the seven-footers. Seven-footers, they bring the ball up the court now. So much emphasis on the three-point line, and hopefully they can get back to where they get defense involved a little more, and lead way to where they come back that offense, because the skill set is ridiculous now with the way these guys are shooting the ball and how they are able to get away with certain things because you can't touch anybody."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9_09-kEMYfk&t=50s

**3. Muggsy Bogues — "It ain't for any individual to call themselves the GOAT." — Muggsy Bogues on the futility of the NBA GOAT debate** [01:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9_09-kEMYfk&t=88s)

Muggsy Bogues: "I'm not the one from the old eras hating the game today. I embrace it. I just know that it was different for each era. And the media always wants to say who the GOAT is, who the this and that is. I'm like, man, you know how many guys played this game? It's disrespectful to talk about who the one guy is, the master of the game of basketball. Everybody had different skill sets. Everybody did this game their way, everybody was great. But you go back from **Wilt Chamberlain**, we ain't played against them, and those guys, **Oscar Robertson**, **Jerry West**. It ain't for any individual to call themselves the GOAT. I think it's, but if you do, I mean that's your right if you feel that way because we always want you to be confident in this era, the different eras that is."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9_09-kEMYfk&t=88s
