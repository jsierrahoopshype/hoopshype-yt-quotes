# Russell Westbrook talks life after NBA retirement — *Good Morning America*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8T-ymiNSBsU

_Speakers identified: Michael Strahan, Russell Westbrook_

**1. Russell Westbrook — "I gave the game everything I had every night I competed at the highest level" — Russell Westbrook on his decision to retire from the NBA** [00:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8T-ymiNSBsU&t=26s)

**Michael Strahan:** "Why now? Is it the right time to retire?"

**Russell Westbrook:** "I just felt for me it was a timing thing. I played 18 full seasons and I gave the game everything I had every night. I competed at the highest level every time I stepped on that floor. I played like it was my last. I don't think you can do much more for the game by doing that."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8T-ymiNSBsU&t=26s

**2. Russell Westbrook — "My energy and effort and focus is something I never could have a night off of" — Russell Westbrook on his consistent intensity throughout his NBA career** [00:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8T-ymiNSBsU&t=53s)

**Michael Strahan:** "Where did you summon this energy to be able to show up week after week and game after game for your whole career?"

**Russell Westbrook:** "It is very important to me to be able to show up and be someone that people can count on. I make all the shots, I make all the right passes, and there is going to be mistakes in the game. But for me, my energy and effort and focus is something I never could have a night off of."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8T-ymiNSBsU&t=53s

**3. Russell Westbrook — "I am just a person that wants to compete at a high level" — Russell Westbrook addresses perceptions of his intensity and competitive nature** [01:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8T-ymiNSBsU&t=71s)

**Michael Strahan:** "Your intensity was probably misunderstood by a lot of people. A lot of people took it as, oh he is hostile, even arrogant. Is that a fair assessment to you?"

**Russell Westbrook:** "No. I am just a person that wants to compete at a high level. I think my intensity is something that people are just not used to seeing someone do every single night."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8T-ymiNSBsU&t=71s

**4. Russell Westbrook — "It was very very important to me to get to college" — Russell Westbrook on his drive and path to college basketball** [01:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8T-ymiNSBsU&t=100s)

**Michael Strahan:** "In high school, you are 5'8. You didn't start on your varsity team until you were a junior. And then you weren't highly recruited. What do you credit your drive to continue?"

**Russell Westbrook:** "It was very very important to me to get to college. Growing up, we couldn't afford for me to go to college. I have a younger brother who I wanted to make sure that I can pave the way and show him the things that he needed to be shown. So I worked my tail off to be able to find a way to get to college and get it paid for."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8T-ymiNSBsU&t=100s

**5. Russell Westbrook — "I have built this kind of shield of protection of the people that truly care about me" — Russell Westbrook on his lack of fear regarding life after basketball** [03:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8T-ymiNSBsU&t=181s)

**Michael Strahan:** "You posed the question in your retirement video, 'Were you ever afraid of what happens after the game stops needing you?' I can say you don't seem to be afraid of the next step. Fear doesn't seem to be in your vocabulary."

**Russell Westbrook:** "I think it is just like I have built this kind of shield of protection of the people that truly care about me and that is my family, the fans and people that really embrace and enjoy what I do."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8T-ymiNSBsU&t=181s

**6. Russell Westbrook — "Legacy is based around what you are able to do with the platform and things you have been given" — Russell Westbrook defines his personal legacy beyond basketball achievements** [03:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8T-ymiNSBsU&t=214s)

**Michael Strahan:** "What do you want your legacy to be?"

**Russell Westbrook:** "I think legacy is something that in sports world as you know we throw around, it is based around championships and trophies and all the things. And I just think opposite of that, I think legacy is based around what you are able to do with the platform and things you have been given and how you help to impact and inspire other people."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8T-ymiNSBsU&t=214s
