## SUMMER WITH THE BEAR | EP2: Team France — *Guerschon Yabusele*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GcbCmxYa0Ac

_Speakers identified: Olivier Mazet, Guerschon Yabusele_

**1. Olivier Mazet — "we decided to not take the offer. It simply came too early compared to the start of free agency." — Olivier Mazet on rejecting a major EuroLeague offer for Yabusele** [00:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GcbCmxYa0Ac&t=4s)

Olivier Mazet: "We received an offer from a very big EuroLeague team. A very attractive financial offer, and we decided to not take the offer. It simply came too early compared to the start of free agency. If it's only a matter of 10 or 15 days, sometimes you can make both timelines work. In this case, there was a one-month gap. The offer was made right after the Final Four, so from late May to July 1st, it just wasn't realistic. We were very transparent and told them we couldn't make a decision yet. If you come back later during the summer, we'll consider it, but we simply couldn't commit at the end of May or the beginning of June."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GcbCmxYa0Ac&t=4s

**2. Guerschon Yabusele — "coming to the French national team without a contract is definitely a unique situation." — Guerschon Yabusele on the unique challenge of free agency during training camp** [01:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GcbCmxYa0Ac&t=76s)

Guerschon Yabusele: "Coming to the French national team without a contract is definitely a unique situation. Rudy and I were laughing about it because we were saying so much is going to happen during this training camp all over the place. Some of the guys here getting traded, signing somewhere, or whatever. It definitely makes this moment feel a little different, because everyone's talking about what's happening in free agency. I'm not really in touch with my agent every day. I give them the freedom to do their job, and I know they'll call me when there's news. I'd rather stay relaxed. If something's meant to happen, it will happen."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GcbCmxYa0Ac&t=76s

**3. Guerschon Yabusele — "I've been thinking about the NBA offer I received... Honestly, I've got to admit, it's less than I expected." — Guerschon Yabusele on evaluating his NBA options and returning to Europe** [07:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GcbCmxYa0Ac&t=463s)

Guerschon Yabusele: "I was just calling because I've been thinking about the NBA offer I received. Honestly, I've got to admit, it's less than I expected. Especially considering all the risks I've taken over the years. I really think making a big return to Europe would be pretty exciting. The Panathinaikos offer really speaks to me. So, I think it's time to come back to Europe. I'm ready. I'm all in. It's a club with huge ambitions. They're on a mission too. So I think it really fits who I am, and I believe we can achieve great things together. We'll talk more about it later, but I just wanted to leave you a message to let you know I'm ready to come back."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GcbCmxYa0Ac&t=463s
