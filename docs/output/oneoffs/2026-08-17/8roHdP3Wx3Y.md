That team, we had **Brent Price**, **Mark Price**, **Chris Whitney**, and **Robert Pack** was my guards. My twos was **Ledell Eackles**, **Calbert Cheaney**, and **Mitchell Butler**. **4. Rasheed Wallace — "Got traded to Portland for Rod Strickland because I guess the Bullets didn't like their guards." — Rasheed Wallace on his trade from Washington to Portland** [04:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8roHdP3Wx3Y&t=277s)

Rasheed Wallace: "Got traded to Portland for **Rod Strickland** because I guess the Bullets didn't like their guards. **Mark Price** retired. They ended up trading **Brent Price**. They got rid of all their guards pretty much, except **Chris Whitney**. They had Rod and **Chris Whitney**. Yeah, it was a big culture shock because now you're going to a predominantly white town, and everything that you were used to before, they ain't doing that out here. The music is different, everything is different."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8roHdP3Wx3Y&t=277s

**5. Rasheed Wallace — "Portland is a great city for family." — Rasheed Wallace on Portland as a family-friendly city** [07:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8roHdP3Wx3Y&t=455s)

Rasheed Wallace: "Portland is a great city for family. If you're starting your family or you have a family already, more adolescent kids going into the teenage year, Portland is a great city. They got so much to do for kids, museums, they got a museum called OMSI. Museum for kids, they got a whole lot of stuff downtown. They right there on many different rivers, and you got the beach, you got the mountains, and you got the sand, the dunes. So Portland has all four climates, and it's like, man, you can do it. If your kids are sitting at home being like, I'm bored, well then damn, you ain't checked outside or something like, yo. But Portland was a great city though, made some lifelong friends out there."

**6. Rasheed Wallace — "The restaurant is called Cafe Du Berry. World's best steak and eggs, bro." — Rasheed Wallace on his favorite steak and eggs spot in Portland** [08:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8roHdP3Wx3Y&t=518s)

Rasheed Wallace: "The restaurant is called Cafe Du Berry. World's best steak and eggs, bro. When I was playing in the league, I would put a contract on that. I'm telling you, this was fire. **Aaron McKie** and **Gary Trent** introduced me to this restaurant. So they was like, man, this joint good as steak and eggs, but I'm like, all right, whatever, like, this steak and eggs, it ain't that wonderful. That was stupendous. Okay, but oh bro, but the one downfall to it, it's like a little sauce that they put on top of the steak. That's the one downfall. The sauce is immaculate, but about a half hour, 40 minutes later, you're going to be addressing your subjects on that throne."
