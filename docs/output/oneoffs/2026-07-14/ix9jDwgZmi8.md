**1. Joe Mazzulla — "I think it's important to be able to put yourself in a position where you're uncomfortable." — Joe Mazzulla on how MMA training influences his coaching philosophy** [01:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ix9jDwgZmi8&t=75s)

Joe Mazzulla: "I think it's important to be able to put yourself in a position where you're uncomfortable. You have to be able to manage your emotions, manage your breathing, and manage your physical response to stress. In MMA, you're constantly in a state of high-intensity decision-making while someone is trying to physically dominate you. That translates directly to the sidelines. When the game is tight, when the pressure is on, you have to maintain that same level of clarity and composure. It's about finding that mental edge where you can still think clearly when everything around you is chaotic."

**2. Joe Mazzulla — "The challenge is not to get comfortable. You have to find new ways to push the standard." — Mazzulla on the Celtics' approach to maintaining championship standards** [04:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ix9jDwgZmi8&t=280s)

Joe Mazzulla: "The challenge is not to get comfortable. You have to find new ways to push the standard. We have a group that is very self-aware, led by **Jayson Tatum** and **Jaylen Brown**, but the league doesn't care what you did last year. Every season is a new entity. We spend the offseason looking at where we can be more efficient, where we can be more disciplined, and how we can evolve our system to stay ahead of the curve. It's not about defending a title; it's about attacking the next one with the same hunger we had before we won."
