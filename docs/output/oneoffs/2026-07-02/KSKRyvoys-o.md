# Inside Boston Celtics' 'remarkably bad deal' that sent Jaylen Brown to 76ers | NBA on NBC — *NBA on NBC*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KSKRyvoys-o

**1. "This deal between Boston and Philadelphia, it's not quite that, but it's just a rung below it." — The Celtics' trade of Jaylen Brown to the 76ers is puzzling** [00:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KSKRyvoys-o&t=36s)

"This deal between Boston and Philadelphia, it's not quite that, but it's just a rung below it. So let's unpack the deal that the Celtics and 76ers made on Wednesday. The 76ers are acquiring **Jaylen Brown**. **Jaylen Brown** is going from the Celtics to the 76ers. Philadelphia is sending back **Paul George**, a 2028 first-round pick, a 2031 first-round pick, and a pair of second-round picks in 2028 and 2030."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KSKRyvoys-o&t=36s

**2. "He is one of the worst contracts in the NBA right now." — Paul George's recent performance and contract make him a poor asset** [01:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KSKRyvoys-o&t=100s)

"A couple of years ago, **Paul George** was an All-Star with the Clippers, a celebrated signing by Philadelphia. He would fit right in there into that wing position between **Tyrese Maxey** and **Joel Embiid**. He would be round peg, round hole type of fit for him. Over the last two years, **Paul George** has played in 78 games. During that time, he has averaged around six fewer points per game than he did in his last season with the Clippers. His shooting percentage has plummeted to around 43 percent. His three-point percentage, he was 41.3 percent his last year with the Clippers. He's owed about 110 million dollars over the next two years. Second year is a player option that he unquestionably is going to pick up. He is one of the worst contracts in the NBA right now."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KSKRyvoys-o&t=100s

**3. "This is a very bad deal for Boston. Full stop. There's no ways to look at it." — The trade is a bad deal for the Boston Celtics** [03:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KSKRyvoys-o&t=202s)

"This is a very bad deal for Boston. Full stop. There's no ways to look at it. I sent a bunch of text messages to a bunch of different NBA types, coaches, executives, scouts, and nobody came back with an affirmative response for Boston. Most came back with some version of, huh, this is the best they could do? And the only takeaway you can have is that the Celtics were done with **Jaylen Brown**. Simple as that."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KSKRyvoys-o&t=202s

**4. "We all know about the Giannis Antetokounmpo stuff. When that fell apart, when they didn't get Giannis." — The Celtics' failed pursuit of Giannis Antetokounmpo led to this trade** [04:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KSKRyvoys-o&t=266s)

"We all know about the **Giannis Antetokounmpo** stuff. When that fell apart, when they didn't get Giannis, if they had really wanted **Jaylen Brown** or wanted to keep **Jaylen Brown**, they could have just stopped talking about him altogether and said, you know what, we want Jaylen, that's our guy. We want to keep him around long-term. He's got three years left in his contract. Won a championship two years ago. The Celtics won a championship two years ago. They could have done all those things. Instead, they pivoted and kept shopping **Jaylen Brown**. They were talking to a lot of teams. They were offering **Jaylen Brown** up to all those teams. They were looking for draft capital, they were looking for players, but they were looking for something. They were looking to deal **Jaylen Brown**. The Celtics were done with **Jaylen Brown**."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KSKRyvoys-o&t=266s

**5. "Jaylen Brown is a star. Jaylen Brown is a bucket." — Celtics depth remains despite the loss of Jaylen Brown** [06:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KSKRyvoys-o&t=391s)

"I say maybe only because the **Mitchell Robinson** is a **Joe Mazzulla** type of guy, rebound the basketball, he's going to help that team. **Mike Conley**, good leader in the locker room, good backup at this stage of his career. But **Jaylen Brown** is a star. **Jaylen Brown** is a bucket. **Jaylen Brown** is arguably the best two-way wing in the league. And the Celtics just got less for **Jaylen Brown** than the Clippers got in a trade for a 35-year-old Kawhi Leonard with one year left in his contract. Think about that. Incredible, incredible."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KSKRyvoys-o&t=391s

**6. "You do a deal with Philadelphia. That's a division rival. That's a team you're going to have to face." — Trading Jaylen Brown to a division rival is a major mistake** [08:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KSKRyvoys-o&t=482s)

"You do a deal with Philadelphia. That's a division rival. That's a team you're going to have to face in the playoffs. You're going to have to eat **Jaylen Brown** like four times a year. Think he's going to be up for those games? And **Jaylen Brown**, alongside Maxey, alongside Embiid, if he can stay on the floor, I'm doing cartwheels if I'm Philadelphia. I'm having a party down Broad Street if I'm Philadelphia."
