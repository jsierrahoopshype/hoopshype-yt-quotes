"There is some talk about what he brings to the table as a versatile defender, as a guy who can shoot it pretty well, who played on that Dallas Mavericks team that wiped the floor with the Wolves in the Western Conference Finals. Maybe there is a little bit of a rich man's Nickeil situation here where when they first made the trade for **Mike Conley**, it was just **Mike Conley**. And yeah, we got Nickeil, but he was a throw-in in the deal. Maybe **Josh Green** emerges as a little bit of a rich man's Nickeil situation where they had to throw **Josh Green** in as well here. But maybe he can really become a contributor on this team and maybe he can really give the Wolves something that they don't have otherwise. He is going to get a chance to contribute and play, I think, some at the 4, some at the 3, and we will see him get minutes. I just want to see how he settles in, how he shoots it, how he defends, but that is a guy that I am going to watch closely early on, training camp, preseason, and into the regular season."
