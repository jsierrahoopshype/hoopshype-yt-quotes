**1. Davion Mitchell — "When you have some greatness or one of the greatest players on your team, the level of everything goes up." — Davion Mitchell on the impact of Giannis Antetokounmpo's arrival in Milwaukee** [06:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d7rYs-wrp-M&t=396s)

Davion Mitchell: "The trade was amazing. When you have some greatness or one of the greatest players on your team, the level of everything goes up automatically. His presence, the gravity that he brings, how much attention he brings, it makes everyone's job a lot easier. More cleaner looks. But the best thing is that we have to learn how to play with him. He is always going to create so many disadvantages for us, but learning how to move, when to move, learning when he wants the ball, what is his spots, those are little things that we still have to learn."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d7rYs-wrp-M&t=396s

**2. Davion Mitchell — "If I speed people up, it makes Giannis's job a lot easier." — Davion Mitchell on learning from Jrue Holiday's defensive point-of-attack pressure** [09:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d7rYs-wrp-M&t=587s)

Davion Mitchell: "Obviously the point of attack defender. If I speed people up, it makes Giannis's job a lot easier. A lot of people are so talented now in the NBA, the slower they go, the better they are. If you speed someone up, they are not really thinking. I am trying to speed someone up, run them down the corner, and Giannis is trailing or sitting back there, and he has nothing to do but try to lay it up or try to challenge him at the rim. I think Giannis is probably going to win that nine times out of ten."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d7rYs-wrp-M&t=587s

**3. Davion Mitchell — "My individual goal is to win Defensive Player of the Year." — Davion Mitchell's individual goal is winning Defensive Player of the Year** [14:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d7rYs-wrp-M&t=866s)

Davion Mitchell: "My individual goal is to win Defensive Player of the Year. It is all I got. No statistical goals, Defensive Player of the Year is just one of my goals."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d7rYs-wrp-M&t=866s

**4. Davion Mitchell — "Playing together is such a mismatch nightmare that people don't understand." — Davion Mitchell on the defensive mismatch nightmare of playing with Bam Adebayo** [15:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d7rYs-wrp-M&t=903s)

Davion Mitchell: "They are both different, obviously, and I think that they can both dominate the game in their own way. But they are also playing together is such a mismatch nightmare that people don't understand. There is not a lot of fives in this league that can guard Bam on a closeout, and there is definitely no five in the league that can guard Giannis on the closeout. I think they can protect the rim, but in a way that a lot of people think that backing up on Giannis is the way, and I think that makes it harder because he uses all his momentum."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d7rYs-wrp-M&t=903s

**5. Davion Mitchell — "Miami's never played to lose and they're always wanting to win." — Davion Mitchell on the Miami Heat's organizational culture of winning** [17:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d7rYs-wrp-M&t=1070s)

Davion Mitchell: "I just think that Miami's never played to lose and they're always wanting to win. They never really thought about the rules change this year, but just the draft. I have been in situations where people are kind of worried about that more than actually winning. I think Miami's organization is all about, we're going to play hard no matter what. We're going to try to win this game if we don't have none of our players, and we're going to try to win this game before the last seed in the NBA. That's what I love about it because you just play the game to win."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d7rYs-wrp-M&t=1070s
