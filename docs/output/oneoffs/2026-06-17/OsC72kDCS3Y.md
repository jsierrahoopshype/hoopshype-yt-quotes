**2. Rachel Nichols — "it's a level of poker that only the best teams can play." — The Celtics' championship-level poker strategy** [01:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OsC72kDCS3Y&t=101s)

**Rachel Nichols:** "It's a level of poker that only the best teams can play. How much of a tweak do you make? The New York Knicks, we're going to get to Game 6 of the Eastern Conference Finals, but fire our coach because we think we're that close. We think it's that much of a tweak to make to then get to and win a title, and by the way, paid off. It's the little tweak of, okay, are we so good that we can just bring in a **Jrue Holiday** and get rid of a **Marcus Smart** or do the kinds of things that they did at the Boston level. When you're playing that level of poker, you're saying, okay, if we get to an NBA Finals and we're facing **Victor Wembanyama**, can we match up with that team? That is where Giannis comes into the equation. Then it's not a **Jaylen Brown** conversation."

**Chris Mannix:** "Well, you're asking me, are you playing for that? If the question is, how do we win a title in today's NBA and are we good enough right now to do it given the level of competition, do you think that the Boston Celtics, as constructed, if they just sort of beef up their interior, but not Giannis beef up their interior, are they good enough?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OsC72kDCS3Y&t=101s
