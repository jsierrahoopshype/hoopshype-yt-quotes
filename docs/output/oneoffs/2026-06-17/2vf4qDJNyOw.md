# “Your Ass Never Was An Athlete Then” — Perk Responds To Vince Goodwill — *Road Trippin' Show*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2vf4qDJNyOw

_Speakers identified: Allie Clifton, Richard Jefferson, Vincent Goodwill, Kendrick Perkins_

**1. Vincent Goodwill — "Now you're legislating parity. The owners are saying you aren't allowed to be great for an extended period." — Vincent Goodwill on why the NBA should not legislate parity** [00:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2vf4qDJNyOw&t=17s)

**Vincent Goodwill:** "If we're going to look at the way the NBA experienced its golden age, those two decades where it went from Magic and Bird and Isaiah in between to **Michael Jordan**, that's where you had the greatest explosion and growth. It's not just because those guys hoarded the championships. Now you're legislating parity. The owners are saying you aren't allowed to be great for an extended period because look at Oklahoma City with Durant and Westbrook and Harden. They weren't allowed to keep their three guys together because the money says, okay, you drafted too well. You drafted three MVPs, you can't hold on to them."

**Kendrick Perkins:** "The reason that the Knicks won this championship is because Minnesota could not afford to hold on to **Karl-Anthony Towns** and he became the final piece for them. So it's going to also cost the Knicks on the back end."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2vf4qDJNyOw&t=17s

**2. Vincent Goodwill — "How do we know that any of the last eight champions are actually validated because they have not done it again?" — Vincent Goodwill questions the validity of recent NBA champions** [01:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2vf4qDJNyOw&t=66s)

Vincent Goodwill: "How do we know that any of the last eight champions are actually validated because they have not done it again? Giannis is itching to get out. Boston is thinking about trading **Jaylen Brown**. They don't believe in their one championship. LeBron's one championship in LA was not enough. So why would it be enough for us?"

**3. Kendrick Perkins — "Validate is the Larry O'Brien that I put on that shelf and it stays there forever." — Richard Jefferson and Kendrick Perkins debate the definition of a participation trophy** [01:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2vf4qDJNyOw&t=84s)

**Richard Jefferson:** "Validate is the **Larry O'Brien** that I put on that shelf and it stays there forever. That's the validation. The Laker thing..."

**Kendrick Perkins:** "Oh, you mean a participation trophy then?"

**Richard Jefferson:** "I'm sorry, **Larry O'Brien** is a participation trophy? It is an actual trophy. You won the league that year."

**4. Kendrick Perkins — "Oklahoma City didn't keep the big three together because James wanted his own team." — Kendrick Perkins clarifies why the Thunder broke up their core** [02:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2vf4qDJNyOw&t=146s)

Kendrick Perkins: "Oklahoma City didn't keep the big three together because James wanted his own team. It had nothing to do with the money. James had went to the Olympics, won a gold medal. **Dwyane Wade** was telling him that he needed his own team. **Kevin Durant** comes back to training camp and he's like we're going to lose James. And I said what the hell you mean? He was like everybody keep telling me he need his own team."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2vf4qDJNyOw&t=146s

**5. Kendrick Perkins — "Minnesota had a business decision to make. They could have kept Rudy or Towns. They chose to keep Rudy." — Kendrick Perkins on Minnesota's business decision to trade Karl-Anthony Towns** [03:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2vf4qDJNyOw&t=216s)

Kendrick Perkins: "Minnesota had a business decision to make. They could have kept Rudy or Towns. They chose to keep Rudy. They could have traded Rudy and got him off the books and kept **Karl-Anthony Towns** and challenged him like the New York Knicks challenged him to play the center position and get the best out of him defensively, which he displayed. But they chose to go with Rudy. So that was Minnesota's fault because they chose Rudy over **Karl-Anthony Towns**."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2vf4qDJNyOw&t=216s

**6. Kendrick Perkins — "When you're on television and you get in the moment and you fill in the table, you go say some bullshit." — Kendrick Perkins criticizes Vince Goodwill's take on championship validation** [04:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2vf4qDJNyOw&t=256s)

Kendrick Perkins: "Sometimes when you're on television and you get in the moment and you fill in the table, you go say some bullshit. That was a bunch of bullshit. It was all the way disrespectful. And when I heard him say it, the first thing came to mind is that, oh, your ass never was an athlete then. You couldn't have never participated or been a basketball player or played on anybody's team talking that type of nonsense. One of them was **Stephen Curry**. And I guarantee you if you go ask **Stephen Curry** which one of them was his greatest championship, nine times out of ten he's going to say the fourth one."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2vf4qDJNyOw&t=256s

**7. Kendrick Perkins — "I heard me on the show say, hey man, this is going to be Victor Wembanyama's biggest test." — Kendrick Perkins on Victor Wembanyama's defensive test** [06:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2vf4qDJNyOw&t=406s)
