# Malik Beasley Tells N3on The Truth About The NBA... — *N3ON*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I29AfBDh9Jo

_Speakers identified: N3on, Malik Beasley_

**1. Malik Beasley — "Physicality is different too. It's not as physical into the playoffs for the NBA. Down there it's physical." — Malik Beasley on the physical intensity of international versus NBA play** [17:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I29AfBDh9Jo&t=1021s)

**N3on:** "When you're in an intense game, a professional game, in the UFC they say not to drink water. They say to put it in your mouth and gargle it and swirl it around and spit it out. Are you drinking water?"

**Malik Beasley:** "I drink half water, half Gatorade because I don't want too many electrolytes in the game. Physicality is different too. It's not as physical into the playoffs for the NBA. Down there it's physical every fucking game. Everyone's locked the fuck in. They got a point to prove."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I29AfBDh9Jo&t=1021s

**2. Malik Beasley — "I actually got subbed out and they were like no, because I had an amazing game." — Malik Beasley on playing through exhaustion in Toronto** [17:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I29AfBDh9Jo&t=1040s)

**N3on:** "Have you ever been tired as fuck and you need to get subbed out but you know you can't and you're just cooked?"

**Malik Beasley:** "I actually got subbed out and they were like no, because I had an amazing game in Toronto. I was like I need to sub and he was like no, keep going. Then there was a free throw and I was like I got to come out."

**3. Malik Beasley — "I want to go to a championship team. I already know there's going to be star players." — Malik Beasley on his desire to join a championship-contending team** [19:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I29AfBDh9Jo&t=1148s)

**N3on:** "What's your ideal team right now? If you had to get a call from any team you could pick right now, who are you picking?"

**Malik Beasley:** "All 30 of them. I'm ready to get back in the league. I want to go to a championship team. I already know there's going to be star players and stuff like that. If I can come off the bench or start, I want to win."

**4. Malik Beasley — "There's nothing better than NBA players. But in terms of culture and the fans screaming, it was insane." — Malik Beasley compares international and NBA competition levels** [20:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I29AfBDh9Jo&t=1213s)

**N3on:** "What's the difference between playing internationally and playing in the NBA competition-wise, intensity-wise?"

**Malik Beasley:** "There's nothing better than NBA players. But in terms of culture and the fans screaming and stuff, it was insane in Puerto Rico. It was almost louder than a playoff game. It was a smaller arena and less fans, so that just shows how much the culture is."
