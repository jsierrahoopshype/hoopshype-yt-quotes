## Charles Bassey reflects on Steph Curry's role in Warriors' OT win | NBC Sports Bay Area — *NBC Sports Bay Area & California*

It's a joy to be a teammate of his."

**2. Charles Bassey — "He attracts so many players, man. He gets double-teamed every game." — How Steph Curry's gravity creates opportunities for teammates** [00:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uxs9DNceg6k&t=26s)

**Reporter:** "What does his gravity help open up for a center like you, those lanes?"

**Charles Bassey:** "It's a lot, man. You can see, man. He attracts so many players, man. He gets double-teamed every game. He definitely changes the game, making the bigs step up and stuff like that. So he's a different human when he's out there, man. It's fun playing with him because I know every time I'm out there and I screen for him, my man is wide open."

**3. Charles Bassey — "I just felt like every time my name was called, I had one job to just go there." — Charles Bassey on maintaining energy during close games** [01:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uxs9DNceg6k&t=64s)

**Reporter:** "When you hear your number called, what are you thinking about the impact you want to make?"

**Charles Bassey:** "I just bring, you know, it was an up and down game for us today. We had the energy coming in, we were up like 20, 25, they came back to the game. They had the energy. We were just up and down, but I just felt like every time my name was called, I had one job to just go there and just bring the energy, you know, change the game, just do what I'm supposed to do out there, defense, offense, just excite the team, which I think I did that."

**4. Charles Bassey — "Steve trusts everybody on this team. He trusts each and every player on this team." — Warriors building rhythm during long home stand** [01:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uxs9DNceg6k&t=86s)
