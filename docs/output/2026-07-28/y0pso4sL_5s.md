# Jalen & Josh On Game 4 Locker Room Convos, Their Greatest Comeback Ever?! — *Roommates Show*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y0pso4sL_5s

_Speakers identified: Jalen Brunson, Josh Hart, Matt Hillman_

**1. Josh Hart — "When you get down 29, you don't go, 'All right, y'all, let's go win the game.'" — Knicks' locker room mindset during 29-point deficit comeback** [00:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y0pso4sL_5s&t=7s)

**Josh Hart:** "When you get down 29, you don't go, 'All right, y'all, let's go win the game.' It's always, 'Okay, we're down 29, let's go down 18 by six minutes left in the third. We get to the fourth, let's be down 12. Let's be down eight points with five minutes left in the fourth.' So we're just chipping away."

**Jalen Brunson:** "One thing to kind of piggyback off that, even when we're down 20 with two minutes left and the game is over, whoever is on the court and we're in a timeout, we're always preaching, 'Hey, finish the game strong, have good habits.' You don't just want to give this game away. Everything counts. You want to continue to do the right things over and over again because that could give you momentum into the next game."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y0pso4sL_5s&t=7s

**2. Josh Hart — "That energy is contagious, especially for us. When the Garden gets loud, we start getting high." — The Garden's contagious energy fueling Knicks' comeback efforts** [02:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y0pso4sL_5s&t=146s)

Josh Hart: "I honestly think that was what helped us. We're down 27, somebody would make a three, and you think it's a two-possession game. The Garden is going crazy. They're out there trying their best to give us energy, and I think that's what kind of helped get us going. Once you start having that momentum, as you know, you've been in the Garden, you guys are there, the Garden is one of the best places to play, and that energy is contagious, especially for us. When the Garden gets loud, we start getting high, we start, you know, it's bumping. The other team, the lights get a little brighter."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y0pso4sL_5s&t=146s

**4. Jalen Brunson — "It's really such a home-field advantage getting a chance to play in New York at Yankee Stadium." — The unique home-field advantage of Yankee Stadium in the postseason** [05:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y0pso4sL_5s&t=307s)

Jalen Brunson: "That's where you want to play, Yankee Stadium in the postseason. You felt the energy."
