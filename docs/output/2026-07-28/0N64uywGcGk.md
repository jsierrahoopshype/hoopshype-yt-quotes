**1. Channing Frye — "I'll get my 15, 15, 7 and 7, go about my business and we're going to win by 30." — Channing Frye on Joel Embiid's potential team fit and health** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0N64uywGcGk&t=0s)

Channing Frye: "When you really think about it, you're just like, where can he go to be appreciated to do the least? If he goes to Cleveland, you're like, okay, he's got to revamp the culture. He's got to do this, he's got to do that, and they're a great team, but they also didn't make any moves this summer. Philadelphia, if you were talking about some of the most aggressive teams when it came to this summer's free agency, you would say Philly might be top three with Toronto and Miami. So with that attitude, you go, let's look top to bottom. They're ready to win. Do they have star power? For sure. Do they have great guards? Yeah. Do you have a great big man, when healthy? And **Joel Embiid**, you got to think, I think last year was the first year he hadn't played with a knee brace in almost seven years. So you would hope his health is getting better. And then you would say, hey, where's a place where I can be appreciated for being out there and saying, oh, you need me to be a guard tonight? Okay, I can do that. You need me to be a forward? Okay, I can do that. But certain nights he could just say, I'm 42. Here you go, **Jaylen Brown**, here you go, **Tyrese Maxey**. I'll get my 15, 15, 7 and 7, go about my business and we're going to win by 30."
