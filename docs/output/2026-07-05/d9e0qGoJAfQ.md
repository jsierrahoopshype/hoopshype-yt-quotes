**1. Muggsy Bogues — "I knew what I brought to the table, I knew what my strength and weaknesses were." — Muggsy Bogues on his height and impact in the NBA** [00:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9e0qGoJAfQ&t=31s)

Muggsy Bogues: "I developed that at an early age. For me, I understood who I was, so it never really bothered me. It was always just the inquisitive mind needed to know. Because I knew what I brought to the table, I knew what my strength and weaknesses were, and I knew what I was capable of. It was just others needed to get on board and understand the impact that I had. So it never really bothered me. It never got to me when folks said you're too small, they talk about your height. Cool, that's understood, that's something that you don't understand, but watch me play."

**2. Muggsy Bogues — "I played 14 years, but I could have played 17. I was signed for three years." — Muggsy Bogues on his 14-year NBA career and injury** [01:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9e0qGoJAfQ&t=85s)

Muggsy Bogues: "People don't understand, I played 14 years, but I could have played 17. I was signed for three years, but my mom passed away, so I decided to walk away from it. At a 5'3" guy, I got hurt in '94 to where, because at first, I was at the top of the leaderboard in terms of assists every year. And back then it wasn't all about the point guards were scoring guards, we were more like facilitators. And I've always been at the top of that. I had a double-double in my career in '94. But when I got hurt, y'all got to hurt Muggsy back then, and that was, but people don't understand, I still had it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9e0qGoJAfQ&t=85s

**3. Muggsy Bogues — "I knew you had 10 seconds to get the ball across half court." — Muggsy Bogues on his defensive pressure as a point guard** [03:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9e0qGoJAfQ&t=218s)

Muggsy Bogues: "It started early in my career when I was eight to nine years old. I had the ability to put pressure because I knew, especially being taught by Mr. **Leon Howard** who taught me and **Reggie Williams** the importance of what that position called for. But I knew you had 10 seconds to get the ball across half court. I knew that it starts with the point guard. Point guard is the one that put everybody in situations and run the team to the extension of the coach. So I always knew if I can disrupt him, it's going to make it tougher for the other guys."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9e0qGoJAfQ&t=218s

**4. Muggsy Bogues — "I had all the schools that was coming at me." — Muggsy Bogues on his high school and college basketball journey** [04:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9e0qGoJAfQ&t=294s)

Muggsy Bogues: "Growing up in the inner city of Baltimore, we always had, and I was always on teams where we won every year. Everything, we always won from 7 to 10 to 11 to 12. We didn't have AAU back then. But when I got to high school, I guess that's when you got the national attention, playing at Dunbar High School. Being around the players that I played with, the **Reggie Williams**, the **David Wingate**, maybe **Reggie Lewis**, may he rest in peace. Being with that type of group, and Coach Wade understood what I was and what I was capable of doing, and he gave me the freedom to be me. I had all the schools that was coming at me. I didn't have the Georgetown because they had a guy by **Michael Jackson** at the time, but Wake Forest, choosing Wake, also allowed me to continue to be on that national stage."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9e0qGoJAfQ&t=294s

**5. Muggsy Bogues — "We were the last collegiate team that won the gold medal before they sent the Dream Team." — Muggsy Bogues on winning the gold medal in 1986** [06:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9e0qGoJAfQ&t=411s)

Muggsy Bogues: "I think the turning point was when we played in the last collegiate team that won the gold medal. We won it in '86. We went ahead and myself, the **David Robinson**, and the **Kenny Smith**, we beat USSR, which is Russia, Sabonis and them. And getting that and being the one that led that team, it really got the NBA on notice in terms of, well damn, he's too small, maybe he can't do that. He can't play on that next level, they're too big. But then going to Port Smith and going to the combine and doing what I do, the same thing was happening there. So that allowed me to be the 12th player chosen in 1987."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9e0qGoJAfQ&t=411s

**6. Muggsy Bogues — "Everybody have a tendency, right? So me knowing that once the ball goes down, you can bring that back up." — Muggsy Bogues on his defensive strategy and timing** [09:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9e0qGoJAfQ&t=545s)

Muggsy Bogues: "Everybody have a tendency, right? So me knowing that once the ball goes down, you can bring that back up. It can't stop it. The dribble goes down, you cannot stop it. And as it goes down, that's when I shoot the gap. And that was something that I understood. And then, of course, guys feel like, you know, and I used to set them up. They think they run by you, I let them go by me because I play defense from behind. And one thing about pressing the ball, nobody want to get stripped at half court."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9e0qGoJAfQ&t=545s
