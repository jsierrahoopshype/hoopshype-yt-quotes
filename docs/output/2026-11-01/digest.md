## Kanye Took Jeezy’s Record and Made “Can’t Tell Me Nothing” — *ALL THE SMOKE*

## From The Bench To The Court... My First NBA Opportunity This Year!! — *Jaylen Clark*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bx6XGyQOVpE

_Speakers identified: Jaylen Clark_

**1. Jaylen Clark — "just because what your current situation is now, you can't stop doing or pushing to achieve and strive." — Jaylen Clark on the importance of staying ready for NBA opportunities** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bx6XGyQOVpE&t=0s)

Jaylen Clark: "I haven't really played at all this year much yet, but just going to show you guys why just because what your current situation is now, you can't stop doing or pushing to achieve and strive. I know I'm going to get a shot eventually this year, so just got to make sure I'm ready. One thing I can say though, I am grateful that the team this year is super fun to be around and everything. That makes the not playing process way easier when you're actually around guys that you really enjoy being around and stuff, and we're just a really good team. Whenever I get my opportunity, I will be ready."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bx6XGyQOVpE&t=0s

**2. Jaylen Clark — "I feel like a year ago me would have definitely been pouting and upset" — Jaylen Clark on maintaining professional mindset despite limited playing time** [05:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bx6XGyQOVpE&t=354s)

Jaylen Clark: "I don't ever like posting after a loss or nothing, but I think this is just a bigger picture type thing. Just keep going. I'm not even on no motivational stuff like I feel like I'm somebody that I'm not or anything, but it's just the fact that I feel like a year ago me would have definitely been pouting and upset because I didn't play in those first three to four games and not going to the gym and shooting and doing everything that I've been doing to know or to be able to take advantage when an opportunity came. Because obviously tonight with **Anthony Edwards** being out and everything, there was time available and I went out there and was able to perform to a way that I knew I was capable of and that I was proud of, moving the needle in the right direction for me."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bx6XGyQOVpE&t=354s

**3. Jaylen Clark — "In the NBA, man, it's all this much separation between you and the next guy" — Jaylen Clark on the competitive nature of NBA roster spots** [07:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bx6XGyQOVpE&t=432s)

Jaylen Clark: "In the NBA, man, it's all this much separation between you and the next guy, especially when you get to the part of the bench that I'm sitting at. It doesn't take much for them to be like, 'All right, well that didn't work, let's go this route.' And then somebody else take your opportunity and now you're traded and moved around and all this stuff and now you find yourself two, three years later out the league. My goal is to play at least 10 years, get the full pension and all the benefits and everything that come with it. So, I just really wanted to show you guys the real and what it takes to be able to make stuff work in this league."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bx6XGyQOVpE&t=432s
