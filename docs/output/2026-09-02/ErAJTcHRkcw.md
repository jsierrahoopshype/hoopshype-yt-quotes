**3. Raymond Felton — "The motherfucker used to have a party every city he went to, and come out and give you 35." — Allen Iverson's legendary status and ability to dominate every city** [02:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ErAJTcHRkcw&t=172s)

Raymond Felton: "I'm going to tell you something even crazier. I don't know if he was doing it back then, but the motherfucker used to have a party every city he went to, and come out and give you 35. It was definitely a flyer out there every single city. We're like, 'Who these come from?' Look over in the corner, it's **Allen Iverson**."

**4. Theo Pinson — "At that point I knew I was like, he can't do no wrong." — Allen Iverson's untouchable status and personality in the locker room** [03:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ErAJTcHRkcw&t=226s)

Theo Pinson: "His first day, I remember his locker was right next to mine in the practice facility. I come in, all his stuff is all over my locker. I look at him, he looks at me, he's like, 'What, punk bitch motherfucker?' I'm like, 'You know what? I'll sit over here. This your locker room, champ. You got it.' There's nothing I can do or say. Then another day he came in, he hit this old ass iPod. At this point, iPods had been touchscreen. Now, he got the one with the little spin on. Only thing he has on his iPod is all **Michael Jackson** songs. That's all he listens to is **Michael Jackson** songs. At that point I knew I was like, he can't do no wrong."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ErAJTcHRkcw&t=226s

**5. Raymond Felton — "Tim Hardaway to me had one of the best crossovers that I seen." — Tim Hardaway's crossover as the toughest guard to defend** [05:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ErAJTcHRkcw&t=304s)

Raymond Felton: "When he first came in his rookie year, we played against him in L.A. It was him, Philly, and **Sam Cassell**. I don't know if Sam was with Phoenix at that time, but he was always saying, 'Hey son, hey son, that motherfucker got that crossover son, he got that crossover.' He says it's going to be left to right, left to right. So the whole time, I'm waiting on that crossover. Man, that shit was so nasty. I mean, **Tim Hardaway** to me had one of the best crossovers that I seen, and then when **Allen Iverson** came, they had to put a rule in for the dude with his crossover. It was, I don't give a fuck what you do, if you go watch his highlights and just watch his crossovers, everybody's body ended up the same way. It was every single time, and you knew it was coming."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ErAJTcHRkcw&t=304s

**6. Raymond Felton — "I think he's the only player who never went to rookie transition camp." — Allen Iverson's unique career and refusal to attend rookie transition camp** [07:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ErAJTcHRkcw&t=440s)

Raymond Felton: "I think he's the only player who never went to rookie transition camp. You know, and they file you every year until you do it. So he just gave them a check every year. I ain't going. Through his whole career, he never went to rookie transition camp. They were still finding him because he never did it. So he just told them, 'Y'all just find me every year because I'm not going.'"
