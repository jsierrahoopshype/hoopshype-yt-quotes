So once I got to basketball, when now you're telling me you want me to get wide, run the floor, I got to catch an outlet pass and finish it, and I have court vision, understanding and running the lanes, getting wide, because, you know, I another humble brag, I led my team in doubles and triples. I think I was second in home runs those two years. And a lot of doubles. So I know how to banana, get that bag, get the second base, or get the third base. So it's just like basketball, you got to get wide as you can, get to that sideline, run the lanes, and get finished. So a lot of those things translate to hand-eye coordination, the vision, finding the rim, stuff like that."
