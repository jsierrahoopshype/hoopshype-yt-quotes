## Victor Wembanyama's $252M Extension + The Moment Vince Carter Knew It Was Time to Retire | Cousins — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ruF4xGLUAaw

_Speakers identified: Vince Carter, Tracy McGrady_

**1. Vince Carter — "This shows that he wants to win. When you leave $50 million on the table, you want to improve your roster." — Victor Wembanyama's contract extension and his mission to win** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ruF4xGLUAaw&t=0s)

**Vince Carter:** "Wembanyama signs five years, $252 million extension. He left $50 million on the table. What does that say about him as a young guy? I think the inevitable, we know he's going to make his money down the line regardless. So he's going to recoup this quickly, but I think he understands at a young age, a couple years in the league, that yeah, I'm working to get my money, but I want to make sure I have the team around me. Wembanyama is going to be on a mission next year, and I think this is the start of Wembanyama on a mission by leaving this money on the table to make sure they can get what they need to go right back to the finals where he can kind of get that bad taste out of his mouth."

**Tracy McGrady:** "This shows that he wants to win. When you leave $50 million on the table, you want to improve your roster to get better talent because you want to win. And it's just that simple. **Jaylen Brunson** did it, left some money on the table to improve his roster, bring his fellow college teammates on board because the chemistry was there, they know how to play off of each other, and it worked. It won a championship this year. Victor is no different."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ruF4xGLUAaw&t=0s

I got traded from Houston to the Knicks half a season, and then I signed a lockout year with the Hawks, then with Detroit. For me, it's, all right, prove to yourself you can make a roster because I'm coming off microfracture. That was a challenge for me. I knew I wasn't going to be an All-NBA player, but it's like, can I get back on a roster? Got on a roster. Now, as you know, and everybody else know, when you're not All-NBA and when you're not that guy anymore, you're coming down on the roster, you're at the end, they're thinking about throwing you and seeing how many minutes they can give you.
