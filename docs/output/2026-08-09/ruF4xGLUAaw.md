# Victor Wembanyama's $252M Extension + The Moment Vince Carter Knew It Was Time to Retire | Cousins — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ruF4xGLUAaw

_Speakers identified: Vince Carter, Tracy McGrady_

**1. Vince Carter — "This shows that he wants to win. When you leave $50 million on the table, you want to improve your roster." — Victor Wembanyama's contract extension and his mission to win** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ruF4xGLUAaw&t=0s)

**Vince Carter:** "Wembanyama signs five years, $252 million extension. He left $50 million on the table. What does that say about him as a young guy? I think the inevitable, we know he's going to make his money down the line regardless. So he's going to recoup this quickly, but I think he understands at a young age, a couple years in the league, that yeah, I'm working to get my money, but I want to make sure I have the team around me. Wembanyama is going to be on a mission next year, and I think this is the start of Wembanyama on a mission by leaving this money on the table to make sure they can get what they need to go right back to the finals where he can kind of get that bad taste out of his mouth."

**Tracy McGrady:** "This shows that he wants to win. When you leave $50 million on the table, you want to improve your roster to get better talent because you want to win. And it's just that simple. **Jaylen Brunson** did it, left some money on the table to improve his roster, bring his fellow college teammates on board because the chemistry was there, they know how to play off of each other, and it worked. It won a championship this year. Victor is no different."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ruF4xGLUAaw&t=0s

**2. Vince Carter — "You have a rotating trio of bigs because we know Wembanyama likes to play on the perimeter sometimes." — San Antonio Spurs need a rotating trio of bigs** [02:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ruF4xGLUAaw&t=153s)

**Vince Carter:** "They need another big so you have a rotating trio of bigs because we know Wembanyama likes to play on the perimeter sometimes, so you can have another big that can be your rebounder or rim protector as well. Then you have two of them, because we saw it at some point there, their big lineup was very effective, especially defensively."

**Tracy McGrady:** "But in the finals, Luke couldn't play in the finals."

**Vince Carter:** "Yeah, so I feel like you go out there, you get another guy out there, so you have a defensive force in Wembanyama, but I feel like you need multiple bigs, especially with a team that is young and athletic like that where you can play so many different ways."

**3. Vince Carter — "I think they need a hybrid big. They need someone that can switch on pick-and-roll." — Spurs need a hybrid big like Naz Reid or Paul Reed** [03:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ruF4xGLUAaw&t=232s)

**Tracy McGrady:** "I think they need a hybrid big. They need someone that can switch on pick-and-roll."

**Vince Carter:** "That's the person I'm visualizing, a **Naz Reid**. He has a high motor, he rebounds, he can shoot the three, he's going to defend. I feel he's like another guy, obviously **Naz Reid** is there, but **Paul Reed** is another guy who had a very good year for Detroit. I don't know how they use him, what his contract looks like, but that could be another guy that they can bring in there."

**4. Tracy McGrady — "I had microfracture surgery in 2009, and after that I played four years after that surgery." — Tracy McGrady on his microfracture surgery and retirement** [04:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ruF4xGLUAaw&t=276s)

Tracy McGrady: "I had microfracture surgery in 2009, and after that I played four years after that surgery. Well, after the surgery, I wanted to come back and prove that I can make a roster, because I knew I wasn't going to be the same player. I got traded from Houston to the Knicks half a season, and then I signed a lockout year with the Hawks, then with Detroit. For me, it's, all right, prove to yourself you can make a roster because I'm coming off microfracture. That was a challenge for me. I knew I wasn't going to be an All-NBA player, but it's like, can I get back on a roster? Got on a roster. Now, as you know, and everybody else know, when you're not All-NBA and when you're not that guy anymore, you're coming down on the roster, you're at the end, they're thinking about throwing you and seeing how many minutes they can give you. You have to be mentally tough."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ruF4xGLUAaw&t=276s

**5. Vince Carter — "COVID hits, so I think it kind of was more of a reality." — Vince Carter on the impact of COVID-19 on his final season** [08:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ruF4xGLUAaw&t=497s)

**Vince Carter:** "COVID hits, so I think it kind of was more of a reality. It was that much of a thought, maybe you're 23, but I think I made the right decision for a lot of reasons. And for those players who played the following year after COVID, they said it was tough. It was tough to move around in life in general for the fact of all the testing that you had to do. I started doing some games for the Hawks and we walking in the empty buildings calling games, it was just one of the weirdest things ever."

**Tracy McGrady:** "Like you, I started to feel that same, regardless of who people felt that I was, you started hearing that, we know what you can bring to the table for us, whatever, but we got to see what these young guys have, we got to see what that looks like. I was caught in the, I wasn't going to ring chase, because I just couldn't, I wanted to play and I felt that I could play."
