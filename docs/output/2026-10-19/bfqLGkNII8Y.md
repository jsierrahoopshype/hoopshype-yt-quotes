**1. Donte DiVincenzo — "I kind of fell in love with this process of being by myself and rehabbing." — Donte DiVincenzo on his mental recovery process from injury** [01:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bfqLGkNII8Y&t=111s)

Donte DiVincenzo: "On basketball being taken away from me unexpectedly, it changes your whole perspective of your career. For me, just being by myself, I never left. I just stayed here all summer, just rehabbed every single day. I kind of fell in love with this process of being by myself and rehabbing. When the guys got back, that was the first gut punch, the mental obstacle for me of being like, damn, I'm not playing right now. They made it so easy for me to feel like I'm actually playing. They really did. Everybody from top to bottom, coaches, front office, players, they included me in everything. So they really made this process so far really easy for me to just come in every day and work."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bfqLGkNII8Y&t=111s

**2. Donte DiVincenzo — "I don't look at anything in terms of timeline. I looked at it as, these are your criteria that you need to hit." — Donte DiVincenzo on his goal-driven approach to injury rehab** [04:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bfqLGkNII8Y&t=250s)

Donte DiVincenzo: "I don't look at anything in terms of timeline of, oh, at week 21 I'll be running or whatever like that. I looked at it as, we sat down and we said, all right, these are your criteria that you need to hit in order to do the next phase. So for me, I'm goal-driven. So I'm looking at it like, okay, I got to calf raise this much or I got to iso hold this much in order to run or in order to walk. And that was better for me versus looking at it at week 21 you're going to run. Well, if week 21 rolls around and you're not ready to run, now you have a mental battle that you're fighting yourself with."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bfqLGkNII8Y&t=250s

**4. Donte DiVincenzo — "As you get stronger within this rehab and you add things, you can't replace things." — Donte DiVincenzo on the repetitive nature of injury rehab** [06:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bfqLGkNII8Y&t=398s)

Donte DiVincenzo: "As you get stronger within this rehab and you add things, you can't replace things. And I think that's what I'm learning is, as you go through this and you get to a strength standpoint of being able to do certain movements or run or be on the court more, that doesn't mean that you're not going to do all the stuff that you did for the months before that in order to still do it again. So the days become a little bit longer, but I think the love and hate comes from, what did I do today? I did a million calf raises. So what am I going to do tomorrow? A million calf raises. It's the repetitive stuff that's the battle."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bfqLGkNII8Y&t=398s
