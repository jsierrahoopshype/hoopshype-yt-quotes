# Vince Carter & T-Mac On Being “One Of The Guys,” Team Camaraderie & Carrying A Franchise — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zUb7atUFA2s

_Speakers identified: Vince Carter, Tracy McGrady_

**1. Tracy McGrady — "When you do stuff like that, you host your teammates at your house with a certain event." — Tracy McGrady on building team camaraderie off the court** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zUb7atUFA2s&t=0s)

**Tracy McGrady:** "I absolutely think the guy can be one of the guys. I felt like I was one of the guys when we were on the road. I'm hanging out with my teammates, we go to dinner, or if we got a couple days in a city, we'll hang out with each other. We even had Yao out. So when you do stuff like that, you host your teammates at your house with a certain event."

**Vince Carter:** "I love to see y'all out. I see Yao walking in Portland one time. We were in Portland one time and we all went out as a team and we took Yao out and we had a good time."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zUb7atUFA2s&t=0s

**2. Vince Carter — "When you build that camaraderie off the court, it translates on the court." — Vince Carter on how off-court relationships translate to on-court performance** [01:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zUb7atUFA2s&t=100s)

**Vince Carter:** "When you build that camaraderie off the court, it translates on the court, especially with you being the guy. We know all the stuff that you go through. We go to a restaurant, we go to the club, they see us, but they also see you. You are comforted with your bros. So I feel like on the court, those guys now will do the same. Look out for you, they'll play hard for you, they'll do anything for you."

**Tracy McGrady:** "You don't have to always go to a guy and be like, 'Hey dog,' or whatever. Or that may be the case and they respond to it because of what you built off the court, or vice versa. You being the man, some of those guys that don't play as many minutes come to you, now you are willing to take that criticism or that guy up in your face and saying what he got to say and you know it's coming from a pure place."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zUb7atUFA2s&t=100s

**3. Vince Carter — "You can't want to be the man and want to have everything and want your team to follow you, but yet you don't break bread with them." — Vince Carter on the importance of leaders breaking bread with teammates** [02:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zUb7atUFA2s&t=153s)

**Vince Carter:** "I've been with a young star in my older years where that was one of the things that was our struggle. I'm like, 'Bro, you can't want to be the man and want to have everything and want your team to follow you, but yet you don't break bread with them.' And I'm not saying you got to go out and be the guy that makes all the money, but you got to be the guy that spent. That ain't it. It's just the time we spend together."

**Tracy McGrady:** "That dynamic will never work. It won't equal winning."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zUb7atUFA2s&t=153s

**4. Tracy McGrady — "I'm expected to play with Grant Hill. Now Grant Hill is out, so things are shifted for me." — Tracy McGrady on adjusting his mindset when teammates were injured** [05:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zUb7atUFA2s&t=306s)

**Tracy McGrady:** "It really got a little tricky too because I'm expected to play with **Grant Hill**. Now **Grant Hill** is out, so things are shifted for me. So how I came in thinking one thing, now I totally got to have a different mindset on how I approach and go about this season."

**Vince Carter:** "Because I had to put more into it and do a little bit more, the responsibility was a little bit heavier. Man, I got to All-Star break, bro. I needed that break. I can't even lie to you."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zUb7atUFA2s&t=306s

**5. Tracy McGrady — "When you lose one of your max players, it's not like you could go get another top player." — Tracy McGrady on the difficulty of replacing max-contract players** [05:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zUb7atUFA2s&t=340s)

**Tracy McGrady:** "You got to think, back then our cap is only maybe 70 million. The team salary cap. So when you lose one of your max players, it's not like you could go get another top player or a mid-level player to bring in, because we don't have the money. So what you started with and when that max player goes, 'All right, this is your team.' And we didn't have the assets to make a trade."

**Vince Carter:** "I should have said the lockout year also, because I'm thinking back to lockout year. Remember your second year, those three games in a row. We were playing three games in a row, six games in seven nights sometimes."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zUb7atUFA2s&t=340s

**6. Tracy McGrady — "It was an accumulation and a build-up for three years of frustration on not having a contending team." — Tracy McGrady on the frustration of playing with non-contending teams** [09:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zUb7atUFA2s&t=552s)

**Tracy McGrady:** "It was an accumulation and a build-up for three years of frustration on not having a contending team. And the reason being, I don't think we drafted well, but also, again, **Grant Hill**, bro. Like, what am I supposed to do? What are we supposed to do when our best player, and I say our best player because Grant was our best player."

**Vince Carter:** "He was that guy."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zUb7atUFA2s&t=552s

**7. Tracy McGrady — "You think if Tim Duncan was off of San Antonio back in the early 2000s, they going to do what they did?" — Tracy McGrady on the impact of losing star players to injury** [10:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zUb7atUFA2s&t=631s)

**Tracy McGrady:** "You think if **Tim Duncan** was off of San Antonio back in the early 2000s, they going to do what they did? You think if Kobe or **Shaq** was out for four years, they win three championships? We seen what happened with the Lakers when **Shaq** left. Right? So, what you want me to do when our best player is gone? I'm supposed to be frustrated, no?"

**Vince Carter:** "You're the teammate, bro."
