**11. Trae Young — "It's not even competing with the person to the left or right of you, it's like competing with yourself" — Trae Young on the internal competition of training on the dunes** [10:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UXLSNbO8w5Q&t=610s)

Trae Young: "There's a lot of reasons why I love going to the dunes. I think obviously the work you put in, but the the energy, the vibe, the setting, the scene. Being able to compete and get to the top of the hill. It's not even competing with the person to the left or right of you, it's like competing with yourself and getting to the top of the hill and getting up there and reaching the top and then sitting there and looking out and seeing the water and just feeling the the breeze from the air, it's like you feel like you accomplished something there. It wasn't easy to accomplish it, nobody made you accomplish it, nobody made you finish and push through, like you did it yourself and I think that's something that's good to have in your mind."
