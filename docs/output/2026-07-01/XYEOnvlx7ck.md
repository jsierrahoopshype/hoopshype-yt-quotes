# Mitchell Robinson signs a 3-year/$47 million deal with the Boston Celtics 🚨 | First Take — *NBA on ESPN*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XYEOnvlx7ck

_Speakers identified: David Jacoby, Wendy Nix, Brian Windhorst, Kendrick Perkins_

**1. David Jacoby — "I don't really know what this means for the Celtics that we figure out what the Jaylen Brown trade looks like." — Mitchell Robinson's impact on the Celtics' roster and Jaylen Brown trade** [00:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XYEOnvlx7ck&t=27s)

**David Jacoby:** "It was great what he did with the Knicks and it was time for them to part. I don't really know what this means for the Celtics that we figure out what the **Jaylen Brown** trade looks like. It seems like this is more signaling of him being out very soon, but we just do not know what that roster is going to look like around **Mitchell Robinson**. I don't think he is a starter-level center that is going to be that much of a difference-maker on the squad, but I also don't know what kind of difference-maker Boston is going to make overall until we see the package they get for **Jaylen Brown**."

**Brian Windhorst:** "This is a pretty interesting move by the Celtics. The Celtics needed center help. They have been thin at the front-line position since they had to lose Al Horford and they traded away Kristaps Porzingis last year. They have signaled that they want Neemias Queta, their terrific pickup from a couple of years ago at low money, to be their future center. So they bring in **Mitchell Robinson** as a backup. This is a quality signing for a backup position. It is a mid-level salary, about what he was making last year, and he is obviously proven that he can be very impactful."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XYEOnvlx7ck&t=27s
