David Dennis Jr.: "First, I am going to throw **Donovan Mitchell** out there. No disrespect to him, but I don't put him in the same category as **Giannis Antetokounmpo** and **Stephen Curry**. **2. David Dennis Jr. — "Steph Curry for me is the best fit for LeBron James." — Steph Curry is the ideal running mate for LeBron James** [01:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_A-2z-n8FAE&t=63s)

David Dennis Jr.: "**Stephen Curry** for me is the best fit for **LeBron James**. It is just the fact that these two guys work hand and fist together, as we have seen. It just works well. The offense is already ready-made. They have played together. They know each other back and forth. They know just by studying each other playing against one another, they know what they are going to do on the court before the other person does it. These guys know how to play together, they know how to win championships against each other, and gold medals with each other. Then add in the **Draymond Green** element of this."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_A-2z-n8FAE&t=63s

**3. Vincent Goodwill — "At 42, 43 years old, how much heavy lifting do you want to have to do?" — LeBron James at 42 cannot carry the heavy lifting** [02:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_A-2z-n8FAE&t=128s)

Vincent Goodwill: "My thing with **LeBron James** is at 42, 43 years old, how much heavy lifting do you want to have to do? If you go and play with **Stephen Curry** and **Draymond Green**, you have to go be the best version of **LeBron James** that you can afford to be. If you go to Miami, they have a better version of that in **Giannis Antetokounmpo**. They have a guy in **Bam Adebayo** that is one of the best two-way players in the league. LeBron has increasingly become a better jump shooter off the ball as he has aged. That is one of the things that has allowed him to be a more effective ball player playing with **Anthony Davis**, playing with **Luka Doncic**."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_A-2z-n8FAE&t=128s

Vincent Goodwill: "Playing with the Miami Heat, the franchise, it feels better to me. They know how to build out a roster.
