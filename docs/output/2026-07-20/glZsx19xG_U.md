**2. Draymond Green — "Rarely do you see 18, 19-year-olds coming to the league as polished as you've seen LeBron James" — AJ Dybantsa's polished game and maturity remind me of LeBron James** [03:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=glZsx19xG_U&t=201s)

Draymond Green: "**AJ Dybantsa** reminds me a lot of **LeBron James** from the aspect of he's been expected to be the number one pick since he's 15 years old. When I say I see a lot of LeBron in him, what I mean by that is rarely do you see 18, 19-year-olds coming to the league as polished as you've seen **LeBron James** coming to the league, as you're seeing **AJ Dybantsa** step into the league. And when I say as polished, yes, from a basketball, from a game perspective, of course, but from the way that they carry themselves, the way they understand what's ahead of them, the business that's ahead of them, and how they need to go about doing it. I see a lot of that in AJ, just buttoned all the way up."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=glZsx19xG_U&t=201s

**3. Draymond Green — "If you've got LeBron James and Steph Curry in a pick and roll, you better go find a role" — Draymond Green on his role in the Warriors' offense with LeBron James** [12:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=glZsx19xG_U&t=725s)

Draymond Green: "If you've got **LeBron James** and Steph Curry in a pick and roll, I'll always have that ability to go get in the pick and roll and make some plays. But if you've got **LeBron James** and Steph Curry in a pick and roll, you better go find a role, you better go find a spot so you can be valuable. And so if those two guys are in a pick and roll, for me, the most valuable spot is going to be in the corner. I think so often you idiots think, oh man, this guy can do this, he can do that. There are roles, and if you cannot figure out how to play a role, you usually fail. And so for me, I know what spot would be open is the corner. So you go to the corner and you knock them down."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=glZsx19xG_U&t=725s

**4. Draymond Green — "Lu Dort is a big loss for OKC. He is one of their better defenders" — The Thunder's trade of Lu Dort is a significant loss for their defense** [19:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=glZsx19xG_U&t=1178s)

Draymond Green: "**Lu Dort** is a big loss for OKC. He is one of their better defenders. He brings a bravado and a toughness to that group. And obviously, I think being one of, if not Shai's best friend in the NBA, they've been together since **Lu Dort**'s rookie year and I think it was Shai's second or third year. Big changes for OKC. When you start losing core pieces like a **Lu Dort**, it changes things, and it's not business as usual and status quo."

**6. Draymond Green — "I find it interesting that the NBA is investigating this because I don't agree with the investigation" — NBA investigation into the Bucks' Gary Trent Jr. signing is unfair** [29:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=glZsx19xG_U&t=1791s)

Draymond Green: "I find it interesting that the NBA is investigating this because I don't agree with the investigation. I think when you look at the lay of the land, when you look at a team like the Milwaukee Bucks, they're ultimately going to need Gary Trent's salary to trade and get value back. Gary Trent did not have a great year last year. There were times where Doc Rivers took him out of the lineup and he collected some DNPs even at times. I don't think he necessarily got to truly show Gary Trent. He's still a fairly young player. I do think at the mid-level that Gary Trent will show that, yeah, of course I'm a mid-level player. I believe that Gary Trent will show that. But as far as the investigation goes, now we got a player getting paid who most probably thought shouldn't have and now it's an investigation. I don't like that. I think it's a slippery slope for the NBA."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=glZsx19xG_U&t=1791s
