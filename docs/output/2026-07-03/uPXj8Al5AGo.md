**2. Mike Brown — "I wanted my staff to be really strong and not afraid and in and in diverse."** [05:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uPXj8Al5AGo&t=305s)

Mike Brown: "The first thing I wanted is I wanted my staff to be really strong and not afraid and in and in diverse. And when I say not afraid, I'm talking about not afraid of me. I don't feel like I have vibes like I'm a tough guy to deal with, but I'm going to be wrong and sometimes I'm going to push, push, push. And if as a staff or as a person on my staff, you feel like that's the wrong thing, you got to be strong enough to hold your ground and keep coming. Eventually, I'll change. So I wanted a strong, diverse staff that wasn't afraid to say what they needed to say, but also they were willing to serve the players because again, it's about the players, but serve the players first."

**3. Mike Brown — "I came in with the idea of Josh coming off the bench."** [07:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uPXj8Al5AGo&t=440s)

Mike Brown: "One of the lows was, and I'm not saying this just because you're sitting here, so I came in with the idea of Josh coming off the bench. And I did it not because, again, this is for me, I don't think you always start your quote-unquote best five players. You start five players and it's about who ends the game. But trying to find the different combinations throughout the course of the game was the part that I was looking at the most. And so bringing Josh off the bench to start and then us going through the struggles that we went through that early, because usually in order to truly believe, it's good to have some success first and then to go backwards, but we almost started going backwards because of it to a certain degree."

**4. Mike Brown — "I thought it was a big deal because I thought this team had a chance."** [09:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uPXj8Al5AGo&t=576s)

Mike Brown: "One of the highs for me was definitely the cup. And I know some people look at it differently. I thought it was a big deal because I thought this team had a chance. And when we won that, especially when you're talking about single-game elimination, it showed that we can have others step up. But winning that game and then winning the other games when it's single elimination, for me, that was a huge, huge high because it showed me that we have what it takes to do it. Now, you got to be lucky and all this other stuff and healthy when the time came to go on your run, but going through that, I was like, hell yeah, boy, yes. We got a chance."
