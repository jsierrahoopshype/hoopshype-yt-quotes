# Cam Johnson Explains Why NBA Shooters Barely Miss — *The Old Man and The Three*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3MzMDtgSnL0

_Speakers identified: Cam Johnson, Pierre Newton, Jesse Childs_

**1. Cam Johnson — "He comes flying off this pin down and he keeps his momentum going and shoots it like that." — James Harden's unique ability to maintain momentum off screens** [00:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3MzMDtgSnL0&t=37s)

Cam Johnson: "When I was watching **James Harden**, I think he was in his 76ers days, but he's been doing it for a long time at this point. One is the pace that he moves off the screens with. And two is when he comes off that screen and gets a catch, he doesn't stop. He comes flying off this pin down or whatever, pinning, and he keeps his momentum going and shoots it like that. So I'm watching that and I'm like, he got pretty good at that shot."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3MzMDtgSnL0&t=37s

**2. Cam Johnson — "We're going on the court and work on these footworks so that you're able to use them." — Using Klay Thompson's footwork to improve shooting efficiency** [01:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3MzMDtgSnL0&t=84s)

Cam Johnson: "I had had a couple coaches in college, specifically one of my assistants at Pitt named Tom Richardson, that would take me into the coach's office, pull up 35 clips of **Klay Thompson** relocating and be like, 'We're going on the court and work on these footworks so that you're able to use them in a game.'"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3MzMDtgSnL0&t=84s

**3. Cam Johnson — "He changes it to a full speed, sprint, shot on the go without messing with his efficiency." — How JJ Redick's shooting form maintains efficiency at full speed** [02:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3MzMDtgSnL0&t=145s)

Cam Johnson: "With this **JJ Redick** stuff, he took what most people turn, slow down, shoot it straight up and down, and he changes it to a full speed, sprint, shot on the go without messing with his efficiency. And now you can call his play for him whenever. He's a threat. When he comes running off, defenders jump, he can go. Defenders jump, he can pass. And a lot of times he did just shoot it. But he got so good at it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3MzMDtgSnL0&t=145s

**4. Cam Johnson — "When he gets the ball in his left hand and he creates space, it's super hard to contest." — Luka Doncic's elite ability to create space for fadeaway shots** [07:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3MzMDtgSnL0&t=465s)

Cam Johnson: "One of the best at shooting this kind of fadeaway three is probably **Luka Doncic**. So when he gets the ball in his left hand and he creates space, it's super hard to contest because he's going this way. And for him, it's just as simple as shooting a regular shot form. So when he gets that space, he has this slight lean this way, or whatever way he's going. And a lot of guys when you're a right-handed shooter, it's very nice and comfortable to fade left."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3MzMDtgSnL0&t=465s

**5. Cam Johnson — "Nikola Jokic can shoot the ball at any shooting pocket." — Nikola Jokic's ability to shoot from any pocket** [12:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3MzMDtgSnL0&t=754s)

Cam Johnson: "The best thing I can say is I think there was a clip of **Nikola Jokic** where he had made like 90% of 1,000 threes. **Nikola Jokic** can shoot the ball at any shooting pocket. And I think it's because he has such a repeatable and very strong base that's very connected. He's extremely connected as a shooter and it is very pure."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3MzMDtgSnL0&t=754s
