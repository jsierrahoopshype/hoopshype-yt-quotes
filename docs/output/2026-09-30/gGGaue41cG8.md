**2. Jason Sudeikis — "I think you go there to get better at doing what you do versus being seen." — Why Chicago is the best place to develop comedy skills** [01:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gGGaue41cG8&t=117s)

Jason Sudeikis: "I think people move to Chicago to get better at doing what you do versus being seen for what you can do. I think going to New York or LA, there are lots of places that are ready to snatch you right up and take you as is, versus Chicago, there is more of a work ethic instilled in you and it almost beats the smart-ass or the bullshit out of you."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gGGaue41cG8&t=117s

**3. Jason Sudeikis — "Anything that he did in a game he had practiced over and over and over." — Jason Sudeikis on Jason Williams' practice habits** [04:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gGGaue41cG8&t=287s)

Jason Sudeikis: "I love that clip that I see all the time for some reason with **Jason Williams** where he is talking about anything that he did in a game he had practiced over and over and over. I felt the same way with jokes. Even though you are improvising, there is something that I have been in this space before and you are just catching up with it."

**4. Jason Sudeikis — "Tina Fey was on the main stage then she got hired away by Adam McKay." — Tina Fey's influence on Sudeikis's career at SNL** [05:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gGGaue41cG8&t=352s)

Jason Sudeikis: "When I first moved to Chicago, **Tina Fey** was on the main stage then she got hired away by **Adam McKay**, who was head writer at SNL. She would go write on SNL. Just to go write. And so we overlapped for a month. But again, at that point I was just a student and I was taking classes and when you take classes you get to watch the shows for free if they got space."

**5. Jason Sudeikis — "I would tell them I cannot come in until noon because I am out until 12." — Sudeikis's work ethic and dedication to watching comedy** [06:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gGGaue41cG8&t=382s)

Jason Sudeikis: "Any real job I had, I would tell them I cannot come in until noon because I am out until 12. I was not drinking or anything at that point. I was not partying at all.
