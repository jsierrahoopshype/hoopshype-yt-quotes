## Josh Reveals His First Thought When Coach Mike Arrived to the Knicks — *Roommates Show*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oq4sMp3l5SQ

_Speakers identified: Josh Hart, Jalen Brunson, Matt Hillman_

**1. Josh Hart — "I'm a do whatever is asked." — Josh Hart on adjusting to Tom Thibodeau's coaching style** [00:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oq4sMp3l5SQ&t=23s)

**Josh Hart:** "I mean, we talked about it in his office. It was one of those things, I'm a do whatever is asked. But in that moment when he was telling me that, I'm just looking at him like, yeah, whatever."

**Jalen Brunson:** "It was one of those things where it was just like, I was a starter the last couple years and I was comfortable with it. And then it was just like, okay, we're going to switch it up. So I'm just like, okay, cool."

**Matt Hillman:** "You kind of make exceptions for those guys. You give those guys leeway. Those are the guys."

**Josh Hart:** "For me, I have no idea how my game will translate into how he wants to play. I'm thinking of that, and then it's like, okay, we're going to bring you off the bench. I'm like, damn, we're going to see."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oq4sMp3l5SQ&t=23s

**2. Josh Hart — "He might not have the answer to the question right there, but he's going to figure it out." — Josh Hart on Tom Thibodeau's collaborative coaching philosophy** [02:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oq4sMp3l5SQ&t=122s)

Josh Hart: "When he talked about his philosophy, the thing that we all as players value the most of him is just, he might not have the answer to the question right there, but he's going to figure it out. He's not just going to sit there. At the end of the day, he's the head coach and he's going to make the call. But how collaborative he is with the other coaches and even us, we can say we're pretty entitled, bougie guys sitting there like, coach, I don't know if we should be doing shootaround. He listened to everybody. I knew it was going to be frustrating, but I thought we can probably make it work because he listens to everybody."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oq4sMp3l5SQ&t=122s

**3. Josh Hart — "Mike is my seventh coach in nine years." — Josh Hart on the instability of playing for seven different coaches** [03:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oq4sMp3l5SQ&t=213s)

Josh Hart: "**Mike** is my seventh coach in nine years. I've obviously had this instability, but then you see different coaches, different philosophies. He's probably been the most collaborative, listening to everybody. At the end of the day, he's going to make that decision that he thinks is best."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oq4sMp3l5SQ&t=213s

**4. Matt Hillman — "I've been able to work for two of arguably the greatest coaches of all time." — Tom Thibodeau on managing media criticism and external noise** [05:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oq4sMp3l5SQ&t=324s)

Matt Hillman: "I don't pay attention to stuff online because one thing I've learned over the course of years is, for me, if people are saying good stuff about you, I'm human, my head can be big already, but it's going to get even bigger. I don't need that to happen, especially during the season. And if they say negative stuff about you, it's going to bring me down. I've been able to work for two of arguably the greatest coaches of all time when it comes to winning championships. **Greg Popovich** has five, **Steve Kerr** has five, I think. I've been around people that have talked about those guys right in front of my face and they're like, man, they should retire. And I'm like, what the hell are you talking about? The dude's won this, he's done that. I just choose not to read it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oq4sMp3l5SQ&t=324s
