**1. Derik Queen — "the physicality, when you make a move, them guys are much stronger, much faster" — Derik Queen on the physical adjustment to the NBA** [02:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iGeYkWl0FAs&t=166s)

Derik Queen: "The most challenging thing was the physicality. Everybody knew I tore a ligament in my wrist, so I couldn't do arms, so I was four straight months of just all legs. But definitely the physicality, when you make a move, them guys are much stronger, much faster, so you have to be efficient in your movements. No wasted movements. Once I figured that out, I felt like I got used to it. I was always good at getting in people's bodies, but it's a different level now, so I had to work back on that."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iGeYkWl0FAs&t=166s

**4. Derik Queen — "my favorite player was LeBron, James Harden. Just watching them" — Derik Queen on his basketball inspirations** [06:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iGeYkWl0FAs&t=386s)

**5. Derik Queen — "James Harden. When I wanted to learn how to dribble, he was on the Rockets" — Derik Queen on his basketball influences** [14:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iGeYkWl0FAs&t=865s)

Derik Queen: "**James Harden**. When I wanted to learn how to dribble, he was on the Rockets doing his one-on-one stuff, and even OKC. I caught the later years of OKC, but when he was on the Rockets, he just used to do his one-on-one, and I was like, I want to do that. Because that, him and LeBron is my favorite player. LeBron, I'm not athletic like him. All three of us can pass and stuff, but those two was my favorite. So I just try to take pieces of their game. And then Jokic and Embiid, later on when I got to high school, I started watching them."

**6. Derik Queen — "Coach Boyle, he's one of the scouts at Orlando now, he's the assistant coach" — Derik Queen on his recruitment to Montverde Academy** [16:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iGeYkWl0FAs&t=996s)

Derik Queen: "Ninth grade, we was playing at this gym, it's fairly new called St. James. My mother was like, no, you're not going down there, because I'm the only child on my mom's side. So she was like, no, you're not going down there."

**8. Derik Queen — "I don't need the ball all the time to be effective in the game" — Derik Queen on his development and playstyle** [20:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iGeYkWl0FAs&t=1221s)

Derik Queen: "I really don't know. My mother and I, we always talk about it, and when people ask about it, she also had a lot of trust in me. She knew to come home when the streetlights came on, or always call probably every hour or something like that. But I just wanted to, I just knew that stuff just wasn't for me. And then when I always kept a basketball, I said this is something I want to do, and I just always, like, focus on it, and then nothing get in between it. I don't need the ball all the time to be effective in the game, but I do like get my teammates involved, make some plays, show a little bit of my game, but like me just needing the ball, like I wasn't necessarily the man, I just fell into that role in college and it all worked out."

**9. Derik Queen — "my mother was just really raised us all, like made sure we was all set" — Derik Queen on his relationship with his mother** [24:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iGeYkWl0FAs&t=1470s)
