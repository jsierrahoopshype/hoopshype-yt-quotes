## Peyton Watson on How He Elevated His Game & His Toughest High School Matchups — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J2oRCtJRzn0

_Speakers identified: Tracy McGrady, Peyton Watson, Vince Carter_

**1. Vince Carter — "watching the game is your cam to learn and be able to take bits and pieces" — Vince Carter on the importance of watching NBA games to learn** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J2oRCtJRzn0&t=0s)

**Vince Carter:** "I got two sons of my own and for so long I was trying to get these boys to watch the game because watching the game is your cam to learn and be able to take bits and pieces of whoever you identify yourself and take bits and pieces of their game and implement that into yours. Did you watch the game and when you were out there playing with your brothers were you trying to do some of those things?"

**Peyton Watson:** "Absolutely. I think that is the name of the game. You never can play exactly like somebody in this game, but you can take things from players' games and you can try to emulate things about players' games. Everybody has their own unique way that they approach the game, but for me it was about going out there watching Kobe shoot fadeaways, watching Kevin Durant come down and shoot hesi pull-ups, watching Tracy McGrady come down, score 13 points in 33 seconds. We are trying to recreate, we are timing ourselves, we are using a timer at home like can I even do this in practice? Can I score 13 points in 33 seconds?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J2oRCtJRzn0&t=0s

**2. Vince Carter — "I played JV my freshman and sophomore year. There were times in my high school basketball career" — Vince Carter on his high school basketball development and confidence** [01:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J2oRCtJRzn0&t=91s)

Vince Carter: "I played JV my freshman and sophomore year. There were times in my high school basketball career where I felt like I don't know if I'm really that nice. I feel that I'm nice, but when I see somebody else that I'm like damn, I got a lot of stuff I need to work on. This dude was polished. He had the feet, the balance on his jump shot. He could use his left hand, his right hand. He was so far advanced than a lot of other high school players I saw. Naturally, you'll be like damn, I got a long ways to go and it kind of mess with your mental and your confidence a little bit."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J2oRCtJRzn0&t=91s

**3. Peyton Watson — "I remember hitting that growth spurt around my sophomore summer and one day it was hard for me" — Peyton Watson on his high school growth and work ethic** [02:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J2oRCtJRzn0&t=148s)

Peyton Watson: "When I was a freshman and a sophomore, I didn't start for my high school team. I remember making varsity and you don't take that lightly at Long Beach Poly, a very storied school. I made the team, but I remember those first two years I was maybe 6-foot-6-foot-1 my freshman year and then my sophomore year I grew to like 6-foot-4. I was looking around and a lot of my contemporaries were bigger than me, stronger than me, they were already dunking. There was a difference in the coordination and how far along that they'd come. I remember feeling like damn, I feel regular out here. I'm used to being one of the best throughout middle school. I always played for a good team, so I'm like I feel regular out here, just your average Joe and it started to mess with me a little bit. The only remedy for that is hard work and everything else is kind of in God's hands. I remember hitting that growth spurt around my sophomore summer and one day it was hard for me to dunk and then the next day I woke up and I was doing 360s and windmills and I remember I was like yeah, it's about to start getting serious."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J2oRCtJRzn0&t=148s

**4. Vince Carter — "I took one picture where I jumped off the table for a dunk. But I said I'm" — Vince Carter on his first dunk and high school confidence** [03:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J2oRCtJRzn0&t=224s)

Vince Carter: "That was my mental training. I'll never forget that seventh grade. We were still junior high at that time. We had to take pictures for the gym. I took one picture where I jumped off the table for a dunk. But I said I'm going to do it for real. I'm talking not even sure if I could do it. I get on the break and I'm the only seventh grader, everybody else are now eighth, ninth grade. I go and I'm the youngest one and I'm like I'm going to try it. We already beating the team, I'm going to try it. I take off and dunk the ball and it was over. From there it went from a one-hand dunk to once I got to high school, that's when I was able to touch like the top of the backboard."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J2oRCtJRzn0&t=224s

Vince Carter: "I first probably like eighth grade is when I first started dunking, right? With the tennis ball, then I graduated from that and went to the soccer ball, then the basketball. My freshman year, I'm going to the whole I'm trying to dunk on everybody. That was the mentality. I didn't care about missing. I didn't care about missing because of my childhood and how I grew up. I did some crazy stuff, swimming in a lake with alligators, doing stuff like that. Jumping off of apartment roofs onto a mattress doing backflips. As a kid, we did all that. **7. Peyton Watson — "I would say Josh Christopher was one that was like in my area, that was somebody that" — Peyton Watson on Josh Christopher as a tough high school opponent** [08:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J2oRCtJRzn0&t=492s)

Peyton Watson: "When I was in high school, I would say **Josh Christopher** was one that was like in my area, that was somebody that was super tough. It gets to some guys where like when they've been playing since the freshman where they'd mastered the high school game and that's he was the epitome of that. He was physical, he was strong, he was shooting tough shots, crossing dudes over, dunking on people. He was doing everything and it was almost like he was a grown man out there in high school and he could control the game. We played against each other in high school and Josh actually pulled up to the game and watched. I remember I had 30 that game and he had like 30 and we was going bar for bar literally. Towards the end of the game, it was a show. They had another kid on their team named Dior Johnson and then my little brother Baldo as well. It was just us four, but we was going at it, Mayfair versus Poly and it was a super classic game, but I just remember after that game taking it away, I'm like Josh is one of them, he's a killer and he did it to everybody too."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J2oRCtJRzn0&t=492s
