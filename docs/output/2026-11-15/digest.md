## Peyton Watson on How He Elevated His Game & His Toughest High School Matchups — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J2oRCtJRzn0

**1. Vince Carter — "watching the game is your cam to learn and be able to take bits and pieces" — Vince Carter on the importance of watching NBA games to learn** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J2oRCtJRzn0&t=0s)

**Vince Carter:** "I got two sons of my own and for so long I was trying to get these boys to watch the game because watching the game is your cam to learn and be able to take bits and pieces of whoever you identify yourself and take bits and pieces of their game and implement that into yours. Did you watch the game and when you were out there playing with your brothers were you trying to do some of those things?"

**Peyton Watson:** "Absolutely. I think that is the name of the game. You never can play exactly like somebody in this game, but you can take things from players' games and you can try to emulate things about players' games. Everybody has their own unique way that they approach the game, but for me it was about going out there watching Kobe shoot fadeaways, watching Kevin Durant come down and shoot hesi pull-ups, watching Tracy McGrady come down, score 13 points in 33 seconds. We are trying to recreate, we are timing ourselves, we are using a timer at home like can I even do this in practice? Can I score 13 points in 33 seconds?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J2oRCtJRzn0&t=0s

Vince Carter: "I played JV my freshman and sophomore year. There were times in my high school basketball career where I felt like I don't know if I'm really that nice. I feel that I'm nice, but when I see somebody else that I'm like damn, I got a lot of stuff I need to work on. This dude was polished. He had the feet, the balance on his jump shot. He could use his left hand, his right hand. He was so far advanced than a lot of other high school players I saw. Naturally, you'll be like damn, I got a long ways to go and it kind of mess with your mental and your confidence a little bit."

Peyton Watson: "When I was a freshman and a sophomore, I didn't start for my high school team. I remember making varsity and you don't take that lightly at Long Beach Poly, a very storied school.
