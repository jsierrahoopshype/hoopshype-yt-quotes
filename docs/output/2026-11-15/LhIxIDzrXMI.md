# The Warriors Were Winning—Now Steph Curry Is Hurt | Chris Mannix & Grant Liffmann — *Sports Illustrated*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LhIxIDzrXMI

_Speakers identified: Chris Mannix, Grant Liffmann_

**1. Grant Liffmann — "This team has shown that even without Steph on the court, they compete." — Warriors' resilience and team dynamics without Steph Curry** [00:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LhIxIDzrXMI&t=57s)

Grant Liffmann: "It is devastating. **Steph Curry** was playing some of the best basketball of his entire career. This guy is 38 years old. They were playing so well, coaching has been on point, the players are responding. **Kevon Looney** has been great, **Kevon Looney** has been doing well, and they are doing this all despite not having two presumed stars, definitely one in **Jimmy Butler** and **Moses Moody** out as well. So, how about three stars? **Chris Paul** and **Kristaps Porzingis** does not exist for that team either. So, they were doing all this, all those vibes are going, and then this happens. This team has shown that even without Steph on the court, they compete. They execute and they play hard."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LhIxIDzrXMI&t=57s

**2. Grant Liffmann — "I actually think they can stay afloat during all this." — Warriors' potential to stay afloat during Steph Curry's absence** [02:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LhIxIDzrXMI&t=163s)

Grant Liffmann: "If they go five and nine and are 500 by the time Steph comes back, you are probably in the play-in world at the bottom of the play-in world at that point, and then you try to make a run at it and **Jimmy Butler** and **Moses Moody** come back after that. So, while it is devastating, I actually think they can stay afloat during all this because at the end of the day, I am a firm believer, if you execute and compete, especially on the defensive end, and give everything you got in a regular season game, you have a good shot of winning."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LhIxIDzrXMI&t=163s

**3. Chris Mannix — "They are really going to have to lean into that defense." — Warriors must rely on defense and Draymond Green's leadership** [04:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LhIxIDzrXMI&t=250s)

Chris Mannix: "They are going to have to radically change how they play and how they try to win. During this eight-game winning streak they had the best defensive rating in the NBA. They are really going to have to lean into that defense because not only is Steph gone but **Brandon Williams** is gone as well. So, their backcourt has been decimated by short-term injury. They are really going to have to lean on **Draymond Green**, who has been outstanding to start the season on that end of the floor. He is going to have to really be a catalyst for this team's success over the next month."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LhIxIDzrXMI&t=250s

**4. Grant Liffmann — "Draymond Green is an all-time guy. He can dictate how a defense moves." — Draymond Green's defensive impact and consistency concerns** [05:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LhIxIDzrXMI&t=313s)

Grant Liffmann: "**Draymond Green** is an all-time guy. He is not just, you know, we talked about the gravity that **Steph Curry** brings offensively, the anti-gravity that **Draymond Green** brings on defense. He is just played free safety, can do so many things, he can dictate how a defense moves. Is he the same defender he was in the past? No, and he cannot bring it every single night and that concerns me a little bit as you go through this because he has to bring it every single night, especially with Steph out."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LhIxIDzrXMI&t=313s

Cryer** is going to be doing, he is going to play a lot because he is two-way, he is one of your guards on the backup. But maybe instead of putting **Kevon Looney** purely as the point and moving forward, you have like a Melton, Looney and you move it. And that is the solid defensive backcourt right there with Horford and Draymond cleaning up the front court and you have Looney who is large."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LhIxIDzrXMI&t=354s

**6. Chris Mannix — "The trade season usually doesn't even open yet." — Trade market timing and Warriors' patience with roster moves** [08:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LhIxIDzrXMI&t=506s)

Chris Mannix: "The trade season usually does not even open yet. **Nick Clifford** was traded, but that was a very unique situation and with the draft lottery changing, it is not like teams just sell right now. So, you are giving away a player that is not playing. So, whoever is taking him, you are probably giving out a player back to the Warriors that is not playing much for you either. You are not taking a starter right now and be like, hey, here you go, we will take a guy who is not playing basketball."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LhIxIDzrXMI&t=506s

**7. Grant Liffmann — "I highly doubt some team is going to be like, hey, you know what, just take my center right now." — Warriors' urgency and potential trade targets** [09:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LhIxIDzrXMI&t=547s)

Grant Liffmann: "The urgency to me, maybe December 15th when that comes around, you can do some smaller moves around the edges. But I highly doubt some team is going to be like, hey, you know what, just take my center right now, unless it is like Milwaukee being like, please take **Miles Turner**. So, in this case, I would say it does not change that much. I think the Warriors are really trying to see if they can use the **Kristaps Porzingis** contract to say, this is the final piece to make a run at it this season."
