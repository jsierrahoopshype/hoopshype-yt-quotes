# Tracy McGrady & Vince Carter Reveal What the NBA Draft Was Really Like | Cousins — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=enk9bYl6MFQ

_Speakers identified: Tracy McGrady, Vince Carter_

**1. Tracy McGrady — "I didn't work out for 20 teams. I visited and talked to about 20 teams." — Tracy McGrady describes his extensive pre-draft workout process** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=enk9bYl6MFQ&t=0s)

Tracy McGrady: "Straight out of high school, we had probably about 20 teams lined up. I remember traveling. I was all over the country. I didn't work out for 20 teams. I visited and talked to about 20 teams. I probably worked out for 17 teams. Some of the teams that I went and worked out for, my back flared up so bad from all the traveling. That's when I discovered that I had a slight case of scoliosis in my back. It was a couple of workouts where I felt like, damn, this is tough. Two of my workouts, **John Calipari** in New Jersey and in Boston with **Rick Pitino**, were the toughest workouts. I couldn't make a shot working out for New Jersey. John had me, one of the toughest, full-court 1-on-1. Everything was like full-court and shooting and I didn't have the legs. I was missing every shot."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=enk9bYl6MFQ&t=0s

**2. Tracy McGrady — "They want to see if you were really in shape." — Tracy McGrady on the true purpose of pre-draft workouts** [01:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=enk9bYl6MFQ&t=101s)

Tracy McGrady: "It wasn't really so much about can you withstand this tough workout, but how are you going to react to it? What is your reaction after we work out? They were trying to see your body language and how you react to things like that. If you could get through it, and there were some guys that couldn't get through it. They want to see if you were really in shape. It was about can you withstand this tough workout, but how are you going to react to it?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=enk9bYl6MFQ&t=101s

**3. Tracy McGrady — "The night before the draft was one of the craziest nights for me." — Tracy McGrady details his pre-draft workout tour** [02:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=enk9bYl6MFQ&t=152s)

Tracy McGrady: "Boston, New Jersey, I went to Toronto, of course. I went to Golden State, Lakers. Worked out for the Lakers. That was a good workout with **Jerry West**. The Bulls, I didn't work out, but they were trying to trade draft night. That was a crazy night for me. The night before the draft was one of the craziest nights for me, based off of where I came from, where I'm at, and what could potentially happen at this moment. I had to go and take a physical the night before. Jerry Krause called my agent and told him, hey, we got to get a physical. So they called a clinic and they snuck me in. It was like midnight. I went in there, did a physical. The next day, draft night, I think it was **Jerry Reinsdorf** and MJ, nixing that trade, because I think that's the time, if you watch The Last Dance, they were talking about trading **Scotty Pippen**. I was a part of that. I was going to be a part of that. That was 1997. That's why I had to go to the clinic and get a physical because Jerry Krause was trying to get rid of Pip. But **Jerry Reinsdorf** was like, nah, we going for one more. And they won that next championship."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=enk9bYl6MFQ&t=152s

**4. Vince Carter — "My year, the Clippers had the number one pick, and then it was the Clippers and then Vancouver." — Vince Carter recalls his draft experience with the Clippers** [05:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=enk9bYl6MFQ&t=317s)

Vince Carter: "My year, the Clippers had the number one pick, and then it was the Clippers and then Vancouver. **Michael Olowokandi** was number one pick, **Mike Bibby** was number two, **Ray LaFrentz** was number three. Antoine was four, and I was five. So, I worked out for, I didn't work out the Clippers, they weren't interested in working out because they were either going to get Bibby or Olowokandi. So I worked out for Vancouver the night before the draft. **Brian Hill** and those brought me in just in case. And then I worked out the Denver three, Toronto four, Golden State five, Dallas, Sacramento. They cut the workout short in Sacramento. They said, yeah, if you make it down here, we're drafting you."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=enk9bYl6MFQ&t=317s

**5. Vince Carter — "You put so much pressure, especially as a young guy, trying to be perfect and make shots." — Vince Carter on the pressure of pre-draft workouts** [06:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=enk9bYl6MFQ&t=376s)

Vince Carter: "You put so much pressure, especially as a young guy, trying to be perfect and make shots. Everything you did wrong, you look over like, ah, no, I really can't handle the ball. I really can't shoot the ball. And for them to say, we've seen enough, we're going to draft you. And I thought Denver, the number three, I thought I had a great workout in Denver where I felt like I could possibly be the number three pick. Golden State, I had a pretty good workout in Sacramento. But Sacramento, I got busy in Sacramento."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=enk9bYl6MFQ&t=376s

**6. Tracy McGrady — "I didn't even work out for Toronto. I visited, talked to Isiah Thomas." — Tracy McGrady on his visit to Toronto** [06:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=enk9bYl6MFQ&t=411s)

Tracy McGrady: "I didn't even work out for Toronto. I visited, talked to **Isiah Thomas** and the same words that you just said, he told me. If you're available around, you know it's time for us to draft, you're coming to Toronto. I didn't work out. I just visited. My back was flared up and we had a good conversation."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=enk9bYl6MFQ&t=411s

**7. Vince Carter — "They'd already seen us. They just wanted to get you in the gym." — Vince Carter on the reality of pre-draft evaluations** [07:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=enk9bYl6MFQ&t=433s)

Vince Carter: "They'd already seen us. They just wanted to get you in the gym doing some of the movement plays and things that they do in their facility, wanted to see you doing that. Other than that, they already knew. And maybe to even see how much better you've gotten since the last time they saw you. So that means, oh, we got one, he's a worker, he puts the work in."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=enk9bYl6MFQ&t=433s

**8. Tracy McGrady — "My purpose was to get my mom and my grandmother out of the hood." — Tracy McGrady on his motivation for going pro** [09:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=enk9bYl6MFQ&t=582s)

Tracy McGrady: "My purpose was to get my mom and my grandmother out of the hood. My grandmother was a custodian at my elementary school and my mom was working at a housekeeper at Disney. So for me it was like, that was priority, that was the purpose of going to the league. But then when you look at KG '95, Kobe '96, looking at them, that was inspiration. If they could do it, hell, I could do it."
