# Can you compare Wemby to Michael Jordan & Kobe?! Sean Elliott says YES! 👀 | The Rich Eisen Show — *NBA on ESPN*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Wtyqvus8Hw

_Speakers identified: Rich Eisen, Sean Elliott, Victor Wembanyama_

**1. Sean Elliott — "Victor Wembanyama who is the alien, he has arrived" — Victor Wembanyama is an alien who has arrived in San Antonio** [00:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Wtyqvus8Hw&t=33s)

Sean Elliott: "It is fitting because it is also about an alien movie and first contact. **Victor Wembanyama** who is the alien, he has arrived. I thought going into this year, I thought we were a mid-50 win team. We did not have Victor for the last 18 games last year. We missed De'Aaron Fox for the last 16 or so, he had finger surgery. Halfway through the season, when I saw other teams go through or when we were winning games on the road convincingly, I thought, we have a chance. I have not really seen a team that is really better than us. Matter of fact, we are better than almost everybody out there."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Wtyqvus8Hw&t=33s

**2. Sean Elliott — "Gregg Popovich has done an unbelievable job. I said it over and over again, he was the coach" — Gregg Popovich deserves credit for the Spurs' successful season** [02:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Wtyqvus8Hw&t=126s)

Sean Elliott: "**Gregg Popovich** has done an unbelievable job. I said it over and over again, he was the coach of the year. We were ESPN projected to win 40 games, we won 62. He did a phenomenal job getting everybody to believe in each other, getting everybody to play on the defensive end of the floor. The hardest thing in this league to do is to get young teams to defend. Everybody is trying to get their numbers, no one is trying to stop anybody. Our guys went out there, accepted their roles, and they defended their butts off the entire year."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Wtyqvus8Hw&t=126s

**3. Sean Elliott — "Dylan Harper, I have been telling people, he is a superstar. He is an absolute superstar" — Dylan Harper is a superstar who plays with incredible poise** [03:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Wtyqvus8Hw&t=190s)

Sean Elliott: "**Dylan Harper**, I have been telling people, he is a superstar. He is an absolute superstar. No doubt about it. He plays well beyond his years. He plays with a lot of poise. He never ever rattled even when things are not going his way. After the second half of the season, he was probably our best three-point shooter and that includes having a guy like **Julian Champagnie** on our team. He defends, he is bigger than most people know, and he finishes extremely well at the basket."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Wtyqvus8Hw&t=190s

**4. Sean Elliott — "He has got Kobe Bryant's drive and determination, motor, heart, killer mentality and a 7'4 plus frame" — Wembanyama has the drive and mentality of Kobe and Jordan** [05:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Wtyqvus8Hw&t=341s)

Sean Elliott: "He has got **Kobe Bryant**'s drive and determination, motor, heart, killer mentality and a 7'4 plus frame. That is what he has got. He has got that guard, that little man motor, that heart like **Avery Johnson** has, where he just wants to get better and better and better. He has got that **Kobe Bryant** drive where he wants to be the very best player in the game and the best player that has ever played. He is always in the gym. He is always working on his game. He has got incredible competitiveness, and that is something that you just do not see very often in bigs. I tell people all the time, he has got Kobe's makeup in a 7'4 plus frame."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Wtyqvus8Hw&t=341s

**5. Sean Elliott — "Before Victor got drafted, he was really on the verge of retirement" — Gregg Popovich was on the verge of retirement before drafting Wembanyama** [09:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Wtyqvus8Hw&t=566s)

Sean Elliott: "Before Victor got drafted, he was really on the verge of retirement. He was looking into the abyss of retirement and that is where he was going. And when we won the lottery that year, there were a whole bunch of players, I was not the only one, a whole bunch of former players that told Pop, he has got to stick around because he has got to give this young kid a great foundation in this league."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Wtyqvus8Hw&t=566s

**6. Sean Elliott — "The Knicks gave us a lot of problems this year. They beat us twice" — The Knicks are a difficult, physical matchup for the Spurs** [12:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Wtyqvus8Hw&t=730s)

Sean Elliott: "The Knicks gave us a lot of problems this year. They beat us twice, beat us in the cup game where there was a lot of skin on the line for everybody involved. **Jalen Brunson** obviously, you do not have to talk too much about him and what he has meant to that franchise and everything he has done. I think he is, like Pop said, he is the head of the snake. You have got to definitely keep him under control, number one. And that means Steph Castle or Devin Vassell, Dylan Harper, or De'Aaron Fox, when those guys are tasked with guarding him, you have got to keep him under control, do not allow him to get to the foul line, make him make tough shots, make him work for absolutely everything and try to limit him getting everybody else involved in the game."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Wtyqvus8Hw&t=730s
