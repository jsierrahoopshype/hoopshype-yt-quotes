# When Will Bam Adebayo Make the All-NBA Leap? — *Road Trippin*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h0wdi26VNqs

_Speakers identified: Allie Clifton, Richard Jefferson, Channing Frye, Kendrick Perkins_

**1. Kendrick Perkins — "At what point is he going to take a leap of, we go look up and Bam consistently could get you 25 or 30?" — Kendrick Perkins questions Bam Adebayo's offensive ceiling and scoring consistency** [00:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h0wdi26VNqs&t=3s)

Kendrick Perkins: "When is Bam going to take that leap from being an All-Star caliber player to an All-NBA caliber player? We can list a whole lot of organizations that have done right by their players because they earned that. Bam has never had to go through a contract negotiation that was a standoff. They always paid him max money on time. Bam has never been in trade rumors. The only reason I am bringing this up is because they drafted him, they trust in him, they have invested in him. **Pat Riley** is 80 plus years old. **Pat Riley** went on the market to get a whale to come and be in the position of where they have been in the last four or five years, playing team, playing team, playing team. It was a point when Bam and **Jimmy Butler** were making it to those finals, there were conversations being had that said, hey, is it time to put Bam on the same level as an **Anthony Davis** or a **Giannis Antetokounmpo**? And we were like, yeah, Bam has got the ceiling, he is going to be there. He has never averaged over 20 points in his career. At what point is he going to take a leap of, we go look up and Bam consistently could get you 25 or 30?"

**2. Channing Frye — "He is not that guy. He has never shown you that." — Channing Frye argues Bam Adebayo is not a high-volume scorer** [00:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h0wdi26VNqs&t=3s)

Channing Frye: "He is not that guy. He has never shown you that. He did it once. He had 80 once, okay, give him that. Now, think about this, the Miami Heat is based on a system of how to play ball, right? We love that **Spo** gets the most out of his lesser known, lesser talented players. Now, they play pass, cut, screen. They usually play slower than everyone else. So now, think about this. Listen, Perk, they are not known to get out and run. So here is the second thing. Giannis is one of the four most talented players that have ever put on a Miami Heat jersey. That system or whatever system that Bam is used to, that **Davion Mitchell** is used to, is out the window."
