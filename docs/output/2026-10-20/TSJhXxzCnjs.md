# LL Cool J Says People Have His Relationship With Jay-Z ALL WRONG — *7PM in Brooklyn*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TSJhXxzCnjs

_Speakers identified: Kazeem Famuyide, Carmelo Anthony, LL Cool J_

**1. LL Cool J — "I can't even say something out of my mouth without people thinking it means something else." — LL Cool J on his relationship and public perception with Jay-Z** [00:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TSJhXxzCnjs&t=35s)

**Kazeem Famuyide:** "Obviously, I think your relationship with **Jay-Z** over the years is one of the most fascinating things because from the Def Jam link to the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame, sauce money battles, all that type of stuff."

**LL Cool J:** "We are cool when we see each other, very cool. But it is like the comments right now. Unfortunately, the way it has been framed out in the world, I cannot even say something out of my mouth without people thinking it means something else. That is just the logic and reason. I do not want to comment because I have love for him and we are very cool, but somehow it is going to get turned into something that is not."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TSJhXxzCnjs&t=35s

**2. LL Cool J — "Rick likes to strip away everything that isn't essential to the music." — LL Cool J on Rick Rubin's production style and influence** [04:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TSJhXxzCnjs&t=275s)

LL Cool J: "Rick produced my first record and it was his first album. I met him when he was a college student at NYU. He was at Five University place in the dorms. I sent my demo in, Rick from the Beastie Boys heard my demo. This is before the Beastie Boys were on a label. My first album, it doesn't say produced by **Rick Rubin**, it says reduced by **Rick Rubin**. Rick likes to strip away everything that isn't essential to the music so that the essence of the artist and the music really stand out. We would do little things like turn the vocals down a little bit lower in the mix so that people would have to turn it up to hear it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TSJhXxzCnjs&t=275s

**3. LL Cool J — "I produced everything from me to Run-DMC to the Beastie Boys." — LL Cool J on his early production work with Run-DMC and the Beastie Boys** [05:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TSJhXxzCnjs&t=357s)

LL Cool J: "For those of you who don't know, he produced everything from me to **Run-DMC** to the Beastie Boys to all of these groups back then. Right after, on the shoulders of **Grandmaster Flash** and the **Treacherous Three** and **Afrika Bambaataa** and Zulu Nation, which was a huge movement, it was around the world but very niche. Myself and **Run-DMC** took the game to the whole world."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TSJhXxzCnjs&t=357s

**4. LL Cool J — "He doesn't play an instrument, he doesn't really program any machines, he just has great taste." — LL Cool J on Rick Rubin's unique ear for music production** [06:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TSJhXxzCnjs&t=401s)

LL Cool J: "Rick is one of the first super producers in hip hop. He doesn't play an instrument, he doesn't really program any machines, he just has great taste. And one thing about the arts, you got to have taste. The kind of taste that is appropriate to the subject matter or the subject that you are dealing with. So Rick can sit with **Johnny Cash** and go get a **Trent Reznor** record and have him redo it, and then he can get with **Run-DMC** and have them do an **Aerosmith** record and redo it. He has taste."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TSJhXxzCnjs&t=401s

**5. LL Cool J — "The most important thing to me when I make a song is how the song makes you feel." — LL Cool J on prioritizing emotional connection in music** [09:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TSJhXxzCnjs&t=582s)

LL Cool J: "One of the things I always focused on, the most important thing to me when I make a song is how the song makes you feel. Not the lyrics, not the music, how does it make you feel? When is the last time you listened to a **James Brown** record and analyzed the lyrics really deeply? It is incredible. When you hear the music, you just listen to the joy of the sound. I want to feel something. When I listen to Marvin Gaye, I feel something. When I listen to different groups, I feel something."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TSJhXxzCnjs&t=582s
