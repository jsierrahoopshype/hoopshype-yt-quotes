Raymond Felton: "I always said that the game is like a every three or four year window. And every team is different on how every NBA year, it starts to become different on how people win championships. Like when I first got to the league, you needed two big men to win a championship. Then it went to you had to have a really good point guard and a big man. Then it went to you had to have a dynamic wing guy. Then it went to you had to have hybrid fours and being able to like have those hybrid fours be the point guard as a defacto, just like **Draymond Green** and win a championship.
