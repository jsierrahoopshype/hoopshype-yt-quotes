# Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady On Their Hall Of Fame Speeches, Emotions & Honoring Their Families — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bv65iZq6kU0

_Speakers identified: Vince Carter, Tracy McGrady_

**1. Vince Carter — "You have to have someone who has been in the Hall of Fame to represent you." — Vince Carter on the importance of having Hall of Famers represent inductees** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bv65iZq6kU0&t=0s)

Vince Carter: "I'm always interested to see the guys that are up there representing the Hall of Fame guys going in. It was a no-brainer for you and **Tracy McGrady**. You were one of the first people there to guide me through what the NBA was like. But then you watch what **Tracy McGrady** had. I remember reading some people like, we need to explain this to people because I don't think everybody understands this. Everybody was talking about how come **Rondo** wasn't up there. I don't think everybody understands. You have to have someone who has been in the Hall of Fame to represent you."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bv65iZq6kU0&t=0s

**2. Tracy McGrady — "This is a time where you are reflecting on your career but you also giving the appreciation." — Tracy McGrady on the emotional weight of reflecting on his career during his speech** [01:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bv65iZq6kU0&t=106s)

Tracy McGrady: "This is a time where you are reflecting on your career but you also giving the appreciation for the people that was along the journey with you. That meant so much to that journey. You are giving that appreciation and then when you actually are delivering your speech, those moments hit you when you bring them up and you are talking about them. Those certain moments hit you and it takes you back to that time and you get those feelings again."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bv65iZq6kU0&t=106s

**3. Vince Carter — "I started to write the stuff down and it was just a piece of paper that had names." — Vince Carter on his meticulous preparation process for his Hall of Fame speech** [03:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bv65iZq6kU0&t=193s)

Vince Carter: "My process for this was the obvious. I started to write the stuff down and it was just a piece of paper that had names. That's how I wanted to go for it because I already knew I had the names for it. My biggest thing was I'm going to say something about everybody, but I don't want to forget somebody. I think I wrote mine 13 times."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bv65iZq6kU0&t=193s

**4. Tracy McGrady — "I started working on that speech a year out because I wanted to make sure." — Tracy McGrady on the anxiety of public speaking and preparing his speech a year in advance** [03:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bv65iZq6kU0&t=205s)

Tracy McGrady: "When I got the call that I was getting inducted into the Hall of Fame, one thing I don't like and athletes don't like is public speaking. I got to stand up there and give a speech, bro. I'm already nervous. I started working on that speech a year out because I wanted to make sure when I stand up there, deliver this speech, that regardless of the emotions that comes over me, I still can stay firm and deliver what I got to because I don't have to look at the prompter. I know what I want to say and how I want to say it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bv65iZq6kU0&t=205s

**5. Vince Carter — "I happened to look up top left and I saw her there taking a picture." — Vince Carter on spotting his grandmother in the audience during his speech** [06:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bv65iZq6kU0&t=366s)

Vince Carter: "What happened is I'm talking, looking in the room and I happened to look up top left and I saw her there taking a picture. I just knew she wasn't in it, but had every intentions and I was like, you know, luckily but like you said, preparation and I mean I went over it and for me it was placement as far as people. I knew I wanted to do the teams so I did the teams in order and then I wanted to add people so it all worked out to where the timeline worked with some of the people."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bv65iZq6kU0&t=366s

**6. Tracy McGrady — "I've seen people just like completely go off script and can't get it back." — Tracy McGrady on the difficulty of maintaining focus while delivering a speech** [07:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bv65iZq6kU0&t=451s)

Tracy McGrady: "I was watching your speech and sometimes you were, because you know we got the prompter, we can see everything that you're saying that obviously people in the stands can't see, but I was watching you and sometimes you were going off script. I've seen people just like completely go off script and can't get it back. Can't get it back and just go to mumbling on with their speech. So you were good at going off script but then, you know, bringing it back."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bv65iZq6kU0&t=451s

**7. Tracy McGrady — "I think when you go into the Hall of Fame it's such a prestigious event." — Tracy McGrady on the prestige of the Hall of Fame and the challenge of fan interactions** [09:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bv65iZq6kU0&t=565s)

Tracy McGrady: "I think when you go into the Hall of Fame it's such a prestigious event to where when I walk in, I want to greet and have conversations with the Hall of Famers, former Hall of Famers, the guys that are getting inducted. I want to have that moment with them exclusively. But when you're there there is so many fans and people, and I don't even know if they're real fans or they just are fans to get stuff signed, memorabilia. You're walking around with the balls, cars, and jerseys and all that stuff. To me that's not the time for that."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bv65iZq6kU0&t=565s

**8. Vince Carter — "For me that was one of the more challenging, dare I say frustrating times." — Vince Carter on the challenges of the Hall of Fame meet-and-greet environment** [10:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bv65iZq6kU0&t=617s)

Vince Carter: "For me that was one of the more challenging, dare I say frustrating times because we're doing the, I don't know, it felt like a meet-and-greet more so than whatever else it could be. But like there were obviously a couple of rooms there and it's people everywhere where the pre-show before the big show and you're walking around and I feel like that day should be the day of celebration, but knowing how you were just talking about how you felt the nerves, making sure you get your format, your speech right, not forgetting people, a lot of that stuff played for me all day."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bv65iZq6kU0&t=617s
