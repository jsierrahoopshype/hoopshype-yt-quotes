# Jadakiss on the Knicks Championship, Giannis to Miami & Hip-Hop's NBA Influence | Cousins — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g0I4a26fJko

_Speakers identified: Vince Carter, Tracy McGrady, Jadakiss_

**1. Jadakiss — "This year was the year for us. As far as the fandom and the people, we didn't believe." — Jadakiss on the Knicks' 53-year championship drought and recent playoff run** [03:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g0I4a26fJko&t=212s)

Jadakiss: "This year was the year for us. As far as the fandom and the people, we didn't believe last year as much as we loved it. We didn't have the fluidity to win it. But this year, and you guys know from playing in the league, you always need a little bit of help. Besides how good your team is, the coach, the training staff, the heavens got to align and you need a little bit of help. So with all them guys being injured and Hartenstein being out, Tatum being out, that helped us and then we had a strong team and it was this year, not or never, but this year or the next few years to come, but we needed this year. This was the year."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g0I4a26fJko&t=212s

**2. Vince Carter — "Mike Brown made the right play, put the ball in Jalen Brunson's hands and everything changed." — Vince Carter and Tracy McGrady on Mike Brown's tactical adjustment for the Knicks** [08:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g0I4a26fJko&t=491s)

**Vince Carter:** "I think that's why the Spurs lost. It's situational basketball. You got to put your big boy pants on. Sometimes you're going to get benched. We've had moments when you've gotten benched at the height of our careers, but at that moment, that was the right play. **Mike Brown** made the right play, put the ball in **Jalen Brunson**'s hands and everything changed. Changed the offense a little bit where now everybody gets a chance to eat and it makes them harder to guard. And I think it made San Antonio easier to guard because of that same situation. **De'Aaron Fox** not making shots."

**Tracy McGrady:** "I wonder, because you know, that was my concern when they hired **Mike Brown**, is because I know he was going to change the offense. And my concern was, were those guys going to buy in? And it was a struggle. It was a struggle in the beginning of the season because **Jalen Brunson** was used to having the ball in his hands and all the other guys were just standing around, you know, catching a grenade."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g0I4a26fJko&t=491s

**5. Jadakiss — "I think Big is responsible, as much as Diddy is responsible, because Diddy is the one who came." — Jadakiss on the impact of The Notorious B.I.G. on his career** [13:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g0I4a26fJko&t=805s)

Jadakiss: "I think Big is responsible, as much as **Diddy** is responsible, because **Diddy** is the one who came and got us and brought us the Ruff Ryders and said, he's the other dudes that's nice from my city. But after getting on and signing with Bad Boy and having a relationship with Big, I think he put the battery in our back to take on this industry and have a career for ourselves when he told us we was, he actually told us verbatim, 'I'm glad y'all down with me.' He made us know that we was nice before we actually really believed in ourselves. He saw something in us that we didn't see and we, that's a timeless, priceless moment to be there with him recording Life After Death double CD."
