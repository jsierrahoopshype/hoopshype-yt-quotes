**7. Ryan Smith — "I don't even call it a workout. I call it dinner." — Ryan Smith on the reality of NBA pre-draft workouts** [09:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TyJoMKt_0Jg&t=560s)

Ryan Smith: "We met with Darryn at the combine. Darryn was awesome in the interview. Darryn wanted to go number one. Everyone wants to go number one. But by the way, we had three different top guys come in. I don't even call it a workout. I call it dinner. I don't even go. I'm like, okay, I'll go to dinner with you because there's no sweat, there's no nothing. They're sitting down with Will watching film. They put on their jersey to check out the size and everything. They take a couple of pictures, they call it a workout, but no one's doing like the three-minute run to see how fast they can go up and down the court."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TyJoMKt_0Jg&t=560s
