**1. Leon Rose — "we were all about changing the culture and getting guys that fit within a winning culture." — Leon Rose on the cultural shift and acquiring Jalen Brunson** [00:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_jqaIxoB3Ko&t=35s)

Leon Rose: "When you look back on it, you think about it. Fortunately, it all turned out the way it turned out. There are always ups and downs and decisions. You are not going to make every decision, you are not going to get every decision right. But we were all about changing the culture and getting guys that fit within a winning culture. The guy to my left, we brought him in, and he had shown what he could do in the final series when Luka went down. I truly believe that he could come into New York, and I knew New York wouldn't phase him because that's a huge piece of all the decisions you make with regard to players: can they handle New York, can they handle the big stage, can they handle the moment?"

**2. Leon Rose — "we had to go through some trial and error, but the best pieces that would fit with Jalen." — Leon Rose on the trial and error of building the roster** [01:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_jqaIxoB3Ko&t=97s)

Leon Rose: "In getting him and being able to figure out the way to bring in the pieces that were going to best fit with Jalen in order to try to take each step. We had some people there already, we had to go through some trial and error, but the best pieces that would fit with Jalen in order to try to take each step. Josh was a huge part of that also. The chemistry just means so much. The way these guys are on the court, it's telepathic. Bringing him in, Cat was another guy that fit great with the group, and OG, who we had brought in just the year before, was another guy that we thought was a two-way player that was going to fit with the group, and then Mikal was the last piece."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_jqaIxoB3Ko&t=97s

**3. Leon Rose — "behind the scenes it was very on the low. It was basically me and their general manager." — Leon Rose on the secrecy and difficulty of the trade process** [04:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_jqaIxoB3Ko&t=288s)

Leon Rose: "Behind the scenes it was very on the low. It was basically me and their general manager handling it. It was right around if I'm not mistaken, between Christmas and New Year's. There are certain people you have to talk to when you get within that 24-hour period, but the agents had to do that. To me, there's no benefit in things being out there. I've always just operated like that. You don't want to affect the guys on the team either, because there's talk all the time. There's behind-the-scenes talk about players and who you like, but it really doesn't mean anything unless it gets somewhere."
