**1. Tyrese Haliburton — "I knew when I resigned my deal here that was something I wanted to bring back." — Tyrese Haliburton on bringing back the Pacers Foundation softball game** [00:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KfK6bYiWj0Q&t=41s)

Tyrese Haliburton: "Today we are bringing back the Pacers Foundation softball game. Over a decade since they did their last game, so it is something that I knew when I resigned my deal here that was something I wanted to bring back. We had a conversation after last season about bringing it back, but obviously I got hurt, so we pushed it to this year. Really excited, we got obviously a lot of us Pacer guys are doing it, a lot of the Colt guys are doing it, which is fun because their season is right around the corner."

**2. TJ McConnell — "I need you to assign me where I am playing so I can get ready today mentally." — TJ McConnell and Tyrese Haliburton debate playing positions for softball** [01:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KfK6bYiWj0Q&t=70s)

**TJ McConnell:** "I need you to assign me where I am playing so I can get ready today mentally."

**Tyrese Haliburton:** "Wherever you want."

**TJ McConnell:** "No, I need to come on. TJ will be catcher."

**Tyrese Haliburton:** "Not an inning. I am not playing catcher. Looking at our team, I feel like you might be one of our better athletes, so I think you need to go to shortstop though."

**TJ McConnell:** "You said that like it was an educated guess. I am one of our better athletes. No, first base seems like a **Tyler Warren** thing, just catch everything and be tall."

**3. Tyrese Haliburton — "It is just important to give back to where I am." — Tyrese Haliburton on his commitment to the Indianapolis community** [03:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KfK6bYiWj0Q&t=234s)

Tyrese Haliburton: "Ever since I have been here, I have become ingrained in Indiana. Over the last couple weeks there has been a lot of flooding here, a lot of people losing homes and stuff like that, and then obviously throughout our state with what is going on in Gary. Most of my family lives here, I live here in the offseasons, this is home for me. So it is just important to give back to where I am, and that has just been something that has been taught to me my whole life."
