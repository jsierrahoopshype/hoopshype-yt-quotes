# Could Duren's Contract Drama Spill Onto the Court? | Chris Mannix & Grant Liffmann — *Sports Illustrated*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yyIPYC0pz3I

_Speakers identified: Chris Mannix, Grant Liffmann_

**1. Chris Mannix — "When you have an opportunity to guarantee yourself $200 million, I don't care about the possibility" — Jalen Duren's decision to sign a five-year, $200 million contract extension** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yyIPYC0pz3I&t=0s)

Chris Mannix: "The big topic yesterday was that the standoff between the Detroit Pistons and **Jalen Duren** has come to an end. It was a game of chicken, and **Jalen Duren** blinked. The Pistons had their offer on the table, five years, $200 million. I was told this offer was out there for a while. **Jalen Duren** wanted more, **Jalen Duren** wanted to be involved in a sign-and-trade. I think a big part of **Jalen Duren** was ready to be done with the Detroit Pistons. When you have an opportunity to guarantee yourself $200 million, I don't care about the possibility that 12 months from now, you can recoup that money and you can sign with whatever team you want. That's a possibility. You could also blow out your ACL. You could also continue to have a decline, like **Jalen Duren** showed somewhat in the postseason."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yyIPYC0pz3I&t=0s

**2. Grant Liffmann — "The nightmare of all front offices is when things get publicized like this because the minute becomes public" — The negative impact of public contract negotiations on team chemistry** [02:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yyIPYC0pz3I&t=121s)

Grant Liffmann: "A lot of this, the nightmare of all front offices is when things get publicized like this because the minute becomes public and then it becomes toxic out there. For players, for agents, for all of this, there's nothing worse than when it gets out in the public because then fans jump in and now anything that happens from here on out, the team loses, if Duren struggles, it's now into this huge thing where everyone's going to have an opinion about it. So that's the worst thing that could have happened in this situation."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yyIPYC0pz3I&t=121s

**3. Chris Mannix — "What Trajan Langdon and the Pistons have said is that Duren has a weight problem, a conditioning problem" — Concerns over Jalen Duren's conditioning and weight issues impacting his performance** [05:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yyIPYC0pz3I&t=301s)

Chris Mannix: "The push to have a weight clause in the contract has the potential to linger even if there is no weight clause in the contract. What **Trajan Langdon** and the Pistons have said is that Duren has a weight problem, a conditioning problem. They have put that spotlight on him, and people are going to be monitoring that all throughout the season. If he has a bad week, it's no longer just a bad week for basketball reasons, maybe it's a bad week for conditioning reasons. And I don't think that the Pistons did themselves any favors by Trajan specifically, who I think has done a good job generally, by referencing Zion. He's the one that put Zion into the universe there saying, I worked in New Orleans with a guy that went through contract stuff there. But that's a nod and a wink to like, Zion was fat, so we made him put a weight clause in that contract."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yyIPYC0pz3I&t=301s

**4. Grant Liffmann — "The East was not very good last year. They upgraded, every team upgraded across the board" — The Detroit Pistons' roster upgrades and the challenge of the Eastern Conference** [06:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yyIPYC0pz3I&t=405s)

But you're banking on health again of Cade playing a full season and being good because he's the only person that's a real generator."
