Jerry Stackhouse: "It coincided with my own personal family, my kids. They're the same age as a lot of the kids that I started to coach when I started coaching in college at Vanderbilt. My son just got out of college, so I know the challenges and the things that this generation deals with. Generation Z is a little different than my generation, but it's not in a bad way. They're so smart. They come from a generation that we were always on the phone, we were pushing buttons, and now they're swiping. It's a different mentality and they want to be included and they want to feel a part of it, but they still want to be coached."

**2. Jerry Stackhouse — "my dad had a great saying, said, 'Beggars can't be choosy.' So for me, it's just getting the opportunity." — Jerry Stackhouse on his approach to head coaching opportunities** [02:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=djgSKUP-2PM&t=170s)

Jerry Stackhouse: "My dad had a great saying, said, 'Beggars can't be choosy.' So for me, it's just getting the opportunity to coach at the highest level. It's a level that I played at for a long time and getting an opportunity to play, I don't really look at one more than another. It's talent. It's different from college where it's about, you can be the greatest coach as you want to, you got to have talent. In the NBA, you're going to have talent. Every team is going to have a certain level of talent and it's about how you're able to mold that and how you're able to lead that."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=djgSKUP-2PM&t=170s

**3. Jerry Stackhouse — "he wants those guys' joy. His messaging was always on point." — Jerry Stackhouse on Steve Kerr's coaching style** [04:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=djgSKUP-2PM&t=276s)

Jerry Stackhouse: "With Steve, it was all about, he wants those guys' joy. His messaging was always on point. With guys from the standpoint of when we lost Steph and we were down, I think he still found a way to find positives in what was happening and using it as growth moments and growth opportunities. We saw our younger guys get better. Probably guys that we weren't counting on going into the season this year, and then when Jimmy went down and when Steph went down, it was like, okay, this is an opportunity for you guys to step up."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=djgSKUP-2PM&t=276s

**4. Jerry Stackhouse — "everything is so Steph-centric. And when he's not in the picture, it throws everything for a loop." — Jerry Stackhouse on the Golden State Warriors' future** [06:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=djgSKUP-2PM&t=376s)

Jerry Stackhouse: "That was tough because you didn't know what was going to happen. I don't even think Steve knew what was going to happen. It's really about the health. Everything is so Steph-centric. And when he's not in the picture, it throws everything for a loop. And I think that's the challenge for the Warriors going forward to try to find a way to not put a heavy tax on Steph and even Draymond at this point of his career and try to find other people that they can play through. I know I've been hearing Kawhi, I've been hearing LeBron. Maybe getting a piece like that can rejuvenate everybody and can fill in the gaps on those times."

**6. Jerry Stackhouse — "that was a little disappointing because as far as I can remember, it's always been someone that has played." — Jerry Stackhouse on not getting the UNC coaching job** [11:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=djgSKUP-2PM&t=715s)

Jerry Stackhouse: "That was a little disappointing because as far as I can remember, it's always been someone that has played in North Carolina or went to North Carolina that had coached in North Carolina during my time. I thought that would be the case again, but it wasn't. But as a guy that's a big proponent of thinking outside of the box and trying different things, I like the hire actually. I would have liked it more if it was myself, because I felt like it would actually resonate and understanding what it takes to wear that uniform and the pride that I have from being a North Carolina Tar Heel."
