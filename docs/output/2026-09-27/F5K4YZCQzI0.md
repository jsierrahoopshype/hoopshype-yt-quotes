# There's One Way to Extend BOTH Towns & Brunson | Chris Mannix & Evan Turner — *Sports Illustrated*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F5K4YZCQzI0

_Speakers identified: Chris Mannix, Evan Turner_

**1. Chris Mannix — "it does seem like the Knicks want him to take something of a discount" — Karl-Anthony Towns' contract extension and the Knicks' long-term roster strategy** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F5K4YZCQzI0&t=0s)

Chris Mannix: "**Karl-Anthony Towns** is entering the final guaranteed year of his contract. He has a player option after this season for about 61 million, but he is extension eligible and has been extension eligible for the last few months. It does not seem like these talks are going anywhere with Towns and the Knicks. Towns is eligible for a four-year, 275 million dollar extension. This money is just wild in today's NBA. Towns is not going to get that, but it does seem like the Knicks want him to take something of a discount so they can continue to build the team around Towns, Brunson, Anunoby, Bridges, and around the championship core that they have."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F5K4YZCQzI0&t=0s

**2. Chris Mannix — "Jalen Brunson has been clear to me and to others that there will not be a discount" — The Knicks' financial constraints regarding Jalen Brunson's upcoming contract extension** [01:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F5K4YZCQzI0&t=114s)

**Chris Mannix:** "You have Brunson's extension coming up next summer. Brunson has been clear to me and to others that there will not be a discount on this deal. He wants to get paid on the back end. Certainly deserves it. Certainly will, if healthy, finish this year as one of the top players in the NBA. So if you have to pay Brunson all that money, and you are paying **OG Anunoby** max money, and you are paying **Mikal Bridges** big money, **Josh Hart** I think is extension eligible as well, and you have an owner in **James Dolan** who says we are not going into the second apron, something here has to give."

**Evan Turner:** "What are you going to go get? There is nothing. What more pieces can you add there if you are going to take away? Say he signs for 250 million, that is legit, but what are you going to add there? You are already five, six, seven deep. Whose contracts are coming up that you need to relinquish? Are you going to go get a steal from somewhere? Your top eight spots are sewn up."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F5K4YZCQzI0&t=114s

**3. Chris Mannix — "it's a de facto hard cap. That's why the NBA has it as it is." — The impact of the NBA's second apron on team roster flexibility** [02:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F5K4YZCQzI0&t=155s)

Chris Mannix: "I am not defending **James Dolan**, but there are reasons not to go into the second apron that have nothing to do with ownership being cheap. It does impact the things you are able to do, trades, signings, all the things. It is a de facto hard cap. That is why the NBA has it as it is. There are lots of reasons to try to keep the contract somewhat manageable."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F5K4YZCQzI0&t=155s

**4. Evan Turner — "I feel like you go all in with this group regardless." — Evan Turner's perspective on the Knicks' championship window and team chemistry** [05:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F5K4YZCQzI0&t=301s)

Evan Turner: "I feel like you go all in with this group regardless. This is a golden group. These guys met years ago. It is a Disney movie and everything. In five years, Olympia is going to fall. That is just how I feel. I don't know who is going to be able to replicate this, especially at a championship level. There is no more repeat teams really, especially the last couple years. Even though the Knicks have been able, I thought they had two chances to possibly win in the past couple years, but I just don't think there is any repeat or anything. I think this is a team you invest all in like the Warriors try to do in their certain situation, just go about your business."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F5K4YZCQzI0&t=301s

**5. Chris Mannix — "When you have an opportunity to win multiple championships, you got to do whatever it takes." — The necessity of financial sacrifice to maintain championship-contending rosters** [08:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F5K4YZCQzI0&t=508s)

Chris Mannix: "When you have an opportunity to win multiple championships, you got to do whatever it takes. Spend whatever you have to do to keep it together. We even just saw it, Boston is in a bit of a different story because the injury that they had with Tatum, but they were going to be an expensive roster. If Tatum doesn't get hurt, I wonder what decision they would have made with all those guys that were making a ton of money. Would they have spent to keep it together and gone into that second apron?"
