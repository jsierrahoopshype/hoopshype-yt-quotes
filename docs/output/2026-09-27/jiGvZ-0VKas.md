**1. Draymond Green — "Steph says he suffered from a rare eye condition during the Warriors dynasty run." — Steph Curry's rare eye condition during the 2018 Warriors championship run** [00:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jiGvZ-0VKas&t=20s)

Draymond Green: "Steph says he suffered from a rare eye condition during the Warriors dynasty run. It was 2018. **Bob Myers** texted me after the game because apparently the telecast kept showing me when I was on the bench and he was like, 'You're just squinting, looking like you couldn't see anything. You should probably go get that checked out.' They told me that I had a rare condition called keratoconus. I remember Steph saying this word before. I don't know if that's the right pronunciation, so internet, thesauruses, and grammar police, excuse me if I'm wrong. What I found out about it was it's a progressive condition that can deteriorate your vision over time and it's irreversible if it goes in the wrong direction. I joked that when I'm done, my memoir should be called 'The Whole Time I Couldn't See.'"

**2. Draymond Green — "This really would set the Milwaukee Bucks franchise back." — Draymond Green on the Milwaukee Bucks' potential salary cap circumvention investigation** [05:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jiGvZ-0VKas&t=315s)

Draymond Green: "The Bucks are reportedly being investigated by the NBA for possible salary cap circumvention in the deal with **Gary Trent Jr.** The punishment could be equally as severe, if not worse than the Clippers' punishment if found guilty. No way this could be a worse punishment, because I guess when you think about it, it is circumvention, because you could get a player for cheap. But it's not the same. I do think the punishment will be strenuous, but this really would set the Milwaukee Bucks franchise back. And I think if this was to happen, I think the Bucks owner would then sell, because unlike Steve Ballmer, who's the wealthiest owner, unlike Steve Ballmer who has a team that's in Los Angeles, the Milwaukee Bucks lose five first-round draft picks and they can never be good for 20 years. So I think you'd see that owner sell the team if this is the case."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jiGvZ-0VKas&t=315s

**3. Draymond Green — "I understand what he's saying when he says the pieces fit together better." — Draymond Green on JJ Redick's comments about the Lakers' roster fit** [07:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jiGvZ-0VKas&t=453s)

Draymond Green: "**JJ Redick** said in his press conference, 'I think we've always tried to give our players clearly defined roles and I think with this group, it's probably the first time in my three years here that I feel like the pieces actually fit together.' I know everybody takes this as a shot at LeBron and be like, 'Oh man, he's talking about LeBron leaving,' and I don't think so. If you think the last two years with LeBron and Luka, that really wasn't a fit. So you have that. Before that, I don't necessarily know what pieces didn't fit, but LeBron and Luka are both ball-dominant players and LeBron had to learn how to play without the ball. And I ultimately don't think it allowed for the best **LeBron James**. So I understand what he's saying when he says the pieces fit together better because they've built a team that's heavily going to play roles with Luka having the ball."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jiGvZ-0VKas&t=453s

**4. Draymond Green — "Even with a teammate that can draw double teams, it's still not that easy to win." — Draymond Green on Trae Young's comments about his Atlanta Hawks teammates** [08:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jiGvZ-0VKas&t=512s)

Draymond Green: "**Trae Young** had a quote about why he never won with the Atlanta Hawks. He said, 'I don't know if I've ever had a teammate that can draw double teams like this,' referring to **Anthony Davis**. Well, what I will say to **Trae Young** is, even with a teammate that can draw double teams, it's still not that easy to win. It is very tough to win. I get what he's saying. He's saying the level of teammates he had. And I agree by the way. I think when you go back to this podcast, when the Atlanta Hawks beat the New York Knicks, I said then, 'Listen, people are going to get excited about this Hawks team and it's really not a conference finals team.' And everybody did, and you saw that it really wasn't a conference finals team. They had a good year, a lot like the Portland Trail Blazers did some years back when they had that really good year and we mopped them in the conference finals."

**5. Draymond Green — "It wasn't any security that we wanted that they were willing to do." — Marcus Smart's comments on the Lakers' contract offer** [09:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jiGvZ-0VKas&t=586s)

Draymond Green: "**Marcus Smart** made a quote about choosing the Rockets over a disrespectful offer from the Lakers. He said, 'First off, I played so well that the offer that was ultimately given didn't match the way that I played, especially when you see guys who played way worse than I did, the offers they're getting.' And then it wasn't any security that we wanted that they were willing to do. What I would take away from this quote and what I think the most important part about this quote is, is the security that **Marcus Smart** wanted. Because **Marcus Smart** should know that in this league, it ain't always about who played better. For instance, he may have played better than **Austin Reaves** in the playoffs. They ain't giving **Marcus Smart** **Austin Reaves**' money because he played better than **Austin Reaves** in the playoffs. That's just not how the NBA works. **Paul Reed** played better than **Jaylen Brown** in the playoffs last year. They're not paying **Paul Reed** **Jaylen Brown**'s money."
