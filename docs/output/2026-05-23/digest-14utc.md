## Caron Butler on Coaching with Heat, Dwyane Wade's Rise to Stradom & Pat Riley Stories — *Run It Back*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVsQayEzWUU

_Speakers identified: Michelle Beadle, Lou Williams, Chandler Parsons, DeMarcus Cousins, Caron Butler_

**1. Caron Butler — "after we broke down the barrier of me being there to actually mentor and usher him down the path" — Caron Butler on mentoring DeMarcus Cousins and breaking down barriers** [00:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVsQayEzWUU&t=5s)

"That's my young fellow right there. It was amazing. After after we broke down the barrier of me being there to actually mentor and usher him down the path of just being a better version of himself on and off the court, I think we just locked in and clicked. But before he had a lot of questions because it was a lot of betrayal before I got there and he had a lot of questions about the staff and people that was around his village. But he opened up and he's he's a real one."

**2. Caron Butler — "I'm in basketball school right now. I've been in basketball school for the last six seasons." — Caron Butler on his head coaching aspirations and learning from Heat staff** [00:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVsQayEzWUU&t=57s)

"100%. I'm in basketball school right now. I've been in basketball school for the last six seasons. We had a lot of success. We had a lot of situations where we had play in situations and I learned a lot. I'm in the proximity of a lot of education around me and obviously the Godfather of the game, **Pat Riley**, **Coach Spo**, can't tell the story of basketball especially in this era without mention him. And **Chris Quinn**, **Malik Allen**, one of the best defensive minds in this space and so many others. So, just being prepared is the biggest thing. As you guys know, I got to put a lot of sweat equity in this and I'm ready for that next step."

**3. Caron Butler — "I was fat as hell. I was out of shape. I wasn't ready." — Caron Butler's initial conditioning struggles and coach's impact** [03:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVsQayEzWUU&t=211s)

"I'm going to tell you like this. For the second I got here when I got drafted, just from the conditioning standpoint, I thought I was in good shape. I thought I was a mental force to be reckoned with. But I found out I was, to be basically blunt with you, I was fat as hell. I was out of shape. I wasn't ready. I wasn't ready for the pace and to play with the motor that he wanted me to play with. And after him coaching **Magic** and after him coaching the Knicks and that whole era and how brutal it was, he took me to another space where I didn't even know existed."

**4. Caron Butler — "being excellent in your space, being the best student of film." — Shaquille O'Neal, Kobe Bryant's influence on Caron Butler's career** [03:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVsQayEzWUU&t=211s)

"And then fast forward to the trade and everything with **Shaquille O'Neal**, everything that he was talking to me about, first time every time being excellent in your space, being the best student of film. And I saw that in real time when I got to the Lakers with **Kobe Bryant**. So it was just like a full circle moment and then fast forward to that, I finally started having All-Star years in Washington because I took the studies and the lessons of Miami, Kobe and then I kind of put it into the fabric and the thread of what I wanted to see myself as and went to Washington and had some of the best years of my career."

**5. Caron Butler — "you put the ball in his hand and you put it in a pick and roll situation and that's when Flash was invented." — Caron Butler describes Dwyane Wade's transformation from shooter to "Flash"** [05:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVsQayEzWUU&t=337s)

"It's crazy. That's a great question. When I was playing AU basketball, **D-Wade** used to always have he had messed up knees back then. He had like two knee braces and everything like that. It was crazy. And all of a sudden you seen his his demonstration that he did on a night to night basis at Marquette. And then all of a sudden he comes to the association, we get a chance to play with him and back in the day they running these floppy sets. They're using them as a two guard they're trying to run them like a shooting guard so **Eddie Jones** used to come off the wheel action and that just wasn't his strength. He wasn't a shooter necessarily. And then all of a sudden you put the ball in his hand and you put it in a pick and roll situation and that's when **Flash** was invented. He starts splitting double teams getting to the paint like no other, finishing from an athletic standpoint over big over the bigs, the fours and fives protecting the rim. And I just knew he was going to be elite and special. And the rest is written. They got an ugly statue out front and he's doing his thing."

**6. Caron Butler — "Good dude, misunderstood. He wanted people to meet his level of his commitment to the game of basketball." — Caron Butler reveals Kobe Bryant's misunderstood personality and commitment to basketball** [07:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVsQayEzWUU&t=427s)

"You know, I always called myself a person. I don't go off the perception that people put out there or forecast it in the air. I like to learn by myself and just maybe it's trial and error, maybe it's a sign of insanity. Kind of like with **DeMarcus** when I came to Sacramento. I ran towards him, because they were like, hey man, that big dude, he's crazy. He's different. And I wanted to see what crazy looked like up close and personal. And I found out he was a just a good dude, just misunderstood. And that was the same way with **Kobe**. Good dude, misunderstood. He wanted people to meet his level of his commitment to the game of basketball. And once I realized that we just we jammed. We spent a lot of time, our family spent a lot of time together and I learned a lot from him, so peace be upon him."

## What Really Happened in Anthony Edwards' Playoff Run? — *Anthony Edwards*

"After Game 6 they were trying to make, you know, a big deal of you dapping them up at the 8-minute mark. Tell us why you did that. I mean, it was no reason why. Everybody just, I feel like everybody just got their own opinion. Yes, 8 minutes in the game, but we're not going back in the game. When you win a playoff series, everybody's celebrating at the end of the game. So they're going to be smiling while we, while I'm pissed off and we just lost. So I was just like, let me go congratulate these boys because I ain't trying to be kicking with y'all after y'all done whooped my ass. I didn't want to go dap them up at all. Then what would they have been saying about me? That would have been a whole another conversation. So I gave them the respect they deserved, man. I could have waited till the end of the game. That's other people's perspective, but I did what I did. That's why I'm me and y'all are whoever y'all are. That's just that simple."
