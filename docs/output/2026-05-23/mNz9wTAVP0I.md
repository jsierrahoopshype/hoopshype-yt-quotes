# What Really Happened in Anthony Edwards' Playoff Run? — *Anthony Edwards*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mNz9wTAVP0I

_Speakers identified: Anthony Edwards, Kendrick Perkins, Justin_

**1. Anthony Edwards — "I was just like, let me go congratulate these boys because I ain't trying to be kicking with y'all." — Anthony Edwards explains his early dap-up of the Spurs after their playoff loss** [0:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mNz9wTAVP0I&t=9s)

"After Game 6 they were trying to make, you know, a big deal of you dapping them up at the 8-minute mark. Tell us why you did that. I mean, it was no reason why. Everybody just, I feel like everybody just got their own opinion. Yes, 8 minutes in the game, but we're not going back in the game. When you win a playoff series, everybody's celebrating at the end of the game. So they're going to be smiling while we, while I'm pissed off and we just lost. So I was just like, let me go congratulate these boys because I ain't trying to be kicking with y'all after y'all done whooped my ass. I didn't want to go dap them up at all. Then what would they have been saying about me? That would have been a whole another conversation. So I gave them the respect they deserved, man. I could have waited till the end of the game. That's other people's perspective, but I did what I did. That's why I'm me and y'all are whoever y'all are. That's just that simple."

**2. Anthony Edwards — "between me and D-Hanz, we the only ones that knew I was for sure playing." — Anthony Edwards reveals he was always going to play Game 1 despite knee injury** [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mNz9wTAVP0I&t=80s)

"Was there any chance you were missing that game? Nah. I think the day before I was feeling good, but I didn't do too much. I did like a workout. I'm like, bro, I'm feeling kind of good. I told **D-Hanz**, like, bro, I can play tomorrow. Well, between me and **D-Hanz**, we the only ones that knew I was for sure playing. But yeah, they just kept it as questionable."

**3. Anthony Edwards — "Game 4, it was perfect in the sense that Wembanyama got thrown out." — Anthony Edwards discusses the impact of Victor Wembanyama's ejection on Game 4** [3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mNz9wTAVP0I&t=235s)

"Kind of walk us through the highs and lows of Game 4. Game 4, it was, it was perfect in the sense that **Wembanyama** got thrown out. But it made it hard on us because now, like, you know how it goes when the team's best player gets thrown out the game. Now everybody playing free."

**4. Anthony Edwards — "I have nothing bad to say about the Spurs, they balled. They balled." — Anthony Edwards acknowledges the Spurs' performance and the developing rivalry** [8:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mNz9wTAVP0I&t=536s)

"Do you feel like it's like a true rivalry becoming between the **Spurs** and the Timberwolves? Hell yeah. Hell yeah. Hell yeah, it's a rivalry. I tip my hat to them young guys. Them young guys came out and balled. Them boys balled. Them boys, I have, I have nothing bad to say about the **Spurs**, they balled. Like, one thing about me, when you beat me, I ain't no hating guy. I ain't got that in my blood. But we'll be back. But yeah, y'all boys did your thing."

**5. Anthony Edwards — "People don't understand the mental part of not being able to play the game that you love." — Anthony Edwards reflects on learning patience and the mental toll of injuries this season** [9:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mNz9wTAVP0I&t=569s)

"What did you learn about yourself this season? This season, I think, I'm going to say I learned just being patient. Everything ain't going to come when you want it to come. I think that's the main thing because like, I wanted to be out there playing bad as hell. Like playing every game, but it wasn't happening. I was supposed to be playing in San Francisco, then my toe got swole. I'm talking about, I barely could walk. And then my knee started swelling up, so I was just like, damn, I can't play. And I think like, people don't understand the mental part of not being able to play the game that you love. It's like that really does mess with you. And I think that's what I had to learn. You really got to just be patient. It wasn't even like no serious injury. But just not being able to play just for two, three games, that really be messing with me because like, that throws my whole algorithm off. Like, I don't be knowing what to do."

**6. Anthony Edwards — "That mean y'all think about me. I feel like it's out y'all norm to shoot back at people." — Anthony Edwards responds to Victor Wembanyama's shoe ad seemingly taking a shot at him** [10:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mNz9wTAVP0I&t=657s)

"Talking about **Wembanyama**, I mean, he started throwing shots with the shoe, with 'Believe This' with the alien sign on it. I don't know if you've seen that. They took a shot at me. From like, okay, cool. So that means y'all think about me. I feel like it's out y'all norm to shoot back at people and win, lose, or draw. I'm going to talk my stuff. So take that. That's why I started this. But we'll be back. Like, we ain't, you know, like, y'all won this time."
