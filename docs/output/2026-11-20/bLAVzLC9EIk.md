**3. Penny Hardaway — "I am rolling with the Diesel. The Diesel's mindset, he was just about dominating you." — Penny Hardaway compares prime Shaquille O'Neal to Victor Wembanyama** [02:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bLAVzLC9EIk&t=153s)

Penny Hardaway: "The 95 version of Shaq was so much more mobile. Not saying that he would stop Wemby because Wemby is just he will be physical obviously because he was still a force but I do not know what Wemby could do with him down low. I do not know if he put Shaq on the perimeter and played the perimeter game but I am rolling with the Diesel. The Diesel's mindset, he was just about dominating you. I am not saying Wemby is not but what Wemby is doing versus what Shaq did in 95, that would be a huge battle. Shaq had some boys to go against that were the Dream, **David Robinson** and all those guys and **Pat Ewing** and those guys. They were not Wemby but at the end of the day, he still had some guys that were pretty good to go against."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bLAVzLC9EIk&t=153s

**4. Penny Hardaway — "I found out that Shaq was leaving during the Olympics." — Penny Hardaway on finding out Shaquille O'Neal was leaving Orlando** [03:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bLAVzLC9EIk&t=227s)

Penny Hardaway: "I found out that Shaq was leaving during the Olympics. He had already committed to going to the Lakers and I did not know. He and I did not speak and one of the international media asked me how does it feel not to have Shaq as a teammate during the press conference and I did not know. I had to kind of play it off. I was like well if that happens then that would be devastating but it had already happened. I did not know Shaq had already signed with the Lakers and I was in the media day and that is how I found out. That crushed me though and then we ended up talking after that but that whole entire Olympics I was like bro I knew the magnitude of that, how bad that was going to be for Orlando and I just could not believe."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bLAVzLC9EIk&t=227s

**5. Penny Hardaway — "In high school, Jalen was already a man amongst boys." — Penny Hardaway on coaching Jalen Duren at Memphis** [04:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bLAVzLC9EIk&t=286s)

Penny Hardaway: "In high school, Jalen was already a man amongst boys and then coming on this level, he was 17 when he got to campus being that size and still so raw but was unbelievable to be coached. He just wanted to make it, man. He was one of the most unselfish guys. When I first knew who he was as a person was **Emoni Bates** who was ranked number one, he and Jalen were both one and two, he joined their AAU team and the coach did not start Emoni so Jalen decided to come off the bench with Emoni to make him feel comfortable and just say hey man, I am with you 100 percent. So when you do that, he did not have to do that. That let me know his character and what type of guy he was. So yeah, that size being 17 was crazy."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bLAVzLC9EIk&t=286s

**6. Penny Hardaway — "He told me, Coach, I want to switch one through five to work on my defense." — Penny Hardaway on Jalen Duren's development in Detroit** [05:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bLAVzLC9EIk&t=346s)

Penny Hardaway: "From day one, we all knew he was going to make it. He was just a hungry young man that just wanted to be the best version of himself. When he came here, he told me, Coach, I want to switch one through five to work on my defense and I said you got to show it to me in practice first. He showed it to me in practice. He said I want to stretch my game out 18, 19 feet. I want to stretch my game out so he worked on his jumper every single day. So it does not surprise me what he is doing and the growth that he has in Detroit. He is just going to do whatever it takes to win but as far as the growth of the game, he just has to continue to work ball handling. You see the game evolving, work out to the perimeter more, be a better passer, be a better ball handler, be a better decision maker and then just keep stretching out until you get to the three on working on your game but don't lose your craft and knowing who you are. You are a beast down low and doing what you do, rebounding and blocking shots."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bLAVzLC9EIk&t=346s
