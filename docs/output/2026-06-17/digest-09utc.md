## “Your Ass Never Was An Athlete Then” — Perk Responds To Vince Goodwill — *Road Trippin*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2vf4qDJNyOw

_Speakers identified: Allie Clifton, Richard Jefferson, Kendrick Perkins, Channing Frye_

**1. Kendrick Perkins — "Oklahoma City didn't keep the big three together because James wanted his own team." — Kendrick Perkins disputes the narrative that James Harden left Oklahoma City** [02:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2vf4qDJNyOw&t=137s)

**Kendrick Perkins:** "First of all, Oklahoma City didn't keep the big three together because James wanted his own team. It had nothing to do with the money. James went to the Olympics, won a gold medal. **Dwyane Wade** was telling him that he needed his own team. **Kevin Durant** comes back to training camp and he's like, we're going to lose James. And I said, what the hell you mean? He was like, everybody keeps telling me he needs his own team."

**Channing Frye:** "But if they could have paid James, he would have stayed, you don't think so?"

**Kendrick Perkins:** "No, it was not. He wanted his own team. The Oklahoma City Thunder offered James like 66 to 68 million. He got to Houston and signed for 80, so it wasn't that much of a big difference. He wanted to be a starter. He didn't want to come in off the bench no more. He wanted to be that guy. So that's why James left, and when he left, he showed that he was that guy."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2vf4qDJNyOw&t=137s

**2. Kendrick Perkins — "Minnesota had a business decision to make. They could have kept Rudy or Towns." — Minnesota's business decision to keep Rudy Gobert over Karl-Anthony Towns** [03:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2vf4qDJNyOw&t=215s)

Kendrick Perkins: "The money part with **Karl-Anthony Towns** is partially true, but Minnesota had a business decision to make. They could have kept Rudy or Towns. They chose to keep Rudy. They could have traded Rudy and got him off the books and kept **Karl-Anthony Towns** and challenged him, like the New York Knicks challenged him, to play the center position and get the best out of him defensively, which he displayed. But they chose to go with Rudy. So that was Minnesota's fault because they chose Rudy over **Karl-Anthony Towns**."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2vf4qDJNyOw&t=215s

**3. Kendrick Perkins — "You disrespect the guys who are champions by saying it's a participation trophy." — Kendrick Perkins criticizes Vince Goodwill's 'participation trophy' take** [04:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2vf4qDJNyOw&t=256s)

Kendrick Perkins: "It was all the way disrespectful. When I heard him say it, the first thing came to mind is that, your ass never was an athlete then. You couldn't have never participated or been a basketball player or played on anybody's team talking that type of nonsense. That was the most asinine thing that I've ever heard. If you're a person that hooped at any level and won at an elite level, then you know that shit hard as hell. I don't give a damn if you can't repeat. Just getting to any finals is hard as hell. And then you disrespect the guys who are champions by saying it's a participation trophy. Like, what the fuck are we talking about? Do you know that one of those eight teams that won over the last eight years was **Stephen Curry**? And I guarantee you, if you go ask **Stephen Curry** which one of them was his greatest championship, nine times out of ten he's going to say the fourth one."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2vf4qDJNyOw&t=256s
