Mahershala Ali: "I think we're in a very different time and we're still trying to acclimate in terms of how to think about it. I think COVID, even maybe even more than the NIL, has really shifted college sports in terms of the eligibility factors that weighed in where therefore kids coming out of high school or even JC are having a more difficult time getting scholarships now. People being able to stay longer because now they're getting paid to be in school. So I think between the combination of COVID and the NIL opportunities, it's made college basketball even more competitive in terms of the spots. I think the thing that college had going for it is the very thing that a lot of us would call BS, but it also is what college ball had going for it in terms of camaraderie and doing it for something bigger than yourself, learning about sacrifice, the pride, loving the school, your teammates and what not, before you end up getting hit with all the, this is how you take care of yourself and this is how you become the best player, this is how you have longevity and all that."
