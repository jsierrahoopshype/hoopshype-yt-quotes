**1. Mahershala Ali — "There's a lot of great actors that don't have Oscars who are also looking for good material." — Mahershala Ali on the limited impact of winning an Oscar on career opportunities** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ko6ThLsvJNc&t=0s)

Mahershala Ali: "There's a lot of great actors that don't have Oscars who are also looking for good material. It doesn't really mean anything. I'm not saying that's no disrespect to the Academy or the award at all, but what I'm actually saying is that Oscar or no Oscar, there's a lot of good actors out there. There's more good actors than there are good material. So it doesn't really mean anything. It's not even that all these people are competing for the same spots, it's just that there's only a handful of all the material made that check certain boxes, and then you got to be right for it. Just because the script is good, it doesn't mean that I'm right for it or that whoever wrote it is thinking about me of all people."

Mahershala Ali: "I remember, and I'm around a bunch of like actor actors, and they were, the Oscars were on. And there was like a TV in like our lobby. And suddenly there, it was like, and I was like so weirded out. It was like, why, why we got to be in this like, we got a different capacity because the color or something, you know?
