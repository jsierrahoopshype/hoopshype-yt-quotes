Tony Allen: "I got to see why they were the way they were. They were hurt at the time, but they still showed up. I think that made it even more of a thing. They just did their thing. **Klay Thompson** just hurt his ACL, so I'm coming in, **Kevon Looney** was hurt, so they were like, 'You're going to be our starter.' **3. Tony Allen — "In Sacramento, it felt like a hobby" — Tony Allen on the difference between the Kings and Warriors organizations** [02:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y08vZSLg46A&t=152s)

Tony Allen: "In Sacramento, it felt like a hobby. It felt like for these guys, they are super fans. That's what it felt like. We like this guy, so I want to go get this guy. It wasn't like we're trying to win, because we weren't. They're not putting the pieces together. When you get to the Warriors, that's what it felt like. It's championship or bust."

**4. Tony Allen — "I got traded that year to Dallas" — Tony Allen on his trade to the Dallas Mavericks** [03:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y08vZSLg46A&t=194s)

Tony Allen: "It didn't change until **Stephen Curry** broke his wrist. Once he broke his wrist, everybody was like, 'Oh, well we're done. We're not going to make it to the playoffs.' So then that's when I got traded. I got traded that year to Dallas. They went to the playoffs. I remember going in and having a talk with Bob and Coach Kerr and they were like, 'No, we really like you. Obviously you've been busting in Sacramento for the last three years. We like you, we know you needed to go to the playoffs. We're obviously not doing that. We're going to send you to Dallas.' I said, 'Well, shit, I'm cool. I need to get to the playoffs.' So we went to Dallas and made it to two playoff series and didn't get to play in neither one of them."
