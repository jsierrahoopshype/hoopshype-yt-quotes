# Vince Carter, T-Mac, Melo, Reggie Miller & Jamal Crawford Debate Who Was Toughest To Guard — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XvPH1q3J0Pg

_Speakers identified: Vince Carter, Tracy McGrady, Carmelo Anthony, Reggie Miller, Jamal Crawford_

**1. Tracy McGrady — "I think for me personally, it would have been Reggie Miller." — Tracy McGrady names Reggie Miller as the toughest player to guard** [00:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XvPH1q3J0Pg&t=43s)

**Tracy McGrady:** "I think for me personally, it would have been **Reggie Miller**. I remember when he was 38, 39 last year and I was with the Knicks and he was still moving. I am like, switch! Switch somebody, anybody."

**Reggie Miller:** "Melo, don't laugh like that. But I'm just saying for real, for me it would be Reggie. I'm telling the truth."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XvPH1q3J0Pg&t=43s

**2. Carmelo Anthony — "When he caught fire, it was over. He was a tough guard." — Carmelo Anthony explains why Vince Carter was a difficult defensive assignment** [02:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XvPH1q3J0Pg&t=126s)

Carmelo Anthony: "**Vince Carter** would probably be VC. If anybody who had the ultra green light, he had a different green light. You would come off a heavy 20-20 pull, he is coming off a hash, fading in threes off pin-downs, he is coming off flippies, he is going to lobs, he is coming off picks. It was a different check with him. When he caught fire, it was over. He was a tough guard because it was mad shots. He is shooting one-legged fadeaways and threes, and the elevation on his shot. That was probably VC for me."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XvPH1q3J0Pg&t=126s

**3. Vince Carter — "When you are in the trenches with him, it is bully ball." — Vince Carter recalls the physical challenge of guarding Carmelo Anthony** [02:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XvPH1q3J0Pg&t=162s)

Vince Carter: "I am going to go with Melo because I competed with him in the West. You hear people talking about him all the time, it is like, your body hurts after playing him. When you are in the trenches with him, it is bully ball. His physicality is like you are in the trenches, like two linemen going at each other every play. And that hurts. And that takes a lot out of you because now you have to go on the other end, you have to score now. So going up against Melo for 40 minutes a night, that is tough."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XvPH1q3J0Pg&t=162s

**4. Reggie Miller — "It was a masterclass of patience, but knowing the ins and outs of the game." — Reggie Miller details the defensive discipline required to guard Carmelo Anthony** [03:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XvPH1q3J0Pg&t=202s)

Reggie Miller: "With Melo, it was more you had to make sure your footwork was correct and you cannot jump or if you reach. Having to guard Reggie is like a masterclass of patience, but knowing the ins and outs of the game and how to maneuver through your teammates. You were so good at, even if your start and stop was so good, you just needed the quick bump to get an advantage. And if you caught up, you would slow down again or you were so good at pulling **Rick Smits** or any big, like **Antonio Davis**, into your line of path before that was illegal."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XvPH1q3J0Pg&t=202s

**5. Reggie Miller — "I made a business decision." — Reggie Miller recalls a specific defensive matchup against Carmelo Anthony** [06:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XvPH1q3J0Pg&t=377s)

Reggie Miller: "There was a game in Indiana, and this is one of our best teams, that we had **Chris Mullin** trying to guard you and maybe **Jalen Rose**, and you were on the wing and I was on the weak side. When you went baseline, the windmill, and I made a business decision."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XvPH1q3J0Pg&t=377s
