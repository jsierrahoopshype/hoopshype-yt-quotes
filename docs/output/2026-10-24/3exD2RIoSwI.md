**3. Richard Frye — "You don't give out grades on the first day of school." — Channing Frye and Richard Jefferson on early season overreactions** [01:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3exD2RIoSwI&t=117s)

**Channing Frye:** "Do you know who the leading scorer in the league is right now?"

**Richard Jefferson:** "You don't give out grades on the first day of school. You saw what happened with Boston, it was a 23-0 run. You see a lot of these teams, ups, downs. Bad teams don't know they are bad yet. **Ty Jerome** goes and drops. That is true. That is why we talk about sample size. You are going to see these crazy numbers, these crazy swings, new players, new teams, new coaches, new system. You are going to see a little bit of chaos in the first 10, 12 games, then things start settling in. That is why around 20 games you give yourself, okay, do we need shooting? Are we banged up? Do we need more size? What does our defense look like? How do we adjust our offense?"
