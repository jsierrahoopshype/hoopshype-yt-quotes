# Derik Queen On Looking Up To James Harden & His Stacked Montverde Team — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5DFHEjdyZzY

_Speakers identified: Tracy McGrady, Derik Queen, Vince Carter_

**1. Derik Queen — "I just try to play like them, but at a different size and I'm not fast like them." — Derik Queen on his basketball inspirations James Harden and LeBron James** [05:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5DFHEjdyZzY&t=323s)

**Tracy McGrady:** "Who was your inspiration? Who did you watch and be like, I like how he plays, I want to integrate that into my game?"

**Derik Queen:** "Really **James Harden**. When I wanted to learn how to dribble, he was on the Rockets doing his one-on-one stuff. Even Oklahoma City, I caught the later years of OKC. But when he was on the Rockets, he just used to do his one-on-one and I was just like, I want to do that. Him and LeBron are my favorite players. LeBron, I'm not athletic like him, but all three of us can pass. Those two were my favorite, so I just try to play like them, but at a different size and I'm not fast like them. I still fry you though."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5DFHEjdyZzY&t=323s

**2. Derik Queen — "He said he watched me play before, he said I was frying and stuff." — Derik Queen on his recruitment to Montverde Academy** [07:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5DFHEjdyZzY&t=454s)

**Vince Carter:** "Tell us how you made your way to Montverde with the caliber of team that you had there?"

**Derik Queen:** "Ninth grade, we were playing at this gym, St. James. It was COVID, the NIBC tournament. We played right before Montverde and Coach Ray, he is one of the scouts at Orlando now, he was the assistant coach. He said he watched me play before, he said I was frying and stuff. He said he needed to get my contact info. Ever since Coach Ray got in touch, he called me nonstop. My mother was like, no, you're not going down there, because I'm the only child on my mom's side. She was like, no, you're not going down there. Then we played at Dematha for the tournament. Something had happened, I forgot what happened, but something had happened and she was like, yeah, you can go."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5DFHEjdyZzY&t=454s

**3. Derik Queen — "We got more. That's some talent, that is some talent." — Derik Queen on the talent level of his Montverde Academy squad** [12:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5DFHEjdyZzY&t=739s)

**Vince Carter:** "Which team of Montverde had the best squad ever?"

**Derik Queen:** "Definitely 24. But 2020 and 2021 had some guys. They had Duren, you said Fino, Nimari. They definitely had some guys, but us, I ain't going to lie, I feel like, because we had other, them teams low-key had everything too, but we got more. That's some talent, that is some talent."
