**2. Brian Windhorst — "it wasn't that they didn't want to keep Mitch, it was that they couldn't afford to keep him" — Brian Windhorst explains how the NBA's new CBA impacts team building** [00:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CviEPQ3KQB4&t=46s)

Brian Windhorst: "In this era where you're talking about the Knicks, it wasn't that they didn't want to keep Mitch, it was that they couldn't afford to keep him. Back before the aprons, it was like, well, if we spent more money, it just meant the owner wrote a bigger check. It really came down to how much is your owner willing to spend. Because if you're willing to spend, you could have everybody. And some teams did. There were teams that paid 300 million dollars or 200 million dollars just in luxury tax."

**3. Brian Windhorst — "they can say to you, we're going to take your draft pick" — Brian Windhorst details the harsh penalties of the NBA's second apron** [02:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CviEPQ3KQB4&t=131s)

Brian Windhorst: "These new rules with the aprons, it doesn't just take away your ability, it's expensive. They start taking stuff away from you. They can say to you, we're going to take your draft pick, and if you spend in the second apron a couple years in a row, we take your draft pick, no matter what your record is, no matter what happens in the lottery, and we put your draft pick to the back. And it's not this year, five years from now. So you're totally mortgaging your present and future."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CviEPQ3KQB4&t=131s

**4. Brian Windhorst — "they're talking about its value relative to how much it costs you on their on your books" — Brian Windhorst on how teams define player value under the new CBA** [03:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CviEPQ3KQB4&t=211s)

Brian Windhorst: "Nowadays in the NBA, when they talk about value, they're not just talking about what the player's contribution is to the team today, this year. They're talking about its value relative to how much it costs you on their on your books. When they say value, they're not saying that you're coming out and you got to win a game and you're either picking **Jaylen Brown** to be on your team or **Derek White**. They're not arguing who's the better player. When they say value, they're looking at what a player's production is versus how much he costs."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CviEPQ3KQB4&t=211s
