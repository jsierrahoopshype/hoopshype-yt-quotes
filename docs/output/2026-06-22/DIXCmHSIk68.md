**1. DeMarcus Cousins — "When it comes down to situations of money and you haven't won a championship for an organization, I have a hard time seeing him saying, 'I'm willing to take less so you guys can go make other moves.'" — DeMarcus Cousins on why Kawhi Leonard won't take a pay cut** [00:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DIXCmHSIk68&t=41s)

DeMarcus Cousins: "This is a two-time NBA champion. He's seen it all, he's done it all, he feels accomplished. He's done everything he can do besides win another championship, but he's already done it. So when it comes down to situations of money and you haven't won a championship for an organization, I have a hard time seeing him saying, 'I'm willing to take less so you guys can go make other moves,' especially with the climate and seeing how everybody is moving around and people are trying to find their way to what their next situation is going to be and what's going to be the best opportunity for themselves. I'm curious to see what Kawhi's decision is. I don't think he's going to take a pay cut to stay there if there's a way that they can figure out the money part. I think he's happy with the Clippers, but if it comes down to money and taking less for somebody else, I don't see it happening."

**5. DeMarcus Cousins — "You go in and get an elite level 3-and-D guy like a Lu Dort, that's what he does." — DeMarcus Cousins on Lu Dort's defensive value to the Lakers** [07:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DIXCmHSIk68&t=441s)

DeMarcus Cousins: "I don't mind it. Lakers got plenty of scoring and what was their Achilles heel this year? We constantly were talking about them on the defensive end, whether they're strong enough on the defensive end to compete in the Western Conference. So you go in and get an elite level 3-and-D guy like a **Lu Dort**, that's what he does and that's what he takes pride in. I think that gives the Lakers a good look. I'm in the same world with the OKC Thunder with **Lu Dort** as I am with **Draymond Green**. I've gotten so accustomed to seeing him with that group, I can't see him anywhere else. But if the Lakers decided that they wanted to get better on the defensive end, **Lu Dort** would be a tremendous start for them."

Chandler Parsons: "This is interesting because I think it works perfect for both sides.
