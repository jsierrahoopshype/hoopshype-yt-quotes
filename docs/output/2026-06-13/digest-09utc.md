## Perk: Stop Calling The San Antonio Spurs DUMB — *Road Trippin*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a80T0xTyJDo

_Speakers identified: Allie Clifton, Kendrick Perkins, Richard Jefferson, Channing Frye_

**1. Kendrick Perkins — "We can't sit up here and call them the dumbest team ever in history of sports." — Kendrick Perkins criticizes media for calling Spurs the dumbest team** [00:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a80T0xTyJDo&t=36s)

Kendrick Perkins: "We have a responsibility as media to be fair all across the board. If someone is not living up to expectations, or you see that something happened, or obviously like the Spurs collapse, we can't sit up here and call them the dumbest team ever in history of sports. That shit is not okay. Because I was part of a team that actually did that to another team in 2008, which was the Lakers. We were down 24 on the road and went and got that game. And so no one would ever fix their mouth to call **Kobe Bryant** dumb or **Phil Jackson** dumb."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a80T0xTyJDo&t=36s

**2. Kendrick Perkins — "When you have one of the youngest teams to get there, you have to have a voice level." — Kendrick Perkins argues for patience with young Spurs roster** [01:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a80T0xTyJDo&t=89s)

Kendrick Perkins: "This is the biggest take. Just like San Antonio went on a run, the New York Knicks went on a run. My thing is this: we could be fair, we could say that some adjustments needed to be made, they needed to stop shooting so many three-point shots. I get all that. But it's a fine line that you cross. The fact that the San Antonio Spurs are here, I think people take that shit for granted. How hard is it to just get to the NBA Finals? How hard is it just to get to the promised land? And when you have one of the youngest teams to get there, you have to have a voice level in that locker room that's unmatched. Your voice has to be heard in order to let make young guys buy into their roles. I can only imagine how hard it is for Miss Johnson through an 82-game season to come in every single day and tell the number two pick in **Dylan Harper**, 'Hey man, I want you to continue to star in your role and coming in off the bench,' when obviously he looks like he should be an NBA starter. I want how hard it is to come in every single day to tell **Vassell** and **Champagnie** or **Kelden Johnson** at times that their minutes are going to be split or you might not play that many minutes. Like, do you know how hard that is?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a80T0xTyJDo&t=89s

**3. Richard Jefferson — "You can be critical of decisions for a game, we cannot make rash headlines." — Richard Jefferson argues against firing coaches during NBA Finals** [03:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a80T0xTyJDo&t=218s)

Richard Jefferson: "You can be critical of decisions for a game, we cannot make rash headlines in the media. We cannot make rash headlines. Oh, fire **Miss Johnson**. Stop it. During NBA Finals, could we have done better? Could we have done things differently? For sure. Even when you win, if you look at film, you go, 'Damn, we could have been better.' That is the part about being on this stage. The, 'Oh, get rid of **De'Aaron Fox**.' For who? Who are you going to go get? Who are you going to go get that's better? Stop it. Okay, go get **Miss Johnson**. For who? Who are you going to go get to make an NBA final? Who? Okay, tell me who. Because obviously, guess what? There's only two coaches in the NBA Finals. This is what I got to tell people. Oh, like, get rid of **Chet Holmgren**. Get rid of **Chet Holmgren** for who? For who? He had a bad series. He's going to approach the game. You know the kid. When you know the person, when you know the kid, if they are committed to being better at basketball and understand their mistakes, watch film, they're only going to get better. You know who you trade? You trade the guy that is complacent. You trade the coach that is complacent."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a80T0xTyJDo&t=218s
