**1. Shams Charania — "The Warriors will continue to withhold checks from Kristaps Porzingis as long as he's on the roster." — Warriors withholding salary from Kristaps Porzingis while he is on the roster** [00:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wxrLiJyg7q0&t=39s)

**Malika Andrews:** "How long do the Warriors plan to continue to not pay Porzingis and are they still trying to trade him?"

**Shams Charania:** "The Warriors will continue to withhold checks from **Kristaps Porzingis** as long as he's on the roster. That's over $218,000 per game that **Kristaps Porzingis** will be losing. The Warriors have ruled that Porzingis is not rendering services to the team. They have wanted him to show up to the team and be around the organization despite not being able to play, but he has not elected to do so as of yet."

**2. Shams Charania — "The Warriors are permitted to continue to discuss Porzingis's contract in the trade market." — Warriors permitted to discuss Porzingis contract in trade market** [01:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wxrLiJyg7q0&t=81s)

Shams Charania: "The Warriors are permitted to continue to discuss Porzingis's contract in the trade market. They're going to exhaust deals with rival teams that are essentially going to want salary cap relief in taking on **Kristaps Porzingis**. If the Warriors are able to find a deal with his near-expiring contract, they could then take on a larger contract, given another team draft capital as well, and for that other team, the upside is being able to clear money, save money overall, because from my understanding, Porzingis is willing to take a significant contract buyout on his deal to save another team a lot of money."
