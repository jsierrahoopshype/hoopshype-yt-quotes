# Josh Donaldson on Winning MVP and Being Misunderstood in MLB — *Brice Butler Studios*

_Speakers identified: Josh Donaldson, Brice Butler_

**1. Josh Donaldson — "the fan base really put their arms around me immediately" — Josh Donaldson on his transition to the Toronto Blue Jays** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WVNuVVxx_zo&t=0s)

Josh Donaldson: "When I went to Canada, being a part of a new team, you are unsure what the environment is going to be. You are going to another country. But as a visiting player, I always loved Toronto. It was a great place to hit and I was excited to be traded there. But the fan base really put their arms around me immediately."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WVNuVVxx_zo&t=0s

**2. Josh Donaldson — "we really put baseball back on the map in Toronto" — Josh Donaldson on his 2015 MVP season with Toronto** [00:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WVNuVVxx_zo&t=39s)

Josh Donaldson: "My very first year there, I come in and have a ton of success, win the MVP award, and we had a great season. We fell short of what we wanted, but we really put baseball back on the map in Toronto. People come up to me all the time in Toronto and say, 'Man, I had so much fun watching you play and bringing those years.' I tell them, I am glad it was fun for you, but I guarantee you it was a lot more fun for me."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WVNuVVxx_zo&t=39s

**3. Josh Donaldson — "I got voted the best player in the league by all the players and coaches" — Josh Donaldson on winning the best player award** [02:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WVNuVVxx_zo&t=138s)

Josh Donaldson: "It was the first time in my career where I got that respect of the work that I had put in. Even more than the MVP award, I got voted the best player in the league by all the players and coaches. MVP is won in each division, but the best player of the league, there is only one. And so I got that award that year and that was also something I never thought was a thing to begin with, but I would have never thought that just because of how I played."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WVNuVVxx_zo&t=138s

**4. Josh Donaldson — "I was so determined to win that I pissed people off" — Josh Donaldson on his intense playing style** [03:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WVNuVVxx_zo&t=193s)

Josh Donaldson: "I was so determined to win that I pissed people off. I did not feel like I would probably win that. And so when that happened, I was like, okay, people are seeing me for who I am as a player and they are appreciating that. And that gave me just as much satisfaction as the other award, the big award."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WVNuVVxx_zo&t=193s

**5. Josh Donaldson — "for me, ego gets left at the door when you walk in" — Josh Donaldson on his reputation for trash talking** [04:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WVNuVVxx_zo&t=287s)

Josh Donaldson: "They probably know how much fun I like to have and the camaraderie, the trash talking, but banter. For me, it is not about me. For me, ego gets left at the door when you walk in. I talk crap to people, they talk crap to me, we have good fun, nothing personal. We go back and forth, we have our time."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WVNuVVxx_zo&t=287s

**6. Josh Donaldson — "in baseball, I don't think you can play angry" — Josh Donaldson on the pressure of playing baseball** [05:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WVNuVVxx_zo&t=352s)

Josh Donaldson: "In baseball, I don't think you can play angry because that works to your detriment. You want to do this so bad, you start putting that pressure on yourself to execute, to play, now everything gets amplified. In baseball, we fail so much that if every time I failed, if I was pissed and throwing stuff, that way, no, for me, it was like, I am trying to find a way in. I am trying to find a weakness, I am trying to find something where I can do that and have success."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WVNuVVxx_zo&t=352s
