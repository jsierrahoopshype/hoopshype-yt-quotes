**1. Derek Harper — "I would put my five up against any five. The team from 94." — Derek Harper compares the 1994 Knicks to the current team** [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w-UPQFiYyow&t=58s)

**Michelle Beadle:** "A lot of talk about is this team better than the 94 team? When you hear that, how do you respond?"

**Derek Harper:** "I don't believe it. I'm one of the old school guys and I think it doesn't matter. You're good when you have to be good. I would put my five, which I have a picture of, I should show you guys, but I would put my five up against any five. The team from 94. We had **Mase**, we had **Oak**, **John**, of course, the Red Man, and then we had **Patrick** and **Charles Smith**. That five, power for pound, player by player, can play with anybody in the league at any time. So who knows how it plays out? I don't."

**2. Derek Harper — "I think he needs a ring because the people that you're considering all-time greats, they have that." — Derek Harper discusses Jalen Brunson's legacy and championship pedigree** [1:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w-UPQFiYyow&t=105s)

**Chandler Parsons:** "So Harper, let me ask you this, in that same vibe then. Obviously, people are praising **Jalen Brunson**. He's been phenomenal ever since he's been there. And there's been talks that he's the greatest Knick. But is he the greatest Knick of all time? Or does he need a ring and a couple more years to solidify that?"

**Derek Harper:** "I think he needs a ring because the people that you're considering all-time greats, they have that. They have championship pedigree. But Jalen is a freak too. We're calling **Wemby**, uh, what do we call him?"

**Michelle Beadle:** "Unicorn."

**Derek Harper:** "Unicorn, whatever you want to call him. Jalen is that same kind of guy because he thinks the game and has an uncanny ability to make plays down the stretch of games. I don't think you can replace that. They took him off the ball, he was still successful scoring the basketball. He just knows how to play. He doesn't force the game, he allows the game to come to him. And I think he's a big reason why they have a chance."

**3. Derek Harper — "We were worried about the wrong thing. You can't be getting ready. You got to be ready to play." — Derek Harper explains why the 1994 Knicks did not win a championship** [4:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w-UPQFiYyow&t=295s)

**Lou Williams:** "Was that the only separator? Was just how great those guys were from stopping you guys from getting a ring?"

**Derek Harper:** "Man, that's the question right there. Why didn't we win in 94? And this is what the separation was for me. I think we, during the finals, there's all kinds of stories come out, come up, who's going to win the MVP, who's going to do this, who's going to do that. That was our problem. We were worried about the wrong thing. You can't be getting ready, which **San Antonio** did last night. They were getting ready to play. You got to be ready to play, right? And that's what our problem was. We weren't on one accord as a team. And they were down in Houston. **Hakeem Olajuwon**, **Max**, **Kenny**, **Otis Thorpe**, all those guys. They had one common goal. Not that we didn't, but I think they were more tied together than we were. We were worried about who's going to be MVP and what if this happens and I was thinking about my contract and just all kinds of stuff. I'm not pointing fingers at anybody, but this is no time to look around. You got to go get it right now. I just don't think we were there."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w-UPQFiYyow&t=295s

**4. Derek Harper — "You got to be initiated into the dog pound. I said, but somebody is going to get hurt." — Derek Harper shares a story about Charles Oakley's initiation ritual** [6:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w-UPQFiYyow&t=386s)

**Lou Williams:** "I got to tell you **Charles Oakley** is he's a urban legend around here. I've heard stories about **Oak**. Man, give me something good. Give me something good about **Oak**, man."

**Derek Harper:** "I got something for you. When I first got to New York, I had to be initiated, like we're in college or something. So **Oak**, **Mase**, **John**, all of them. They had me on the elevator in Boston. He tells me, this is the best time for us to do it. I said, do what? Like, what you talking about? He says this is the best time to initiate you. You got to be initiated into the dog pound. I said, man, I hear the little game you all playing. I said, but somebody is going to get hurt. I don't know who, but somebody is going to get hurt. And when I tell you, I've never been locked up like this in a choke hold ever in my life than that particular moment. Because **Oak** grabbed me and I couldn't move, man. Couldn't move, could not breathe, and was trying to get through it, man. But yeah, **Oak** and that's a different breed. Ask **San Antonio** and **Dale Davis**. They'll tell you better than anybody."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w-UPQFiYyow&t=386s
