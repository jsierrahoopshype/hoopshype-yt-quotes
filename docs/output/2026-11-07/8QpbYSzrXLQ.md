**Matt Barnes:** "So walk me through that process."

Ryan Clark: "There was a family feud show they did, where it was NFL Live and then another show. I wasn't invited. And I ask people. And it's not like people don't know I'm a part of this. I'm a huge part of this. And so you text with people, you ask why, you wonder why. And there was always one person within the building who I'm still close to who was always honest, even if it was hard. Very simply, when I ask about that situation, they did, they didn't ask for you. Cool. But that's a, you should know. And then when you talk to people who are still there, who are still making decisions, I go straight to the source. Well, the other two people are already on this show, and so we only had three spots. A lot of talking and lying, basically. Let's be real. Just lying, because the entire time you're setting up this thing, and I'm going to have to be honest with myself, I'm too stupid to recognize it."

**3. Ryan Clark — "The award was for this piece I worked on, I pitched about Greg Brooks Jr." — Ryan Clark on winning a NAMIC award for his Greg Brooks Jr. feature** [03:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8QpbYSzrXLQ&t=195s)

Ryan Clark: "We won an award, and it wasn't a Sports Emmy or anything like that. The award was for this piece I worked on, I pitched about **Greg Brooks Jr.** I grew up with his father. He played for LSU, he had a brain tumor. Was fine one week, the next week severely impaired. Atlanta flew him out, did all this stuff with him and **Raheem Morris**. And so we covered it and we did a piece. The piece wins an award for diversity. My showrunner and producer hit me right away, hey we won this award. Congrats, like you pitched that, you fought for that, you helped us produce that. I'll let you know when social puts it out. A couple weeks go by and it never happens."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8QpbYSzrXLQ&t=195s

**4. Ryan Clark — "Was there something that happened with Disney and ESPN like an upfront where they were announcing the Super Bowl?" — Ryan Clark on being excluded from Disney and ESPN Super Bowl promotional material** [05:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8QpbYSzrXLQ&t=349s)

**Matt Barnes:** "Was there something that happened with Disney and ESPN like an upfront where they were announcing the Super Bowl and what they were doing with the programming and your name wasn't mentioned?"

**Ryan Clark:** "So the thing that happened was they did a commercial first. And when they did the commercial, Marcus and Laura are in it, and then there's little puppets of all these different people and I'm not involved. But most of the people that are on Monday Night Countdown, they're a part of it. And so that was kind of a thing. And there's people around me who are part of my team who are mentioning it, but I've gotten so used to being treated a certain way and not put on the front that I wasn't even tripping about it."

**5. Ryan Clark — "And then we went to Fanatics Fest and Disney had something that was posted and again it was about the Super Bowl" — Ryan Clark on being excluded from Disney's Fanatics Fest promotion** [06:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8QpbYSzrXLQ&t=400s)

Ryan Clark: "And then we went to Fanatics Fest and Disney had something that was posted and again it was about the Super Bowl and I wasn't on it. So I say all that to say that this is the plan. We were, before we decided to do the deal with Netflix, we were talking to another company. And we were talking to the head of that company, and he just kept telling me that I needed to go meet with a certain executive, who I met with before. And he was like, you know man, it's a lot of changes coming. And I knew it was a lot of changes, NFL Network was coming over, but I was still like, you know. But I did, I reached out and I was going to go have the meeting. And like quiet as kept, I still have, I don't have an issue with this person, right? Because like I don't consider that person a friend. We were talking business and they were trying to be friendly in that, because that's how you build a partnership."

**6. Ryan Clark — "You literally got let go while you were working on air and it was meant to paint you in a certain picture." — Ryan Clark on the timing of his firing during a live broadcast** [08:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8QpbYSzrXLQ&t=493s)

**Matt Barnes:** "The moment you find out, because it's during a commercial break, correct? Tell me what you immediately think because obviously, whether you're let go or not, whether there's a plot behind it, there's shit they were cooking it up. Other people got let go the following day. You literally got let go while you were working on air and it was meant to paint you in a certain picture."

**Ryan Clark:** "Yeah, I think the hard part about that is it's because you weren't doing the right thing. Because they weren't approaching this in the professional business-like manner that they should, I had to be called on air. Because, you know, and I said this before, you know, when it's leaked to a group of individuals that are already snakes, like they're going to do snake-like things. That's why I don't own a snake, because I feel like eventually it's going to bite me."

**7. Ryan Clark — "I was riding down the street leaving my neighborhood and I'm getting a text message like, 'Hey, you ready to hop on for SportsCenter?'" — Ryan Clark on the immediate aftermath of his ESPN firing** [10:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8QpbYSzrXLQ&t=613s)

Ryan Clark: "I was riding down the street leaving my neighborhood and I'm getting a text message like, 'Hey, you ready to hop on for SportsCenter?' And I was like, 'Oh no, bro, I got fired.' And the producer hits me with a laugh out loud, 'I don't believe you.' And I was like, 'No, this is real.' Two minutes later he goes, 'I am so sorry.' Right, so that's the, that's processing it. But then the next day, man, like I'm at these training camps and I'm doing shows and all that, because in truth, you know, Coach Tomlin, one of his favorite saying is, 'A lot of most people don't care about your problems and a lot of folks glad you got them.' That was how I saw it."
