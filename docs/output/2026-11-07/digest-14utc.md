## Josh Donaldson on Changing His Swing, Fighting MLB Demotions + Becoming a Toronto Blue Jays MVP — *Brice Butler Studios*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJLsG2rnwKQ

_Speakers identified: Josh Donaldson, Brice Butler_

**1. Josh Donaldson — "your best coach is going to be yourself because you know what's going on in here." — Josh Donaldson on the importance of self-awareness in professional sports** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJLsG2rnwKQ&t=0s)

Josh Donaldson: "For me, I'm sure you have been told the same thing. Your best coach is going to be yourself because you know what's going on in here. It's good to have eyes that you trust and a voice that you trust that when things start to hit the fan, you can go to and it's not that far off. Let's get some direction. Once I started figuring out the questions to why, because I'd always say why is that happening? Why am I doing that? A coach wouldn't really have that answer for me, or maybe they did, and then I'd say, well, the other part of that is first there's a why, second there's a how. Why am I doing that? Okay, well, this is why you're doing that. Okay, well, how do I fix it? A lot of coaches don't always have that answer to how do you fix it because they don't know what's going on here."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJLsG2rnwKQ&t=0s

**2. Josh Donaldson — "what helped me take one of my biggest steps was taking the emotion out of it." — Josh Donaldson on taking the emotion out of professional performance** [03:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJLsG2rnwKQ&t=220s)

Josh Donaldson: "I had to do a lot of looking and reflecting in the mirror of what is going on. At the end of the day, I had to shape what was success for me, and it was a lot differently than what I was being taught. Second off, I had to be able to look myself in the mirror every night and say, did you put your best foot forward? If the answer was yes, then I was able to sleep. But if the answer was ever no, that was when the wheels kept turning. What helped me take one of my biggest steps was taking the emotion out of it. On the field, you are locked in. You are in that fight mode. But whenever I was able to take a step back and have this 30,000-foot view of what was going on, now I'm not emotional about what happened."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJLsG2rnwKQ&t=220s

**3. Josh Donaldson — "I stopped and I said it very calmly like this. Mom, it's not their fault." — Josh Donaldson on accepting responsibility after being sent down to Triple-A** [05:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJLsG2rnwKQ&t=358s)

Josh Donaldson: "The last time I got sent down in Triple-A, I remember I called my mom after. I called, I said, hey mom, I got sent down to Triple-A. My mom, being a mom, she wants to blame everybody except for me. You got to call your agent, it's your agent's fault, the GM, it's the GM's fault, it's the coach's fault. I just stopped and I said it very calmly like this. Mom, it's not their fault. They gave me a jersey. It's not their fault. I had an opportunity. Now, it might not have been the opportunity I wanted, or what I was told, but I still had an opportunity. It's up to me to take advantage of that opportunity when it's put in front of me. I don't want you to waste any sleep on this because I'm not going to stop until I figure it out."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJLsG2rnwKQ&t=358s

**4. Josh Donaldson — "even if you did, you were like, I don't care what they say to me." — Brice Butler and Josh Donaldson discuss handling coaching friction** [08:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJLsG2rnwKQ&t=506s)

**Brice Butler:** "Did you feel a lot of friction when you were trying to make those changes from your coaches? Or was it one of those things where you were like, even if you did, you were like, I don't care what they say to me. They're moving me down, I got to kid up and I have to be able to give them results. And as long as I can give them those results, they're going to shut up."

**Josh Donaldson:** "That's what happened. But I went about it and thankfully in the Oakland A's organization, the minor leagues at the time, they had really good people there. They had really good coaches that cared. But also at the same time, I had developed relationships with these people. It wasn't something to where it was, I don't know you, I don't respect you, screw off. They knew that I respected them, but I think what ultimately how it came down to was like, I need to take control of this because I've let other people kind of put their hands on me and try to mold me in what they think I should do, and it's not working."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJLsG2rnwKQ&t=506s

**5. Josh Donaldson — "as an athlete, you never want to get boxed into like not being coachable." — Josh Donaldson on avoiding being labeled as uncoachable** [10:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJLsG2rnwKQ&t=616s)

Josh Donaldson: "As an athlete, you never want to get boxed into like not being coachable. If you get that label and you're not producing early, you're done. Because now you don't have somebody fighting for you in the meetings. I took that route when I was younger, of like I'm going to be coachable. If anybody says anything to me, I'm going to try it, I'm going to do it. They're going to see that I'm working on that. But at the end of the day, I'm getting bounced around from here to here to here to here. Now it's like, what do I want to do? Who do I see myself as? How do I go about getting there? They might not see me as that, but I do, and that's all that matters."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJLsG2rnwKQ&t=616s
