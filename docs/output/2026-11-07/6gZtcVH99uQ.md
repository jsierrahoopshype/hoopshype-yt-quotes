Chandler Parsons: "My only problem with **Cooper Flagg** is when the ball is not going to the basket and he is inefficient, you can see it weighs on him. **3. DeMarcus Cousins — "I think that is completely unfair, man. We are talking about a kid that cannot even buy a drink." — DeMarcus Cousins defends Cooper Flagg against unfair expectations for a 19-year-old** [02:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6gZtcVH99uQ&t=128s)

DeMarcus Cousins: "I think that is completely unfair, man. We are talking about a kid that cannot even buy a drink yet. Give the guy some time. You are comparing a 19-year-old kid to 30-year-old guys in their prime. He is still learning. He is still learning how to be a winner. He is still learning how to be a superstar. He is still learning how to impact the game on nights he is having bad nights. That is a process that everybody that came through this league had to go through."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6gZtcVH99uQ&t=128s

**4. Chandler Parsons — "If I am Dallas, I want Kyrie Irving in Dallas." — Chandler Parsons discusses whether the Mavericks should keep or trade Kyrie Irving** [06:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6gZtcVH99uQ&t=403s)

Chandler Parsons: "If I am Cooper Flagg, I want **Kyrie Irving** in Dallas. I want someone to kind of take some of that pressure off. I want someone to handle the ball to not just allow double team after double team every time I get in the paint because you have one of the best scoring guards of all time on the floor at the same time as you. I personally, I think Cooper would want that and I think **Kyrie Irving** helps him win basketball games and keeps them more relevant and has them a better chance to win at the end of the day."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6gZtcVH99uQ&t=403s

**5. DeMarcus Cousins — "I am in the middle of the road because we are talking about a young budding superstar." — DeMarcus Cousins is undecided on whether the Mavericks should trade Kyrie Irving** [06:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6gZtcVH99uQ&t=415s)

DeMarcus Cousins: "I am in the middle of the road because we are talking about a young budding superstar in a **Cooper Flagg**. I think it is beneficial for him to have somebody like a **Kyrie Irving** around where he can get some game from. He can understand the NBA champion and all of those things. On the flip side of that coin, I would like to see **Kyrie Irving** go somewhere at this stage in his career where he is around veterans, where he is around a real group that is going to give him an opportunity to get back to the top of the mountain like he has been before. So I am in the middle of the road with what the Mavs could do with **Kyrie Irving**."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6gZtcVH99uQ&t=415s

**6. DeMarcus Cousins — "You have all these up-and-coming stars that are learning how to be a star from world players." — DeMarcus Cousins emphasizes the importance of learning from veteran players** [10:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6gZtcVH99uQ&t=607s)
