# Giannis Antetokounmpo and Khris Middleton REACT to THE TRADE — *Thanalysis Show*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GCo3RVqsrW0

_Speakers identified: Thanasis Antetokounmpo, Giannis Antetokounmpo, Khris Middleton_

**1. Giannis Antetokounmpo — "I've never played an NBA game ever in my career without him." — Giannis Antetokounmpo on the shock of the Jrue Holiday trade** [00:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GCo3RVqsrW0&t=2s)

**Thanasis Antetokounmpo:** "Did you guys talk? What was the, what was the..."

**Giannis Antetokounmpo:** "For me it was very weird. It was very weird because the day that it happened, I'm not on social media. He knows too. I don't follow what's going on. I don't have Shams in my notifications like other players. It was the team bus. We had a game in Charlotte. I'm on the team bus and Khris always sits behind me. These last couple of years he was always behind me on the right side. So I'm like, 'Hey, hey, hold on, Khris.' He told me, 'Hey man, he got, he got, he got traded.' I said, 'For real, for real?' He said, 'Yeah.' I said, 'Damn.' The whole week was just weird because I've never played an NBA game ever in my career without him."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GCo3RVqsrW0&t=2s

**2. Khris Middleton — "I got to drive home after I got traded. Went to one of those rental car companies." — Khris Middleton on his emotions following the trade to Washington** [01:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GCo3RVqsrW0&t=77s)

Khris Middleton: "As far as what was going through my mind when it happened, of course a lot of emotions, man. Pissed, angry, happy, sad, all the above. One thing I was happy about, I got to drive home after I got traded. Went to one of those rental car companies, hopped in a car and drove three hours back home. Spent a day or two there and then went to D.C. once everything got finalized. On that drive, it was just a lot of reminiscing from day one since I got there all the way up until losing against Indiana in the playoffs. It was a ride I think I needed, some time by myself where you can sit back and reflect on things and get things out of the way."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GCo3RVqsrW0&t=77s

**3. Giannis Antetokounmpo — "I started getting emotional. So that's why you saw me, I don't know if you guys remember." — Giannis Antetokounmpo on the emotional impact of the trade** [02:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GCo3RVqsrW0&t=165s)

Giannis Antetokounmpo: "After the road trip was over, I was on Twitter. I was seeing me and Khris, and then Khris and his stats, and then Khris, and me and Khris, me, Khris, and Jrue. I'm just like, 'Damn, fuck.' I started getting emotional. So that's why you saw me, I don't know if you guys remember, last year that I reposted like a bunch of tweets of Khris. It's because I was on the plane because I was thinking about the same thing. I was just reminiscing all the years that went by and all the moments that we've had."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GCo3RVqsrW0&t=165s

**4. Thanasis Antetokounmpo — "I will never, never take for granted any advice you gave me, anything we sit down." — Thanasis Antetokounmpo on the human side of NBA trades** [04:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GCo3RVqsrW0&t=281s)

Thanasis Antetokounmpo: "When I heard the news, it was actually like 2:00 in the morning. I just woke up and went downstairs. I literally went downstairs, bro. I went downstairs and I FaceTime you and I called you. I was like, 'Man, I will never, never take for granted any advice you gave me, anything we sit down, any dinner we had, any on the road, the wins we had.' Like, just us being together because this is what the NBA is. It's something so special that sometimes just because it's human, it's a human trait to take stuff for granted and just push on the next day and the next day. But I will never take this for granted."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GCo3RVqsrW0&t=281s

**5. Giannis Antetokounmpo — "I took my knee was hurting so I stayed out and the team played without me." — Giannis Antetokounmpo recalls Khris Middleton's 51-point game** [06:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GCo3RVqsrW0&t=368s)

Giannis Antetokounmpo: "I think it was year six for me, year seven for Khris. It was a game against Washington, the team that he plays for. I took my knee was hurting so I stayed out and the team played without me. All of a sudden like while I'm getting treatment and stuff, it's like, 'Hey man, Khris got 20 in the half.' I said, 'Damn.' Then he's like, 'Oh, he got 25.' I said, 'Sheesh, hoop it.' He got 30. I said, 'Ooh, I know he going to be happy tomorrow, shit. He might get us some watches too.' 35. I said, 'No, you got to calm down, Khris. Calm down, bro.' 40, 45, 50, 51. 51 points. I have this picture on my phone. He had like a paper that he wrote 51. I said, 'Bro, what the...'"
