# Lou & Chandler's BIGGEST Takeaways from NBA Draft — *Run It Back*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NyfG9qzZlH8

_Speakers identified: Chandler Parsons, Lou Williams, Michelle Beadle_

**1. Chandler Parsons — "I think he is the most NBA ready prospect to go and help a team immediately" — Cam Boozer is the most NBA-ready prospect in the draft** [00:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NyfG9qzZlH8&t=39s)

Chandler Parsons: "I always say the draft is where you start, not where you finish. And this kid could very well have been the number one pick. And I think he is the most NBA ready prospect to go and help a team immediately just because of his size, his physicality, the way he plays the game. I think he is more NBA ready than maybe an **AJ Dybantsa** and a **Darryn Peterson**. But the upside and the talent of those two are undeniable."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NyfG9qzZlH8&t=39s

**2. Lou Williams — "Instead of competing against Darryn Peterson and AJ Dybantsa about who is going to be the number one pick" — Cam Boozer must focus on being impactful for the Grizzlies** [02:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NyfG9qzZlH8&t=120s)

Lou Williams: "He should have confidence in himself and like Chandler just mentioned, he should feel like he is the number one pick. But outside of yesterday, that pick is the only time that is going to matter. Instead of competing against **Darryn Peterson** and **AJ Dybantsa** about who is going to be the number one pick, you got to compete with the league now. So now your focus has to switch from not worrying about where you were drafted to being the most impactful player that you can be for the team that you got drafted on."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NyfG9qzZlH8&t=120s

**3. Chandler Parsons — "I am hyped for AJ, I think he deserved that and being the number one pick" — Dylan Harper's draft position does not define his future success** [03:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NyfG9qzZlH8&t=216s)

Chandler Parsons: "Were we watching the playoffs and the finals being like, oh, **Dylan Harper** was the second pick, not the first pick? No. That is cool now, it is a great story. I am hyped for AJ, I think he deserved that and being the number one pick. That is a huge honor and that is a big step for him. But like Lou just said, now that is over. When they go and they play the Celtics this year, you think they are going to give a damn where he was drafted or what he was picked?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NyfG9qzZlH8&t=216s

**4. Lou Williams — "Why not shoot for the stars? Michael Jordan is the best of the best" — Cam Boozer should embrace the confidence of wanting to be great** [04:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NyfG9qzZlH8&t=287s)

Lou Williams: "Why not shoot for the stars? **Michael Jordan** is the best of the best. If you want to be that caliber player and you want to motivate yourself to do so, you got to put some of this stuff out in the air. You got to manifest some of this. So I do not have a problem with him saying it. He definitely won't be the first player to say it when it comes to comparing yourself to the greatest of all time and wanting to be that. So if I am the Chicago Bulls fan base, I am looking like, all right, slow down a little bit young fellow, but also, I am cool with you being confident."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NyfG9qzZlH8&t=287s

**5. Chandler Parsons — "At the seventh pick, this is an unreal value. I am super surprised that Brooklyn didn't take him" — Darius Acuff Jr. is an unbelievable value at the seventh pick** [09:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NyfG9qzZlH8&t=588s)

Chandler Parsons: "They got to win games, right? In Sacramento, they got to be a relevant team and they have a lot of work to do to kind of build that team up to a competitive team. But at the seventh pick, this is an unreal value. I am super surprised that Brooklyn didn't take him at six. Kind of had a feeling that the Clippers were going to go with someone bigger with having **Darius Garland** there as their point guard. But this kid is unbelievable. The way he can score the basketball, he led the SEC in scoring and assist in his freshman year."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NyfG9qzZlH8&t=588s

**6. Lou Williams — "They just have proven time and time again as an organization, they know how to pick talent" — Kings have a history of picking talent but struggle with retention** [11:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NyfG9qzZlH8&t=677s)

Lou Williams: "Great pick for the Sacramento Kings. They just have proven time and time again as an organization, they know how to pick talent. They do not know how to retain talent. They do not know how to put these guys in a position to become All-Stars and to become first and second and third team all NBA in a Kings jersey. They usually do so when they leave the Kings. That is the problem that I have for this for **Darius Acuff Jr.** and in his future."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NyfG9qzZlH8&t=677s

**7. Chandler Parsons — "I think he is the perfect fit there, but also, Yaxel, he is 23 years old" — Yaxel Lendeborg is a perfect fit for the Warriors' immediate needs** [13:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NyfG9qzZlH8&t=821s)

Chandler Parsons: "I think they definitely want to go and get **Trey Murphy** and I do not think they are done trying to acquire him. I think he is the perfect fit there, but also, Yaxel, he is 23 years old. He is one of the oldest guys that has been drafted in the first round and he meets their timeline right now with Draymond, with **Stephen Curry** to go in and help immediately. He is coming off a hell of a season, player of the year in that conference from Michigan, national champion."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NyfG9qzZlH8&t=821s
