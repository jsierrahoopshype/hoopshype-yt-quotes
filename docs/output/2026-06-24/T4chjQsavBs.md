# Mahershala Ali Almost Took Steve Nash’s Roster Spot at Santa Clara — *Mind the Game*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T4chjQsavBs

_Speakers identified: LeBron James, Mahershala Ali, Steve Nash_

**1. Mahershala Ali — "I'm trying to play that type of basketball, not this three-point, all the time." — Mahershala Ali on his recruitment and playing style at Santa Clara** [01:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T4chjQsavBs&t=91s)

**Mahershala Ali:** "I was being recruited coming out of high school. By the grace of God, **Dell Demps**, former Pelicans GM, my high school alma mater, saw me playing one day. He was like, who is recruiting you? Basically, nobody was. He got me to a camp and then I was suddenly being recruited by a bunch of schools. So over the course of that next year, certain coaches are coming to your house. You have to narrow down to what five schools you are going to go visit. I'm trying to play that type of basketball, not this three-point, all the time. It was too much. I needed some freedom."

**Steve Nash:** "Now they get paid."

**Mahershala Ali:** "There you go. Yes. So, my grandfather and I were basically, my grandmother was still working at that time, and it would be my grandfather and I doing my recruiting visits as coaches would come by. So now it's getting serious, and it's getting very close to the signing date time. I played for what was equivalent to a high school **Bobby Knight**. Our coach had a seatbelt on his chair because he had gotten kicked out of games, so he would get up and the whole chair would get up with him. **Bobby Knight** was basically my coach's idol. So now, in the summer, I'm playing with **Jason Kidd**, and Jason and I are on a team, and 11 out of the 13 of our kids went D1 out of that team. We played against **Stephen Francis** and them on ESPN and all kinds of stuff. So it was a good time."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T4chjQsavBs&t=91s

**2. Mahershala Ali — "For us to be successful your freshman year, you're going to have to average 22 points a game." — Ernie Kent's high expectations for Mahershala Ali's freshman year** [03:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T4chjQsavBs&t=227s)

Mahershala Ali: "**Ernie Kent** comes in and he had played at Oregon and he was an All-American and all that. He was a brother. I never played for a brother before other than in AAU. So I'm just like, I'm about to play for a Black coach too. Oh snap, never had this experience. And he goes, 'Mahershala, for us to be successful your freshman year, you're going to have to average 22 points a game.' 22 points a game a freshman year for us to be successful? Okay coach, good to meet you, we'll give us some thought."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T4chjQsavBs&t=227s

**3. Mahershala Ali — "Good athlete, solid defender. Don't handle the ball very well, you don't shoot it well enough." — Dick Davey's blunt assessment of Mahershala Ali's game** [04:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T4chjQsavBs&t=261s)

Mahershala Ali: "**Dick Davey** comes to the house. He says, 'Good athlete, solid defender. Don't handle the ball very well, you don't shoot it well enough. You won't play your freshman year, but we'll take you.' After he left, my grandfather could have kicked his ass walking out. I was pissed. So the answer essentially was like, I'm going to Santa Clara, I'm going to the same mirrors. It's like a no-brainer, no-brainer."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T4chjQsavBs&t=261s

**4. Steve Nash — "He would negative recruit. One to be honest, but two, he wanted to get guys that could take it." — Steve Nash on Dick Davey's negative recruiting strategy** [07:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T4chjQsavBs&t=421s)

Steve Nash: "Coach Davey came to see me play late senior year. Probably he'd seen you playing way more than he saw me. Came up to see me play in the provincial state championship. Saw me play like a noon game, we went to a diner after, and he's like, 'You got to be the worst defender I've ever seen.' But, 'we'll take you, but we got to work a lot.' So he was who he was. We always said as players and as a program, he would negative recruit. One to be honest, but two, he wanted to get guys that could come that would be able to respond to the coaching. He wanted to get guys that could take it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T4chjQsavBs&t=421s
