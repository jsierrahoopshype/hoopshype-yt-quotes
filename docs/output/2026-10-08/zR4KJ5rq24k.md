# Lou Williams On His Sixth Man Legacy & Being Snubbed From The All-Star Game — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zR4KJ5rq24k

_Speakers identified: Vince Carter, Tracy McGrady, Lou Williams_

**1. Lou Williams — "I put myself first and I think it's appropriate for all three of us to all feel like we're the best." — Lou Williams on his legacy as an all-time sixth man** [00:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zR4KJ5rq24k&t=15s)

**Vince Carter:** "So you've made a career of six-man, you've won the award three times. Where do you rank yourself as one of the all-time six-men in the history of the game?"

**Lou Williams:** "Well, obviously in the conversation. I put myself first and I saw Jamal on here and I think it's appropriate for all three of us, Manu included, to all feel like we're the best. I think that's fair."

**Tracy McGrady:** "I think Manu started too many games though."

**Lou Williams:** "And he's to come in at the 10-minute mark. That's another thing. He's to come in at the 10-minute mark in the first quarter, ladies and gentlemen. We've seen enough from Manu."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zR4KJ5rq24k&t=15s

**2. Lou Williams — "I feel like I got robbed twice. My last year with the Sixers, I let that team in scoring." — Lou Williams on being snubbed from the All-Star game** [02:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zR4KJ5rq24k&t=148s)

Lou Williams: "I feel like I got robbed twice. My last year with the Sixers, I let that team in scoring. We snuck into the playoff situation at that time. It was no play-in at that time, so it was a six seed. I let that team in scoring and I thought that was the first opportunity that I had to be an All-Star that got robbed from me. **Doug Collins**, who was the head coach at that time, he called me and said, 'Hey, we think it'll be better for our team moving forward. I'm going to call these coaches and I'm going to rally around **Andre Iguodala** to be an All-Star because we think it'll take our team further if he's the guy that represents us.' To Dre's credit, he was the best player on our team and he was promoted as such. I didn't have an issue with him doing that for Andre. I had an issue with him taking that opportunity away from me, and I felt like I earned it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zR4KJ5rq24k&t=148s

**3. Lou Williams — "I was averaging about 22, 23 a game off the bench and I didn't make it." — Lou Williams on his second All-Star snub with the Clippers** [04:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zR4KJ5rq24k&t=240s)

Lou Williams: "The second one was maybe my second sixth man with the Clippers, that I was averaging about 22, 23 a game off the bench and I didn't make it. I didn't make it then and it was kind of an uproar about me not making the All-Star game and I think **Jimmy Butler** got in at that last spot. He had some injuries and then he ended up sitting out of the game and I didn't get the reserve spot either. Those two times I felt like would have really put me in a different conversation when it comes to sixth men."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zR4KJ5rq24k&t=240s

**4. Lou Williams — "Probably when I won my first one in Toronto. I was fighting for my career when I won my first sixth man." — Lou Williams on embracing the sixth man role** [05:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zR4KJ5rq24k&t=308s)

Lou Williams: "Probably when I won my first one in Toronto. I was fighting for my career when I won my first sixth man. I had torn my ACL two seasons before that, fought to get back on my feet, fought to get back, and I was in my 12th year. Now mind you, I'd been on the bench a long time before that, but that's when I embraced it, when I thought, okay, this was late in the game for me. Because before that, I was just like, look, I'm just going to make it embarrassing that I'm even coming off the bench. I'm just going to feel, and I'm going to let everybody else talk about it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zR4KJ5rq24k&t=308s

**5. Lou Williams — "Doc Rivers was the first guy to do that. It actually got to the point where me and him would have conversations." — Lou Williams on Doc Rivers' role in his sixth man success** [06:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zR4KJ5rq24k&t=376s)

Lou Williams: "**Doc Rivers** was the first guy to do that. It actually got to the point where me and him would have conversations like, 'Bro, I really don't want to start you anymore because you're so good coming off the bench.' It gave me an opportunity to win the Sixth Man of the Year because that's your that's your lane."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zR4KJ5rq24k&t=376s

**6. Lou Williams — "I've lost more sixth man than I've won. I've came in second four times." — Lou Williams on losing Sixth Man of the Year voting** [07:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zR4KJ5rq24k&t=443s)

Lou Williams: "I've lost more sixth man than I've won. I've came in second four times. I've lost to **James Harden**, I've lost to **J.R. Smith**, and then I lost to **Eric Gordon**, and I lost to Trez, my teammate. I've lost twice to two of my teammates."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zR4KJ5rq24k&t=443s

**7. Lou Williams — "Different styles of preparation. AI for me, I was so young and my mind was so impressionable." — Lou Williams on learning from AI, Kobe, and Kawhi** [08:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zR4KJ5rq24k&t=513s)

Lou Williams: "Different styles of preparation. AI for me, I was so young and my mind was so impressionable that I just I used to look at him with the biggest eyes like, how you get away with that? It got to the point, man, I trained one day, AI went was was going off and both of y'all been superstars, so y'all y'all know how y'all get from time to time when y'all don't feel like shit is right. Y'all can start tripping a little bit. And so AI going on one of his he going on one of his tangents and I'm just looking at him, I'm looking at him and Mo Cheeks argue back and forth. I'm literally like this, ping pong, looking at them argue. And soon as they got done, trainer said, 'Hey, come here.' He said, 'Hey, I see that look on your face. Just know, them his rules. They not for everybody else.'"
