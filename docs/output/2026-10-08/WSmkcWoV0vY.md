# Lou Williams On Becoming A National Prospect & Choosing His Hometown Friends Over Pace Academy — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WSmkcWoV0vY

_Speakers identified: Tracy McGrady, Lou Williams, Vince Carter_

**1. Lou Williams — "I was more of a baseball guy at that time." — Lou Williams on his early sports background in Memphis** [01:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WSmkcWoV0vY&t=63s)

**Vince Carter:** "Lou, when you were in Memphis, were you playing any sports at that time?"

**Lou Williams:** "When I was in Memphis, I was more of a baseball guy at that time. I got raised by the village, so anybody that can keep me out of street activity, they would take me under my wing. If it was football season, I'm at football practice. If it's baseball season, I'm at baseball practice. Basketball, I'm at basketball practice. I'm wherever I need to be to stay out of the streets."

**Tracy McGrady:** "That was my story. Keep going."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WSmkcWoV0vY&t=63s

**2. Lou Williams — "Baseball was my first original passion. I thought I was going to hit home runs for a living." — Lou Williams on his early baseball passion and transition to basketball** [01:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WSmkcWoV0vY&t=94s)

Lou Williams: "Baseball was my first original passion. I thought I was going to hit home runs for a living, and they just started pitching faster and faster. I couldn't hit the regular ones, and then I was playing hood baseball. I would get a hit and I won't stop running. I'm in between bases, I'm juking. My coach said, you can't do that, man. You got to take your base. Then I'd go up there and I'd try to bunt whenever I wanted to because I was fast. My baseball coach is actually my basketball coach, so he said, look, we're going over here."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WSmkcWoV0vY&t=94s

**3. Lou Williams — "I had no decorum with my game. I was just out there doing what I want." — Lou Williams on his aggressive, non-traditional style of play** [02:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WSmkcWoV0vY&t=133s)

Lou Williams: "I had no decorum with my game. I was just out there doing what I want. I wasn't listening to the coaching and it just wasn't working for me at such a young age. So my baseball coach is actually my basketball coach. He said, look, we're going over here. Six, seven years old, I'm giving them 25 and 30 balls and he was like, we're going to stick to this. I want to give a special shout out to Coach Leon, passed away recently, but he's a person in my neighborhood that put a basketball in my hands originally."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WSmkcWoV0vY&t=133s

**4. Lou Williams — "Anybody that's on a small town, on a little small team making some noise, the big dogs going to come and find them." — Lou Williams on his early development and the AAU circuit** [04:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WSmkcWoV0vY&t=245s)

Lou Williams: "I'm a new kid in the neighborhood and so my mom was just signing me up for basketball programs here and there. Y'all know AAU culture, anybody that's on a small town, on a little small team making some noise, the big dogs going to come and find them. Big fish eat little fish. I was on a little local team, we were called the Swanny Players. We were out in the suburbs, and we started making some noise and the Georgia Stars came along and they was like, we want to absorb this whole team."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WSmkcWoV0vY&t=245s

**5. Lou Williams — "I had this visual in my head of Will Smith being at Bel-Air with his jacket inside out." — Lou Williams on choosing his local high school over elite academies** [09:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WSmkcWoV0vY&t=550s)

Lou Williams: "It was a tough decision. I remember going to Pace and going to that cafeteria. I said, this is different. They got an actual arena. Pace Academy got the arena with the circle seating. But man, I just had this visual in my head of **Will Smith** being at Bel-Air with his jacket inside out. I could never get that visual out of my head and I was like, I'm going to be **Will Smith**, bro. If I go to this school, I'm going to be one of the minorities, I'm going to be the athlete, but I'm going to stand out and I didn't want to have that feeling."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WSmkcWoV0vY&t=550s
