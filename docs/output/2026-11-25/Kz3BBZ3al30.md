**3. Chris Mannix — "Derrick White's shooting is not a short-term thing. He's shooting less than 30% from the field." — Derrick White's declining shooting performance is a major concern** [03:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Kz3BBZ3al30&t=187s)

Chris Mannix: "I am concerned, though, about **Derrick White**. **Derrick White**'s shooting is not a short-term thing. I think **Payton Pritchard** will figure it out, and they need him to figure it out because he is a shot creator for that team. But **Derrick White**'s shooting numbers, that is a real problem for this team because as good as he is defensively and as solid as he is as a playmaker, he's got to make open shots."

**4. Evan Turner — "I think his confidence has been affected. He had at least two or three seconds to drive that ball" — Derrick White's confidence and role in the Celtics' offense** [03:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Kz3BBZ3al30&t=225s)

Evan Turner: "I think his confidence has been affected. You look at the Sacramento game, he had at least two or three seconds to drive that ball, attack, and try to make a play. I just feel as though ever since last season, he's been trying to find his shot and kind of been inside his head. But it's so necessary because, like we said prior, you can't rely on a 36, 37-year-old **Paul George** to be shooting and playing out of his mind, averaging mid-20s. He's been a great three-and-D guy, he'll be a great third option, but I think **Derrick White** definitely has to step up in order for this team to be anything, space the floor, but at the same time, take pressure off **Jayson Tatum** and even **Payton Pritchard**."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Kz3BBZ3al30&t=225s

Chris Mannix: "Teams are going to continue to do this to Tatum. They're going to continue to pressure him, to blitz him, to send double teams at him. Assist-to-turnover ratio, obviously, not as good: 2.7 the first 12 games, less than one, 0.86 over the last three. Teams are just going to try everything they can to take the ball out of Tatum's hands."

**8. Chris Mannix — "Derrick White's shooting is a concern for me. It's a real concern for me with this shooting." — Derrick White's shooting decline from championship season to present** [08:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Kz3BBZ3al30&t=534s)

Chris Mannix: "I mentioned the decline of **Derrick White** to keep it focused on him for a second. 23-24, the year the Celtics won the championship, he shot 46% from the field, close to 40% from three. Still good, still good numbers. Last season, 39.4% from the field, 32.7% from three. This year, so far, 41% from the field, 28.9% from three.
