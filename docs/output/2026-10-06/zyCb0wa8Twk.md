# LeBron Revisits His Time With The Los Angeles Lakers — *Mind the Game*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zyCb0wa8Twk

_Speakers identified: LeBron James, Steve Nash_

**1. LeBron James — "to come to a historical franchise that has some of the greatest basketball players that we've ever seen" — LeBron James on the challenge of joining the historic Lakers franchise** [00:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zyCb0wa8Twk&t=9s)

LeBron James: "I came to the Lakers, and a lot of people said I was just coming to be in Hollywood and do movies. But I've always loved a huge challenge. To come to a historical franchise that has some of the greatest basketball players that we've ever seen in the history of sports, to be a part of that, was a super-duper big thing to me. It was super cool and humbling at the same time. The expectations around here in Los Angeles when it comes to the Lakers is to win championships. I heard that, I understood that, and for us to be able to win one, that was something I would never forget."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zyCb0wa8Twk&t=9s

**2. Steve Nash — "I still think about not being able to do the parade" — LeBron James on missing out on a championship parade in Los Angeles** [01:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zyCb0wa8Twk&t=76s)

**Steve Nash:** "I was going to say, scale of 1 to 10, how much does that bother you?"

**LeBron James:** "That bothers me a 20. Just to be able to celebrate with the Laker faithful. I don't know what the route would have been. I'm thinking go down Olympic, all the way down Olympic and end up downtown right by Staples Center. I remember calling it crypto and shit, I call it Staples Center. It would have been super-duper dope, man. But yeah, we did that. That banner is in that arena for forever, along with the other ones that have been able to go up in the history of the Laker franchise."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zyCb0wa8Twk&t=76s

**3. Steve Nash — "a superteam is just a collection of players and personalities that have had a lot of success" — Defining the components of a successful NBA superteam** [02:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zyCb0wa8Twk&t=144s)

**Steve Nash:** "Let's talk a little bit about the term superteam. People debate about what is a superteam. The reality is, I think a superteam, we should probably all agree, is just a collection of players and personalities that have had a lot of success. That have either been All-Stars or heliocentric parts of their team. But the reality is, it's not the names, it's where are they at this stage in their career. Even teams going under the radar, like the Lakers who won with **Gasol**, **Bynum**, **Odom**. These are teams you could consider superteams."

**LeBron James:** "Absolutely, it's the 60s. It's the Lakers, Celtics, you make them superteams."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zyCb0wa8Twk&t=144s

**4. LeBron James — "they didn't get an opportunity to really mesh, they didn't really get an opportunity to play much" — LeBron James on the failed Brooklyn Nets superteam experiment** [04:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zyCb0wa8Twk&t=250s)

LeBron James: "You could look at the Brooklyn team with **Darren Williams**, **Joe Johnson**, **Kevin Garnett**, **Paul Pierce**, **Jason Terry**. That team, they didn't get an opportunity to really mesh, they didn't really get an opportunity to play much. I think they played maybe one or two years, and for the most part they were all just past their prime. So it's about where you are in your career and the success that you're able to have. You can get guys that come together and it just doesn't work out."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zyCb0wa8Twk&t=250s

**5. LeBron James — "are we bringing our personal egos to the table for the collective group" — LeBron James on the importance of accountability and team mission** [06:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zyCb0wa8Twk&t=383s)

LeBron James: "One, it needs to be what is our mission? What is our day-to-day mission? Are we bringing our personal egos to the table for the collective group, or are we bringing our egos to the table for the collective individual? Because obviously we're all got to this point in our career for a reason. But if we're coming together for one common goal, now if things happen and transpire, we can look each other in the eye and say, okay, this is what we said, this is how we said we was going to go about it. And that's accountability."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zyCb0wa8Twk&t=383s
