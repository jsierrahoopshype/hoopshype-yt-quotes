# Wolves 1st game; LaMelo insights | The Jon Krawczynski Show — *The Jon Krawczynski Show*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uQqIJFLtiPI

_Speakers identified: Jon Krawczynski, Jim Souhan_

**1. Jon Krawczynski — "It was a much faster-paced offense. It was a lot of hit-ahead passes." — LaMelo Ball's debut with the Timberwolves shows a new offensive style** [01:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uQqIJFLtiPI&t=61s)

**Jim Souhan:** "What was the feel last night? You got to be there for the first iteration, the first game of **LaMelo Ball**'s Timberwolves career. What struck you?"

**Jon Krawczynski:** "It was a much faster-paced offense. It was a lot of hit-ahead passes. There were lobs being thrown all over the court. You really saw some of **LaMelo Ball**'s orchestration, the impact of having a point guard who can really quarterback an entire offense and see things that other players cannot see. The shots that he was creating for others was really encouraging."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uQqIJFLtiPI&t=61s

**2. Jon Krawczynski — "He is that kind of a player in terms of really athletic, really aggressive." — Jonathan Kuminga's athleticism adds a new dimension to the team** [03:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uQqIJFLtiPI&t=228s)

**Jim Souhan:** "**Jonathan Kuminga**, does he help with replace some of the physicality lost by **Julius Randle** or is he that kind of a player?"

**Jon Krawczynski:** "He is that kind of a player in terms of really athletic, really aggressive. There are things that he brings to the table athletically and just explosion-wise that we are not here with **Julius Randle** and **Naz Reid**. The challenge that Kuminga will face is you can see that he is a big strong guy, but against the biggest teams, and the Bucks had a lot of big dudes out there last night, he is small in comparison to the real big powerful forwards and centers."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uQqIJFLtiPI&t=228s

**3. Jon Krawczynski — "I've never seen LaMelo mad since we've been here. Like, he's always happy." — LaMelo Ball's leadership style and impact on team chemistry** [06:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uQqIJFLtiPI&t=392s)

**Jim Souhan:** "Is he the kind of point guard who is going to intentionally try to keep people happy or is he the kind of point guard who just makes the play that he thinks is there to be made even if it's taking his own shot?"

**Jon Krawczynski:** "The dichotomy of this whole Timberwolves experience is **Anthony Edwards** is the force of nature in the middle. He commands the room. He has this big presence that fills every nook and cranny. With LaMelo, there was a real sense of everyone looking his way for a smile, for a laugh, for a kind of validation that this is going to be fun. I asked Kuminga about that after the game, and Jonathan Kuminga is kind of a quiet, thoughtful, thoughtful dude, and he's just like, 'I've never seen LaMelo mad since we've been here. Like, he's always happy.' There is something to that, Jim. There's something to that kind of a spirit with a team."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uQqIJFLtiPI&t=392s

**4. Jon Krawczynski — "He is the only other big like who is viable on the roster right now." — Yuan Barrenje's role as a backup center and need for development** [14:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uQqIJFLtiPI&t=870s)

**Jim Souhan:** "Could this be a big year for him or is this going to be another growing year for him?"

**Jon Krawczynski:** "It kind of has to be a big year for him, Jim. Like, I mean, he is the only other big like who is viable on the roster right now. They did not bring another another veteran, like a **Kevin Love** style or anybody like that yet to have a kind of in case of emergency. They want him to play a lot, and he's going to need to play. He's going to need to give them rebounding, rim protection, and some kind of opportunistic offense."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uQqIJFLtiPI&t=870s

**5. Jon Krawczynski — "They got smaller. They don't have Naz Reid's muscle, they don't have Julius Randle's muscle." — Rebounding remains a critical issue for the team's defensive success** [20:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uQqIJFLtiPI&t=1205s)

**Jim Souhan:** "Rebounding continues to be an issue for this team. How big an issue is it and is there a solution on the roster?"

**Jon Krawczynski:** "It's a big issue. They got smaller. They don't have **Naz Reid**'s muscle, they don't have Julius Randle's muscle. They're going to have to really focus on boxing out more and not just trying to out jump people for the rebounds because Kuminga is 6'7, he's not 6'10. They have size at the other positions like Ant is a big two guard, Jaden is a big small forward, and LaMelo is a big point guard. So they have to be able to leverage that size to kind of attack the glass and rebound as a group."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uQqIJFLtiPI&t=1205s

**6. Jon Krawczynski — "Olivia Miles, her superpower is getting to the lane, destroying defenses and still getting whatever she wants." — Olivia Miles' injury impact and need for improved shooting range** [27:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uQqIJFLtiPI&t=1628s)

**Jim Souhan:** "What do you think of LaMelo's defense?"

**Jon Krawczynski:** "**Olivia Miles**, her superpower is getting to the lane, destroying defenses and still getting whatever she wants. You watch that series, she didn't even try to go into the paint. She obviously, obviously the calf injury limited her to a point where she couldn't function. I really think about the only thing Olivia needs to really work on hard, other than just getting stronger, which is typical for all young pros, if she adds a little more arc to her three-point shot, she'll have even more range and be even more unstoppable."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uQqIJFLtiPI&t=1628s
