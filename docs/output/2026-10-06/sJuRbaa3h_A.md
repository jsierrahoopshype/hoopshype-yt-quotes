**Rachel Nichols:** "Do you think that **Jim Dolan** saying I absolutely will not go into the second apron is a money cheapness thing? Do you think it's a principle thing? **Chris Mannix:** "I don't think Dolan is cheap. He's not been afraid to open up his wallet."

**4. Rachel Nichols — "I really believe that Josh Hart is a multiplier for Jalen Brunson both on and off the court" — Josh Hart is a multiplier for Jalen Brunson** [05:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sJuRbaa3h_A&t=357s)

Rachel Nichols: "It's a little bit of the **Draymond Green** principle with the Golden State Warriors. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sJuRbaa3h_A&t=357s

**5. Chris Mannix — "The one guy I'm actually more concerned with is Deuce McBride" — Deuce McBride is a priority for a long-term deal** [09:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sJuRbaa3h_A&t=556s)

Chris Mannix: "The one guy I'm actually more concerned with is **Deuce McBride**. He's a big part of their bench. If he has a great year, he is going to be way out of their price range. That's a guy I would want to work hard to get on a long-term deal that makes sense. It's like what Boston did with **Peyton Pritchard** back when they gave **Peyton Pritchard** what is now the greatest contract in the NBA in terms of value for dollar."

**6. Rachel Nichols — "You don't want contract extension talks trickling into the season because whenever there's uncertainty, there's going to be a lot of questions" — Josh Hart's comments on contract extensions** [10:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sJuRbaa3h_A&t=620s)

**Rachel Nichols:** "I looked up the **Josh Hart** quote because I wanted it exactly: 'You don't want contract extension talks trickling into the season because whenever there's uncertainty, there's going to be a lot of questions. And I thought matching that quote up to what **Stephen Castle** told GQ when he was like, basically we got beaten by the power of friendship. He was like, team chemistry is what beat us."

**Chris Mannix:** "It is delicate, but the why is that it is a very small needle to thread to be able to keep the entire team together. If they just cave and go to Towns, you're peeling off Mikal Bridges in a year."
