**1. Darren Waller — how college athletes used clean pee to pass drug tests**
[00:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lVtXbDB2quY&t=9s)

**Darren Waller:** "So I started taking note of the vets at school. I was like, why y'all bringing like a little like a 5-hour energy bottle to the practice facility every day? Because we had to walk, it's probably like a half a mile walk from the locker room to where the practice field was. And then it was like, you got to stay ready. But they start hitting you with the random drug tests. They're like, you got to get you a network, have someone on the team that got clean piss. Find a way to take care of them so you're not just going to them and..."

**Matt Barnes:** "And it has to be warm. It has to be a certain temperature."

**Darren Waller:** "No cold piss, they know it ain't yours. You bring the bottle with you to practice and right after practice would end, you put it right in your crotch and get to the back of the line so you can let time for it to simmer like it's a crockpot in your compression shorts. So then when I get up to the front of the line, it's like, I'm good."

**Matt Barnes:** "So you guys would put little 5-hour energy to someone else's pee in your tights and then that's how you would pass the test?"

**Darren Waller:** "Yep."

**2. Darren Waller — Darren Waller's rookie year struggle with love for football**
[02:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lVtXbDB2quY&t=145s)

"That was rookie year, right after the draft. At that time, I'd been going through college, dealing with issues and just really numb to a lot of things in life. So by the time I got drafted to the league, I don't feel like I had access to the feelings of being excited for the opportunity. I just saw it as more of, 'this is just pressure and more work.' He hit me in a moment of reality I wasn't expecting him to say that: 'If you don't love football, just walk out.' It just hit me in my heart. I was like, 'I really don't know if I love it. I'm good at it. I know I'm good at it.' It hit me in my heart. I was just like, 'Really? He's talking to me.' But you can't just stand up and be like, 'Coach, I'm going to take you up on that offer. I'm going to go ahead and bounce. Appreciate you opening my eyes.' You can't do that in that moment. So I got to find a way to ride this out. But that moment was real for me."

**3. Darren Waller — Professional athletes masking struggles and suffering in silence**
[03:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lVtXbDB2quY&t=227s)

**Matt Barnes:** "I think obviously us all being professional athletes at one point, the pressures and everything that comes with the glamour that everyone thinks it is, there's a lot behind the scenes that they don't understand. Did you run across other teammates? How many guys do you think struggle and suffer from similar things you did?"

**Darren Waller:** "I would say a lot. You got to be good at masking it. A lot of guys, you come in that environment, it's a hyper-competitive environment. You can't show signs of weakness. So it's like, I don't want to show this here. This could be used against me. They could want to find somebody else that maybe has their stuff together that they'll put in my space. So I got to hold this stuff. I got to provide, I got to do whatever it is. And those are all legit reasons to show up and not show those signs of weakness. So I totally get it. But it's a lot of dudes suffering in silence. You see how many dudes like **Vincent Jackson**, they found him dead in the hotel room, dead at 40. Players dying 41, dudes offing themselves at 25."

**4. Darren Waller — External success doesn't always reflect internal struggles for athletes**
[03:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lVtXbDB2quY&t=227s)

**Matt Barnes:** "Even the kid that played for the Cowboys, he had his best game. And a week later, he take his own life."

**Darren Waller:** "So I don't think especially for my story, if people take anything away, it's like you can see the external success and things are going up, but that doesn't always dictate about what somebody dealing with."

**5. Darren Waller — NFL's painkiller culture evolution from "Wild West" to regulated Toradol use.**
[05:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lVtXbDB2quY&t=310s)

**Matt Barnes:** "Obviously, we know in the NBA, what they would try to do to keep us on the court. The NFL has notoriously been known for the painkiller culture within that. Was everything readily available if you needed it, if you asked for it, in those training facilities?"

**Darren Waller:** "I think it's a lot different now. If you ask someone old OGs in the NFL, that stuff sounded crazy. They had like a popcorn bowl of Perc 30s. You could just walk in and take whatever you want. That stuff sounded like the Wild West. Now it's definitely more regulated. They asking you questions, they make sure they going through their documents and then they'll give it to you. But they always have the Toradol on game day. Everybody taking Toradol on game day. So, it's still there, but yeah."

**6. Matt Barnes — Toradol's dangers, Eric Armstead's brother's lawsuit, and NFL's pain management hypocrisy.**
[05:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lVtXbDB2quY&t=310s)

**Matt Barnes:** "You know what's crazy is how bad, I remember a friend of mine who **Eric Armstead**'s brother, he's older than him. **Darren Waller:** "Yeah, and it's another layer of like, they want you, they want to push you out there and get the most out of you, but then they also want to hold those injuries over you when it's time to pay you or it's time to talk for you to get a bag. They want to find any reason they can to not give it to you."

**7. Darren Waller — Darren Waller on public perception versus his personal struggles**
[08:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lVtXbDB2quY&t=520s)

**Jalen Rose:** "Four game suspension in 2016, 2017 you take that whole year off. To some people it might look like Darren is spiraling, his career is over. Not knowing what you're going through. How was hearing that but also you knowing what was actually going on in real time and not being able to defend yourself?"

**Darren Waller:** "Yeah, that stuff definitely stung for real. Seeing people, I was definitely more on the internet, searching my name, searching what people were saying back then and it's like, yeah, it's tough because I'm trying my best to hold things together just in my life, but I'm struggling and I'm using these things to cope with what I'm dealing with. But most people's reaction, they see that, they see your name go across the ticker, they're like, this guy's bullshitting, as opposed to being like, what's going on in his life? Having an understanding. But I get it. And yeah, I got banned for a year in 2017. And after that, it's just like I'm getting faced with real life. Like, all right, football looks like it's just over with at this point. And yeah, I didn't even think it'd even come back into the picture."
