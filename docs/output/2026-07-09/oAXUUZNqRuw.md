# Is there a case that Jayson Tatum should of been traded instead 👀 — *To The Baha*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oAXUUZNqRuw

_Speakers identified: Theo Pinson, Raymond Felton, Charlie Villanueva_

**1. Theo Pinson — "You're basically holding the team hostage with those two guys and they haven't won." — debating if the Celtics made a financial decision regarding their roster** [02:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oAXUUZNqRuw&t=150s)

**Theo Pinson:** "Brad says it is difficult to build a team with 70% of the cap in two players. Are you calling bullshit?"

**Raymond Felton:** "Bullshit. That is bullshit. I get the money aspect of it. 70% of the cap is tied up to two guys. I think the league is going away from that. You're basically holding the team hostage with those two guys and they haven't won. They got a first-round exit. They basically said you're replaceable."

**Theo Pinson:** "Do you think it was a money thing or do you think it was a player thing?"

**Charlie Villanueva:** "I think it is a little bit of both. I think like he said, part of it is a lot of the money is tied up into two guys, so it is hard to go sign other pieces to help you win, help you be successful. We have all talked about that."

**2. Theo Pinson — "When you start getting things like that happening, words being said, media starting these narratives, it can mess up a locker room." — discussing the impact of trade rumors on the Celtics locker room** [03:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oAXUUZNqRuw&t=225s)

**Theo Pinson:** "I think **Jaylen Brown** subliminally did some stuff and put a narrative out there and that sometimes can mess up a locker room. I think if you look at the Boston Celtics over the last few years, they have been a team that has been together, liked each other. I don't know how they feel when they step off the court. But when you start getting things like that happening, words being said, media starting these narratives, it can mess up a locker room. You definitely don't want a locker room tension full of two of your best players."

**Raymond Felton:** "So you got to pick one. We all know they was going to pick **Jayson Tatum**. **Jayson Tatum** being the franchise guy since forever. So we know they was going to pick him, so you know **Jaylen Brown** is the odd man out. Great player. I would love to have him on my team."
