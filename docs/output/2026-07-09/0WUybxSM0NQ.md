# Watch Out For The Jazz - Darryn Peterson & Will Hardy Talk Raising Expectations In Utah — *NBA on ESPN*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0WUybxSM0NQ

_Speakers identified: Tim MacMahon, Darryn Peterson, Will Hardy_

**1. Will Hardy — "For us to be successful, not just he and I's relationship, but our team, we need to all see it the same way." — Will Hardy on the importance of building a shared defensive vision** [02:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0WUybxSM0NQ&t=120s)

**Tim MacMahon:** "What are you emphasizing in him during that film session?"

**Will Hardy:** "We're talking a lot about defense. We're trying to unlock some things that Darryn definitely has within him. We're just trying to make sure that we're seeing it the same way. For us to be successful, not just he and I's relationship, but our team, we need to all see it the same way."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0WUybxSM0NQ&t=120s

**2. Will Hardy — "When you watch the NBA playoffs, you need multiple ball-handlers, and I think they have a tremendous amount of potential together." — Will Hardy on the potential of a Darryn Peterson and Keyonte George backcourt** [05:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0WUybxSM0NQ&t=332s)

**Tim MacMahon:** "How do you see the dynamic with him and **Keyonte George** in the backcourt?"

**Will Hardy:** "I think it's really exciting. They're both talented players. They can both play on and off the ball. It gives us some flexibility. When you watch the NBA playoffs, you need multiple ball-handlers, and I think they have a tremendous amount of potential together, both sides of the ball."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0WUybxSM0NQ&t=332s

**3. Will Hardy — "Darryn represents all the things that we want to represent as an organization to our fans, to our community." — Will Hardy on Darryn Peterson's character and future potential** [07:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0WUybxSM0NQ&t=450s)

**Tim MacMahon:** "What excites you most about him?"

**Will Hardy:** "He's obviously a very talented player and that gets talked about a lot, but getting to know him, his demeanor, his willingness to learn, be coached—obviously I think he can get so much better. But the way that he goes about it every day, the way he carries himself, I think Darryn represents all the things that we want to represent as an organization to our fans, to our community. I think he's the type of person that we want in our organization and that's just as important as the type of player that you are."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0WUybxSM0NQ&t=450s

**4. Will Hardy — "The quicker I can understand how he sees it and the quicker he can understand how I see it, it's just going to help us communicate." — Will Hardy on the necessity of frequent one-on-one film sessions** [10:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0WUybxSM0NQ&t=654s)

**Tim MacMahon:** "How often do you anticipate the one-on-one film sessions?"

**Will Hardy:** "Frequently. I think it's going to be a big part of the early part of our relationship. I think the quicker I can understand how he sees it and the quicker he can understand how I see it, it's just going to help us communicate when there's 18,000 fans screaming during the game and it's harder to have a controlled conversation."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0WUybxSM0NQ&t=654s
