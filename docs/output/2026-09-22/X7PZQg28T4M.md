**3. Kendrick Perkins — "I think that when you add Kawhi Leonard, one, you bring the defensive prowess up." — Kawhi Leonard's impact on Raptors team dynamics and defensive identity** [03:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X7PZQg28T4M&t=195s)

Kendrick Perkins: "They needed that push to jump in Toronto. They had the moments where it was like, what team are we watching? Are they the scary team or is Atlanta the scary team? They had those moments and I think that when you add **Kawhi Leonard**, one, you bring the defensive prowess up, but I think that you change the tilt. A lot of the tilt that happened that kind of screwed them up when they had BI operating in those spots, he was just a little young, wasn't having as good of a shooting night. I think Kawhi fixes a lot of that for them and if they were already scary with Scottie Barnes in the league, they are going to be jumping."

**4. Richard Jefferson — "115 million dollars for a player with those durability issues in his mid-30s, that is a massive contract." — Richard Jefferson questions the risk of Kawhi Leonard's contract extension** [03:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X7PZQg28T4M&t=233s)

Richard Jefferson: "Getting **Kawhi Leonard** raises the ceiling to championship possibilities for Toronto. Let's not act like this is not a massive risk. This is a guy who has played 60 plus games three times in the last nine years. Now, one of those, 60 on the nose, was in Toronto and they led him to a title, but 115 million dollars for a player with those durability issues in his mid-30s, that is a massive contract. That's not a discount deal. A discount deal is **Kevin Durant**, two-year 90 million dollar extension. This is 25 million more than that."
