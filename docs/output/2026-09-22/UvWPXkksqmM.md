**Kendrick Perkins:** "Are you a roach or a moth? And that's really it, right? When the lights get bright, do you go towards it, or when the lights get bright do you go high underneath the fridge? There's a lot of roaches in the league, and you know why they play good during the regular season? They are on boo-boo ass teams playing in front of 5,000 people like you're at some weird mid-major. When the playoffs happen, you're good and you're the only game that everyone is watching for the most part. So everything about your craft that you've been working on is seen. Are you ready for that moment? Are you fucking scared? Are you a roach?"

**Kendrick Perkins:** "A coach told me that, and he said be a moth. Be ready to die to go into that light. So yeah, it got me hyped, but hey, it changed my whole attitude, because at the end of the day, you got to go down swinging."

**5. Richard Jefferson — "My rookie year, I'm 21 years old, we go to the finals, lose to the Lakers, we get swept." — Richard Jefferson on the pressure of being a starter at 22** [06:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UvWPXkksqmM&t=365s)

Richard Jefferson: "We know the stars that have won championships, they've proven. It's the players that can play around those stars that dictated. So now I'm starting at 22 years old and the standard is the NBA finals. So if we lose in the second round, it's like we shouldn't have traded Keith, we shouldn't have made this trade. This young player, if I got to the second round and my eyes got too big, if I got to the conference finals and my eyes were like, oh, what's going on?
