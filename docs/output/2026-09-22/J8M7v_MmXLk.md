**4. Anfernee Simons — "Until you become that, you need to learn how to be able to play a role." — Anfernee Simons on embracing a sixth-man role and professional growth** [03:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J8M7v_MmXLk&t=221s)

Anfernee Simons: "My mindset going into the game is I've already competed at a high level with starters, and it's going to be even easier for me when I come into the game playing against second units. There's a lot of guys that helped me along the way, whether it was **Norm Powell**, **CJ McCollum**, those guys that didn't necessarily come into the league starting. **Damian Lillard** came into the league and immediately he was a starter and he became an All-Star pretty quickly. But for guys like that, they had to work their way up. At an early age, I kind of gravitated to them as well because I was in the same position where I might have a lot of potential coming in, I might not be playing, I might be a couple years out from really contributing. **Rodney Hood**, **Evan Turner**, those guys helped me understand what it took to be a real role player. They always felt like you're a starter, but for right now, until you become that, you need to learn how to be able to play a role and do it well."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J8M7v_MmXLk&t=221s

**5. Vince Carter — "Marvin Williams, I'll give you a name who was a sixth man in Atlanta." — Vince Carter on Marvin Williams's transition to a sixth-man role** [05:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J8M7v_MmXLk&t=336s)

Vince Carter: "**Marvin Williams**, I'll give you a name who was a sixth man in Atlanta, ended up being a top three pick because they saw his potential. Can you take what is given to you and buy in and make it work? And there's a lot of guys who's done that. And it's been proven over the years. Look at **Mano Ginobili**. **Mano Ginobili** is probably one of the best sixth men of all times. I mean we can name others, of course, **Jamal Crawford**, you know, but those are guys that, yeah, we rely on this guy just like we rely on our starters. He's our sixth starter."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J8M7v_MmXLk&t=336s

**6. Tracy McGrady — "Before you walk through these doors, drop your ego. Don't bring that in here." — Tracy McGrady on the importance of checking your ego in the NBA** [06:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J8M7v_MmXLk&t=403s)

Tracy McGrady: "Before you walk through these doors, drop your ego. Don't bring that in here. You got to understand that that's the role that's given to you. It may not be what you want, but this is the sacrifices that has happened to certain players where I feel like I'm a starter, but damn, we don't need you to play that role. We need you to play this role right here and you have to be able to adapt to that. It's a mind fuck for a lot of cats because they can't do that, they can't make that change. But that's why you see guys can't last in this league, man, because they think there's something else that a team might not be conducive to how they play basketball. If you can't adapt to that role, you can't last. It's as simple as that."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J8M7v_MmXLk&t=403s

**7. Anfernee Simons — "I want to walk through any door in any organization and be the last organization to say, man, he handled this stuff well." — Anfernee Simons on maintaining professionalism and confidence after being traded** [09:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J8M7v_MmXLk&t=541s)

Anfernee Simons: "I understood the trade. Obviously, everybody can get emotional behind it and ask the question why they don't want me no more, but you got to understand in the business side from that trade it was pretty much self-explanatory. They needed a big, obviously **JT** ended up coming back, and so that's what they felt the need that they felt like they needed. It made sense. I couldn't be completely mad. Of course, I'm going to be mad because I wanted to play in the playoffs. But you understood, and those type of situations shouldn't affect your confidence to yourself and if it affect your professionalism. I always felt like first, I want to be able to be professional. I want to walk through the door, walk through any door in any organization and be the last organization to say, man, he handled this stuff well. He's a pro's pro, and that just spreads around the league."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J8M7v_MmXLk&t=541s
