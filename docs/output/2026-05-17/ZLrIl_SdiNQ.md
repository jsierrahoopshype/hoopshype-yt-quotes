**1. Chandler Parsons — Chandler Parsons' initial knee injury against Indiana and diagnosis**
<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZLrIl_SdiNQ&t=13s" target="_blank" rel="noopener">00:13</a>

"The first knee injury I ever had, we were playing in Indiana. I remember **David West** had these baggy shorts, and I went up for a layup and my foot got caught in his shorts and I hit the ground really, really hard on the inside of my knee. I was playing so good that night, like 30-something points, and I was cooking. I fell and I used to wear these long tights, like the long leggings, and I remember I ripped the legging and my knee was killing me. We had played OKC on a back-to-back the next night. I used to put the Normatec on. We fly to OKC and the same thing again. I'm just hoping, I'm just in the zone, adrenaline's running. I feel nothing. I just remember after that back-to-back, I wake up the next day and I can't move, I can't walk. I went and got an MRI and from that fall, I had like a dime-sized piece of cartilage missing from the inside of my knee. And that's when they were trying to give me all my options and they said, 'You're going to have to have surgery, but if you can just rest for two, three weeks.' This is right before the playoffs start. 'Rest for two or three weeks, strengthen your quads, your glutes, your hamstrings. This is not going to get worse.' So, dime-sized piece of cartilage, not going to get worse."

**2. Chandler Parsons — Chandler Parsons' re-injury in playoffs required microfracture surgery**
<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZLrIl_SdiNQ&t=13s" target="_blank" rel="noopener">00:13</a>

"Three weeks later, we're on the Mavs, we get Houston first round. I had just left Houston. I'm like, there's no way I'm not playing in this series. So I do the proper rehab, I get it. Game 1, I remember the second play of the game, I go up, I dunk, I land and I just feel something in my knee just pop. And I knew it was so bad. I go, I get an MRI that dime-sized piece of cartilage that the doctor told me was not going to get worse, went to a quarter-sized piece of cartilage, which now required microfracture surgery."

**5. Chandler Parsons — Chandler Parsons explains his free agency decision between Orlando, Portland, and Memphis**
<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZLrIl_SdiNQ&t=324s" target="_blank" rel="noopener">05:24</a>

"Then after this, that happens, and that's where this whole misconception of like, oh, he didn't know about the injuries or they didn't know about the injuries. They fully knew about the injuries. The only thing they didn't do in that free agency meeting was it was coming down to Orlando, Portland, and Memphis. They all offered me the same amount of years and same money. And Portland had just gone through **Brandon Roy** and **Greg Oden** and all these injured guys where they were like on the fence. Orlando was my hometown, they weren't very good. I think it might even been like a three-year deal with a four-year team option. And then there was Memphis, who on paper, I was the perfect fit. You guys had this culture here. You guys were missing that shooting wing that can fill the gaps between you guys and **Mike** and **Marc**."
