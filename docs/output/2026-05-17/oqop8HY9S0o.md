**2. Kendrick Perkins — Kendrick Perkins believes Luka Doncic can improve his conditioning like Paul Pierce did**
<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oqop8HY9S0o&t=79s" target="_blank" rel="noopener">01:19</a>

"You're never gonna just get ripped up, right? In order for **Paul** to make the run that he did and go capture a championship and Finals MVP, before then, I had spent four years with **Paul**. One of those years we were trash, the other years we were in the thick of things still making the postseason. I've never seen **Paul** condition like he did in my life. After games in the regular season, wind sprint bike with that fan on. Getting to practice extra early, I'm watching him run them sprints on that treadmill with a level on 15 incline. Because he was building himself up for the long haul. And guess what? By the time we got to the Finals, his body was so conditioned that he was able to go toe-to-toe with **Kobe**. I'm not taking away from **Luka**'s greatness, but I do believe that **Luka** can take it up another notch, his body-wise, condition-wise. I do, because us wag-body guys, we have to be very careful and we got to be on point with our stuff so much because we prone to have these soft tissue injuries. We just not built like a **LeBron James**."
