_Speakers identified: Chandler Parsons_

**1. Chandler Parsons — Chandler Parsons recounts horrific car accident and severe injuries**
<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1-yDI1XlmSI&t=0s" target="_blank" rel="noopener">00:00</a>

"The accident was crazy. I was driving home from practice in Atlanta and I was on a feeder road getting on the highway and just got blindsided, knocked unconscious, real bad. I remember I woke up and I couldn't move my neck. I tore my labrum. I had herniated two disks in my back. I had traumatic brain injury, which is scary enough even just talking about brain."

**2. Chandler Parsons — Chandler Parsons describes legal threats and other driver's fault**
<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1-yDI1XlmSI&t=0s" target="_blank" rel="noopener">00:00</a>

"I remember I went back to the hospital. I go there and they're like, 'Hey, you need to lawyer up. The guy's in a coma that hit you. Once he figures out who you are, what you were driving, he's going to sue you.' And I'm like, 'The fuck you at me?' The next day, you find out this dude, it was his third DUI. He had a bottle of Titos in the passenger seat and he was just fully drunk and was obviously at fault."

**3. Chandler Parsons — Chandler Parsons' difficult choice to end NBA career**
<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1-yDI1XlmSI&t=0s" target="_blank" rel="noopener">00:00</a>

"I remember I got in this thing where it was an insurance policy lawsuit to where I still can't really talk about it today, but I basically had to make a decision to settle with the insurance company and never play in the NBA again or not and try and play with everything that happened with my knees, one or two more years. That was kind of it. That was right during COVID, so it was a weird time. It was the bubble year and Atlanta didn't make the bubble, so our season was done. I basically did the lawsuit and that was kind of a wrap on the NBA."
