# Amar’e Stoudemire On Making The Hall Of Fame, Knicks Era & 2007 Suns Suspension — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3sP3z6VHJTs

_Speakers identified: Vince Carter, Tracy McGrady, Amar’e Stoudemire_

**1. Amar’e Stoudemire — "I was driving and I got a phone call from one of the board members of the Hall of Fame." — Amar’e Stoudemire on the surreal experience of being inducted into the Hall of Fame** [00:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3sP3z6VHJTs&t=31s)

Amar’e Stoudemire: "I was driving. I was here in Miami and I was on my way, I was going somewhere, I forgot where I was going, but I was driving and I got a phone call from one of the board members of the Hall of Fame and they were saying, I got some good news for you. And they were saying you're going to be inducted into the 2026 Naismith Basketball Hall of Fame. And I was like, what? You know what I'm saying? I was out here, I was excited. I started calling my mom, I called my family. They all were excited about it. It was like a surreal moment."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3sP3z6VHJTs&t=31s

**2. Amar’e Stoudemire — "It's a similar moment as getting that call from the Hall of Fame." — Comparing the feeling of being drafted to being inducted into the Hall of Fame** [01:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3sP3z6VHJTs&t=92s)

Amar’e Stoudemire: "It's around the same to be quite frank with you because when I was drafted, that moment is like, all right, well, I made it, right? Out of all the hard work and the path it took to get to the NBA wasn't an easy path. And then to get that call or to get that announcement on stage with the ninth pick, it's a similar moment as getting that call from the Hall of Fame. It's like, oh snap, this is real. You know what I'm saying? And so I would say it's right up there close to being the same feeling as I got when I was drafted."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3sP3z6VHJTs&t=92s

**3. Amar’e Stoudemire — "To see that you were able to accomplish what you accomplished from high school gave me the inspiration." — How Tracy McGrady’s success inspired Amar’e Stoudemire’s own career path** [02:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3sP3z6VHJTs&t=164s)

Amar’e Stoudemire: "When I was in high school, I was a freshman at Lake Wales High School and I knew I had potential to do something in basketball. I knew I had a chance to hopefully make it to the NBA. At that time you are dreaming to make it to the NBA. I was 15 years old and I started hanging around the wrong crowd and I was like, no, I'm starting to go down the wrong path. So I went to my coach, Coach Hayes at the time, who was the JV coach, and I said, Coach Hayes, I got to get out of here. I can't stick around. Because Lake Wales is a small town and you got to try to change the environment. And so Coach Hayes said, well, I got two options for you. I got Oak Hill Academy or Mount Zion. So I said, well, what's up with Oak Hill Academy? He said, well, Oak Hill Academy is an all-boys school. Why, I ain't going there. And then he said, well, Mount Zion is what **Tracy McGrady** went. So I said, Tracy, then T-Mac went out of high school? He said, yeah, he went out of high school. I said, well, that's why, that's why I want to go. You know what I'm saying? Because T-Mac, you paved the way for that, bro. So for me to see that you was able to accomplish what you accomplished from high school gave me the inspiration that I can try to do the same thing."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3sP3z6VHJTs&t=164s

**4. Amar’e Stoudemire — "I saw my former coach Mike D'Antoni as the head coach in New York." — Amar’e Stoudemire on his decision to sign with the New York Knicks** [05:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3sP3z6VHJTs&t=338s)

Amar’e Stoudemire: "When I looked at free agency, I saw my former coach **Mike D'Antoni** as the head coach in New York and I figured, at least he knows my game and I can be my complete self if I go to New York. And I was raised in New York, right? I moved from Florida in the third grade and I spent from my third grade to my eighth grade year in New York. So I always had this passion for New York anyways. So I wanted to go to New York to not only display my full talent, but also to play in New York. When I first got to New York, obviously we didn't have a great team. New York was in a drought, no All-Stars, no playoffs in a decade or so. And when I got there, I was like, the Knicks are back. Everyone might have thought I was joking, but I was dead serious about what I was trying to accomplish and I was able to display my full talents."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3sP3z6VHJTs&t=338s

**5. Amar’e Stoudemire — "After I signed, we brought in Carmelo and then after Melo we brought in more players." — The impact of Carmelo Anthony and Chauncey Billups on the Knicks** [07:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3sP3z6VHJTs&t=436s)

Amar’e Stoudemire: "After I signed, we brought in Carmelo and then after Melo we brought in more players and then we got **Chauncey Billups**. These guys took that energy that we started with them and then now you see what happened, right? It won a championship this year. So I feel proud of that, bro, because I felt like I was a part of that wave of the new energy in New York and I was very excited for those guys."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3sP3z6VHJTs&t=436s

**6. Amar’e Stoudemire — "To have Mike in New York at the time when I signed there was great for me." — The importance of having Mike D'Antoni as a coach in New York** [09:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3sP3z6VHJTs&t=595s)

Amar’e Stoudemire: "To have Mike in New York at the time when I signed there was great for me because he knew my game from the times we spent in Phoenix. And the goals that we had in mind there in Phoenix and he knew my leadership qualities and what I brought to the table. And so for me to go to New York and to still be coachable at that time and for him to allow me to be the leader that he know I could be for those young players. We had guys **Raymond Felton**, **Danilo Gallinari**, **Wilson Chandler**. These guys were young players that wanted to taste success and for me to be able to kind of add that leadership for them and under **Mike D'Antoni** who we both were on the same page with leadership was helpful for all of us."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3sP3z6VHJTs&t=595s

**7. Amar’e Stoudemire — "I think that was the same series when Nash got jammed up into the scoreboard." — Reflecting on the 2007 Suns-Spurs playoff series and the suspension** [11:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3sP3z6VHJTs&t=666s)

Amar’e Stoudemire: "We had a team that was cohesive and together and focused and I think that was the same series when Nash got jammed up into the scoreboard and I think I stepped on the basketball court and so did Boris Diaw, we got suspended. And at that time, we didn't know the rules about the stepping on the court thing. And I felt like it was a harsh call to suspend us. They could have gave us a warning, man, I think that still would have held heavy weight when it came to like setting the tone for guys not stepping on the basketball court. I think they wanted to suspend us and say, hey, this is what happens when you do it and make like an example out of us. But I think that was a little bit too harsh of a penalty because we both competing for a championship in the playoffs. But that year was a year I think was a year we could have definitely won a championship because we had the Spurs on the ropes, man, we had them."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3sP3z6VHJTs&t=666s
