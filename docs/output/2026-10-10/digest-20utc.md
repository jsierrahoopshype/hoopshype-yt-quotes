## Bob Myers On Navigating The End Of A Dynasty & Rebuilding For The Future — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JfK7talh5aY

_Speakers identified: Tracy McGrady, Vince Carter, Bob Myers_

**1. Bob Myers — "he was good enough to help a team win, but he didn't have to put a team on his back" — Jason Kidd's role as a supporting star in Dallas with Dirk Nowitzki** [01:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JfK7talh5aY&t=68s)

**Tracy McGrady:** "I look at **Jason Kidd** in Dallas. He was at the end, but he had Dirk. And he was still **Jason Kidd**, and he was good enough to help a team win, but he didn't have to put a team on his back."

**Bob Myers:** "But you know how rare that is. And I don't remember if he took less money or how he ended up there, but there's a hard part where you two, I could ask you guys, to be who you were as players, you have to have an ego. That's fine. You have to believe you're the best."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JfK7talh5aY&t=68s

**2. Vince Carter — "It took me a while to accept the changing of the guard for me" — Vince Carter on the difficulty of transitioning to a bench role** [02:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JfK7talh5aY&t=132s)

Vince Carter: "That was the hardest transition for me. It took me a while to accept the changing of the guard for me as far as the changing of the role. And then, as I became a six-man and a role player, the diminishing minutes to the back end of my career, loving the game of basketball was more important than anything else. If I'm not playing this game, what would I be doing myself? The towel wasn't completely bone dry. So, I'm like, all right, you have to figure out how to exist. You have to buy in."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JfK7talh5aY&t=132s

**3. Bob Myers — "Trading Andre Iguodala, who had won three championships with the team, to sign D'Angelo Russell" — Bob Myers on the difficulty of trading Andre Iguodala to Memphis** [06:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JfK7talh5aY&t=362s)

Bob Myers: "Trading **Andre Iguodala**, who had won three championships with the team, in 2019 to sign **D'Angelo Russell**. The way he took it, the professionalism, made it, as far as a player handling a tough thing, I've never seen one do it as well as he did in that moment. To call a guy that, when you talk about personal, making it personal, and to say we are trading you to Memphis, and he knew it was not a team that was trying to win. By the way, he ended up going to the Heat and going to the finals again after that. But Andre looked at me and he said, I understand. You got to do what you got to do. And it wasn't a bitter, I mean, of course I'm sure he felt a certain way, but it wasn't like it wasn't fair. It wasn't right. When you do things where a player does so much for you and your organization, and you make a decision where he deserved better."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JfK7talh5aY&t=362s

**4. Bob Myers — "I would walk in the locker room before a playoff game and I would look around at my teammates" — Bob Myers on the importance of having players who want to win** [09:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JfK7talh5aY&t=582s)

**Bob Myers:** "I always would walk in the locker room before a playoff game and I would look around and you'd pass like four guys and you'd stop on one guy and you'd go, I'm glad he's going to die out here to win. As many of those guys as you can get, that gives you the best shot. When I was with the Warriors, I'd stop at like Klay, Steph, Draymond, **Shaun Livingston**, all these guys. It was easy."

**Vince Carter:** "I already know. These guys are already."

**Bob Myers:** "We were down 10 at the half in elimination games at halftime against Houston. And I walk in there and look around and I was as an executive, you're so helpless. I can't play. If we're down 10 and you guys, I go, Vince, Tracy, we're fucked. And you go, relax. I go, relax? How the fuck? You go, we got this. And I go, what do you got? Why, if you got it so good, why are we down 10 at half? There's a feel from the flow of the first half. You know how they're playing. You know the necessary adjustments you need to make, and then you tell yourself, you're going to play better in the second half."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JfK7talh5aY&t=582s
