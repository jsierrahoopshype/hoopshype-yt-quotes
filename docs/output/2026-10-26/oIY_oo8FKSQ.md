**1. Jay King — "Jason is with us now, Jason is the one we're going to focus on, and Jason is our guy." — Joe Mazzulla's perspective on the Jaylen Brown and Jayson Tatum dynamic** [01:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oIY_oo8FKSQ&t=94s)

**Wosny Lambre:** "I was reading your latest piece up on The Athletic and you guys talked to **Joe Mazzulla** about the sniping that's been going on back and forth. I want to read some of these Mazzulla quotes to you guys. Quote: 'These two guys, they were together for nine seasons and they had great, great moments together. And at the end of the day, nobody is perfect. We can all get better. We all did great things. We all did things that we want to be better at. So I think it's more just focusing on the greatness that those two were able to achieve and the fact that they're both great people.' You're a Mazzulla-ologist, Jay. What are we supposed to glean from that?"

**Jay King:** "I think the more interesting part was what he said next, which was that they're both very different people, they act differently, they operate differently. And then he said basically, as much as I appreciate what Jaylen did for us, Jason is with us now, Jason is the one we're going to focus on, and Jason is our guy. And I think that's where he stands on it. He's like, you know what, they can do whatever they want to do together, but at this point, I'm the coach of the Boston Celtics. Jaylen's not on my team anymore, and I love the guy, I respect him, I think he's a great person, but also, to me, that was kind of like flip the middle finger, and we're moving on with our guy Jason."

And so, yeah, I thought that was like, I don't know if it was intentional, probably wasn't intentional, but like, it was kind of cutting, cutting Jaylen where, where it would probably hurt after, he thought he proved himself last year as capable of being the guy for Boston. Obviously they traded him right after that, and now, now there are a lot of raw emotions, and so it's, it's funny because there were three years in a row when the Celtics were just robots, that no drama, and now, now it's, it's drama. **3. Jay King — "Jayson Tatum has not been one of those stars who wants to have a hand in everything." — Jayson Tatum's involvement in Celtics front office roster decisions** [07:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oIY_oo8FKSQ&t=465s)

**Wosny Lambre:** "Does it ring true to you, the idea that **Jayson Tatum** was caught way off guard when **Jaylen Brown** got traded? I found out just like the rest of you guys. I had no idea anything like this was in the works. Does that have even a morsel of truth to it to you?"

**Jay King:** "By the time Jaylen got traded, it was already in the media that he was being shopped. It was not clear that he was definitely getting traded, but I think everybody knew at that point there was a real chance that Jaylen got traded. That said, Jayson has not been one of those stars who wants to have a hand in everything in an organization. In the past, they traded **Javonte Green**, his closest friend in Boston. He has said before how he does not really want to be involved in stuff like that and just trusts Brad. I do believe he was not fully advocating for Jaylen to go."

**4. Jay King — "Jaylen probably just never felt appropriately valued by Boston. I'm talking about never." — Historical friction regarding Jaylen Brown's valuation by the Celtics** [07:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oIY_oo8FKSQ&t=465s)

Jay King: "It goes back a long way to where Jaylen probably just never felt appropriately valued by Boston. I'm talking about never. So his second year, he starts on a team that goes to the Eastern Conference Finals Game 7, loses to LeBron in Game 7, and he played great that whole run. He comes back the next year, **Jayson Tatum** is starting, and he gets moved to the bench. He was probably like, what the hell? Why am I the one who gets moved to the bench? I think there were just a bunch of times when the way he saw himself was not the way the team saw him. In 2022, they go to the Finals, then they have talks about trading for **Kevin Durant**. Those become public. So he has got to be like, what the hell? That summer, **Kevin Durant** and **Jayson Tatum** were working out together. I think along the way there was a lot of stuff that Jaylen moved past."
