**5. Jon Krawczynski — "I think that what he wants to do is be multifaceted as an offensive player." — Jaden McDaniels developing his playmaking and offensive versatility** [05:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9KD55W_bNnQ&t=307s)

**Jim Souhan:** "Is there a skill that **Jaden McDaniels** lacks or needs to develop more, or is it just a matter of opportunity at this point?"

**Jon Krawczynski:** "Yeah, I mean, I do think what he's talked about and what he worked on this summer a lot was playmaking. Not just being a scorer, not just being able to take maybe the weakest defender on the floor and back him down and rise up over him and shoot, but also getting to be a player that has to be a threat in multiple ways on offense. And we're seeing him start to look for **Rudy Gobert** on the short roll, kind of near the rim. We're seeing him try to move the ball with a little bit more purpose. And so I think that what he wants to do is be multifaceted as an offensive player. And I think a lot of times for a player in his role, you say, okay, he's going to be the third, fourth option, the ball is going to swing to him later in the clock after the defense has really keyed on **Anthony Edwards** and **LaMelo Ball** and then he's just going to rise up and take the shot or make a play for himself. But he seems to be focused on trying to make plays for others as well and trying to be more of a full part and component of this offense from a passing standpoint, from a cutting standpoint, and just being kind of like everything that you need an offensive player to be. And we saw him take huge strides last season, especially in the playoffs against Denver, of being a real weapon. And now, if he can kind of just get himself into the flow of the half-court offense where he is moving it just as much as he is benefiting from that movement, I think it's going to be a really big thing for what this offense can do."
