## Tina Thompson On Building The WNBA & Becoming The First No. 1 Draft Pick — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V6XhzXd5q_c

_Speakers identified: Tracy McGrady, Tina Thompson, Vince Carter_

**1. Tina Thompson — "You guys are rewriting history because in those moments, we were not sacrificial lambs. We were happy." — Tina Thompson on the WNBA's inaugural season and player sacrifices** [00:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V6XhzXd5q_c&t=46s)

Tina Thompson: "The thing is that it's a very interesting dynamic because now when you talk to everyone post our experience and now you're seeing what the ladies of the WNBA are doing now and how they're setting an incredible standard for the generations to come behind, everybody uses the word sacrifice. Oh, we sacrificed so much. And for me, it's like, you guys are rewriting history because in those moments, we were not sacrificial lambs. We were happy. All those players, when I came to the WNBA, it's our inaugural season, I was probably one of a handful of players that were college drafted players. Everybody else on the team were veteran players who had extensive WNBA careers. **Cynthia Cooper** was my teammate. Cynthia had played more years professionally than I had played basketball in my entire life."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V6XhzXd5q_c&t=46s

**2. Tina Thompson — "The goal for us was to create a foundation of longevity so that little girls could come." — Tina Thompson on the responsibility of establishing WNBA longevity** [02:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V6XhzXd5q_c&t=131s)

Tina Thompson: "We did have a responsibility because the goal for us was to create a foundation of longevity so that little girls could come and play behind us. I played 17 of those seasons. My first, I played 17 seasons. I retired in 2013 in Seattle. It was kind of one of those things where you have so much gratitude and you have a responsibility because at that time there had been not one professional women's league in America that had been successful. So we wanted to be the success."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V6XhzXd5q_c&t=131s

**3. Tina Thompson — "He birthed it, it was his idea and he created a model that allowed us." — Tina Thompson credits Commissioner Stern for the WNBA's creation** [03:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V6XhzXd5q_c&t=192s)

Tina Thompson: "We definitely have to credit **Commissioner Stern**, rest in peace, of his vision of creating a league like for women. I mean, he birthed it, it was his idea and he created a model that allowed us to kind of have that opportunity and that foundation."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V6XhzXd5q_c&t=192s

**4. Tina Thompson — "I learned at a very young age that pressure is self-imposed. So I really don't feel pressure." — Tina Thompson on self-imposed pressure and professional responsibility** [05:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V6XhzXd5q_c&t=336s)

Tina Thompson: "Me personally, I learned at a very young age that pressure is self-imposed. So I really don't feel pressure. I do believe that there is a responsibility because I feel like when you properly prepare, then pressure is just kind of a word. I was a worker. I am a worker. All I did was work. So in the moments where I had to be the best version of myself, I did so because my preparation worked, allowed me to have a place of comfort and peace. But I would say that there was a huge responsibility because if we did not get it right, then it would be very hard to replicate that moment."

Tina Thompson: "You look at us back then and we were a player's league. I could easily say that we were blue collar in the sense that we were very fan-friendly. We were doing appearances and doing events and making sure that people showed up. We kept the conversation going like, 'Hey, have you heard about us?' We were the doers. You got to get out and campus, baby."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V6XhzXd5q_c&t=426s

**6. Tina Thompson — "There was an allocation draft like in a sense where there was this kind of initial draft." — Tina Thompson explains the inaugural WNBA allocation draft process** [09:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V6XhzXd5q_c&t=546s)
