# We Come Up With Some Season-Saving Moves For the Thunder | Chris Mannix & Evan Turner — *Sports Illustrated*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wjd6tjvkeo8

_Speakers identified: Chris Mannix, Evan Turner_

**1. Chris Mannix — "I can't fathom a scenario where he comes back from that type of injury." — Jalen Williams' injury timeline and the Thunder's playoff outlook** [00:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wjd6tjvkeo8&t=22s)

Chris Mannix: "We saw **Jalen Williams** had the surgery. I found it interesting that they said he'd be re-evaluated before the end of the season. I can't fathom a scenario where he comes back from that type of injury. That was a **Gordon Hayward** type of injury. And even the dislocation, the fracture, you don't drop a guy like that back into a playoff situation. Are you crazy? I'm not sure why, maybe they did that for **Jalen Williams**, but there's no way **Jalen Williams** comes back."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wjd6tjvkeo8&t=22s

**2. Chris Mannix — "Make a fucking deal, man. Make a deal, fellas. You got to make it happen." — How aggressive should the Thunder be in the trade market?** [01:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wjd6tjvkeo8&t=83s)

**Chris Mannix:** "In your opinion, how aggressive does Oklahoma City need to be over the next couple of months? I'll put it this way: one being no big deal, you got enough pieces as it is, or 10, make a fucking deal, man. Make a deal, fellas. You got to make it happen."

**Evan Turner:** "I'm 50/50 on it because I still want to see how they continue to trend up. Because I do believe you're still lacking in size, but you still got some pretty good pieces with what you have."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wjd6tjvkeo8&t=83s

**3. Evan Turner — "I keep screaming this dude's name and it's terrible to keep saying it, but it's like, all right, go get Zach LaVine." — The Thunder's need for a star like Zach LaVine** [02:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wjd6tjvkeo8&t=175s)

Evan Turner: "I don't know where you would go to make that trade that would stop the bleeding. Do you try to go back and get Wiggins? Do you try to go back and get Dort? Or do you ask guys to step up and you have enough time for the Hartensteins to try to implement themselves, the Bennett Sturts to try to figure out whatever occurs? But I think you still have an All-NBA player in **Chet Holmgren**. You still have a solid big man in **Isaiah Hartenstein**. And then you also just have the reigning MVP. I think you have enough to try to carry you defensively, but they're a system team. I think they can step up. I just don't know what they would get in return that would definitely help and kind of stop the bleeding. You need another star. I keep screaming this dude's name and it's terrible to keep saying it, but it's like, all right, go get **Zach LaVine**. He's going to get you 20."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wjd6tjvkeo8&t=175s

**4. Chris Mannix — "Brian Windhorst kind of did this thing where he said, 'Don't be surprised if they make a run at somebody they've already had.'" — Brian Windhorst's comments on potential Thunder trade targets** [03:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wjd6tjvkeo8&t=206s)

Chris Mannix: "**Brian Windhorst** kind of did this thing where he said, 'Don't be surprised if they make a run at somebody they've already had,' which basically means Wiggins or Dort. They're not going to get **Isaiah Joe** back; that doesn't solve their problem."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wjd6tjvkeo8&t=206s

**5. Chris Mannix — "Is there a way with the right draft capital you could pry Mikal Bridges loose?" — Could the Thunder trade for Mikal Bridges?** [06:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wjd6tjvkeo8&t=382s)

**Chris Mannix:** "I'm looking at New York right now, and I'm wondering, is there a way with the right draft capital you could pry **Mikal Bridges** loose? Bridges, I don't think he's playing great to start the season either. Is there a way? You can see how it would make sense. It would help get them to the finals, help the Knicks win a championship."

**Evan Turner:** "That happens. The fifth man is going to swing up and down."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wjd6tjvkeo8&t=382s

**6. Evan Turner — "I believe Jalen Williams, we don't give him enough credit, but he's been balling." — Evaluating Jalen Williams' impact and potential fit in Atlanta** [08:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wjd6tjvkeo8&t=517s)

Evan Turner: "I believe **Jalen Williams**, we don't give him enough credit, but he's been balling. He's been balling last year in the playoffs. His jump shot has continued to develop. But on the court, he's been physical, he's been anchoring that interior defense even just as a brawling bully. So I believe he would fit well in Atlanta and I think it will both help them. You get rid of your third big to move up your young guy and then bring back a piece that they might not want."
