So now that you start thinking as the Thunder and **Sam Presti**, like is he the one we try and get off of? We're going to have to pay Mitchell, we're going to have to pay **Jared McCain**. This opens up the door for Bennett Durz, who I love, the rookie from Iowa. He's going to get some minutes now. **Jared McCain** has been great. I still think they're going to be fine because I think their talent level, I think SGA can carry them to a top two seed even without those guys."

**3. DeMarcus Cousins — "the gift that they do have is the depth of that team" — DeMarcus Cousins on Thunder's depth and Nikola Topic's potential** [03:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JYcy_U_sIeY&t=206s)

DeMarcus Cousins: "Injuries suck as an individual, you don't want to see anybody go through that. Him missing so much time last year, putting in all the work to return, you can see it on his face, it was just a look of defeat. I can't believe this is happening again. I know the situation, I know the feelings, so you have to feel for the guy. But moving forward as far as OKC, and even with **Cason Wallace**, it sucks. Thank God he was able to sign this extension, get his money. But when it comes to OKC, the gift that they do have is the depth of that team. You can see **Alex Caruso** easily sliding into that **Cason Wallace** type of role. Even the **Jared McCain** can easily slide into that role. As far as **Jalen Williams**, that's a big piece that's missing for them. He's obviously their clear-cut second option, All-Star, All-NBA type of player, so it's hard to replace that. But one of the luxuries of OKC is the draft picks they do have and also like I said, the depth. They got a kid by the name of **Nikola Topic** who his career started off early, he went through a cancer diagnosis, I believe, overcame that. But this is a super, super talented kid that hasn't really got that opportunity yet by being so far and going through his health complications. I can see him kind of sliding into that role and creating a name for himself."
