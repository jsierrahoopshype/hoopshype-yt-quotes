**2. Channing Frye — "If I believe that Anthony Edwards is my guy, think about what he has done since he has been there." — Minnesota Timberwolves' need to build around Anthony Edwards** [04:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gtmhdTHz30k&t=255s)

He is good for the culture, good for the city of Minnesota. He gets you on TV. He is allowing other players to develop also, like **Jaden McDaniels**. To me, you have to keep giving yourself a chance. If you don't like **Rudy Gobert**, give me somebody else like that. If you don't like whoever is going to play the four now, you can plug and play a power forward or even another bigger wing."

**3. Channing Frye — "Energy can't be destroyed. So if something traumatic happens somewhere and it has nowhere to go, what happens?" — Channing Frye's theory on ghosts and spiritual energy** [05:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gtmhdTHz30k&t=343s)

Channing Frye: "Energy can't be destroyed. So if something traumatic happens somewhere and it has nowhere to go, what happens to it? I think as human beings, we are electrical human beings. If anybody has ever gone into a house and been like, this place has bad juju, and then you find out something happened in the house, that is what I think on ghosts and spirits. I am a Christian, I believe in **Jesus**, but I also believe that at times, people live certain ways, either really good or really bad on this earth, and that leaves a certain trail of energy. The more you are tapped into that, the more you allow the good stuff to come in and the bad stuff to stay away from you. Always be aware of your energy."

**4. Channing Frye — "We wanted to prove that we could still go to the playoffs without LeBron." — The 2019 Cavaliers' goal to prove they could succeed without LeBron James** [06:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gtmhdTHz30k&t=399s)

Channing Frye: "After I got traded, went to the Lakers, I thought I was going to be on the Lakers and that just didn't work out. I had two choices in 2019: go to the Toronto Raptors, which they ended up winning that year, or go back to Cleveland. The big thing when I was talking with **Richard Jefferson**, Kevin, Tristan, and **Kyle Korver** was, hey, we wanted to prove that we could still go to the playoffs without LeBron. That we were still a good team. **George Hill** was on that team too. That was exciting to me. Those are my brothers. The city of Cleveland is always going to be a special place in my heart."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gtmhdTHz30k&t=399s

**5. Channing Frye — "I was a raw talent. Coach O was like, we are going to make you just this." — Channing Frye's development as a shooter in Phoenix** [11:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gtmhdTHz30k&t=678s)

Channing Frye: "I was not always a three-point shooter. I only played two years of high school basketball because I broke my hand my sophomore year. In Arizona, the junior high I went to was 7-8-9. My development was very quick. When I went to UVA, I was a raw talent. Coach O was like, we are going to make you just this. So I became a post-up player. I would shoot mid-range, but he was not a believer in me shooting threes at all. Could I shoot? Probably, but it wasn't the style back then. In the early 2000s, it was not the style. In New York, I was a center. My second year in New York, they started to make me play out by the threes, so I had to learn. I had been working on that for three years, and **Alvin Gentry** and **Steve Kerr** saw that I had been working on it. **Nate McMillan**, **Isaiah Thomas**, and **Larry Brown** were not giving me the green light. I had a set amount of shots I could get because everything was a play, whereas in Phoenix, everything was based on defense."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gtmhdTHz30k&t=678s

**6. Channing Frye — "When we were in NBA Finals or even in the playoffs, when we were at home, that place was rocking." — The impact of home court advantage and silencing opposing crowds** [14:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gtmhdTHz30k&t=870s)

Channing Frye: "It matters immensely. When we were in NBA Finals or even in the playoffs, when we were at home, that place was rocking. The adrenaline never stops. When you are on the road, you have to create adrenaline. When you are at home, the crowd feels your adrenaline and they keep it and they raise it up. You are sleeping in your own bed, you are eating your own food, you are going through your own routine. It is a huge factor. I used to love silencing a crowd and talking crap. I just pick one person and tell them to shut the hell up. It was the best."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gtmhdTHz30k&t=870s

**7. Channing Frye — "If you watch this playoffs, hand-checking is back. The amount of physicality that they let these boys play with." — The return of physicality in the NBA playoffs** [15:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gtmhdTHz30k&t=943s)

Channing Frye: "I think if you watch this playoffs, hand-checking is back. The amount of physicality that they let these boys play with. You look at the ratings, you look at the scores. You can't bring back hand-checking for 82 games, but you can allow the play to be more physical each and every year as these young players start to get better and better. Yes, hand-checking will be back, it just won't be called hand-checking."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gtmhdTHz30k&t=943s

**8. Channing Frye — "Everyone on our team would tell you James Jones by far had the biggest impact on the team." — James Jones's leadership impact on the 2016 Cavaliers** [16:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gtmhdTHz30k&t=1015s)

Channing Frye: "Kevin is the most underrated. Kevin or Tristan was the most underrated. Kevin because of his sacrifice and Tristan because of him just knowing his role and starring in that. Everyone on our team would tell you **James Jones** by far had the biggest impact on the team as the 15th man, being able to talk to us, keep us motivated, keep it real with us, calm us down, explain the game. He was just a genius when it comes to basketball."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gtmhdTHz30k&t=1015s

**9. Channing Frye — "I had asked for a trade earlier in that year, and they told me I wasn't going to play." — Why Channing Frye didn't play in the 2009 playoffs for the Blazers** [18:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gtmhdTHz30k&t=1085s)

Channing Frye: "I had asked for a trade earlier in that year, and they told me I wasn't going to play a lot in that series. I felt like when you look at Greg, Joel, LaMarcus, where I was for the team, who I was, was not that in that style of how **Nate McMillan** wanted to play, and I wasn't mature enough to understand my situation. Now that I'm older, I was just checked out. So they were not going to put me in, because if they put me in and I do well, now you got to play me, and I'm not committed to that team next year. So they had paid **Travis Outlaw**, that's why he went in instead of me."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gtmhdTHz30k&t=1085s

**10. Channing Frye — "The most important season is the off-season because it sets the tone for the regular season." — Media patience and the importance of the off-season for development** [18:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gtmhdTHz30k&t=1138s)

Channing Frye: "No, they are just more access and opinions from everyone. So you hear what you want to hear. But if you were to take a consensus, most of the time, it is either 50/50, I believe in this guy or I don't, or it is 90 I hate him, 10 he is great. If you are a developing young player, like **Paolo Banchero**, he is a developing young player. The problem is we are so, I don't want to say we are educated about basketball, a lot of now retired guys are like, you should have worked on this thing last year or this year. And so when you are that good and you have one glaring issue, the greats should be able to see your body of work that you have done over the summer. The most important season is the off-season because it sets the tone for the regular season. For us as media, it is not that we are impatient, we just expect something different, unless you are a role player. If you are a guy like Paolo, we are just asking, what kind of jumper? Just get him. It doesn't need to be crazy. It just needs to be like, oh I can see he has worked on that. Like **Jaylen Brown**, they went at his right hand, all of a sudden now you are like, damn, **Jaylen Brown**, he could go left a little bit. Now he is probably going to work on it this summer, he is going to be even better at that."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gtmhdTHz30k&t=1138s
