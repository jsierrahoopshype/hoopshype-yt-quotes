# The Knicks embody Kobe's MAMBA MENTALITY 🐍 - Danny Green is impressed with New York | SportsCenter — *NBA on ESPN*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kEG2Qf_MSOQ

_Speakers identified: Karl-Anthony Towns, Danny Green, Tim McMahon, Michael C. Wright_

**1. Danny Green — "They're not satisfied. They're still hungry. They're taking on and embodying that Kobe Mamba mentality." — Danny Green on Knicks' Mamba mentality and hunger** [02:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kEG2Qf_MSOQ&t=133s)

**Host:** "Danny, I just went through the numbers for the Knicks, but which one is the most impressive to you?"

**Danny Green:** "All the numbers are impressive. They're doing an unbelievable job of staying focused and locked in. That's the most impressive thing to me is their mindset, right? They're not satisfied. They're still hungry. They're taking on and embodying that **Kobe** Mamba mentality, right? They flipped the switch somewhere in the playoffs, which most people don't predict. Early in the season, I was like, they don't look together, they don't look like they're cohesive. But in that Atlanta series, they had somehow find a way to flip a switch and turn it on and then have not turned it off since. And even when they're up 3-0 or up 30 points, they're not satisfied and looking forward or looking ahead like, oh, we're in the finals or we're in the championship. Their expectation is a lot higher and again, they're showing that they're hungry and not satisfied even when they're up big in games."

**2. Danny Green — "Actually, they're my favorites to win it right now. The way the West is going and how they're battling out." — Danny Green favors Knicks to win NBA Finals** [03:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kEG2Qf_MSOQ&t=189s)

**Host:** "We know what's coming up next. Quickly, how much of a chance do you give the Knicks against the **Spurs** or **Thunder** in the finals?"

**Danny Green:** "Actually, they're my favorites to win it right now. The way the West is going and how they're battling out, they're beating each other up. They don't seem 100% healthy, especially **OKC**. **San Antonio** has some injuries, some guys banged up, but they're playing on those injuries. And even through the regular season when you've seen that **San Antonio**'s matched up with the Knicks throughout the in-season cup final, they were struggling with them. That double big matchup gives **Wemby** some troubles. And when **OKC**, if they're down **J-Dub** or **AJ Mitchell**, it's hard to create offense with those guys being out. And the Knicks are so deep with **Shamet**, **Deuce McBride**, those guys come off the bench. I like the Knicks to be the favorites in this final series, whoever comes out of the West."

**3. Danny Green — "If you want to be a championship team, you have to compete with the New York Knicks." — Danny Green on Cavaliers' necessary offseason changes to compete** [03:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kEG2Qf_MSOQ&t=236s)

**Host:** "Let's get to the Cavs because they get embarrassed in the conference finals, Danny. What work do they need to do this offseason?"

**Danny Green:** "A lot needs to happen, a lot needs to change. They had a great season, big picture, and I love what they did. They got to the Eastern Finals, but you can't be satisfied, right? So you got to make changes now. If you want to be a championship team, you have to compete with the **New York Knicks**. You have to compete with **Oklahoma City Thunder** and the **San Antonio Spurs**. So that means you got to find the right pieces around you, around **Donovan Mitchell**, whoever you keep on board. I like **Donovan Mitchell** and **Evan Mobley** to keep. Everybody else expendable. But you got to find three and D wings, some young athleticism, some guys, some bigs that can help you and compete with the **New York Knicks**, to compete with the **San Antonio Spurs**, **Oklahoma City Thunder**. So they got to find a roster and whatever else they make decisions wise, front office or coaching wise, to be able to be at the highest level and be contention. But all in all, they had a great season, but it's time to make moves and get better this summer."

**4. Tim McMahon — "AJ Mitchell out again. He has not been ruled out for the series, but the typical recovery time from a calf strain tells you that his return for the West Finals is pretty unlikely at best." — Tim McMahon updates on Jalen Williams and AJ Mitchell's injuries** [05:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kEG2Qf_MSOQ&t=352s)

**Host:** "Tim, let's start with those Thunder. Their offense Game 4, awful. They scored just 82 points in large part, no **Jalen Williams** and the **AJ Mitchell**'s injuries. What more can you tell us about their availability tonight?"

**Tim McMahon:** "Howdy partners. **AJ Mitchell** out again. He has not been ruled out for the series, but the typical recovery time from a calf strain tells you that his return for the West Finals is pretty unlikely at best. Now, **Jalen Williams** is questionable once again, just like he was the previous two games. He missed both those games, was officially ruled out 45 minutes before tip-off after going through what were pretty light pre-game warm-ups, basically spot shooting sessions. Now, there was a change in his injury designation. They had been listing him with left hamstring soreness. That is now left hamstring injury management with a strain. And basically, it's an acknowledgment of what was obvious. He aggravated the strain that he suffered in the second game this postseason."

**5. Danny Green — "They got to create offense with their defense. Get easy baskets, get out on transition, get out and running." — Danny Green advises Thunder to create offense from defense** [06:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kEG2Qf_MSOQ&t=409s)

**Host:** "So Danny, how can **OKC** generate more offense if two of their best off-dribble creators are sidelined?"

**Danny Green:** "They got to do what they've been doing and what they've been done for the last couple years. That's create offense with their defense. They got to be active, they got to get deflections, they got to get into people, be intense and be physical and not turn the ball over, which will help, so they get more shots on goal. But they got to create offense with their defense. Get easy baskets, get out on transition, get out and running, get some open looks for the young guys with **Caruso** and **Cason Wallace** and **Jaylen McCan**, even **Jalen Williams**, big **Jalen Williams**, to knock down threes. It's easier to get uncontested looks when you're in transition. And also you don't have a set defense of the **Spurs** and **Wemby** at the rim when you get transition. So they got to continue to do what they've always done in their DNA is be physical and create offense with their defense. So if they do that, they give themselves a chance. And if they take care of the ball, even better chance of them to win at home."

**6. Michael C. Wright — "He basically told them, hey, this is BS. We're not playing San Antonio Spurs basketball." — Michael C. Wright on Popovich's fiery speech changing Spurs' mindset** [08:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kEG2Qf_MSOQ&t=494s)

**Host:** "Joining the party now is our NBA reporter Michael C. Wright, who is covering the Spurs. All right, Michael, San Antonio had a complete 180-degree turnaround after Game 4 after Greg **Pop**ovich had some choice words for the Spurs after Game 3. How did **Pop** help set the tone for the series moving forward?"

**Michael C. Wright:** "Well, let me tell you this about **Pop**. CEO **RC Buford** told me that **Pop** is at his best during the darkest days. Well, after Game 3, those were dark days. They had basically been punked by the **Oklahoma City Thunder**. And basically, **Pop** came into the locker room for the first time all season. All the players were there, the staff, GM **Brian Wright**, everybody was there. And he basically told them, hey, this is BS. We're not playing **San Antonio Spurs** basketball. So that whole speech was about recalibrating the mindset going into this whole thing because look, the Spurs had just come off a 60-win regular season and **Pop** was like, is this how you guys want to go out? Well, obviously, that's not the case. They do not want to go out that way. They want to come out on top. So just a mindset change and it was beneficial for the **San Antonio Spurs** in Game 4."
