**Gabby Williams:** "It's less now than it was before. Before it was really difficult because also personally my role was so different. **6. Draymond Green — "playing year-round is just not sustainable for me anymore" — Gabby Williams explains her decision to join the Unrivaled League** [27:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RhXVkHLb9Yg&t=1626s)

**Draymond Green:** "This will now be the first year that you don't play in Europe during the off-season. You recently just signed a multi-year deal to play in the Unrivaled League. What was it that intrigued you to join Unrivaled as opposed to going to Europe where you've built a huge name for yourself?"

**Gabby Williams:** "It's weird not seeing everyone in EuroLeague right now. I would have loved to have continued with Fenerbahçe in Turkey. They were a great club, they really took care of us. Obviously, I would love to add more EuroLeague titles to my resume, but I think at this stage of my career, I'm just kind of thinking about longevity. And yeah, playing year-round is just not sustainable for me anymore. I think back in the day when our salaries weren't as high in the W, that was just kind of the thing you did. It wasn't, you didn't think twice about it, everyone just played year-round. When I first started playing, your contract would literally say like, after your last game you have 48 hours to get to the overseas. Yes, it was even worse back then. So now, I'm like, I need to rehab certain things, I need to focus on my body, I need to have a bit of an off-season. Even just as much as the travel or personal development, because when you're playing year-round you're not doing PD work. So I just kind of have to unfortunately switch my mindset to that, but I'm also really intrigued by Unrivaled and I'm always interested in something that invests so much in women's sports and has grown so quickly and it's pushed the envelope."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RhXVkHLb9Yg&t=1626s
