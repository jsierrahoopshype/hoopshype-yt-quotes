**5. Carmelo Anthony — "It starts when people aren't completely bought in to what the message is." — How a lack of buy-in leads to losing the locker room** [06:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nMoNd4u8PVg&t=377s)

Carmelo Anthony: "It starts when people aren't completely bought in to what the message is. I really feel like that stems from a coach not being certain in what he wants and what his vision is. If you are not certain, how is the player going to be certain in what you are trying to tell us to do? So it starts a lot of clicks in a locker room and people get to talking and get to chatting and not believing in the game plan and not believing in the coach and the team's identity."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nMoNd4u8PVg&t=377s

**6. Carmelo Anthony — "You feel it before anybody else can see it. The attention to details is not there." — The signs of a coach losing the locker room** [07:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nMoNd4u8PVg&t=439s)

Carmelo Anthony: "You feel it before anybody else can see it. The attention to details is not there. Film session is quiet. There is no noise in the film session. Nobody is in the timeouts paying attention. It is all of these things that are just a snowball effect. And the minute you lose that locker room, there is close to almost 100% of the guys that just completely lose it. Nobody is successful in losing the locker room and coming back."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nMoNd4u8PVg&t=439s

**7. Carmelo Anthony — "If you lose a locker room, it is because of certain reasons. It is because of ego." — Why coaches lose the locker room due to ego and lack of connection** [08:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nMoNd4u8PVg&t=487s)

Carmelo Anthony: "If you lose a locker room, it is because of certain reasons. It is because of ego. It is because of vision. It is because of no connection. Whatever it is, there is so many reasons that you could lose that locker room. You might not like the team, and that comes off. You might not like some of the players, and that comes off. That energy comes off, no matter if you try to hide it. So once you lose that, it is gone. It is gone forever."
