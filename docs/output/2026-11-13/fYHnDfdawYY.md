**1. Isaiah Stewart — "I was trying to go play against the best players in the country." — Isaiah Stewart on his decision to transfer to La Lumiere** [08:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fYHnDfdawYY&t=490s)

**Tony Allen:** "How you get to Indiana, that transfer, playing high school down there?"

**Isaiah Stewart:** "I was trying to go play against the best players in the country. So I kind of outgrew that space in New York, being the best player in the area. I went to La Lumiere, which was a top prep school in the country. It was in the woods, so it was cold too. I transferred there, we played against the top players, top teams, and in my senior year, we were number one in the country, knocking heads off anybody."

**3. Isaiah Stewart — "I feel like I got my welcome to NBA moment in training camp." — Isaiah Stewart on his welcome to the NBA moment** [20:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fYHnDfdawYY&t=1241s)

**Tony Allen:** "Was there any welcome to the NBA moment like for the culture, the way to be a professional to you when you got to the league?"

**Isaiah Stewart:** "I always say, I feel like I got my welcome to NBA moment in training camp. We doing drills, like one-on-one drills. I'm going against **Blake Griffin**, **Jeremy Grant**, and **Mason Plumlee**. And yeah, they were kicking my and then I went back to the hotel that night and I'm like, damn, I don't know if I'm good enough. The way they made me felt that day, but I just stuck with it and just kept showing up every day and just trying to learn as much as I can from them."

**4. Isaiah Stewart — "We lost like 28 games in a row one year, only won 14 games." — Isaiah Stewart on the Pistons' rebuilding process** [22:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fYHnDfdawYY&t=1354s)

**Tony Allen:** "How was your time in Detroit?"

**Isaiah Stewart:** "It was a great time. I got to Detroit when we was at the bottom and we were building. We lost like 28 games in a row one year, only won 14 games, and then, you know, we stuck it out. It took five years to finally win in the league, get to the offs, and we did that back-to-back years. But when the new representation came, it kind of left a sour taste in my mouth. You talk about identity and building the identity of a team and then, it didn't work out. It was kind of bitter-sweet because I just feel like there was no better place for me to get traded to than here. I resonate with the city and I know what I do in the court is going to be felt throughout the city."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fYHnDfdawYY&t=1354s

**5. Isaiah Stewart — "I went to the back, ran to the back, they closed the doors." — Isaiah Stewart on his altercation with LeBron James** [28:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fYHnDfdawYY&t=1702s)

**Tony Allen:** "Was it personal what he did?"

**Isaiah Stewart:** "To this day, I'll always say 100%. I know it was personal. You wind up, you hit me, he got a good hit, and I'm bleeding. So, I definitely think it was personal. I mean, I seen the interview of him saying it wasn't. So, hell yeah. He gave me a hit and then, I'm like, hold up, hold up. And then I got up, and when I seen blood, that's when I just blacked, man. I blacked and I wanted to draw blood back. And obviously, being a rookie, there's a lot of stuff going through your mind when that's happening, what you should do, how you should act. But man, once I seen that blood down my face, I just lost it. So, I'm obviously I'm chasing him, trying to get to him, you know, around the court and then, I knew I wasn't getting to him. So I said, I went to the back, ran to the back, they closed the doors. They said, yo, you got to clean these stitches up. I cleaned the stitches up, I got off the table and I'm still trying to go over to their side and then my guy, Troy Weaver, he said, look, you need to be the person we drafted. And I said, and when he said that, it just clicked and I just said, let me just let me just stop it right here."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fYHnDfdawYY&t=1702s
