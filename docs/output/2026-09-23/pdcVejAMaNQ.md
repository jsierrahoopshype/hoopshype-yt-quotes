# Lou Williams Opens Up on His Sixth Man Legacy, NBA Journey & What Happened in the Bubble — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pdcVejAMaNQ

_Speakers identified: Vince Carter, Tracy McGrady, Lou Williams_

**1. Lou Williams — "I've lost more Sixth Man than I've won. I've came in second four times." — Lou Williams on his runner-up finishes for Sixth Man of the Year** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pdcVejAMaNQ&t=0s)

Lou Williams: "I don't think people realize that I've lost more Sixth Man than I've won. I've came in second four times. I've been a runner-up four times. I've lost to **James Harden**, I've lost to **JR Smith**, and then I lost to **Eric Gordon**, and I lost to Trez, my teammate. I've lost twice to two of my teammates."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pdcVejAMaNQ&t=0s

**2. Lou Williams — "It was the misconception that I snuck out of the bubble. That was the funny part." — Lou Williams on the misconception about leaving the NBA bubble** [03:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pdcVejAMaNQ&t=186s)

Lou Williams: "Looking back at COVID and those times, I think everybody in the bubble would have did what I did if you had the opportunity to leave. It was the misconception that I snuck out of the bubble. That was the funny part that came out of it. I didn't get fined, I didn't get suspended or nothing. It was just, at that time, COVID, everything that was out of the bubble, everybody was sensitive about it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pdcVejAMaNQ&t=186s

**3. Lou Williams — "I'm originally from Memphis, Tennessee. A lot of people don't really know that." — Lou Williams on his upbringing in Memphis and basketball journey** [06:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pdcVejAMaNQ&t=367s)

Lou Williams: "I'm originally from Memphis, Tennessee. A lot of people don't really know that. I say I'm from Atlanta, but I'm from Memphis, Tennessee. I was an outside kid, I was an inner-city kid, I was dabbling, dabbling, just being a little adventurous. Some things was happening in my life early on. My father had passed away when I was eight, my brother had got sentenced to some substantial jail time around that same year in my life, and so around nine, my mom was like, 'We up out of here,' ended up moving me to Atlanta. So I landed in Atlanta when I was nine years old."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pdcVejAMaNQ&t=367s

**4. Lou Williams — "Sebastian Telfair, 2003. We were at USA, out in Colorado, training for Junior Olympics." — Lou Williams on realizing he was a professional prospect** [17:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pdcVejAMaNQ&t=1051s)

Lou Williams: "**Sebastian Telfair**, 2003. We were at USA, out in Colorado, training for Junior Olympics or World Games, and we were watching the draft together. At 13, **Sebastian Telfair** went, and that was the first time I thought to myself, 'Oh shit, I can do this.' At that time, it was guys like you, Vince and Tracy, already physically there. You're 6'7, 6'8, 6'9, y'all are built like men. That was the first time I saw a guy my size have that opportunity, and I'm like, 'Man, if the door is open for guys at this size, and I think I am who I am, I'm out of here. I'm going.'"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pdcVejAMaNQ&t=1051s

**5. Lou Williams — "I didn't want to waste no time with college. And so, it never was appeasing to me." — Lou Williams on his decision to skip college for the NBA** [21:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pdcVejAMaNQ&t=1273s)

Lou Williams: "I didn't want to waste no time with college. And so, it never was appeasing to me. I wanted to know my visits, I didn't care about the girls, I knew the league had better girls, I went tripping on that. I didn't care about the parties, I knew the league party better, I knew all of that. And so, I just got to a point I was anti-college. I was anti-school."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pdcVejAMaNQ&t=1273s

**6. Lou Williams — "The most challenging thing for me my rookie year was just to be able to find a role." — Lou Williams on the challenges of his rookie season** [27:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pdcVejAMaNQ&t=1643s)

Lou Williams: "The most challenging thing for me my rookie year was just to be able to find a role, be able to find my place in being an 18-year-old kid, not knowing you're going to play, but knowing you're expected to show up, knowing you're expected to do all of the extra things. How do you stay motivated and how do I stick if I'm not going to play being so young? And having a coach in Mo Cheeks that thought I was a pass-first guard. The way that I played, the way that you can get the most out of me, I wasn't really getting trained to be who I was naturally."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pdcVejAMaNQ&t=1643s

**7. Lou Williams — "I feel like I got robbed twice. My last year with the Sixers, I thought I let that team in scoring." — Lou Williams on his Sixth Man of the Year awards** [33:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pdcVejAMaNQ&t=2016s)

Lou Williams: "I feel like I got robbed twice. My last year with the Sixers, I thought I let that team in scoring. We had snuck into the playoff situation at that time, it was no play-in at that time, so we was a six seed. I felt like I got robbed twice. With the Clippers, that I was averaging about 22, 23 a game or something like that, off the bench, and I didn't make it. I didn't make it then, and it was kind of like an uproar about me not making the All-Star game, and I think Jimmy Butler got in at that last, that last spot, and he had some injuries, and then he ended up sitting out of the game, and I didn't get the reserve spot either. And so, those two times I felt like would have really put me in a different conversation when it comes to Sixth Men."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pdcVejAMaNQ&t=2016s

**8. Lou Williams — "We just didn't respond well to the bubble, dog. We were up 3-1 on Denver." — Lou Williams on the Clippers' failure in the bubble** [46:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pdcVejAMaNQ&t=2789s)

Lou Williams: "We just didn't respond well to the bubble, dog. We were up 3-1 on Denver. We gave away three straight games. In the time that we gave away three straight games, we had so many different distractions around us, and I'm not going to make no excuses for us. I'm going to be very clear about that. What's happened has happened, but if you're asking me in retrospect, what the cause of us not getting there was, the week that we lost, we had a bunch of distractions from a lot of different places."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pdcVejAMaNQ&t=2789s
