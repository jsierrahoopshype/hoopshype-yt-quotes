**3. Vince Carter — "That 85 to 90 Mike was we never seen him. That Mike, the versatility, the speed." — Vince Carter on Michael Jordan's unparalleled versatility in the 80s** [04:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QlIbd2FYqQU&t=263s)

**Tracy McGrady:** "So you're talking about **Phil Jackson** era."

**4. Vince Carter — "I think he came up with that suited Mike to be in the triangle." — Vince Carter on how the triangle offense elevated Michael Jordan** [05:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QlIbd2FYqQU&t=347s)

**Vince Carter:** "I think he came up with that suited Mike to be in the triangle because of double team, so much attention that you receive. So now putting you in that triangle, you could see all angles of where defenses are coming from the double team. And for me, I think having that system is what, Mike is going to be Mike, but I think in terms of winning and taking him to another level, that triangle definitely put him there."

**Tracy McGrady:** "So when I'm saying near, do you think Phil brought that to Kobe? Like, yo, you should do some of these stuff Mike was doing because it works in this triangle offense."

**5. Fabolous — "Singles for me were like selling me as the artist that you wanted to buy into." — Fabolous on how singles defined his artistic image** [10:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QlIbd2FYqQU&t=608s)

Fabolous: "Singles for me were like selling me as the artist that you wanted to buy into. I would make songs, especially in that time.
