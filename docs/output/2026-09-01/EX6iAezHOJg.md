**2. Jim Souhan — "he worked out a deal with the Utah Jazz, sends Josh Green there, gets John Collins and Cody Williams back" — The benefits of the Timberwolves' trade with the Utah Jazz** [02:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EX6iAezHOJg&t=162s)

Jim Souhan: "He worked out a deal with the Utah Jazz, sends **Josh Green** there, gets **John Collins** and **Cody Williams** back, and it is the best of both worlds because Collins came back and he only had one year left on his deal. When you waive and stretch Collins, you are only putting $2 million on the salary cap for this year and next versus $5 million for the next three years had it been **Josh Green** that was waived. But then you also get **Cody Williams**, who is a young guy, he was a first-round draft pick, he has some athleticism, he had some moments at the end of last season for Utah. And so it gives you just another guy to take a look at and just see if, hey, maybe he can help us."
