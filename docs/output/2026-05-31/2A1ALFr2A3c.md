# RJ Explains Why Jalen Brunson Has the MOST to Gain in the NBA Finals — *Road Trippin*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2A1ALFr2A3c

_Speakers identified: Allie Clifton, Richard Jefferson_

**1. Richard Jefferson — "When I look at who has the most to gain from how many opportunities will he get" — Jalen Brunson has the most to gain in the NBA Finals** [00:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2A1ALFr2A3c&t=22s)

**Allie Clifton:** "Currently right now, let's look at **Willis Reed**, two chips, two Finals MVP, seven... No, we're not talking about him. You said about Brunson. I said with the players that will be playing in the Finals, SGA, Wembanyama, or Brunson. Who has the most to gain?"

**Richard Jefferson:** "1,000%. I think if we were to kind of lay it out, the Knicks are having a magical season. The way their players are shooting, everything coming together, kind of the tweak of the offense, historic run. But I look at it as, Wembanyama, we feel like Wembanyama might have eight more chances at this. **Shai Gilgeous-Alexander**, he's already won a championship and has two MVPs in his back pocket. Brunson is a guy that hasn't won an MVP, hasn't won a Finals MVP, hasn't won a championship. So when I look at who has the most to gain from how many opportunities will he get, this Knicks team, maybe they win two championships over the next five years. But we're not looking at them as the consistent favorite out of their conference, and they wouldn't be the favorite here. So when you get that moment and you get that opportunity, it needs to be seized."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2A1ALFr2A3c&t=22s

**2. Richard Jefferson — "His greatness in my opinion is going to be determined over the next five to seven years" — Wembanyama's greatness will be determined over the next five to seven years** [02:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2A1ALFr2A3c&t=156s)

Richard Jefferson: "Wembanyama, I wish he had a 15 or 20-year career. But history tells us that guys that are 7'4" and higher don't really have that long of a window. So over the next five or six years, Wembanyama has to max out. His greatness in my opinion is going to be determined over the next five to seven years. I just don't know at 7'5" and I hope I'm wrong, but like you say, with the facts, my opinion could change. But these are facts. If you look at the history of greats, like **Yao Ming** and all those guys over 7', they didn't have that long window. But **Shaquille O'Neal** made a great point. I saw him on a podcast. He said this reminds him, whether it's OKC or the Spurs, it's going to remind him of when Detroit beat the Lakers in 2004. They were playing some great basketball, walked into the Finals as a serious-ass underdog."
