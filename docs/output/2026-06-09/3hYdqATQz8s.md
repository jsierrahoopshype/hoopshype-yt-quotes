# Tim Legler breaks down the keys to the San Antonio Spurs' Game 3 win over the Knicks | SC with SVP — *NBA on ESPN*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3hYdqATQz8s

_Speakers identified: Scott Van Pelt, Tim Legler, Mike Brown, Jalen Brunson, Karl-Anthony Towns, Jeremy Lin_

**1. Tim Legler — "Up one, two minutes to go, they lose game one. Tie game with the ball, they lose game two." — Spurs' messaging and confidence after losing first two games** [00:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3hYdqATQz8s&t=22s)

**Scott Van Pelt:** "San Antonio could come here knowing they battled for two games. Frankly, they probably should be cosmically one-one. But that doesn't mean that shows up tonight. What did they show you tonight?"

**Tim Legler:** "They were confident from the beginning. When you go back and look at the first two games, the messaging from Miss Johnson was simple. Up one, two minutes to go, they lose game one. Tie game with the ball, they lose game two. That's the messaging and it gives you the belief. Remember guys, we won more than 60 games this year. We can go in there and win a game."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3hYdqATQz8s&t=22s

**2. Tim Legler — "They have made Jalen Brunson work for everything he gets. He is dribbling the basketball against elite defenders." — Knicks' struggle to contain Jalen Brunson against elite defenders** [01:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3hYdqATQz8s&t=94s)

**Scott Van Pelt:** "The third quarter, there was a resolve and an intent again from San Antonio that showed itself. What were the keys there?"

**Tim Legler:** "Sometimes your friend is halftime. If that quarter had lasted another four minutes, they might have been down 15. That's the way the Knicks were rolling. But they had a chance to go in down seven. You kind of reset it. One thing that they have figured out through three games, they have made **Jalen Brunson** work for everything he gets. He is dribbling the basketball against elite defenders. They are laterally quick, they are bigger than him, they are stronger."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3hYdqATQz8s&t=94s

**3. Mike Brown — "I never thought I'd be in the NBA Finals and see a team get 24 free throw attempts." — Mike Brown criticizes free throw disparity in NBA Finals** [02:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3hYdqATQz8s&t=161s)

Mike Brown: "**Mitch Johnson** and the Spurs, they won the game tonight. They came and took the game. But I will say this, I never thought I'd be in the NBA Finals and see a team get 24 free throw attempts in the second half to another team's eight. San Antonio is a great team. They are a great team. It's going to lower our odds big time if we play Game 4 and in the second half they get 24 free throw attempts to our eight."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3hYdqATQz8s&t=161s

**4. Tim Legler — "This is so difficult to officiate because of the amount of physicality that's taking place all over the floor." — Tim Legler assesses Mike Brown's complaints about officiating** [04:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3hYdqATQz8s&t=253s)

**Scott Van Pelt:** "How reasonable is that assessment from coach Brown?"

**Tim Legler:** "I am not seeing a one-way whistle. This is so difficult to officiate because of the amount of physicality that's taking place all over the floor. Sometimes it just seemed like a little selective on a particular play that you see a lot. Very difficult job for these officials."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3hYdqATQz8s&t=253s

**5. Jalen Brunson — "We turned the ball over a lot first and foremost, but then also we were fouling a lot." — Jalen Brunson attributes Knicks' loss to turnovers and fouls** [06:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3hYdqATQz8s&t=402s)

Jalen Brunson: "We turned the ball over a lot first and foremost, but then also we were fouling a lot and put them on the line about 30 times. With our live ball turnovers and they got them on transition and they were capitalizing off of those. So we were definitely fouling a lot."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3hYdqATQz8s&t=402s

**6. Karl-Anthony Towns — "It's always disappointing when you lose. You always want to step on that court and win." — Karl-Anthony Towns on the disappointment of losing Game 3** [07:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3hYdqATQz8s&t=422s)

Karl-Anthony Towns: "It's always disappointing when you lose. You always want to step on that court and win. It's always about being in the present and to be in the position that we were going into halftime, to come out in the third the way we did, and to not find a way to win the game, obviously is disappointing."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3hYdqATQz8s&t=422s

**7. Jeremy Lin — "They are constantly in his body. They are shading him right. They are just making him work for everything." — Jeremy Lin on Spurs' defensive strategy against Jalen Brunson** [08:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3hYdqATQz8s&t=483s)

**Scott Van Pelt:** "What are the Spurs effectively doing to make it difficult for **Jalen Brunson**?"

**Jeremy Lin:** "They are picking up almost full court. They are constantly in his body. They are shading him right. They are just making him work for everything. One simple adjustment I would love to see the Knicks and **Mike Brown** make is just how can we get him involved? How can we get him in actions where he doesn't have to bring the ball up or start with the ball with the entire defense set looking at him?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3hYdqATQz8s&t=483s

**8. Jeremy Lin — "They tried to keep Victor Wembanyama as close to the paint and he was responsible for passing people off." — Jeremy Lin on Spurs' defensive adjustment in Game 3** [09:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3hYdqATQz8s&t=591s)

**Scott Van Pelt:** "Explain to the audience what they did on defense."

**Jeremy Lin:** "Their Game 3 adjustment on the defensive side was they tried to keep **Victor Wembanyama** as close to the paint and he was responsible for passing people off and he would take whoever was in the corner. He was responsible for the corner closeout. That was something new. It gave the Knicks a little bit of a hesitation and I am fully prepared and expecting that **Mike Brown** will have that figured out for Game 4."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3hYdqATQz8s&t=591s
