**3. Monty McCutchen — "Fair and equitable are not the same thing. Equitable would mean the exact same amount of free throws." — Monty McCutchen explains the difference between equitable and fair officiating** [01:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OFh2hFdfkWQ&t=94s)

**Malika Andrews:** "You said that equity and it's not the same, equity is not the same as fairness."

**Monty McCutchen:** "Fair and equitable are not the same thing. Equitable would mean the exact same amount of free throws, which is being referenced here. But fair is calling similar plays similarly. And we've got to make sure that for our consistency as referees, that if the same types of plays are taking place, that they're called the same."

**7. Monty McCutchen — "Any good referee, when they're coming into the best time of the year, is aware of what is possible." — Monty McCutchen on the need for referees to let action dictate decisions** [06:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OFh2hFdfkWQ&t=361s)

Monty McCutchen: "Any good referee, when they're coming into the best time of the year, is aware of what is possible, but they have to let the action actually dictate their decisions. **8. Monty McCutchen — Monty McCutchen addresses the confusion during the Jalen Brunson inbound play** [08:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OFh2hFdfkWQ&t=522s)

**Malika Andrews:** "Jaylen Brunson throws the ball off of **Devin Vassell** and the ruling is out of bounds, Knicks ball. But then **Mitch Johnson** signals to **Dylan Harper** to sub in to give them additional time to potentially challenge, but **Dylan Harper** just ran onto the court and the ball was inbounded. So what should have been the ruling?"

**Monty McCutchen:** "There's a really important part of refereeing that is running the game. You have to run the game with a certain authority that isn't overly authoritative, which can be off-putting, but also not to be in a situation in which you allow confusion to breathe, which is exactly what happened. I would have loved for it to have been a little more authoritative and really run that situation. I think we contributed to the confusion by not being more authoritative in that regard."
