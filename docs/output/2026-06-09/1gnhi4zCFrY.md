**7. Marcus Spears — "When a crowd is packed way before, it's just natural. You start engaging with people." — Impact of a packed crowd on player routine and focus** [05:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1gnhi4zCFrY&t=304s)

Marcus Spears: "I will say like, going to a lot of NBA games and playing in games, when the crowd starts to come in, you're usually at a point where you're getting a real good lather, you're getting ready to, and then you go back in a huddle in the locker room and you come out and you're ready to warm up and play the game.
