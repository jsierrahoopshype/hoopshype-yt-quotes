# Boogie CALLS OUT Wemby After Lackluster First Game — *Run It Back*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BZBJGKYW1b8

_Speakers identified: Michelle Beadle, Chandler Parsons, DeMarcus Cousins, Lou Williams_

**1. Chandler Parsons — "I don't really like it when he takes a bunch of outside jumpers and doesn't get to the free throw line." — Chandler Parsons on Victor Wembanyama's need for more aggressive play** [00:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BZBJGKYW1b8&t=19s)

Chandler Parsons: "Sometimes he floats, sometimes he settles. I don't really like it when he takes a bunch of outside jumpers and doesn't get to the free throw line. So at least when the basket wasn't falling last night, he was getting to the free throw line. But it is weird at his size and his ability and the way he moves, he can't get offensive rebound, tip dunk, he can't get to his little baby hook. I still want to see him work on that when he gets a mismatch in the post because when you're talking about Wemby, he doesn't have to get to a mismatch. Whoever is guarding him, it's already a mismatch. So he doesn't need to be waiting for everything. And I think like this when I watch **Jayson Tatum** and **Luka Doncic** play, these guys are so gifted and they can score in so many different ways, but sometimes they let the defense off the hook and they don't get to the right spots and they don't get downhill. And I think Wemby is a product of that where he just settles too much."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BZBJGKYW1b8&t=19s

**2. Lou Williams — "I don't see any dedicated sets that San Antonio is running to get Wemby the basketball." — Lou Williams on the Spurs' lack of structured sets for Wembanyama** [01:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BZBJGKYW1b8&t=102s)

Lou Williams: "I'm still going to split it from where I was last year. Half of it is going to be coaching, half of it is going to be Wemby needing to be more aggressive. Maybe I missed it, but I don't see any dedicated sets that San Antonio is running to get Wemby the basketball. I see him maybe eating off the land or them running plays for other guys, but run something where there's a back screen for a lob or something. Run something where there's a quick duck in for him to catch the ball two feet from the rim. Something of that sort to get him easy baskets, especially when you look at a game last night, he doesn't get his first field goal until the third quarter. If you want to win games, you know this guy has to be an intricate part of your offense."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BZBJGKYW1b8&t=102s

**3. DeMarcus Cousins — "I would just like to see him simplify his game. It's a lot of plays where he's just working so hard." — DeMarcus Cousins on simplifying Victor Wembanyama's offensive game** [03:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BZBJGKYW1b8&t=183s)

DeMarcus Cousins: "I would just like to see him simplify his game. It's a lot of plays where he's just working so hard to even get a shot attempt off. And I think he forgets that he's the tallest guy on the floor. His game can be so simple sometimes to where he can just catch at the free throw line, turn around and shoot it. No tween-tweens, no crossover dribbles, no ducking the show, literally turn around and shoot over the top of the defender. The same thing goes for each block. Turn around, shoot over the defender. I don't know what's being taught behind closed doors with the Spurs. I don't know if it's even being taught or worked on. But for him to continue to get better, and he does need to get better, he's far from a finished product. There's too many legends that have come through that organization that played the same position to where he shouldn't be topics of conversation. **LaMarcus Aldridge** was one of the best turnaround shooting big men this game has ever seen. Why isn't he on staff and working with this kid every single day? **Tim Duncan** with the bank shot from anywhere on the floor, anywhere in the paint. Why isn't this being added to Wemby's game?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BZBJGKYW1b8&t=183s
