# Olivia Miles On Preparing For Her First WNBA Playoffs & How Soccer Shaped Her Game — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

_Speakers identified: Vince Carter, Olivia Miles, Tracy McGrady_

**1. Olivia Miles — "It'll be like an evolution for me, I think. I'll have to become a different person." — Olivia Miles on the mental and physical evolution required for WNBA playoffs** [00:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_oqbz0FTOQ&t=47s)

**Vince Carter:** "How excited are you to play in the playoffs? It's one thing being drafted, playing in the WNBA, making the All-Star team, but now it's that back end of where people earn their name and their money."

**Olivia Miles:** "I'm excited. It'll be like an evolution for me, I think. I'll have to become a different person, mentally, physically, emotionally, become stronger, and be able to take on more adversity and learn quickly. No one's going to care that I'm a rookie in that moment; they're still going to want to beat me."

**Tracy McGrady:** "The great thing about a series is the chess match. Once your career goes on, you're going to understand how you need to really break down each quarter for yourself and for your team."

**2. Olivia Miles — "I'm excited for the high-stakes environment. It's really high-risk, high-reward, and you've got to show your best stuff." — Olivia Miles on the intensity of home and road playoff environments** [01:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_oqbz0FTOQ&t=72s)

Olivia Miles: "I'm excited because, as you know in college, you go to these regional sites and there's no real connection to your hometown or your college. I'm excited that you play games at home and when you play games on the road, you have the fan base rooting against you and you have the fan base rooting for you. It's going to be crazy, it's going to be intense. I'm excited for the high-stakes environment. It's really high-risk, high-reward, and you've got to show your best stuff. I love the idea of a series because then you can play chess."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_oqbz0FTOQ&t=72s

**3. Tracy McGrady — "You're going to understand how you need to really break down each quarter for yourself and for your team." — Tracy McGrady on the importance of film study in playoff series** [02:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_oqbz0FTOQ&t=124s)

Tracy McGrady: "You're going to understand how you need to really break down each quarter for yourself and for your team. It might be a half where you're dissecting and seeing something totally different in how you approach the game. **Fe** might have it going, so we're going to feed **Fe**. **Fe** and **K-Mag** might have it going, so I'm going to milk them. I'm going to get those girls going. Then the second half, they might be struggling. Then you might have to turn it up on the offensive end, but find some way to get these girls involved because you can't go away from them, because in the playoffs, you need about two or three."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_oqbz0FTOQ&t=124s

**4. Olivia Miles — "Soccer has done so much for me physically, mentally, emotionally. I think the biggest thing is the health." — Olivia Miles on how soccer agility and conditioning helped her basketball career** [07:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_oqbz0FTOQ&t=454s)

Olivia Miles: "Soccer has done so much for me physically, mentally, emotionally. I think the biggest thing for me is the health of my body because I'm working on different movements and agility, working different muscles, and getting my body used to different sprints and speeds and the lateral movements. The agility of soccer really helped me, and obviously the conditioning is really good in soccer, so it translated. But I think also for me, mentally, having a three-month period where I'm not in basketball season and I'm doing something completely different allowed me to avoid burnout. I got a different set of perspectives, people, coaches, mindsets. I got to switch gears to a different sport."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_oqbz0FTOQ&t=454s

**5. Tracy McGrady — "Some of her passes are like J-Kidd. She's throwing the ball to get you open." — Tracy McGrady on Olivia Miles' elite passing ability and vision** [09:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_oqbz0FTOQ&t=564s)

Tracy McGrady: "Some of her passes are like **J-Kidd**. She's throwing the ball to get you open. You don't even know it's coming. You don't even think you're open, but she sees that angle. Whether it's right-hand or left-hand, your skill set is crazy in the passing, too, to be able to make some of those left-handed passes."
