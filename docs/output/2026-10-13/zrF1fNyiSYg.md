**5. Iman Shumpert — "I want the numbers to give me some insight about how I'm going to make you a better player." — Dean Oliver on the role of analytics in modern NBA coaching** [23:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrF1fNyiSYg&t=1387s)

**Iman Shumpert:** "Do you feel like there is parts of using analytics that shouldn't be used?"

**Dean Oliver:** "I think people distort things. I think people will exaggerate things to make a point. Like you say, it's show business. Sometimes show business is exaggerating things to make a point. I think when you're doing media, that's one thing. You can exaggerate to make a point and I think that's a lot of what we hear. But when you're on a team, when I'm coaching you, I don't want to lie to you. I want the numbers to give me some insight about how I'm going to make you a better player. And hopefully you're getting that from tape, hopefully we're getting that from other people's independent perspectives too. But we're just trying to put it together for the team to make the players better, not only who you are as an individual, but how you play with your teammates."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrF1fNyiSYg&t=1387s

**6. Dean Oliver — "It needs to be a conversation. I think that is the most important thing." — Dean Oliver on using analytics to evaluate clutch play** [27:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrF1fNyiSYg&t=1637s)

Dean Oliver: "It needs to be a conversation. I think that is the most important thing. Why is this different from what I see with my eyes? And I'll give you a couple reasons why it might be. One is clutch play. **Jalen Brunson**, we all on the Knicks can play in the clutch. But he like, we also know that he's not the greatest defender. He's a smaller guy. We also know that like, okay, there's not a lot of clutch plays in a lot of games. Like a lot of games come down to like, okay, they're up by 17 and with five minutes to go. Like you're not going to get all that. And he may take some plays off because he can. So if we're looking at clutch play, yeah, **Jalen Brunson** has been great for sure, but that's not the only criteria. Sometimes you want to be Mr. First Quarter. And first quarter, like I can tell you most of the best players in the NBA, you're either Jokic, you're Doncic, you're Giannis, you're SGA. That start off hitting you in the mouth."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrF1fNyiSYg&t=1637s

**7. Dean Oliver — "When the narratives have already been generated and the numbers don't necessarily support it, you have to swim upstream." — Dean Oliver on evaluating player performance beyond box score stats** [29:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrF1fNyiSYg&t=1775s)

Dean Oliver: "I think a lot of times the narrative has already been built. And I think when the narratives have already been generated and the numbers don't necessarily support it, you have to swim upstream. Once that happens, it's tough. And sometimes like who is the MVP gets decided fairly or like **Asia Wilson** last year, not this year so much. **Asia Wilson** wasn't playing great in the WNBA in the first half of the season. She was still good, but she's always good. She picked it up late. And so how much should the second half of the season count versus the first half of the season? And like okay, the narrative started getting built when they went on like a 12 game winning streak or something like that. And like okay, should she be MVP when the piece of **Asia Wilson** was having a great season herself? That kind of stuff."
