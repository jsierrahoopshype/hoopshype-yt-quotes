**4. Stephen Jackson — "James Harrison has me to think and he has Joey Porter to think for his success." — How a pregame brawl led to James Harrison's breakout game** [06:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eQYijv2W5ak&t=377s)

Stephen Jackson: "**James Harrison** has me to think and he has **Joey Porter** to think for his success in terms of how everything happened. We were playing Cleveland Browns in Cleveland. It's the pregame, we on this side of the 50, they on that side of the 50. There was a fullback for the Cleveland Browns, and he's talking crazy to me. He's talking about my fullback, and he's like, your fullback, we, he ain't nothing. I'm the baddest fullback in the league. Your guy ain't nothing. Because I was a fullback, I got love for my fullback. My fullback was **Dad Crider**, and he's a quiet guy. He's not about to create no issue. He's not about to jump up. Well, I'm wolfing, I'm right back at him. Well, **Joey Porter** is on the other side, so he see me getting at it with this dude. He come all the way over because he want all the smoke. He's looking for it. So, he's like, what? What's up? What's going on? So then, their fullback, his name was Green, he comes up, and he's wolfing, and Joey says, I don't like you anyway. And he hits him. And this is pregame, so it's a melee. And so now, Joey gets thrown out of the game before it even starts. And **James Harrison** has to then come in and play. And that's how he ended up having like two sacks in the game."
