**2. Tracy McGrady — "I've never drank before a game in my life. It was New Year's. So I did have a drink." — Tracy McGrady on drinking alcohol before a game** [01:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DUltQEsK8oU&t=87s)

**Vince Carter:** "How did you feel in the second half?"

**Tracy McGrady:** "I will say this, I've never drank before a game in my life. It was New Year's. So I did have a drink, a few drinks the night before the game. Never done that in my career, haven't done it since. So I tell people that was probably what allowed me to have the night I had."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DUltQEsK8oU&t=87s

**3. Tracy McGrady — "My thing was just let's make it close. I'm going to be a little more aggressive." — Tracy McGrady on his mindset during his 62-point game** [02:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DUltQEsK8oU&t=121s)

**Vince Carter:** "Coming out of the third quarter, you said what?"

**Tracy McGrady:** "Let's make it close. Let's get it close. I'm going to be a little more aggressive. But let's just get the game close within maybe 10 at the fourth quarter. They were rolling. They were playing well. It was when they had Zach, Amar'e, Alex Caruso, all them guys, IO, Vucevic. So they were playing well. Next thing you know, the first shot starts going in, now I'm able to get to the rim, now I'm scoring, now the shots that I've been missing for the past week are starting to fall."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DUltQEsK8oU&t=121s

**4. Tracy McGrady — "My dad worked for the Mets, so every spring break, that's where I was." — Tracy McGrady on his baseball background and father's influence** [04:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DUltQEsK8oU&t=270s)

**Vince Carter:** "When did you fall in love with baseball?"

**Tracy McGrady:** "My whole life. My dad worked for the Mets, so every spring break, that's where I was. I was at spring training. It was me down there with **Jose Reyes**, **David Wright**, **Pedro Martinez**, and I'm taking batting practice. I'm in the bullpen sessions. I'm in the locker room all the summers. He's coaching in the minor leagues, so I'm traveling with guys. **Jacob deGrom** wasn't **Jacob deGrom** at that time, he was my dad coached him. So I was around him a lot, which then he turned out to be who he is, which I think is pretty dope."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DUltQEsK8oU&t=270s

**5. Tracy McGrady — "I hurt myself, I broke my wrist, and then next thing you know, I just was like, man." — Tracy McGrady on why he switched from baseball to basketball** [07:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DUltQEsK8oU&t=443s)

**Vince Carter:** "So how did basketball creep in?"

**Tracy McGrady:** "I hurt myself, I broke my wrist, and then next thing you know, I just was like, man. You know when you play it for so long as a kid, you just kind of get tired of it. I was around it for so long. And then next thing you know, I was just like, man, I want to hoop. And that year I went from three offers basketball-wise to the next year I went to Brewster Academy, I had 26."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DUltQEsK8oU&t=443s
