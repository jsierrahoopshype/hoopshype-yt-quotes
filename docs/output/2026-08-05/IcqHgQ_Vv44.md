# Richard Jefferson Predicts Brandon Miller’s FIRST ALL-STAR Season — *Road Trippin*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IcqHgQ_Vv44

_Speakers identified: Richard Jefferson, Brandon Miller, Allie Clifton, Channing Frye_

**1. Richard Jefferson — "If you're healthy at the start of the season, I think this year could be your All-Star season." — Richard Jefferson on Brandon Miller's potential for an All-Star season** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IcqHgQ_Vv44&t=0s)

Richard Jefferson: "The next step for elite players, which I believe you have the ability, I think that again, I don't know when you're going to be healthy, but if you're healthy at the start of the season, I think this year could be your All-Star season. The ability to manipulate the game, where it's watching film, it's, hey, this is what this team does. When you take the step from I'm a very good player, I'm a talented player to I'm an All-Star to I'm a playoff player, it's, okay, this is what they're trying to do. They're going to double team, they're going to do this. How can I manipulate the game? That is the one thing that I think is the hardest gap from people being a very good player that's an All-Star three, four years to the guy that becomes a future Hall of Famer."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IcqHgQ_Vv44&t=0s

**2. Brandon Miller — "I go in the workouts, it's more mental, mental than anything." — Brandon Miller on the mental approach to improving his game** [01:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IcqHgQ_Vv44&t=86s)

Brandon Miller: "Mainly it's, you know, I go in the workouts, it's more mental, mental than anything. I think the talent is always going to show, the work is always going to show. And then on Wednesdays, me and **Josh Longstaff** actually watch film, and I will actually pick what we watch that day. More than likely the times it's opponents, know where I want the ball and numbers games a little bit, stuff like that. But mainly it's just a mental thing. I feel like players are locked in in the games and you can get fatigue sometimes, and it's just how are you going to respond to how are you going to still impact the game when you're tired."
