# Chris Rock Crashed Jason Sudeikis' SNL Audition — *ALL THE SMOKE*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ou9cYK95jd4

_Speakers identified: Jason Sudeikis, Matt Barnes, Stephen Jackson_

**1. Jason Sudeikis — "Chris Rock comes walking in with Jeff Ross, because he is getting ready to host the MTV Video Awards" — Jason Sudeikis on his first SNL audition experience with Chris Rock** [00:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ou9cYK95jd4&t=34s)

Jason Sudeikis: "I did that audition my very first time for SNL back in 2003 at Stand Up New York. As I am about to go on stage, **Chris Rock** comes walking in with **Jeff Ross**, because he is getting ready to host the MTV Video Awards. He is just going up to try out stuff. He did not know it was an SNL audition. He was just walking in and he is like, 'Oh shit, then there is this old boss, and friends, and what not.' He is doing his thing. I remember saying before, he goes, 'Are you supposed to go up next?' I go, 'Is this your big shot?' I go, 'Kind of.' He goes, 'Sorry.' Then he comes out, so he basically, I was seven out of 12. A bunch of people had already gone. I remember at that point having a Diet Coke and rum because I was like, 'Okay, I am going to, this, there is a flag on the play. If I do not get it, this is great. Why to follow **Chris Rock**.' I had my excuse to tell to the bar."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ou9cYK95jd4&t=34s

**2. Jason Sudeikis — "What you have right now is this real amazing opportunity for original thought to be there" — Jason Sudeikis on the opportunity for original thought in comedy** [02:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ou9cYK95jd4&t=124s)

Jason Sudeikis: "What you have right now is this real amazing opportunity for original thought to be there. There is no middle man. There is no middle woman. There is no middle anything. It is direct to the world. But is it ready for the world? That is the trick. Is there enough there? Because you have a real opportunity right now. Everybody as a discerning viewer has a lot of options. As an artist, you have to be able to discern between smoke, heat, and fire. Smoke you can choke on. Heat can fool you. Fire can burn you up, but it also can heat an entire house or bar. It can keep you safe. It gives you light, lets you see. Heat cannot help you see."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ou9cYK95jd4&t=124s

**3. Jason Sudeikis — "Will Forte is salt of the earth. Incredibly talented, one of one, one of the most original voices" — Jason Sudeikis on Will Forte's comedic talent and work ethic** [04:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ou9cYK95jd4&t=260s)

Jason Sudeikis: "**Will Forte**. I love that guy. He is close to a lot of people. He has a tremendous engine. That guy is salt of the earth. Incredibly talented, one of one, one of the most original voices of anybody. He would drive you nuts. You would stay there until 8:00 a.m. on a writing night. I am done at 4:00. I used to be, my first few years, if I was there 10 years, first five years I could probably rock until 7:00 a.m. Seth was really smart. Seth would go to sleep from 4:00 a.m. to 8:00 a.m. and then wake up and crush something. Forte would just stay up the whole time and would kind of just bullshit around and cause havoc and second-guess every vowel. I just want to go home. But through it, every time, something interesting would come out of it. It would not always make it on the show, and if it did, would not always crush, but it was always original. It was always his 100%."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ou9cYK95jd4&t=260s

**4. Jason Sudeikis — "To get to work with the Farrelly brothers while on SNL, to get to work with Owen" — Jason Sudeikis on working with the Farrelly brothers on Hall Pass** [06:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ou9cYK95jd4&t=367s)

Jason Sudeikis: "The biggest one was I got to do this movie Hall Pass while still at SNL. That movie founded its audience in a way. To get to work with the Farrelly brothers while on SNL, to get to work with Owen. JB was in there, **Larry Campbell**, **Stephen Merchant**. **Christina Applegate**, who is an absolute legend, her and I did several movies together. That was the first, if I remember, we had done one before, but that was one where we were in together the most. I would do SNL on Saturday night, Sunday fly down to Atlanta, work all day Monday, Tuesday, fly out Tuesday, fly to SNL, try to write something if I had something on Tuesday night, be there for the read-through on Wednesday because Lauren would not let me miss the read-throughs because he liked the way I would do the read-throughs and he thought it was important I was there. Whatever my last sketch was in the read-through, you read like 45 sketches, if I was like the 38th sketch, I would bounce. I would fly back down to Atlanta, I would work there Thursday, and then I would come back Friday morning to then rehearse all day Friday, do the show Saturday, and then do it again. I did that probably six out of 12 weeks."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ou9cYK95jd4&t=367s

**5. Jason Sudeikis — "Then I started to do a lot of these movies with New Line, which is a shingle of Warner Brothers" — Jason Sudeikis on his film collaborations with Jason Bateman and Charlie Day** [08:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ou9cYK95jd4&t=502s)

Jason Sudeikis: "Then I started to do a lot of these movies with New Line, which is a shingle of Warner Brothers, and then I got to do Horrible Bosses one through that. Then we had the Millers and Horrible Bosses two. Horrible Bosses one was a whole different thing, but it had a similar vibe because it was me, **Charlie Day**, and **Jason Bateman**. Those are three dudes that I go to war with. **Jason Bateman** is the funniest shit, smartest shit, does not fuck around, is so excited when people are kicking ass and wants everybody to kick ass. By his own admission, I think we all would have had more scuffles with him in his drinking days, but again, he is like, 'I was an asshole.' Self-proclaimed, and that is part of the reason. And just, yeah, like, does not like, Jason can't do stuff, but I am always like, Jason can't write because Charlie is a great writer and I have got a knack for it, but just knows when it is bullshit or when something is not right, and is not mean about it, but just like, straight shooter. And so funny, so quick and so dry and so specific."
