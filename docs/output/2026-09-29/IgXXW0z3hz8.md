# “They’re Being Babied. They’re Being Coddled.” John Smoltz on What’s WRONG With Modern MLB — *Brice Butler Studios*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IgXXW0z3hz8

_Speakers identified: John Smoltz, Brice Butler_

**1. John Smoltz — "They’re being propped up, they’re being babied, they’re being coddled. They’re given the keys to everything." — John Smoltz on the coddled nature of modern professional athletes** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IgXXW0z3hz8&t=0s)

John Smoltz: "They’re being propped up, they’re being babied, they’re being coddled. They’re given the keys to everything. They’re getting a ton of money. They’re chasing their reward system. And so, when you’re brought up that way, not that everybody gets a trophy type mindset, you’re not going to have to do those kind of things. You’ll be told, don’t play. If you don’t feel well, don’t play. That would drive me crazy. And that would be something that I could not look my teammate in the eyes knowing that 80% of me is better than 100% of some other dude."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IgXXW0z3hz8&t=0s

**2. John Smoltz — "we will never see four of them ever again." — John Smoltz on the unique greatness of the 1990s Braves rotation** [01:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IgXXW0z3hz8&t=96s)

**Brice Butler:** "What pitcher in today’s game do you think has what you guys had in the Atlanta Braves?"

**John Smoltz:** "There is no doubt that we will never see four of them ever again. **Justin Verlander**, **Clayton Kershaw**. You can put **Charlie Morton** in there, he’s been pitching forever now and learned a lot from the greatest. **Zack Wheeler** from Philadelphia, **Max Scherzer**, **Nathan Eovaldi**. These are guys who have lived through two eras and they know how to pitch without their best stuff. You can probably put **Gerrit Cole** in there too. When those players leave our game, we will never see that kind of pitcher ever again. To me, that’s the saddest statement that baseball has to deal with and try to figure out how they can change because we’re just not going to see it ever again."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IgXXW0z3hz8&t=96s

**3. John Smoltz — "Hank Aaron is all by himself. There is no one that comes close." — John Smoltz on the Mount Rushmore of Atlanta Braves players** [02:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IgXXW0z3hz8&t=163s)

John Smoltz: "First of all, I don’t even have to fudge this one in there because I think he’s one of the greatest human beings, is **Dale Murphy**. I think **Dale Murphy** epitomized what the Atlanta Braves were when they were terrible. He never got the chance to be on the mountaintop when we got good. But he was the Atlanta Braves. And **Hank Aaron** is all by himself. There is no one that comes close. He’s on top of Mount Rushmore. And then, **Phil Niekro** was the guy from the mound that everyone respected, Hall of Famer. But we’re building a pretty good resume between **Chipper Jones** and I think **Andrew Jones** belongs in the Hall of Fame 100%. **Fred McGriff** only played a few years with us, but he’s in the Hall of Fame."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IgXXW0z3hz8&t=163s

**4. John Smoltz — "It’s the hardest sport to play. It’s way harder than hockey." — John Smoltz on the physical demands of playing professional football** [05:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IgXXW0z3hz8&t=315s)

John Smoltz: "It’s hard for me to go away from basketball as athletes, only because football has a restriction of so many games. You would never see any football players if you had to play more than 17 games. It’s the hardest sport to play. It’s way harder than hockey. But I think you could teach **LeBron James** at a tight end inside the 10-yard line to dominate that 10-yard line. I’m surprised a football team hasn’t tried to expose that. Now, don’t get me wrong, the DBs are off the charts in football. Those athletes could stop a 6’5” guy with their athleticism, but it’s hard for me to believe that they could stop a 6’10” athletic freak just inside the 20-yard line."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IgXXW0z3hz8&t=315s
