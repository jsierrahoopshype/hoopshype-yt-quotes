# God Shammgod on Training Young Kobe, Kyrie Irving’s Handles & Playing With Ron Artest In High School — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

**1. God Shammgod — "Ron was actively crazy but wasn't crazy in high school." — God Shammgod on Ron Artest's high school reputation** [00:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fNfSuiutXRk&t=15s)

God Shammgod: "You know what's so crazy about Ron? Ron was actively crazy but wasn't crazy in high school. So that's why when Ron got in the NBA, people were like, 'Yo, you know Ron tough and he crazy.' I'm like, 'Ron? Like, no man.' I used to have to go snatch Ron by his jersey. Like, 'Come on man, get over here, what you doing man?' Because Ron was two years younger than me. I'm like, 'Man, come sit down man, what are you doing, bro?' Because coach would be like, 'Man, go get Ron.' I'm like, 'Ron, sit down.'"

**2. God Shammgod — "Ron was like he wasn't the NBA but less. He always worked hard." — God Shammgod on Ron Artest's development and work ethic** [00:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fNfSuiutXRk&t=38s)

God Shammgod: "When Ron first came to La Salle, Ron was like he wasn't the NBA but less. Like, he didn't do nothing really good, nothing really exceptional. He always worked hard, but he really couldn't dribble at this time, he really couldn't shoot at this time. But he would see me come to the gym every morning at 6:00 and workout. And then me and him would work out and do stuff. I would talk to him about stuff, and then he always had massive heart. So I would be like, 'Oh, come on Ron, I got you.' So he just became my little brother for real."

**4. God Shammgod — "We play in the tournament up there, we play against Kobe up there." — God Shammgod on playing against Kobe Bryant in high school** [04:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fNfSuiutXRk&t=279s)

God Shammgod: "We go to Rhode Island tournament. This when I first committed to Providence. And we playing in the tournament up there, we play against Kobe up there. And Kobe, I'm telling him, I'm like, 'Yo,' I'm like, 'Yo,' because Kobe played with me at ABCD. And his father comes up to me and his father is like, 'Hey, can you show my son how to dribble?' And I was like, 'Okay, whatever.' Like, a lot of people always ask me to show somebody how to dribble, so I'm like, 'Okay, cool.' So we play a game, and like I said, I just got announced to be McDonald's. So I'm like, 'Yeah, this my team.' We get on the court, this dude is shooting. I didn't know who he is. This when he first came, I'm like, 'Who is this dude shooting all the balls?' This is my show. And he like shooting all the balls, talking like Jordan, walking like Jordan. And you know how kids be. We like, 'Man, who this nigga think he is? He think he Jordan, bro?'"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fNfSuiutXRk&t=279s
