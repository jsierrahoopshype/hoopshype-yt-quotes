# T-Mac Honors Amar’e at the Hall of Fame + Does an MVP Mean More Than a Championship? — *Cousins with Vince Carter & Tracy McGrady*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KgK4tDdSAOk

_Speakers identified: Vince Carter, Tracy McGrady_

**1. Tracy McGrady — "Competitors always are putting everything into it. I know Kobe, he got that same approach. AI, he has that same approach." — Tracy McGrady defends the competitive drive of past NBA stars** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KgK4tDdSAOk&t=0s)

**Tracy McGrady:** "The whole ring thing, I get it. We are not in that conversation because we were not in those situations. But you are not going to sit here and tell me that if we were in that situation, we could not win a championship."

**Vince Carter:** "Especially when you know who you got to deal with on the other side of the ball."

**Tracy McGrady:** "Exactly. Competitors always are putting everything into it. I know Kobe, he got that same approach. AI, he has that same approach. **Paul Pierce**, Vince, they got that same approach. So, just because this is a Wednesday night in December, that does not mean anything? No, this means a lot. Competitors always are putting everything into it."

**2. Tracy McGrady — "One thing I do not like, and athletes do not like, is public speaking. I am already nervous." — Tracy McGrady on the anxiety of public speaking at the Hall of Fame** [07:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KgK4tDdSAOk&t=449s)

Tracy McGrady: "I am a Gemini, so when I got the call that I was getting inducted into the Hall of Fame, one thing I do not like, and athletes do not like, is public speaking. I am already nervous. I am talking about, I have not even hung up the phone yet. I am already nervous. Damn, it is the thought. It is the thought. So I am already nervous as hell. Mind you, we are a whole year out. I bull-shoot you not, I started working on that speech a year out. I started working on it because I wanted to make sure when I stand up there and deliver this speech, that regardless of the emotions that comes over me, I still can stay firm and deliver what I got to because I do not have to look at the prompter. I know what I want to say and how I want to say it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KgK4tDdSAOk&t=449s

**3. Tracy McGrady — "Yes, the MVP of the regular season has more weight than a championship because it is individual." — Tracy McGrady argues regular season MVP holds more weight than a championship** [24:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KgK4tDdSAOk&t=1474s)

**Vince Carter:** "And the reason I am asking that, because everybody at the end of the day, you think about this, when we talk about the totality of a player's career, and even when you talk about Hall of Fame, and when you talk about the best players, the first thing they say, oh, oh, but he did not get a ring, or he got a ring."

**Tracy McGrady:** "Name the 10th or the 11th player on the Chicago Bulls or the Los Angeles Lakers or the Detroit Pistons or the Boston Celtics. Who are they? Name me any of those team, name me the 11th or the 12th guy."

**Vince Carter:** "I mean, I can't right now."

**Tracy McGrady:** "He got a ring. And you can't even think of him. He has a ring, and you can't even think of him. He has a ring. Does he have an MVP? Absolutely not. No. So, yes, the MVP of the regular season has more weight than a championship because it is individual. It is ownership. It is management. It is health. It is all this shit that plays into winning a championship. So, yeah, I mean, MVP, bro, that is you putting in some work against all of the other great players of that season. That is playing the same schedule, the same 82 games. You are playing the same teams three and four times, sometimes two. So, come on, man. No, bro. I am going with the MVP. We all want to win a championship, but that is a collective thing. That MVP, that is individual."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KgK4tDdSAOk&t=1474s
