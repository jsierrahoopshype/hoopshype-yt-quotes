## Explaining Steph Curry, Ayesha Curry's Charity Impact In Oakland — *Dalton Johnson*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ceoJWsMaiCc

_Speakers identified: Dalton Johnson, Christopher Helfrich, Aneel Bhusri_

**1. Christopher Helfrich — "the generosity that Workday has brought to this event and that's allowed it to grow" — Growth and impact of the Eat. Learn. Play. charity event** [00:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ceoJWsMaiCc&t=5s)

**Dalton Johnson:** "Starting with you, Chris, when I say the number seven, how exciting does that feel to get to, we're closing on a decade doing this, of course with Aneel as well too?"

**Christopher Helfrich:** "Time flies. It is pretty surreal that we're at seven years and to see the way that this event has grown. It's pretty remarkable. The generosity that Workday has brought to this event and that's allowed it to grow into something that's raised well over $10 million now that's 100% of that goes right back out into the community. It's remarkable, it's gratifying, and it's also the most fun day of the year."

**2. Aneel Bhusri — "It all starts with this guy and the Currys. We're just the enabler." — Aneel Bhusri on the Currys' community impact in Oakland** [00:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ceoJWsMaiCc&t=51s)

**Dalton Johnson:** "Aneel, do you allow yourself to look back at the beginning of this and see how much it's grown to these stages?"

**Aneel Bhusri:** "I haven't recently until I drove up here today and I thought, my god, the seventh year. It all starts with this guy and the Currys. We're just the enabler. We bring together a bunch of people that are willing to invest in what Steph and Ayesha and **Chris** want to do in Oakland and the Bay Area for kids and it's pretty amazing what they've done."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ceoJWsMaiCc&t=51s

**3. Christopher Helfrich — "over 10,000 kids every day are going and playing, learning and growing on these state of the art spaces" — Eat. Learn. Play. impact on Oakland school playground renovations** [01:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ceoJWsMaiCc&t=108s)

**Dalton Johnson:** "Just 10 days ago was the 25th school year that Eat. Learn. Play. has transformed in Oakland. When I say 25, what does that number mean to you?"

**Christopher Helfrich:** "It means that there's a lot of kids who are finally getting the spaces that they deserve. When we started remodeling schoolyards across the entire district, there's 49 elementary schools, really the play spaces were just blacktop and an old playground and a couple basketball hoops. But now, through this work, over 10,000 kids every day are going and playing, learning and growing on these state of the art spaces. That ambition was really driven by Workday and Aneel. When we did our second, it was Aneel who called me the next day and said that was amazing, can we do all of them? And that's been the journey that we've been on over the last couple of years."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ceoJWsMaiCc&t=108s
