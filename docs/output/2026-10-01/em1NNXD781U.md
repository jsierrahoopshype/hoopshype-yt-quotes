**1. John Smoltz — "We were all allowed to go through it, we were allowed to learn from it." — John Smoltz on the early struggles of the Braves dynasty** [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=em1NNXD781U&t=0s)

John Smoltz: "It didn't always start out that way. We all took our lumps. **Glavine** almost lost 20 games. I was two and nine my first time in the big leagues. We were allowed to go through it, we were allowed to learn from it. We weren't coddled, we weren't put handcuffs on us. They said go. And it served us so well that when we got a better team, we knew we were going to be better."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=em1NNXD781U&t=0s

**2. John Smoltz — "I was supposed to be the best pitcher on the staff because I had the best stuff." — John Smoltz on the burden of being the team's best pitcher** [00:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=em1NNXD781U&t=52s)

John Smoltz: "I had the burden of I was supposed to be the best pitcher on the staff because I had the best stuff. Well, that's a burden when you don't deliver statistically in the eyes of somebody else when they don't know what you're going through. So, I had the best stuff, but the best pitchers were **Glavine** and **Maddux**. Where I gained advantage was in the postseason. My stuff could play out in the postseason a little bit more than theirs."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=em1NNXD781U&t=52s

**3. John Smoltz — "For five years, I didn't breathe. I whipped myself so bad. I didn't enjoy anything." — John Smoltz on his self-criticism and learning to enjoy the game** [01:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=em1NNXD781U&t=93s)

John Smoltz: "For five years, I didn't breathe. I whipped myself so bad. I didn't enjoy anything. And it was the wrong way to go about it. I had to learn through my Italian grandmother, who didn't know nothing about baseball, but every time I'd see her, she goes, you're miserable. You never smile. This can't be fun. I had to try to explain to her like, Grandma, I got to have that snarl. That guy at the plate wants to take my head off. I got to look mean. You know I'm not mean. She goes, yeah, but you never smile."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=em1NNXD781U&t=93s

**4. John Smoltz — "There is a point in every game for the rest of my life that I'm going to choose to smile." — John Smoltz on his decision to smile and enjoy the game** [02:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=em1NNXD781U&t=131s)

John Smoltz: "I said, there is a point in every game for the rest of my life that I'm going to choose to smile to remember to enjoy that this is still not all work. And I started to learn how to become a better version of that competitive, fierce, give me the ball, give me the shot. I don't trust anybody else. It's not an ego thing. It's just I wanted to be in that spot. And so when that happened for me in the postseason, that's when my assets became, they came out and worked."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=em1NNXD781U&t=131s

**5. John Smoltz — "If everyone wants to be the hero, they're in trouble." — John Smoltz on the aggressive mindset required for postseason play** [02:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=em1NNXD781U&t=176s)

John Smoltz: "In the playoffs, if everyone wants to be the hero, they're in trouble. Everyone gets a little more aggressive. They know it counts more. They know there's more eyeballs on them. Some people don't want to be in that spot. I thrived in it. I love pressure. And so my ability to spin the baseball and throw a little harder worked better for me in the postseason because if the hitters are more aggressive and they want to swing, that's advantage me."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=em1NNXD781U&t=176s

**6. John Smoltz — "The regular season is a battle that you're trying to survive. The postseason's a war." — John Smoltz on the difference between regular season and postseason pitching** [03:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=em1NNXD781U&t=213s)

John Smoltz: "Whereas **Maddux** and **Glavine** were already elite. They already had command. Well, in the postseason, the strike zone shrinks and the hitters start taking away some of the things that make them, but they still had great postseason careers. It's just my stuff played up. If I faced a hitter and it was 3-1 count, I'm going to challenge them with a fastball. In the postseason, no sir, you're getting a slider. To me, the postseason was not a guarantee for another one, so pitch that game like it's your last. The regular season is a battle that you're trying to survive. The postseason's a war. You got to win it."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=em1NNXD781U&t=213s

**7. John Smoltz — "We shocked everyone in '91. It was an epic World Series." — John Smoltz on the Braves' World Series experiences in the 90s** [06:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=em1NNXD781U&t=365s)

John Smoltz: "We shocked everyone in '91. It was an epic World Series. Of course, I pitched Game 7 and we lost one to nothing in 11 innings. It was an epic. We lost all four games by one run. So, it shocked everyone. In '92, we go back and we lose to **Joe Carter**'s Blue Jays. And you know, at the end of the day, those were all one-run losses. Well, '93, even though we didn't get to the World Series, we lost to the Phillies and there's no reason, no way we should have lost. So, now we've had three straight years of dominance."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=em1NNXD781U&t=365s

**8. John Smoltz — "We should have won in '96. And we would have spun a four out of five like the Yankees did." — John Smoltz on the heartbreak of the 1996 World Series loss** [07:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=em1NNXD781U&t=447s)

John Smoltz: "We should have won in '96. And we would have spun a four out of five like the Yankees did without a doubt. But when we lost in '96, that heartbreaking loss, we changed the dynamics of our team forever. We traded guys away, we started going in a different direction, and guess what, we still got back two more times. And it's just crazy to think that we could have 14 straight divisional titles, which probably will never be topped, and have only one championship."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=em1NNXD781U&t=447s

**9. John Smoltz — "We were built around pitching and if we got some timely hits, we would win." — John Smoltz on the Braves' pitching-heavy team identity** [08:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=em1NNXD781U&t=521s)

John Smoltz: "I couldn't believe, you know, we were almost last in runs, almost last in average, last in offensively a lot of categories and yet we won our division by like 29 games. We were first in everything in pitching. So, we were built around pitching and if we got some timely hits, we would win. Five of the six games against Cleveland were one-run games. And we ironically won the only championship one to nothing on **Tom Glavine**'s gem and we lost so many one-to-nothing games that flipped it the other way."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=em1NNXD781U&t=521s
