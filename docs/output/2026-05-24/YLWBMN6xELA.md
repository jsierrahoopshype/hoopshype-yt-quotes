# Tim Legler says Knicks can go toe-to-toe with both the Spurs & Thunder 👀 | SC with SVP — *NBA on ESPN*

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YLWBMN6xELA

_Speakers identified: Tim Legler, Scott Van Pelt, Jalen Brunson_

**1. Tim Legler — "the balance across the board of the ways they can beat you in the paint, from the three, and with their defense now, which has turned into an elite defense, it's just too much for the Cavaliers." — Tim Legler explains Knicks' balanced offense and elite defense overwhelming Cavaliers** [00:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YLWBMN6xELA&t=27s)

"Yeah, look, the **Cavaliers** have run into a better team that is playing their best basketball. And when you run into that and you've got this kind of balance up and down your starting lineup, very difficult to figure out a way to limit them because there's nothing that you can really do to expose some part of their team offensively. They tried that in Game 1. They weren't going to send help on **Brunson**. You saw how that worked out at the end of the game. Game 2, they're going to give him all kinds of traffic and **Josh Hart** goes off. And then in this game it was a little bit of both. So I just think the balance across the board of the ways they can beat you in the paint, from the three, and with their defense now, which has turned into an elite defense, it's just too much for the **Cavaliers**."

**2. Tim Legler — "you don't want to play any more games than you have to because the one thing that could derail something is somebody getting hurt." — Scott Van Pelt and Tim Legler discuss Knicks' urgency to finish series for rest** [01:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YLWBMN6xELA&t=100s)

**Scott Van Pelt:** "If you're New York and you recognize that maybe it's going to go for a while in the West. As exciting as it is to get to the finals, how much urgency is there? Let's get this thing done Monday and get as much rest as we can."

**Tim Legler:** "I think right now you're going to see them with their foot on the accelerator in Game 4, without question. There's not going to be a letup by this team. And the reason is this, **Scott**, when you're in this situation, you don't want to play any more games than you have to because the one thing that could derail something is somebody getting hurt. So just do the math. That's one less game you have to play, then get on to the finals. So they're going to come out with the right sense of urgency on Monday. There's no question about that."

**3. Tim Legler — "The Knicks have changed that narrative. The Knicks are now a team that can absolutely go toe-to-toe with those two teams." — Tim Legler asserts Knicks are an NBA championship-caliber team** [03:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YLWBMN6xELA&t=194s)

"There's no doubt, **Scott**, whoever comes out of the West, and I think everybody thought for a long time it was a foregone conclusion, whoever wins the West, that's your NBA champion. The **Knicks** have changed that narrative. The **Knicks** are now a team that can absolutely go toe-to-toe with those two teams, who by the way, still have a lot of work to do. So who knows what those teams will do to each other, trying to secure that spot in the finals across the New York **Knicks**. But **Scott**, think about this. You're talking about a team in the **Knicks** that is number two offensive rated team amongst all the playoff teams, number one defensive rated team, number one in three-point percentage, and they get about 60 points a night in the paint. Also number one in fast break points. There's just across the board, the totality of what they're hitting you with, nobody has had close to enough answers."

**4. Jalen Brunson — "it's fun playing in those type of environments for sure." — Jalen Brunson finds joy in winning in hostile road environments** [05:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YLWBMN6xELA&t=351s)

**Scott Van Pelt:** "Is there something to the fact that as great as the Garden is, and we talk about it all the time, maybe me too much sometimes? But is there maybe something that's even more fun about going on the road into a hostile environment and shutting a place up? Seems like maybe."

**Jalen Brunson:** "Yeah, playing in the Garden is amazing. There's no place like it. But when you're on the road with your teammates, and it's just you guys, you got some fans that travel, but it's a real hostile environment, it's fun playing in those type of environments for sure."

**5. Jalen Brunson — "We're not thinking about resting, we're thinking about what can we do to be better." — Jalen Brunson emphasizes Knicks' focus on winning, not resting** [07:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YLWBMN6xELA&t=429s)

"Yeah, I think our mindset is most important. We're not thinking about resting, we're thinking about what can we do to be better, what can we do to finish the possession, finish the quarter, finish the half, finish the game. That has to be our mindset. We can't look ahead at all, especially against these guys. Regardless of what it is now, it's 0-0. We got to go out there and win."
