**1. Tim McMahon — "They are throwing bodies at him, they are trying to wear him down, they are trying to make him play in crowds." — Victor Wembanyama's physical toll and heavy minutes in the series.** [0:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sNhqdkR9f0Y&t=38s)

"They are throwing bodies at him, they are trying to wear him down, they are trying to make him play in crowds. But we are talking about a series that is every other day."

**2. Tim McMahon — "OKC goes a legit 12 or 13 deep and a lot of these guys are coming into the game like the scoring is almost a bonus." — OKC's deep roster and relentless defensive pressure on Wembanyama.** [0:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sNhqdkR9f0Y&t=38s)

"OKC just has such a deep team. You see the depth with how lopsided the bench scoring is. They just scored 76 bench points, but you also feel that depth defensively, especially **Victor Wembanyama**. He is dealing with **Isaiah Hartenstein** and it is **Chet Holmgren**. Then coming off the bench, there is an **Alex Caruso**. There is a **Jalen Williams**, when the other **Jalen Williams** is healthy, he will take a turn on them. As **Chet Holmgren** said, the fresh legs out there really are able to try to wear him down, but they are not putting fresh legs on floor just to wear him down. As Chet so eloquently said, the dudes that are going in the game are dudes. OKC goes a legit 12 or 13 deep and a lot of these guys are coming into the game like the scoring is almost a bonus. These are physical, tough players and they are really making **Wemby** fight for every inch on the floor. Six dunks in Game 1, a total of two in Games 2 and 3."

**3. Tim McMahon — "Spurs game plan designed like, 'Hey, we want those guys shooting those shots because we don't want Shai Gilgeous-Alexander...'" — Spurs' defensive strategy against Shai Gilgeous-Alexander and Alex Caruso's regular season struggles.** [02:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sNhqdkR9f0Y&t=139s)

"A lot of these three-pointers, these are Spurs game plan designed like, 'Hey, we want those guys shooting those shots because we don't want **Shai Gilgeous-Alexander** to be able to get where he wants to go.' They are really crowding things, clogging the driving lanes against Shai, and they are invitation threes, or at least they were. **Alex Caruso** was 5 of 21 during the regular season on threes against the Spurs. The game plan worked. He was 2 of 12 on Christmas."

**4. Tim McMahon — "He is lights out in this series, but he is the epitome of a playoff riser." — Alex Caruso's playoff rise and the Thunder's confident role players.** [02:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sNhqdkR9f0Y&t=139s)

"He is lights out in this series, but he is the epitome of a playoff riser. He shot better than 40% from three in last year's playoff run, which obviously ended in a championship for the Thunder. They have got shooters. **Isaiah Joe** is barely playing this series. He is a shooter. **Jared McCain**, I am not so sure that **Daryl Morey** sold high after all. He just has zero fear. But he comes in and his teammates say, 'You know what is going up,' and you always feel like it is going in when the ball hits his hands. They have got all these guys who know their roles, they play with confidence, they play with freedom, and they are taking the shots that are presented to them because the Spurs don't want to let the two-time MVP dominate the game."

**5. Tim McMahon — "Make him play in crowds, get him off the ball. To sum it up briefly, it is get him off the ball." — Spurs' strategy to get Shai Gilgeous-Alexander off the ball** [3:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sNhqdkR9f0Y&t=223s)

"Make him play in crowds, get him off the ball. To sum it up briefly, it is get him off the ball. **Steph Castle** is taking the primary assignment. They are giving him a lot of help, which he is usually not a guy who gets a lot of help. That is the kind of weapon that **Shai Gilgeous-Alexander** is. They are doubling often, they are doubling randomly, so it is not predictable for **Shai Gilgeous-Alexander**. They are basically daring him, make quote-unquote the right play, make the right read, get off the ball, don't try to force anything. It hasn't been the biggest scoring series for **Shai Gilgeous-Alexander**, just like by the way it wasn't last series against the Lakers, but he is making the right plays, he is getting his teammates open shots and he has had 12 assists in two of these games during the series. That is a playoff career high. So that just proves that he is reading the game and he is if you don't want to let him dominate, he is going to trust his teammates and his teammates delivered last series, they are delivering again right now."

**6. Tim McMahon — "This is the biggest test of Victor Wembanyama's career." — Victor Wembanyama's biggest career test against the Thunder** [04:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sNhqdkR9f0Y&t=297s)

"This is a huge test. This is the biggest test of **Victor Wembanyama**'s career. The Spurs came into this series with a ton of confidence. They beat the Thunder four out of five times during the regular season. That included three wins in 12 days after the Thunder got off to that 24 and 1 start. They spanked their butts in front of a national TV audience on Christmas Day in OKC's gym, but the playoffs are a different beast."

**7. Tim McMahon — "This is not a healthy Spurs team. De'Aaron Fox is clearly not 100%." — Spurs' injury woes, Wembanyama's advantages unexploited, playoff pressure** [04:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sNhqdkR9f0Y&t=297s)

"Let's just also call it like it is for the Spurs. This is not a healthy Spurs team. They have a blank injury report right now, but **De'Aaron Fox** is clearly not 100%. He is playing through a high ankle sprain. **Dillon Harper** is playing through adductor soreness. He is clearly not 100%. As Mr. Johnson said today, **Victor Wembanyama** creates a lot of the advantages and then those Spurs guards throughout the course of the year have been great at exploiting them. That hasn't really happened in this series. Fox missed the first couple games, Harper wasn't the same player in Game 3, but there are no excuses. There is pressure and for a team that is trying to skip the line, trying to win a championship in their first playoffs together, I agree with you. You can't let this thing go back to OKC down 3-1."

**10. Shaquille O'Neal — "I'm going to need Victor Wembanyama to do more. Game 1 he had 41, 24, they won. Game 2, 21, 26, it's not enough." — Shaquille O'Neal demands more dominance from Victor Wembanyama** [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sNhqdkR9f0Y&t=485s)

I do not want to see a turnaround. He has to be aggressive, super aggressive, so they can panic with him and free up the other guys."

**11. Kenny Smith — "They have 11 guys on their team I would believe that if you got they got 20 points, no one would be surprised." — Kenny Smith praises OKC's 'special others' for their scoring depth** [8:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sNhqdkR9f0Y&t=535s)
