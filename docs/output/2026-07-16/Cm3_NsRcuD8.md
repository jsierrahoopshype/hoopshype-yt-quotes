**1. Chris Mannix — "We are seeing a player who is not concerned about money, whose contract will not affect the books" — LeBron James is making decisions based on personal preference, not money** [00:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cm3_NsRcuD8&t=56s)

Chris Mannix: "We are seeing a player who is not concerned about money, whose contract will not affect the books of whatever team that he signs with. Make a decision purely based on what his personal preferences are. At this point, I proudly say I have no idea. I was sitting in Vegas having conversations with a lot of different people over the course of the last few days, and I can tell you they have no idea. Some of these people are from teams who are in the mix for **LeBron James**, told me they have no idea. Whatever he decides to do, and whenever he decides to do it, it will absolutely come out of nowhere for most people."

**2. Rachel Nichols — "Nobody has additional information. When someone says, oh, I think it's kind of trending toward Cleveland" — League executives have no inside information on LeBron James's next team** [01:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cm3_NsRcuD8&t=102s)

**Rachel Nichols:** "All of these aggregation stories where you see it's trending toward Cleveland, or people around the league think it's Cleveland's to lose, conversations being had around the league between league executives with each other, with reporters, are the exact same conversation you and I are having. Nobody has additional information. When someone says, oh, I think it's kind of trending toward Cleveland because of course where else can you see him going, it's such a good story, that is their opinion on how good a story it is and whether that would appeal to **LeBron James**. It has nothing to do about whether his decision is actually trending toward Cleveland."

**Chris Mannix:** "From the executive side, players, those conversations are happening for sure. People are calling LeBron, LeBron is calling people, but absolutely from an executive side, you are not getting those things."
